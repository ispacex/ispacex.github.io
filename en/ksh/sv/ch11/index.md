<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v11.1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जगदिदमथवा सुहृदो बन्धुजनो वा न भवति मम किमपि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jagadidamathavā suhṛdo bandhujano vā na bhavati mama kimapi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वं पुनरेतत्सर्वं यदा तदा कोऽपरो मेऽस्तु॥११.१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvaṁ punaretatsarvaṁ yadā tadā ko'paro me'stu||11.1||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Neither<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>idam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>world<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-jagat" class="pv-gl">jagat</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>nor<rp> (</rp><rt>athavā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>friends<rp> (</rp><rt>suhṛdaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>nor<rp> (</rp><rt>vā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>kin<rp> (</rp><rt>bandhu-janaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>mean<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to me<rp> (</rp><rt>mama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>anything<rp> (</rp><rt>kimapi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>But<rp> (</rp><rt>punaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>when<rp> (</rp><rt>yadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>all this<rp> (</rp><rt>etatsarvaṁ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>then<rp> (</rp><rt>tadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>who<rp> (</rp><rt>kaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>else<rp> (</rp><rt>aparaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is needed<rp> (</rp><rt>astu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||11.1||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v11.2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वामिन्महेश्वरस्त्वं साक्षात्सर्वं जगत्त्वमेवेति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svāminmaheśvarastvaṁ sākṣātsarvaṁ jagattvameveti|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वस्त्वेव सिद्धिमेत्विति याच्ञा तत्रापि याच्ञैव॥११.२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vastveva siddhimetviti yācñā tatrāpi yācñaiva||11.2||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>"Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-svamin" class="pv-gl">svāmin</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>You are Maheśvara in person<rp> (</rp><rt>sākṣāt maheśvaraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>You alone<rp> (</rp><rt>tvameva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>the whole<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>world<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-jagat" class="pv-gl">jagat</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>", — <ruby>thus<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>And the request<rp> (</rp><rt>yācñā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>that this very thing<rp> (</rp><rt>vastveva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>be fulfilled<rp> (</rp><rt>siddhimetu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>and it<rp> (</rp><rt>tatrāpi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is still the same request<rp> (</rp><rt>yācñā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||11.2||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v11.3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्रिभुवनाधिपतित्वमपीह यत्तृणमिव प्रतिभाति भवज्जुषः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tribhuvanādhipatitvamapīha yattṛṇamiva pratibhāti bhavajjuṣaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">किमिव तस्य फलं शुभकर्मणो भवति नाथ भवत्स्मरणादृते॥११.३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kimiva tasya phalaṁ śubhakarmaṇo bhavati nātha bhavatsmaraṇādṛte||11.3||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>if<rp> (</rp><rt>yat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>lordship over the three worlds<rp> (</rp><rt>tri-bhuvana-adhipatitvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>seems<rp> (</rp><rt>pratibhāti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>here<rp> (</rp><rt>iha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>a blade of grass<rp> (</rp><rt>tṛṇam iva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to one devoted to You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-juṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>then what<rp> (</rp><rt>kimiva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>fruit<rp> (</rp><rt>phalam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is there<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for his<rp> (</rp><rt>tasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>good deeds<rp> (</rp><rt>śubha-karmaṇaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>except<rp> (</rp><rt>ṛte</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>remembrance of You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-smaraṇāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||11.3||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v11.4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">येन नैव भवतोऽस्ति विभिन्नं किञ्चनापि जगतां प्रभवश्च।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yena naiva bhavato'sti vibhinnaṁ kiñcanāpi jagatāṁ prabhavaśca|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वद्विजृम्भितमतोऽद्भुतकर्मस्वप्युदेति न तव स्तुतिबन्धः॥११.४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvadvijṛmbhitamato'dbhutakarmasvapyudeti na tava stutibandhaḥ||11.4||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Rasa<rp> (</rp><rt>yena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of the separate<rp> (</rp><rt>vibhinnam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> there is nothing <span class="pv-w">(kiñcana api... na eva asti)</span>, <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the birth of worlds<rp> (</rp><rt>jagatām prabhavaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>Your unfolding<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-vijṛmbhitam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>therefore<rp> (</rp><rt>ataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>upon Your wondrous deeds<rp> (</rp><rt>adbhuta-karmasu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>arises<rp> (</rp><rt>udeti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to You<rp> (</rp><rt>tava</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>a hymn of praise<rp> (</rp><rt>stuti-bandhaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||11.4||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v11.5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वन्मयोऽस्मि भवदर्चननिष्ठः सर्वदाहमिति चाप्यविरामम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvanmayo'smi bhavadarcananiṣṭhaḥ sarvadāhamiti cāpyavirāmam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भावयन्नपि विभो स्वरसेन स्वप्नगोऽपि न तथा किमिव स्याम्॥११.५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhāvayannapi vibho svarasena svapnago'pi na tathā kimiva syām||11.5||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>“I<rp> (</rp><rt>aham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>am made of You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-mayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>I<rp> (</rp><rt>asmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>am devoted to the worship of You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-arcana-niṣṭhaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>” — <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>though<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I contemplate<rp> (</rp><rt>bhāvayan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>without interruption<rp> (</rp><rt>aviramam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>why then<rp> (</rp><rt>kimiva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>All-pervading One<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vibhu" class="pv-gl">vibho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>am I not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>such<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by my own taste<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-rasena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>when going into sleep<rp> (</rp><rt>svapna-gaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||11.5||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v11.6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ये मनागपि भवच्चरणाब्जोद्भूतसौरभलवेन विमृष्टः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ye manāgapi bhavaccaraṇābjodbhūtasaurabhalavena vimṛṣṭaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तेषु विस्रमिव भवति समस्तं भोगजातममरैरपि मृग्यम्॥११.६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Teṣu visramiva bhavati samastaṁ bhogajātamamarairapi mṛgyam||11.6||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>For those<rp> (</rp><rt>teṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who<rp> (</rp><rt>ye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even slightly<rp> (</rp><rt>manāk api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>are touched<rp> (</rp><rt>vimṛṣṭaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> by a grain of <ruby>fragrance rising from Your lotus-feet<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-caraṇa-abja-udbhūta-saurabha-lavena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>all<rp> (</rp><rt>samastam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the multitude of pleasures<rp> (</rp><rt>bhoga-jātam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which is sought<rp> (</rp><rt>mṛgyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by the immortals<rp> (</rp><rt>amaraiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>becomes<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>like<rp> (</rp><rt>iva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>raw meat<rp> (</rp><rt>visram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||11.6||</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v11.7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हृदि ते न तु विद्यतेऽन्यदन्यद्वचने कर्मणि चान्यदेव शम्भो।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Hṛdi te na tu vidyate'nyadanyadvacane karmaṇi cānyadeva śambho|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">परमार्थसतोऽप्य् अनुग्रहो वा यदि वा निग्रह एक एव कार्यः॥११.७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Paramārthasato'py anugraho vā yadi vā nigraha eka eva kāryaḥ||11.7||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Śambhu<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-shambhu" class="pv-gl">śambho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>in You<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> there is not(na tu) <ruby>one thing<rp> (</rp><rt>vidyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the heart<rp> (</rp><rt>anyat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>another<rp> (</rp><rt>hṛdi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in speech<rp> (</rp><rt>anyat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the deed<rp> (</rp><rt>vacane</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>a third<rp> (</rp><rt>anyadeva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in action<rp> (</rp><rt>karmaṇi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Since You exist in the highest truth<rp> (</rp><rt>parama-artha-sataḥ api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>then one should do<rp> (</rp><rt>kāryaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>but one thing alone<rp> (</rp><rt>ekaḥ eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>grace<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-anugraha" class="pv-gl">anugrahaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>or<rp> (</rp><rt>vā yadi vā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>punishment<rp> (</rp><rt>nigrahaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||11.7||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v11.8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मूढोऽस्मि दुःखकलितोऽस्मि जरादिदोषभीतोऽस्मि शक्तिरहितोऽस्मि तवाश्रितोऽस्मि।</span><br />

<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शम्भो तथा कलय शीघ्रमुपैमि येन सर्वोत्तमां धुरमपोज्झितदुःखमार्गः॥११.८॥</span><br />

<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वंशान्तरालपतितानि जलैकदेशखण्डानि मौक्तिकमणित्वमिवोद्वहन्ति॥११.९॥</span><br />

<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शिशिरमयूखशेखर तथा कुरु येन मम क्षतमरणोऽणिमादिकमुपैमि यथा विभवम्॥११.१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śiśiramayūkhaśekhara tathā kuru yena mama kṣatamaraṇo'ṇimādikamupaimi yathā vibhavam||11.10||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>ah<rp> (</rp><rt>bata</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>what<rp> (</rp><rt>kimiva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is attained<rp> (</rp><rt>labhyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>those<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>people<rp> (</rp><rt>janaiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who<rp> (</rp><rt>ye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even for a moment<rp> (</rp><rt>kṣaṇam api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>feignedly<rp> (</rp><rt>kaitavāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>are devoted<rp> (</rp><rt>ratāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to Your<rp> (</rp><rt>tava</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>name<rp> (</rp><rt>nāmni</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>? <ruby>Wearing the cool-rayed one in his crown<rp> (</rp><rt>śiśira-mayūkha-śekhara</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>make<rp> (</rp><rt>kuru</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>so<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>yena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I, with death destroyed<rp> (</rp><rt>kṣata-maraṇaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>may come<rp> (</rp><rt>upaimi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to the wealth<rp> (</rp><rt>vibhavam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>aṇimā and other perfections<rp> (</rp><rt>aṇimā-ādikam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||11.10||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v11.11" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शम्भो शर्व शशङ्कशेखर शिव त्र्यक्षाक्षमालाधर श्रीमन्नुग्रकपाललाञ्छन लसद्भीमत्रिशूलायुध।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śambho śarva śaśaṅkaśekhara śiva tryakṣākṣamālādhara śrīmannugrakapālalāñchana lasadbhīmatriśūlāyudha|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कारुण्याम्बुनिधे त्रिलोकरचनाशीलोग्रशक्त्यात्मक श्रीकण्ठाशु विनाशयाशुभभरानाधत्स्वसिद्धिं पराम्॥११.११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kāruṇyāmbunidhe trilokaracanāśīlograśaktyātmaka śrīkaṇṭhāśu vināśayāśubhabharānādhatsvasiddhiṁ parām||11.11||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1">Śambhu, Śarva, wearing the moon in his crown, Śiva, the Three-eyed, holding the rosary, the blessed one, marked with the terrible skull, with the shining fierce trident <span class="pv-w">(śambho śarva śaśāṅka-śekhara śiva tri-akṣa akṣa-mālā-dhara śrīmat ugra-kapāla-lāñchana lasat-bhīma-triśūla-āyudha)</span>! <ruby>Ocean of mercy<rp> (</rp><rt>kāruṇya-ambu-nidhe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, consisting <ruby>of the fierce Power, whose custom is to build the three worlds<rp> (</rp><rt>tri-loka-racanā-śīla-ugra-śakti-ātmaka</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Śrīkaṇṭha<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-shrikantha" class="pv-gl">śrīkaṇṭha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>Quickly<rp> (</rp><rt>āśu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>destroy<rp> (</rp><rt>vināśaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the burden of the inauspicious<rp> (</rp><rt>aśubha-bharān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and grant<rp> (</rp><rt>ādhatsva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the supreme<rp> (</rp><rt>parām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>perfection<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-siddhi" class="pv-gl">siddhim</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||11.11||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v11.12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्किं नाथ भवेन्न यत्र भगवान्निर्मातृतामश्नुते भावः स्यात्किमु तस्य चेतनवतो नाशास्ति यं शङ्करः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatkiṁ nātha bhavenna yatra bhagavānnirmātṛtāmaśnute bhāvaḥ syātkimu tasya cetanavato nāśāsti yaṁ śaṅkaraḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इत्थं ते परमेश्वराक्षतमहाशक्तेः सदा संश्रितः संसारेऽत्र निरन्तराधिविधुरः क्लिश्याम्यहं केवलम्॥११.१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Itthaṁ te parameśvarākṣatamahāśakteḥ sadā saṁśritaḥ saṁsāre'tra nirantarādhividhuraḥ kliśyāmyahaṁ kevalam||11.12||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Lord, what<rp> (</rp><rt>tatkiṁ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavet</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that in which<rp> (</rp><rt>yatra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>would not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>attain<rp> (</rp><rt>aśnute</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>creative power<rp> (</rp><rt>nirmātṛtām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>? <ruby>And is there<rp> (</rp><rt>kimu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>an animate<rp> (</rp><rt>cetanavataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>being<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhava" class="pv-gl">bhāvaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>whom<rp> (</rp><rt>yam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>would not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>be<rp> (</rp><rt>asti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with Śaṅkara<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-shankara" class="pv-gl">śaṅkaraḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>? <ruby>Thus<rp> (</rp><rt>ittham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Supreme Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-parameshvara" class="pv-gl">parama-īśvara</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>to Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>whose great Power is unbroken<rp> (</rp><rt>akṣata-mahā-śakteḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>I always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>cling<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁśritaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>but<rp> (</rp><rt>kevalam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in this<rp> (</rp><rt>atra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>cycle<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-samsara" class="pv-gl">saṁsāre</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I suffer<rp> (</rp><rt>kliśyāmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I<rp> (</rp><rt>aham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with unceasing anguish<rp> (</rp><rt>nirantara-ādhi-vidhuraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||11.12||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v11.13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यद्यप्यत्र वरप्रदोद्धततमाः पीडाजरामृत्यव एते वा क्षणमासतां बहुमतः शब्दादिरेवास्थिरः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yadyapyatra varapradoddhatatamāḥ pīḍājarāmṛtyava ete vā kṣaṇamāsatāṁ bahumataḥ śabdādirevāsthiraḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्रापि स्पृहयामि सन्ततसुखाकाङ्क्षी चिरं स्थास्नवे भोगास्वादयुतत्वदङ्घ्रिकमलध्यानाग्र्य जीवातवे॥११.१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatrāpi spṛhayāmi santatasukhākāṅkṣī ciraṁ sthāsnave bhogāsvādayutatvadaṅghrikamaladhyānāgrya jīvātave||11.13||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1">Let (yadi api) <ruby>here<rp> (</rp><rt>atra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>pain, old age and death<rp> (</rp><rt>pīḍā-jarā-mṛtyavaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>the most fierce of those who bestow gifts<rp> (</rp><rt>vara-prada-uddhata-tamāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>these very ones<rp> (</rp><rt>ete</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>or<rp> (</rp><rt>vā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>let for a moment<rp> (</rp><rt>kṣaṇam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>remain<rp> (</rp><rt>āsatām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>highly valued<rp> (</rp><rt>bahu-mataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the impermanent<rp> (</rp><rt>asthiraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>sound and so forth<rp> (</rp><rt>śabda-ādiḥ eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>And yet<rp> (</rp><rt>tatrāpi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I, longing for unbroken happiness<rp> (</rp><rt>santata-sukha-ākāṅkṣī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>for long<rp> (</rp><rt>ciram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>yearn<rp> (</rp><rt>spṛhayāmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for that which stands firm<rp> (</rp><rt>sthāsnave</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>for that life<rp> (</rp><rt>jīvātave</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, the first thing in which is the contemplation of Your lotus-feet together with the taste of pleasures <span class="pv-note">— the stanza is obscure: the connection of the second half with the first admits another division too —</span> <span class="pv-w">(bhoga-āsvāda-yuta-tvat-aṅghri-kamala-dhyāna-agrya)</span>||11.13||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v11.14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हे नाथ प्रणतार्तिनाशनपटो श्रेयोनिधे धूर्जटे दुःखैकायतनस्य जन्ममरणत्रस्तस्य मे साम्प्रतम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">He nātha praṇatārtināśanapaṭo śreyonidhe dhūrjaṭe duḥkhaikāyatanasya janmamaraṇatrastasya me sāmpratam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तच्चेष्टस्व यथा मनोज्ञविषयास्वादप्रदा उत्तमा जीवन्नेव समश्नुवेऽहमचलाः सिद्धीस्त्वदर्चापरः॥११.१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tacceṣṭasva yathā manojñaviṣayāsvādapradā uttamā jīvanneva samaśnuve'hamacalāḥ siddhīstvadarcāparaḥ||11.14||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>he nātha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>skilled in destroying the grief of those who have taken refuge<rp> (</rp><rt>praṇata-ārti-nāśana-paṭo</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>treasury of good<rp> (</rp><rt>śreyaḥ-nidhe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Dhūrjaṭi<rp> (</rp><rt>dhūrjaṭe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>Now<rp> (</rp><rt>sāmpratam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the sole receptacle of grief<rp> (</rp><rt>duḥkha-eka-āyatanasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>terrified by birth and death<rp> (</rp><rt>janma-maraṇa-trastasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>strive<rp> (</rp><rt>ceṣṭasva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for that<rp> (</rp><rt>tat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>so that<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I<rp> (</rp><rt>aham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>devoted to the worship of You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-arcā-paraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>while still alive<rp> (</rp><rt>jīvan eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>may attain<rp> (</rp><rt>samaśnuve</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the highest<rp> (</rp><rt>uttamāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>unwavering<rp> (</rp><rt>acalāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>perfections<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-siddhi" class="pv-gl">siddhīḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>granting the taste of objects dear to the heart<rp> (</rp><rt>manojña-viṣaya-āsvāda-pradāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||11.14||</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v11.15" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नमो मोहमहाध्वान्तध्वंसनानन्यकर्मणे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Namo mohamahādhvāntadhvaṁsanānanyakarmaṇe|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वप्रकाशातिशयप्रकाशायेन्दुलक्ष्मणे॥११.१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvaprakāśātiśayaprakāśāyendulakṣmaṇe||11.15||</span></p>
