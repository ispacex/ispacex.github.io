# Glossary of terms

*This dictionary is not in the book: it was compiled for the Russian translation from those very
Sanskrit annotations that appear in parentheses next to each Russian word. Selection was necessary:
there are over five thousand annotations, and most often they contain function words —
`api` "also" two hundred and eight times. What remains here is what the reader stumbles over.*

The vocabulary of the hymns is not the same as that of the [Parātriśikā-vivaraṇa](/en/ksh/pv/glossary/).
There it is a treatise, and its vocabulary is scholastic. Here there are verses, and the most frequent word of the entire
book is `bhakti`, devotion: ninety-four times. After it come its flavor,
nectar, and worship. Scholastic concepts are also present, but they stand where Utpaladeva
relies on them.

*The last column leads to those strophes where the term sounds most densely. The reverse
path also exists: the Sanskrit word in the translation's footnote itself leads here.*

*The ♪ button pronounces the term. The sound is synthesized in advance from the Devanagari spelling using the voice **Lekha** (hi_IN) — this is not a recording of a reader and not a reference for pronunciation: be careful with aspirated, retroflex, and long vowels.*

*The voice reads according to the rules of Hindi, and Hindi swallows the final short "a":
`शिव` he pronounced "śiv". Therefore, to a word ending in a consonant, a
visarga is appended — and the voice pronounces the nominative case, `śivaḥ`, by which the word is
named aloud. The only price for this is: at the end a slight aspiration is heard,
which is not present in the spelling above the button.*
