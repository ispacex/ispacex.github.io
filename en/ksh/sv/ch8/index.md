<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v8.1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यः प्रसादलव ईश्वरस्थितो या च भक्तिरिव मामुपेयुषी।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yaḥ prasādalava īśvarasthito yā ca bhaktiriva māmupeyuṣī|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तौ परस्परसमन्वितौ कदा तादृशे वपुषि रूढिमेष्यतः॥८.१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tau parasparasamanvitau kadā tādṛśe vapuṣi rūḍhimeṣyataḥ||8.1||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>That<rp> (</rp><rt>yaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>drop of grace<rp> (</rp><rt>prasāda-lavaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which abides in the Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>īśvara-sthitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>yā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>devotion<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakti" class="pv-gl">bhaktiḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which as it were<rp> (</rp><rt>iva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>has approached<rp> (</rp><rt>upeyuṣī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>me<rp> (</rp><rt>mām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>when<rp> (</rp><rt>kadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>they two<rp> (</rp><rt>tau</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>united with each other<rp> (</rp><rt>paraspara-samanvitau</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>will rise<rp> (</rp><rt>rūḍhim eṣyataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in such a<rp> (</rp><rt>tādṛśe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>body<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vapus" class="pv-gl">vapuṣi</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||8.1||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v8.2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वत्प्रभुत्वपरिचर्वणजन्मा कोऽप्युदेतु परितोषरसोऽन्तः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvatprabhutvaparicarvaṇajanmā ko'pyudetu paritoṣaraso'ntaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वकालमिह मे परमस्तु ज्ञानयोगमहिमादि विदूरे॥८.२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvakālamiha me paramastu jñānayogamahimādi vidūre||8.2||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Let it arise<rp> (</rp><rt>udetu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>within<rp> (</rp><rt>antaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>some<rp> (</rp><rt>kaḥ api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>juice of perfect contentment<rp> (</rp><rt>paritoṣa-rasaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>born from the tasting of Your dominion<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-prabhutva-paricarvaṇa-janmā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>and the greatness of knowledge, yoga, and the rest<rp> (</rp><rt>jñāna-yoga-mahimā-ādi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>let it be<rp> (</rp><rt>astu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>here<rp> (</rp><rt>iha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>at all times<rp> (</rp><rt>sarva-kālam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>only<rp> (</rp><rt>param</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>far away<rp> (</rp><rt>vidūre</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||8.2||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v8.3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">लोकवद्भवतु मे विषयेषु स्फीत एव भगवन्परितर्षः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Lokavadbhavatu me viṣayeṣu sphīta eva bhagavanparitarṣaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">केवलं तव शरीरतयैतान्लोकयेयमहमस्तविकल्पः॥८.३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kevalaṁ tava śarīratayaitānlokayeyamahamastavikalpaḥ||8.3||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>may I have<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>be<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavatu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>toward objects<rp> (</rp><rt>viṣayeṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>a thirst<rp> (</rp><rt>paritarṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>as abundant<rp> (</rp><rt>sphītaḥ eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>as with all<rp> (</rp><rt>loka-vat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>only<rp> (</rp><rt>kevalam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>may I<rp> (</rp><rt>aham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>devoid of discriminating thought<rp> (</rp><rt>asta-vikalpaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>see<rp> (</rp><rt>lokayeyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>them<rp> (</rp><rt>etān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>as Your<rp> (</rp><rt>tava</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>body<rp> (</rp><rt>śarīratayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||8.3||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v8.4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">देहभूमिषु तथा मनसि त्वं प्राणवर्त्मनि च भेदमुपेते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dehabhūmiṣu tathā manasi tvaṁ prāṇavartmani ca bhedamupete|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">संविदः पथिषु तेषु च तेन स्वात्मना मम भव स्फुटरूपः॥८.४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Saṁvidaḥ pathiṣu teṣu ca tena svātmanā mama bhava sphuṭarūpaḥ||8.4||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, who have come to division (bhedam upete) <ruby>in the stages of the body<rp> (</rp><rt>deha-bhūmiṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and also<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the mind<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-manas" class="pv-gl">manasi</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>on the path of breath<rp> (</rp><rt>prāṇa-vartmani</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>on those<rp> (</rp><rt>teṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>paths<rp> (</rp><rt>pathiṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-samvid" class="pv-gl">saṁvidaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>become<rp> (</rp><rt>bhava</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for me<rp> (</rp><rt>mama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>tena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Self of Yours<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-ātmanā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>whose form is clear<rp> (</rp><rt>sphuṭa-rūpaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||8.4||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v8.5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">निजनिजेषु पदेषु पतन्त्विमाः करणवृत्तय उल्लसिता मम।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nijanijeṣu padeṣu patantvimāḥ karaṇavṛttaya ullasitā mama|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">क्षणमपीश मनागपि मैव भूत्त्वदविभेदरसक्षतिसाहसम्॥८.५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kṣaṇamapīśa manāgapi maiva bhūttvadavibhedarasakṣatisāhasam||8.5||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Let these<rp> (</rp><rt>imāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>my<rp> (</rp><rt>mama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>surged-up<rp> (</rp><rt>ullasitāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>activities of the sense organs<rp> (</rp><rt>karaṇa-vṛttayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>fall<rp> (</rp><rt>patantu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>each to its own<rp> (</rp><rt>nija-nijeṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>place<rp> (</rp><rt>padeṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-ishvara" class="pv-gl">īśa</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>let there not be<rp> (</rp><rt>mā eva bhūt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even for a moment<rp> (</rp><rt>kṣaṇam api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and not even a hair’s breadth<rp> (</rp><rt>manāk api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of audacity to harm the rasa of non-differentiation from You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-avibheda-rasa-kṣati-sāhasam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||8.5||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v8.6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">लघुमसृणसिताच्छशीतलं भवदावेशवशेन भावयन्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Laghumasṛṇasitācchaśītalaṁ bhavadāveśavaśena bhāvayan|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वपुरखिलपदार्थपद्धतेर्व्यवहारानतिवर्तयेय तान्॥८.६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vapurakhilapadārthapaddhatervyavahārānativartayeya tān||8.6||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>By the power of entering into You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-āveśa-vaśena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>contemplating<rp> (</rp><rt>bhāvayan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the body<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vapus" class="pv-gl">vapuḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of the entire sequence of things<rp> (</rp><rt>akhila-padārtha-paddhateḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> as <ruby>light, smooth, white, transparent, and cool<rp> (</rp><rt>laghu-masṛṇa-sita-accha-śītalam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — may I not transgress (na ativartayeya) <ruby>those<rp> (</rp><rt>tān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>customary practices<rp> (</rp><rt>vyavahārān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||8.6||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v8.7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विकसतु स्ववपुर्भवदात्मकं समुपयान्तु जगन्ति ममाङ्गताम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vikasatu svavapurbhavadātmakaṁ samupayāntu jaganti mamāṅgatām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">व्रजतु सर्वमिदं द्वयवल्गितं स्मृतिपथोपगमेऽप्यनुपाख्यताम्॥८.७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vrajatu sarvamidaṁ dvayavalgitaṁ smṛtipathopagame'pyanupākhyatām||8.7||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Let it open<rp> (</rp><rt>vikasatu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the own body<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-vapuḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>consisting of You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-ātmakam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>let the worlds<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-jagat" class="pv-gl">jaganti</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>become<rp> (</rp><rt>samupayāntu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>my<rp> (</rp><rt>mama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>limbs<rp> (</rp><rt>aṅgatām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>let all<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>idam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>dance of duality<rp> (</rp><rt>dvaya-valgitam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>go<rp> (</rp><rt>vrajatu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>into namelessness<rp> (</rp><rt>anupākhyatām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>when it comes<rp> (</rp><rt>upagame</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>onto the path of memory<rp> (</rp><rt>smṛti-patha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||8.7||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v8.8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">समुदियादपि तादृशतावकानन विलोक परामृतसम्प्लवः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Samudiyādapi tādṛśatāvakānana viloka parāmṛtasamplavaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मम घटेत यथा भवदद्वयाप्रथनघोरदरीपरिपूरणम्॥८.८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mama ghaṭeta yathā bhavadadvayāprathanaghoradarīparipūraṇam||8.8||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Thou, whose face is such<rp> (</rp><rt>tādṛśa-tāvaka-ānana</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>look<rp> (</rp><rt>viloka</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>May<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>rise<rp> (</rp><rt>samudiyāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the flood of supreme nectar<rp> (</rp><rt>para-amṛta-samplavaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>so that<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for me<rp> (</rp><rt>mama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>might come to pass<rp> (</rp><rt>ghaṭeta</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> the filling of the terrible <ruby>cave where non-duality with Thee does not shine<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-advaya-aprathana-ghora-darī-paripūraṇam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||8.8||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v8.9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अपि कदाचन तावकसङ्गमामृतकणाच्छुरणेन तनीयसा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Api kadācana tāvakasaṅgamāmṛtakaṇācchuraṇena tanīyasā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सकललोकसुखेषु पराङ्मुखो न भवितास्म्युभयच्युत एव किम्॥८.९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sakalalokasukheṣu parāṅmukho na bhavitāsmyubhayacyuta eva kim||8.9||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>What if<rp> (</rp><rt>kim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I, turning away<rp> (</rp><rt>parāṅ-mukhaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from all worldly joys<rp> (</rp><rt>sakala-loka-sukheṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for the sake of the subtlest<rp> (</rp><rt>tanīyasā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>trace of a drop of nectar of meeting with Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tāvaka-saṅgama-amṛta-kaṇa-ācchuraṇena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>fallen at any time<rp> (</rp><rt>api kadācana</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>shall I not become<rp> (</rp><rt>na bhavitā asmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>deprived of both<rp> (</rp><rt>ubhaya-cyutaḥ eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||8.9||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v8.10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सततमेव भवच्चरणाम्बुजाकरचरस्य हि हंसवरस्य मे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Satatameva bhavaccaraṇāmbujākaracarasya hi haṁsavarasya me|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उपरि मूलतलादपि चान्तरादुपनमत्वज भक्तिमृणालिका॥८.१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Upari mūlatalādapi cāntarādupanamatvaja bhaktimṛṇālikā||8.10||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Unborn One<rp> (</rp><rt>aja</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>To me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the best of swans<rp> (</rp><rt>haṁsa-varasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, who ceaselessly (satatam eva) <ruby>swims in the lake of Your lotus-feet<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-caraṇa-ambuja-ākara-carasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>may it be granted<rp> (</rp><rt>upanamatu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the stalk of the lotus of devotion<rp> (</rp><rt>bhakti-mṛṇālikā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and from above<rp> (</rp><rt>upari</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from the very bottom of the root<rp> (</rp><rt>mūla-talāt api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and from within<rp> (</rp><rt>antarāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||8.10||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v8.11" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उपयान्तु विभो समस्तवस्तून्यपि चिन्ताविषयं दृशः पदं च।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Upayāntu vibho samastavastūnyapi cintāviṣayaṁ dṛśaḥ padaṁ ca|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मम दर्शनचिन्तनप्रकाशामृतसाराणि परं परिस्फुरन्तु॥८.११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mama darśanacintanaprakāśāmṛtasārāṇi paraṁ parisphurantu||8.11||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O All-Pervading<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vibhu" class="pv-gl">vibho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>may all<rp> (</rp><rt>samastāni</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>things<rp> (</rp><rt>vastūni</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>become<rp> (</rp><rt>upayāntu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>an object of thought<rp> (</rp><rt>cintā-viṣayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that which the eyes see<rp> (</rp><rt>dṛśaḥ padam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>and may they in me<rp> (</rp><rt>mama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>shine<rp> (</rp><rt>parisphurantu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with but one<rp> (</rp><rt>param</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>essence of the nectarous light of seeing and thought<rp> (</rp><rt>darśana-cintana-prakāśa-amṛta-sārāṇi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||8.11||</p>
