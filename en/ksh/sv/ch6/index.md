# Hymn 6 — Adhvavisphuraṇākhyaṁ ṣaṣṭhaṁ stotram

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Hymn 5 — Svabalanideśanākhyaṁ pañcamaṁ stotram](/en/ksh/sv/ch5/) · [Hymn 7 — Vidhuravijayanāmadheyaṁ saptamaṁ stotram →](/en/ksh/sv/ch7/)</p>

<p class="pv-copy nosearch"><button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-sa">Copy Sanskrit</button> <button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-iast">Copy transliteration</button></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v6.1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">क्षणमात्रमपीशान वियुक्तस्य त्वया मम।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kṣaṇamātramapīśāna viyuktasya tvayā mama|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">निबिडं तप्यमानस्य सदा भूया दृशः पदम्॥६.१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nibiḍaṁ tapyamānasya sadā bhūyā dṛśaḥ padam||6.1||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-ishvara" class="pv-gl">īśāna</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>to me<rp> (</rp><rt>mama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>separated<rp> (</rp><rt>viyuktasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even for a moment<rp> (</rp><rt>kṣaṇa-mātram api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and fiercely<rp> (</rp><rt>nibiḍam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>burning<rp> (</rp><rt>tapyamānasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>be<rp> (</rp><rt>bhūyāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that which the eyes see<rp> (</rp><rt>dṛśaḥ padam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||6.1||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v6.2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वियोगसारे संसारे प्रियेण प्रभुणा त्वया।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Viyogasāre saṁsāre priyeṇa prabhuṇā tvayā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अवियुक्तः सदैव स्यां जगतापि वियोजितः॥६.२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aviyuktaḥ sadaiva syāṁ jagatāpi viyojitaḥ||6.2||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>In the cycle<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-samsara" class="pv-gl">saṁsāre</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>whose essence is separation<rp> (</rp><rt>viyoga-sāre</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>may I be<rp> (</rp><rt>syām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>unseparated<rp> (</rp><rt>aviyuktaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the beloved<rp> (</rp><rt>priyeṇa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-prabhu" class="pv-gl">prabhuṇā</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>even if<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>separated<rp> (</rp><rt>viyojitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from all the world<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-jagat" class="pv-gl">jagatā</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||6.2||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v6.3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कायवाङ्मनसैर्यत्र यामि सर्वं त्वमेव तत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kāyavāṅmanasairyatra yāmi sarvaṁ tvameva tat|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इत्येष परमार्थोऽपि परिपूर्णोऽस्तु मे सदा॥६.३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ityeṣa paramārtho'pi paripūrṇo'stu me sadā||6.3||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>"Wherever<rp> (</rp><rt>yatra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I may go<rp> (</rp><rt>yāmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with body, speech, and mind<rp> (</rp><rt>kāya-vāk-manasaiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>all<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>tat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>Thou Thyself<rp> (</rp><rt>tvameva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>" — <ruby>let this<rp> (</rp><rt>eṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>highest truth<rp> (</rp><rt>parama-arthaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>be<rp> (</rp><rt>astu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>full<rp> (</rp><rt>paripūrṇaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||6.3||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v6.4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">निर्विकल्पो महानन्दपूर्णो यद्वद्भवांस्तथा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nirvikalpo mahānandapūrṇo yadvadbhavāṁstathā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवत्स्तुतिकरी भूयादनुरूपैव वाङ्मम॥६.४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavatstutikarī bhūyādanurūpaiva vāṅmama||6.4||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>As<rp> (</rp><rt>yadvat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thou<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>without discriminating thought<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-nirvikalpa" class="pv-gl">nirvikalpaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>full of great bliss<rp> (</rp><rt>mahā-ānanda-pūrṇaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>so too<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>may it become<rp> (</rp><rt>bhūyāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and my<rp> (</rp><rt>mama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>speech<rp> (</rp><rt>vāk</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>offering praise to Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-stuti-karī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>worthy of Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>anurūpā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||6.4||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v6.5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवदावेशतः पश्यन्भावं भावं भवन्मयम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavadāveśataḥ paśyanbhāvaṁ bhāvaṁ bhavanmayam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विचरेयं निराकाङ्क्षः प्रहर्षपरिपूरितः॥६.५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vicareyaṁ nirākāṅkṣaḥ praharṣaparipūritaḥ||6.5||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>From entering into Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-āveśataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>seeing<rp> (</rp><rt>paśyan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>each thing<rp> (</rp><rt>bhāvam bhāvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>as consisting of Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-mayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>may I wander<rp> (</rp><rt>vicareyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>without desires<rp> (</rp><rt>nirākāṅkṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>filled with delight<rp> (</rp><rt>praharṣa-paripūritaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||6.5||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v6.6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भगवन्भवतः पूर्णं पश्येयमखिलं जगत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhagavanbhavataḥ pūrṇaṁ paśyeyamakhilaṁ jagat|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तावतैवास्मि सन्तुष्टस्ततो न परिखिद्यसे॥६.६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tāvataivāsmi santuṣṭastato na parikhidyase||6.6||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>may I see<rp> (</rp><rt>paśyeyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the entire<rp> (</rp><rt>akhilam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>world<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-jagat" class="pv-gl">jagat</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>full<rp> (</rp><rt>pūrṇam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>With this alone<rp> (</rp><rt>tāvatā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I<rp> (</rp><rt>asmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>am satisfied<rp> (</rp><rt>santuṣṭaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>therefore<rp> (</rp><rt>tataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>do not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>trouble Yourself<rp> (</rp><rt>parikhidyase</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||6.6||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v6.7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विलीयमानास्त्वय्येव व्योम्नि मेघलवा इव।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vilīyamānāstvayyeva vyomni meghalavā iva|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भावा विभान्तु मे शश्वत्क्रमनैर्मल्यगामिनः॥६.७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhāvā vibhāntu me śaśvatkramanairmalyagāminaḥ||6.7||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>May they shine<rp> (</rp><rt>vibhāntu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>things<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhava" class="pv-gl">bhāvāḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>dissolving<rp> (</rp><rt>vilīyamānāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> in You alone (tvayi eva), <ruby>like<rp> (</rp><rt>iva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>shreds of clouds<rp> (</rp><rt>megha-lavāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the sky<rp> (</rp><rt>vyomni</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>gradually<rp> (</rp><rt>krama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>going toward purity<rp> (</rp><rt>nairmalya-gāminaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>forever<rp> (</rp><rt>śaśvat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||6.7||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v6.8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वप्रभाप्रसरध्वस्तापर्यन्तध्वान्तसन्ततिः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svaprabhāprasaradhvastāparyantadhvāntasantatiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सन्ततं भातु मे कोऽपि भवमध्याद्भवन्मणिः॥६.८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Santataṁ bhātu me ko'pi bhavamadhyādbhavanmaṇiḥ||6.8||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>May shine<rp> (</rp><rt>bhātu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>unceasingly<rp> (</rp><rt>santatam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from the midst of existence<rp> (</rp><rt>bhava-madhyāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>some<rp> (</rp><rt>kaḥ api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Your jewel<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-maṇiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, which by the flood of its own <ruby>light has shattered the endless succession of darkness<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-prabhā-prasara-dhvasta-aparyanta-dhvānta-santatiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||6.8||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v6.9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कां भूमिकां नाधिशेषे किं तत्स्याद्यन्न ते वपुः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kāṁ bhūmikāṁ nādhiśeṣe kiṁ tatsyādyanna te vapuḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">श्रान्तस्तेनाप्रयासेन सर्वतस्त्वामवाप्नुयाम्॥६.९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śrāntastenāprayāsena sarvatastvāmavāpnuyām||6.9||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>On which<rp> (</rp><rt>kām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>step<rp> (</rp><rt>bhūmikām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>do You not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>recline<rp> (</rp><rt>adhiśeṣe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>? <ruby>What<rp> (</rp><rt>kim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is<rp> (</rp><rt>syāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>tat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which<rp> (</rp><rt>yat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Your<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>body<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vapus" class="pv-gl">vapuḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>? <ruby>Therefore<rp> (</rp><rt>tena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I, weary<rp> (</rp><rt>śrāntaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>may attain<rp> (</rp><rt>avāpnuyām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from everywhere<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>without effort<rp> (</rp><rt>aprayāsena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||6.9||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v6.10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवदङ्गपरिष्वङ्गसम्भोगः स्वेच्छयैव मे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavadaṅgapariṣvaṅgasambhogaḥ svecchayaiva me|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">घटतामियति प्राप्ते किं नाथ न जितं मया॥६.१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ghaṭatāmiyati prāpte kiṁ nātha na jitaṁ mayā||6.10||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>May there befall<rp> (</rp><rt>ghaṭatām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the enjoyment of embracing Your body<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-aṅga-pariṣvaṅga-sambhogaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>by Your will alone<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-icchayā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>when so much is attained<rp> (</rp><rt>prāpte</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>as this<rp> (</rp><rt>iyati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>what<rp> (</rp><rt>kim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>conquered<rp> (</rp><rt>jitam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by me<rp> (</rp><rt>mayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||6.10||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v6.11" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रकटीभव नान्याभिः प्रार्थनाभिः कदर्थनाः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prakaṭībhava nānyābhiḥ prārthanābhiḥ kadarthanāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कुर्मस्ते नाथ ताम्यन्तस्त्वामेव मृगयामहे॥६.११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kurmaste nātha tāmyantastvāmeva mṛgayāmahe||6.11||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Appear<rp> (</rp><rt>prakaṭī-bhava</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>We do not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>annoy<rp> (</rp><rt>kadarthanāḥ kurmaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with other<rp> (</rp><rt>anyābhiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>requests<rp> (</rp><rt>prārthanābhiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>languishing<rp> (</rp><rt>tāmyantaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>we seek<rp> (</rp><rt>mṛgayāmahe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thee alone<rp> (</rp><rt>tvām eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||6.11||</p>
