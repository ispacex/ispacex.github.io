# Hymn 7 — Vidhuravijayanāmadheyaṁ saptamaṁ stotram

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Hymn 6 — Adhvavisphuraṇākhyaṁ ṣaṣṭhaṁ stotram](/en/ksh/sv/ch6/) · [Hymn 8 — Alaukikodbalanākhyamaṣṭamaṁ stotram →](/en/ksh/sv/ch8/)</p>

<p class="pv-copy nosearch"><button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-sa">Copy Sanskrit</button> <button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-iast">Copy transliteration</button></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v7.1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वय्यानन्दसरस्वति समरसतामेत्य नाथ मम चेतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvayyānandasarasvati samarasatāmetya nātha mama cetaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">परिहरतु सकृदियन्तं भेदाधीनं महानर्थम्॥७.१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pariharatu sakṛdiyantaṁ bhedādhīnaṁ mahānartham||7.1||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>may my<rp> (</rp><rt>mama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-citta" class="pv-gl">cetaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>having entered<rp> (</rp><rt>etya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>into unity of taste<rp> (</rp><rt>sama-rasatām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvayi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>river of bliss<rp> (</rp><rt>ānanda-sarasvati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>at once<rp> (</rp><rt>sakṛt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>may cast off<rp> (</rp><rt>pariharatu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>iyantam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>great calamity<rp> (</rp><rt>mahā-anartham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which is dependent on distinction<rp> (</rp><rt>bheda-adhīnam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||7.1||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v7.2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एतन्मम न त्विदमिति रागद्वेषादिनिगडदृढमूले।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Etanmama na tvidamiti rāgadveṣādinigaḍadṛḍhamūle|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नाथ भवन्मयतैक्यप्रत्ययपरशुः पतत्वन्तः॥७.२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nātha bhavanmayataikyapratyayaparaśuḥ patatvantaḥ||7.2||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>“This<rp> (</rp><rt>etat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is mine<rp> (</rp><rt>mama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and this<rp> (</rp><rt>idam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> is not (na tu)” — <ruby>thus<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; and upon the firm <ruby>root of these fetters, passion, hatred, and the rest<rp> (</rp><rt>rāga-dveṣa-ādi-nigaḍa-dṛḍha-mūle</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>let it fall<rp> (</rp><rt>patatu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>within<rp> (</rp><rt>antaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> the axe — <ruby>the certainty that all is You and one with You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-mayatā-aikya-pratyaya-paraśuḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||7.2||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v7.3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">गलतु विकल्पकलङ्कावली समुल्लसतु हृदि निरर्गलता।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Galatu vikalpakalaṅkāvalī samullasatu hṛdi nirargalatā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भगवन्नानन्दरसप्लुतास्तु मे चिन्मयी मूर्तिः॥७.३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhagavannānandarasaplutāstu me cinmayī mūrtiḥ||7.3||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Пусть стечёт<rp> (</rp><rt>galatu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>вереница пятен различающей мысли<rp> (</rp><rt>vikalpa-kalaṅka-āvalī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>пусть воссияет<rp> (</rp><rt>samullasatu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>в сердце<rp> (</rp><rt>hṛdi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>беспрепятственность<rp> (</rp><rt>nirargalatā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>Господь<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>пусть мой<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>состоящий из Сознания<rp> (</rp><rt>cin-mayī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>облик<rp> (</rp><rt>mūrtiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>будет<rp> (</rp><rt>astu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>залит соком блаженства<rp> (</rp><rt>ānanda-rasa-plutā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||7.3||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v7.4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">रागादिमयभवाण्डकलुठितं त्वद्भक्तिभावनाम्बिका तैस्तैः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Rāgādimayabhavāṇḍakaluṭhitaṁ tvadbhaktibhāvanāmbikā taistaiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आप्याययतु रसैर्मां प्रवृद्धपक्षो यथा भवामि खगः॥७.४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Āpyāyayatu rasairmāṁ pravṛddhapakṣo yathā bhavāmi khagaḥ||7.4||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Let the mother — contemplation of devotion to You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-bhakti-bhāvanā-ambikā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>nourish<rp> (</rp><rt>āpyāyayatu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>me<rp> (</rp><rt>mām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, rolling <ruby>in the egg of existence, compounded of passion and the rest<rp> (</rp><rt>rāga-ādi-maya-bhava-āṇḍa-kaluṭhitam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>with these and those<rp> (</rp><rt>taiḥ taiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>juices<rp> (</rp><rt>rasaiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>so that<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I may become<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavāmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>a bird<rp> (</rp><rt>khagaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with grown wings<rp> (</rp><rt>pravṛddha-pakṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||7.4||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v7.5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वच्चरणभावनामृतरससारास्वादनैपुणं लभताम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvaccaraṇabhāvanāmṛtarasasārāsvādanaipuṇaṁ labhatām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चित्तमिदं निःशेषितविषयविषासङ्गवासनावधि मे॥७.५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Cittamidaṁ niḥśeṣitaviṣayaviṣāsaṅgavāsanāvadhi me||7.5||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Let this<rp> (</rp><rt>idam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>my<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-citta" class="pv-gl">cittam</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, in which the trace of attachment to the poison of objects is utterly <ruby>destroyed<rp> (</rp><rt>niḥśeṣita-viṣaya-viṣa-āsaṅga-vāsanā-avadhi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>may attain<rp> (</rp><rt>labhatām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> the skill of tasting the very <ruby>essence of the nectarous juice of contemplating Your feet<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-caraṇa-bhāvanā-amṛta-rasa-sāra-āsvāda-naipuṇam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||7.5||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v7.6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वद्भक्तितपनदीधितिसंस्पर्शवशान्ममैष दूरतरम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvadbhaktitapanadīdhitisaṁsparśavaśānmamaiṣa dūrataram|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चेतोमणिर्विमूञ्चतुरागादिकतप्तवह्निकणान्॥७.६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Cetomaṇirvimūñcaturāgādikataptavahnikaṇān||7.6||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Let this<rp> (</rp><rt>eṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>my<rp> (</rp><rt>mama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>jewel-consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>cetaḥ-maṇiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>from the touch of the rays of the sun of devotion to Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-bhakti-tapana-dīdhiti-saṁsparśa-vaśāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>shall cast away<rp> (</rp><rt>vimuñcatu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>as far as possible<rp> (</rp><rt>dūra-taram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the sparks of fire heated by passion and the rest<rp> (</rp><rt>rāga-ādika-tapta-vahni-kaṇān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||7.6||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v7.7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तस्मिन्पदे भवन्तं सततमुपश्लोकयेयमत्युच्चैः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tasminpade bhavantaṁ satatamupaślokayeyamatyuccaiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हरिहर्यश्वविरिञ्चा अपि यत्र बहिः प्रतीक्षन्ते॥७.७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Hariharyaśvaviriñcā api yatra bahiḥ pratīkṣante||7.7||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>May I proclaim at the top of my voice<rp> (</rp><rt>ati-uccaiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>unceasingly<rp> (</rp><rt>satatam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>praise<rp> (</rp><rt>upaślokayeyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavantam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in that<rp> (</rp><rt>tasmin</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>state<rp> (</rp><rt>pade</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>where<rp> (</rp><rt>yatra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Hari, Indra and Viriñca<rp> (</rp><rt>hari-haryaśva-viriñcāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>wait<rp> (</rp><rt>pratīkṣante</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>outside<rp> (</rp><rt>bahiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||7.7||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v7.8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तिमदजनितविभ्रमवशेन पश्येयमविकलं करणैः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktimadajanitavibhramavaśena paśyeyamavikalaṁ karaṇaiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शिवमयमखिलं लोकं क्रियाश्च पूजामयी सकलाः॥७.८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śivamayamakhilaṁ lokaṁ kriyāśca pūjāmayī sakalāḥ||7.8||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1">Силою <ruby>головокружения, рождённого хмелем преданности<rp> (</rp><rt>bhakti-mada-janita-vibhrama-vaśena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>да увижу я<rp> (</rp><rt>paśyeyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>органами чувств<rp> (</rp><rt>karaṇaiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>без изъяна<rp> (</rp><rt>avikalam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>весь<rp> (</rp><rt>akhilam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>мир<rp> (</rp><rt>lokam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>состоящим из Śiva<rp> (</rp><rt>śiva-mayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>и<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>все<rp> (</rp><rt>sakalāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>дела<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-kriya" class="pv-gl">kriyāḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>состоящими из почитания<rp> (</rp><rt>pūjā-mayī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||7.8||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v7.9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मामकमनोगृहीतत्वद्भक्तिकुलाङ्गनाणिमादिसुतान्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Māmakamanogṛhītatvadbhaktikulāṅganāṇimādisutān|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सूत्वा सुबद्धमूला ममेति बुद्धिं दृढीकुरुताम्॥७.९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sūtvā subaddhamūlā mameti buddhiṁ dṛḍhīkurutām||7.9||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1">May the noble <ruby>wife — devotion to You, taken by my mind<rp> (</rp><rt>māmaka-manaḥ-gṛhīta-tvat-bhakti-kula-aṅganā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>having given birth<rp> (</rp><rt>sūtvā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to sons, anima and other perfections<rp> (</rp><rt>aṇimā-ādi-sutān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>become firmly rooted<rp> (</rp><rt>su-baddha-mūlā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and strengthen<rp> (</rp><rt>dṛḍhī-kurutām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the thought<rp> (</rp><rt>buddhim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>"this is my home"<rp> (</rp><rt>mameti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||7.9||</p>
