<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13.1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सङ्ग्रहेन सुखदुःखलक्षणं मां प्रति स्थितमिदं शृणु प्रभो।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Saṅgrahena sukhaduḥkhalakṣaṇaṁ māṁ prati sthitamidaṁ śṛṇu prabho|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सौख्यमेष भवता समागमः स्वामिना विरह एव दुःखिता॥१३.१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Saukhyameṣa bhavatā samāgamaḥ svāminā viraha eva duḥkhitā||13.1||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-prabhu" class="pv-gl">prabho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>hear<rp> (</rp><rt>śṛṇu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in brief<rp> (</rp><rt>saṅgraheṇa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this very thing<rp> (</rp><rt>idam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which<rp> (</rp><rt>yat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>stands<rp> (</rp><rt>sthitam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with me<rp> (</rp><rt>mām prati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>as the mark of happiness and sorrow<rp> (</rp><rt>sukha-duḥkha-lakṣaṇam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>happiness<rp> (</rp><rt>saukhyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>this very<rp> (</rp><rt>eṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>meeting<rp> (</rp><rt>samāgamaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavatā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-svamin" class="pv-gl">svāminā</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>sorrow<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-duhkha" class="pv-gl">duḥkhitā</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>only<rp> (</rp><rt>eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>separation<rp> (</rp><rt>virahaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||13.1||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13.2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अन्तरप्यतितरामणीयसी या त्वदप्रथनकालिकास्ति मे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Antarapyatitarāmaṇīyasī yā tvadaprathanakālikāsti me|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तामपीश परिमृज्य सर्वतः स्वं स्वरूपममलं प्रकाशय॥१३.२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tāmapīśa parimṛjya sarvataḥ svaṁ svarūpamamalaṁ prakāśaya||13.2||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-ishvara" class="pv-gl">īśa</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>yā... tām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>most subtle<rp> (</rp><rt>atitarām aṇīyasī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>cloud of ignorance about You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-aprathana-kālikā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>that is<rp> (</rp><rt>asti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>within<rp> (</rp><rt>antaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>wipe away<rp> (</rp><rt>parimṛjya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from everywhere<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and reveal<rp> (</rp><rt>prakāśaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>my own<rp> (</rp><rt>svam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>spotless<rp> (</rp><rt>amalam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>nature<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-rūpam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||13.2||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13.3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तावके वपुषि विश्वनिर्भरे चित्सुधारसमये निरत्यये।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tāvake vapuṣi viśvanirbhare citsudhārasamaye niratyaye|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तिष्ठतः सततमर्चतः प्रभुं जीवितं मृतमथान्यदस्तु मे॥१३.३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tiṣṭhataḥ satatamarcataḥ prabhuṁ jīvitaṁ mṛtamathānyadastu me||13.3||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>May there be for me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who stands<rp> (</rp><rt>tiṣṭhataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and constantly<rp> (</rp><rt>satatam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>worshipping<rp> (</rp><rt>arcataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-prabhu" class="pv-gl">prabhum</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in Your<rp> (</rp><rt>tāvake</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>body<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vapus" class="pv-gl">vapuṣi</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>full of the universe<rp> (</rp><rt>viśva-nirbhare</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>woven from the nectarous juice of Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>cit-sudhā-rasa-maye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>indestructible<rp> (</rp><rt>niratyaye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>let there be<rp> (</rp><rt>astu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>whether life<rp> (</rp><rt>jīvitam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>whether<rp> (</rp><rt>atha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>death<rp> (</rp><rt>mṛtam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>whether something else<rp> (</rp><rt>anyat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||13.3||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13.4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ईश्वरोऽहमहमेव रूपवान्पण्डितोऽस्मि सुभगोऽस्मि कोऽपरः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Īśvaro'hamahameva rūpavānpaṇḍito'smi subhago'smi ko'paraḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मत्समोऽस्ति जगतीति शोभते मानिता त्वदनुरागिणः परम्॥१३.४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Matsamo'sti jagatīti śobhate mānitā tvadanurāgiṇaḥ param||13.4||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>“I<rp> (</rp><rt>aham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>the lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-ishvara" class="pv-gl">īśvaraḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, I alone (aham eva) <ruby>am beautiful<rp> (</rp><rt>rūpavān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>I<rp> (</rp><rt>asmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>am learned<rp> (</rp><rt>paṇḍitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>I am fortunate<rp> (</rp><rt>subhagaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who else<rp> (</rp><rt>kaḥ aparaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the world<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-jagat" class="pv-gl">jagati</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is<rp> (</rp><rt>asti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>equal to me<rp> (</rp><rt>mat-samaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?” — <ruby>thus<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>shines<rp> (</rp><rt>śobhate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>pride<rp> (</rp><rt>mānitā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and only<rp> (</rp><rt>param</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in one who is in love with Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-anurāgiṇaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||13.4||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13.5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">देवदेव भवदद्वयामृताख्यातिसंहरणलब्धजन्मना।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Devadeva bhavadadvayāmṛtākhyātisaṁharaṇalabdhajanmanā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तद्यथास्थितपदार्थसंविदा मां कुरुष्व चरणार्चनोचितम्॥१३.५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadyathāsthitapadārthasaṁvidā māṁ kuruṣva caraṇārcanocitam||13.5||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>God of gods<rp> (</rp><rt>deva-deva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>by that consciousness of things as they are<rp> (</rp><rt>tat yathā-sthita-padārtha-saṁvidā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, which is born from the destruction of <ruby>ignorance of the nectar of non-duality with Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-advaya-amṛta-akhyāti-saṁharaṇa-labdha-janmanā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>make<rp> (</rp><rt>kuruṣva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>me<rp> (</rp><rt>mām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>fit for the worship of Thy feet<rp> (</rp><rt>caraṇa-arcana-ucitam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||13.5||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13.6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ध्यायते तदनु दृश्यते ततः स्पृश्यते च परमेश्वरः स्वयम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dhyāyate tadanu dṛśyate tataḥ spṛśyate ca parameśvaraḥ svayam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यत्र पूजनमहोत्सवः स मे सर्वदास्तु भवतोऽनुभावतः॥१३.६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yatra pūjanamahotsavaḥ sa me sarvadāstu bhavato'nubhāvataḥ||13.6||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Let<rp> (</rp><rt>astu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>mine<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>by Thy power<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavataḥ anubhāvataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>may that<rp> (</rp><rt>saḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>great festival of worship<rp> (</rp><rt>pūjana-mahā-utsavaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>where<rp> (</rp><rt>yatra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is contemplated<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-dhyana" class="pv-gl">dhyāyate</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>then<rp> (</rp><rt>tad-anu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is seen<rp> (</rp><rt>dṛśyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and afterwards<rp> (</rp><rt>tataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>also<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is touched<rp> (</rp><rt>spṛśyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the Self<rp> (</rp><rt>svayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Supreme Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-parameshvara" class="pv-gl">parama-īśvaraḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||13.6||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13.7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यद्यथास्थितपदार्थदर्शनं युष्मदर्चनमहोत्सवश्च यः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yadyathāsthitapadārthadarśanaṁ yuṣmadarcanamahotsavaśca yaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">युग्ममेतदितरेतराश्रयं भक्तिशालिषु सदा विजृम्भते॥१३.७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yugmametaditaretarāśrayaṁ bhaktiśāliṣu sadā vijṛmbhate||13.7||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Видение вещей как они есть<rp> (</rp><rt>yat yathā-sthita-padārtha-darśanam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>и<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>великий праздник почитания Тебя<rp> (</rp><rt>yuṣmat-arcana-mahā-utsavaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>эта<rp> (</rp><rt>etat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>двоица<rp> (</rp><rt>yugmam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>одно на другом стоящая<rp> (</rp><rt>itaretara-āśrayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>всегда<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>раскрывается<rp> (</rp><rt>vijṛmbhate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>у тех, кто полон преданности<rp> (</rp><rt>bhakti-śāliṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||13.7||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13.8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्तदिन्द्रियमुखेन सन्ततं युष्मदर्चनरसायनासवम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tattadindriyamukhena santataṁ yuṣmadarcanarasāyanāsavam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वभावचषकेषु पूरितेष्वापिबन्नपि भवेयमुन्मदः॥१३.८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvabhāvacaṣakeṣu pūriteṣvāpibannapi bhaveyamunmadaḥ||13.8||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>May I be<rp> (</rp><rt>bhaveyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>intoxicated<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-unmada" class="pv-gl">unmadaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>unceasingly<rp> (</rp><rt>santatam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>drink<rp> (</rp><rt>āpiban</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>through these and those senses<rp> (</rp><rt>tat-tat-indriya-mukhena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the intoxicating potion of worshipping You<rp> (</rp><rt>yuṣmat-arcana-rasāyana-āsavam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>from the filled<rp> (</rp><rt>pūriteṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>cups of all things<rp> (</rp><rt>sarva-bhāva-caṣakeṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||13.8||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13.9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अन्यवेद्यमणुमात्रमस्ति न स्वप्रकाशमखिलं विजृम्भते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anyavedyamaṇumātramasti na svaprakāśamakhilaṁ vijṛmbhate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यत्र नाथ भवतः पुरे स्थितं तत्र मे कुरु सदा तवार्चितुः॥१३.९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yatra nātha bhavataḥ pure sthitaṁ tatra me kuru sadā tavārcituḥ||13.9||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>where<rp> (</rp><rt>yatra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>there is not even an atom<rp> (</rp><rt>na asti aṇu-mātram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of what is knowable by another<rp> (</rp><rt>anya-vedyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>where everything<rp> (</rp><rt>akhilam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>unfolds<rp> (</rp><rt>vijṛmbhate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>as self-luminous<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-prakāśam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>in that<rp> (</rp><rt>tatra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Your<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>city<rp> (</rp><rt>pure</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>grant<rp> (</rp><rt>kuru</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who worships You<rp> (</rp><rt>tava arcituḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>a place forever<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||13.9||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13.10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दासधाम्नि विनियोजितोऽप्यहं स्वेच्छयैव परमेश्वर त्वया।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dāsadhāmni viniyojito'pyahaṁ svecchayaiva parameśvara tvayā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दर्शनेन न किमस्मि पात्रितः पादसंवहनकर्मणापि वा॥१३.१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Darśanena na kimasmi pātritaḥ pādasaṁvahanakarmaṇāpi vā||13.10||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The Supreme Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-parameshvara" class="pv-gl">parama-īśvara</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>though<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I<rp> (</rp><rt>aham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>am appointed<rp> (</rp><rt>viniyojitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>by Thy will alone<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-icchayā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>to the station of a servant<rp> (</rp><rt>dāsa-dhāmni</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>why then<rp> (</rp><rt>kim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>am I not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>deemed worthy<rp> (</rp><rt>pātritaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>neither of beholding<rp> (</rp><rt>darśanena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>nor<rp> (</rp><rt>vā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even of<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the work of massaging Thy feet<rp> (</rp><rt>pāda-saṁvahana-karmaṇā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||13.10||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13.11" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शक्तिपातसमये विचारणं प्राप्तमीश न करोषि कर्हिचित्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śaktipātasamaye vicāraṇaṁ prāptamīśa na karoṣi karhicit|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अद्य मां प्रति किमागतं यतः स्वप्रकाशनविधौ विलम्बसे॥१३.११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Adya māṁ prati kimāgataṁ yataḥ svaprakāśanavidhau vilambase||13.11||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13.13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वामिसौधमभिसन्धिमात्रतो निर्विबन्धमधिरुह्य सर्वदा।</span><br />
