# Hymn 1 — Bhaktivilāsākhyaṁ stotram

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Table of contents](/en/ksh/sv/) · [Hymn 2 — Sarvātmaparibhāvanākhyaṁ dvitīyaṁ stotram →](/en/ksh/sv/ch2/)</p>

<p class="pv-copy nosearch"><button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-sa">Copy Sanskrit</button> <button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-iast">Copy transliteration</button></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1.1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न ध्यायतो न जपतः स्याद्यस्याविधिपूर्वकम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na dhyāyato na japataḥ syādyasyāvidhipūrvakam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एवमेव शिवाभासस्तं नुमो भक्तिशालिनम्॥१.१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evameva śivābhāsastaṁ numo bhaktiśālinam||1.1||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>We praise<rp> (</rp><rt>numaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>him<rp> (</rp><rt>tam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> the devotee <span class="pv-note">— lit. full of devotion —</span> <span class="pv-w">(bhakti-śālinam)</span>, <ruby>in whom<rp> (</rp><rt>yasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the Radiance of Śiva<rp> (</rp><rt>śiva-ābhāsaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>arises<rp> (</rp><rt>syāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> in this way <span class="pv-note">— that is, by the sole Grace of Śiva —</span> (evam eva), <ruby>without any preliminary means<rp> (</rp><rt>a-vidhi-pūrvakam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>without meditation<rp> (</rp><rt>na dhyāyataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(and)_ <ruby>without repetition of mantras<rp> (</rp><rt>na japataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||1.1||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1.2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आत्मा मम भवद्भक्तिसुधापानयुवापि सन्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ātmā mama bhavadbhaktisudhāpānayuvāpi san|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">लोकयात्रारजोरागात्पलितैरिव धूसरः॥१.२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Lokayātrārajorāgātpalitairiva dhūsaraḥ||1.2||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Although<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>my<rp> (</rp><rt>mama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-atman" class="pv-gl">ātmā</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and there is<rp> (</rp><rt>san</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> a young man drinking the Nectar of devotion to You <span class="pv-note">— that is, devotion directed toward You —</span> <span class="pv-w">(bhavat-bhakti-sudhā-pāna-yuvā)</span>, it _(nevertheless)_ <ruby>grey<rp> (</rp><rt>dhūsaraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from gray hair<rp> (</rp><rt>palitaiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, so to speak <span class="pv-note">— that is, it is an image, not an actual case —</span> <span class="pv-w">(iva)</span>— because it was colored by the dust _(by name)_ worldly life <span class="pv-note">— that is, everyday existence —</span> <span class="pv-w">(loka-yātrā-rajas-rāgāt)</span>||1.2||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1.3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">लब्धतत्सम्पदां भक्तिमतां त्वत्पुरवासिनाम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Labdhatatsampadāṁ bhaktimatāṁ tvatpuravāsinām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सञ्चारो लोकमार्गेऽपि स्यात्तयैव विजृम्भया॥१.३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sañcāro lokamārge'pi syāttayaiva vijṛmbhayā||1.3||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>on the worldly path<rp> (</rp><rt>loka-mārge</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the movement<rp> (</rp><rt>sañcāraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of devotees<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakta" class="pv-gl">bhaktimatām</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who have obtained Your Wealth<rp> (</rp><rt>labdha-tvat-sampadām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(and)_ <ruby>who dwell in Your City<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-pura-vāsinām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>is partaken<rp> (</rp><rt>syāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(of all)_ <ruby>by that very same unfolding<rp> (</rp><rt>tayā eva vijṛmbhayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||1.3||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1.4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">साक्षाद्भवन्मये नाथ सर्वस्मिन्भुवनान्तरे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sākṣādbhavanmaye nātha sarvasminbhuvanāntare|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">किं न भक्तिमतां क्षेत्रं मन्त्रः क्वैषां न सिद्ध्यति॥१.४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kiṁ na bhaktimatāṁ kṣetraṁ mantraḥ kvaiṣāṁ na siddhyati||1.4||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, in all _(this)_ world (sarvasmin bhuvana-antare), <ruby>which is Yourself alone<rp> (</rp><rt>sākṣāt-bhavat-maye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>what<rp> (</rp><rt>kim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>place<rp> (</rp><rt>kṣetram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> belonging to the devotees <span class="pv-note">— or rather: “not for the devotees” —</span> <span class="pv-w">(<a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakta" class="pv-gl">bhaktimatām</a>)</span>? Where _(is)_ <ruby>the mantra<rp> (</rp><rt>mantraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>that does not yield them fruit<rp> (</rp><rt>eṣām na siddhyati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||1.4||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1.5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जयन्ति भक्तिपीयूषरसासववरोन्मदाः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jayanti bhaktipīyūṣarasāsavavaronmadāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अद्वितीया अपि सदा त्वद्द्वितीया अपि प्रभो॥१.५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Advitīyā api sadā tvaddvitīyā api prabho||1.5||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-prabhu" class="pv-gl">prabho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, let victory be to those _(devotees)_ <span class="pv-w">(jayanti)</span> who are intoxicated with the finest wine _(made)_ <ruby>from the nectarous ambrosia of devotion<rp> (</rp><rt>bhakti-pīyūṣa-rasa-āsava-vara-unmadāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(and)_ who, though having no second <span class="pv-note">— that is, their essential nature is extraordinary, having no equal —</span> <span class="pv-w">(advitīyāḥ api)</span>, yet <span class="pv-note">— that is, even so —</span> are accompanied by Thee <span class="pv-note">— that is, Thou art their second —</span> <span class="pv-w">(tvat-dvitīyāḥ api)</span> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>!||1.5||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1.6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अनन्तानन्दसिन्धोस्ते नाथ तत्त्वं विदन्ति ते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anantānandasindhoste nātha tattvaṁ vidanti te|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तादृशा एव ये सान्द्रभक्त्यानन्दरसाप्लुताः॥१.६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tādṛśā eva ye sāndrabhaktyānandarasāplutāḥ||1.6||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>Only<rp> (</rp><rt>eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> those _(people)_ <span class="pv-note">— that is, those devotees whose nature is Your nature —</span> <span class="pv-w">(te... tādṛśāḥ)</span> <ruby>know<rp> (</rp><rt>vidanti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the Essence<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-tattva" class="pv-gl">tattva</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of Your Ocean of infinite Bliss<rp> (</rp><rt>ananta-ānanda-sindhoḥ te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>those who<rp> (</rp><rt>ye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> are <ruby>completely flooded with the juice of Joy of dense devotion<rp> (</rp><rt>sāndra-bhakti-ānanda-rasa-āplutāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||1.6||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1.7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वमेवात्मेश सर्वस्य सर्वश्चात्मनि रागवान्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvamevātmeśa sarvasya sarvaścātmani rāgavān|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इति स्वभावसिद्धांस्त्वद्भक्तिं जानञ्जयेज्जनः॥१.७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Iti svabhāvasiddhāṁstvadbhaktiṁ jānañjayejjanaḥ||1.7||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>You Yourself<rp> (</rp><rt>tvameva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-ishvara" class="pv-gl">īśa</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>the Self<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-atman" class="pv-gl">ātmā</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of all<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>everyone<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is in love with his own self<rp> (</rp><rt>ātmani rāgavān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Whoever knows<rp> (</rp><rt>jānan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>that devotion to You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-bhaktim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is established by the very nature of things<rp> (</rp><rt>iti svabhāva-siddhān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>that person<rp> (</rp><rt>janaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>indeed conquers<rp> (</rp><rt>jayet</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||1.7||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1.8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नाथ वेद्यक्षये केन न दृश्योऽस्येककः स्थितः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nātha vedyakṣaye kena na dṛśyo'syekakaḥ sthitaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वेद्यवेदकसङ्क्षोभेऽप्यसि भक्तैः सुदर्शनः॥१.८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vedyavedakasaṅkṣobhe'pyasi bhaktaiḥ sudarśanaḥ||1.8||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>when the knowable vanishes<rp> (</rp><rt>vedya-kṣaye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>by whom<rp> (</rp><rt>kena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>are You not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>seen<rp> (</rp><rt>dṛśyaḥ asi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>abiding<rp> (</rp><rt>sthitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>alone<rp> (</rp><rt>ekakaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>? <ruby>But also<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the very turmoil of the knowable and the knower<rp> (</rp><rt>vedya-vedaka-saṅkṣobhe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>You<rp> (</rp><rt>asi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>are clearly seen<rp> (</rp><rt>su-darśanaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by the devoted<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakta" class="pv-gl">bhaktaiḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||1.8||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1.9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अनन्तानन्दसरसी देवी प्रियतमा यथा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anantānandasarasī devī priyatamā yathā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अवियुक्तास्ति ते तद्वदेका त्वद्भक्तिरस्तु मे॥१.९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aviyuktāsti te tadvadekā tvadbhaktirastu me||1.9||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>As<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the Goddess<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-devi" class="pv-gl">devī</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the lake of infinite bliss<rp> (</rp><rt>ananta-ānanda-sarasī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>most beloved<rp> (</rp><rt>priya-tamā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>is inseparable<rp> (</rp><rt>aviyuktā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>so too<rp> (</rp><rt>tad-vat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>may there be<rp> (</rp><rt>astu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the sole<rp> (</rp><rt>ekā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>devotion to Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-bhaktiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||1.9||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1.10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्व एव भवल्लाभहेतुर्भक्तिमतां विभो।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarva eva bhavallābhaheturbhaktimatāṁ vibho|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">संविन्मार्गोऽयमाह्लाददुःखमोहैस्त्रिधा स्थितः॥१.१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Saṁvinmārgo'yamāhlādaduḥkhamohaistridhā sthitaḥ||1.10||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O All-pervading<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vibhu" class="pv-gl">vibho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>for devotees<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakta" class="pv-gl">bhaktimatām</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>every<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvaḥ eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>ayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>path of consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁvid-mārgaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>standing<rp> (</rp><rt>sthitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>threefold<rp> (</rp><rt>tridhā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>joy, pain, and delusion<rp> (</rp><rt>āhlāda-duḥkha-mohaiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>is the cause of attaining You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-lābha-hetuḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||1.10||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1.11" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवद्भक्त्यमृतास्वादाद्बोधस्य स्यात्परापि या।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavadbhaktyamṛtāsvādādbodhasya syātparāpi yā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दशा सा मां प्रति स्वामिन्नासवस्येव शुक्तता॥१.११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Daśā sā māṁ prati svāminnāsavasyeva śuktatā||1.11||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>yā... sā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>highest<rp> (</rp><rt>parā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>state<rp> (</rp><rt>daśā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of knowledge<rp> (</rp><rt>bodhasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which is born<rp> (</rp><rt>syāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from tasting the nectar of devotion to You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-bhakti-amṛta-āsvādāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>for me<rp> (</rp><rt>māṁ prati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-svamin" class="pv-gl">svāmin</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>is but sourness in an intoxicating drink<rp> (</rp><rt>āsavasya iva śuktatā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||1.11||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1.12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवद्भक्तिमहाविद्या येषामभ्यासमागता।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavadbhaktimahāvidyā yeṣāmabhyāsamāgatā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विद्याविद्योभयस्यापि ता एते तत्त्ववेदिनः॥१.१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vidyāvidyobhayasyāpi tā ete tattvavedinaḥ||1.12||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Those<rp> (</rp><rt>ete</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>in whom<rp> (</rp><rt>yeṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>has entered<rp> (</rp><rt>āgatā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>into constant practice<rp> (</rp><rt>abhyāsam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the great wisdom — devotion to Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-bhakti-mahā-vidyā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>they<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and know the essence<rp> (</rp><rt>tattva-vedinaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of both<rp> (</rp><rt>ubhayasyāpi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>both of knowledge and of ignorance<rp> (</rp><rt>vidyā-avidyā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||1.12||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1.13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आमुलाद्वाग्लता सेयं क्रमविस्फारशालिनी।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Āmulādvāglatā seyaṁ kramavisphāraśālinī|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वद्भक्तिसुधया सिक्ता तद्रसाढ्यफलास्तु मे॥१.१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvadbhaktisudhayā siktā tadrasāḍhyaphalāstu me||1.13||</span></p>
