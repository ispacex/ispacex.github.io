# Hymn 5 — Svabalanideśanākhyaṁ pañcamaṁ stotram

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वत्पादपद्मसम्पर्कमात्रसम्भोगसङ्गिनम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvatpādapadmasamparkamātrasambhogasaṅginam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">गलेपादिकया नाथ मां स्ववेश्म प्रवेशय॥५.१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Galepādikayā nātha māṁ svaveśma praveśaya||5.1||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>me<rp> (</rp><rt>mām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, attached to enjoyment by <ruby>mere touch of Your lotus-feet<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-pāda-padma-samparka-mātra-sambhoga-saṅginam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>lead<rp> (</rp><rt>praveśaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>into Your house<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-veśma</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>even by force, with a foot on the neck<rp> (</rp><rt>gale-pādikayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||5.1||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवत्पादाम्बुजरजोराजिरञ्जितमूर्धजः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavatpādāmbujarajorājirañjitamūrdhajaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अपाररभसारब्धनर्तनः स्यामहं कदा॥५.२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Apārarabhasārabdhanartanaḥ syāmahaṁ kadā||5.2||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>When<rp> (</rp><rt>kadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I<rp> (</rp><rt>aham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>shall be<rp> (</rp><rt>syām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> one whose hair is <ruby>colored with streaks of dust from Your lotus feet<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-pāda-ambuja-rajaḥ-rāji-rañjita-mūrdhajaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>one who has set out dancing with an immeasurable surge<rp> (</rp><rt>apāra-rabhasa-ārabdha-nartanaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||5.2||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वदेकनाथो भगवन्नियदेवार्थये सदा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvadekanātho bhagavanniyadevārthaye sadā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वदन्तर्वसतिर्मूको भवेयं मान्यथा बुधः॥५.३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvadantarvasatirmūko bhaveyaṁ mānyathā budhaḥ||5.3||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>having You as the sole master<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-eka-nāthaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>I always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>ask<rp> (</rp><rt>arthaye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for only one thing<rp> (</rp><rt>iyadeva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>may I be<rp> (</rp><rt>bhaveyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>mute<rp> (</rp><rt>mūkaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>dwelling within You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-antar-vasatiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>but not<rp> (</rp><rt>mā anyathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>learned<rp> (</rp><rt>budhaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||5.3||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अहो सुधानिधे स्वामिन्नहो मृष्ट त्रिलोचन।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aho sudhānidhe svāminnaho mṛṣṭa trilocana|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अहो स्वादो विरूपक्षेत्येव नृत्येयमारटन्॥५.४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aho svādo virūpakṣetyeva nṛtyeyamāraṭan||5.4||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>«О<rp> (</rp><rt>aho</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>treasury of nectar<rp> (</rp><rt>sudhā-nidhe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-svamin" class="pv-gl">svāmin</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>O<rp> (</rp><rt>aho</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>sweet<rp> (</rp><rt>mṛṣṭa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Three-eyed<rp> (</rp><rt>tri-locana</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>Oh, taste, o Virūpākṣa!<rp> (</rp><rt>aho svādo virūpākṣa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>» — <ruby>so<rp> (</rp><rt>ityeva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>crying out<rp> (</rp><rt>āraṭan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Let me dance.<rp> (</rp><rt>nṛtyeyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||5.4||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वत्पादपद्मसंस्पर्शपरिमीलितलोचनः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvatpādapadmasaṁsparśaparimīlitalocanaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विजृम्भेयभवद्भक्तिमदिरामदघूर्णितः॥५.५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vijṛmbheyabhavadbhaktimadirāmadaghūrṇitaḥ||5.5||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1">With eyes closed <ruby>from the touch of Your lotus feet<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-pāda-padma-saṁsparśa-parimīlita-locanaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>reeling from the intoxication of the wine of devotion to You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-bhakti-madirā-mada-ghūrṇitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>may I unfold<rp> (</rp><rt>vijṛmbheya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||5.5||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चित्तभूभृद्भुवि विभो वसेयं क्वापि यत्र सा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Cittabhūbhṛdbhuvi vibho vaseyaṁ kvāpi yatra sā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">निरन्तरत्वत्प्रलापमयी वृत्तिर्महारसा॥५.६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nirantaratvatpralāpamayī vṛttirmahārasā||5.6||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O All-pervading<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vibhu" class="pv-gl">vibho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>may I dwell<rp> (</rp><rt>vaseyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> somewhere (kva api) <ruby>on the earth of the mountain of consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>citta-bhūbhṛt-bhuvi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>where<rp> (</rp><rt>yatra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>sā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>full of the great rasa<rp> (</rp><rt>mahā-rasā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>activity of consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>vṛttiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is entirely made of continuous babble about You<rp> (</rp><rt>nirantara-tvat-pralāpa-mayī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||5.6||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यत्र देवीसमेतस्त्वमासौधादा च गोपुरात्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yatra devīsametastvamāsaudhādā ca gopurāt|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बहुरूपः स्थितस्तस्मिन्वास्तव्यः स्यामहं पुरे॥५.७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bahurūpaḥ sthitastasminvāstavyaḥ syāmahaṁ pure||5.7||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>May I be<rp> (</rp><rt>syām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I<rp> (</rp><rt>aham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>an inhabitant<rp> (</rp><rt>vāstavyaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of that<rp> (</rp><rt>tasmin</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>city<rp> (</rp><rt>pure</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>where<rp> (</rp><rt>yatra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thou<rp> (</rp><rt>tvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>together with the Goddess<rp> (</rp><rt>devī-sametaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>standest<rp> (</rp><rt>sthitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in manifold forms<rp> (</rp><rt>bahu-rūpaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>from the palaces<rp> (</rp><rt>ā-saudhāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>up to the very gates<rp> (</rp><rt>ā gopurāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||5.7||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">समुल्लसन्तु भगवन्भवद्भानुमरीचयः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Samullasantu bhagavanbhavadbhānumarīcayaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विकसत्वेष यावन्मे हृत्पद्मः पूजनाय ते॥५.८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vikasatveṣa yāvanme hṛtpadmaḥ pūjanāya te||5.8||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>may they shine forth<rp> (</rp><rt>samullasantu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the rays of Your sun<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-bhānu-marīcayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>so that<rp> (</rp><rt>yāvat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>eṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>my<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>lotus of the heart<rp> (</rp><rt>hṛt-padmaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>has opened<rp> (</rp><rt>vikasatu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to You<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for worship<rp> (</rp><rt>pūjanāya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||5.8||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रसीद भगवन्येन त्वत्पदे पतितं सदा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prasīda bhagavanyena tvatpade patitaṁ sadā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मनो मे तत्तदास्वाद्य क्षीवेदिव गलेदिव॥५.९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mano me tattadāsvādya kṣīvediva galediva||5.9||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Have mercy<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-prasada" class="pv-gl">prasīda</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>so that<rp> (</rp><rt>yena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>my<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>mind<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-manas" class="pv-gl">manaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>forever<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>fallen<rp> (</rp><rt>patitam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>at Your foot<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-pade</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>tasting<rp> (</rp><rt>āsvādya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this and that<rp> (</rp><rt>tattad</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>as if<rp> (</rp><rt>iva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>intoxicated<rp> (</rp><rt>kṣīvet</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and as if<rp> (</rp><rt>iva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>melting<rp> (</rp><rt>galet</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||5.9||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रहर्षाद्वाथ शोकाद्वा यदि कुङ्याद्धटादपि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Praharṣādvātha śokādvā yadi kuṅyāddhaṭādapi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बाह्यादथान्तराद्भावात्प्रकटीभव मे प्रभो॥५.१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bāhyādathāntarādbhāvātprakaṭībhava me prabho||5.10||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-prabhu" class="pv-gl">prabho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>appear<rp> (</rp><rt>prakaṭī-bhava</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>from delight<rp> (</rp><rt>praharṣāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>or<rp> (</rp><rt>vā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from<rp> (</rp><rt>atha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>grief<rp> (</rp><rt>śokāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>whether<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from a wall<rp> (</rp><rt>kuḍyāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>whether from a pot<rp> (</rp><rt>ghaṭāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>from a thing<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhava" class="pv-gl">bhāvāt</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>external<rp> (</rp><rt>bāhyāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>or<rp> (</rp><rt>atha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>internal<rp> (</rp><rt>āntarāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||5.10||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.11" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बहिरप्यन्तरपि तत्स्यन्दमानं सदास्तु मे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bahirapyantarapi tatsyandamānaṁ sadāstu me|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवत्पादाम्बुजस्पर्शामृतमत्यन्तशीतलम्॥५.११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavatpādāmbujasparśāmṛtamatyantaśītalam||5.11||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>May there be<rp> (</rp><rt>astu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>tat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>flowing<rp> (</rp><rt>syandamānam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>both outside<rp> (</rp><rt>bahiḥ api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and inside<rp> (</rp><rt>antaḥ api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the nectar of touching Your lotus feet<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-pāda-ambuja-sparśa-amṛtam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>immeasurably cool<rp> (</rp><rt>atyanta-śītalam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||5.11||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वत्पादसंस्पर्शसुधासरसोऽन्तर्निमज्जनम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvatpādasaṁsparśasudhāsaraso'ntarnimajjanam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कोऽप्येष सर्वसम्भोगलङ्घी भोगोऽस्तु मे सदा॥५.१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ko'pyeṣa sarvasambhogalaṅghī bhogo'stu me sadā||5.12||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Let there be<rp> (</rp><rt>astu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>eṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>some<rp> (</rp><rt>kaḥ api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>enjoyment<rp> (</rp><rt>bhogaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>surpassing all enjoyments<rp> (</rp><rt>sarva-sambhoga-laṅghī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — the immersion into the lake of <ruby>nectar, which is the touch of Your feet<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-pāda-saṁsparśa-sudhā-sarasaḥ antar-nimajjanam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||5.12||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">निवेदितमुपादत्स्व रागादि भगवन्मया।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Niveditamupādatsva rāgādi bhagavanmayā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आदाय चामृतीकृत्य भुङ्क्ष्व भक्तजनैः समम्॥५.१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ādāya cāmṛtīkṛtya bhuṅkṣva bhaktajanaiḥ samam||5.13||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>accept<rp> (</rp><rt>upādatsva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>what is offered<rp> (</rp><rt>niveditam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by me<rp> (</rp><rt>mayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>passion and the rest<rp> (</rp><rt>rāga-ādi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>having taken<rp> (</rp><rt>ādāya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>turning into nectar<rp> (</rp><rt>amṛtī-kṛtya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>taste<rp> (</rp><rt>bhuṅkṣva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>together<rp> (</rp><rt>samam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with the devotees<rp> (</rp><rt>bhakta-janaiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||5.13||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अशेषभुवनाहारनित्यतृप्तः सुखासनम्।</span><br />
