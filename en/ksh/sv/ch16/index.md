# Hymn 16 — Pāśān-udbhedanāma ṣoḍaśaṁ stotram

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Hymn 15 — Bhaktistotranāma pañcadaśaṁ stotram](/en/ksh/sv/ch15/) · [Hymn 17 — Divyakrīḍābahumānanāma saptadaśaṁ stotram →](/en/ksh/sv/ch17/)</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16.1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न किञ्चिदेव लोकानां भवदावरणं प्रति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na kiñcideva lokānāṁ bhavadāvaraṇaṁ prati|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न किञ्चिदेव भक्तानां भवदावरणं प्रति॥१६.१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na kiñcideva bhaktānāṁ bhavadāvaraṇaṁ prati||16.1||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>For the world<rp> (</rp><rt>lokānām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>there is nothing<rp> (</rp><rt>na kiñcid eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>that would become a cover for You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-āvaraṇam prati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>For the devotees<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakta" class="pv-gl">bhaktānām</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>there is nothing<rp> (</rp><rt>na kiñcid eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>that would become a cover for You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-āvaraṇam prati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <span class="pv-note">— both lines in the original coincide word for word, except the first: the difference is only in whom it concerns —</span> ||16.1||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16.2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अप्युपायक्रमप्राप्यः सङ्कुलोऽपि विशेषणैः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Apyupāyakramaprāpyaḥ saṅkulo'pi viśeṣaṇaiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तिभाजां भवानात्मा सकृच्छुद्धोऽवभासते॥१६.२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktibhājāṁ bhavānātmā sakṛcchuddho'vabhāsate||16.2||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Though<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>You are attainable<rp> (</rp><rt>prāpyaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>through a sequence of means<rp> (</rp><rt>upāya-krama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>though<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and obstructed<rp> (</rp><rt>saṅkulaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by definitions<rp> (</rp><rt>viśeṣaṇaiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>to those who partake of devotion<rp> (</rp><rt>bhakti-bhājām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>their self<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-atman" class="pv-gl">ātmā</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>appear<rp> (</rp><rt>avabhāsate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>at once<rp> (</rp><rt>sakṛt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>pure<rp> (</rp><rt>śuddhaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||16.2||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16.3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जयन्तोऽपि हसन्त्येते जिता अपि हसन्ति च।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jayanto'pi hasantyete jitā api hasanti ca|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवद्भक्तिसुधापानमत्ताः केऽप्येव ये प्रभो॥१६.३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavadbhaktisudhāpānamattāḥ ke'pyeva ye prabho||16.3||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-prabhu" class="pv-gl">prabho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>these<rp> (</rp><rt>ete</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — whoever (ke api eva) <ruby>they<rp> (</rp><rt>ye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> may be — <ruby>are intoxicated by drinking the nectar of devotion to Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-bhakti-sudhā-pāna-mattāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>conquering<rp> (</rp><rt>jayantaḥ api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>they laugh<rp> (</rp><rt>hasanti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>conquered<rp> (</rp><rt>jitāḥ api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>they laugh<rp> (</rp><rt>hasanti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||16.3||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16.4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शुष्ककं मैव सिद्धेय मैव मुच्येय वापि तु।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śuṣkakaṁ maiva siddheya maiva mucyeya vāpi tu|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वादिष्ठपरकाष्टाप्तत्वद्भक्तिरसनिर्भरः॥१६.४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svādiṣṭhaparakāṣṭāptatvadbhaktirasanirbharaḥ||16.4||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>May I not become<rp> (</rp><rt>mā eva... siddheya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>perfect in a dry manner<rp> (</rp><rt>śuṣkakam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>nor<rp> (</rp><rt>vā api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>indeed<rp> (</rp><rt>mā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>be liberated<rp> (</rp><rt>mucyeya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>but<rp> (</rp><rt>tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> may I be filled with the juice of devotion <ruby>to You, having reached the sweetest highest limit<rp> (</rp><rt>svādiṣṭha-para-kāṣṭhā-āpta-tvat-bhakti-rasa-nirbharaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||16.4||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16.5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यथैवज्ञातपूर्वोऽयं भवद्भक्तिरसो मम।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yathaivajñātapūrvo'yaṁ bhavadbhaktiraso mama|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">घटितस्तद्वदीशान स एव परिपुष्यतु॥१६.५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ghaṭitastadvadīśāna sa eva paripuṣyatu||16.5||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-ishvara" class="pv-gl">īśāna</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>as<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>has formed<rp> (</rp><rt>ghaṭitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in me<rp> (</rp><rt>mama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>ayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>juice of devotion to You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-bhakti-rasaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>previously unknown<rp> (</rp><rt>ajñāta-pūrvaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>likewise<rp> (</rp><rt>tad-vat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>may it<rp> (</rp><rt>saḥ eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>also grow<rp> (</rp><rt>paripuṣyatu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||16.5||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16.6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सत्येन भगवन्नान्यः प्रार्थनाप्रसरोऽस्ति मे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Satyena bhagavannānyaḥ prārthanāprasaro'sti me|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">केवलं स तथा कोऽपि भक्त्यावेशोऽस्तु मे सदा॥१६.६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kevalaṁ sa tathā ko'pi bhaktyāveśo'stu me sadā||16.6||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>in truth<rp> (</rp><rt>satyena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> I have no (na asti me) <ruby>other<rp> (</rp><rt>anyaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>scope for requests<rp> (</rp><rt>prārthanā-prasaraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Only<rp> (</rp><rt>kevalam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>let there be<rp> (</rp><rt>astu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>saḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>certain<rp> (</rp><rt>kaḥ api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>such<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>entrance into devotion<rp> (</rp><rt>bhakti-āveśaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||16.6||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16.7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तिक्षीवोऽपि कुप्येयं भवायानुशयीय च।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktikṣīvo'pi kupyeyaṁ bhavāyānuśayīya ca|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तथा हसेयं उद्यां च रटेयं च शिवेत्यलम्॥१६.७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tathā haseyaṁ udyāṁ ca raṭeyaṁ ca śivetyalam||16.7||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Drunk with devotion<rp> (</rp><rt>bhakti-kṣīvaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>may I rage<rp> (</rp><rt>kupyeyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>may I repent<rp> (</rp><rt>anuśayīya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of existence<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavāya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and likewise<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>may I laugh<rp> (</rp><rt>haseyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>may I rise<rp> (</rp><rt>udyām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>may I shout<rp> (</rp><rt>raṭeyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to my fill<rp> (</rp><rt>alam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: “Śiva!” <span class="pv-w">(śiva iti)</span>||16.7||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16.8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विषमस्थोऽपि स्वस्थोऽपि रुदन्नपि हसन्नपि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Viṣamastho'pi svastho'pi rudannapi hasannapi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">गम्भीरोऽपि विचित्तोऽपि भवेयं भक्तितः प्रभो॥१६.८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Gambhīro'pi vicitto'pi bhaveyaṁ bhaktitaḥ prabho||16.8||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-prabhu" class="pv-gl">prabho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>may I be<rp> (</rp><rt>bhaveyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>through devotion<rp> (</rp><rt>bhaktitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>both in distress<rp> (</rp><rt>viṣama-sthaḥ api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and at peace with myself<rp> (</rp><rt>svasthaḥ api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, and weeping (rudan api), and laughing (hasan api), <ruby>and profound<rp> (</rp><rt>gambhīraḥ api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and out of my mind<rp> (</rp><rt>vicittaḥ api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||16.8||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16.9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तानां नास्ति संवेद्यं त्वदन्तर्यदि वा बहिः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktānāṁ nāsti saṁvedyaṁ tvadantaryadi vā bahiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चिद्धर्मा यत्र न भवान्निर्विकल्पः स्थितः स्वयम्॥१६.९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ciddharmā yatra na bhavānnirvikalpaḥ sthitaḥ svayam||16.9||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>For the devotees<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakta" class="pv-gl">bhaktānām</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> there is no (na asti) <ruby>knowable<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁvedyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>neither<rp> (</rp><rt>yadi vā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>within You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-antaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>nor<rp> (</rp><rt>vā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>outside<rp> (</rp><rt>bahiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>such that where<rp> (</rp><rt>yatra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>You Yourself<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavān... svayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>whose law is Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>cit-dharmā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>would not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>stand<rp> (</rp><rt>sthitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>without discriminating thought<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-nirvikalpa" class="pv-gl">nirvikalpaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||16.9||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16.10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्ता निन्दानुकरेऽपि तवामृतकणैरिव।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktā nindānukare'pi tavāmṛtakaṇairiva|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हृष्यन्त्येवान्तराविद्धास्तीक्ष्णरोमाञ्चसूचिभिः॥१६.१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Hṛṣyantyevāntarāviddhāstīkṣṇaromāñcasūcibhiḥ||16.10||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The devotees<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakta" class="pv-gl">bhaktāḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>pierced from within<rp> (</rp><rt>antar-āviddhāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by the sharp needles of bristling bodily hairs<rp> (</rp><rt>tīkṣṇa-romāñca-sūcibhiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>rejoice<rp> (</rp><rt>hṛṣyanti eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>when in their presence the reviling of You is mimicked<rp> (</rp><rt>tava nindā-anukare</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>as if<rp> (</rp><rt>iva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by drops of nectar<rp> (</rp><rt>amṛta-kaṇaiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||16.10||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16.11" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दुःखापि वेदना भक्तिमतां भोगाय कल्पते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Duḥkhāpi vedanā bhaktimatāṁ bhogāya kalpate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">येषां सुधार्द्रा सर्वैव संवित्त्वच्चन्द्रिकामयी॥१६.११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yeṣāṁ sudhārdrā sarvaiva saṁvittvaccandrikāmayī||16.11||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>And<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>bitter<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-duhkha" class="pv-gl">duḥkhā</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>sensation<rp> (</rp><rt>vedanā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>goes<rp> (</rp><rt>kalpate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>into enjoyment<rp> (</rp><rt>bhogāya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for those devotees<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakta" class="pv-gl">bhakti-matām</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>in whom<rp> (</rp><rt>yeṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>all<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-samvid" class="pv-gl">saṁvid</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>moist with nectar<rp> (</rp><rt>sudhā-ārdrā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>consists of Your moonlight<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-candrikā-mayī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||16.11||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16.12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यत्र तत्रोपरुद्धानां भक्तानां बहिरन्तरे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yatra tatroparuddhānāṁ bhaktānāṁ bahirantare|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">निर्व्याजं त्वद्वपुःस्पर्शरसास्वादसुखं समम्॥१६.१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nirvyājaṁ tvadvapuḥsparśarasāsvādasukhaṁ samam||16.12||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>У преданных<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakta" class="pv-gl">bhaktānām</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>где бы их ни заперли<rp> (</rp><rt>yatra tatra uparuddhānām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>снаружи<rp> (</rp><rt>bahiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>и внутри<rp> (</rp><rt>antare</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>ровно<rp> (</rp><rt>samam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>и без подделки<rp> (</rp><rt>nirvyājam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> счастье <ruby>вкушения сока прикосновения к Твоему телу<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-vapuḥ-sparśa-rasa-āsvāda-sukham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||16.12||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16.13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तवेश भक्तेरर्चायां दैन्यांशं द्वयसंश्रयम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Taveśa bhakterarcāyāṁ dainyāṁśaṁ dvayasaṁśrayam|</span><br />

<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktirbhaktiḥ pare bhaktirbhaktirnāma samutkaṭā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तारं विरौमि यत्तीव्रा भक्तिर्मेऽस्तु परं त्वयि॥१६.२५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tāraṁ viraumi yattīvrā bhaktirme'stu paraṁ tvayi||16.25||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Devotion<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakti" class="pv-gl">bhaktiḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>devotion<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakti" class="pv-gl">bhaktiḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>devotion<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakti" class="pv-gl">bhaktiḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>to the Supreme<rp> (</rp><rt>pare</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>devotion<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakti" class="pv-gl">bhaktiḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>by itself<rp> (</rp><rt>nāma</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>fierce<rp> (</rp><rt>samutkaṭā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>Therefore<rp> (</rp><rt>yat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I cry out<rp> (</rp><rt>viraumi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>at the top of my voice<rp> (</rp><rt>tāram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>let there be<rp> (</rp><rt>astu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>toward You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvayi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>intense<rp> (</rp><rt>tīvrā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>devotion<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakti" class="pv-gl">bhaktiḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>and only that<rp> (</rp><rt>param</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||16.25||</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16.26" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यतोऽस्मि सर्वशोभानां प्रसवावनिरीश तत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yato'smi sarvaśobhānāṁ prasavāvanirīśa tat|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वयि लग्नमनर्घं स्याद्रत्नं वा यदि वा तृणम्॥१६.२६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvayi lagnamanarghaṁ syādratnaṁ vā yadi vā tṛṇam||16.26||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-ishvara" class="pv-gl">īśa</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>since<rp> (</rp><rt>yataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I<rp> (</rp><rt>asmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>the soil that gives birth to all beauties<rp> (</rp><rt>sarva-śobhānām prasava-avaniḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>tat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>which clings<rp> (</rp><rt>lagnam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvayi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>will become<rp> (</rp><rt>syāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>priceless<rp> (</rp><rt>anargham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>whether a gem<rp> (</rp><rt>ratnam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>or<rp> (</rp><rt>vā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>a blade of grass<rp> (</rp><rt>tṛṇam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>or<rp> (</rp><rt>yadi vā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||16.26||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16.27" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आवेदकादा च वेद्याद्येषां संवेदनाध्वनि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Āvedakādā ca vedyādyeṣāṁ saṁvedanādhvani|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवता न वियोगोऽस्ति ते जयन्ति भवज्जुषः॥१६.२७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavatā na viyogo'sti te jayanti bhavajjuṣaḥ||16.27||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Let them triumph<rp> (</rp><rt>jayanti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>those<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who are devoted to Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-juṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>for whom<rp> (</rp><rt>yeṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>on the path of consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁvedana-adhvani</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>from the knower<rp> (</rp><rt>āvedakāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to the knowable<rp> (</rp><rt>ā... vedyāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — there is no (na asti) <ruby>separation<rp> (</rp><rt>viyogaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavatā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||16.27||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16.28" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">संसारसदसो बाह्ये कैश्चित्त्वं परिरभ्यसे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Saṁsārasadaso bāhye kaiścittvaṁ parirabhyase|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वामिन्परैस्तु तत्रैव ताम्यद्भिस्त्यक्तयन्त्रणैः॥१६.२८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svāminparaistu tatraiva tāmyadbhistyaktayantraṇaiḥ||16.28||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-svamin" class="pv-gl">svāmin</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>some<rp> (</rp><rt>kaiścid</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>embrace<rp> (</rp><rt>parirabhyase</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>outside<rp> (</rp><rt>bāhye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the assembly of the cycle<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁsāra-sadasaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>but<rp> (</rp><rt>tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>others<rp> (</rp><rt>paraiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — in that very place (tatra eva), <ruby>languishing<rp> (</rp><rt>tāmyadbhiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and having cast off the rein<rp> (</rp><rt>tyakta-yantraṇaiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||16.28||</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16.29" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पानाशनप्रसाधनसम्भुक्तसमस्तविश्वया शिवया।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pānāśanaprasādhanasambhuktasamastaviśvayā śivayā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रलयोत्सवसरभसया दृढमुपगूढं शिवं वन्दे॥१६.२९॥</span><br />

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>I praise<rp> (</rp><rt>vande</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Śiva<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-shiva" class="pv-gl">śivam</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>firmly<rp> (</rp><rt>dṛḍham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>embraced<rp> (</rp><rt>upagūḍham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Śivā<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-shiva" class="pv-gl">śivayā</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who in the swift rapture of dissolution<rp> (</rp><rt>pralaya-utsava-sarabhasayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> drank and devoured <ruby>the entire universe instead of drink, food, and adornment<rp> (</rp><rt>pāna-aśana-prasādhana-sambhukta-samasta-viśvayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||16.29||</p>
