<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9.1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कदा नवरसार्द्रार्द्रसम्भोगास्वादनोत्सुकम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kadā navarasārdrārdrasambhogāsvādanotsukam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रवर्तेत विहायान्यन्मम त्वत्स्पर्शने मनः॥९.१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pravarteta vihāyānyanmama tvatsparśane manaḥ||9.1||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>When<rp> (</rp><rt>kadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>my<rp> (</rp><rt>mama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>mind<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-manas" class="pv-gl">manaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, thirsting to taste <ruby>the delight, ever moist with fresh and fresher juice<rp> (</rp><rt>nava-rasa-ārdra-ārdra-sambhoga-āsvādana-utsukam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>having abandoned<rp> (</rp><rt>vihāya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the other<rp> (</rp><rt>anyat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>will turn<rp> (</rp><rt>pravarteta</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to touching You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-sparśane</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||9.1||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9.2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वदेकरक्तस्त्वत्पादपूजामात्रमहाधनः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvadekaraktastvatpādapūjāmātramahādhanaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कदा साक्षात्करिष्यामि भवन्तमयमुत्सुकः॥९.२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kadā sākṣātkariṣyāmi bhavantamayamutsukaḥ||9.2||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>When<rp> (</rp><rt>kadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I<rp> (</rp><rt>ayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>in love with You alone<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-eka-raktaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, for whom the great <ruby>wealth is but the worship of Your feet<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-pāda-pūjā-mātra-mahā-dhanaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>longing<rp> (</rp><rt>utsukaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>shall I behold directly<rp> (</rp><rt>sākṣāt-kariṣyāmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavantam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||9.2||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9.3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">गाढानुरागवशतो निरपेक्षीभूतमानसोऽस्मि कदा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Gāḍhānurāgavaśato nirapekṣībhūtamānaso'smi kadā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पटपटिति विघटिताखिलमहार्गलस्त्वामुपैष्यामि॥९.३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Paṭapaṭiti vighaṭitākhilamahārgalastvāmupaiṣyāmi||9.3||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>When then<rp> (</rp><rt>kadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I, with a mind become independent of everything<rp> (</rp><rt>nirapekṣī-bhūta-mānasaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by the force of dense love<rp> (</rp><rt>gāḍha-anurāga-vaśataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>with a crack<rp> (</rp><rt>paṭa-paṭiti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>having torn off all the great bolts<rp> (</rp><rt>vighaṭita-akhila-mahā-argalaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>shall I come<rp> (</rp><rt>upaiṣyāmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||9.3||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9.4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वसंवित्सारहृदयाधिष्ठानाः सर्वदेवताः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svasaṁvitsārahṛdayādhiṣṭhānāḥ sarvadevatāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कदा नाथ वशीकुर्यां भवद्भक्तिप्रभावतः॥९.४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kadā nātha vaśīkuryāṁ bhavadbhaktiprabhāvataḥ||9.4||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>When<rp> (</rp><rt>kadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>by the power of devotion to You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-bhakti-prabhāvataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>shall I subjugate<rp> (</rp><rt>vaśī-kuryām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>all deities<rp> (</rp><rt>sarva-devatāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>whose abode is the heart of one’s own consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-saṁvid-sāra-hṛdaya-adhiṣṭhānāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||9.4||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9.5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कदा मे स्याद्विभो भूरि भक्त्यानन्दरसोत्सवः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kadā me syādvibho bhūri bhaktyānandarasotsavaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यदालोकसुखानन्दी पृथङ्नामापि लप्स्यते॥९.५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yadālokasukhānandī pṛthaṅnāmāpi lapsyate||9.5||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>When<rp> (</rp><rt>kadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the All-pervading One<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vibhu" class="pv-gl">vibho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>will be<rp> (</rp><rt>syāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>an abundant<rp> (</rp><rt>bhūri</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>festival of the rasa of the bliss of devotion<rp> (</rp><rt>bhakti-ānanda-rasa-utsavaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>he after whom<rp> (</rp><rt>yadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and one rejoicing in worldly happiness<rp> (</rp><rt>loka-sukha-ānandī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>will obtain<rp> (</rp><rt>lapsyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>a special<rp> (</rp><rt>pṛthak</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> name <span class="pv-note">— the second half of the stanza is obscure: it admits also “will not obtain” —</span> <span class="pv-w">(nāma api)</span>?||9.5||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9.6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ईश्वरमभयमुदारं पूर्णमकारणमपह्नुतात्मानम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Īśvaramabhayamudāraṁ pūrṇamakāraṇamapahnutātmānam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सहसाभिज्ञाय कदा स्वामिजनं लज्जयिष्यामि॥९.६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sahasābhijñāya kadā svāmijanaṁ lajjayiṣyāmi||9.6||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>When<rp> (</rp><rt>kadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>suddenly<rp> (</rp><rt>sahasā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>having recognized<rp> (</rp><rt>abhijñāya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-ishvara" class="pv-gl">īśvaram</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>fearless<rp> (</rp><rt>abhayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>generous<rp> (</rp><rt>udāram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>full<rp> (</rp><rt>pūrṇam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>causeless<rp> (</rp><rt>akāraṇam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>concealing Himself<rp> (</rp><rt>apahnuta-ātmānam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>shall I put to shame<rp> (</rp><rt>lajjayiṣyāmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the masters of this world<rp> (</rp><rt>svāmi-janam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||9.6||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9.7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कदा कामपि तां नाथ तव वल्लभतामियाम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kadā kāmapi tāṁ nātha tava vallabhatāmiyām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यया मां प्रति न क्वापि युक्तं ते स्यात्पलायितुम्॥९.७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yayā māṁ prati na kvāpi yuktaṁ te syātpalāyitum||9.7||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>When<rp> (</rp><rt>kadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>shall I obtain<rp> (</rp><rt>iyām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>tām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>certain<rp> (</rp><rt>kām api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>favour from Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tava vallabhatām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>by which<rp> (</rp><rt>yayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>it would no longer be fitting at all<rp> (</rp><rt>na kvāpi... yuktam syāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to flee<rp> (</rp><rt>palāyitum</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from me<rp> (</rp><rt>mām prati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||9.7||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9.8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्त्वतोऽशेषजन्तूनां भवत्पूजामयात्मनाम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tattvato'śeṣajantūnāṁ bhavatpūjāmayātmanām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दृष्ट्यानुमोदितरसाप्लावितः स्यां कदा विभो॥९.८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dṛṣṭyānumoditarasāplāvitaḥ syāṁ kadā vibho||9.8||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>When<rp> (</rp><rt>kadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the All-pervading One<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vibhu" class="pv-gl">vibho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>shall I be<rp> (</rp><rt>syām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>flooded<rp> (</rp><rt>āplāvitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with the rasa born of the approving glance<rp> (</rp><rt>dṛṣṭyā anumodita-rasa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>upon all beings without exception<rp> (</rp><rt>aśeṣa-jantūnām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who in truth<rp> (</rp><rt>tattvataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>consist of the worship of Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-pūjā-maya-ātmanām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||9.8||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9.9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ज्ञानस्य परमा भूमिर्योगस्य परमा दशा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jñānasya paramā bhūmiryogasya paramā daśā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वद्भक्तिर्या विभो कर्हि पूर्ण मे स्यात्तदर्थिता॥९.९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvadbhaktiryā vibho karhi pūrṇa me syāttadarthitā||9.9||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>All-pervading<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vibhu" class="pv-gl">vibho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>devotion to You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-bhaktiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which<rp> (</rp><rt>yā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is the highest<rp> (</rp><rt>paramā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>stage<rp> (</rp><rt>bhūmiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of knowledge<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-jnana" class="pv-gl">jñānasya</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and the highest<rp> (</rp><rt>paramā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>state<rp> (</rp><rt>daśā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of yoga<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-yoga" class="pv-gl">yogasya</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>when indeed<rp> (</rp><rt>karhi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>will<rp> (</rp><rt>syāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>full<rp> (</rp><rt>pūrṇam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>be its seeking<rp> (</rp><rt>tad-arthitā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||9.9||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9.10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सहसैवसाद्य कदा गाढमवष्टभ्य हर्षविवशोऽहम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sahasaivasādya kadā gāḍhamavaṣṭabhya harṣavivaśo'ham|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वच्चरणवरनिधानं सर्वस्य प्रकटयिष्यामि॥९.१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvaccaraṇavaranidhānaṁ sarvasya prakaṭayiṣyāmi||9.10||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>When<rp> (</rp><rt>kadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I<rp> (</rp><rt>aham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>suddenly<rp> (</rp><rt>sahasā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>having obtained<rp> (</rp><rt>āsādya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>firmly<rp> (</rp><rt>gāḍham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>having grasped<rp> (</rp><rt>avaṣṭabhya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the great treasure — Your feet<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-caraṇa-vara-nidhānam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>overwhelmed with joy<rp> (</rp><rt>harṣa-vivaśaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>shall I reveal it<rp> (</rp><rt>prakaṭayiṣyāmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to all<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||9.10||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9.11" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">परितः प्रसरच्छुद्धत्वदालोकमयः कदा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Paritaḥ prasaracchuddhatvadālokamayaḥ kadā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्यां यथेश न किञ्चिन्मे मायाच्छायाबिलं भवेत्॥९.११॥</span><br />

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9.12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आत्मसात्कृतनिःशेषमण्डलो निर्व्यपेक्षकः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ātmasātkṛtaniḥśeṣamaṇḍalo nirvyapekṣakaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कदा भवेयं भगवंस्त्वद्भक्तगणनायकः॥९.१२॥</span><br />

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9.13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नाथ लोकाभिमानानामपूर्वं त्वं निबन्धनम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nātha lokābhimānānāmapūrvaṁ tvaṁ nibandhanam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">महाभिमानः कर्हि स्यां त्वद्भक्तिरसपूरितः॥९.१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mahābhimānaḥ karhi syāṁ tvadbhaktirasapūritaḥ||9.13||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Thou<rp> (</rp><rt>tvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>unprecedented<rp> (</rp><rt>apūrvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>foundation<rp> (</rp><rt>nibandhanam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of all worldly pride<rp> (</rp><rt>loka-abhimānānām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>When then<rp> (</rp><rt>karhi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>shall I become<rp> (</rp><rt>syām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I, filled with the juice of devotion to Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-bhakti-rasa-pūritaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>with great pride<rp> (</rp><rt>mahā-abhimānaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||9.13||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9.14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अशेषविषयाशून्यश्रीसमाश्लेषसुस्थितः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aśeṣaviṣayāśūnyaśrīsamāśleṣasusthitaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शयीयमिव शीताङ्घ्रिकुशेशययुगे कदा॥९.१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śayīyamiva śītāṅghrikuśeśayayuge kadā||9.14||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>When<rp> (</rp><rt>kadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> shall I, firmly established <ruby>in the embrace of Śrī, not empty for any object<rp> (</rp><rt>aśeṣa-viṣaya-aśūnya-śrī-samāśleṣa-susthitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>as it were<rp> (</rp><rt>iva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>lie down<rp> (</rp><rt>śayīyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the pair of cool lotuses of Your feet<rp> (</rp><rt>śīta-aṅghri-kuśeśaya-yuge</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||9.14||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9.15" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्त्यासवसमृद्धायास्त्वत्पूजाभोगसम्पदः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktyāsavasamṛddhāyāstvatpūjābhogasampadaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कदा पारं गमिष्यामि भविष्यामि कदा कृती॥९.१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kadā pāraṁ gamiṣyāmi bhaviṣyāmi kadā kṛtī||9.15||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>When will<rp> (</rp><rt>kadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I reach<rp> (</rp><rt>gamiṣyāmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the other shore<rp> (</rp><rt>pāram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of wealth — the enjoyment of worshipping Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-pūjā-bhoga-sampadaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>grown on the intoxication of devotion<rp> (</rp><rt>bhakti-āsava-samṛddhāyāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>? <ruby>When will<rp> (</rp><rt>kadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I become<rp> (</rp><rt>bhaviṣyāmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>one who has fulfilled his purpose<rp> (</rp><rt>kṛtī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||9.15||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9.16" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आनन्दबाष्पपूरस्खलितपरिभ्रान्तगद्गदाक्रन्दः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ānandabāṣpapūraskhalitaparibhrāntagadgadākrandaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हासोल्लासितवदनस्त्वत्स्पर्शरसं कदाप्स्यामि॥९.१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Hāsollāsitavadanastvatsparśarasaṁ kadāpsyāmi||9.16||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>When<rp> (</rp><rt>kadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>shall I attain<rp> (</rp><rt>āpsyāmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the rasa of touching You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-sparśa-rasam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — with a cry that stumbles, <ruby>falters and trembles from the flood of tears of bliss<rp> (</rp><rt>ānanda-bāṣpa-pūra-skhalita-paribhrānta-gadgada-ākrandaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>with a face blossoming with laughter<rp> (</rp><rt>hāsa-ullāsita-vadanaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||9.16||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9.17" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पशुजनसमानवृत्तामवधूय दशामिमां कदा शम्भो।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Paśujanasamānavṛttāmavadhūya daśāmimāṁ kadā śambho|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आस्वादयेय तावकभक्तोचितमात्मनो रूपम्॥९.१७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Āsvādayeya tāvakabhaktocitamātmano rūpam||9.17||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Śambhu<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-shambhu" class="pv-gl">śambho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>when<rp> (</rp><rt>kadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>having shaken off<rp> (</rp><rt>avadhūya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>imām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>state<rp> (</rp><rt>daśām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>in which I behave like cattle<rp> (</rp><rt>paśu-jana-samāna-vṛttām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>shall I taste<rp> (</rp><rt>āsvādayeya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>my<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-atman" class="pv-gl">ātmanaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>form<rp> (</rp><rt>rūpam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>befitting Your devotee<rp> (</rp><rt>tāvaka-bhakta-ucitam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||9.17||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9.18" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">लब्धाणिमादिसिद्धिर्विगलितसकलोपतापसन्त्रासः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Labdhāṇimādisiddhirvigalitasakalopatāpasantrāsaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वद्भक्तिरसायनपानक्रिढानिष्टः कदासीय॥९.१८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvadbhaktirasāyanapānakriḍhāniṣṭaḥ kadāsīya||9.18||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>When<rp> (</rp><rt>kadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>shall I be<rp> (</rp><rt>āsīya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>having attained aṇimā and other perfections<rp> (</rp><rt>labdha-aṇimā-ādi-siddhiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>with all fear and every fever melted away<rp> (</rp><rt>vigalita-sakala-upatāpa-santrāsaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, absorbed <ruby>in the play — the drinking of the elixir, the rasa of devotion to Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-bhakti-rasāyana-pāna-krīḍā-niṣṭhaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||9.18||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9.19" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नाथ कदा स तथाविध आक्रन्दो मे समुच्चरेद्वाचि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nātha kadā sa tathāvidha ākrando me samuccaredvāci|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यत्समनन्तरमेव स्फुरति पुरस्तावकी मूर्तिः॥९.१९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yatsamanantarameva sphurati purastāvakī mūrtiḥ||9.19||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>when<rp> (</rp><rt>kadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in my<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>speech<rp> (</rp><rt>vāci</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>will burst forth<rp> (</rp><rt>samuccaret</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>saḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>cry<rp> (</rp><rt>ākrandaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of such a kind<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā-vidhaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, immediately after which (yat-samanantaram eva) <ruby>before<rp> (</rp><rt>puraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>flares up<rp> (</rp><rt>sphurati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thy<rp> (</rp><rt>tāvakī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>form<rp> (</rp><rt>mūrtiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||9.19||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9.20" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">गाढगाढभवदङ्घ्रिसरोजालिङ्गनव्यसनतत्परचेताः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Gāḍhagāḍhabhavadaṅghrisarojāliṅganavyasanatatparacetāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वस्त्ववस्त्विदमयत्नत एव त्वां कदा समवलोकयितास्मि॥९.२०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vastvavastvidamayatnata eva tvāṁ kadā samavalokayitāsmi||9.20||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>When<rp> (</rp><rt>kadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> I, with a mind devoted to the passion <ruby>of embracing ever more and more firmly Your lotus-feet<rp> (</rp><rt>gāḍha-gāḍha-bhavat-aṅghri-saroja-āliṅgana-vyasana-tatpara-cetāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>without any effort<rp> (</rp><rt>ayatnataḥ eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>shall see<rp> (</rp><rt>samavalokayitā asmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — that very thing and non-thing (vastu avastu idam)?||9.20||</p>
