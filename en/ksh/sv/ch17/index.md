<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अहो कोऽपि जयत्येष स्वादुः पूजामहोत्सवः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aho ko'pi jayatyeṣa svāduḥ pūjāmahotsavaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यतोऽमृतरसास्वादमश्रूण्यपि ददत्यलम्॥१७.१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yato'mṛtarasāsvādamaśrūṇyapi dadatyalam||17.1||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O<rp> (</rp><rt>aho</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>Victorious is<rp> (</rp><rt>jayati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>eṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>some<rp> (</rp><rt>kaḥ api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>sweet<rp> (</rp><rt>svāduḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>great festival of worship<rp> (</rp><rt>pūjā-mahā-utsavaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>from which<rp> (</rp><rt>yataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>tears<rp> (</rp><rt>aśrūṇi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>abundantly<rp> (</rp><rt>alam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>give<rp> (</rp><rt>dadati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the taste of nectarous juice<rp> (</rp><rt>amṛta-rasa-āsvādam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||17.1||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">व्यापाराः सिद्धिदाः सर्वे ये त्वत्पूजापुरःसराः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vyāpārāḥ siddhidāḥ sarve ye tvatpūjāpuraḥsarāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तानां त्वन्मयाः सर्वे स्वयं सिद्धय एव ते॥१७.२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktānāṁ tvanmayāḥ sarve svayaṁ siddhaya eva te||17.2||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>All<rp> (</rp><rt>sarve</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>actions<rp> (</rp><rt>vyāpārāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which<rp> (</rp><rt>ye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>have the worship of You going before them<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-pūjā-puraḥ-sarāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>give perfection<rp> (</rp><rt>siddhi-dāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>but for the devotees<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakta" class="pv-gl">bhaktānām</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>all<rp> (</rp><rt>sarve</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>they<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>consisting of You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-mayāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>by themselves<rp> (</rp><rt>svayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and indeed are<rp> (</rp><rt>eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>perfections<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-siddhi" class="pv-gl">siddhayaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||17.2||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वदा सर्वभावेषु युगपत्सर्वरूपिणम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvadā sarvabhāveṣu yugapatsarvarūpiṇam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वामर्चयन्त्यविश्रन्तं ये ममैतेऽधिदेवताः॥१७.३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvāmarcayantyaviśrantaṁ ye mamaite'dhidevatāḥ||17.3||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Those<rp> (</rp><rt>ete</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who<rp> (</rp><rt>ye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in all things<rp> (</rp><rt>sarva-bhāveṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>at once<rp> (</rp><rt>yugapat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>worship<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-arcana" class="pv-gl">arcayanti</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>having all forms<rp> (</rp><rt>sarva-rūpiṇam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>without respite<rp> (</rp><rt>aviśrāntam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>they are my<rp> (</rp><rt>mama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>supreme deities<rp> (</rp><rt>adhi-devatāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||17.3||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ध्यानायसतिरस्कारसिद्धस्त्वत्स्पर्शनोत्सवः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dhyānāyasatiraskārasiddhastvatsparśanotsavaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूजाविधिरिति ख्यातो भक्तानां स सदास्तु मे॥१७.४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūjāvidhiriti khyāto bhaktānāṁ sa sadāstu me||17.4||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Let<rp> (</rp><rt>astu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>be that<rp> (</rp><rt>saḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>rite of worship<rp> (</rp><rt>pūjā-vidhiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which is known<rp> (</rp><rt>khyātaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>among devotees<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakta" class="pv-gl">bhaktānām</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>the festival of touching You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-sparśana-utsavaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>attained<rp> (</rp><rt>siddhaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by the rejection of the toil of meditation<rp> (</rp><rt>dhyāna-āyasa-tiraskāra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||17.4||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तानां समतासारविषुवत्समयः सदा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktānāṁ samatāsāraviṣuvatsamayaḥ sadā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वद्भावरसपीयूषरसेन्नैषां सदार्चनम्॥१७.५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvadbhāvarasapīyūṣarasennaiṣāṁ sadārcanam||17.5||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>For the devotees<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakta" class="pv-gl">bhaktānām</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>eternal<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>worship<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-arcana" class="pv-gl">arcanam</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>their<rp> (</rp><rt>eṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>eternal<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>equinox<rp> (</rp><rt>viṣuvat-samayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>whose essence is evenness<rp> (</rp><rt>samatā-sāra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>with the nectarous juice of the rasa of being by Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-bhāva-rasa-pīyūṣa-rasena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||17.5||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यस्यानारम्भपर्यन्तौ न च कालक्रमः प्रभो।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yasyānārambhaparyantau na ca kālakramaḥ prabho|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूजात्मासौ क्रिया तस्याः कर्तारस्त्वज्जुषः परम्॥१७.६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūjātmāsau kriyā tasyāḥ kartārastvajjuṣaḥ param||17.6||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-prabhu" class="pv-gl">prabho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>asau</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>action<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-kriya" class="pv-gl">kriyā</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which<rp> (</rp><rt>yasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>has neither<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>beginning nor end<rp> (</rp><rt>anārambha-paryantau</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>no sequence of time<rp> (</rp><rt>kāla-kramaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>that indeed is worship<rp> (</rp><rt>pūjā-ātmā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>and they perform<rp> (</rp><rt>kartāraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>it<rp> (</rp><rt>tasyāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>only<rp> (</rp><rt>param</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>devotees of Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-juṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||17.6||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ब्रह्मादीनामपीशास्ते ते च सौभाग्यभागिनः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Brahmādīnāmapīśāste te ca saubhāgyabhāginaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">येषां स्वप्नेऽपि मोहेऽपि स्थितस्त्वत्पूजनोत्सवः॥१७.७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yeṣāṁ svapne'pi mohe'pi sthitastvatpūjanotsavaḥ||17.7||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-ishvara" class="pv-gl">īśa</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>those<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>for whom<rp> (</rp><rt>yeṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in sleep<rp> (</rp><rt>svapne</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in delusion<rp> (</rp><rt>mohe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>stands<rp> (</rp><rt>sthitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the festival of worshipping You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-pūjana-utsavaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>those<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>rule<rp> (</rp><rt>īśate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>over Brahmā and others<rp> (</rp><rt>brahma-ādīnām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>they<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>obtain fortune<rp> (</rp><rt>saubhāgya-bhāginaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||17.7||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जपतां जुह्वतां स्नातां ध्यायतां न च केवलम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Japatāṁ juhvatāṁ snātāṁ dhyāyatāṁ na ca kevalam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तानां भवदभ्यर्चामहो यावद्यदा तदा॥१७.८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktānāṁ bhavadabhyarcāmaho yāvadyadā tadā||17.8||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O<rp> (</rp><rt>aho</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>Not only<rp> (</rp><rt>na ca kevalaṁ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>when they whisper<rp> (</rp><rt>japatām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>offer sacrifice<rp> (</rp><rt>juhvatām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>bathe<rp> (</rp><rt>snātām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and contemplate<rp> (</rp><rt>dhyāyatām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>for the devotees<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakta" class="pv-gl">bhaktānām</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the worship of Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-abhyarcā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>proceeds as long as they exist<rp> (</rp><rt>yāvat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and at any time<rp> (</rp><rt>yadā tadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||17.8||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवत्पूजासुधास्वादसम्भोगसुखिनः सदा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavatpūjāsudhāsvādasambhogasukhinaḥ sadā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इन्द्रादीनामथ ब्रह्ममुख्यानामस्ति कः समः॥१७.९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Indrādīnāmatha brahmamukhyānāmasti kaḥ samaḥ||17.9||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Who<rp> (</rp><rt>kaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is<rp> (</rp><rt>asti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>equal<rp> (</rp><rt>samaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to him who always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> is happy <ruby>with the enjoyment of the taste of the nectar of worshipping Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-pūjā-sudhā-āsvāda-sambhoga-sukhinaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>among Indra and others<rp> (</rp><rt>indra-ādīnām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>or<rp> (</rp><rt>atha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>among the foremost with Brahmā<rp> (</rp><rt>brahma-mukhyānām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||17.9||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जगत्क्षोभैकजनके भवत्पूजामहोत्सवे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jagatkṣobhaikajanake bhavatpūjāmahotsave|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यत्प्राप्यं प्राप्यते किञ्चिद्भक्ता एव विदन्ति तत्॥१७.१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yatprāpyaṁ prāpyate kiñcidbhaktā eva vidanti tat||17.10||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>In the great festival of worship of You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-pūjā-mahā-utsave</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the sole parent of the world's agitation<rp> (</rp><rt>jagat-kṣobha-eka-janake</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>is obtained<rp> (</rp><rt>prāpyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>something<rp> (</rp><rt>kiñcid</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>yat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is altogether attainable<rp> (</rp><rt>prāpyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>and know<rp> (</rp><rt>vidanti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>tat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>only<rp> (</rp><rt>eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the devotees<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakta" class="pv-gl">bhaktāḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||17.10||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.11" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वद्धाम्नि चिन्मये स्थित्वा षट्त्रिंशत्तत्त्वकर्मभिः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvaddhāmni cinmaye sthitvā ṣaṭtriṁśattattvakarmabhiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कायवाक्चित्तचेष्टाद्यैरर्चये त्वां सदा विभो॥१७.११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kāyavākcittaceṣṭādyairarcaye tvāṁ sadā vibho||17.11||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>All-pervading<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vibhu" class="pv-gl">vibho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>standing<rp> (</rp><rt>sthitvā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in Thy abode<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-dhāmni</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>consisting of Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>cin-maye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>may I worship<rp> (</rp><rt>arcaye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with the acts of the thirty-six tattvas<rp> (</rp><rt>ṣaṭ-triṁśat-tattva-karmabhiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>with movements of body, speech, consciousness, and the like<rp> (</rp><rt>kāya-vāk-citta-ceṣṭā-ādyaiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||17.11||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवत्पूजामयासङ्गसम्भोगसुखिनो मम।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavatpūjāmayāsaṅgasambhogasukhino mama|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रयातु कालः सकलोऽप्यनन्तोऽपीयदर्थये॥१७.१२॥</span><br />

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-ishvara" class="pv-gl">īśa</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>wonder<rp> (</rp><rt>citram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>worship<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-arcana" class="pv-gl">arcanam</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvayi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>become one's own self<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-ātma-bhūte</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>by the force of the agitation of devotion<rp> (</rp><rt>bhakti-kṣobha-vaśāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>does not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>lead<rp> (</rp><rt>yāvat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to humiliation<rp> (</rp><rt>dainyāya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>yet the highest<rp> (</rp><rt>param</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>fruit<rp> (</rp><rt>phalam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of poverty<rp> (</rp><rt>dīnatāyāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> is indeed humiliation||17.39||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.40" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उपचारपदं पूजा केषाञ्चित्त्वत्पदाप्तये।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Upacārapadaṁ pūjā keṣāñcittvatpadāptaye|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तानां भवदैकात्म्यनिर्वृत्तिप्रसरस्तु सः॥१७.४०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktānāṁ bhavadaikātmyanirvṛttiprasarastu saḥ||17.40||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>For some<rp> (</rp><rt>keṣāñcid</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>worship<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-puja" class="pv-gl">pūjā</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>a place of ritual services<rp> (</rp><rt>upacāra-padam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for the attainment of Your state<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-pada-āptaye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>and for devotees<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakta" class="pv-gl">bhaktānām</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>it<rp> (</rp><rt>saḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>an outpouring of peace from unity with You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-aikātmya-nirvṛtti-prasaraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||17.40||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.41" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अप्यसम्बद्धरूपार्चाभक्त्युन्मादनिरर्गलैः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Apyasambaddharūpārcābhaktyunmādanirargalaiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वितन्यमाना लभते प्रतिष्ठां त्वयि कामपि॥१७.४१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vitanyamānā labhate pratiṣṭhāṁ tvayi kāmapi||17.41||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>And.<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>veneration<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-arcana" class="pv-gl">arcā</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of incoherent appearance<rp> (</rp><rt>asambaddha-rūpa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>performed<rp> (</rp><rt>vitanyamānā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> those <ruby>whom nothing holds in the madness of devotion<rp> (</rp><rt>bhakti-unmāda-nirargalaiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>acquires<rp> (</rp><rt>labhate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvayi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>a certain<rp> (</rp><rt>kām api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>support<rp> (</rp><rt>pratiṣṭhām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||17.41||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.42" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वादुभक्तिरसास्वादस्तब्धीभूतमनश्च्युताम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svādubhaktirasāsvādastabdhībhūtamanaścyutām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शम्भो त्वमेव ललितः पूजानां किल भाजनम्॥१७.४२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śambho tvameva lalitaḥ pūjānāṁ kila bhājanam||17.42||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Śambhu<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-shambhu" class="pv-gl">śambho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>You alone<rp> (</rp><rt>tvameva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>beautiful<rp> (</rp><rt>lalitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and are<rp> (</rp><rt>kila</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the vessel<rp> (</rp><rt>bhājanam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of those acts of worship<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-puja" class="pv-gl">pūjānām</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, that slip from the mind, <ruby>stunned by the taste of the sweet juice of devotion<rp> (</rp><rt>svādu-bhakti-rasa-āsvāda-stabdhī-bhūta-manaḥ-cyutām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||17.42||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.43" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">परिपूर्णानि शुद्धानि भक्तिमन्ति स्थिराणि च।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Paripūrṇāni śuddhāni bhaktimanti sthirāṇi ca|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवत्पूजाविधौ नाथ साधनानि भवन्तु मे॥१७.४३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavatpūjāvidhau nātha sādhanāni bhavantu me||17.43||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>may my<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>means<rp> (</rp><rt>sādhanāni</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the rite of worshipping Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-pūjā-vidhau</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>be<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavantu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>utterly complete<rp> (</rp><rt>paripūrṇāni</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>pure<rp> (</rp><rt>śuddhāni</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>full of devotion<rp> (</rp><rt>bhaktimanti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>steadfast<rp> (</rp><rt>sthirāṇi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||17.43||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.44" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अशेषपूजासत्कोशे त्वत्पूजाकर्मणि प्रभो।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aśeṣapūjāsatkośe tvatpūjākarmaṇi prabho|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अहो करणवृन्दस्य कापि लक्ष्मीर्विजृम्भते॥१७.४४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aho karaṇavṛndasya kāpi lakṣmīrvijṛmbhate||17.44||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-prabhu" class="pv-gl">prabho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>o<rp> (</rp><rt>aho</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>In the matter of worshiping You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-pūjā-karmaṇi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>in this good treasury of all worships<rp> (</rp><rt>aśeṣa-pūjā-sat-kośe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>unfolds<rp> (</rp><rt>vijṛmbhate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>certain<rp> (</rp><rt>kā api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Śrī<rp> (</rp><rt>lakṣmīḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the dream of the sense organs<rp> (</rp><rt>karaṇa-vṛndasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||17.44||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.45" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एषा पेशलिमा नाथ तवैव किल दृश्यते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Eṣā peśalimā nātha tavaiva kila dṛśyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विश्वेश्वरोऽपि भृत्यैर्यदर्च्यसे यश्च लभ्यसे॥१७.४५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Viśveśvaro'pi bhṛtyairyadarcyase yaśca labhyase||17.45||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>eṣā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>subtlety<rp> (</rp><rt>peśalimā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is seen<rp> (</rp><rt>dṛśyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>indeed<rp> (</rp><rt>kila</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> in Thee alone (tava eva): <ruby>being the Lord of the universe<rp> (</rp><rt>viśva-īśvaraḥ api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Thou art worshipped<rp> (</rp><rt>arcyase</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by servants<rp> (</rp><rt>bhṛtyaiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by them indeed<rp> (</rp><rt>yaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>art attained<rp> (</rp><rt>labhyase</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||17.45||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.46" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सदामुर्त्तादमूर्त्ताद्वा भावाद्यद्वाप्यभावतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sadāmurttādamūrttādvā bhāvādyadvāpyabhāvataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उत्थेयान्मे प्रशस्तस्य भवत्पूजामहोत्सवः॥१७.४६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Uttheyānme praśastasya bhavatpūjāmahotsavaḥ||17.46||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>May<rp> (</rp><rt>uttheyāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>of the praised one<rp> (</rp><rt>praśastasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the great festival of worshipping You arises<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-pūjā-mahā-utsavaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>from a thing<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhava" class="pv-gl">bhāvāt</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with a form<rp> (</rp><rt>amūrtāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>or<rp> (</rp><rt>vā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>without a form<rp> (</rp><rt>amūrtāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>or<rp> (</rp><rt>yad vā api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even from non-being<rp> (</rp><rt>abhāvataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||17.46||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.47" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कामक्रोधाभिमानैस्त्वामुपहरीकृतैः सदा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kāmakrodhābhimānaistvāmupaharīkṛtaiḥ sadā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">येऽर्चयन्ति नमस्तेभ्यस्तेषां तुष्टोऽस्मि तत्त्वतः॥१७.४७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ye'rcayanti namastebhyasteṣāṁ tuṣṭo'smi tattvataḥ||17.47||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Homage<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-namas" class="pv-gl">namaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to those<rp> (</rp><rt>tebhyaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who<rp> (</rp><rt>ye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>worship<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-arcana" class="pv-gl">arcayanti</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with desire, anger and pride<rp> (</rp><rt>kāma-krodha-abhimānaiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>turned into an offering<rp> (</rp><rt>upahārī-kṛtaiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>by them<rp> (</rp><rt>teṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I<rp> (</rp><rt>asmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>am pleased<rp> (</rp><rt>tuṣṭaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in truth<rp> (</rp><rt>tattvataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||17.47||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.48" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जयत्येष भवद्भक्तिभाजां पूजाविधिः परः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jayatyeṣa bhavadbhaktibhājāṁ pūjāvidhiḥ paraḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यस्तृणैः क्रियमानोऽपि रत्नैरेवोपकल्पते॥१७.४८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yastṛṇaiḥ kriyamāno'pi ratnairevopakalpate||17.48||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Victorious<rp> (</rp><rt>jayati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>eṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>highest<rp> (</rp><rt>paraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>rite of worship<rp> (</rp><rt>pūjā-vidhiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>among those possessed of devotion to Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-bhakti-bhājām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>being performed<rp> (</rp><rt>kriyamāṇaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with blades of grass<rp> (</rp><rt>tṛṇaiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>it serves<rp> (</rp><rt>upakalpate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>as if with jewels<rp> (</rp><rt>ratnaiḥ eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||17.48||</p>
