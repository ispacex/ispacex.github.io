# Hymn 4 — Surasodbalākhyaṁ caturthaṁ stotram

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4.1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चपलमसि यदपि मानस तत्रापि श्लाघ्यसे यतो भजसे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Capalamasi yadapi mānasa tatrāpi ślāghyase yato bhajase|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शरणानामपि शरणं त्रिभुवनगुरुमम्बिकाकान्तम्॥४.१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śaraṇānāmapi śaraṇaṁ tribhuvanagurumambikākāntam||4.1||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Though<rp> (</rp><rt>yadapi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>you are fickle<rp> (</rp><rt>capalamasi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>O mind<rp> (</rp><rt>mānasa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>still<rp> (</rp><rt>tatrāpi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>you are praised<rp> (</rp><rt>ślāghyase</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>for that<rp> (</rp><rt>yataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>you honor<rp> (</rp><rt>bhajase</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the refuge<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-sharana" class="pv-gl">śaraṇam</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of refuges<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-sharana" class="pv-gl">śaraṇānām</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the teacher of the three worlds<rp> (</rp><rt>tri-bhuvana-gurum</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the beloved of Ambikā<rp> (</rp><rt>ambikā-kāntam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||4.1||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4.2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उल्लङ्घ्य विविधदैवतसोपानक्रममुपेय शिवचरणान्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ullaṅghya vividhadaivatasopānakramamupeya śivacaraṇān|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आश्रित्याप्यधरतरां भूमिं नाद्यापि चित्रमुज्झामि॥४.२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Āśrityāpyadharatarāṁ bhūmiṁ nādyāpi citramujjhāmi||4.2||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Wonder<rp> (</rp><rt>citram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>having stepped over<rp> (</rp><rt>ullaṅghya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the staircase of various deities<rp> (</rp><rt>vividha-daivata-sopāna-kramam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and having reached<rp> (</rp><rt>upeya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the feet of Śiva<rp> (</rp><rt>śiva-caraṇān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>I, having clung<rp> (</rp><rt>āśritya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to the much lower earth<rp> (</rp><rt>adhara-tarām bhūmim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even now<rp> (</rp><rt>nādyāpi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I do not abandon it<rp> (</rp><rt>ujjhāmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||4.2||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4.3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रकटय निजमध्वानं स्थगयतरामखिललोकचरितानि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prakaṭaya nijamadhvānaṁ sthagayatarāmakhilalokacaritāni|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यावद्भवामि भगवंस्तव सपदि सदोदितो दासः॥४.३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yāvadbhavāmi bhagavaṁstava sapadi sadodito dāsaḥ||4.3||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Reveal<rp> (</rp><rt>prakaṭaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>my path<rp> (</rp><rt>nijam adhvānam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>hide more securely<rp> (</rp><rt>sthagaya-tarām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>all the ways of the world<rp> (</rp><rt>akhila-loka-caritāni</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>so that<rp> (</rp><rt>yāvat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I may become<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavāmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>at once<rp> (</rp><rt>sapadi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thy<rp> (</rp><rt>tava</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>ever-rising<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā-uditaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>servant<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-dasa" class="pv-gl">dāsaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||4.3||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4.4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शिव शिव शम्भो शङ्कर शरणागतवत्सलाशु कुरु करुणाम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śiva śiva śambho śaṅkara śaraṇāgatavatsalāśu kuru karuṇām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तव चरणकमलयुगलस्मरणपरस्य हि सम्पदोऽदूरे॥४.४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tava caraṇakamalayugalasmaraṇaparasya hi sampado'dūre||4.4||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Śiva, Śiva, Śambhu, Śaṅkara<rp> (</rp><rt>śiva śiva śambho śaṅkara</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>loving those who have come under your protection<rp> (</rp><rt>śaraṇa-āgata-vatsala</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>Quickly<rp> (</rp><rt>āśu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>grant<rp> (</rp><rt>kuru</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>mercy<rp> (</rp><rt>karuṇām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>for<rp> (</rp><rt>hi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> for one who is <ruby>devoted to remembrance of the pair of Your lotus-feet<rp> (</rp><rt>tava caraṇa-kamala-yugala-smaraṇa-parasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>riches<rp> (</rp><rt>sampadaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>are not far<rp> (</rp><rt>adūre</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||4.4||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4.5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तावकाङ्घ्रिकमलासनलीना ये यथारुचि जगद्रचयन्ति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tāvakāṅghrikamalāsanalīnā ye yathāruci jagadracayanti|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ते विरिञ्चिमधिकारमलेनालिप्तमस्ववशमीश हसन्ति॥४.५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Te viriñcimadhikāramalenāliptamasvavaśamīśa hasanti||4.5||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-ishvara" class="pv-gl">īśa</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>those<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who<rp> (</rp><rt>ye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>having dissolved on the seat of Your lotus-feet<rp> (</rp><rt>tāvaka-aṅghri-kamala-āsana-līnāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>create<rp> (</rp><rt>racayanti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the world<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-jagat" class="pv-gl">jagat</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>as they please<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā-ruci</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>they laugh<rp> (</rp><rt>hasanti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>at Viriñci<rp> (</rp><rt>viriñcim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>stained with the dirt of office<rp> (</rp><rt>adhikāra-malena āliptam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and not master of himself<rp> (</rp><rt>asva-vaśam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||4.5||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4.6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वत्प्रकाशवपुषो न विभिन्नं किं चन प्रभवति प्रतिभातुम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvatprakāśavapuṣo na vibhinnaṁ kiṁ cana prabhavati pratibhātum|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्सदैव भगवन्परिलब्धोऽसीश्वर प्रकृतितोऽपि विदूरः॥४.६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatsadaiva bhagavanparilabdho'sīśvara prakṛtito'pi vidūraḥ||4.6||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Nothing<rp> (</rp><rt>kiṁ cana</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>separate<rp> (</rp><rt>vibhinnam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from Your body of light<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-prakāśa-vapuṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>cannot<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>appear<rp> (</rp><rt>prabhavati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>before<rp> (</rp><rt>pratibhātum</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Therefore<rp> (</rp><rt>tat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>You<rp> (</rp><rt>asi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>are always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> already attained<ruby> (<rp></rp>parilabdhaḥ<rt></rt>)<rp></rp>, </ruby>O Sovereign<ruby> (<rp></rp><rt>īśvara<a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-ishvara" class="pv-gl"></a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>though<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by Your very nature<rp> (</rp><rt>prakṛtitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>You are far<rp> (</rp><rt>vidūraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||4.6||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4.7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पादपङ्कजरसं तव केचिद्भेदपर्युषितवृत्तिमुपेताः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pādapaṅkajarasaṁ tava kecidbhedaparyuṣitavṛttimupetāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">के चनापि रसयन्ति तु सध्यो भातमक्षतवपुर्द्वयशून्यम्॥४.७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ke canāpi rasayanti tu sadhyo bhātamakṣatavapurdvayaśūnyam||4.7||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The nectar of Your lotus-feet<rp> (</rp><rt>tava pāda-paṅkaja-rasam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>some<rp> (</rp><rt>kecid</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>taste<rp> (</rp><rt>rasayanti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>having fallen into a state hardened by discrimination<rp> (</rp><rt>bheda-paryuṣita-vṛttim upetāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>while<rp> (</rp><rt>tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>others<rp> (</rp><rt>ke canāpi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>freshly<rp> (</rp><rt>sadyaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>as it shone forth<rp> (</rp><rt>bhātam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>with body untouched<rp> (</rp><rt>akṣata-vapuḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>empty of duality<rp> (</rp><rt>dvaya-śūnyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||4.7||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4.8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नाथ विद्युदिव भाति विभाते या कदा चन ममामृतदिग्धा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nātha vidyudiva bhāti vibhāte yā kadā cana mamāmṛtadigdhā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सा यदि स्थिरतरैव भवेत्तत्पूजितोऽसि विधिवत्किमुतान्यत्॥४.८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sā yadi sthirataraiva bhavettatpūjito'si vidhivatkimutānyat||4.8||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>yā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which sometimes<rp> (</rp><rt>kadā cana</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>shines<rp> (</rp><rt>bhāti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in me<rp> (</rp><rt>mama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>at dawn<rp> (</rp><rt>vibhāte</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>like lightning<rp> (</rp><rt>vidyudiva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>anointed with nectar<rp> (</rp><rt>amṛta-digdhā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>if<rp> (</rp><rt>yadi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>it<rp> (</rp><rt>sā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>were to become<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavet</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even a little more stable<rp> (</rp><rt>sthira-tarā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>then<rp> (</rp><rt>tat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>You<rp> (</rp><rt>asi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>would be honoured<rp> (</rp><rt>pūjitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>according to all rules<rp> (</rp><rt>vidhi-vat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>What need then of another?<rp> (</rp><rt>kimutānyat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||4.8||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4.9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वमस्यपरमस्ति न किञ्चिद्वस्त्ववस्तु यदि वेति महत्या।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvamasyaparamasti na kiñcidvastvavastu yadi veti mahatyā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रज्ञाय व्यवसितोऽत्र यथैव त्वं तथैव भव सुप्रकटो मे॥४.९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prajñāya vyavasito'tra yathaiva tvaṁ tathaiva bhava suprakaṭo me||4.9||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>You<rp> (</rp><rt>asi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>everything<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and there is nothing else<rp> (</rp><rt>aparam asti na kiñcit</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>whether a thing or a non-thing<rp> (</rp><rt>vastu avastu yadi vā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>As<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>You are determined<rp> (</rp><rt>vyavasitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>here<rp> (</rp><rt>atra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by great understanding<rp> (</rp><rt>mahatyā prajñayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>so<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and appear to me in visible form<rp> (</rp><rt>bhava su-prakaṭaḥ me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||4.9||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4.10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वेच्छयैव भगवन्निजमार्गे कारितः पदमहं प्रभुनैव।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svecchayaiva bhagavannijamārge kāritaḥ padamahaṁ prabhunaiva|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्कथं जनवदेव चरामि त्वत्पदोचितमवैमि न किञ्चित्॥४.१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatkathaṁ janavadeva carāmi tvatpadocitamavaimi na kiñcit||4.10||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>by Your own will<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-icchayā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>by the will of the Master<rp> (</rp><rt>prabhunā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>I<rp> (</rp><rt>aham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>have been placed by the foot<rp> (</rp><rt>kāritaḥ padam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>on Your path<rp> (</rp><rt>nija-mārge</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Why then<rp> (</rp><rt>tatkathaṁ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>do I walk<rp> (</rp><rt>carāmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>like an ordinary man<rp> (</rp><rt>janavadeva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and do not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>know<rp> (</rp><rt>avaimi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>anything<rp> (</rp><rt>kiñcid</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>that would befit Your foot<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-pada-ucitam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||4.10||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4.11" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कोऽपि देव हृदि तेषु तावको जृम्भते सुभगभाव उत्तमः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ko'pi deva hṛdi teṣu tāvako jṛmbhate subhagabhāva uttamaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वत्कथाम्बुदनिनादचातका येन तेऽपि सुभगीकृताश्चिरम्॥४.११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvatkathāmbudaninādacātakā yena te'pi subhagīkṛtāściram||4.11||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O God<rp> (</rp><rt>deva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>in their<rp> (</rp><rt>teṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>hearts<rp> (</rp><rt>hṛdi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>unfolds<rp> (</rp><rt>jṛmbhate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>some<rp> (</rp><rt>kaḥ api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thy<rp> (</rp><rt>tāvakaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>highest<rp> (</rp><rt>uttamaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>state of blissful beauty<rp> (</rp><rt>subhaga-bhāvaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>by which<rp> (</rp><rt>yena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>those<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> cātakas, <ruby>that cry at the thunder of the clouds of tales about Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-kathā-ambuda-nināda-cātakāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>for long<rp> (</rp><rt>ciram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>are made blissful<rp> (</rp><rt>subhagī-kṛtāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||4.11||</p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Where<rp> (</rp><rt>yatra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>sets<rp> (</rp><rt>astamayameti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>saḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>sun<rp> (</rp><rt>vivasvān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>together<rp> (</rp><rt>saha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with all<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvaiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>with the moon<rp> (</rp><rt>candramaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and others<rp> (</rp><rt>prabhṛtibhiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>there triumphs<rp> (</rp><rt>vijayate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>a certain<rp> (</rp><rt>kā api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>night of Śiva<rp> (</rp><rt>śiva-rātriḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>whose form shines with the outpouring of its own light<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-prabhā-prasara-bhāsvara-rūpā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||4.22||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4.23" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अप्युपार्जितमहं त्रिषु लोकेष्वधिपत्यममरेश्वर मन्ये।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Apyupārjitamahaṁ triṣu lokeṣvadhipatyamamareśvara manye|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नीरसं तदखिलं भवदङ्घ्रिस्पर्शनामृतरसेन विहीनम्॥४.२३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nīrasaṁ tadakhilaṁ bhavadaṅghrisparśanāmṛtarasena vihīnam||4.23||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The Lord of immortals<rp> (</rp><rt>amara-īśvara</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the obtained<rp> (</rp><rt>upārjitam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>lordship<rp> (</rp><rt>adhipatyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>over the three<rp> (</rp><rt>triṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>worlds<rp> (</rp><rt>lokeṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I<rp> (</rp><rt>aham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>consider<rp> (</rp><rt>manye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>all<rp> (</rp><rt>akhilam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>tat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>tasteless<rp> (</rp><rt>nīrasam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>since it is devoid<rp> (</rp><rt>vihīnam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of the nectarous juice of touching Your feet<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-aṅghri-sparśana-amṛta-rasena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||4.23||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4.24" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बत नाथ दृढोऽयमात्मबन्धो भवदख्यातिमयस्त्वयैव कॢप्तः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bata nātha dṛḍho'yamātmabandho bhavadakhyātimayastvayaiva kḷptaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यदयं प्रथमानमेव मे त्वामवधीर्य श्लथते न लेशतोऽपि॥४.२४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yadayaṁ prathamānameva me tvāmavadhīrya ślathate na leśato'pi||4.24||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Ах<rp> (</rp><rt>bata</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Владыка<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>крепка<rp> (</rp><rt>dṛḍhaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>эта<rp> (</rp><rt>ayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>привязь к самому себе<rp> (</rp><rt>ātma-bandhaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>сотканная из незнания о Тебе<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-akhyāti-mayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>и Тобою же<rp> (</rp><rt>tvayā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>слаженная<rp> (</rp><rt>kḷptaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>ведь<rp> (</rp><rt>yat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>она<rp> (</rp><rt>ayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>не ослабевает<rp> (</rp><rt>ślathate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>ни на волос<rp> (</rp><rt>na leśataḥ api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>пренебрегая<rp> (</rp><rt>avadhīrya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Тобою<rp> (</rp><rt>tvām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>у меня<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>уже сияющим<rp> (</rp><rt>prathamānam eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||4.24||</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4.25" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">महताममरेश पूज्यमानोऽप्यनिशं तिष्ठसि पूजकैकरूपः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mahatāmamareśa pūjyamāno'pyaniśaṁ tiṣṭhasi pūjakaikarūpaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बहिरन्तरपीह दृश्यमानः स्फुरसि द्रष्टृशरीर एव शश्वत्॥४.२५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bahirantarapīha dṛśyamānaḥ sphurasi draṣṭṛśarīra eva śaśvat||4.25||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Lord of the immortals<rp> (</rp><rt>amara-īśa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>though<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the great<rp> (</rp><rt>mahatām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and worship Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>pūjyamānaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>unceasingly<rp> (</rp><rt>aniśam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Thou remainest<rp> (</rp><rt>tiṣṭhasi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the single form of the worshipper<rp> (</rp><rt>pūjaka-eka-rūpaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>And<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>here<rp> (</rp><rt>iha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>visible<rp> (</rp><rt>dṛśyamānaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>without and within<rp> (</rp><rt>bahiḥ antaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Thou shinest<rp> (</rp><rt>sphurasi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>śaśvat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>only<rp> (</rp><rt>eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the body of the seer<rp> (</rp><rt>draṣṭṛ-śarīraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||4.25||</p>
