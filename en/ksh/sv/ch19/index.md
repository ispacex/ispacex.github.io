# Hymn 19 — Udyotanābhidhānamekonaviṁśaṁ stotram

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19.1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रार्थनाभूमिकातीतविचित्रफलदायकः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prārthanābhūmikātītavicitraphaladāyakaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जयत्यपूर्ववृत्तान्तः शिवः सत्कल्पपादपः॥१९.१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jayatyapūrvavṛttāntaḥ śivaḥ satkalpapādapaḥ||19.1||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Victorious<rp> (</rp><rt>jayati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Śiva<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-shiva" class="pv-gl">śivaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the good wish-fulfilling tree<rp> (</rp><rt>sat-kalpa-pādapaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>whose tale is unprecedented<rp> (</rp><rt>apūrva-vṛttāntaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, bestowing wondrous <ruby>fruits that surpass even the very stage of asking<rp> (</rp><rt>prārthanā-bhūmikā-atīta-vicitra-phala-dāyakaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||19.1||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19.2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्ववस्तुनि च यैकनिधानात्स्वात्मनस्त्वदखिलं किल लभ्यम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvavastuni ca yaikanidhānātsvātmanastvadakhilaṁ kila labhyam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अस्य मे पुनरसौ निजा आत्मा न त्वमेव घटसे परमास्ताम्॥१९.२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Asya me punarasau nijā ātmā na tvameva ghaṭase paramāstām||19.2||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>И<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>во всякой вещи<rp> (</rp><rt>sarva-vastuni</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>из собственной самости<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-ātmanaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>единственной сокровищницы<rp> (</rp><rt>eka-nidhānāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>поистине<rp> (</rp><rt>kila</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>обретаемо<rp> (</rp><rt>labhyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>всё Твоё<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-akhilam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Но<rp> (</rp><rt>punaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> у меня (asya me) <ruby>эта<rp> (</rp><rt>asau</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>самость<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-atman" class="pv-gl">ātmā</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>своя<rp> (</rp><rt>nijā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, а Ты (tvam eva) <ruby>в неё не<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>укладываешься<rp> (</rp><rt>ghaṭase</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Пусть уж<rp> (</rp><rt>param āstām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> так||19.2||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19.3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ज्ञानकर्ममयचिद्वपुरात्मा सर्वथैष परमेश्वर एव।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jñānakarmamayacidvapurātmā sarvathaiṣa parameśvara eva|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्याद्वपुस्तु निखिलेषु पदार्थेष्वेषु नाम न भवेत्किमुतान्यत्॥१९.३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Syādvapustu nikhileṣu padārtheṣveṣu nāma na bhavetkimutānyat||19.3||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>This<rp> (</rp><rt>eṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>self<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-atman" class="pv-gl">ātmā</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, whose <ruby>body is Consciousness, consisting of knowledge and action<rp> (</rp><rt>jñāna-karma-maya-cit-vapuḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>in all respects<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is indeed<rp> (</rp><rt>eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the Supreme Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-parameshvara" class="pv-gl">parama-īśvaraḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>And were<rp> (</rp><rt>syāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the body<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vapus" class="pv-gl">vapuḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in all<rp> (</rp><rt>nikhileṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>these<rp> (</rp><rt>eṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>things<rp> (</rp><rt>padārtheṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>it would not even be by name<rp> (</rp><rt>nāma</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>be<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavet</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>how much less<rp> (</rp><rt>kimuta</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of another<rp> (</rp><rt>anyat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <span class="pv-note">— the stanza is obscure —</span> ||19.3||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19.4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विषमार्तिमुषानेन फलेन त्वद्दृगात्मना।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Viṣamārtimuṣānena phalena tvaddṛgātmanā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अभिलीय पथा नाथ ममास्तु त्वन्मयी गतिः॥१९.४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Abhilīya pathā nātha mamāstu tvanmayī gatiḥ||19.4||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>by this<rp> (</rp><rt>anena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>fruit<rp> (</rp><rt>phalena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>carrying away heavy anguish<rp> (</rp><rt>viṣama-ārti-muṣā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which is the very sight of Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-dṛk-ātmanā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>having dissolved<rp> (</rp><rt>abhilīya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>on the path<rp> (</rp><rt>pathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>let there be<rp> (</rp><rt>astu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for me<rp> (</rp><rt>mama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>consisting of Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-mayī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>movement<rp> (</rp><rt>gatiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||19.4||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19.5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवदमलचरणचिन्तारत्नलतालङ्कृता कदा सिद्धिः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavadamalacaraṇacintāratnalatālaṅkṛtā kadā siddhiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सिद्धजनमानसानां विस्मयजननी घटेत मम भवतः॥१९.५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Siddhajanamānasānāṁ vismayajananī ghaṭeta mama bhavataḥ||19.5||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>When will<rp> (</rp><rt>kadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>there come together<rp> (</rp><rt>ghaṭeta</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for me<rp> (</rp><rt>mama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that perfection<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-siddhi" class="pv-gl">siddhiḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, adorned with the vine of <ruby>gems — the thought of Your immaculate feet<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-amala-caraṇa-cintā-ratna-latā-alaṅkṛtā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which gives birth to wonder<rp> (</rp><rt>vismaya-jananī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and in the minds of the perfected<rp> (</rp><rt>siddha-jana-mānasānām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||19.5||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19.6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कर्हि नाथ विमलं मुखबिम्बं तावकं समवलोकयितास्मि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Karhi nātha vimalaṁ mukhabimbaṁ tāvakaṁ samavalokayitāsmi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यत्स्रवत्यमृतपूरमपूर्वं यो निमज्जयति विश्वमशेषम्॥१९.६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yatsravatyamṛtapūramapūrvaṁ yo nimajjayati viśvamaśeṣam||19.6||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>when indeed<rp> (</rp><rt>karhi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>shall I see<rp> (</rp><rt>samavalokayitā asmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thy<rp> (</rp><rt>tāvakam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>spotless<rp> (</rp><rt>vimalam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>orb of the face<rp> (</rp><rt>mukha-bimbam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>from which<rp> (</rp><rt>yat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>flows forth<rp> (</rp><rt>sravati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>unprecedented<rp> (</rp><rt>apūrvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>stream of nectar<rp> (</rp><rt>amṛta-pūram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and which<rp> (</rp><rt>yaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>submerges<rp> (</rp><rt>nimajjayati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the entire<rp> (</rp><rt>aśeṣam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>universe<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vishva" class="pv-gl">viśvam</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||19.6||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19.7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ध्यातमात्रमुदितं तव रूपं कर्हि नाथ परमामृतपूरैः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dhyātamātramuditaṁ tava rūpaṁ karhi nātha paramāmṛtapūraiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूरयेत्त्वदविभेदविमोक्षाख्यातिदूरविवराणि सदा मे॥१९.७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūrayettvadavibhedavimokṣākhyātidūravivarāṇi sadā me||19.7||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>when indeed<rp> (</rp><rt>karhi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thy<rp> (</rp><rt>tava</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>form<rp> (</rp><rt>rūpam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>arising from mere contemplation<rp> (</rp><rt>dhyāta-mātra-uditam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>forever<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>will fill<rp> (</rp><rt>pūrayet</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> those distant crevices of ignorance <ruby>concerning non-distinction from Thee and liberation<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-avibheda-vimokṣa-akhyāti-dūra-vivarāṇi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with streams of supreme nectar<rp> (</rp><rt>parama-amṛta-pūraiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||19.7||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19.8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वदीयानुत्तररसासङ्गसन्त्यक्तचापलम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvadīyānuttararasāsaṅgasantyaktacāpalam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नाद्यापि मे मनो नाथ कर्हि स्यादस्तु शीघ्रतः॥१९.८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nādyāpi me mano nātha karhi syādastu śīghrataḥ||19.8||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>my<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>mind<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-manas" class="pv-gl">manaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, having abandoned fickleness <ruby>from attachment to Your supreme rasa<rp> (</rp><rt>tvadīya-anuttara-rasa-āsaṅga-santyakta-cāpalam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>even now<rp> (</rp><rt>nādyāpi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> is not so. <ruby>When indeed<rp> (</rp><rt>karhi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>will it become<rp> (</rp><rt>syāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> that? <ruby>Let it be<rp> (</rp><rt>astu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>quickly<rp> (</rp><rt>śīghrataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||19.8||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19.9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मा शुष्ककटुकान्येव परं सर्वाणि सर्वदा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mā śuṣkakaṭukānyeva paraṁ sarvāṇi sarvadā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तवोपहृत्य लब्धानि द्वन्द्वान्यप्यापतन्तु मे॥१९.९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tavopahṛtya labdhāni dvandvānyapyāpatantu me||19.9||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Let not<rp> (</rp><rt>mā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>only<rp> (</rp><rt>param</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>dry and bitter<rp> (</rp><rt>śuṣka-kaṭukāni eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>let all<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvāṇi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>pairs of opposites<rp> (</rp><rt>dvandvāni api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>offered<rp> (</rp><rt>upahṛtya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to You<rp> (</rp><rt>tava</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and received back<rp> (</rp><rt>labdhāni</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>fall<rp> (</rp><rt>āpatantu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||19.9||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19.10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नाथ साम्मुख्यमायान्तु विशुद्धास्तव रश्मयः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nātha sāmmukhyamāyāntu viśuddhāstava raśmayaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यावत्कायमनस्तापतमोभिः परिलुप्यताम्॥१९.१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yāvatkāyamanastāpatamobhiḥ parilupyatām||19.10||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>may Thy<rp> (</rp><rt>tava</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>purest<rp> (</rp><rt>viśuddhāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>rays<rp> (</rp><rt>raśmayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>turn their face toward me<rp> (</rp><rt>sāmmukhyam āyāntu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>so that<rp> (</rp><rt>yāvat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>may perish<rp> (</rp><rt>parilupyatām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the darkness of the heat of body and mind<rp> (</rp><rt>kāya-manaḥ-tāpa-tamobhiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||19.10||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19.11" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">देव प्रसीद यावन्मे त्वन्मार्गपरिपन्थिकाः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Deva prasīda yāvanme tvanmārgaparipanthikāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">परमार्थमुषो वश्या भूयासुर्गुणतस्कराः॥१९.११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Paramārthamuṣo vaśyā bhūyāsurguṇataskarāḥ||19.11||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O God<rp> (</rp><rt>deva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>have mercy<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-prasada" class="pv-gl">prasīda</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>so that<rp> (</rp><rt>yāvat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>may become<rp> (</rp><rt>bhūyāsuḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>submissive<rp> (</rp><rt>vaśyāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>those thief-guṇas<rp> (</rp><rt>guṇa-taskarāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>that oppose Your path<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-mārga-paripanthikāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and steal the highest truth<rp> (</rp><rt>parama-artha-muṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||19.11||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19.12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वद्भक्तिसुधासारैर्मानसमापूर्यतां ममाशु विभो।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvadbhaktisudhāsārairmānasamāpūryatāṁ mamāśu vibho|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यावदिमा उह्यन्तां निःशेषासारवासनाः प्लुत्वा॥१९.१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yāvadimā uhyantāṁ niḥśeṣāsāravāsanāḥ plutvā||19.12||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>All-pervading<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vibhu" class="pv-gl">vibho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>may my<rp> (</rp><rt>mama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>mind<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-manas" class="pv-gl">mānasam</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>quickly<rp> (</rp><rt>āśu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>be filled<rp> (</rp><rt>āpūryatām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with the essence of the nectar of devotion to Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-bhakti-sudhā-sāraiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>so that<rp> (</rp><rt>yāvat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>these<rp> (</rp><rt>imāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>worthless habits<rp> (</rp><rt>niḥśeṣa-asāra-vāsanāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>flooded<rp> (</rp><rt>plutvā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>might be carried away<rp> (</rp><rt>uhyantām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||19.12||</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19.13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मोक्षदशायां भक्तिस्त्वयि कुत इव मर्त्यधर्मिणोऽपि न सा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mokṣadaśāyāṁ bhaktistvayi kuta iva martyadharmiṇo'pi na sā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">राजति ततोऽनुरूपामारोपय सिद्धिभूमिकामज माम्॥१९.१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Rājati tato'nurūpāmāropaya siddhibhūmikāmaja mām||19.13||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Whence<rp> (</rp><rt>kutaḥ iva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>could devotion<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakti" class="pv-gl">bhaktiḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvayi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>arise in the state of liberation<rp> (</rp><rt>mokṣa-daśāyām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>? <ruby>It<rp> (</rp><rt>sā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>does not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>shine<rp> (</rp><rt>rājati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in one whose law is mortality<rp> (</rp><rt>martya-dharmiṇaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Therefore<rp> (</rp><rt>tataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>O Unborn One<rp> (</rp><rt>aja</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>raise<rp> (</rp><rt>āropaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>me<rp> (</rp><rt>mām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to the fitting<rp> (</rp><rt>anurūpām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>stage of perfection<rp> (</rp><rt>siddhi-bhūmikām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||19.13||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19.14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सिद्धिलवलाभलुब्धं मामवलेपेन मा विभो संस्थाः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Siddhilavalābhalubdhaṁ māmavalepena mā vibho saṁsthāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">क्षामस्त्वद्भक्तिमुखे प्रोल्लसदणिमादिपक्षतो मोक्षः॥१९.१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kṣāmastvadbhaktimukhe prollasadaṇimādipakṣato mokṣaḥ||19.14||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>All-pervading<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vibhu" class="pv-gl">vibho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>do not<rp> (</rp><rt>mā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>place<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁsthāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>me<rp> (</rp><rt>mām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>greedy for a crumb of perfection<rp> (</rp><rt>siddhi-lava-lābha-lubdham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>in arrogance<rp> (</rp><rt>avalepena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Liberation<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-moksa" class="pv-gl">mokṣaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>meager<rp> (</rp><rt>kṣāmaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> beside the wing of aṇimā <ruby>and the rest, playing in the mouth of devotion to Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-bhakti-mukhe prollasat-aṇimā-ādi-pakṣataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||19.14||</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19.15" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दासस्य मे प्रसीदतु भगवानेतावदेव ननु याचे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dāsasya me prasīdatu bhagavānetāvadeva nanu yāce|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दाता त्रिभुवननाथो यस्य न तन्मादृशां दृशो विषयः॥१९.१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dātā tribhuvananātho yasya na tanmādṛśāṁ dṛśo viṣayaḥ||19.15||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>May he be gracious<rp> (</rp><rt>prasīdatu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>a servant<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-dasa" class="pv-gl">dāsasya</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>for<rp> (</rp><rt>nanu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I ask<rp> (</rp><rt>yāce</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>only for this<rp> (</rp><rt>etāvat eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>yasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the giver<rp> (</rp><rt>dātā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the Lord of the three worlds<rp> (</rp><rt>tri-bhuvana-nāthaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>was not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>tat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which is beyond the field of vision<rp> (</rp><rt>dṛśaḥ viṣayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of such as me<rp> (</rp><rt>mādṛśām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||19.15||</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19.16" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वद्वपुःस्मृतिसुधारसपूर्णे मानसे तव पदाम्बुजयुग्मम्।</span><br />

<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मामके विकसदस्तु सदैव प्रस्रवन्मधु किमप्यतिलोकम्॥१९.१६॥</span><br />

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Let<rp> (</rp><rt>astu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in my<rp> (</rp><rt>māmake</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>mind<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-manas" class="pv-gl">mānase</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>full of the nectarous juice of remembrance of Your body<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-vapuḥ-smṛti-sudhā-rasa-pūrṇe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>unfolds<rp> (</rp><rt>vikasat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> the pair of Your lotus-feet (tava pada-ambuja-yugmam), <ruby>distilling<rp> (</rp><rt>prasravat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> a certain (kim api) <ruby>supramundane<rp> (</rp><rt>ati-lokam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>honey<rp> (</rp><rt>madhu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||19.16||</p>
