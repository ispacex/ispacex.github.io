<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15.1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्रिमलक्षालिनो ग्रन्थाः सन्ति तत्पारगास्तथा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Trimalakṣālino granthāḥ santi tatpāragāstathā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">योगिनः पण्डिताः स्वस्थास्त्वद्भक्ता एव तत्त्वतः॥१५.१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yoginaḥ paṇḍitāḥ svasthāstvadbhaktā eva tattvataḥ||15.1||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>There are<rp> (</rp><rt>santi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>books<rp> (</rp><rt>granthāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>washing away the three impurities<rp> (</rp><rt>tri-mala-kṣāliṇaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and there are also<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>those who have crossed them<rp> (</rp><rt>tat-pāra-gāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>yogins<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-yoga" class="pv-gl">yoginaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>but in truth<rp> (</rp><rt>tattvataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the learned<rp> (</rp><rt>paṇḍitāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and those established in themselves<rp> (</rp><rt>svasthāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>only<rp> (</rp><rt>eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thy devotees<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-bhaktāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||15.1||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15.2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मायीयकालनियतिरागाद्याहारतर्पिताः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Māyīyakālaniyatirāgādyāhāratarpitāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चरन्ति सुखिनो नाथ भक्तिमन्तो जगत्तटे॥१५.२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Caranti sukhino nātha bhaktimanto jagattaṭe||15.2||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, satiated with food — māyā, <ruby>time, necessity, passion, and so forth<rp> (</rp><rt>māyīya-kāla-niyati-rāga-ādi-āhāra-tarpitāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>the devotees<rp> (</rp><rt>bhaktimantaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>walk<rp> (</rp><rt>caranti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>happy<rp> (</rp><rt>sukhinaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>along the shore of the world<rp> (</rp><rt>jagat-taṭe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||15.2||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15.3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">रुदन्तो वा हसन्तो वा त्वामुच्चैः प्रलपन्त्यमी।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Rudanto vā hasanto vā tvāmuccaiḥ pralapantyamī|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्ताः स्तुतिपदोच्चारोपचाराः पृथगेव ते॥१५.३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktāḥ stutipadoccāropacārāḥ pṛthageva te||15.3||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Weeping<rp> (</rp><rt>rudantaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>or<rp> (</rp><rt>vā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, laughing<ruby> (<rp></rp>hasantaḥ<rt></rt>)<rp></rp> </ruby>or<ruby> (<rp></rp>vā<rt></rt>)<rp></rp></ruby>, these<ruby> (<rp></rp>amī<rt></rt>)<rp></rp></ruby> <ruby>devotees<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakta" class="pv-gl">bhaktāḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>loudly<rp> (</rp><rt>uccaiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>prattle<rp> (</rp><rt>pralapanti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>the rite of uttering words of praise<rp> (</rp><rt>stuti-pada-uccāra-upacārāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for them<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is quite distinct<rp> (</rp><rt>pṛthak eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||15.3||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15.4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न विरक्तो न चापीशो मोक्षाकाङ्क्षी त्वदर्चकः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na virakto na cāpīśo mokṣākāṅkṣī tvadarcakaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवेयमपि तूद्रिक्तभक्त्यासवरसोन्मदः॥१५.४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaveyamapi tūdriktabhaktyāsavarasonmadaḥ||15.4||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Твой почитатель<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-arcakaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>не<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>отрешён<rp> (</rp><rt>viraktaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>и<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> не (na api) <ruby>владыка<rp> (</rp><rt>īśaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>и освобождения не ищет<rp> (</rp><rt>mokṣa-ākāṅkṣī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. Но (api tu) <ruby>да буду<rp> (</rp><rt>bhaveyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> я хмелен от переливающегося <ruby>через край хмельного сока преданности<rp> (</rp><rt>udrikta-bhakti-āsava-rasa-unmadaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||15.4||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15.5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बाह्यं हृदय एवान्तरभिहृत्यैव योऽर्चति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bāhyaṁ hṛdaya evāntarabhihṛtyaiva yo'rcati|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वामीश भक्तिपीयूषरसपूरैर्नमामि तम्॥१५.५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvāmīśa bhaktipīyūṣarasapūrairnamāmi tam||15.5||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-ishvara" class="pv-gl">īśa</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>him<rp> (</rp><rt>tam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who<rp> (</rp><rt>yaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>having drawn in<rp> (</rp><rt>abhihṛtya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the external<rp> (</rp><rt>bāhyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>inward, into the very heart<rp> (</rp><rt>hṛdaye eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>worships<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-arcana" class="pv-gl">arcati</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>him I praise<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-namas" class="pv-gl">namāmi</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with streams of the nectarous juice of devotion<rp> (</rp><rt>bhakti-pīyūṣa-rasa-pūraiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||15.5||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15.6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">धर्माधर्मात्मनोरन्तः क्रिययोर्ज्ञानयोस्तथा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dharmādharmātmanorantaḥ kriyayorjñānayostathā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सुखदुःखात्मनोर्भक्ताः किमप्यास्वादयन्त्यहो॥१५.६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sukhaduḥkhātmanorbhaktāḥ kimapyāsvādayantyaho||15.6||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>О<rp> (</rp><rt>aho</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>Преданные<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakta" class="pv-gl">bhaktāḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>вкушают<rp> (</rp><rt>āsvādayanti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>нечто<rp> (</rp><rt>kimapi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>внутри<rp> (</rp><rt>antaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>закона и беззакония<rp> (</rp><rt>dharma-adharma-ātmanoḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>действия и<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>знания<rp> (</rp><rt>kriyayoḥ jñānayoḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>счастья и горя<rp> (</rp><rt>sukha-duḥkha-ātmanoḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||15.6||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15.7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चराचरपितः स्वामिनप्यन्धा अपि कुष्ठिनः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Carācarapitaḥ svāminapyandhā api kuṣṭhinaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शोभन्ते परमुद्दामभवद्भक्तिविभूषणाः॥१५.७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śobhante paramuddāmabhavadbhaktivibhūṣaṇāḥ||15.7||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Father of the moving and the motionless<rp> (</rp><rt>cara-acara-pitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-svamin" class="pv-gl">svāmin</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>Even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the blind<rp> (</rp><rt>andhāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the lepers<rp> (</rp><rt>kuṣṭhinaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>shine<rp> (</rp><rt>śobhante</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, if <ruby>their ornament is unbridled devotion to Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>param uddāma-bhavat-bhakti-vibhūṣaṇāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||15.7||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15.8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शिलोञ्छपिच्छकशिपुविच्छायाङ्गा अपि प्रभो।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śiloñchapicchakaśipuvicchāyāṅgā api prabho|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवद्भक्तिमहोष्मणो राजराजमपीशते॥१५.८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavadbhaktimahoṣmaṇo rājarājamapīśate||15.8||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-prabhu" class="pv-gl">prabho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> those whose bodies have grown dull <ruby>from gleaning grains and from feathers serving as their bed<rp> (</rp><rt>śila-uñcha-piccha-kaśipu-vicchāya-aṅgāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>from the great heat of devotion to You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-bhakti-mahā-ūṣmaṇaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>rule<rp> (</rp><rt>īśate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>over the king of kings<rp> (</rp><rt>rāja-rājam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||15.8||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15.9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सुधार्द्रायां भवद्भक्तौ लुठताप्यारुरुक्षुणा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sudhārdrāyāṁ bhavadbhaktau luṭhatāpyārurukṣuṇā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चेतसैव विभोऽर्चन्ति केचित्त्वामभितः स्थिताः॥१५.९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Cetasaiva vibho'rcanti kecittvāmabhitaḥ sthitāḥ||15.9||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>All-pervading<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vibhu" class="pv-gl">vibho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>others<rp> (</rp><rt>kecit</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>though<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>rolling<rp> (</rp><rt>luṭhatā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in devotion to You, moist with nectar<rp> (</rp><rt>sudhā-ārdrāyām bhavat-bhaktau</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>with single-minded consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>cetasā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>desiring to ascend higher<rp> (</rp><rt>ārurukṣuṇā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>worship<rp> (</rp><rt>arcanti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>standing<rp> (</rp><rt>sthitāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>around<rp> (</rp><rt>abhitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||15.9||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15.10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">रक्षणीयं वर्धनीयं बहुमान्यमिदं प्रभो।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Rakṣaṇīyaṁ vardhanīyaṁ bahumānyamidaṁ prabho|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">संसारदुर्गतिहरं भवद्भक्तिमहाधनम्॥१५.१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Saṁsāradurgatiharaṁ bhavadbhaktimahādhanam||15.10||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-prabhu" class="pv-gl">prabho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>idam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>great wealth — devotion to You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-bhakti-mahā-dhanam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which removes the misfortune of the cycle of existence<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁsāra-durgati-haram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>is to be preserved<rp> (</rp><rt>rakṣaṇīyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>nurtured<rp> (</rp><rt>vardhanīyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and held in high esteem<rp> (</rp><rt>bahu-mānyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||15.10||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15.11" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नाथ ते भक्तजनता यद्यपि त्वयि रागिणी।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nātha te bhaktajanatā yadyapi tvayi rāgiṇī|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तथापीर्ष्यां विहायास्यास्तुष्टास्तु स्वामिनी सदा॥१५.११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tathāpīrṣyāṁ vihāyāsyāstuṣṭāstu svāminī sadā||15.11||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, though(yadi api) <ruby>the host of Your devotees<rp> (</rp><rt>te bhakta-janatā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is enamoured<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-raga" class="pv-gl">rāgiṇī</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvayi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>let the Mistress<rp> (</rp><rt>svāminī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>nevertheless<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>having abandoned<rp> (</rp><rt>vihāya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>jealousy<rp> (</rp><rt>īrṣyām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>towards him<rp> (</rp><rt>asyāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>be<rp> (</rp><rt>astu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>content<rp> (</rp><rt>tuṣṭā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||15.11||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15.12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवद्भावः पुरो भावी प्राप्ते त्वद्भक्तिसम्भवे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavadbhāvaḥ puro bhāvī prāpte tvadbhaktisambhave|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">लब्धे दुग्धमहाकुम्भे हता दधनि गृध्नुता॥१५.१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Labdhe dugdhamahākumbhe hatā dadhani gṛdhnutā||15.12||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>When attained<rp> (</rp><rt>prāpte</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the birth of devotion to Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-bhakti-sambhave</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>ahead<rp> (</rp><rt>puraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>surely will be<rp> (</rp><rt>bhāvī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and being by Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-bhāvaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>once obtained<rp> (</rp><rt>labdhe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the great pitcher of milk<rp> (</rp><rt>dugdha-mahā-kumbhe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>killed<rp> (</rp><rt>hatā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is greed<rp> (</rp><rt>gṛdhnutā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for curds<rp> (</rp><rt>dadhani</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||15.12||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15.13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">किमियं न सिद्धिरतुला किं वा मुख्यं न सौख्यमास्रवति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kimiyaṁ na siddhiratulā kiṁ vā mukhyaṁ na saukhyamāsravati|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तिरुपचीयमाना येयं शम्भोः सदातनी भवति॥१५.१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktirupacīyamānā yeyaṁ śambhoḥ sadātanī bhavati||15.13||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Is<rp> (</rp><rt>kim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>iyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>incomparable<rp> (</rp><rt>atulā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>perfection<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-siddhi" class="pv-gl">siddhiḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>? <ruby>Or<rp> (</rp><rt>kiṁ vā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>does<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>it not pour forth<rp> (</rp><rt>āsravati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the principal<rp> (</rp><rt>mukhyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>happiness<rp> (</rp><rt>saukhyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>yā iyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>devotion<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakti" class="pv-gl">bhaktiḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Śambhu<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-shambhu" class="pv-gl">śambhoḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>grows<rp> (</rp><rt>upacīyamānā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and lasts forever<rp> (</rp><rt>sadātanī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||15.13||</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15.14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मनसि मलिने मदीयेमग्ना त्वद्भक्तिमणिलता कष्टम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Manasi maline madīyemagnā tvadbhaktimaṇilatā kaṣṭam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न निजानपि तनुते तानपौरुषेयान्स्वसम्पदुल्लासान्॥१५.१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na nijānapi tanute tānapauruṣeyānsvasampadullāsān||15.14||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Alas<rp> (</rp><rt>kaṣṭam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>In my<rp> (</rp><rt>madīye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>impure<rp> (</rp><rt>maline</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>mind<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-manas" class="pv-gl">manasi</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>has sunk<rp> (</rp><rt>magnā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the vine of the gem — devotion to You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-bhakti-maṇi-latā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>it does not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>spread forth<rp> (</rp><rt>tanute</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>its own<rp> (</rp><rt>nijān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>those<rp> (</rp><rt>tān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>outpourings of its wealth<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-sampat-ullāsān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which are not of human origin<rp> (</rp><rt>apauruṣeyān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||15.14||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15.15" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तिर्भगवति भवति त्रिलोकनाथे ननूत्तमा सिद्धिः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktirbhagavati bhavati trilokanāthe nanūttamā siddhiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">किं त्वणिमादिकविरहात्सैव न पूर्णेति चिन्ता मे॥१५.१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kiṁ tvaṇimādikavirahātsaiva na pūrṇeti cintā me||15.15||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>For<rp> (</rp><rt>nanu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>devotion<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakti" class="pv-gl">bhaktiḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to the Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the Master of the three worlds<rp> (</rp><rt>tri-loka-nāthe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>is the highest<rp> (</rp><rt>uttamā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>perfection<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-siddhi" class="pv-gl">siddhiḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>But<rp> (</rp><rt>kiṁ tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>my concern<rp> (</rp><rt>cintā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>that from the lack of aṇimā and the rest<rp> (</rp><rt>aṇimā-ādika-virahāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>it<rp> (</rp><rt>sā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>full<rp> (</rp><rt>pūrṇā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||15.15||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15.16" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बाह्यतोऽन्तरपि चोत्कटोन्मिषत्त्र्यम्बकस्तवकसौरभाः शुभाः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bāhyato'ntarapi cotkaṭonmiṣattryambakastavakasaurabhāḥ śubhāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वासयन्त्यपि विरुद्धवासनान्योगिनो निकटवासिनोऽखिलान्॥१५.१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vāsayantyapi viruddhavāsanānyogino nikaṭavāsino'khilān||15.16||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Йогины<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-yoga" class="pv-gl">yoginaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>благоуханные благим<rp> (</rp><rt>śubhāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, у кого мощно <ruby>раскрывается благоухание соцветия Трёхокого<rp> (</rp><rt>utkaṭa-unmiṣat-tryambaka-stavaka-saurabhāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>и снаружи<rp> (</rp><rt>bāhyataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>и<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>внутри<rp> (</rp><rt>antaḥ api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>пропитывают<rp> (</rp><rt>vāsayanti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>собою и<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>всех<rp> (</rp><rt>akhilān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>живущих поблизости<rp> (</rp><rt>nikaṭa-vāsinaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>даже с противоположными наклонностями<rp> (</rp><rt>viruddha-vāsanān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||15.16||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15.17" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ज्योतिरस्ति कथयापि न किञ्चिद्विश्वमप्यतिसुषुप्तमशेषम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jyotirasti kathayāpi na kiñcidviśvamapyatisuṣuptamaśeṣam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यत्र नाथ शिवरात्रिपदेऽस्मिन्नित्यमर्चयति भक्तजनस्त्वाम्॥१५.१७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yatra nātha śivarātripade'sminnityamarcayati bhaktajanastvām||15.17||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>where<rp> (</rp><rt>yatra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>however one might say<rp> (</rp><rt>kathayā api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>there is no<rp> (</rp><rt>na asti kiñcid</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>light<rp> (</rp><rt>jyotiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the entire<rp> (</rp><rt>aśeṣam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>universe<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vishva" class="pv-gl">viśvam</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in deepest sleep<rp> (</rp><rt>ati-suṣuptam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>in this<rp> (</rp><rt>asmin</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>state of the night of Śiva<rp> (</rp><rt>śiva-rātri-pade</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the host of devotees<rp> (</rp><rt>bhakta-janaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>eternally<rp> (</rp><rt>nityam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>worships<rp> (</rp><rt>arcayati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||15.17||</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15.18" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सत्त्वं सत्यगुणे शिवे भगवति स्फारीभवत्वर्चने चूडायां विलसन्तु शङ्करपदप्रोद्यद्रजःसङ्चयाः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sattvaṁ satyaguṇe śive bhagavati sphārībhavatvarcane cūḍāyāṁ vilasantu śaṅkarapadaprodyadrajaḥsaṅcayāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">रागादिस्मृतिवासनामपि समुच्छेत्तुं तमो जृम्भतां शम्भो मे भवतात्त्वदात्मविलये त्रैगुण्यवर्गोऽथवा॥१५.१८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Rāgādismṛtivāsanāmapi samucchettuṁ tamo jṛmbhatāṁ śambho me bhavatāttvadātmavilaye traiguṇyavargo'thavā||15.18||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>May sattva<rp> (</rp><rt>sattvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>expand<rp> (</rp><rt>sphārī-bhavatu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in worship<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-arcana" class="pv-gl">arcane</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of Śiva<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-shiva" class="pv-gl">śive</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with true qualities<rp> (</rp><rt>satya-guṇe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>may on the crown<rp> (</rp><rt>cūḍāyām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>play<rp> (</rp><rt>vilasantu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>heaps of dust raised from the feet of Śaṅkara<rp> (</rp><rt>śaṅkara-pada-prodyat-rajaḥ-sañcayāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>may tamas<rp> (</rp><rt>tamaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>also unfold<rp> (</rp><rt>jṛmbhatām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>to eradicate<rp> (</rp><rt>samucchettum</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the trace of memory of passion and the rest<rp> (</rp><rt>rāga-ādi-smṛti-vāsanām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Śambhu<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-shambhu" class="pv-gl">śambho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>may<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavatāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the whole triad of guṇas<rp> (</rp><rt>traiguṇya-vargaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>or<rp> (</rp><rt>athavā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>indeed it — go into dissolution in Your self<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-ātma-vilaye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||15.18||</p>

<span class="pv-iast">Itthaṁ vyartho'smi jātaḥ śaśidharacaraṇākrāntikāntottamāṅgastvadbhaktaśceti tanme kuru sapadi mahāsampado dīrghadīrghāḥ||15.19||</span></p>
