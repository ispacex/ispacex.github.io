<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14.1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जयलक्ष्मीनिधानस्य निजस्य स्वामिनः पुरः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jayalakṣmīnidhānasya nijasya svāminaḥ puraḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जयोद्घोषणपीयूषरसमास्वादये क्षणम्॥१४.१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jayodghoṣaṇapīyūṣarasamāsvādaye kṣaṇam||14.1||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Before<rp> (</rp><rt>puraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the own<rp> (</rp><rt>nijasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-svamin" class="pv-gl">svāminaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the treasury of Śrī victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya-lakṣmī-nidhānasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>may I taste<rp> (</rp><rt>āsvādaye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even a moment<rp> (</rp><rt>kṣaṇam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the nectarous juice of the proclamation of victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya-udghoṣaṇa-pīyūṣa-rasam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||14.1||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14.2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जयैकरुद्रैकशिव महादेव महेश्वर।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jayaikarudraikaśiva mahādeva maheśvara|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पार्वतीप्रणयिञ्छर्व सर्वगीर्वाणपूर्वज॥१४.२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pārvatīpraṇayiñcharva sarvagīrvāṇapūrvaja||14.2||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Победа<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>единому Rudra, единому Śiva<rp> (</rp><rt>eka-rudra-eka-śiva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Mahādeva<rp> (</rp><rt>mahā-deva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Maheśvara<rp> (</rp><rt>mahā-īśvara</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>любящий Pārvatī<rp> (</rp><rt>pārvatī-praṇayin</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Śarva<rp> (</rp><rt>śarva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>рождённый прежде всех богов<rp> (</rp><rt>sarva-girvāṇa-pūrva-ja</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>!||14.2||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14.3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जय त्रैलोक्यनाथैकलाञ्छनालिकलोचन।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jaya trailokyanāthaikalāñchanālikalocana|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जय पीतर्तलोकार्तिकालकूटाङ्ककन्धर॥१४.३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jaya pītartalokārtikālakūṭāṅkakandhara||14.3||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to you, whose sole <ruby>mark is the eye on the forehead, lord of the three worlds<rp> (</rp><rt>trailokya-nātha-eka-lāñchana-alika-locana</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to you, whose neck is marked by <ruby>kālakūṭa, drunk for the sake of the suffering world’s anguish<rp> (</rp><rt>pīta-ārta-loka-ārti-kāla-kūṭa-aṅka-kandhara</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>!||14.3||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14.4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जय मूर्तत्रिशक्त्यात्मिशतशूलोल्लसत्कर।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jaya mūrtatriśaktyātmiśataśūlollasatkara|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जयेच्छामात्रसिद्दार्थपूजार्हचरणाम्बुज॥१४.४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jayecchāmātrasiddārthapūjārhacaraṇāmbuja||14.4||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to you, in whose hand shines the trident — the embodied triple Power <span class="pv-note">— at the source stands «ātmiśataśūla»; I read it as «ātma-triśūla»: «śata-śūla», «a hundred spears», does not fit among the epithets—</span> <span class="pv-w">(mūrta-tri-śakti-ātma-triśūla-ullasat-kara)</span>! <ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to you, whose lotus-feet are worthy of worship, <ruby>for by mere desire they fulfill the purpose<rp> (</rp><rt>icchā-mātra-siddha-artha-pūjā-arha-caraṇa-ambuja</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>!||14.4||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14.5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जय शोभशतस्यन्दिलोकोत्तरवपुर्धर।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jaya śobhaśatasyandilokottaravapurdhara|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जयैकजटिकाक्षीणगङ्गाकृत्यात्तभस्मक॥१४.५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jayaikajaṭikākṣīṇagaṅgākṛtyāttabhasmaka||14.5||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to you, whose otherworldly body exudes hundreds of beauties<rp> (</rp><rt>śobhā-śata-syandi-loka-uttara-vapuḥ-dhara</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to you, who took the ashes <ruby>from the deed of Gaṅgā, exhausted in a single lock of hair<rp> (</rp><rt>eka-jaṭikā-kṣīṇa-gaṅgā-kṛtya-ātta-bhasmaka</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>!||14.5||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14.6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जय क्षीरोदपर्यस्तज्योत्स्नाच्छायानुलेपन।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jaya kṣīrodaparyastajyotsnācchāyānulepana|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जयेश्वराङ्गसङ्गोत्थरत्नकान्ताहिमण्डन॥१४.६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jayeśvarāṅgasaṅgottharatnakāntāhimaṇḍana||14.6||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to you, whose anointing is the shadow of <ruby>moonlight spread over the milky ocean<rp> (</rp><rt>kṣīra-uda-paryasta-jyotsnā-chāyā-anulepana</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to you, whose ornament is the serpent with the gemlike <ruby>splendor arisen from the touch of the Lord’s body<rp> (</rp><rt>īśvara-aṅga-saṅga-uttha-ratna-kānta-ahi-maṇḍana</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>!||14.6||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14.7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जयाक्षयैकशीतांशुकलासदृशसंश्रय।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jayākṣayaikaśītāṁśukalāsadṛśasaṁśraya|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जय गङ्गासदार्ब्धविश्वैश्वर्याभिषेचन॥१४.७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jaya gaṅgāsadārbdhaviśvaiśvaryābhiṣecana||14.7||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to you, whose refuge <ruby>is like the undecaying crescent of the cool-rayed one<rp> (</rp><rt>akṣaya-eka-śīta-aṁśu-kalā-sadṛśa-saṁśraya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to you, whom the Ganges <ruby>eternally bathes for sovereignty over the universe<rp> (</rp><rt>gaṅgā-sadā-ārabdha-viśva-aiśvarya-abhiṣecana</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>!||14.7||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14.8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जयाधराङ्गसंस्पर्शपावनीकृतगोकुल।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jayādharāṅgasaṁsparśapāvanīkṛtagokula|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जय भक्तिमदाबद्धगोष्ठीनियतसन्निधे॥१४.८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jaya bhaktimadābaddhagoṣṭhīniyatasannidhe||14.8||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to you, <ruby>by whose touch the cow-herd is sanctified<rp> (</rp><rt>adhara-aṅga-saṁsparśa-pāvanī-kṛta-go-kula</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to you, whose presence is inseparable <ruby>from the assembly bound by the intoxication of devotion<rp> (</rp><rt>bhakti-mada-ābaddha-goṣṭhī-niyata-sannidhe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>!||14.8||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14.9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जय स्वेच्छातपोदेशविप्रलम्भितबालिश।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jaya svecchātapodeśavipralambhitabāliśa|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जय गौरीपरिष्वङ्गयोग्यसौभाग्यभाजन॥१४.९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jaya gaurīpariṣvaṅgayogyasaubhāgyabhājana||14.9||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to you, who of your own will deceived the <ruby>simpletons by instruction on asceticism<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-icchā-tapaḥ-deśa-vipralambhita-bāliśa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to you, vessel of fortune, worthy of the embrace of Gaurī<rp> (</rp><rt>gaurī-pariṣvaṅga-yogya-saubhāgya-bhājana</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>!||14.9||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14.10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जय भक्तिरसार्द्रार्द्रभावोपायनलम्पट।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jaya bhaktirasārdrārdrabhāvopāyanalampaṭa|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जय भक्तिमदोद्दामभक्तवाङ्नृत्ततोषित॥१४.१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jaya bhaktimadoddāmabhaktavāṅnṛttatoṣita||14.10||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to You, greedy for the gift — for the state, <ruby>moist and moist with the juice of devotion<rp> (</rp><rt>bhakti-rasa-ārdra-ārdra-bhāva-upāyana-lampaṭa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to You, delighted by the speech and dance of the <ruby>devotee, unrestrained in the intoxication of devotion<rp> (</rp><rt>bhakti-mada-uddāma-bhakta-vāk-nṛtta-toṣita</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>!||14.10||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14.11" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जय ब्रह्मादिदेवेशप्रभावप्रभवव्यय।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jaya brahmādideveśaprabhāvaprabhavavyaya|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जयलोकेश्वरश्रेणिशिरोविधृतशासन॥१४.११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jayalokeśvaraśreṇiśirovidhṛtaśāsana||14.11||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to you, from whom arise the birth<ruby> and destruction of the might of Brahmā and other lords of the gods<rp> (</rp><rt>brahma-ādi-deva-īśa-prabhāva-prabhava-vyaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to you, whose <ruby>command is borne on the heads of the hosts of world-lords<rp> (</rp><rt>loka-īśvara-śreṇi-śiraḥ-vidhṛta-śāsana</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>!||14.11||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14.12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जयसर्वजगन्न्यस्तस्वमुद्राव्यक्तवैभव।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jayasarvajagannyastasvamudrāvyaktavaibhava|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जयात्मदानपर्यन्तविश्वेश्वरमहेश्वर॥१४.१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jayātmadānaparyantaviśveśvaramaheśvara||14.12||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to You, whose greatness is revealed <ruby>by Your own seal placed upon the entire world<rp> (</rp><rt>sarva-jagat-nyasta-sva-mudrā-vyakta-vaibhava</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to You, Lord of the universe and Great <ruby>Lord, whose limit is to give Your very Self<rp> (</rp><rt>ātma-dāna-paryanta-viśva-īśvara-mahā-īśvara</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>!||14.12||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14.13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जय त्रैलोक्यसर्गेच्छावसरासद्द्वितीयक।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jaya trailokyasargecchāvasarāsaddvitīyaka|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जयैश्वर्यभरोद्वाहदेवीमात्रसहायक॥१४.१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jayaiśvaryabharodvāhadevīmātrasahāyaka||14.13||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to you, who have <ruby>no second at the hour of desiring to create the three worlds<rp> (</rp><rt>trailokya-sarga-icchā-avasara-asat-dvitīyaka</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to you, whose only helper is <ruby>the Goddess, bearing with You the burden of sovereignty<rp> (</rp><rt>aiśvarya-bhara-udvāha-devī-mātra-sahāyaka</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>!||14.13||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14.14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जयाक्रमसमाक्रान्तसमस्तभुवनत्रय।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jayākramasamākrāntasamastabhuvanatraya|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जयाविगीतमाबालगीयमानेश्वरध्वने॥१४.१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jayāvigītamābālagīyamāneśvaradhvane||14.14||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to you, who without a single step have encompassed all three worlds<rp> (</rp><rt>akrama-samākrānta-samasta-bhuvana-traya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to you, <ruby>whose name of the Lord is sung, without contradiction, even by children<rp> (</rp><rt>avigīta-ā-bāla-gīyamāna-īśvara-dhvane</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>!||14.14||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14.15" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जयानुकम्पादिगुणानपेक्षसहजोन्नते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jayānukampādiguṇānapekṣasahajonnate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जय भीष्ममहामृत्युघटनापूर्वभैरव॥१४.१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jaya bhīṣmamahāmṛtyughaṭanāpūrvabhairava||14.15||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to you, exalted by nature, needing neither <ruby>compassion nor other virtues<rp> (</rp><rt>anukampā-ādi-guṇa-anapekṣa-sahaja-unnate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to you, unprecedented <ruby>Bhairava, who arranged the terrible great death<rp> (</rp><rt>bhīṣma-mahā-mṛtyu-ghaṭanā-apūrva-bhairava</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>!||14.15||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14.16" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जय विश्वक्षयोच्चण्डक्रियानिष्परिपन्थिक।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jaya viśvakṣayoccaṇḍakriyāniṣparipanthika|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जय श्रेयःशतगुणानुगनामानुकीर्तन॥१४.१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jaya śreyaḥśataguṇānuganāmānukīrtana||14.16||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to you, who have <ruby>no rival in the fierce act of destroying the universe<rp> (</rp><rt>viśva-kṣaya-uccaṇḍa-kriyā-niṣparipanthika</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>Victory<rp> (</rp><rt>jaya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to you, whose name, <ruby>if repeated, leads to a hundredfold good<rp> (</rp><rt>śreyaḥ-śata-guṇa-anuga-nāma-anukīrtana</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>!||14.16||</p>
