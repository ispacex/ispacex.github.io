<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12.1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सहकारि न किञ्चिदिष्यते भवतो न प्रतिबन्धकं दृषि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sahakāri na kiñcidiṣyate bhavato na pratibandhakaṁ dṛṣi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवतैव हि सर्वमाप्लुतं कथमद्यापि तथापि नेक्षसे॥१२.१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavataiva hi sarvamāplutaṁ kathamadyāpi tathāpi nekṣase||12.1||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>For Your<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>vision<rp> (</rp><rt>dṛśi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>no<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>aid is needed<rp> (</rp><rt>iṣyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>any<rp> (</rp><rt>kiñcid</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>auxiliary<rp> (</rp><rt>sahakāri</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and there is no obstacle<rp> (</rp><rt>pratibandhakam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>For<rp> (</rp><rt>hi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by You alone<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavatā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>everything<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is flooded<rp> (</rp><rt>āplutam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>how then<rp> (</rp><rt>katham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>do You even now<rp> (</rp><rt>adyāpi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>still<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>see<rp> (</rp><rt>īkṣase</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||12.1||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12.2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अपि भावगणादपीन्द्रियप्रचयादप्यवबोधमध्यतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Api bhāvagaṇādapīndriyapracayādapyavabodhamadhyataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रभवन्तमपि स्वतः सदा परिपश्येयमपोढविश्वकम्॥१२.२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prabhavantamapi svataḥ sadā paripaśyeyamapoḍhaviśvakam||12.2||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>May I always contemplate<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā... paripaśyeyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Him who arises<rp> (</rp><rt>prabhavantam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from the multitude of things<rp> (</rp><rt>bhāva-gaṇāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from the gathering of the senses<rp> (</rp><rt>indriya-pracayāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from the midst of consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>avabodha-madhyataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>and yet Himself from Himself<rp> (</rp><rt>svataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>having cast off the universe<rp> (</rp><rt>apoḍha-viśvakam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||12.2||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12.3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कथं ते जायेरन्कथमपि च ते दर्शनपथं व्रजेयुः केनापि प्रकृतिमहताङ्केन खचितः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kathaṁ te jāyerankathamapi ca te darśanapathaṁ vrajeyuḥ kenāpi prakṛtimahatāṅkena khacitaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तथोत्थायोत्थाय स्थलजलतृणादेरखिलतः पदार्थद्यान्सृष्टिस्रवदमृतपूरैर्विकिरसि॥१२.३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tathotthāyotthāya sthalajalatṛṇāderakhilataḥ padārthadyānsṛṣṭisravadamṛtapūrairvikirasi||12.3||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>How<rp> (</rp><rt>katham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>they<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>would be born<rp> (</rp><rt>jāyeran</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> how indeed (katham api) <ruby>would they go out<rp> (</rp><rt>vrajeyuḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>onto the path of vision<rp> (</rp><rt>darśana-patham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>? <ruby>Marked<rp> (</rp><rt>khacitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> with some (kena api) <ruby>sign, great by its very nature<rp> (</rp><rt>prakṛti-mahatā aṅkena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Thou thus<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>rising and rising<rp> (</rp><rt>utthāya utthāya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>scatterest forth<rp> (</rp><rt>vikirasi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>those<rp> (</rp><rt>tān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>things<rp> (</rp><rt>padārthān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>dry land, water, grass, and so forth<rp> (</rp><rt>sthala-jala-tṛṇa-ādeḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>from everywhere<rp> (</rp><rt>akhilataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — with streams of nectar flowing from creation <span class="pv-note">— the stanza is obscure: the subject of the first half remains unnamed —</span> <span class="pv-w">(sṛṣṭi-sravat-amṛta-pūraiḥ)</span>||12.3||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12.4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">साक्षत्कृत भवद्रूपप्रसृतामृततर्पिताः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sākṣatkṛta bhavadrūpaprasṛtāmṛtatarpitāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उन्मूलिततृषो मत्ता विचरन्ति यथारुचि॥१२.४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Unmūlitatṛṣo mattā vicaranti yathāruci||12.4||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Those who have directly seen<rp> (</rp><rt>sākṣāt-kṛta</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, satiated with <ruby>the nectar flowing from Your form<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-rūpa-prasṛta-amṛta-tarpitāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>with thirst uprooted<rp> (</rp><rt>unmūlita-tṛṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>intoxicated<rp> (</rp><rt>mattāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>they wander<rp> (</rp><rt>vicaranti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>as they please<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā-ruci</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||12.4||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12.5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न तदा न सदा न चैकदेत्यपि सा यत्र न कालधीर्भवेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na tadā na sadā na caikadetyapi sā yatra na kāladhīrbhavet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदिदं भवदीयदर्शनं न च नित्यं न च कथ्यतेऽन्यथा॥१२.५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadidaṁ bhavadīyadarśanaṁ na ca nityaṁ na ca kathyate'nyathā||12.5||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Ни «тогда»<rp> (</rp><rt>na tadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>ни «всегда»<rp> (</rp><rt>na sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>ни<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>«однажды»<rp> (</rp><rt>na ekadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>там<rp> (</rp><rt>yatra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>не<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>бывает<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavet</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>и<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>той<rp> (</rp><rt>sā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>мысли о времени<rp> (</rp><rt>kāla-dhīḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Вот это<rp> (</rp><rt>tadidaṁ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>видение Тебя<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavadīya-darśanam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>и не вечно<rp> (</rp><rt>na ca nityaṁ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>и иначе<rp> (</rp><rt>anyathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>не<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>назовёшь<rp> (</rp><rt>kathyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||12.5||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12.6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वद्विलोकनसमुत्कचेतसो योगसिद्धिरियती सदास्तु मे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvadvilokanasamutkacetaso yogasiddhiriyatī sadāstu me|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यद्विशेयमभिसन्धिमात्रतस्त्वत्सुधासदनमर्चनाय ते॥१२.६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yadviśeyamabhisandhimātratastvatsudhāsadanamarcanāya te||12.6||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Let there be<rp> (</rp><rt>astu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>such<rp> (</rp><rt>iyatī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>perfection of yoga<rp> (</rp><rt>yoga-siddhiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>for one whose mind soars from beholding Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-vilokana-samutka-cetasaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>yat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by mere intention alone<rp> (</rp><rt>abhisandhi-mātrataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I might enter<rp> (</rp><rt>viśeyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thy nectarous abode<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-sudhā-sadanam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>to worship<rp> (</rp><rt>arcanāya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||12.6||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12.7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">निर्विकल्पभवदीयदर्शनप्रप्तिफुल्लमनसां महात्मनाम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nirvikalpabhavadīyadarśanapraptiphullamanasāṁ mahātmanām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उल्लसन्ति विमलानि हेलया चेष्टितानि च वचांसि च स्फुटम्॥१२.७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ullasanti vimalāni helayā ceṣṭitāni ca vacāṁsi ca sphuṭam||12.7||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>In great souls<rp> (</rp><rt>mahā-ātmanām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, whose minds have blossomed from<ruby>the attained vision of You without differentiating thought<rp> (</rp><rt>nirvikalpa-bhavadīya-darśana-prāpti-phulla-manasām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>playfully<rp> (</rp><rt>helayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>clearly<rp> (</rp><rt>sphuṭam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>shine<rp> (</rp><rt>ullasanti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>pure<rp> (</rp><rt>vimalāni</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>deeds<rp> (</rp><rt>ceṣṭitāni</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>words<rp> (</rp><rt>vacāṁsi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||12.7||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12.8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवन्भवदीयपादयोर्निवसन्नन्तर एव निर्भयः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavanbhavadīyapādayornivasannantara eva nirbhayaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवभूमिषु तासु तास्वहं प्रभुमर्चेयमनर्गलक्रियः॥१२.८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavabhūmiṣu tāsu tāsvahaṁ prabhumarceyamanargalakriyaḥ||12.8||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Being<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and living<rp> (</rp><rt>nivasan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>within<rp> (</rp><rt>antaraḥ eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thy<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavadīya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>feet<rp> (</rp><rt>pādayoḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>fearless<rp> (</rp><rt>nirbhayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>may I worship<rp> (</rp><rt>arceyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-prabhu" class="pv-gl">prabhum</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>on these and those stages of existence<rp> (</rp><rt>bhava-bhūmiṣu tāsu tāsu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>with actions unhindered<rp> (</rp><rt>anargala-kriyaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||12.8||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12.9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवदङ्घ्रिसरोरुहोदरे परिलीनो गलितपरैषणः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavadaṅghrisaroruhodare parilīno galitaparaiṣaṇaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अतिमात्रमधूपयोगतः परितृप्तो विचरेयमिच्छया॥१२.९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Atimātramadhūpayogataḥ paritṛpto vicareyamicchayā||12.9||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Dissolved<rp> (</rp><rt>parilīnaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the lotus cup of Your feet<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-aṅghri-saroruha-udare</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>with other desires fallen away<rp> (</rp><rt>galita-para-eṣaṇaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>completely satiated<rp> (</rp><rt>paritṛptaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by the honey drunk beyond measure<rp> (</rp><rt>atimātra-madhu-upayogataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>may I wander<rp> (</rp><rt>vicareyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>at will<rp> (</rp><rt>icchayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||12.9||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12.10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यस्य दम्भादिव भवत्पूजासङ्कल्प उत्थितः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yasya dambhādiva bhavatpūjāsaṅkalpa utthitaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तस्याप्यवश्यमुदितं सन्निधानं तवोचितम्॥१२.१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tasyāpyavaśyamuditaṁ sannidhānaṁ tavocitam||12.10||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>In whom<rp> (</rp><rt>yasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the intention to worship You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-pūjā-saṅkalpaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>arose<rp> (</rp><rt>utthitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>as if<rp> (</rp><rt>iva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from hypocrisy<rp> (</rp><rt>dambhāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in him<rp> (</rp><rt>tasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>surely<rp> (</rp><rt>avaśyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>arose<rp> (</rp><rt>uditam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the presence<rp> (</rp><rt>sannidhānam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, worthy of You (tava ucitam)||12.10||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12.11" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भगवन्नितरानपेक्षिणा नितरामेकरसेन चेतसा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhagavannitarānapekṣiṇā nitarāmekarasena cetasā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सुलभं सकलोपशायिनं प्रभुमातृप्ति पिबेयमस्मि किम्॥१२.११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sulabhaṁ sakalopaśāyinaṁ prabhumātṛpti pibeyamasmi kim||12.11||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>with consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-citta" class="pv-gl">cetasā</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>dependent on nothing else<rp> (</rp><rt>itara-anapekṣiṇā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and entirely<rp> (</rp><rt>nitarām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of a single flavor<rp> (</rp><rt>eka-rasena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>what<rp> (</rp><rt>kim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>prevents me<rp> (</rp><rt>asmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from drinking<rp> (</rp><rt>pibeyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to satiety<rp> (</rp><rt>ā-tṛpti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the easily accessible<rp> (</rp><rt>sulabham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-prabhu" class="pv-gl">prabhum</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>reclining in all<rp> (</rp><rt>sakala-upaśāyinam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||12.11||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12.12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वया निराकृतं सर्वं हेयमेतत्तदेव तु।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvayā nirākṛtaṁ sarvaṁ heyametattadeva tu|</span><br />

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>arises<rp> (</rp><rt>udeti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>sā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>thought<rp> (</rp><rt>matiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which<rp> (</rp><rt>yā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>would not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>be<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>consisting of Your will<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-icchā-mayī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>auspicious<rp> (</rp><rt>śubham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>whether<rp> (</rp><rt>atha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>or otherwise<rp> (</rp><rt>itarat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>is accomplished<rp> (</rp><rt>ācaryate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by the Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavatā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in this way<rp> (</rp><rt>evam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Therefore<rp> (</rp><rt>ataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I<rp> (</rp><rt>asmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>consist of You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-ātmakaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and, on earth<rp> (</rp><rt>bhuvi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>as<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>so<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>wandering<rp> (</rp><rt>sañcaran</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>I stand<rp> (</rp><rt>sthitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>unceasingly<rp> (</rp><rt>aniśam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> in the festival of worship of <ruby>Your immaculate feet, which is unobstructed<rp> (</rp><rt>abādhita-tvat-amala-aṅghri-pūjā-utsavaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||12.22||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12.23" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवदीयगभीरभाषितेषु प्रतिभा सम्यगुदेतु मे पुरोऽतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavadīyagabhīrabhāṣiteṣu pratibhā samyagudetu me puro'taḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदनुष्ठितशक्तिरप्यतस्तद्भवदर्चाव्यसनं च निर्विरामम्॥१२.२३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadanuṣṭhitaśaktirapyatastadbhavadarcāvyasanaṁ ca nirvirāmam||12.23||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Let from now on<rp> (</rp><rt>ataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>before me<rp> (</rp><rt>puraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>my<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>properly<rp> (</rp><rt>samyak</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>may arise<rp> (</rp><rt>udetu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>insight<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-pratibha" class="pv-gl">pratibhā</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>into Your profound utterances<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavadīya-gabhīra-bhāṣiteṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and from it<rp> (</rp><rt>ataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>also<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the power to accomplish them<rp> (</rp><rt>tat-anuṣṭhita-śaktiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>unremitting<rp> (</rp><rt>nirvirāmam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>devotion to worshipping You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-arcā-vyasanam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||12.23||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12.24" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">व्यवहारपदेऽपि सर्वदा प्रतिभात्वर्थकलाप एष माम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vyavahārapade'pi sarvadā pratibhātvarthakalāpa eṣa mām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवतोऽवयवो यथा न तु स्वत एवादरणीयतां गतः॥१२.२४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavato'vayavo yathā na tu svata evādaraṇīyatāṁ gataḥ||12.24||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Let it be<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the state of common usage<rp> (</rp><rt>vyavahāra-pade</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>all this<rp> (</rp><rt>eṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>aggregate of objects<rp> (</rp><rt>artha-kalāpaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>appears<rp> (</rp><rt>pratibhātu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to me<rp> (</rp><rt>mām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>as<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Your<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>part<rp> (</rp><rt>avayavaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — and not (na tu) <ruby>as that which has become worthy of reverence<rp> (</rp><rt>ādaraṇīyatām gataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by itself<rp> (</rp><rt>svataḥ eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||12.24||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12.25" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मनसि स्वरसेन यत्र तत्र प्रचरत्यप्यहमस्य गोचरेषु।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Manasi svarasena yatra tatra pracaratyapyahamasya gocareṣu|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रसृतोऽप्यविलोल एव युष्मत्परिचर्याचतुरः सदा भवेयम्॥१२.२५॥</span><br />

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Though<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the mind<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-manas" class="pv-gl">manasi</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with its own taste<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-rasena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and runs<rp> (</rp><rt>pracarati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> here and there (yatra tatra), <ruby>though<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I<rp> (</rp><rt>aham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>am also spread<rp> (</rp><rt>prasṛtaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>through its<rp> (</rp><rt>asya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>domains<rp> (</rp><rt>gocareṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>may I be<rp> (</rp><rt>bhaveyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>unwavering<rp> (</rp><rt>avilolaḥ eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and skillful in serving You<rp> (</rp><rt>yuṣmat-paricaryā-caturaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||12.25||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12.26" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भगवन्भवदिच्छयैव दासस्तव जातोऽस्मि परस्य नात्र शक्तिः।</span><br />

<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कथमेष तथापि वक्त्रबिम्बं तव पश्यामि न जातु चित्रमेतत्॥१२.२६॥</span><br />

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>by Your will alone<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-icchayā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I<rp> (</rp><rt>asmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>have become<rp> (</rp><rt>jātaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Your<rp> (</rp><rt>tava</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>servant<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-dasa" class="pv-gl">dāsaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>power<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-shakti" class="pv-gl">śaktiḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of another<rp> (</rp><rt>parasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>here<rp> (</rp><rt>atra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>there is none<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>How then<rp> (</rp><rt>katham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>yet<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I<rp> (</rp><rt>eṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>never<rp> (</rp><rt>jātu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>do not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>see<rp> (</rp><rt>paśyāmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the orb<rp> (</rp><rt>bimbam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of Your<rp> (</rp><rt>tava</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>face<rp> (</rp><rt>vaktra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>? <ruby>This indeed<rp> (</rp><rt>etat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is a wonder<rp> (</rp><rt>citram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||12.26||</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12.27" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">समुत्सुकास्त्वां प्रति ये भवन्तं प्रत्यर्थरूपादवलोकयन्ति।</span><br />

<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तेषामहो किं तदुपस्थितं स्यात्किं साधनं वा फलितं भवेत्तत्॥१२.२७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Teṣāmaho kiṁ tadupasthitaṁ syātkiṁ sādhanaṁ vā phalitaṁ bhavettat||12.27||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Those<rp> (</rp><rt>ye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who<rp> (</rp><rt>ye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>yearning<rp> (</rp><rt>samutsukāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvām prati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>see<rp> (</rp><rt>avalokayanti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavantam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the form of every thing<rp> (</rp><rt>artha-rūpāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>ah<rp> (</rp><rt>aho</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>what<rp> (</rp><rt>kim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for them<rp> (</rp><rt>teṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>could remain unattained<rp> (</rp><rt>tat upasthitam syāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>? <ruby>What<rp> (</rp><rt>kim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>means<rp> (</rp><rt>sādhanam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>or<rp> (</rp><rt>vā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>what fruit<rp> (</rp><rt>tat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> is not (na bhavet) <ruby>produced<rp> (</rp><rt>phalitam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||12.27||</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12.28" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भावा भावतया सन्तु भवद्भावेन मे भव।</span><br />

<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तथा न किञ्चिदप्यस्तु न किञ्चिद्भवतोऽन्यथा॥१२.२८॥</span><br />
