# Hymn 10 — Avicchedabhaṅgākhyaṁ daśamaṁ stotram

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Hymn 9 — Svātantryavijayākhyaṁ navamaṁ stotram](/en/ksh/sv/ch9/) · [Hymn 11 — Autsukyaviśvasitanāmaikādaśaṁ stotram →](/en/ksh/sv/ch11/)</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न सोढव्यमवश्यं ते जगदेकप्रभोरिदम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na soḍhavyamavaśyaṁ te jagadekaprabhoridam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">माहेश्वराश्च लोकानामितरेषां समाश्च यत्॥१०.१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Māheśvarāśca lokānāmitareṣāṁ samāśca yat||10.1||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>To thee<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the sole Lord of the world<rp> (</rp><rt>jagat-eka-prabhoḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>certainly<rp> (</rp><rt>avaśyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>should be endured<rp> (</rp><rt>soḍhavyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this very<rp> (</rp><rt>idam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>yat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the people of the Great Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>māheśvarāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>are equal<rp> (</rp><rt>samāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to other<rp> (</rp><rt>itareṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>people<rp> (</rp><rt>lokānām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||10.1||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ये सदैवानुरागेण भवत्पादानुगामिनः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ye sadaivānurāgeṇa bhavatpādānugāminaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यत्र तत्र गता भोगांस्ते कांश्चिदुपभुञ्जते॥१०.२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yatra tatra gatā bhogāṁste kāṁścidupabhuñjate||10.2||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Those<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who<rp> (</rp><rt>ye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with love<rp> (</rp><rt>anurāgeṇa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>follow Your footsteps<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-pāda-anugāminaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>wherever they may go<rp> (</rp><rt>yatra tatra gatāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>they enjoy<rp> (</rp><rt>upabhuñjate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>some<rp> (</rp><rt>kāṁścid</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>pleasures<rp> (</rp><rt>bhogān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||10.2||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भर्ता कालान्तको यत्र भवांस्तत्र कुतो रुजः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhartā kālāntako yatra bhavāṁstatra kuto rujaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्र चेतरभोगाशा का लक्ष्मीर्यत्र तावकी॥१०.३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatra cetarabhogāśā kā lakṣmīryatra tāvakī||10.3||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Where<rp> (</rp><rt>yatra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>the lord<rp> (</rp><rt>bhartā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>destroying time itself<rp> (</rp><rt>kāla-antakaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>there<rp> (</rp><rt>tatra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>whence<rp> (</rp><rt>kutaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>could diseases arise<rp> (</rp><rt>rujaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>? <ruby>And<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>what<rp> (</rp><rt>kā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>there<rp> (</rp><rt>tatra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>hope for other pleasures<rp> (</rp><rt>itara-bhoga-āśā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>where<rp> (</rp><rt>yatra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>Your<rp> (</rp><rt>tāvakī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>fortune<rp> (</rp><rt>lakṣmīḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||10.3||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">क्षनमात्रसुखेनापि विभुर्येनासि लभ्यसे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kṣanamātrasukhenāpi vibhuryenāsi labhyase|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदैव सर्वः कालोऽस्य त्वदानन्देन पूर्यते॥१०.४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadaiva sarvaḥ kālo'sya tvadānandena pūryate||10.4||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>All-pervading<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vibhu" class="pv-gl">vibhuḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>it is You<rp> (</rp><rt>asi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>who are to be attained<rp> (</rp><rt>labhyase</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even by the happiness of a single moment<rp> (</rp><rt>kṣaṇa-mātra-sukhena api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>by that very thing<rp> (</rp><rt>yena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>immediately<rp> (</rp><rt>tadā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>all<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the time<rp> (</rp><rt>kālaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of this person<rp> (</rp><rt>asya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is filled<rp> (</rp><rt>pūryate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with Your bliss<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-ānandena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||10.4||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आनन्दरसबिन्दुस्ते चन्द्रमा गलितो भुवि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ānandarasabinduste candramā galito bhuvi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सूर्यस्तथा ते प्रसृतः संहारी तेजसः कणः॥१०.५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sūryastathā te prasṛtaḥ saṁhārī tejasaḥ kaṇaḥ||10.5||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The moon<rp> (</rp><rt>candramāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>is Your<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>drop of the juice of bliss<rp> (</rp><rt>ānanda-rasa-binduḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>fallen<rp> (</rp><rt>galitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to the earth<rp> (</rp><rt>bhuvi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>and the sun<rp> (</rp><rt>sūryaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>likewise<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>a spread-out<rp> (</rp><rt>prasṛtaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>spark<rp> (</rp><rt>kaṇaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of Your<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>heat<rp> (</rp><rt>tejasaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>bringing destruction<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁhārī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||10.5||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बलिं यामस्तृतीयाय नेत्रायास्मै तव प्रभो।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Baliṁ yāmastṛtīyāya netrāyāsmai tava prabho|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अलौकिकस्य कस्यापि माहात्म्यस्यैकलक्ष्मणे॥१०.६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Alaukikasya kasyāpi māhātmyasyaikalakṣmaṇe||10.6||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-prabhu" class="pv-gl">prabho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>we offer<rp> (</rp><rt>yāmaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>a sacrifice<rp> (</rp><rt>balim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to this<rp> (</rp><rt>asmai</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Your<rp> (</rp><rt>tava</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>third<rp> (</rp><rt>tṛtīyāya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>eye<rp> (</rp><rt>netrāya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>the sole mark<rp> (</rp><rt>eka-lakṣmaṇe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> of a certain (kasya api) <ruby>otherworldly<rp> (</rp><rt>alaukikasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>greatness<rp> (</rp><rt>māhātmyasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||10.6||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तेनैव दृष्टोऽसि भवद्दर्शनाद्योऽतिहृष्यति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tenaiva dṛṣṭo'si bhavaddarśanādyo'tihṛṣyati|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कथञ्चिद्यस्य वा हर्षः कोऽपि तेन त्वमीक्षितः॥१०.७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kathañcidyasya vā harṣaḥ ko'pi tena tvamīkṣitaḥ||10.7||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1">Тобою (tena eva) <ruby>увиден<rp> (</rp><rt>dṛṣṭaḥ asi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>тот, кто<rp> (</rp><rt>yaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>от лицезрения Тебя<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-darśanāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>не помнит себя от радости<rp> (</rp><rt>ati-hṛṣyati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>а<rp> (</rp><rt>vā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>у кого<rp> (</rp><rt>yasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>есть<rp> (</rp><rt>kaḥ api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>хоть какая-нибудь<rp> (</rp><rt>kathañcid</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>радость<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-harsa" class="pv-gl">harṣaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>тем<rp> (</rp><rt>tena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Ты<rp> (</rp><rt>tvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>и увиден<rp> (</rp><rt>īkṣitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||10.7||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">येषां प्रसन्नोऽसि विभो यैर्लब्धं हृदयं तव।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yeṣāṁ prasanno'si vibho yairlabdhaṁ hṛdayaṁ tava|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आकृष्य त्वत्पुरात्तैस्तु बाह्यमाभ्यन्तरीकृतम्॥१०.८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ākṛṣya tvatpurāttaistu bāhyamābhyantarīkṛtam||10.8||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>All-pervading<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vibhu" class="pv-gl">vibho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>those<rp> (</rp><rt>yeṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>to whom You are gracious<rp> (</rp><rt>prasannaḥ asi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>they<rp> (</rp><rt>yaiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>by whom is obtained<rp> (</rp><rt>labdham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Your<rp> (</rp><rt>tava</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>heart<rp> (</rp><rt>hṛdayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>they indeed<rp> (</rp><rt>taiḥ tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>having drawn out<rp> (</rp><rt>ākṛṣya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the external from Your city<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-purāt... bāhyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>made it internal<rp> (</rp><rt>ābhyantarī-kṛtam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||10.8||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वदृते निखिलं विश्वं समदृग्यातमीक्ष्यताम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvadṛte nikhilaṁ viśvaṁ samadṛgyātamīkṣyatām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ईश्वरः पुनरेतस्य त्वमेको विषमेक्षणः॥१०.९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Īśvaraḥ punaretasya tvameko viṣamekṣaṇaḥ||10.9||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Without You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-ṛte</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>all<rp> (</rp><rt>nikhilam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the universe<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vishva" class="pv-gl">viśvam</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is seen<rp> (</rp><rt>īkṣyatām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>as having come to an even gaze<rp> (</rp><rt>sama-dṛk-yātam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>but You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>the sole<rp> (</rp><rt>ekaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-ishvara" class="pv-gl">īśvaraḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of it<rp> (</rp><rt>etasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, gazing unevenly <span class="pv-note">— «viṣamekṣaṇa» at once “gazing unevenly” and “three-eyed”: the third eye makes the count odd —</span> <span class="pv-w">(viṣama-īkṣaṇaḥ)</span>||10.9||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आस्तां भवत्प्रभावेण विना सत्तैव नास्ति यत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Āstāṁ bhavatprabhāveṇa vinā sattaiva nāsti yat|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वद्दूषणकथा येषां त्वदृते नोपपद्यते॥१०.१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvaddūṣaṇakathā yeṣāṁ tvadṛte nopapadyate||10.10||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Let us leave aside<rp> (</rp><rt>āstām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>yat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which without Your power<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-prabhāvena vinā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>has no being at all<rp> (</rp><rt>sattā eva na asti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>for those<rp> (</rp><rt>yeṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even speech that censures You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-dūṣaṇa-kathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>without You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-ṛte</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>does not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>come to be<rp> (</rp><rt>upapadyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||10.10||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.11" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बाह्यान्तरान्तरायालीकेवले चेतसि स्थितिः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bāhyāntarāntarāyālīkevale cetasi sthitiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वयि चेत्स्यान्मम विभो किमन्यदुपयुज्यते॥१०.११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvayi cetsyānmama vibho kimanyadupayujyate||10.11||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>All-pervading<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vibhu" class="pv-gl">vibho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>if<rp> (</rp><rt>ced</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>my<rp> (</rp><rt>mama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>abiding<rp> (</rp><rt>sthitiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>will be<rp> (</rp><rt>syāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvayi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>in the consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-citta" class="pv-gl">cetasi</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, remaining <ruby>alone, free from obstacles external and internal<rp> (</rp><rt>bāhya-āntara-antarāya-alīka-kevale</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>what<rp> (</rp><rt>kim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>else<rp> (</rp><rt>anyat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is still needed<rp> (</rp><rt>upayujyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||10.11||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अन्ये भ्रमन्ति भगवन्नात्मन्येवातिदुःस्थिताः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anye bhramanti bhagavannātmanyevātiduḥsthitāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अन्ये भ्रमन्ति भगवन्नात्मन्येवातिसुस्थिताः॥१०.१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anye bhramanti bhagavannātmanyevātisusthitāḥ||10.12||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Some<rp> (</rp><rt>anye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>wander<rp> (</rp><rt>bhramanti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>in the very self<rp> (</rp><rt>ātmani eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>being exceedingly ill-established<rp> (</rp><rt>ati-duḥsthitāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>others<rp> (</rp><rt>anye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>wander<rp> (</rp><rt>bhramanti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>in the very self<rp> (</rp><rt>ātmani eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>being exceedingly well-established<rp> (</rp><rt>ati-susthitāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||10.12||</p>
