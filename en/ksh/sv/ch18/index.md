# Hymn 18 — Āviṣkāranāmāṣṭādaśaṁ stotram

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18.1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जगतोऽन्तरतो भवन्तमाप्त्वा पुनरेतद्भवतोऽन्तराल्लभन्ते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jagato'ntarato bhavantamāptvā punaretadbhavato'ntarāllabhante|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जगदीश तवैव भक्तिभाजो न हि तेषामिह दूरतोऽस्ति किञ्चित्॥१८.१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jagadīśa tavaiva bhaktibhājo na hi teṣāmiha dūrato'sti kiñcit||18.1||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Lord of the world<rp> (</rp><rt>jagat-īśa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>having attained<rp> (</rp><rt>āptvā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavantam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from within<rp> (</rp><rt>antarataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of the world<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-jagat" class="pv-gl">jagataḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>those who share devotion to You<rp> (</rp><rt>tava eva bhakti-bhājaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>again<rp> (</rp><rt>punaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>obtain<rp> (</rp><rt>labhante</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>etat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>world from within You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavataḥ antarāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>For<rp> (</rp><rt>hi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for them<rp> (</rp><rt>teṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>here<rp> (</rp><rt>iha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>there is nothing<rp> (</rp><rt>na asti kiñcit</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>far away<rp> (</rp><rt>dūrataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||18.1||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18.2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">क्वचिदेव भवान्क्वचिद्भवानी सकलार्थक्रमगर्भिणी प्रधाना।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kvacideva bhavānkvacidbhavānī sakalārthakramagarbhiṇī pradhānā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">परमार्थपदे तु नैव देव्या भवतो नापि जतत्त्रयस्य भेदः॥१८.२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Paramārthapade tu naiva devyā bhavato nāpi jatattrayasya bhedaḥ||18.2||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1">Somewhere (kvacid eva) — <ruby>Thou<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>somewhere<rp> (</rp><rt>kvacid</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>Bhavānī<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhavani" class="pv-gl">bhavānī</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the primary<rp> (</rp><rt>pradhānā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>pregnant with the sequence of all things<rp> (</rp><rt>sakala-artha-krama-garbhiṇī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>But<rp> (</rp><rt>tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the state of the highest truth<rp> (</rp><rt>parama-artha-pade</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> there is no (na eva) <ruby>difference<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bheda" class="pv-gl">bhedaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>neither of the Goddess<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-devi" class="pv-gl">devyāḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, nor (na api) <ruby>of Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>nor of the three worlds<rp> (</rp><rt>jagat-trayasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||18.2||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18.3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नो जानते सुभगमप्यवलेपवन्तो लोकाः प्रयत्नसुभगा निखिल हि भावाः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">No jānate subhagamapyavalepavanto lokāḥ prayatnasubhagā nikhila hi bhāvāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चेतः पुनर्यदिदमुद्यतमप्यवैति नैवात्मरूपमिह हा तदहो हतोऽस्मि॥१८.३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Cetaḥ punaryadidamudyatamapyavaiti naivātmarūpamiha hā tadaho hato'smi||18.3||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>People<rp> (</rp><rt>lokāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>full of arrogance<rp> (</rp><rt>avalepavantaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>do not<rp> (</rp><rt>no</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>know<rp> (</rp><rt>jānate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the beautiful<rp> (</rp><rt>subhagam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>for<rp> (</rp><rt>hi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>all<rp> (</rp><rt>nikhilāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>things<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhava" class="pv-gl">bhāvāḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>are beautiful through effort<rp> (</rp><rt>prayatna-subhagāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>And<rp> (</rp><rt>punaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-citta" class="pv-gl">cetaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which<rp> (</rp><rt>yat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>here<rp> (</rp><rt>iha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>idam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> and has arisen (udyatam api), — <ruby>yet it does not<rp> (</rp><rt>naiva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>recognise<rp> (</rp><rt>avaiti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>its own form<rp> (</rp><rt>ātma-rūpam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Alas<rp> (</rp><rt>hā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>! <ruby>Oh<rp> (</rp><rt>aho</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>I am undone<rp> (</rp><rt>hataḥ asmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||18.3||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18.4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवन्मयस्वात्मनिवासलब्धसम्पद्भराभ्यर्चितयुष्मदङ्घ्रिः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavanmayasvātmanivāsalabdhasampadbharābhyarcitayuṣmadaṅghriḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न भोजनाच्छादनमप्यजस्रमपेक्षते यस्तमहं नतोऽस्मि॥१८.४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na bhojanācchādanamapyajasramapekṣate yastamahaṁ nato'smi||18.4||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>I bow<rp> (</rp><rt>nataḥ asmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>before him<rp> (</rp><rt>tam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who<rp> (</rp><rt>yaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, having honored Your feet with the burden of wealth obtained from <ruby>life in one's own self, consisting of You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-maya-sva-ātma-nivāsa-labdha-sampat-bhara-abhyarcita-yuṣmat-aṅghriḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>does not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>need<rp> (</rp><rt>apekṣate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>constantly<rp> (</rp><rt>ajasram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in food and clothing<rp> (</rp><rt>bhojana-ācchādanam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||18.4||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18.5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सदा भवद्देहनिवासस्वस्थोऽप्यन्तः परं दह्यत एष लोकः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sadā bhavaddehanivāsasvastho'pyantaḥ paraṁ dahyata eṣa lokaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तवेच्छया तत्कुरु मे यथात्र त्वदर्चनानन्दमयो भवेयम्॥१८.५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tavecchayā tatkuru me yathātra tvadarcanānandamayo bhaveyam||18.5||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>This<rp> (</rp><rt>eṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>person<rp> (</rp><rt>lokaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>though<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>established by life in Your body<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-deha-nivāsa-svasthaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>inside<rp> (</rp><rt>antaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>only<rp> (</rp><rt>param</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>burns<rp> (</rp><rt>dahyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>By Your will<rp> (</rp><rt>tava icchayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>make<rp> (</rp><rt>kuru</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>so<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>tat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I here<rp> (</rp><rt>atra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>may become<rp> (</rp><rt>bhaveyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>consisting of the bliss of worshipping You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-arcana-ānanda-mayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||18.5||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18.6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वरसोदितयुष्मदङ्घ्रिपद्मद्वयपूजामृतपानसक्तचित्तः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svarasoditayuṣmadaṅghripadmadvayapūjāmṛtapānasaktacittaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सकार्थचयेष्वहं भवेयम्सुखसंस्पर्शनमात्रलोकयात्रः॥१८.६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sakārthacayeṣvahaṁ bhaveyamsukhasaṁsparśanamātralokayātraḥ||18.6||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>May I be<rp> (</rp><rt>bhaveyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, with a mind attached to drinking the nectar of worship of the pair of Your <ruby>lotus-feet, risen by their own taste<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-rasa-udita-yuṣmat-aṅghri-padma-dvaya-pūjā-amṛta-pāna-sakta-cittaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>such that in all heaps of objects<rp> (</rp><rt>sakala-artha-cayeṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the worldly path is but a touch of happiness<rp> (</rp><rt>sukha-saṁsparśana-mātra-loka-yātraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||18.6||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18.7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सकलव्यवहारगोचरे स्फुटमन्तः स्पुरति त्वयि प्रभो।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sakalavyavahāragocare sphuṭamantaḥ spurati tvayi prabho|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उपयान्त्यपयान्ति चानिशं मम वस्तूनि विभान्तु सर्वदा॥१८.७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Upayāntyapayānti cāniśaṁ mama vastūni vibhāntu sarvadā||18.7||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-prabhu" class="pv-gl">prabho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>when You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvayi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>clearly<rp> (</rp><rt>sphuṭam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>shine<rp> (</rp><rt>sphurati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>within<rp> (</rp><rt>antaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>in the domain of all everyday activity<rp> (</rp><rt>sakala-vyavahāra-gocare</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>may my<rp> (</rp><rt>mama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>things<rp> (</rp><rt>vastūni</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which constantly<rp> (</rp><rt>aniśam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>come<rp> (</rp><rt>upayānti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>go<rp> (</rp><rt>apayānti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>shine<rp> (</rp><rt>vibhāntu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||18.7||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18.8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सततमेव तवैव पुरेऽथवाप्यरहितो विचरेयमहं त्वया।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Satatameva tavaiva pure'thavāpyarahito vicareyamahaṁ tvayā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">क्षणलवोऽप्यथ मा स्म भवेत्स मे न विजये ननु यत्र भवन्मयः॥१८.८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kṣaṇalavo'pyatha mā sma bhavetsa me na vijaye nanu yatra bhavanmayaḥ||18.8||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Yes, I wander<rp> (</rp><rt>vicareyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> incessantly (satatam eva) in Thy (tava eva) <ruby>city<rp> (</rp><rt>pure</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>or<rp> (</rp><rt>athavā api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>not separated<rp> (</rp><rt>arahitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tvayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>And<rp> (</rp><rt>atha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>let there not be<rp> (</rp><rt>mā sma bhavet</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>saḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>moment or fraction of a moment<rp> (</rp><rt>kṣaṇa-lavaḥ api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>in which<rp> (</rp><rt>yatra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I, consisting of Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-mayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>would not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> conquer — the second half of the stanza is obscure —<span class="pv-note"> </span><span class="pv-w">(vijaye)</span>||18.8||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18.9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवदङ्गपरिस्रवत्सुशीतामृतपूरैर्भरिते समन्ततोऽपि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavadaṅgaparisravatsuśītāmṛtapūrairbharite samantato'pi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवदर्चनसम्पदेह भक्तास्तव संसारसरोऽन्तरे चरन्ति॥१८.९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavadarcanasampadeha bhaktāstava saṁsārasaro'ntare caranti||18.9||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The devotees<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakta" class="pv-gl">bhaktāḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>move<rp> (</rp><rt>caranti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>here<rp> (</rp><rt>iha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>within the lake of the cycle of existence<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁsāra-saraḥ-antare</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which by the wealth of worshipping You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-arcana-sampadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is filled<rp> (</rp><rt>bharite</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>on all sides<rp> (</rp><rt>samantataḥ api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> with streams of the coolest <ruby>nectar oozing from Your body<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-aṅga-parisravat-su-śīta-amṛta-pūraiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||18.9||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18.10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">महामन्त्रतरुच्छायाशीतले त्वन्महावने।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mahāmantratarucchāyāśītale tvanmahāvane|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">निजात्मनि सदा नाथ वसेयं तव पूजकः॥१८.१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nijātmani sadā nātha vaseyaṁ tava pūjakaḥ||18.10||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>may I live<rp> (</rp><rt>vaseyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in my own self<rp> (</rp><rt>nija-ātmani</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>in Your great forest<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-mahā-vane</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>cool with the shade of the great mantra tree<rp> (</rp><rt>mahā-mantra-taru-chāyā-śītale</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>Your worshipper<rp> (</rp><rt>tava pūjakaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||18.10||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18.11" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रतिवस्तु समस्तजीवतः प्रतिभासि प्रतिभामयो यथा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prativastu samastajīvataḥ pratibhāsi pratibhāmayo yathā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मम नाथ तथा पुरः प्रथां व्रज नेत्रत्रयशूलशोभितः॥१८.११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mama nātha tathā puraḥ prathāṁ vraja netratrayaśūlaśobhitaḥ||18.11||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18.12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अभिमानचरूपहारतो ममताभक्तिभरेण कल्पितात्।</span><br />

<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सकलं जनवृत्तमाचरेयं रसयन्सर्वत एव किञ्चनापि॥१८.१३॥</span><br />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18.14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवदीयमिहास्तु वस्तु तत्त्वं विवरीतुं क इवात्र पात्रमर्थे।</span><br />

<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इदमेव हि नामरूपचेष्टाद्यसमं ते हरते हरोऽसि यस्मात्॥१८.१४॥</span><br />

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Who<rp> (</rp><rt>kaḥ iva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>here<rp> (</rp><rt>atra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is fit<rp> (</rp><rt>pātram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to reveal<rp> (</rp><rt>vivarītum</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the essence<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-tattva" class="pv-gl">tattvam</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of Your<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavadīyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>thing<rp> (</rp><rt>vastu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>? <ruby>For<rp> (</rp><rt>hi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> this (idam eva) — <ruby>name, form, movement, and so on<rp> (</rp><rt>nāma-rūpa-ceṣṭā-ādi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>You<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>carry away<rp> (</rp><rt>harate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>without remainder<rp> (</rp><rt>asamam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>for<rp> (</rp><rt>yasmāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>You<rp> (</rp><rt>asi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>are indeed Hara<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-hara" class="pv-gl">haraḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||18.14||</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18.15" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शान्तये न सुखलिप्सुता मनाग्भक्तिसम्भृतमदेषु तैः प्रभोः।</span><br />

<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मोक्षमार्गणफलापि नार्थना स्मर्यते हृदयहारिणः पुरः॥१८.१५॥</span><br />

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>In those<rp> (</rp><rt>taiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>in whom<rp> (</rp><rt>taiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the intoxication of devotion is gathered<rp> (</rp><rt>bhakti-sambhṛta-madeṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>not a hair's breadth<rp> (</rp><rt>manāk</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>there is<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>thirst for happiness<rp> (</rp><rt>sukha-lipsutā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for the sake of peace<rp> (</rp><rt>śāntaye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>before<rp> (</rp><rt>puraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the Stealer of the heart<rp> (</rp><rt>hṛdaya-hāriṇaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-prabhu" class="pv-gl">prabhoḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is remembered<rp> (</rp><rt>smaryate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even the request<rp> (</rp><rt>arthanā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with the fruit of seeking liberation<rp> (</rp><rt>mokṣa-mārgaṇa-phalā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||18.15||</p>

<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तिभाजनजनस्य साखिला त्वत्सनाथमनसो महोत्सवः॥१८.१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktibhājanajanasya sākhilā tvatsanāthamanaso mahotsavaḥ||18.16||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Wakefulness<rp> (</rp><rt>jāgarat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>another<rp> (</rp><rt>itarat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>state<rp> (</rp><rt>daśā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>or<rp> (</rp><rt>athavā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>any<rp> (</rp><rt>kā api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>other<rp> (</rp><rt>parā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>whatever it may be<rp> (</rp><rt>yā api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for a brief halt<rp> (</rp><rt>manāk avasthiteḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>all<rp> (</rp><rt>sā akhilā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this, for a person devoted<rp> (</rp><rt>bhakti-bhājana-janasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>whose mind has You as its lord<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-sanātha-manasaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>is a great festival<rp> (</rp><rt>mahā-utsavaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||18.16||</p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Nothing<rp> (</rp><rt>kiñcid</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>separate<rp> (</rp><rt>vibhinnam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> was not (na ca) <ruby>created<rp> (</rp><rt>asṛjyata</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>atha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>nothing other than happiness<rp> (</rp><rt>sukha-itarat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>here<rp> (</rp><rt>atra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>was<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>made<rp> (</rp><rt>nirmitam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दलितदुर्जयसंशयवैरिणस्त्वदवलोकनमस्तु निरन्तरम्॥१८.१९॥</span><br />
