<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नथं त्रिभुवननाथं भूतिसितं त्रिनयनं त्रिशूलधरम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nathaṁ tribhuvananāthaṁ bhūtisitaṁ trinayanaṁ triśūladharam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उपवीतीकृतभोगिनमिन्दुकलाशेखरं वन्दे॥२०.१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Upavītīkṛtabhoginamindukalāśekharaṁ vande||20.1||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>I praise<rp> (</rp><rt>vande</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātham</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the Lord of the three worlds<rp> (</rp><rt>tri-bhuvana-nātham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>white from ashes<rp> (</rp><rt>bhūti-sitam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>three-eyed<rp> (</rp><rt>tri-nayanam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>holding a trident<rp> (</rp><rt>triśūla-dharam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who turned the serpent into a sacred thread<rp> (</rp><rt>upavītī-kṛta-bhoginam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>wearing the crescent moon in his crown<rp> (</rp><rt>indu-kalā-śekharam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||20.1||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नौमि निजतनुविनिस्मरदंशुकपरिवेषधवलपरिधानम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Naumi nijatanuvinismaradaṁśukapariveṣadhavalaparidhānam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विलसत्कपालमालाकल्पितनृत्तोत्सवाकल्पम्॥२०.२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vilasatkapālamālākalpitanṛttotsavākalpam||20.2||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>I praise<rp> (</rp><rt>naumi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> Him whose garment <ruby>is white with the radiance streaming from His own body<rp> (</rp><rt>nija-tanu-vinisarat-aṁśuka-pariveṣa-dhavala-paridhānam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, Him whose festive attire for <ruby>the dance is fashioned from a gleaming garland of skulls<rp> (</rp><rt>vilasat-kapāla-mālā-kalpita-nṛtta-utsava-ākalpam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||20.2||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वन्दे तान्दैवतं येषां हरश्चेष्टा हरोचिताः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vande tāndaivataṁ yeṣāṁ haraśceṣṭā harocitāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हरैकप्रवणाः प्राणाः सदा सौभाग्यसद्मनाम्॥२०.३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Haraikapravaṇāḥ prāṇāḥ sadā saubhāgyasadmanām||20.3||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>I praise<rp> (</rp><rt>vande</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>them<rp> (</rp><rt>tān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>deities<rp> (</rp><rt>daivatam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>whose<rp> (</rp><rt>yeṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>deeds<rp> (</rp><rt>ceṣṭāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>Hara<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-hara" class="pv-gl">haraḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and worthy of Hara<rp> (</rp><rt>hara-ucitāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and breaths<rp> (</rp><rt>prāṇāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>inclined to one Hara<rp> (</rp><rt>hara-eka-pravaṇāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>to these abodes of bliss<rp> (</rp><rt>saubhāgya-sadmanām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||20.3||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">क्रीडितं तव महेश्वरतायाः पृष्ठतोऽन्यदिदमेव यथैतत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Krīḍitaṁ tava maheśvaratāyāḥ pṛṣṭhato'nyadidameva yathaitat|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इष्टमात्रघटितेष्ववदानेष्वात्मना परमुपायमुपैमि॥२०.४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Iṣṭamātraghaṭiteṣvavadāneṣvātmanā paramupāyamupaimi||20.4||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>This<rp> (</rp><rt>idam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>play<rp> (</rp><rt>krīḍitam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of Your<rp> (</rp><rt>tava</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>lordship<rp> (</rp><rt>maheśvara-tāyāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>such is this<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā etat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and everything else<rp> (</rp><rt>anyat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>behind it<rp> (</rp><rt>pṛṣṭhataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. In <ruby>deeds that are accomplished by mere desire<rp> (</rp><rt>iṣṭa-mātra-ghaṭiteṣu avadāneṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>I myself<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-atman" class="pv-gl">ātmanā</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>attain<rp> (</rp><rt>upaimi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the highest<rp> (</rp><rt>param</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>means<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-upaya" class="pv-gl">upāyam</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||20.4||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वद्धाम्नि विश्ववन्द्येऽस्मिन्नियति क्रीडने सति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvaddhāmni viśvavandye'sminniyati krīḍane sati|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तव नाथ कियान्भूयान्नानन्दरससम्भवः॥२०.५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tava nātha kiyānbhūyānnānandarasasambhavaḥ||20.5||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>when<rp> (</rp><rt>sati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in this<rp> (</rp><rt>asmin</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thy abode<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-dhāmni</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>honoured by the whole universe<rp> (</rp><rt>viśva-vandye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>there takes place<rp> (</rp><rt>krīḍane</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>such a<rp> (</rp><rt>iyati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>play — how great<rp> (</rp><rt>kiyān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>indeed<rp> (</rp><rt>bhūyān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is Thine<rp> (</rp><rt>tava</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>surge of the juice of bliss<rp> (</rp><rt>ānanda-rasa-sambhavaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>!||20.5||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कथं स सुभगो मा भूद्यो गौर्या वल्लभो हरः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kathaṁ sa subhago mā bhūdyo gauryā vallabho haraḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हरोऽपि मा भूदथ किं गौर्याः परमवल्लभः॥२०.६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Haro'pi mā bhūdatha kiṁ gauryāḥ paramavallabhaḥ||20.6||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>How then<rp> (</rp><rt>katham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>not<rp> (</rp><rt>mā bhūt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>be happy<rp> (</rp><rt>subhagaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>he<rp> (</rp><rt>saḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who<rp> (</rp><rt>yaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>Hara<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-hara" class="pv-gl">haraḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the beloved<rp> (</rp><rt>vallabhaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Gaurī<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-gauri" class="pv-gl">gauryāḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>? <ruby>And<rp> (</rp><rt>atha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>how<rp> (</rp><rt>kim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Hara<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-hara" class="pv-gl">haraḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>not<rp> (</rp><rt>mā bhūt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>be the supreme beloved<rp> (</rp><rt>parama-vallabhaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Gaurī<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-gauri" class="pv-gl">gauryāḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||20.6||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ध्यानामृतमयं यस्य स्वात्ममूलमनश्वरम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dhyānāmṛtamayaṁ yasya svātmamūlamanaśvaram|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">संविल्लतास्तथारूपास्तस्य कस्यापि सत्तरोः॥२०.७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Saṁvillatāstathārūpāstasya kasyāpi sattaroḥ||20.7||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Of that<rp> (</rp><rt>tasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> certain (kasya api) <ruby>good tree<rp> (</rp><rt>sat-taroḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>whose root is one’s own self<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-ātma-mūlam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>imperishable<rp> (</rp><rt>anaśvaram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>consisting of the nectar of contemplation<rp> (</rp><rt>dhyāna-amṛta-mayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>and the vines of consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁvid-latāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>are likewise<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā-rūpāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||20.7||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तिकण्डूसमुल्लासावसरे परमेश्वर।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktikaṇḍūsamullāsāvasare parameśvara|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">महानिकषपाषाणस्थूणा पूजैव जायते॥२०.८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mahānikaṣapāṣāṇasthūṇā pūjaiva jāyate||20.8||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The Supreme Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-parameshvara" class="pv-gl">parama-īśvara</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>at the hour when the itch of devotion playfully arises<rp> (</rp><rt>bhakti-kaṇḍū-samullāsa-avasare</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>worship itself<rp> (</rp><rt>pūjā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is born<rp> (</rp><rt>jāyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>as a pillar of the great touchstone<rp> (</rp><rt>mahā-nikaṣa-pāṣāṇa-sthūṇā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||20.8||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सदा स्र्ष्टिविनोदाय सदा स्थितिसुखासिने।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sadā srṣṭivinodāya sadā sthitisukhāsine|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सदा त्रिभुवनाहारतृप्ताय स्वामिने नमः॥२०.९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sadā tribhuvanāhāratṛptāya svāmine namaḥ||20.9||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Homage<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-namas" class="pv-gl">namaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to the Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-svamin" class="pv-gl">svāmine</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who eternally<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>delights in creation<rp> (</rp><rt>sṛṣṭi-vinodāya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>eternally<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>sits in the bliss of abiding<rp> (</rp><rt>sthiti-sukha-āsine</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>eternally<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>satisfied with the food of the three worlds<rp> (</rp><rt>tri-bhuvana-āhāra-tṛptāya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||20.9||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न क्वापि गत्वा हित्वापि न किञ्चिदिदमेव ये।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na kvāpi gatvā hitvāpi na kiñcididameva ye|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भव्यं त्वद्धाम पश्यन्ति भव्यास्तेभ्यो नमो नमः॥२०.१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavyaṁ tvaddhāma paśyanti bhavyāstebhyo namo namaḥ||20.10||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Homage<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-namas" class="pv-gl">namaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, homage<ruby> (<rp></rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-namas" class="pv-gl">namaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to those<ruby> (<rp></rp><rt>tebhyaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> benevolent<ruby> (<rp></rp><rt>bhavyāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, who<ruby> (<rp></rp><rt>ye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, nowhere<ruby> (<rp></rp><rt>na kvāpi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> not going<ruby> (<rp></rp><rt>gatvā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> and<ruby> (<rp></rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> nothing<ruby> (<rp></rp><rt>na kiñcid</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> not leaving<ruby> (<rp></rp><rt>hitvā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, see<ruby> (<rp></rp><rt>paśyanti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> this very<ruby> (<rp></rp><rt>idameva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> benevolent<ruby> (<rp></rp><rt>bhavyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> Thy abode<ruby> (<rp></rp><rt>tvat-dhāma</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||20.10||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.11" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तिलक्ष्मीसमृद्धानां किमन्यदुपयाचितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktilakṣmīsamṛddhānāṁ kimanyadupayācitam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एतया वा दरिद्रणां किमन्यदुपयाचितम्॥२०.११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Etayā vā daridraṇāṁ kimanyadupayācitam||20.11||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>What else<rp> (</rp><rt>kimanyat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>could be begged<rp> (</rp><rt>upayācitam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by those who are rich in the Śrī of devotion<rp> (</rp><rt>bhakti-lakṣmī-samṛddhānām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>? <ruby>And<rp> (</rp><rt>vā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>what else<rp> (</rp><rt>kimanyat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>could be begged<rp> (</rp><rt>upayācitam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by those who are poor in it<rp> (</rp><rt>etayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>destitute<rp> (</rp><rt>daridrāṇām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||20.11||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दुःखान्यपि सुखायन्ते विषमप्यमृतायते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Duḥkhānyapi sukhāyante viṣamapyamṛtāyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मोक्षायते च संसारो यत्र मार्गः स शङ्करः॥२०.१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mokṣāyate ca saṁsāro yatra mārgaḥ sa śaṅkaraḥ||20.12||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Where<rp> (</rp><rt>yatra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and sorrows<rp> (</rp><rt>duḥkhāni api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>become happiness<rp> (</rp><rt>sukhāyante</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and poison<rp> (</rp><rt>viṣam api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>nectar<rp> (</rp><rt>amṛtāyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the cycle of existence<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-samsara" class="pv-gl">saṁsāraḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>liberation<rp> (</rp><rt>mokṣāyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>that one<rp> (</rp><rt>saḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>path<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-marga" class="pv-gl">mārgaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and is Śaṅkara<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-shankara" class="pv-gl">śaṅkaraḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||20.12||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मूले मध्येऽवसाने च नास्ति दुःखं भवज्जुषां।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mūle madhye'vasāne ca nāsti duḥkhaṁ bhavajjuṣāṁ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तथापि वयमीशान सीदामः कथमुच्यताम्॥२०.१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tathāpi vayamīśāna sīdāmaḥ kathamucyatām||20.13||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>For those devoted to You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-juṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> there is (na asti) <ruby>no sorrow<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-duhkha" class="pv-gl">duḥkham</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>neither at the beginning<rp> (</rp><rt>mūle</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>nor<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the middle<rp> (</rp><rt>madhye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>nor at the end<rp> (</rp><rt>avasāne</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>And yet<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-ishvara" class="pv-gl">īśāna</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>we<rp> (</rp><rt>vayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>languish<rp> (</rp><rt>sīdāmaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Let it be said<rp> (</rp><rt>ucyatām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>how this<rp> (</rp><rt>katham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||20.13||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ज्ञानयोगादिनान्येषामप्यपेक्षितुमर्हति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jñānayogādinānyeṣāmapyapekṣitumarhati|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रकाशः स्वैरिणामिव भवान्भक्तिमतां प्रभो॥२०.१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prakāśaḥ svairiṇāmiva bhavānbhaktimatāṁ prabho||20.14||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-prabhu" class="pv-gl">prabho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Thou<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the Light<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-prakasha" class="pv-gl">prakāśaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of the devoted<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakta" class="pv-gl">bhakti-matām</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>needest<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>not<rp> (</rp><rt>apekṣitum arhati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>another<rp> (</rp><rt>anyeṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>in knowledge, yoga, and the like<rp> (</rp><rt>jñāna-yoga-ādinā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>as<rp> (</rp><rt>iva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>wanton women<rp> (</rp><rt>svairiṇām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||20.14||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.15" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तानां नार्तयो नाप्यस्त्याध्यानं स्वात्मनस्तव।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktānāṁ nārtayo nāpyastyādhyānaṁ svātmanastava|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तथाप्यस्ति शिवेत्येतत्किमप्येषां बहिर्मुखे॥२०.१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tathāpyasti śivetyetatkimapyeṣāṁ bahirmukhe||20.15||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>For devotees<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-bhakta" class="pv-gl">bhaktānām</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>there is no<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>affliction<rp> (</rp><rt>ārtayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, nor (na api) <ruby>contemplation<rp> (</rp><rt>ādhyānam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of their own self<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-ātmanaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>of Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tava</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>And yet<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>there is<rp> (</rp><rt>asti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in them<rp> (</rp><rt>eṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>something<rp> (</rp><rt>kimapi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>turned outward<rp> (</rp><rt>bahiḥ-mukhe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>this very thing<rp> (</rp><rt>etat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — “Śiva!” <span class="pv-w">(śiva iti)</span>||20.15||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.16" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वाभासावभासो यो विमर्शवलितोऽखिलम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvābhāsāvabhāso yo vimarśavalito'khilam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अहमेतदिति स्तौमि तां क्रियाशक्तिमीश ते॥२०.१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ahametaditi staumi tāṁ kriyāśaktimīśa te||20.16||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-ishvara" class="pv-gl">īśa</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>I praise<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-stuti" class="pv-gl">staumi</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>tām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Your<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Power of Action<rp> (</rp><rt>kriyā-śaktim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which<rp> (</rp><rt>yaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is the light of all phenomena<rp> (</rp><rt>sarva-ābhāsa-avabhāsaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>enveloped by the echo<rp> (</rp><rt>vimarśa-valitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>“All<rp> (</rp><rt>akhilam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>etat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>I<rp> (</rp><rt>aham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>”||20.16||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.17" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वर्तन्ते जन्तवोऽशेषा अपि ब्रह्मेन्द्रविष्णवः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vartante jantavo'śeṣā api brahmendraviṣṇavaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ग्रसमानास्ततो वन्दे देव विश्वं भवन्मयम्॥२०.१७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Grasamānāstato vande deva viśvaṁ bhavanmayam||20.17||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O God<rp> (</rp><rt>deva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>all<rp> (</rp><rt>aśeṣāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>creatures<rp> (</rp><rt>jantavaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Brahma, Indra and Viṣṇu<rp> (</rp><rt>brahma-indra-viṣṇavaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>live<rp> (</rp><rt>vartante</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>devouring<rp> (</rp><rt>grasamānāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Therefore<rp> (</rp><rt>tataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>I praise<rp> (</rp><rt>vande</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the universe<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vishva" class="pv-gl">viśvam</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>consisting of You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-mayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||20.17||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.18" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सतो विनाशसम्बन्धान्मत्परं निखिलं मृषा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sato vināśasambandhānmatparaṁ nikhilaṁ mṛṣā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एवमेवोद्यते नाथ त्वया संहारलीलया॥२०.१८॥</span><br />

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Therefore<rp> (</rp><rt>yat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that the existent<rp> (</rp><rt>sataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is bound to destruction<rp> (</rp><rt>vināśa-sambandhāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>everything<rp> (</rp><rt>nikhilam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>except me<rp> (</rp><rt>mat-param</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is false<rp> (</rp><rt>mṛṣā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Just so<rp> (</rp><rt>evameva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Thou<rp> (</rp><rt>tvayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>dost proclaim<rp> (</rp><rt>udyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>through the play of withdrawal<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁhāra-līlayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||20.18||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.19" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ध्यातमात्मुपतिष्ठत एव त्वद्वपुर्वरद भक्तिधनानाम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dhyātamātmupatiṣṭhata eva tvadvapurvarada bhaktidhanānām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अप्यचिन्त्यमखिलाद्भुतचिन्ताकर्तृतां प्रति च ते विजयन्ते॥२०.१९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Apyacintyamakhilādbhutacintākartṛtāṁ prati ca te vijayante||20.19||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Giver of gifts<rp> (</rp><rt>varada</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Your<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>body<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vapus" class="pv-gl">vapuḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>appears<rp> (</rp><rt>upatiṣṭhate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to those whose wealth is devotion<rp> (</rp><rt>bhakti-dhanānām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>from mere contemplation<rp> (</rp><rt>dhyāta-mātram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>And<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>inconceivable<rp> (</rp><rt>acintyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Your<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>deeds triumph<rp> (</rp><rt>vijayante</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — in regard to <ruby>being the maker of every wondrous thought<rp> (</rp><rt>akhila-adbhuta-cintā-kartṛtām prati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||20.19||</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.20" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तावकभक्तिरसासवसेकादिव सुखितमर्ममण्डलस्फुरितैः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tāvakabhaktirasāsavasekādiva sukhitamarmamaṇḍalasphuritaiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नृत्यति वीरजनो निशि वेतालकुलैः कृतोत्साहः॥२०.२०॥</span><br />

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>As if<rp> (</rp><rt>iva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from the sprinkling of the intoxicating juice of devotion to Thee<rp> (</rp><rt>tāvaka-bhakti-rasa-āsava-sekāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the host of heroes<rp> (</rp><rt>vīra-janaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>whose happy knots of the body have quivered<rp> (</rp><rt>sukhita-marma-maṇḍala-sphuritaiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>dances<rp> (</rp><rt>nṛtyati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by night<rp> (</rp><rt>niśi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>inspired<rp> (</rp><rt>kṛta-utsāhaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by the hosts of vetālas<rp> (</rp><rt>vetāla-kulaiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||20.20||</p>

<span class="pv-iast">Tenāparyantamimaṁ kālaṁ dṛḍhamakhilameva bhaviṣīṣṭa||20.21||</span></p>
