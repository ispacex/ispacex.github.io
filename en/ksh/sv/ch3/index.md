<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सदसत्त्वेन भावानां युक्ता या द्वितयी गतिः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sadasattvena bhāvānāṁ yuktā yā dvitayī gatiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तामुल्लङ्घ्य तृतीयस्मै नमश्चित्राय शम्भवे॥३.१॥</span><br />

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आसुरर्षिजनादस्मिन्नस्वतन्त्रे जगत्त्रये।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Āsurarṣijanādasminnasvatantre jagattraye|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वतन्त्रास्ते स्वतन्त्रस्य ये तवैवानुजीविनः॥३.२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svatantrāste svatantrasya ye tavaivānujīvinaḥ||3.2||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>In this<rp> (</rp><rt>asmin</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>triple world<rp> (</rp><rt>jagat-traye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>unfree<rp> (</rp><rt>asvatantre</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>up to the asuras and sages<rp> (</rp><rt>ā-sura-ṛṣi-janāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>free<rp> (</rp><rt>svatantrāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>are those<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who<rp> (</rp><rt>ye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>live by Thee alone<rp> (</rp><rt>tava eva anujīvinaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>of the free<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-svatantrya" class="pv-gl">svatantrasya</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||3.2||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अशेषविश्वखचितभवद्वपुरनुस्मृतिः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aśeṣaviśvakhacitabhavadvapuranusmṛtiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">येषां भवरुजामेकं भेषजं ते सुखासिनः॥३.३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yeṣāṁ bhavarujāmekaṁ bheṣajaṁ te sukhāsinaḥ||3.3||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Те<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>блаженствуют<rp> (</rp><rt>sukha-āsinaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>у кого<rp> (</rp><rt>yeṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> памятование <ruby>о Твоём теле, усыпанном всею вселенной<rp> (</rp><rt>aśeṣa-viśva-khacita-bhavat-vapuḥ-anusmṛtiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>единственное<rp> (</rp><rt>ekam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>лекарство<rp> (</rp><rt>bheṣajam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>от болезни бытия<rp> (</rp><rt>bhava-rujām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||3.3||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सितातपत्रं यस्येन्दुः स्वप्रभापरिपूरितः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sitātapatraṁ yasyenduḥ svaprabhāparipūritaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चामरं स्वर्धुनीस्रोतः स एकः परमेश्वरः॥३.४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Cāmaraṁ svardhunīsrotaḥ sa ekaḥ parameśvaraḥ||3.4||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>He<rp> (</rp><rt>saḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>alone<rp> (</rp><rt>ekaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is the Supreme Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-parameshvara" class="pv-gl">parama-īśvaraḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>whose<rp> (</rp><rt>yasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>white umbrella<rp> (</rp><rt>sita-ātapatram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>the moon<rp> (</rp><rt>induḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>filled with His own light<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-prabhā-paripūritaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and the fan<rp> (</rp><rt>cāmaram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>the stream of the heavenly river<rp> (</rp><rt>svardhunī-srotaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||3.4||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रकाशां शीतलामेकां शुद्धां शशिकलामिव।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prakāśāṁ śītalāmekāṁ śuddhāṁ śaśikalāmiva|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दृशं वितर मे नाथ कामप्यमृतवाहिनीम्॥३.५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dṛśaṁ vitara me nātha kāmapyamṛtavāhinīm||3.5||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>grant<rp> (</rp><rt>vitara</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>me<rp> (</rp><rt>me</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>some<rp> (</rp><rt>kām api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>vision<rp> (</rp><rt>dṛśam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>bearing nectar<rp> (</rp><rt>amṛta-vāhinīm</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>luminous<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-prakasha" class="pv-gl">prakāśām</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>cool<rp> (</rp><rt>śītalām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>single<rp> (</rp><rt>ekām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>pure<rp> (</rp><rt>śuddhām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>like a crescent moon<rp> (</rp><rt>śaśi-kalām iva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||3.5||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वच्चिदानन्दजलधेश्च्युताः संवित्तिविप्रुषः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvaccidānandajaladheścyutāḥ saṁvittivipruṣaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इमाः कथं मे भगवन्नामृतास्वादसुन्दराः॥३.६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Imāḥ kathaṁ me bhagavannāmṛtāsvādasundarāḥ||3.6||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>how indeed<rp> (</rp><rt>katham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>these<rp> (</rp><rt>imāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>sprays of consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁvitti-vipruṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>fallen off<rp> (</rp><rt>cyutāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from Your ocean of Consciousness-Bliss<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-cit-ānanda-jaladheḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>are not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>beautiful to me<rp> (</rp><rt>me... sundarāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the taste of nectar<rp> (</rp><rt>amṛta-āsvāda</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||3.6||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वयि रागरसे नाथ न मग्नं हृदयं प्रभो।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvayi rāgarase nātha na magnaṁ hṛdayaṁ prabho|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">येषामहृदया एव तेऽवज्ञस्पदमीदृशाः॥३.७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yeṣāmahṛdayā eva te'vajñaspadamīdṛśāḥ||3.7||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-natha" class="pv-gl">nātha</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the Master<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-prabhu" class="pv-gl">prabho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>those whose<rp> (</rp><rt>yeṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>heart<rp> (</rp><rt>hṛdayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>immersed<rp> (</rp><rt>magnam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvayi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>in the juice of love<rp> (</rp><rt>rāga-rase</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>those<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>indeed<rp> (</rp><rt>eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>are heartless<rp> (</rp><rt>ahṛdayāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and such<rp> (</rp><rt>īdṛśāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>are worthy of contempt<rp> (</rp><rt>avajñā-aspadam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||3.7||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रभुणा भवता यस्य जातं हृदयमेलनम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prabhuṇā bhavatā yasya jātaṁ hṛdayamelanam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्राभवीणां विभूतीनां परमेकः स भाजनम्॥३.८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prābhavīṇāṁ vibhūtīnāṁ paramekaḥ sa bhājanam||3.8||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>У кого<rp> (</rp><rt>yasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>случилась<rp> (</rp><rt>jātam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>встреча сердца<rp> (</rp><rt>hṛdaya-melanam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>с Тобою<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavatā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Господом<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-prabhu" class="pv-gl">prabhuṇā</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>тот<rp> (</rp><rt>saḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>один<rp> (</rp><rt>ekaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>и есть высший<rp> (</rp><rt>param</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>сосуд<rp> (</rp><rt>bhājanam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>владычных совершенств<rp> (</rp><rt>prābhavīṇām vibhūtīnām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||3.8||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हर्षाणामथ शोकानां सर्वेषां प्लावकः समम्।</span><br />

<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवद्ध्यानामृतापूरो निम्नाणिम्नभुवामिव॥३.९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavaddhyānāmṛtāpūro nimnāṇimnabhuvāmiva||3.9||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>The flood of nectar from contemplating You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-dhyāna-amṛta-āpūraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>equally<rp> (</rp><rt>samam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>floods<rp> (</rp><rt>plāvakaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>all<rp> (</rp><rt>sarveṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>joys<rp> (</rp><rt>harṣāṇām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>atha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>sorrows<rp> (</rp><rt>śokānām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>like<rp> (</rp><rt>iva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>lowlands and higher places<rp> (</rp><rt>nimna-animna-bhuvām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||3.9||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">केव न स्याद्दृशा तेषां सुखसम्भारनिर्भरा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Keva na syāddṛśā teṣāṁ sukhasambhāranirbharā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">येषामात्माधिकेनेश न क्वापि विरहस्त्वया॥३.१०॥</span><br />

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-ishvara" class="pv-gl">īśa</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>what indeed<rp> (</rp><rt>kā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>vision<rp> (</rp><rt>dṛśā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>will be<rp> (</rp><rt>syāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for those<rp> (</rp><rt>teṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>full with a breast of happiness<rp> (</rp><rt>sukha-sambhāra-nirbharā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>for whom<rp> (</rp><rt>yeṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>nowhere<rp> (</rp><rt>na kvāpi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is there separation<rp> (</rp><rt>virahaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who is dearer than one's own self<rp> (</rp><rt>ātma-adhikena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>?||3.10||</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.11" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">गर्जामि बत नृत्यामि पूर्णा मम मनोरथाः।</span><br />

<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वामी ममैष घटितो यत्त्वमत्यन्तरोचनः॥३.११॥</span><br />

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>I roar<rp> (</rp><rt>garjāmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>ah!<rp> (</rp><rt>bata</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>I dance<rp> (</rp><rt>nṛtyāmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>full<rp> (</rp><rt>pūrṇāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>my<rp> (</rp><rt>mama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>desires<rp> (</rp><rt>manaḥ-rathāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>for<rp> (</rp><rt>yat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thou<rp> (</rp><rt>tvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>shining immeasurably<rp> (</rp><rt>atyanta-rocanaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>hast become<rp> (</rp><rt>ghaṭitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this very<rp> (</rp><rt>eṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>my<rp> (</rp><rt>mama</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Lord<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-svamin" class="pv-gl">svāmī</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||3.11||</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नान्यद्वेद्यं क्रिया यत्र नान्यो योगो विदा च यत्।</span><br />

<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ज्ञानं स्यात्किं तु विश्वैकपूर्णा चित्त्वं विजृम्भते॥३.१२॥</span><br />

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Where<rp> (</rp><rt>yatra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>action<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-kriya" class="pv-gl">kriyā</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>another knowable<rp> (</rp><rt>na anyat vedyaṁ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>union<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-yoga" class="pv-gl">yogaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>not<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>another<rp> (</rp><rt>anyaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>than the knower himself<rp> (</rp><rt>vidā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>what<rp> (</rp><rt>kim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>there<rp> (</rp><rt>yat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>would be<rp> (</rp><rt>syāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>knowledge<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-jnana" class="pv-gl">jñānam</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>? <ruby>But<rp> (</rp><rt>tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>cit-tvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>full of the universe as one<rp> (</rp><rt>viśva-eka-pūrṇā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>unfolds<rp> (</rp><rt>vijṛmbhate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <span class="pv-note">— the stanza is obscure: “kiṁ tu” is read both as “but” and as “what then” —</span>||3.12||</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दुर्जयानामनन्तानां दुःखानां सहसैव ते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Durjayānāmanantānāṁ duḥkhānāṁ sahasaiva te|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हस्तात्पलायिता येषां वाचि शश्वच्छिवध्वनिः॥३.१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Hastātpalāyitā yeṣāṁ vāci śaśvacchivadhvaniḥ||3.13||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>From the hand<rp> (</rp><rt>hastāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of countless<rp> (</rp><rt>anantānām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>sufferings<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-duhkha" class="pv-gl">duḥkhānām</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which cannot be overcome<rp> (</rp><rt>durjayānām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>immediately<rp> (</rp><rt>sahasā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>fled<rp> (</rp><rt>palāyitāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>those<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>in whose<rp> (</rp><rt>yeṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>speech<rp> (</rp><rt>vāci</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>constantly<rp> (</rp><rt>śaśvat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>sounds Śiva<rp> (</rp><rt>śiva-dhvaniḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||3.13||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उत्तमः पुरुषोऽन्योऽस्ति युष्मच्छेषविशेषितः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Uttamaḥ puruṣo'nyo'sti yuṣmaccheṣaviśeṣitaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वं महापुरुषस्त्वेको निःशेषपुरुषाश्रयः॥३.१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvaṁ mahāpuruṣastveko niḥśeṣapuruṣāśrayaḥ||3.14||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Есть<rp> (</rp><rt>asti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>и другой<rp> (</rp><rt>anyaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>высочайший<rp> (</rp><rt>uttamaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>пуруша<rp> (</rp><rt>puruṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>отличённый тем, что он — остаток от Тебя<rp> (</rp><rt>yuṣmat-śeṣa-viśeṣitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Ты же<rp> (</rp><rt>tvaṁ tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>Великий Пуруша<rp> (</rp><rt>mahā-puruṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>один<rp> (</rp><rt>ekaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>опора всех пуруш без остатка<rp> (</rp><rt>niḥśeṣa-puruṣa-āśrayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||3.14||</p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.15" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जयन्ति ते जगद्वन्द्या दासास्ते जगतां विभो।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jayanti te jagadvandyā dāsāste jagatāṁ vibho|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">संसारार्णव एवैष येषां क्रीडामहासरः॥३.१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Saṁsārārṇava evaiṣa yeṣāṁ krīḍāmahāsaraḥ||3.15||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>O All-pervading<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-vibhu" class="pv-gl">vibho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>may victory be theirs<rp> (</rp><rt>jayanti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>those<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thy servants<rp> (</rp><rt>dāsāḥ te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>to whom the world bows<rp> (</rp><rt>jagat-vandyāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>those for whom<rp> (</rp><rt>yeṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>indeed<rp> (</rp><rt>eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>eṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>ocean of saṁsāra<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁsāra-arṇavaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is a great pond for play<rp> (</rp><rt>krīḍā-mahā-saraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||3.15||</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.16" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आसतां तावदन्यानि दैन्यानीह भवज्जुषाम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Āsatāṁ tāvadanyāni dainyānīha bhavajjuṣām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वमेव प्रकटीभूया इत्यनेनैव लज्ज्यते॥३.१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvameva prakaṭībhūyā ityanenaiva lajjyate||3.16||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Let the<rp> (</rp><rt>āsatām tāvat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>other<rp> (</rp><rt>anyāni</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>humiliations<rp> (</rp><rt>dainyāni</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of those devoted to You<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavat-juṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>remain here<rp> (</rp><rt>iha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>but already from this alone<rp> (</rp><rt>anenaiva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>one is ashamed<rp> (</rp><rt>lajjyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>«Appear then<rp> (</rp><rt>prakaṭī-bhūyāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Thou Thyself<rp> (</rp><rt>tvameva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»||3.16||</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.17" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मत्परं नास्ति तत्रापि जापकोऽस्मि तदैक्यतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Matparaṁ nāsti tatrāpi jāpako'smi tadaikyataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्त्वेन जप इत्यक्षमालया दिशसि क्वचित्॥३.१७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tattvena japa ityakṣamālayā diśasi kvacit||3.17||</span></p>

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>“There is<rp> (</rp><rt>nāsti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>nothing<rp> (</rp><rt>tatrāpi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>higher than me<rp> (</rp><rt>mat-param</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>I am<rp> (</rp><rt>asmi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the whisperer<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-japa" class="pv-gl">jāpakaḥ</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>from unity with That<rp> (</rp><rt>tad-aikyataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Whisper<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-japa" class="pv-gl">japa</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>according to truth<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-tattva" class="pv-gl">tattvena</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>” — <ruby>thus<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>You sometimes<rp> (</rp><rt>kvacit</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>point<rp> (</rp><rt>diśasi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with a rosary<rp> (</rp><rt>akṣa-mālayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||3.17||</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.18" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सतोऽवश्यं परमसत्सच्च तस्मात्परं प्रभो।</span><br />

<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वं चासतः सतश्चान्यस्तेनासि सदसन्मयः॥३.१८॥</span><br />

<p class="pv-tr" markdown="1"><ruby>Выше<rp> (</rp><rt>param</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>сущего<rp> (</rp><rt>sataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>непременно<rp> (</rp><rt>avaśyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>не-сущее<rp> (</rp><rt>asat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>и<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>сущее<rp> (</rp><rt>sat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>выше<rp> (</rp><rt>param</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>него<rp> (</rp><rt>tasmāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Господь<rp> (</rp><rt><a href="/en/ksh/sv/glossary/#t-prabhu" class="pv-gl">prabho</a></rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Ты же<rp> (</rp><rt>tvaṁ ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>иной<rp> (</rp><rt>anyaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>чем не-сущее<rp> (</rp><rt>asataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>и<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>чем сущее<rp> (</rp><rt>sataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, — <ruby>тем самым<rp> (</rp><rt>tena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Ты<rp> (</rp><rt>asi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>состоишь из сущего и не-сущего<rp> (</rp><rt>sat-asat-mayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||3.18||</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.19" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सहस्रसूर्यकिरणाधिकशुद्धप्रकाशवान्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sahasrasūryakiraṇādhikaśuddhaprakāśavān|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अपि त्वं सर्वभुवनव्यापकोऽपि न दृश्यसे॥३.१९॥</span><br />
