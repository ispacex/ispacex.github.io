# Glossary of terms

*This dictionary is not found in the treatise. It has been compiled from the very Sanskrit annotations
that appear in parentheses next to each Russian word: annotations in twenty-two chapters
**8,525**, and at the top by frequency — function words, `ca`, `tatra`, `tu`. Here
what remains is that which the reader stumbles over.*

> **Whose work this is.** The Russian translation of the *Tantrasāra* is by **Gabriel
> Pradīpaka**; it is reproduced here unchanged, and not a line of it did we write.
> The dictionary entries are selected from his notes, but **the interpretations are written for this
> site** and are not presented as his words. For the precise meaning, go where the term
> is discussed: the last column leads directly to those paragraphs.

*The glossary is shared with that of the "Tantrāloka"](/en/ksh/ta/glossary/), and this is not laziness:
"Tantrasāra" is the same "Tantrāloka," condensed by Abhinavagupta himself into a single book.
To explain `tattva` on one page of the site in one set of words, and on the neighboring page
in another, would mean contradicting oneself for no reason. The thirty articles here
are nonetheless original—almost all of them ritualistic: of the "Tantrāloka," only the
doctrinal half exists in Russian, whereas the "Tantrasāra" is translated in full, including the ritual from
ablution to the Kaulika yajña.*

*The reverse path also exists: a Sanskrit word in the translation's interlinear gloss leads
here by itself.*

*The ♪ button pronounces the term. The sound is synthesized in advance from the Devanagari spelling using the voice **Lekha** (hi_IN) — this is not a recording of a reader and not a reference for pronunciation: be careful with aspirated, retroflex, and long vowels.*

*The voice reads according to the rules of Hindi, and Hindi swallows the final short "a":
`शिव` he pronounced "śiv". Therefore, to a word ending in a consonant, a
visarga is appended — and the voice pronounces the nominative case, `śivaḥ`, by which the word is
named aloud. The only price for this is: at the end a slight aspiration is heard,
which is not present in the spelling above the button.*
