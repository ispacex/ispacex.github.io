## Строфы 1–10 {#s1}

<p class="pv-copy nosearch pv-anchor" id="s1-sa"><button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-sa">Копировать санскрит</button> <button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-iast">Копировать транслитерацию</button></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथ विंशोऽधिकारः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Atha Viṁśo'dhikāraḥ|</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथ पिण्डादिभेदेन शाक्तं विज्ञानमुच्यते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Atha piṇḍādibhedena śāktaṁ vijñānamucyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">योगिनां योगसिध्यर्थं सङ्क्षेपान्न तु विस्तरात्॥१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yogināṁ yogasidhyarthaṁ saṅkṣepānna tu vistarāt||1||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पिण्डं शरीरमित्युक्तं तद्वछक्तिशिवात्मनोः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Piṇḍaṁ śarīramityuktaṁ tadvachaktiśivātmanoḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ब्रह्मानन्दो बलं तेजो वीर्यमोजश्च कीर्त्यते॥२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Brahmānando balaṁ tejo vīryamojaśca kīrtyate||2||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अज्ञानेन निरुद्धं तदनाद्येव सदात्मनः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ajñānena niruddhaṁ tadanādyeva sadātmanaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदाविभूतये सर्वमनिरुद्धं प्रवर्तते॥३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadāvibhūtaye sarvamaniruddhaṁ pravartate||3||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तेनाविर्भाव्यमानं तत्पूर्वावस्थां परित्यजत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tenāvirbhāvyamānaṁ tatpūrvāvasthāṁ parityajat|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">याः संवित्तीरवाप्नोति ता अधस्तात्प्रकीर्तिताः॥४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yāḥ saṁvittīravāpnoti tā adhastātprakīrtitāḥ||4||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदेव पदमिच्छन्ति सर्वार्थावगतिर्यतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadeva padamicchanti sarvārthāvagatiryataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तस्मात्सञ्जायते नित्यं नित्यमेव शिवात्मनोः॥५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tasmātsañjāyate nityaṁ nityameva śivātmanoḥ||5||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदेव रूपमित्युक्तमात्मनश्च विनश्वरम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadeva rūpamityuktamātmanaśca vinaśvaram|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">रूपातीतं तदेवाहुर्यतोक्षाविषयं परम्॥६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Rūpātītaṁ tadevāhuryatokṣāviṣayaṁ param||6||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भावनां तस्य कुर्वीत नमस्कृत्य गुरुं बुधः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhāvanāṁ tasya kurvīta namaskṛtya guruṁ budhaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तावदालोचयेद्वस्तु यावत्पदमनामयम्॥७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tāvadālocayedvastu yāvatpadamanāmayam||7||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नैवं न चैवं नाप्येवं नापि चैवमपि स्फुटम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Naivaṁ na caivaṁ nāpyevaṁ nāpi caivamapi sphuṭam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चेतसा योगयुक्तेन यावत्तदिदमप्यलम्॥८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Cetasā yogayuktena yāvattadidamapyalam||8||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कृत्वा तन्मयमात्मानं सर्वाक्षार्थविवर्जितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kṛtvā tanmayamātmānaṁ sarvākṣārthavivarjitam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मुहूर्तं तिष्ठते यावत्तावत्कम्पः प्रजायते॥९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Muhūrtaṁ tiṣṭhate yāvattāvatkampaḥ prajāyate||9||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भ्रमणोद्भवनिद्राश्च किञ्चिदानन्द इत्यपि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhramaṇodbhavanidrāśca kiñcidānanda ityapi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्र यत्नेन सन्दध्याच्चेतः परफलेच्छया॥१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatra yatnena sandadhyāccetaḥ paraphalecchayā||10||</span></p>

## Строфы 11–20 {#s2}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s2-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदेतदात्मनो रूपं शिवेन प्रकटीकृतम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadetadātmano rūpaṁ śivena prakaṭīkṛtam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यत्र तु यच्चं विज्ञेयं शिवात्मकमपि स्थितम्॥११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yatra tu yaccaṁ vijñeyaṁ śivātmakamapi sthitam||11||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तद्रूपोद्बलकत्वेन स्थितमित्यवधारयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadrūpodbalakatvena sthitamityavadhārayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्समभ्यसतो नित्यं स्थूलपिण्डाद्युपाश्रयात्॥१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatsamabhyasato nityaṁ sthūlapiṇḍādyupāśrayāt||12||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चतुर्भेदत्वमायाति भक्त्याभिन्नमपि स्वतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Caturbhedatvamāyāti bhaktyābhinnamapi svataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्थूलपिण्डे द्विधा प्रोक्तं बाह्याभ्यन्तरभेदतः॥१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sthūlapiṇḍe dvidhā proktaṁ bāhyābhyantarabhedataḥ||13||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भौतिकं बाह्यमिच्छन्ति द्वितीयं चातिवाहिकम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhautikaṁ bāhyamicchanti dvitīyaṁ cātivāhikam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्राद्योपाश्रयाद्योगी ससंवित्तिरपि स्फुटान्॥१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatrādyopāśrayādyogī sasaṁvittirapi sphuṭān||14||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बाह्यार्थान्सम्प्रगृह्णाति किञ्चिदाध्यात्मिकानपि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bāhyārthānsampragṛhṇāti kiñcidādhyātmikānapi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">द्वितीयोपाश्रयात्तत्त्वभावार्थान्सम्प्रपद्यते॥१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dvitīyopāśrayāttattvabhāvārthānsamprapadyate||15||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ईक्षते च स्वदेहान्तः पीठक्षेत्रादिकं स्फुटम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Īkṣate ca svadehāntaḥ pīṭhakṣetrādikaṁ sphuṭam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वरूपालोचनादस्य यत्किञ्चिदुपजायते॥१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svarūpālocanādasya yatkiñcidupajāyate||16||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्र चेतः स्थिरीकुर्वंस्तदेव सकलं लभेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatra cetaḥ sthirīkurvaṁstadeva sakalaṁ labhet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तेन तत्र न कुर्वीत चैतदुत्तमवाञ्छया॥१७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tena tatra na kurvīta caitaduttamavāñchayā||17||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पिण्डद्वयविनिर्मुक्ता किञ्चित्तद्वासनान्विता।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Piṇḍadvayavinirmuktā kiñcittadvāsanānvitā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विज्ञानकेवलान्तस्था पदमित्यभिधीयते॥१८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vijñānakevalāntasthā padamityabhidhīyate||18||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यतं एतामनुपाप्तो विज्ञानक्रमयोगतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yataṁ etāmanupāpto vijñānakramayogataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">रूपोदयातिविज्ञानपदत्वं प्रतिपद्यते॥१९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Rūpodayātivijñānapadatvaṁ pratipadyate||19||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एतच्चतुर्विधं ज्ञेयं चतुर्धार्थप्रतिश्रयात्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Etaccaturvidhaṁ jñeyaṁ caturdhārthapratiśrayāt|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स च तत्त्वादिसंवित्तिपूर्वस्तत्पतितावधिः॥२०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sa ca tattvādisaṁvittipūrvastatpatitāvadhiḥ||20||</span></p>

## Строфы 21–30 {#s3}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s3-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पदभावविनिर्मुक्ता किञ्चित्तदनुवर्जिता।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Padabhāvavinirmuktā kiñcittadanuvarjitā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अवस्था स्वस्वरूपस्य प्रकाशकरणी यतः॥२१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Avasthā svasvarūpasya prakāśakaraṇī yataḥ||21||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v22" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तेन सारूप्यमित्युक्ता रूपस्थं यत्तदान्वितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tena sārūpyamityuktā rūpasthaṁ yattadānvitam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उदितादिप्रभेदेन तदप्युक्तं चतुर्विधम्॥२२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Uditādiprabhedena tadapyuktaṁ caturvidham||22||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v23" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ज्ञानोदया च देवेशि ममत्वात्तत्फलप्रदम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jñānodayā ca deveśi mamatvāttatphalapradam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अमुना क्रमयोगेन अन्तरा येषु सन्दधत्॥२३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Amunā kramayogena antarā yeṣu sandadhat||23||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v24" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चेतः शुद्धमवाप्नोति रूपातीतं परं पदम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Cetaḥ śuddhamavāpnoti rūpātītaṁ paraṁ padam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चतुर्विधं तदप्युक्तं संवित्तिफलभेदतः॥२४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Caturvidhaṁ tadapyuktaṁ saṁvittiphalabhedataḥ||24||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v25" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्रिविधं तत्समभ्यस्य सर्वसिद्धिफलेच्छया।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Trividhaṁ tatsamabhyasya sarvasiddhiphalecchayā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चतुर्थात्तु तनुं त्यक्त्वा तत्क्षणादपवृज्यते॥२५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Caturthāttu tanuṁ tyaktvā tatkṣaṇādapavṛjyate||25||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v26" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इति पिण्डादिभेदेन शिवज्ञानमुदाहृतम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Iti piṇḍādibhedena śivajñānamudāhṛtam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">योगाभ्यासविधानेन मन्त्रविद्यागणं शृणु॥२६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yogābhyāsavidhānena mantravidyāgaṇaṁ śṛṇu||26||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v27" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूर्वोक्तविधिसन्नद्धः प्रदेशे पूर्वचोदिते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūrvoktavidhisannaddhaḥ pradeśe pūrvacodite|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नाभ्यादिपञ्चदेशानां परार्णं क्वापि चिन्तयत्॥२७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nābhyādipañcadeśānāṁ parārṇaṁ kvāpi cintayat||27||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v28" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वरूपेण प्रभाभात्प्रकाशिततनूदरम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svarūpeṇa prabhābhātprakāśitatanūdaram|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दीप्तिभिस्तस्य तीव्राभिराब्रह्मभुवनं ततः॥२८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dīptibhistasya tīvrābhirābrahmabhuvanaṁ tataḥ||28||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<span class="pv-iast">Mādhavaśca mahādevi ṣaṣṭhaḥ samparikīrtitaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विश्वा विश्वेश्वरी चैव हाराद्री वीरनायिका॥५९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Viśvā viśveśvarī caiva hārādrī vīranāyikā||59||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v60" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अम्बा गुर्वीति योगिन्यो बीजैस्तैरेव षट् स्मृताः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ambā gurvīti yoginyo bījaistaireva ṣaṭ smṛtāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अन्योन्यवलिताः सर्वे स्वाम्यावरणभेदतः॥६०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anyonyavalitāḥ sarve svāmyāvaraṇabhedataḥ||60||</span></p>

## Строфы 61–62.5 {#s7}
