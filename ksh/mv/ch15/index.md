## Строфы 1–10 {#s1}

<p class="pv-copy nosearch pv-anchor" id="s1-sa"><button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-sa">Копировать санскрит</button> <button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-iast">Копировать транслитерацию</button></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथ पञ्चदशोऽधिकारः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Atha Pañcadaśo'dhikāraḥ|</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथ वागिन्द्रियादीनां मनोन्तानामनुक्रमात्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Atha vāgindriyādīnāṁ manontānāmanukramāt|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">धारणाः सम्प्रवक्ष्यामि दशैकां च समासतः॥१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dhāraṇāḥ sampravakṣyāmi daśaikāṁ ca samāsataḥ||1||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वदनान्तं नमः शब्दमात्मनश्चिन्तयेद्बुधः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vadanāntaṁ namaḥ śabdamātmanaścintayedbudhaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">गृहीतवाक्त्वमभ्येति मौनेन मधुसूदनि॥२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Gṛhītavāktvamabhyeti maunena madhusūdani||2||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वत्रास्खलिता वाणी षड्भिर्मासैः प्रवर्तते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvatrāskhalitā vāṇī ṣaḍbhirmāsaiḥ pravartate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वशास्त्रार्थवेत्तृत्वं वत्सरादुपजायते॥३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvaśāstrārthavettṛtvaṁ vatsarādupajāyate||3||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वागेवास्य प्रवर्तेत काव्यालङ्कारभूषिता।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vāgevāsya pravarteta kāvyālaṅkārabhūṣitā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्रिभिरब्दैः स्वयं कर्ता शास्त्राणां सम्प्रजायते॥४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tribhirabdaiḥ svayaṁ kartā śāstrāṇāṁ samprajāyate||4||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्रैव चिन्तयेद्देहं स्वकीयमनुरूपतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatraiva cintayeddehaṁ svakīyamanurūpataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भूयस्तमेव धवलमीषत्तेजोवभासितम्॥५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhūyastameva dhavalamīṣattejovabhāsitam||5||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">रसान्तः सोमबिम्बादितेजोन्तं तमनुस्मरेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Rasāntaḥ somabimbāditejontaṁ tamanusmaret|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वं फलमवाप्नोति वागावरणजं क्रमात्॥६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvaṁ phalamavāpnoti vāgāvaraṇajaṁ kramāt||6||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पाणौ च तं समादाय षण्मासाद्दूरसंस्थितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pāṇau ca taṁ samādāya ṣaṇmāsāddūrasaṁsthitam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वस्तु गृह्णात्यसन्देहात्त्र्यब्दात्पारेऽपि वारिधेः॥७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vastu gṛhṇātyasandehāttryabdātpāre'pi vāridheḥ||7||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्रात्मदेहं पूर्वं तु पद्माभमनुचिन्तयन्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatrātmadehaṁ pūrvaṁ tu padmābhamanucintayan|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सव्यापारादिभेदेन चतुर्दशकमादरात्॥८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Savyāpārādibhedena caturdaśakamādarāt||8||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूर्वोक्तकालनियमात्पूर्वोक्तेनैव वर्त्मना।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūrvoktakālaniyamātpūrvoktenaiva vartmanā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वं फलमवाप्नोति हस्तावृतिसमाश्रितम्॥९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvaṁ phalamavāpnoti hastāvṛtisamāśritam||9||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पादावेवंविधो ध्यायन्वत्सरत्रयमादरात्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pādāvevaṁvidho dhyāyanvatsaratrayamādarāt|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मुहूर्तेन समुद्रान्तामश्रान्तो भ्रमति क्षितिम्॥१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Muhūrtena samudrāntāmaśrānto bhramati kṣitim||10||</span></p>

## Строфы 11–20 {#s2}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s2-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चतुर्दश समभ्यर्च्य स्वदेहादिकमभ्यसन्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Caturdaśa samabhyarcya svadehādikamabhyasan|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्राप्नोति पूर्ववत्सर्वं फलं पादावृतिस्थितम्॥११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prāpnoti pūrvavatsarvaṁ phalaṁ pādāvṛtisthitam||11||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पायावपि मनस्तत्त्वं स्थिरीकुर्वन्नवाप्स्यति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pāyāvapi manastattvaṁ sthirīkurvannavāpsyati|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मासेन तद्भवव्याधिविमुक्तिमविलम्बतः॥१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Māsena tadbhavavyādhivimuktimavilambataḥ||12||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पुण्यश्लोकत्वमाप्नोति त्रिभिरब्दैरनादरात्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Puṇyaślokatvamāpnoti tribhirabdairanādarāt|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चतुर्दशविधं चात्र पूर्ववत्फलमाप्स्यति॥१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Caturdaśavidhaṁ cātra pūrvavatphalamāpsyati||13||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वरूपतः स्मरेल्लिङ्गं मासमात्राज्जितेन्द्रियः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svarūpataḥ smarelliṅgaṁ māsamātrājjitendriyaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">षड्भिर्मासैरनायासादिच्छाकामित्वमाप्नुयात्॥१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ṣaḍbhirmāsairanāyāsādicchākāmitvamāpnuyāt||14||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चतुर्दशविधे भेदे तत्राभ्यस्ते महामतिः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Caturdaśavidhe bhede tatrābhyaste mahāmatiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">लिङ्गावरणजं सर्वं पूर्ववल्लभते फलम्॥१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Liṅgāvaraṇajaṁ sarvaṁ pūrvavallabhate phalam||15||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वजिह्वामिन्दुवर्णाभां चिन्तयेद्दशभिर्दिनैः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svajihvāminduvarṇābhāṁ cintayeddaśabhirdinaiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्राप्नोत्यनुभवं योगी जिह्वाभवमिवात्मनः॥१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prāpnotyanubhavaṁ yogī jihvābhavamivātmanaḥ||16||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आस्वादयति दूरस्थं षण्मासादेकमानसः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Āsvādayati dūrasthaṁ ṣaṇmāsādekamānasaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वत्सरैस्तु त्रिभिः साक्षाल्लेढ्यसौ परमामृतम्॥१७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vatsaraistu tribhiḥ sākṣālleḍhyasau paramāmṛtam||17||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">येनासौ भवति योगी जरामरणवर्जितः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yenāsau bhavati yogī jarāmaraṇavarjitaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अपेयादिप्रसक्तोऽपि न पापैः परिभूयते॥१८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Apeyādiprasakto'pi na pāpaiḥ paribhūyate||18||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूर्ववत्सर्वमन्यच्च स्वदेहाद्यनुचिन्तयन्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūrvavatsarvamanyacca svadehādyanucintayan|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">फलमाप्नोत्यसन्देहाद्रसनावृतिसम्भवम्॥१९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Phalamāpnotyasandehādrasanāvṛtisambhavam||19||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कनकाभं स्वकं घ्राणमनुचिन्तयतः शनैः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kanakābhaṁ svakaṁ ghrāṇamanucintayataḥ śanaiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दिवसैर्दशभिर्घ्राणशून्यतानुभवो भवेत्॥२०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Divasairdaśabhirghrāṇaśūnyatānubhavo bhavet||20||</span></p>

## Строфы 21–30 {#s3}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s3-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">षण्मासाद्गन्धमाघ्राति दूरस्थस्यापि वस्तुनः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ṣaṇmāsādgandhamāghrāti dūrasthasyāpi vastunaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">घातयेद्गन्धमाघ्राय यस्य दुष्टो भविष्यति॥२१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ghātayedgandhamāghrāya yasya duṣṭo bhaviṣyati||21||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v22" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वत्सरैस्तु त्रिभिर्दिव्यं गन्धमासाद्य योगवित्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vatsaraistu tribhirdivyaṁ gandhamāsādya yogavit|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जरामरणनैर्गुण्ययुक्तो दिव्यत्वमर्हति॥२२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jarāmaraṇanairguṇyayukto divyatvamarhati||22||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v23" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वमन्यद्यथोद्दिष्टं तथैव च विचिन्तयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvamanyadyathoddiṣṭaṁ tathaiva ca vicintayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">क्रमिकं फलमाप्नोति घ्राणावरणमास्थितम्॥२३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kramikaṁ phalamāpnoti ghrāṇāvaraṇamāsthitam||23||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v24" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उदयादित्यसङ्काशे चिन्तयंश्चक्षुषी निजे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Udayādityasaṅkāśe cintayaṁścakṣuṣī nije|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दशाहाच्चक्षुषो रक्तस्रावानुभवमाप्स्यति॥२४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Daśāhāccakṣuṣo raktasrāvānubhavamāpsyati||24||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v25" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वेदना महती चास्य ललाटे सम्प्रजायते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vedanā mahatī cāsya lalāṭe samprajāyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न भेतव्यं महादेवि न चाभ्यासं परित्यजेत्॥२५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na bhetavyaṁ mahādevi na cābhyāsaṁ parityajet||25||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v26" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सन्त्यजन्नन्धतामेति तेन यत्नाअत्समभ्यसेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Santyajannandhatāmeti tena yatnāatsamabhyaset|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">षड्भिर्मासैर्महायोगी दिव्यदृष्टिः प्रजायते॥२६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ṣaḍbhirmāsairmahāyogī divyadṛṣṭiḥ prajāyate||26||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v27" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">छिद्रां प्रपश्यते भूमिं कटाहान्तामतन्द्रितः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Chidrāṁ prapaśyate bhūmiṁ kaṭāhāntāmatandritaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आध्रुवान्तमथोर्ध्वं च करामलकवद्बुधः॥२७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ādhruvāntamathordhvaṁ ca karāmalakavadbudhaḥ||27||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v28" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वत्सरैस्तु त्रिभिर्योगी ब्रह्माण्डान्तं प्रपश्यति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vatsaraistu tribhiryogī brahmāṇḍāntaṁ prapaśyati|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदन्तर्योगिनीज्ञानं शरीरस्थं प्रजायते॥२८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadantaryoginījñānaṁ śarīrasthaṁ prajāyate||28||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v29" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वदेहादिकमन्यच्च पूर्वोक्तं पूर्ववत्स्मरन्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svadehādikamanyacca pūrvoktaṁ pūrvavatsmaran|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नयनावृतिजं सर्वमाप्नोतीति किमद्भुतम्॥२९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nayanāvṛtijaṁ sarvamāpnotīti kimadbhutam||29||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v30" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वत्राञ्जनपत्राभां निस्तरङ्गां त्वचं स्मरन्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvatrāñjanapatrābhāṁ nistaraṅgāṁ tvacaṁ smaran|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शस्त्रैरपि न मासेन हन्तुं शक्यो भविष्यति॥३०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śastrairapi na māsena hantuṁ śakyo bhaviṣyati||30||</span></p>

## Строфы 31–40 {#s4}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s4-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">षण्मासादतितीव्रेण नाग्निनाप्येष दह्यते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ṣaṇmāsādatitīvreṇa nāgnināpyeṣa dahyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वत्सरत्रितयाद्योगी वज्रोपलविषादिभिः॥३१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vatsaratritayādyogī vajropalaviṣādibhiḥ||31||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v32" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पीड्यते न कदाचित्स्यादजरामरतां गतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pīḍyate na kadācitsyādajarāmaratāṁ gataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्पर्शादृतिपविज्ञानगीतवच्च चतुर्दश॥३२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sparśādṛtipavijñānagītavacca caturdaśa||32||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v33" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भेदाः सह फलैर्ज्ञेयाः पूर्वकालानुसारतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhedāḥ saha phalairjñeyāḥ pūrvakālānusārataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">किन्त्वत्र चिन्तयेद्देहं स्वदेहादिभिरावृतम्॥३३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kintvatra cintayeddehaṁ svadehādibhirāvṛtam||33||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v34" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सन्दधानः स्वकं चेतः श्रोत्राकाशे विचक्षणः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sandadhānaḥ svakaṁ cetaḥ śrotrākāśe vicakṣaṇaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दूराच्छ्रवणविज्ञानं षण्मासादुपजायते॥३४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dūrācchravaṇavijñānaṁ ṣaṇmāsādupajāyate||34||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v35" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्रिभिः संवत्सरैर्देवि ब्रह्माण्डान्तरुदीरितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tribhiḥ saṁvatsarairdevi brahmāṇḍāntarudīritam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शृणोति स स्फुटं सर्वं जरामरणवर्जितः॥३५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śṛṇoti sa sphuṭaṁ sarvaṁ jarāmaraṇavarjitaḥ||35||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v36" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्राकाशोक्तवत्सर्वं स्वदेहाद्यनुचिन्तयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatrākāśoktavatsarvaṁ svadehādyanucintayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">श्रोत्रावरणजं सर्वं फलमाप्नोति पूर्ववत्॥३६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śrotrāvaraṇajaṁ sarvaṁ phalamāpnoti pūrvavat||36||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v37" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मनोवतीमतो वक्ष्ये धारणां सर्वसिद्धिदाम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Manovatīmato vakṣye dhāraṇāṁ sarvasiddhidām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यया संसिद्धया देवि सर्वसिद्धिफलं लभेत्॥३७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yayā saṁsiddhayā devi sarvasiddhiphalaṁ labhet||37||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v38" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मन एव मनुष्याणां कारणं बन्धमोक्षयोः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mana eva manuṣyāṇāṁ kāraṇaṁ bandhamokṣayoḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तस्मात्तदभ्यसेन्मन्त्री यदीच्छेन्मोक्षम्व्ययम्॥३८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tasmāttadabhyasenmantrī yadīcchenmokṣamvyayam||38||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v39" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदर्धचन्द्रसङ्काशमधोवक्त्रं हृदि स्थितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadardhacandrasaṅkāśamadhovaktraṁ hṛdi sthitam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चिन्तयन्मासमात्रेण प्रतिभां प्रतिपत्स्यते॥३९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Cintayanmāsamātreṇa pratibhāṁ pratipatsyate||39||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v40" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अकस्मात्पस्यते किञ्चिदकस्माच्छृणुते तथा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Akasmātpasyate kiñcidakasmācchṛṇute tathā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वेन्द्रियात्मकं ज्ञानमकस्माच्च क्वचित्क्वचित्॥४०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvendriyātmakaṁ jñānamakasmācca kvacitkvacit||40||</span></p>

## Строфы 41–47 {#s5}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s5-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वस्वकेन्द्रियविज्ञानं सम्पश्येद्वत्सरत्रयात्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svasvakendriyavijñānaṁ sampaśyedvatsaratrayāt|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवते योगयुक्तस्य योगिनः सुपरिस्फुटम्॥४१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavate yogayuktasya yoginaḥ suparisphuṭam||41||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v42" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वदेहादिकमप्यत्र पूर्वोक्तवदनुस्मरन्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svadehādikamapyatra pūrvoktavadanusmaran|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चित्तावरणविज्ञानं प्राप्य सोमगुणं लभेत्॥४२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Cittāvaraṇavijñānaṁ prāpya somaguṇaṁ labhet||42||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v43" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इत्येकादश गीतानि समभ्यस्यानि ते तथा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ityekādaśa gītāni samabhyasyāni te tathā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इन्द्रियाणि यतः सर्वं फलमेव प्रतिष्ठितम्॥४३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Indriyāṇi yataḥ sarvaṁ phalameva pratiṣṭhitam||43||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v44" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बन्धमोक्षाबुभावेताविन्द्रियाणां जगुर्बुधाः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bandhamokṣābubhāvetāvindriyāṇāṁ jagurbudhāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विगृहीतानि बन्धाय विमुक्तानि विमुक्तये॥४४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vigṛhītāni bandhāya vimuktāni vimuktaye||44||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v45" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एतानि व्यापके भावे यदा स्युर्मनसा सह।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Etāni vyāpake bhāve yadā syurmanasā saha|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विमुक्तानीति विद्वद्भिर्ज्ञातव्यानि तदा प्रिये॥४५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vimuktānīti vidvadbhirjñātavyāni tadā priye||45||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v46" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यदा तु विषये क्वापि प्रदेशान्तरवर्तिनि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yadā tu viṣaye kvāpi pradeśāntaravartini|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">संस्थितानि तदा तानि बद्धानीति प्रचक्षते॥४६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Saṁsthitāni tadā tāni baddhānīti pracakṣate||46||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v47" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इत्ययं द्विविधो भावः शुद्धाशुद्धप्रभेदतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ityayaṁ dvividho bhāvaḥ śuddhāśuddhaprabhedataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इन्द्रियाणां समाख्यातः सिद्धयोगीश्वरीमते॥४७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Indriyāṇāṁ samākhyātaḥ siddhayogīśvarīmate||47||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इति श्रीमालिनीविजयोत्तरेऽक्षधारणाधिकारः पञ्चदशः॥१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Iti śrīmālinīvijayottare'kṣadhāraṇādhikāraḥ pañcadaśaḥ||15||</span></p>
