## Строфы 1–10 {#s1}

<p class="pv-copy nosearch pv-anchor" id="s1-sa"><button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-sa">Копировать санскрит</button> <button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-iast">Копировать транслитерацию</button></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथ चतुर्दशोऽधिकारः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Atha Caturdaśo'dhikāraḥ|</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथ गन्धादिपूर्वाणां तन्मात्राणामनुक्रमात्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Atha gandhādipūrvāṇāṁ tanmātrāṇāmanukramāt|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">धारणाः सम्प्रवक्ष्यामि तत्फलानां प्रसिद्धये॥१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dhāraṇāḥ sampravakṣyāmi tatphalānāṁ prasiddhaye||1||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पीतकं गन्धतन्मात्रं तुर्याश्रं पर्वसंमितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pītakaṁ gandhatanmātraṁ turyāśraṁ parvasaṁmitam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नासारन्ध्राग्रगं ध्यायेद्वज्रलाञ्छनलाञ्छितम्॥२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nāsārandhrāgragaṁ dhyāyedvajralāñchanalāñchitam||2||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दशमाद्दिवसादूर्ध्वं योगिनोऽनन्यचेतसः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Daśamāddivasādūrdhvaṁ yogino'nanyacetasaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कोऽपि गन्धः समायाति द्विधाभूतोऽप्यनेकधा॥३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ko'pi gandhaḥ samāyāti dvidhābhūto'pyanekadhā||3||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततोऽस्य ऋतुमात्रेण शुधो गन्धः स्थिरीभवेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tato'sya ṛtumātreṇa śudho gandhaḥ sthirībhavet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">षड्भिर्मासैः स्वयं गन्धमय एव भविष्यति॥४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ṣaḍbhirmāsaiḥ svayaṁ gandhamaya eva bhaviṣyati||4||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यो यत्र रोचते गन्धस्तं तत्र कुरुते भृशम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yo yatra rocate gandhastaṁ tatra kurute bhṛśam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्र्यब्दात्सिद्धिमवाप्नोति प्रेरितां पाञ्चभौतिकीम्॥५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tryabdātsiddhimavāpnoti preritāṁ pāñcabhautikīm||5||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदूर्ध्वमात्मनो रूपं तत्र सञ्चिन्तयेद्धृदि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadūrdhvamātmano rūpaṁ tatra sañcintayeddhṛdi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">गन्धावरणविज्ञानं त्रिभिरब्दैरवाप्नुयात्॥६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Gandhāvaraṇavijñānaṁ tribhirabdairavāpnuyāt||6||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ईषद्दीप्तियुतं तत्र तन्मण्डलविवर्जितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Īṣaddīptiyutaṁ tatra tanmaṇḍalavivarjitam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ध्यायन्प्रपश्यते सर्वान्गन्धावरणवासिनः॥७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dhyāyanprapaśyate sarvāngandhāvaraṇavāsinaḥ||7||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">धरातत्त्वोक्तबिम्बाभं तत्रैवमनुचिन्तयन्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dharātattvoktabimbābhaṁ tatraivamanucintayan|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्समानत्वमभ्येति पूर्ववद्द्वितये स्थिरे॥८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatsamānatvamabhyeti pūrvavaddvitaye sthire||8||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वरूपं तत्र सञ्चिन्त्य भासयन्तमधः स्थितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svarūpaṁ tatra sañcintya bhāsayantamadhaḥ sthitam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदीशत्वमवाप्नोति पूर्वोक्तेनैव वर्त्मना॥९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadīśatvamavāpnoti pūrvoktenaiva vartmanā||9||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">धरातत्त्वोक्तवत्सर्वमत ऊर्ध्वमनुस्मरन्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dharātattvoktavatsarvamata ūrdhvamanusmaran|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तद्रूपं फलमाप्नोति गन्धावरणसंस्थितम्॥१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadrūpaṁ phalamāpnoti gandhāvaraṇasaṁsthitam||10||</span></p>

## Строфы 11–20 {#s2}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s2-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">रसरूपामतो वक्ष्ये धारणां योगिसेविताम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Rasarūpāmato vakṣye dhāraṇāṁ yogisevitām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यया सर्वरसावाप्तिर्योगिनः सम्प्रजायते॥११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yayā sarvarasāvāptiryoginaḥ samprajāyate||11||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जलबुद्बुदसङ्काशं जिह्वायां चाग्रतः स्थितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jalabudbudasaṅkāśaṁ jihvāyāṁ cāgrataḥ sthitam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चिन्तयेद्रसतन्मात्रं जिह्वाग्राधारमात्मनः॥१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Cintayedrasatanmātraṁ jihvāgrādhāramātmanaḥ||12||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सुशीतं षड्रसं चिन्त्यं तद्गतेनान्तरात्मना।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Suśītaṁ ṣaḍrasaṁ cintyaṁ tadgatenāntarātmanā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततोऽस्य मासमात्रेण रसास्वादः प्रवर्तते॥१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tato'sya māsamātreṇa rasāsvādaḥ pravartate||13||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">लवणादीन्परित्यज्य यदा मधुरतां गतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Lavaṇādīnparityajya yadā madhuratāṁ gataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदा तन्निगिरन्योगी षण्मासान्मृत्युजिद्भवेत्॥१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadā tannigiranyogī ṣaṇmāsānmṛtyujidbhavet||14||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जराव्याधिविनिर्मुक्तः कृष्णकेशोऽच्युतद्युतिः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jarāvyādhivinirmuktaḥ kṛṣṇakeśo'cyutadyutiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जीवेदाचन्द्रतारार्कमभ्यस्यंश्च क्वचित्क्वचित्॥१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jīvedācandratārārkamabhyasyaṁśca kvacitkvacit||15||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूर्वोक्तबुद्बुदाकारं स्वरूपमनुचिन्तयन्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūrvoktabudbudākāraṁ svarūpamanucintayan|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नीरावरणविज्ञानमाप्नोतीति किमद्भुतम्॥१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nīrāvaraṇavijñānamāpnotīti kimadbhutam||16||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तमेव द्युतिसंयुक्तं ध्यायन्न्नाधारवर्जितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tameva dyutisaṁyuktaṁ dhyāyannnādhāravarjitam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पद्येत वत्सरैः सर्वां रसावरणमाश्रिताम्॥१७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Padyeta vatsaraiḥ sarvāṁ rasāvaraṇamāśritām||17||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जलतत्त्वोक्तबिम्बादि तदूर्ध्वमनुचिन्तयन्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jalatattvoktabimbādi tadūrdhvamanucintayan|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूर्वोक्तं सर्वमाप्नोति रसावरणजं स्फुटम्॥१८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūrvoktaṁ sarvamāpnoti rasāvaraṇajaṁ sphuṭam||18||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अतो रूपवतीं वक्ष्ये दिव्यदृष्टिप्रदां शुभाम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ato rūpavatīṁ vakṣye divyadṛṣṭipradāṁ śubhām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">धारणां सर्वसिद्ध्यर्थं रूपतन्मात्रमाश्रिताम्॥१९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dhāraṇāṁ sarvasiddhyarthaṁ rūpatanmātramāśritām||19||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एकान्तस्थो यदा योगी विनिमीलितलोचनः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ekāntastho yadā yogī vinimīlitalocanaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शरत्सन्ध्याभ्रसङ्काशं यत्तु किञ्चित्प्रपश्यति॥२०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śaratsandhyābhrasaṅkāśaṁ yattu kiñcitprapaśyati||20||</span></p>

## Строфы 21–30 {#s3}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s3-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्र चेतः समाधाय यावदास्ते दशाहकम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatra cetaḥ samādhāya yāvadāste daśāhakam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तावत्प्रपश्यते तत्र बिन्दून्सूक्ष्मतमानपि॥२१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tāvatprapaśyate tatra bindūnsūkṣmatamānapi||21||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v22" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">केचित्तत्र सिता रक्ताः पीता नीलास्तथा परे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kecittatra sitā raktāḥ pītā nīlāstathā pare|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तान्दृष्ट्वा तत्र सन्दध्यान्मनोऽत्यन्तमनन्यधीः॥२२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tāndṛṣṭvā tatra sandadhyānmano'tyantamananyadhīḥ||22||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v23" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">षण्मासात्पश्यते तेषु रूपाणि सुबहून्यपि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ṣaṇmāsātpaśyate teṣu rūpāṇi subahūnyapi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्र्यब्दात्तान्येव तेजोभिः प्रदीप्तानि स्थिराणि च॥२३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tryabdāttānyeva tejobhiḥ pradīptāni sthirāṇi ca||23||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v24" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तान्यभ्यस्यंस्ततो द्व्यब्दाद्बिम्बाकाराणि पश्यति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tānyabhyasyaṁstato dvyabdādbimbākārāṇi paśyati|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततोऽब्दात्पश्यते तेजः षण्मासात्पुरुषाकृति॥२४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tato'bdātpaśyate tejaḥ ṣaṇmāsātpuruṣākṛti||24||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v25" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्रिमासाद्व्यापकं तेजो मासात्सर्वं विसर्पितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Trimāsādvyāpakaṁ tejo māsātsarvaṁ visarpitam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कालक्रमाच्च पूर्वोक्तं रूपावरणमाश्रितम्॥२५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kālakramācca pūrvoktaṁ rūpāvaraṇamāśritam||25||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v26" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्सर्वं फलमाप्नोति दिव्यदृष्टिश्च जायते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatsarvaṁ phalamāpnoti divyadṛṣṭiśca jāyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इतीयं कल्पनाशून्या धारणा कृतकोदिता॥२६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Itīyaṁ kalpanāśūnyā dhāraṇā kṛtakoditā||26||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v27" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दशपञ्चविधो भेदः स्वयमेवात्र जायते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Daśapañcavidho bhedaḥ svayamevātra jāyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यतोऽस्यां निश्चयं कुर्यात्किमन्यैः शास्त्रडम्बरैः॥२७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yato'syāṁ niścayaṁ kuryātkimanyaiḥ śāstraḍambaraiḥ||27||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v28" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अत स्पर्शवतीमन्यां कथयामि तवाधुना।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ata sparśavatīmanyāṁ kathayāmi tavādhunā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">धारणां तु यया योगी वज्रदेहः प्रजायते॥२८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dhāraṇāṁ tu yayā yogī vajradehaḥ prajāyate||28||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v29" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">षट्कोणमण्डलान्तः स्थमात्मानं परिभावयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ṣaṭkoṇamaṇḍalāntaḥ sthamātmānaṁ paribhāvayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">रूक्षमञ्जनसङ्काशं प्रत्यंशं स्फुरिताकुलम्॥२९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Rūkṣamañjanasaṅkāśaṁ pratyaṁśaṁ sphuritākulam||29||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v30" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततोऽस्य दशभिर्देवि दिवसैस्त्वचि सर्वतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tato'sya daśabhirdevi divasaistvaci sarvataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवेत्पिपीलिकास्पर्शस्ततस्तमनुचिन्तयन्॥३०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavetpipīlikāsparśastatastamanucintayan||30||</span></p>

## Строфы 31–40 {#s4}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s4-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वज्रदेहत्वमासाद्य पूर्वोक्तं पूर्ववल्लभेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vajradehatvamāsādya pūrvoktaṁ pūrvavallabhet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूर्वोक्तमण्डलाकारं पूर्वरूपं विचिन्तयन्॥३१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūrvoktamaṇḍalākāraṁ pūrvarūpaṁ vicintayan||31||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v32" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्पर्शतत्त्वावृतिज्ञानं लभन्केन निवार्यते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sparśatattvāvṛtijñānaṁ labhankena nivāryate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हीनमण्डलमात्मानं ध्यायेत्तत्पतिसिद्धये॥३२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Hīnamaṇḍalamātmānaṁ dhyāyettatpatisiddhaye||32||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v33" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यया संसिद्धया सर्वस्पर्शवेदी भविष्यति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yayā saṁsiddhayā sarvasparśavedī bhaviṣyati|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कर्णौ पिधाय यत्नेन निमीलितविलोचनः॥३३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Karṇau pidhāya yatnena nimīlitavilocanaḥ||33||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v34" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यं शृणोति महाघोषं चेतस्तत्रानुसन्धयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yaṁ śṛṇoti mahāghoṣaṁ cetastatrānusandhayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दीप्यते जाठरो वह्निस्ततोऽस्य दशभिर्दिनैः॥३४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dīpyate jāṭharo vahnistato'sya daśabhirdinaiḥ||34||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v35" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दूराच्छ्रवणविज्ञानं षण्मासादुपजायते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dūrācchravaṇavijñānaṁ ṣaṇmāsādupajāyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यस्तस्यान्ते ध्वनिर्मन्दः किञ्चित्किञ्चिद्विभाव्यते॥३५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yastasyānte dhvanirmandaḥ kiñcitkiñcidvibhāvyate||35||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v36" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सकलात्मा स विज्ञेयस्तदभ्यासादनन्यधीः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sakalātmā sa vijñeyastadabhyāsādananyadhīḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शब्दावरणविज्ञानमाप्नोति स्थिरतां गतम्॥३६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śabdāvaraṇavijñānamāpnoti sthiratāṁ gatam||36||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v37" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यः पुनः श्रूयते शब्दस्तदन्ते शङ्खनादवत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yaḥ punaḥ śrūyate śabdastadante śaṅkhanādavat|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रलयाकलरूपं तदभ्यस्यं तत्फलेप्सुभिः॥३७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pralayākalarūpaṁ tadabhyasyaṁ tatphalepsubhiḥ||37||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v38" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स एवातितरामन्यशब्दप्रच्छादको यदा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sa evātitarāmanyaśabdapracchādako yadā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विज्ञानाकल इत्युक्तस्तदासावपराजिते॥३८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vijñānākala ityuktastadāsāvaparājite||38||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v39" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मनोह्लादकरो योऽन्यस्तदन्ते संविभाव्यते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Manohlādakaro yo'nyastadante saṁvibhāvyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स मन्त्र इति विज्ञेयो योगिभिर्योगकाङ्क्षिभिः॥३९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sa mantra iti vijñeyo yogibhiryogakāṅkṣibhiḥ||39||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v40" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततस्तु श्रूयते योऽन्यः शान्तघण्टानिनादवत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatastu śrūyate yo'nyaḥ śāntaghaṇṭāninādavat|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स मन्त्रेश इति प्रोक्तः सर्वसिद्धिफलप्रदः॥४०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sa mantreśa iti proktaḥ sarvasiddhiphalapradaḥ||40||</span></p>

## Строфы 41–43.5 {#s5}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s5-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">घण्टानादविरामान्ते यः शब्दः सम्प्रजायते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ghaṇṭānādavirāmānte yaḥ śabdaḥ samprajāyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मन्त्रेशेशपदं तद्धि सिद्धीनां कारणं महत्॥४१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mantreśeśapadaṁ taddhi siddhīnāṁ kāraṇaṁ mahat||41||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v42" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अनिलेनाहता वीणा यादृङ्नादं विमुञ्चति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anilenāhatā vīṇā yādṛṅnādaṁ vimuñcati|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तादृशो यो ध्वनिस्तत्र तं विद्याच्छाम्भवं पदम्॥४२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tādṛśo yo dhvanistatra taṁ vidyācchāmbhavaṁ padam||42||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v43" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पृथग्वा क्रमशो वापि सर्वानेतान्समभ्यसेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pṛthagvā kramaśo vāpi sarvānetānsamabhyaset|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्राप्नोति सर्ववित्सिद्धिः शब्दावरणमाश्रिताः॥४३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prāpnoti sarvavitsiddhiḥ śabdāvaraṇamāśritāḥ||43||</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इत्येताः कथिताः पञ्च तन्मात्राणां तु धारणाः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ityetāḥ kathitāḥ pañca tanmātrāṇāṁ tu dhāraṇāḥ|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इति श्रीमालिनीविजयोत्तरे तन्त्रे तन्मात्रधारणाधिकारश्चतुर्दशः॥१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Iti śrīmālinīvijayottare tantre tanmātradhāraṇādhikāraścaturdaśaḥ||14||</span></p>
