## Строфы 1–10 {#s1}

<p class="pv-copy nosearch pv-anchor" id="s1-sa"><button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-sa">Копировать санскрит</button> <button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-iast">Копировать транслитерацию</button></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथ त्रयोविंशतितमोऽधिकारः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Atha Trayoviṁśatitamo'dhikāraḥ|</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथातः परमं गुह्यं कथयामि तवाधुना।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Athātaḥ paramaṁ guhyaṁ kathayāmi tavādhunā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सद्योपलब्धिजनकं योगिनां योगसिद्धये॥१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sadyopalabdhijanakaṁ yogināṁ yogasiddhaye||1||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूर्वन्यासेन सन्नद्धश्चित्तं श्रोत्रे निवेशयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūrvanyāsena sannaddhaścittaṁ śrotre niveśayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">निवाते स्वल्पवाते वा बाह्यशब्दविवर्जिते॥२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nivāte svalpavāte vā bāhyaśabdavivarjite||2||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततस्तत्र शृणोत्येष योगी ध्वनिमनावृतम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatastatra śṛṇotyeṣa yogī dhvanimanāvṛtam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सुविशुद्धस्य कांस्यस्य हतस्येह मुहुर्मुहुः॥३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Suviśuddhasya kāṁsyasya hatasyeha muhurmuhuḥ||3||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यमाकर्ण्य महादेवि पुण्यपापैः प्रमुच्यते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yamākarṇya mahādevi puṇyapāpaiḥ pramucyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्र सन्धाय चैतन्यं षण्मासाद्योगवित्तमः॥४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatra sandhāya caitanyaṁ ṣaṇmāsādyogavittamaḥ||4||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">रुतं पक्षिगणस्यापि प्रस्फुटं वेत्त्ययत्नतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Rutaṁ pakṣigaṇasyāpi prasphuṭaṁ vettyayatnataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दूराच्छ्रवणविज्ञानं वत्सरेणास्य जायते॥५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dūrācchravaṇavijñānaṁ vatsareṇāsya jāyate||5||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वकामफलावाप्तिर्वत्सरत्रितयेन च।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvakāmaphalāvāptirvatsaratritayena ca|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सिद्ध्यतीति किमाश्चर्यमनायासेन सिद्ध्यति॥६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Siddhyatīti kimāścaryamanāyāsena siddhyati||6||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथवा ग्रहणे मासि कृत्वा सूर्यं तु पृष्ठतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Athavā grahaṇe māsi kṛtvā sūryaṁ tu pṛṣṭhataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूर्वन्यासेन सन्नद्धः किञ्चिद्भित्तिमदाश्रितः॥७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūrvanyāsena sannaddhaḥ kiñcidbhittimadāśritaḥ||7||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">लक्षयेदात्मनश्छायां मस्तकोर्ध्वमनाहताम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Lakṣayedātmanaśchāyāṁ mastakordhvamanāhatām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">धूमवर्तिविनिष्क्रान्तां तद्गतेनान्तरात्मना॥८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dhūmavartiviniṣkrāntāṁ tadgatenāntarātmanā||8||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">याति तन्मयता तत्र योगयुक्तो यथा यथा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yāti tanmayatā tatra yogayukto yathā yathā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तथा तथास्य महती सा वित्तिरुपजायते॥९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tathā tathāsya mahatī sā vittirupajāyate||9||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततस्तत्र महातेजः स्फुरत्किरणसन्निभम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatastatra mahātejaḥ sphuratkiraṇasannibham|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पश्यते यत्र दृष्टेऽपि सर्वपापक्षयो भवेत्॥१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Paśyate yatra dṛṣṭe'pi sarvapāpakṣayo bhavet||10||</span></p>

## Строфы 11–20 {#s2}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s2-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदस्याभ्यसतो मासात्सर्वत्र प्रविसर्पति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadasyābhyasato māsātsarvatra pravisarpati|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ज्वालामालाकुलाकारा दिशः सर्वाः प्रपश्यति॥११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jvālāmālākulākārā diśaḥ sarvāḥ prapaśyati||11||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">षण्मासमभ्यसन्योगी सर्वज्ञत्वमवाप्नुयात्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ṣaṇmāsamabhyasanyogī sarvajñatvamavāpnuyāt|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अब्दं दिव्यतनुर्भूत्वा शिववन्मोदते चिरम्॥१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Abdaṁ divyatanurbhūtvā śivavanmodate ciram||12||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथ जात्यः प्रवक्ष्यन्ते सपूर्वासनशाश्वताः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Atha jātyaḥ pravakṣyante sapūrvāsanaśāśvatāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हीं क्ष्लां क्ष्वीं वं तथा क्षं च पञ्चकस्य यथाक्रमम्॥१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Hīṁ kṣlāṁ kṣvīṁ vaṁ tathā kṣaṁ ca pañcakasya yathākramam||13||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हं यं रं लं तथा वं च पञ्चकस्यापरस्य च।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Haṁ yaṁ raṁ laṁ tathā vaṁ ca pañcakasyāparasya ca|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ऋं ॠं ऌं ॡं तथा ॐ औं हः अं आकर्णिकावधौ॥१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ṛṁ ṝṁ ḷṁ ḹṁ tathā oṁ auṁ haḥ aṁ ākarṇikāvadhau||14||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">केसरेषु भकारान्ता हं हां हिं हीं च हुं तथा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kesareṣu bhakārāntā haṁ hāṁ hiṁ hīṁ ca huṁ tathā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हूं हें हैं च दलेष्वेवं स्वसञ्ज्ञाभिश्च शक्तयः॥१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Hūṁ heṁ haiṁ ca daleṣvevaṁ svasañjñābhiśca śaktayaḥ||15||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मण्डलत्रितये शेषं सूक्ष्मं प्रेतस्य कल्पयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Maṇḍalatritaye śeṣaṁ sūkṣmaṁ pretasya kalpayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ज्रकारं शूलशृङ्गाणामित्येतत्परिकीर्तितम्॥१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jrakāraṁ śūlaśṛṅgāṇāmityetatparikīrtitam||16||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अनुक्तासनयोगेषु सर्वत्रैवं प्रकल्पयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anuktāsanayogeṣu sarvatraivaṁ prakalpayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नमः स्वाहा तथा वौषट् हुं वषट् फट् च जातयः॥१७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Namaḥ svāhā tathā vauṣaṭ huṁ vaṣaṭ phaṭ ca jātayaḥ||17||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रायश्चित्तेषु सर्वेषु जपेन्मालामखण्डिताम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prāyaścitteṣu sarveṣu japenmālāmakhaṇḍitām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भिन्नां वाप्यथवाभिन्नामतिक्रमबलाबलाम्॥१८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhinnāṁ vāpyathavābhinnāmatikramabalābalām||18||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सकृज्जपात्समारभ्य यावल्लक्षत्रयं प्रिये।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sakṛjjapātsamārabhya yāvallakṣatrayaṁ priye|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्राणवृत्तिनिरोधेन ततः परतरं क्वचित्॥१९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prāṇavṛttinirodhena tataḥ parataraṁ kvacit||19||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सदा भ्रमणशीलानां पीठक्षेत्रादिकं बहिः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sadā bhramaṇaśīlānāṁ pīṭhakṣetrādikaṁ bahiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रयोगं सम्प्रवक्ष्यामि सुखसिद्धिफलप्रदम्॥२०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prayogaṁ sampravakṣyāmi sukhasiddhiphalapradam||20||</span></p>

## Строфы 21–30 {#s3}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s3-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नासाक्रान्तं महाप्राणं दण्डरूपं सबिन्दुकम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nāsākrāntaṁ mahāprāṇaṁ daṇḍarūpaṁ sabindukam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तद्वद्गुह्यं च कुर्वीत विद्येयं द्व्यक्षरां मता॥२१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadvadguhyaṁ ca kurvīta vidyeyaṁ dvyakṣarāṁ matā||21||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v22" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अस्याः पूर्वोक्तविधिना कृतसेवः प्रसन्नधीः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Asyāḥ pūrvoktavidhinā kṛtasevaḥ prasannadhīḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पीठादिकं भ्रमेत्सिद्ध्यै नान्यथा वीरवन्दिते॥२२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pīṭhādikaṁ bhrametsiddhyai nānyathā vīravandite||22||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v23" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्प्रदेशं समासाद्य मन्त्रैरात्मानमादरात्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatpradeśaṁ samāsādya mantrairātmānamādarāt|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विद्यया वेष्टयेत्स्थानं रक्तसूत्रसमानया॥२३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vidyayā veṣṭayetsthānaṁ raktasūtrasamānayā||23||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v24" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बहुधानन्यचित्तस्तु सबाह्याभ्यन्तरं बुधः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bahudhānanyacittastu sabāhyābhyantaraṁ budhaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततस्तत्र क्वचित्क्षेत्रे योगिन्या भीमविक्रमाः॥२४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatastatra kvacitkṣetre yoginyā bhīmavikramāḥ||24||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v25" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">समागत्य प्रयच्छन्ति सम्प्रदायं स्वकं स्वकम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Samāgatya prayacchanti sampradāyaṁ svakaṁ svakam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">येनासौ लब्धमात्रेण सम्प्रदायेन सुब्रते॥२५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yenāsau labdhamātreṇa sampradāyena subrate||25||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v26" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्समानबलो भूत्वा भुङ्क्ते भोगान्यथेप्सितान्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatsamānabalo bhūtvā bhuṅkte bhogānyathepsitān|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथवा कृतसेवस्तु लक्षमेकं जपेत्सुधीः॥२६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Athavā kṛtasevastu lakṣamekaṁ japetsudhīḥ||26||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v27" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तर्पयित्वा दशांशेन क्षुद्रकर्मसु योजयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tarpayitvā daśāṁśena kṣudrakarmasu yojayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्रोच्चारितमात्रेयं विषक्षयकरीं भवेत्॥२७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatroccāritamātreyaṁ viṣakṣayakarīṁ bhavet||27||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v28" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चक्रवद्भ्रममाणैषा योनौ रक्तां विचिन्तयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Cakravadbhramamāṇaiṣā yonau raktāṁ vicintayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">गमागमक्रमाद्वापि विन्द … … … वारिता॥२८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Gamāgamakramādvāpi vinda … … … vāritā||28||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v29" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्रस्थश्चाशु सङ्घातविघाताकुञ्चनेन तु।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatrasthaścāśu saṅghātavighātākuñcanena tu|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">क्षणादनन्यचित्तस्तु क्षोभयेदुर्वशीमपि॥२९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kṣaṇādananyacittastu kṣobhayedurvaśīmapi||29||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v30" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कृतसेवाविधिर्वाथ लक्षत्रयजपेन तु।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kṛtasevāvidhirvātha lakṣatrayajapena tu|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">महतीं श्रियमाधत्ते पद्मश्रीफलतर्पिता॥३०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mahatīṁ śriyamādhatte padmaśrīphalatarpitā||30||</span></p>

## Строфы 31–40 {#s4}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s4-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">षडुत्थासनसंस्थाना साधिताप्युक्तवर्त्मना।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ṣaḍutthāsanasaṁsthānā sādhitāpyuktavartmanā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वसिद्धिकरी देवी मन्त्रिणामुपजायते॥३१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvasiddhikarī devī mantriṇāmupajāyate||31||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v32" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शूलपद्मविधिं मुक्त्वा नवात्माद्यं च सप्तकम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śūlapadmavidhiṁ muktvā navātmādyaṁ ca saptakam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">षडुत्थमासनं दद्यात्सर्वचक्रविधौ बुधः॥३२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ṣaḍutthamāsanaṁ dadyātsarvacakravidhau budhaḥ||32||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v33" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मुद्रा च महती योज्या हृद्बीजेनोपचारकम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mudrā ca mahatī yojyā hṛdbījenopacārakam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथान्यत्सम्प्रवक्ष्यामि स्वप्नज्ञानमनुत्तमम्॥३३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Athānyatsampravakṣyāmi svapnajñānamanuttamam||33||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v34" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हृच्चक्रे तन्मयो भूत्वा रात्रौ रात्रावनन्यधीः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Hṛccakre tanmayo bhūtvā rātrau rātrāvananyadhīḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मासादूर्ध्वं महादेवि स्वप्ने यत्किञ्चिदीक्षते॥३४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Māsādūrdhvaṁ mahādevi svapne yatkiñcidīkṣate||34||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v35" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्तथ्यं जायते तस्य ध्यानयुक्तस्य योगिनः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tattathyaṁ jāyate tasya dhyānayuktasya yoginaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्रैव यदि कालस्य नियमेन रतो भवेत्॥३५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatraiva yadi kālasya niyamena rato bhavet||35||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v36" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदा प्रथमयामे तु वत्सरेण शुभाशुभम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadā prathamayāme tu vatsareṇa śubhāśubham|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">षट्त्रिमासेन क्रमशो द्वितीयादिष्वनुक्रमात्॥३६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ṣaṭtrimāsena kramaśo dvitīyādiṣvanukramāt||36||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v37" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अरुणोदयवेलायां दशाहेन फलं लभेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aruṇodayavelāyāṁ daśāhena phalaṁ labhet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सङ्कल्पपूर्वकेऽप्येवं परेषामात्मनोऽपि वा॥३७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Saṅkalpapūrvake'pyevaṁ pareṣāmātmano'pi vā||37||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v38" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">क्वचित्कार्ये समुत्पन्ने सुप्तज्ञानमुपाक्रमेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kvacitkārye samutpanne suptajñānamupākramet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इत्येतत्कथितं देवि सिद्धयोगीश्वरीमतम्॥३८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ityetatkathitaṁ devi siddhayogīśvarīmatam||38||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v39" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नातः परतरं ज्ञानं शिवाद्यवनिगोचरे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nātaḥ parataraṁ jñānaṁ śivādyavanigocare|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">य एवं तत्त्वतो वेद स शिवो नात्र संशयः॥३९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ya evaṁ tattvato veda sa śivo nātra saṁśayaḥ||39||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v40" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तस्य पादरजः मूर्ध्नि घृतं पापप्रशान्तये।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tasya pādarajaḥ mūrdhni ghṛtaṁ pāpapraśāntaye|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एतच्छ्रुत्वा महादेवी परं सन्तोषमागता॥४०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Etacchrutvā mahādevī paraṁ santoṣamāgatā||40||</span></p>

## Строфы 41–44.5 {#s5}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s5-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एवं क्षमापयामास प्रणिपत्य पुनः पुनः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ kṣamāpayāmāsa praṇipatya punaḥ punaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इति वः सर्वमाख्यातं मालिनीविजयोत्तरम्॥४१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Iti vaḥ sarvamākhyātaṁ mālinīvijayottaram||41||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v42" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ममैतत्कथितं देव्या योगामृतमनुत्तमम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mamaitatkathitaṁ devyā yogāmṛtamanuttamam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवद्भिरपि नाख्येयमशिष्याणामिदं महत्॥४२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavadbhirapi nākhyeyamaśiṣyāṇāmidaṁ mahat||42||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v43" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न चापि परशिष्याणामपरीक्ष्य प्रयत्नतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na cāpi paraśiṣyāṇāmaparīkṣya prayatnataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वथैतत्समाख्यातं योगाभ्यासरतात्मनाम्॥४३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvathaitatsamākhyātaṁ yogābhyāsaratātmanām||43||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v44" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रयतानां विनीतानां शिवैकार्पितचेतसाम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prayatānāṁ vinītānāṁ śivaikārpitacetasām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कार्त्तिकेयात्समासाद्य ज्ञानामृतमिदं महत्॥४४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kārttikeyātsamāsādya jñānāmṛtamidaṁ mahat||44||</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मुनयो योगमभ्यस्य परां सिद्धिमुपागताः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Munayo yogamabhyasya parāṁ siddhimupāgatāḥ|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इति श्रीमालिनीविजयोत्तरे तन्त्रे त्रयोविंशतितमोऽधिकारः॥२३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Iti śrīmālinīvijayottare tantre trayoviṁśatitamo'dhikāraḥ||23||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">समाप्तं चेदं मालिनीविजयोत्तरं नाम महातन्त्रम्॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Samāptaṁ cedaṁ mālinīvijayottaraṁ nāma mahātantram||</span></p>
