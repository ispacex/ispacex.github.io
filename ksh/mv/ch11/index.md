<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथातः सम्प्रवक्ष्यामि दीक्षां परमदुर्लभाम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Athātaḥ sampravakṣyāmi dīkṣāṁ paramadurlabhām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भुक्तिमुक्तिकरीं सम्यक्सद्यः प्रत्ययकारिकाम्॥१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhuktimuktikarīṁ samyaksadyaḥ pratyayakārikām||1||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नास्यां मण्डलकुण्डादि किञ्चिदप्युपयुज्यते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nāsyāṁ maṇḍalakuṇḍādi kiñcidapyupayujyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न च न्यासादिकं पूर्वं स्नानादि च यथेच्छया॥२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na ca nyāsādikaṁ pūrvaṁ snānādi ca yathecchayā||2||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रविश्य यागसदनं सूपलिप्तं सुचर्चितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Praviśya yāgasadanaṁ sūpaliptaṁ sucarcitam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्राङ्मुखोदङ्मुखो वापि सुपुष्पाम्बरभूषितः॥३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prāṅmukhodaṅmukho vāpi supuṣpāmbarabhūṣitaḥ||3||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दीप्तां शक्तिमनुस्मृत्य पादाग्रान्मस्तकावधि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dīptāṁ śaktimanusmṛtya pādāgrānmastakāvadhi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">महामुद्राप्रयोगेन निर्दग्धां चिन्तयेत्तनुम्॥४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mahāmudrāprayogena nirdagdhāṁ cintayettanum||4||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अनुलोमप्रयोगाच्च मालिनीममृतप्रभाम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anulomaprayogācca mālinīmamṛtaprabhām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चिन्तयेत्तनुनिष्पत्त्यै तद्ध्यानगतमानसः॥५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Cintayettanuniṣpattyai taddhyānagatamānasaḥ||5||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शोध्याध्वानं ततो देहे पूर्वोक्तमनुचिन्तयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śodhyādhvānaṁ tato dehe pūrvoktamanucintayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततः संशोध्य वस्तूनि शक्त्यैवामृततां नयेत्॥६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tataḥ saṁśodhya vastūni śaktyaivāmṛtatāṁ nayet||6||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">परासम्पुटमध्यस्थां मालिनीं सर्वकर्मसु।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Parāsampuṭamadhyasthāṁ mālinīṁ sarvakarmasu|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">योजयेत विधानज्ञः परां वा केवलाम्प्रिये॥७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yojayeta vidhānajñaḥ parāṁ vā kevalāmpriye||7||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">गणेशं पूजयित्वा तु पुरा विघ्नप्रशान्तये।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Gaṇeśaṁ pūjayitvā tu purā vighnapraśāntaye|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततः स्वगुरुमारभ्य पूजयेद्गुरुपद्धतिम्॥८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tataḥ svagurumārabhya pūjayedgurupaddhatim||8||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">गणेशाधस्ततः सर्वं यजेन्मन्त्रकदम्बकम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Gaṇeśādhastataḥ sarvaṁ yajenmantrakadambakam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्पतीनां ततोऽर्धं च तत्रैव परिपूजयेत्॥९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatpatīnāṁ tato'rdhaṁ ca tatraiva paripūjayet||9||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अन्त्याधः पूजयेद्विद्यां तद्वद्विद्यां महेश्वरीम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Antyādhaḥ pūjayedvidyāṁ tadvadvidyāṁ maheśvarīm|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मन्त्रविद्यागणस्यान्तः कुलशक्तिं निवेशयेत्॥१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mantravidyāgaṇasyāntaḥ kulaśaktiṁ niveśayet||10||</span></p>

## Строфы 11–20 {#s2}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s2-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूर्वयाम्यापरोदक्षु माहेश्यादिचतुष्टयम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūrvayāmyāparodakṣu māheśyādicatuṣṭayam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इन्द्राणीपूर्वकं तद्वदैशादिदल अन्तगम्॥११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Indrāṇīpūrvakaṁ tadvadaiśādidala antagam||11||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्रोपरि न्यसेद्देवं कूटरूपाणुनामुना।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatropari nyaseddevaṁ kūṭarūpāṇunāmunā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जीवं दण्डसमाक्रान्तं शूलस्योपरि संस्थितम्॥१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jīvaṁ daṇḍasamākrāntaṁ śūlasyopari saṁsthitam||12||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दक्षाङ्गुलि ततोऽधस्तात्ततो वामपयोधरम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dakṣāṅguli tato'dhastāttato vāmapayodharam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नाभिकण्ठौ ततोऽधस्ताद्वामस्कन्धविभूषणौ॥१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nābhikaṇṭhau tato'dhastādvāmaskandhavibhūṣaṇau||13||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शिवजिह्वान्वितः पश्चात्तदूर्ध्वोष्ठेन चोपरि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śivajihvānvitaḥ paścāttadūrdhvoṣṭhena copari|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वयोगिनीचक्राणामधिपोऽयमुदाहृतः॥१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvayoginīcakrāṇāmadhipo'yamudāhṛtaḥ||14||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अस्याप्युच्चारणादेव संवित्तिः स्यात्परोदिता।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Asyāpyuccāraṇādeva saṁvittiḥ syātparoditā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततो वीराष्टकं पश्चाच्छक्त्युक्तविधिना यजेत्॥१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tato vīrāṣṭakaṁ paścācchaktyuktavidhinā yajet||15||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रभूतैर्विविधैरिष्ट्वा गन्धधूपैः स्रगादिभिः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prabhūtairvividhairiṣṭvā gandhadhūpaiḥ sragādibhiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">श्रीकारपूर्वकं नाम पादान्तं परिकल्पयेत्॥१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śrīkārapūrvakaṁ nāma pādāntaṁ parikalpayet||16||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततः शिष्यं समाहूय बहुधा सुपरीक्षितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tataḥ śiṣyaṁ samāhūya bahudhā suparīkṣitam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">रुद्रशक्त्या तु सम्प्रोक्ष्य देवाग्रे विनिवेशयेत्॥१७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Rudraśaktyā tu samprokṣya devāgre viniveśayet||17||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भूजौ तस्य समालोक्य रुद्रशक्त्या प्रदीपयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhūjau tasya samālokya rudraśaktyā pradīpayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तथैवाप्यर्पयेत्पुष्पं करयोर्गन्धदिग्धयोः॥१८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tathaivāpyarpayetpuṣpaṁ karayorgandhadigdhayoḥ||18||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">निरालम्बौ तु तौ ध्यात्वा शक्त्याकृष्टौ विचिन्तयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nirālambau tu tau dhyātvā śaktyākṛṣṭau vicintayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शक्तिमन्त्रितनेत्रेण बद्ध्वा नेत्रे तु पूर्ववत्॥१९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śaktimantritanetreṇa baddhvā netre tu pūrvavat||19||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततः प्रक्षेपयेत्पुष्पं सा शक्तिस्तत्करस्थिता।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tataḥ prakṣepayetpuṣpaṁ sā śaktistatkarasthitā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यत्र तत्पतते पुष्पं तत्कुलं तस्य लक्षयेत्॥२०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yatra tatpatate puṣpaṁ tatkulaṁ tasya lakṣayet||20||</span></p>

## Строфы 21–30 {#s3}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s3-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मुखमुद्घाट्य तं पश्चात्पादयोः प्रतिपातयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mukhamudghāṭya taṁ paścātpādayoḥ pratipātayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततोऽस्य मस्तके चक्रं हस्तयोश्चार्च्य योगवित्॥२१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tato'sya mastake cakraṁ hastayoścārcya yogavit||21||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v22" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तद्धस्तौ प्रेरयेच्छक्त्या यावन्मूर्धान्तमागतौ।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Taddhastau prerayecchaktyā yāvanmūrdhāntamāgatau|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शिवहस्तविधिः प्रोक्तः सद्यः प्रत्ययकारकः॥२२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śivahastavidhiḥ proktaḥ sadyaḥ pratyayakārakaḥ||22||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v23" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चरुकं दापयेत्पश्चात् खर्जूरादिफलोद्भवम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Carukaṁ dāpayetpaścāt kharjūrādiphalodbhavam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शक्त्यालम्बां तनुं कृत्वा स्थापयेदग्रतः शिशोः॥२३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śaktyālambāṁ tanuṁ kṛtvā sthāpayedagrataḥ śiśoḥ||23||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v24" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पुष्पक्षेपप्रयोगेन हस्तमाकृस्य दक्षिणम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Puṣpakṣepaprayogena hastamākṛsya dakṣiṇam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चरुकं ग्राहयेन्मन्त्री तद्ध्यानगतमानसः॥२४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Carukaṁ grāhayenmantrī taddhyānagatamānasaḥ||24||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v25" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शिवहस्तप्रयोगेन समारोप्य मुखं नयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śivahastaprayogena samāropya mukhaṁ nayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अनेनैव विधानेन क्षीरवृक्षसमुद्भवम्॥२५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anenaiva vidhānena kṣīravṛkṣasamudbhavam||25||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v26" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दन्तकाष्ठं ददेद्देवि षोडशाङ्गुलमायतम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dantakāṣṭhaṁ dadeddevi ṣoḍaśāṅgulamāyatam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एतेषां चालनान्मन्त्री शक्तिपातं परीक्षयेत्॥२६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Eteṣāṁ cālanānmantrī śaktipātaṁ parīkṣayet||26||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v27" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मन्दतीव्रादिभेदेन मन्दतीव्रादिकाद्बुधः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mandatīvrādibhedena mandatīvrādikādbudhaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इत्ययं समयी प्रोक्तः संस्थितोक्तेन वर्त्मना॥२७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ityayaṁ samayī proktaḥ saṁsthitoktena vartmanā||27||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v28" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चिकीर्षुश्च यदा दीक्षामस्यैवार्पितमानसः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Cikīrṣuśca yadā dīkṣāmasyaivārpitamānasaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदिष्ट्वा पूर्ववद्योगी कुलेशं तमनुक्रमात्॥२८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadiṣṭvā pūrvavadyogī kuleśaṁ tamanukramāt||28||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v29" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सम्पूज्य पूर्ववच्छिष्यमृजुदेहे विलोकयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sampūjya pūrvavacchiṣyamṛjudehe vilokayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शक्तिं सञ्चिन्त्य पादाग्रान्मस्तकान्तं विचक्षणः॥२९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śaktiṁ sañcintya pādāgrānmastakāntaṁ vicakṣaṇaḥ||29||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v30" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शोध्याध्वानं ततो न्यस्य सर्वाध्वव्याप्तिभावताम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śodhyādhvānaṁ tato nyasya sarvādhvavyāptibhāvatām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शक्तितत्त्वादिभेदेन पूर्वोक्तेन च वर्त्मना॥३०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śaktitattvādibhedena pūrvoktena ca vartmanā||30||</span></p>

## Строфы 31–40 {#s4}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s4-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उपविश्य ततस्तस्य विधानमिदमाचरेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Upaviśya tatastasya vidhānamidamācaret|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मूलशोध्यात्समारभ्य शक्तिं दीप्तानलप्रभाम्॥३१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mūlaśodhyātsamārabhya śaktiṁ dīptānalaprabhām||31||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v32" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">योजयेच्छोध्यसंशुद्धिभावनागतमानसः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yojayecchodhyasaṁśuddhibhāvanāgatamānasaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एवं सर्वाणि शोध्यानि निर्दहन्तीमनामयाम्॥३२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ sarvāṇi śodhyāni nirdahantīmanāmayām||32||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v33" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शिवे सञ्चिन्तयेल्लीनां निष्कले सकलेऽपि वा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śive sañcintayellīnāṁ niṣkale sakale'pi vā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">योगिना योजिता मार्गे स्वजातीयस्य पोषणम्॥३३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yoginā yojitā mārge svajātīyasya poṣaṇam||33||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v34" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कुरुते निर्दहत्यन्यद्भिन्नजातिकदम्बकम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kurute nirdahatyanyadbhinnajātikadambakam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अनया शोध्यमानस्य शिष्यस्यास्य महामतिः॥३४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anayā śodhyamānasya śiṣyasyāsya mahāmatiḥ||34||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v35" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">लक्षयेच्चिह्नसङ्घातमानन्दादिकमादरात्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Lakṣayeccihnasaṅghātamānandādikamādarāt|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आनन्द उद्भवः कम्पो निद्रा घूर्णिश्च पञ्चमी॥३५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ānanda udbhavaḥ kampo nidrā ghūrṇiśca pañcamī||35||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v36" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एवमाविष्ट्या शक्त्या मन्दतीव्रादिभेदतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evamāviṣṭyā śaktyā mandatīvrādibhedataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पाशस्तोभपशुग्राहौ प्रकुर्वीत यथेच्छया॥३६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pāśastobhapaśugrāhau prakurvīta yathecchayā||36||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v37" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">गृहीतस्य पुनः कुर्यान्नियोगं शेषभुक्तये।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Gṛhītasya punaḥ kuryānniyogaṁ śeṣabhuktaye|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथवा कस्यचिन्नायमावेशः सम्प्रजायते॥३७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Athavā kasyacinnāyamāveśaḥ samprajāyate||37||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v38" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदेनं युगपच्छक्त्या सबाह्याभ्यन्तरे दहेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadenaṁ yugapacchaktyā sabāhyābhyantare dahet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तया सन्दह्यमानोऽसौ च्छिन्नमूल इव द्रुमः॥३८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tayā sandahyamāno'sau cchinnamūla iva drumaḥ||38||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v39" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पतते काश्यपीपृष्ठे आक्षेपं वा करोत्यसौ।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Patate kāśyapīpṛṣṭhe ākṣepaṁ vā karotyasau|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यस्य त्वेवमपि स्यान्न तं चैवोपलवत्त्यजेत्॥३९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yasya tvevamapi syānna taṁ caivopalavattyajet||39||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v40" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पृथक्तत्त्वविधौ दीक्षां योग्यतावश्यवर्तिनः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pṛthaktattvavidhau dīkṣāṁ yogyatāvaśyavartinaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्त्वाभ्यासविधानेन वक्ष्यमाणेन कारयेत्॥४०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tattvābhyāsavidhānena vakṣyamāṇena kārayet||40||</span></p>

## Строфы 41–47 {#s5}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s5-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इति सन्दीक्षितस्यास्य मुमुक्षोः शेषवर्तनम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Iti sandīkṣitasyāsya mumukṣoḥ śeṣavartanam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कुलक्रमेष्टिरादेश्या पञ्चावस्थासमन्विता॥४१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kulakrameṣṭirādeśyā pañcāvasthāsamanvitā||41||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v42" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बुहुक्षोस्तु प्रकुर्वीत सम्यग्योगाभिषेचनम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Buhukṣostu prakurvīta samyagyogābhiṣecanam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्रेष्ट्वा पूर्ववद्देहं विस्तीर्णैर्विबुधैर्बुधैः॥४२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatreṣṭvā pūrvavaddehaṁ vistīrṇairvibudhairbudhaiḥ||42||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v43" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हेमादिदीपकानष्टौ घृतेनारुणवर्तिकान्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Hemādidīpakānaṣṭau ghṛtenāruṇavartikān|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कुलाष्टकेन सम्बोध्य शिवं शङ्खे प्रपूजयेत्॥४३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kulāṣṭakena sambodhya śivaṁ śaṅkhe prapūjayet||43||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v44" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वरत्नौषधीगर्भे गन्धाम्बुपरिपूरिते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvaratnauṣadhīgarbhe gandhāmbuparipūrite|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तेनाभिषेचयेत्तं तु शिवहस्तोक्तवर्त्मना॥४४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tenābhiṣecayettaṁ tu śivahastoktavartmanā||44||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v45" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आचार्यस्याभिषेकोऽयमधिकारपदान्वितः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ācāryasyābhiṣeko'yamadhikārapadānvitaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कुर्यात्पिण्डादिभिस्तद्वच्चतुःषष्टिं प्रदीपकान्॥४५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kuryātpiṇḍādibhistadvaccatuḥṣaṣṭiṁ pradīpakān||45||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v46" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अभिषिक्तविधावेव सर्वयोगिगणेन तु।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Abhiṣiktavidhāveva sarvayogigaṇena tu|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विदितौ भवतस्तत्र गुरुर्मोक्षप्रदो भवेत्॥४६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Viditau bhavatastatra gururmokṣaprado bhavet||46||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v47" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अनयोः कथयेज्ज्ञानं त्रिविधं सर्वमप्यलम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anayoḥ kathayejjñānaṁ trividhaṁ sarvamapyalam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वकीयाज्ञां ददेद्योगी स्वक्रियाकरणं प्रति॥४७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svakīyājñāṁ dadedyogī svakriyākaraṇaṁ prati||47||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इति श्रीमालिनीविजयोत्तरे तन्त्रे दीक्षाधिकार एकादशः॥११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Iti śrīmālinīvijayottare tantre dīkṣādhikāra ekādaśaḥ||11||</span></p>
