## Строфы 1–10 {#s1}

<p class="pv-copy nosearch pv-anchor" id="s1-sa"><button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-sa">Копировать санскрит</button> <button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-iast">Копировать транслитерацию</button></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथ सप्तदशोऽधिकारः</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Atha Saptadaśo'dhikāraḥ</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथैतत्सर्वमुद्दिष्टं यदि न स्फुटतां व्रजेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Athaitatsarvamuddiṣṭaṁ yadi na sphuṭatāṁ vrajet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्फुटीकृते स्थिते तत्र न मनस्तिष्ठते स्फुटम्॥१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sphuṭīkṛte sthite tatra na manastiṣṭhate sphuṭam||1||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">गतिभङ्गं ततस्तस्य प्राणायामेन कारयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Gatibhaṅgaṁ tatastasya prāṇāyāmena kārayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स च पञ्चविधः प्रोक्तः पूरकादिप्रभेदतः॥२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sa ca pañcavidhaḥ proktaḥ pūrakādiprabhedataḥ||2||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूरकः कुम्भकश्चैव रेचको ह्यपकर्षकः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūrakaḥ kumbhakaścaiva recako hyapakarṣakaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उत्कर्षः पञ्चमो ज्ञेयस्तदभ्यासाय योगिभिः॥३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Utkarṣaḥ pañcamo jñeyastadabhyāsāya yogibhiḥ||3||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूरकः पूरणाद्वायोर्द्वेधा षोढा च गीयते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūrakaḥ pūraṇādvāyordvedhā ṣoḍhā ca gīyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वभावपूरणादेको विरेच्यान्यः प्रपूरितः॥४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svabhāvapūraṇādeko virecyānyaḥ prapūritaḥ||4||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नासामुखोर्ध्वतालूनां रन्ध्रभेदाद्विभिद्यते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nāsāmukhordhvatālūnāṁ randhrabhedādvibhidyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भिन्नः षोढात्वमभ्येति पुनर्भेदैरनन्तताम्॥५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhinnaḥ ṣoḍhātvamabhyeti punarbhedairanantatām||5||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कुम्भः पञ्चविधो ज्ञेयस्तत्रैकः पुरितादनु।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kumbhaḥ pañcavidho jñeyastatraikaḥ puritādanu|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विधृतो रेचकात्पश्चाद्द्वितीयः परिकीर्तितः॥६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vidhṛto recakātpaścāddvitīyaḥ parikīrtitaḥ||6||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">द्वयोरन्ते द्वयं चान्यत्स्वभावस्थश्च पञ्चमः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dvayorante dvayaṁ cānyatsvabhāvasthaśca pañcamaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्थानान्तरप्रभेदेन गच्छत्येषोऽप्यनन्तताम्॥७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sthānāntaraprabhedena gacchatyeṣo'pyanantatām||7||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">रेचकः पूर्ववज्ज्ञेयो द्विधाभूतः षडात्मकः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Recakaḥ pūrvavajjñeyo dvidhābhūtaḥ ṣaḍātmakaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्थानसंस्तम्भितो वायुस्तस्मादुत्कृष्य नीयते॥८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sthānasaṁstambhito vāyustasmādutkṛṣya nīyate||8||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">योऽन्यप्रदेशसम्प्राप्त्यै स उत्कर्षक इष्यते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yo'nyapradeśasamprāptyai sa utkarṣaka iṣyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तस्मादपि पुनः स्थानं यतो नीतस्तदाहृतः॥९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tasmādapi punaḥ sthānaṁ yato nītastadāhṛtaḥ||9||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अपकर्षक इत्युक्तो द्वावप्येतावनेकधा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Apakarṣaka ityukto dvāvapyetāvanekadhā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एषामभ्यसनं कुर्यात्पद्मकाद्यासनस्थितः॥१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Eṣāmabhyasanaṁ kuryātpadmakādyāsanasthitaḥ||10||</span></p>

## Строфы 11–20 {#s2}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s2-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अधमः सकृदुद्घातो मध्यमः सिद्धिदो मतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Adhamaḥ sakṛdudghāto madhyamaḥ siddhido mataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ज्येष्ठः स्याद्यस्त्रिरुद्घातः स च द्वादशमात्रकः॥११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jyeṣṭhaḥ syādyastrirudghātaḥ sa ca dvādaśamātrakaḥ||11||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्रिर्जानुवेष्टनान्मात्रात्रिगुणछोटिकात्रयात्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Trirjānuveṣṭanānmātrātriguṇachoṭikātrayāt|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अजितां नाक्रमेन्मात्रां वायुदोषनिवृत्तये॥१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ajitāṁ nākramenmātrāṁ vāyudoṣanivṛttaye||12||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रत्यङ्गधारणाद्वायुं न च चक्षुषि धारयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pratyaṅgadhāraṇādvāyuṁ na ca cakṣuṣi dhārayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नाभिहृत्तालुकण्ठस्थे विवृते मरुति क्रमात्॥१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nābhihṛttālukaṇṭhasthe vivṛte maruti kramāt||13||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चतस्रो धारणा ज्ञेया शिख्यम्व्वीशामृतात्मिकाः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Catasro dhāraṇā jñeyā śikhyamvvīśāmṛtātmikāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यद्यत्र चिन्तयेद्द्रव्यं तत्तत्सर्वगतं स्मरेत्॥१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yadyatra cintayeddravyaṁ tattatsarvagataṁ smaret||14||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बिन्दुनादात्मकं रूपमीशानीं धारणां श्रितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bindunādātmakaṁ rūpamīśānīṁ dhāraṇāṁ śritam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अमृतायाः स्मरेदिन्दुं कालत्यागोक्तवर्त्मना॥१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Amṛtāyāḥ smaredinduṁ kālatyāgoktavartmanā||15||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">धारणाभिरिहैताभिर्योगी योगपथे स्थितः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dhāraṇābhirihaitābhiryogī yogapathe sthitaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हेयं वस्तु परित्यज्य यायात्पदमनुत्तमम्॥१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Heyaṁ vastu parityajya yāyātpadamanuttamam||16||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्रिवेदद्बीन्दुसङ्ख्यातसमुद्घातास्त्विमा मताः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Trivedadbīndusaṅkhyātasamudghātāstvimā matāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एताभिरप्यधोऽप्युक्तं फलं प्राप्नोत्यनुत्तमम्॥१७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Etābhirapyadho'pyuktaṁ phalaṁ prāpnotyanuttamam||17||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">योगाङ्गत्वे समानेऽपि तर्को योगाङ्गमुत्तमम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yogāṅgatve samāne'pi tarko yogāṅgamuttamam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हेयाद्यालोचना तस्मात्तत्र यत्नः प्रशस्यते॥१८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Heyādyālocanā tasmāttatra yatnaḥ praśasyate||18||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मार्गे चेतः स्थिरीभूते हेयेऽपि विषयेच्छया।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mārge cetaḥ sthirībhūte heye'pi viṣayecchayā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रेर्यं तेनानयेत्तावद्यावत्पदमनामयम्॥१९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Preryaṁ tenānayettāvadyāvatpadamanāmayam||19||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदर्थभावनायुक्तं मनोध्यानमुदाहृतम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadarthabhāvanāyuktaṁ manodhyānamudāhṛtam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदेव परमं ज्ञानं भावनामयमिष्यते॥२०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadeva paramaṁ jñānaṁ bhāvanāmayamiṣyate||20||</span></p>

## Строфы 21–30 {#s3}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s3-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मुहूर्तादेव तत्रस्थः समाधिं प्रतिपद्यते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Muhūrtādeva tatrasthaḥ samādhiṁ pratipadyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्रापि च सुनिष्पन्ने फलं प्राप्नोत्यभीप्सितम्॥२१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatrāpi ca suniṣpanne phalaṁ prāpnotyabhīpsitam||21||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v22" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यत्किञ्चिच्चिन्तयेद्वस्तु नान्यत्वं प्रतिपद्यते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yatkiñciccintayedvastu nānyatvaṁ pratipadyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तेन तन्मयतामाप्य भवेत्पश्चादभाववत्॥२२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tena tanmayatāmāpya bhavetpaścādabhāvavat||22||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v23" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पञ्चतामिव सम्प्राप्तस्तीव्रैरपि न चाल्यते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pañcatāmiva samprāptastīvrairapi na cālyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततः शब्दादिभिर्योगी योगिनीकुलनन्दनः॥२३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tataḥ śabdādibhiryogī yoginīkulanandanaḥ||23||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v24" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इत्यनेन विधानेन प्रत्याहृत्य मनो मुहुः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ityanena vidhānena pratyāhṛtya mano muhuḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्राणायामादिकं सर्वं कुर्याद्योगप्रसिद्धये॥२४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prāṇāyāmādikaṁ sarvaṁ kuryādyogaprasiddhaye||24||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v25" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वमप्यथवा भोगं मन्यमानो विरूपकम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvamapyathavā bhogaṁ manyamāno virūpakam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वशरीरं परित्यज्य शाश्वतं पदमृच्छति॥२५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svaśarīraṁ parityajya śāśvataṁ padamṛcchati||25||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v26" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदा पूर्वोदितं न्यासं कालानलसमप्रभम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadā pūrvoditaṁ nyāsaṁ kālānalasamaprabham|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विपरीतविधानेन कुर्या … … द्वियुग्मताम्॥२६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Viparītavidhānena kuryā … … dviyugmatām||26||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v27" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आग्नेयीं धारणां कृत्वा सर्वमर्मप्रतापिनीम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Āgneyīṁ dhāraṇāṁ kṛtvā sarvamarmapratāpinīm|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूरयेद्वायुना देहमङ्गुष्ठान्मस्तकान्तिकम्॥२७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūrayedvāyunā dehamaṅguṣṭhānmastakāntikam||27||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v28" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तमुत्कृष्य ततोऽङ्गुष्ठाद्ब्रह्मरन्ध्रान्तमानयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tamutkṛṣya tato'ṅguṣṭhādbrahmarandhrāntamānayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">छेदयेत्सर्वमर्माणि मन्त्रेनानेन योगवित्॥२८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Chedayetsarvamarmāṇi mantrenānena yogavit||28||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v29" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जीवमादिद्विजारूढं शिरोमालादिसंयुतम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jīvamādidvijārūḍhaṁ śiromālādisaṁyutam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कृत्वा तदग्रे कुर्वीत द्विजमाद्यमजीवकम्॥२९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kṛtvā tadagre kurvīta dvijamādyamajīvakam||29||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v30" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इत्येषा कथिता कालरात्रिर्मर्मनिकृन्तनी।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ityeṣā kathitā kālarātrirmarmanikṛntanī|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नैनां समुच्चरेद्देवि य इच्छेद्दीर्घजीवितम्॥३०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Naināṁ samuccareddevi ya iccheddīrghajīvitam||30||</span></p>

## Строфы 31–40 {#s4}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s4-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शतार्धोच्चारयोगेन जायते मूर्ध्नि वेदना।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śatārdhoccārayogena jāyate mūrdhni vedanā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एवं प्रत्ययमालोच्य मृत्युजिद्ध्यानमाश्रयेत्॥३१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ pratyayamālocya mṛtyujiddhyānamāśrayet||31||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v32" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">निपीड्य तं ततस्त्रत्र बिन्दुनादादिचिन्तकम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nipīḍya taṁ tatastratra bindunādādicintakam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वेगादुत्कृष्य तत्रस्थकालरात्रीं विसर्जयेत्॥३२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vegādutkṛṣya tatrasthakālarātrīṁ visarjayet||32||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v33" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">… … … सिद्धयोगीश्वरी मते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">… … … siddhayogīśvarī mate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्सकाशाद्भवेत्सिद्धिः सर्वमन्त्रोक्तलक्षणा॥३३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatsakāśādbhavetsiddhiḥ sarvamantroktalakṣaṇā||33||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v34" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदेव मन्त्ररूपेण म[नुष्यैः] समुपास्यते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadeva mantrarūpeṇa ma[nuṣyaiḥ] samupāsyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एष ते ज्ञेयसद्भावः कथितः सुरवन्दिते॥३४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Eṣa te jñeyasadbhāvaḥ kathitaḥ suravandite||34||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v35" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अभक्तस्य गुहस्यापि नाख्येयो जातुचिन्मया।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Abhaktasya guhasyāpi nākhyeyo jātucinmayā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उदरं सर्वमापूर्य ब्रह्मरन्ध्रान्तमागतम्॥३५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Udaraṁ sarvamāpūrya brahmarandhrāntamāgatam||35||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v36" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वायुं भ्रमणयोगेन ततस्तं प्रेरयेत्तथा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vāyuṁ bhramaṇayogena tatastaṁ prerayettathā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यावत्प्राणप्रदेशान्तं योगिनां मनसेप्सितम्॥३६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yāvatprāṇapradeśāntaṁ yogināṁ manasepsitam||36||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v37" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">व्याप्यते पुनरा [वृत्य] तथैव नाभिमण्डलम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vyāpyate punarā [vṛtya] tathaiva nābhimaṇḍalam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एवं समभ्यसेत्तावद्यावद्वासरसप्तकम्॥३७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ samabhyasettāvadyāvadvāsarasaptakam||37||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v38" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदाप्रभृति संयुक्तः कर्षयेत्त्रिदशानपि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadāprabhṛti saṁyuktaḥ karṣayettridaśānapi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अनेनाकृष्य विज्ञानं सर्वयोगिनिषेवितम्॥३८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anenākṛṣya vijñānaṁ sarvayoginiṣevitam||38||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v39" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">गृह्णीयाद्योगयुक्तात्मा किमन्यैः क्षुद्रशासनैः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Gṛhṇīyādyogayuktātmā kimanyaiḥ kṣudraśāsanaiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रथमं महती घूर्णिरभ्यासात्तस्य जायते॥३९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prathamaṁ mahatī ghūrṇirabhyāsāttasya jāyate||39||</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततः प्रकम्पो देवेशि ज्वलतीव ततोऽप्यणुः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tataḥ prakampo deveśi jvalatīva tato'pyaṇuḥ|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इति श्रीमालिनीविजयोत्तरे तन्त्रे सप्तदशोऽधिकारः॥१७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Iti śrīmālinīvijayottare tantre saptadaśo'dhikāraḥ||17||</span></p>
