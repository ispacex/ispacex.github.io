## Строфы 1–10 {#s1}

<p class="pv-copy nosearch pv-anchor" id="s1-sa"><button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-sa">Копировать санскрит</button> <button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-iast">Копировать транслитерацию</button></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथ अष्टमोऽधिकारः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Atha Aṣṭamo'dhikāraḥ|</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथातः सम्प्रवक्ष्यामि यजनं सर्वकामदम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Athātaḥ sampravakṣyāmi yajanaṁ sarvakāmadam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यस्य दर्शनमात्रेण योगिनीसंमतो भवेत्॥१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yasya darśanamātreṇa yoginīsaṁmato bhavet||1||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्रादौ यागसदनं शुभक्षेत्रे मनोरमम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatrādau yāgasadanaṁ śubhakṣetre manoramam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कारयेदग्निकुण्डेन वर्तुलेन समन्वितम्॥२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kārayedagnikuṇḍena vartulena samanvitam||2||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पञ्चविंशतिपर्वेण समन्तादर्धनाभिना।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pañcaviṁśatiparveṇa samantādardhanābhinā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तुर्यांशमेखलेनापि पर्वौष्ठेन सुशोभिना॥३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Turyāṁśamekhalenāpi parvauṣṭhena suśobhinā||3||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततः स्नात्वा जितद्वन्द्वो भावस्नानेन मन्त्रवित्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tataḥ snātvā jitadvandvo bhāvasnānena mantravit|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तच्च षड्विधमुद्दिष्टं भस्मस्नानाद्यनुक्रमात्॥४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tacca ṣaḍvidhamuddiṣṭaṁ bhasmasnānādyanukramāt||4||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भस्मस्नानं महास्त्रेण भस्म सप्ताभिमन्त्रितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhasmasnānaṁ mahāstreṇa bhasma saptābhimantritam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मलस्नानाय संहारक्रमेणोद्धूलयेत्तनुम्॥५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Malasnānāya saṁhārakrameṇoddhūlayettanum||5||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विद्याङ्गैः पञ्चभिः पश्चाच्छिरः प्रभृति गुण्ठयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vidyāṅgaiḥ pañcabhiḥ paścācchiraḥ prabhṛti guṇṭhayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अभिषेकं तु कुर्वीत मूलेनैव षडङ्गिना॥६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Abhiṣekaṁ tu kurvīta mūlenaiva ṣaḍaṅginā||6||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततोऽवासाः सुवासा वा हस्तौ पादौ च धावयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tato'vāsāḥ suvāsā vā hastau pādau ca dhāvayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आचम्य मार्जनं कुर्याद्विद्यया भूरिवर्णया॥७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ācamya mārjanaṁ kuryādvidyayā bhūrivarṇayā||7||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न्यासं कृत्वा तु सामान्यमघमर्षं द्वितीयया।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nyāsaṁ kṛtvā tu sāmānyamaghamarṣaṁ dvitīyayā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उपस्थानं च मालिन्या जपेच्चैकाक्षरां पराम्॥८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Upasthānaṁ ca mālinyā japeccaikākṣarāṁ parām||8||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जलस्नानेऽपि चास्त्रेण मृदं सप्ताभिमन्त्रिताम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jalasnāne'pi cāstreṇa mṛdaṁ saptābhimantritām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूर्ववत्तनुमालभ्य मलस्नानं समाचरेत्॥९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūrvavattanumālabhya malasnānaṁ samācaret||9||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विधिस्नानादिकं चात्र पूर्ववत् किन्तु वारिणा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vidhisnānādikaṁ cātra pūrvavat kintu vāriṇā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">साधारणविधिस्नातो विद्यात्रितयमन्त्रितम्॥१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sādhāraṇavidhisnāto vidyātritayamantritam||10||</span></p>

## Строфы 11–20 {#s2}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s2-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तोयं विनिक्षिपेन्मूर्ध्नि मन्त्रस्नानाय मन्त्रवित्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Toyaṁ vinikṣipenmūrdhni mantrasnānāya mantravit|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">रजसा गोधुतेनैव वायव्यं स्नानमाचरेत्॥११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Rajasā godhutenaiva vāyavyaṁ snānamācaret||11||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">महास्त्रमुच्चरन् गच्छेद्ध्यानयुक् पदसप्तकम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mahāstramuccaran gaccheddhyānayuk padasaptakam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदेव पुनरागच्छेदनुस्मृत्य परापराम्॥१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadeva punarāgacchedanusmṛtya parāparām||12||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वर्षातपसमायोगाद्दिव्योऽप्येवंविधो मतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Varṣātapasamāyogāddivyo'pyevaṁvidho mataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">किन्तु तत्र परां मन्त्री स्रवन्तीममृतं स्मरेत्॥१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kintu tatra parāṁ mantrī sravantīmamṛtaṁ smaret||13||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अस्त्रेणाङ्गुष्ठमूलात्तु वह्निमुत्थाप्य निर्दहेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Astreṇāṅguṣṭhamūlāttu vahnimutthāpya nirdahet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वतनुं प्लावयेत्पश्चात्परयैवामृतेन तु॥१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svatanuṁ plāvayetpaścātparayaivāmṛtena tu||14||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सूर्यादौ मन्त्रमादाय गच्छेदस्त्रमनुस्मरन्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sūryādau mantramādāya gacchedastramanusmaran|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यागवेश्मास्त्रसंशुद्धं विशेच्छुचिरनाकुलः॥१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yāgaveśmāstrasaṁśuddhaṁ viśecchuciranākulaḥ||15||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्र द्वारपतीन् पूज्य महास्त्रेणाभिमन्त्रितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatra dvārapatīn pūjya mahāstreṇābhimantritam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पुष्पं विनिक्षिपेद्ध्यात्वा ज्वलद्विघ्नप्रशान्तये॥१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Puṣpaṁ vinikṣipeddhyātvā jvaladvighnapraśāntaye||16||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दशस्वपि ततोऽस्त्रेण दिक्षु सङ्कल्प्य रक्षणम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Daśasvapi tato'streṇa dikṣu saṅkalpya rakṣaṇam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रविशेद्यागसदनं वह्निवद्वह्निसंयुतम्॥१७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Praviśedyāgasadanaṁ vahnivadvahnisaṁyutam||17||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूर्वास्यः सौम्यवक्त्रो वा विशेषन्यासमारभेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūrvāsyaḥ saumyavaktro vā viśeṣanyāsamārabhet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्रादावस्त्रमन्त्रेण कालानलसमत्विषा॥१८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatrādāvastramantreṇa kālānalasamatviṣā||18||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अङ्गुष्ठाग्रात्तनुं दग्धां सबाह्याभ्यन्तरां स्मरेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aṅguṣṭhāgrāttanuṁ dagdhāṁ sabāhyābhyantarāṁ smaret|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विकीर्यमाणं तद्भस्म ध्यात्वा कवचवायुना॥१९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vikīryamāṇaṁ tadbhasma dhyātvā kavacavāyunā||19||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शिवबिन्दुसमाकारमात्मानमनुचिन्तयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śivabindusamākāramātmānamanucintayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततोऽस्य योजयेच्छक्तिं सोऽहमित्यपराजितः॥२०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tato'sya yojayecchaktiṁ so'hamityaparājitaḥ||20||</span></p>

## Строфы 21–30 {#s3}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s3-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विद्यामूर्तिं ततो दध्यान्मन्त्रेणानेन शाङ्करि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vidyāmūrtiṁ tato dadhyānmantreṇānena śāṅkari|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दण्डाक्रान्तं महाप्राणं दण्डारूढं सनाभिकम्॥२१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Daṇḍākrāntaṁ mahāprāṇaṁ daṇḍārūḍhaṁ sanābhikam||21||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v22" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नितम्बं तदधस्ताच्च वामस्तनमधः पुनः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nitambaṁ tadadhastācca vāmastanamadhaḥ punaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कण्ठं च वामशिखरं वाममुद्राविभूषितम्॥२२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kaṇṭhaṁ ca vāmaśikharaṁ vāmamudrāvibhūṣitam||22||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v23" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बिन्द्वर्धचन्द्रखं नादशक्तिबिन्दुविभूषितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bindvardhacandrakhaṁ nādaśaktibinduvibhūṣitam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एष पिण्डवरो देवि नवात्मक इति श्रुतः॥२३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Eṣa piṇḍavaro devi navātmaka iti śrutaḥ||23||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v24" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वसिद्धिकरश्चायं सरहस्यमुदाहृतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvasiddhikaraścāyaṁ sarahasyamudāhṛtaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एष त्र्यर्णोज्झितोऽधस्ताद्दीर्घैः षड्भिः स्वरैर्युतः॥२४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Eṣa tryarṇojjhito'dhastāddīrghaiḥ ṣaḍbhiḥ svarairyutaḥ||24||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v25" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">षडङ्गानि हृदादीनि जातिभेदेन कल्पयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ṣaḍaṅgāni hṛdādīni jātibhedena kalpayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">क्षयरवलबीजैश्च दीप्तैर्बिन्दुविभूषितैः॥२५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kṣayaravalabījaiśca dīptairbinduvibhūṣitaiḥ||25||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v26" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वक्त्राणि कल्पयेत्पूर्वमूर्ध्ववक्त्रादितः क्रमात्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vaktrāṇi kalpayetpūrvamūrdhvavaktrāditaḥ kramāt|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रत्यङ्गविधिसिद्ध्यर्थं ललाटादिष्वथो न्यसेत्॥२६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pratyaṅgavidhisiddhyarthaṁ lalāṭādiṣvatho nyaset||26||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v27" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अ ललाटे द्वितीयं च वक्त्रे सम्परिकल्पयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">A lalāṭe dvitīyaṁ ca vaktre samparikalpayet|</span><br />

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v117" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूर्णां च पूर्ववद्दद्याच्छिवाग्नेरपरो विधिः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūrṇāṁ ca pūrvavaddadyācchivāgneraparo vidhiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शिवरूपं तमालोक्य तस्यात्मान्तः कृतिः क्रमात्॥११७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śivarūpaṁ tamālokya tasyātmāntaḥ kṛtiḥ kramāt||117||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v118" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कुर्यादन्तः कृतिं मन्त्री ततो होमं समारभेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kuryādantaḥ kṛtiṁ mantrī tato homaṁ samārabhet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मूलं शतेन सन्तर्प्य तदङ्गानि षडङ्गतः॥११८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mūlaṁ śatena santarpya tadaṅgāni ṣaḍaṅgataḥ||118||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v119" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शेषाणां मन्त्रजातीनां दशांशेनैव तर्पणम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śeṣāṇāṁ mantrajātīnāṁ daśāṁśenaiva tarpaṇam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततः प्रवेसयेच्छिष्याञ्शुचीन्स्नातानुपोषितान्॥११९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tataḥ pravesayecchiṣyāñśucīnsnātānupoṣitān||119||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v120" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रणम्य देवदेवेशं चतुष्टयगतं क्रमात्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Praṇamya devadeveśaṁ catuṣṭayagataṁ kramāt|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पञ्चगव्यं चरुं दद्याद्दन्तधावनमेव च॥१२०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pañcagavyaṁ caruṁ dadyāddantadhāvanameva ca||120||</span></p>

## Строфы 121–130 {#s13}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s13-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हृदयेन चरोः सिद्धिर्याज्ञिकैः क्षीरतण्डुलैः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Hṛdayena caroḥ siddhiryājñikaiḥ kṣīrataṇḍulaiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सम्पातं सप्तभिर्मन्त्रैस्ततः षड्भागभाजितम्॥१२१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sampātaṁ saptabhirmantraistataḥ ṣaḍbhāgabhājitam||121||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v122" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शिवाग्निगुरुशिष्याणां वार्धानीकुम्भयोः समम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śivāgniguruśiṣyāṇāṁ vārdhānīkumbhayoḥ samam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दन्तकाष्ठं ततो दद्यात्क्षीरवृक्षसमुद्भवम्॥१२२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dantakāṣṭhaṁ tato dadyātkṣīravṛkṣasamudbhavam||122||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v123" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तस्य पातः शुभः प्राचीसौम्यैशाप्योर्ध्वदिग्गतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tasya pātaḥ śubhaḥ prācīsaumyaiśāpyordhvadiggataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अशुभोऽन्यत्र तत्रापि होमोऽष्टशतिको भवेत्॥१२३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aśubho'nyatra tatrāpi homo'ṣṭaśatiko bhavet||123||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v124" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बहिःकर्म ततः कुर्याद्दिक्षु सर्वासु दैशिकः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bahiḥkarma tataḥ kuryāddikṣu sarvāsu daiśikaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ॐ क्षः क्षः सर्वभूतेभ्यः स्वाहेति मनुनामुना॥१२४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Oṁ kṣaḥ kṣaḥ sarvabhūtebhyaḥ svāheti manunāmunā||124||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v125" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">समाचम्य कृतन्यासः समभ्यर्च्य च शङ्करम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Samācamya kṛtanyāsaḥ samabhyarcya ca śaṅkaram|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">... गृहे शुचिः॥१२५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">... gṛhe śuciḥ||125||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v126" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न्यासं कृत्वा तु शिष्याणामात्मनश्च विशेषतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nyāsaṁ kṛtvā tu śiṣyāṇāmātmanaśca viśeṣataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रभाते नित्यकर्मादि कृत्वा स्वप्नं विचारयेत्॥१२६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prabhāte nityakarmādi kṛtvā svapnaṁ vicārayet||126||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v127" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शुभं प्रकाशयेत्तेषामशुभे होममाचरेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śubhaṁ prakāśayetteṣāmaśubhe homamācaret|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततः पुष्पफलादीनां सुवेशाभरणाः स्त्रियः॥१२७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tataḥ puṣpaphalādīnāṁ suveśābharaṇāḥ striyaḥ||127||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v128" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आपदुत्तरणं चैव शुभदेशावरोहणम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Āpaduttaraṇaṁ caiva śubhadeśāvarohaṇam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मद्यपानं शिरश्छेदमाममांसस्य भक्षणम्॥१२८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Madyapānaṁ śiraśchedamāmamāṁsasya bhakṣaṇam||128||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v129" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">देवतादर्शनं साक्षात्तथा विष्ठानुलेपनम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Devatādarśanaṁ sākṣāttathā viṣṭhānulepanam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एवं विधं शुभं दृष्ट्वा सिद्धिं प्राप्नोत्यभीप्सिताम्॥१२९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ vidhaṁ śubhaṁ dṛṣṭvā siddhiṁ prāpnotyabhīpsitām||129||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v130" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एतदेवान्यथाभूतं दुःस्वप्न इति कीर्त्यते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Etadevānyathābhūtaṁ duḥsvapna iti kīrtyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पक्वमांसाशनाभ्यङ्गगर्तादिपतनादिकम्॥१३०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pakvamāṁsāśanābhyaṅgagartādipatanādikam||130||</span></p>

## Строфы 131–135 {#s14}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s14-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तन्त्रोक्तां निष्कृतिं कृत्वा द्विजत्वापादनं ततः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tantroktāṁ niṣkṛtiṁ kṛtvā dvijatvāpādanaṁ tataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">देवाग्निगुरुदेवीनां पूजां कृत्वा सदा बुधः॥१३१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Devāgnigurudevīnāṁ pūjāṁ kṛtvā sadā budhaḥ||131||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v132" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एतेषामनिवेद्यैव न किञ्चिदपि भक्षयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Eteṣāmanivedyaiva na kiñcidapi bhakṣayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">देवद्रव्यं गुरुद्रव्यं चण्डीद्रव्यं च वर्जयेत्॥१३२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Devadravyaṁ gurudravyaṁ caṇḍīdravyaṁ ca varjayet||132||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v133" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">निष्फलं नैव चेष्टेत मुहूर्तमपि मन्त्रवित्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Niṣphalaṁ naiva ceṣṭeta muhūrtamapi mantravit|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">योगाभ्यासरतो भूयान्मन्त्राभ्यासरतोऽपि वा॥१३३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yogābhyāsarato bhūyānmantrābhyāsarato'pi vā||133||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v134" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इत्येवमादिसमयाञ्श्रावयित्वा विसर्जयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ityevamādisamayāñśrāvayitvā visarjayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">देवदेवं ततः स्नानं शिष्याणामात्मनोऽपि वा॥१३४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Devadevaṁ tataḥ snānaṁ śiṣyāṇāmātmano'pi vā||134||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v135" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कारयेच्छिवकुम्भेन सर्वदुष्कृतहारिणा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kārayecchivakumbhena sarvaduṣkṛtahāriṇā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इत्येतत्सामयं कर्म समासात्परिकीर्तितम्॥१३५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ityetatsāmayaṁ karma samāsātparikīrtitam||135||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इति श्रीमालिनीविजयोत्तरे तन्त्रे समयाधिकारोऽष्टमः॥८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Iti śrīmālinīvijayottare tantre samayādhikāro'ṣṭamaḥ||8||</span></p>
