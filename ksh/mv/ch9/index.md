<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मायान्तेऽध्वनि तामेव शुद्धे विद्यां विचक्षणः॥५७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Māyānte'dhvani tāmeva śuddhe vidyāṁ vicakṣaṇaḥ||57||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v58" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तस्यां सन्तर्पणं कृत्वा शिष्यमस्त्रेण ताडयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tasyāṁ santarpaṇaṁ kṛtvā śiṣyamastreṇa tāḍayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आलभ्य हृदये विद्वाञ्छिवहस्तेन तं पुनः॥५८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ālabhya hṛdaye vidvāñchivahastena taṁ punaḥ||58||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v59" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ग्रहणं तस्य कुर्वीत रश्मिमात्रावियोगतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Grahaṇaṁ tasya kurvīta raśmimātrāviyogataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नाडीमार्गेण गत्वा तु हंहृन्मन्त्रपुटीकृतम्॥५९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nāḍīmārgeṇa gatvā tu haṁhṛnmantrapuṭīkṛtam||59||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v60" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कृत्वात्मस्थं ततो योनौ गर्भाधानं विचिन्तयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kṛtvātmasthaṁ tato yonau garbhādhānaṁ vicintayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्र्यर्णार्धाक्षरया मन्त्री सर्वगर्भक्रियान्वितम्॥६०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tryarṇārdhākṣarayā mantrī sarvagarbhakriyānvitam||60||</span></p>

## Строфы 61–70 {#s7}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s7-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भोग्यभोक्तृत्वसामर्थ्यनिष्पत्त्या जननं बुधः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhogyabhoktṛtvasāmarthyaniṣpattyā jananaṁ budhaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दक्षशृङ्गस्थया मन्त्री प्रकुर्वीत सुलोचने॥६१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dakṣaśṛṅgasthayā mantrī prakurvīta sulocane||61||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v62" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पिबनीपूर्विकाभिश्च अस्त्राद्यैः परयापि च।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pibanīpūrvikābhiśca astrādyaiḥ parayāpi ca|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सम्यगाहुतयो दद्याद्दश पञ्च विचक्षणः॥६२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Samyagāhutayo dadyāddaśa pañca vicakṣaṇaḥ||62||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v63" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततोऽस्यापरया कार्यं पाशविच्छेदनं बुधैः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tato'syāparayā kāryaṁ pāśavicchedanaṁ budhaiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भुवनेशमथामन्त्र्य तत्त्वेश्वरमथापि वा॥६३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhuvaneśamathāmantrya tattveśvaramathāpi vā||63||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v64" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भोगभागा... पश्चात्तमिदमादिशेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhogabhāgā... paścāttamidamādiśet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भुवनेश त्वया नास्य साधकस्य शिवाज्ञया॥६४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhuvaneśa tvayā nāsya sādhakasya śivājñayā||64||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v65" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रतिबन्धः प्रकर्तव्यो यातुः पदमनामयम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pratibandhaḥ prakartavyo yātuḥ padamanāmayam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उत्क्षेपणं ततः कुर्यात्तयैवाध्युष्टवर्णया॥६५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Utkṣepaṇaṁ tataḥ kuryāttayaivādhyuṣṭavarṇayā||65||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v66" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अव्याप्तिमन्त्रसंयोगात्पृथङ् मार्गविशुद्धये।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Avyāptimantrasaṁyogātpṛthaṅ mārgaviśuddhaye|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दद्यादेकैकशो ध्यात्वा आहुतीनां त्रयं त्रयम्॥६६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dadyādekaikaśo dhyātvā āhutīnāṁ trayaṁ trayam||66||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v67" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततः पूर्णाहुतिं दद्यात्परया वौषडन्तया।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tataḥ pūrṇāhutiṁ dadyātparayā vauṣaḍantayā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शिशुमुत्क्षिप्य चात्मस्थं तद्देहस्थं च कारयेत्॥६७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śiśumutkṣipya cātmasthaṁ taddehasthaṁ ca kārayet||67||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v68" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आहुतीनां त्रयं दद्याद्दत्त्वा पूर्णाहुतिं बुधः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Āhutīnāṁ trayaṁ dadyāddattvā pūrṇāhutiṁ budhaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">महापाशुपतास्त्रेण विलोमादिविशुद्धये॥६८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mahāpāśupatāstreṇa vilomādiviśuddhaye||68||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v69" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विसर्जयित्वा वागीशीं तत्त्वं तु तदनन्तरम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Visarjayitvā vāgīśīṁ tattvaṁ tu tadanantaram|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विलीनं भावयेच्छुद्धमशुद्धे परमेश्वरि॥६९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vilīnaṁ bhāvayecchuddhamaśuddhe parameśvari||69||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v70" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कालान्तव्याप्तिसंशुद्धौ कृतायामेवमादरात्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kālāntavyāptisaṁśuddhau kṛtāyāmevamādarāt|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बाहुपाशं तु तं छित्त्वा होमयेदाज्यसंयुतम्॥७०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bāhupāśaṁ tu taṁ chittvā homayedājyasaṁyutam||70||</span></p>

## Строфы 71–80 {#s8}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s8-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मायातत्त्वे विशुद्धे तु कण्ठपाशे तथा बुधः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Māyātattve viśuddhe tu kaṇṭhapāśe tathā budhaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मायान्तमार्गसंशुद्धौ दीक्षाकर्मणि सर्वतः॥७१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Māyāntamārgasaṁśuddhau dīkṣākarmaṇi sarvataḥ||71||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v72" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">क्रियास्वनुक्तमन्त्रासु योजयेदपरां बुधः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kriyāsvanuktamantrāsu yojayedaparāṁ budhaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विद्यादिसकलान्ते च तद्वदेव परान्पराम्॥७२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vidyādisakalānte ca tadvadeva parānparām||72||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v73" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">योजयेन्नैश्वरादूर्ध्वं पिबन्यादिकमष्टकम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yojayennaiśvarādūrdhvaṁ pibanyādikamaṣṭakam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न चापि सकलादूर्ध्वमङ्गषट्कं विचक्षणः॥७३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na cāpi sakalādūrdhvamaṅgaṣaṭkaṁ vicakṣaṇaḥ||73||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v74" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">निष्कले परया कार्यं यत्किञ्चिद्विधिचोदितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Niṣkale parayā kāryaṁ yatkiñcidvidhicoditam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विशुद्धे सकलान्ते तु शिखां छित्त्वा विचक्षणः॥७४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Viśuddhe sakalānte tu śikhāṁ chittvā vicakṣaṇaḥ||74||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v75" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हुत्वा चाज्यं ततः शिष्यं स्नापयेदनुपूर्वतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Hutvā cājyaṁ tataḥ śiṣyaṁ snāpayedanupūrvataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आचम्याभ्यर्च्य देवेशं स्रुवमापूर्य सर्पिषा॥७५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ācamyābhyarcya deveśaṁ sruvamāpūrya sarpiṣā||75||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v76" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कृत्वा शिष्यं तथात्मस्थं मूलमन्त्रमनुस्मरेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kṛtvā śiṣyaṁ tathātmasthaṁ mūlamantramanusmaret|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शिवशक्तिं तथात्मानं शिष्यं सर्पिस्तथानलम्॥७६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śivaśaktiṁ tathātmānaṁ śiṣyaṁ sarpistathānalam||76||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v77" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एकीकुर्वञ्छनैर्गच्छेद्द्वादशान्तमनन्यधीः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ekīkurvañchanairgaccheddvādaśāntamananyadhīḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्र कुम्भकमास्थाय ध्यायन्सकलनिष्कलम्॥७७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatra kumbhakamāsthāya dhyāyansakalaniṣkalam||77||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v78" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तिष्ठेत्तावदनुद्विग्नो यावदाज्यक्षयो भवेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tiṣṭhettāvadanudvigno yāvadājyakṣayo bhavet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एवं युक्तः परे तत्त्वे गुरुणा शिवमूर्तिना॥७८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ yuktaḥ pare tattve guruṇā śivamūrtinā||78||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v79" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न भूयः पशुतामेति दग्धमायानिबन्धनः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na bhūyaḥ paśutāmeti dagdhamāyānibandhanaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विधिरेष समाख्यातो दीक्षाकर्मणि भौवने॥७९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vidhireṣa samākhyāto dīkṣākarmaṇi bhauvane||79||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v80" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इतराध्वविधिं मुक्त्वा शिवयोगविधिं तथा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Itarādhvavidhiṁ muktvā śivayogavidhiṁ tathā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विलोमकर्म सन्त्यज्य द्विगुणस्तत्त्ववर्त्मनि॥८०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vilomakarma santyajya dviguṇastattvavartmani||80||</span></p>

## Строфы 81–82.5 {#s9}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s9-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तद्वच्च वर्णमार्गेऽपि चतुर्धा पदवर्त्मनि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadvacca varṇamārge'pi caturdhā padavartmani|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अष्टधा मन्त्रमार्गेऽपि कलाख्येऽपि च तद्द्विधा॥८१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aṣṭadhā mantramārge'pi kalākhye'pi ca taddvidhā||81||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v82" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्रिखण्डे विंशतिगुणः स एव परिकीर्तितः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Trikhaṇḍe viṁśatiguṇaḥ sa eva parikīrtitaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इति सर्वाध्वसंशुद्धिः समासात्परिकीर्तिता॥८२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Iti sarvādhvasaṁśuddhiḥ samāsātparikīrtitā||82||</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">साधकाचार्ययोः कर्म कथ्यमानमतः शृणु।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sādhakācāryayoḥ karma kathyamānamataḥ śṛṇu|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इति श्रीमालिनीविजयोत्तरे तन्त्रे क्रियादीक्षाधिकारो नवमोऽधिकारः॥९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Iti śrīmālinīvijayottare tantre kriyādīkṣādhikāro navamo'dhikāraḥ||9||</span></p>
