## Строфы 1–10 {#s1}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथ एकविंशतितमोऽधिकारः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Atha Ekaviṁśatitamo'dhikāraḥ|</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथातः परमं गुह्यं शिवज्ञानामृतोत्तमम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Athātaḥ paramaṁ guhyaṁ śivajñānāmṛtottamam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">व्याधिमृत्युविनाशाय योगिनामुपवर्ण्यते॥१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vyādhimṛtyuvināśāya yogināmupavarṇyate||1||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">षोडशारे खगे चक्रे चन्द्रकल्पितकर्णिके।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ṣoḍaśāre khage cakre candrakalpitakarṇike|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वरूपेण परां तत्र स्रवन्तीममृतं स्मरेत्॥२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svarūpeṇa parāṁ tatra sravantīmamṛtaṁ smaret||2||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूर्वन्यासेन सन्नद्धः क्षणमेकं विचक्षणः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūrvanyāsena sannaddhaḥ kṣaṇamekaṁ vicakṣaṇaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततस्तु रसनां नीत्वा लम्बके विनियोजयत्॥३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatastu rasanāṁ nītvā lambake viniyojayat||3||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्रवन्तममृतं दिव्यं चन्द्रबिम्बसितं स्मरेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sravantamamṛtaṁ divyaṁ candrabimbasitaṁ smaret|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मुखमापूर्यते तस्य किञ्चिल्लवणवारिणा॥४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mukhamāpūryate tasya kiñcillavaṇavāriṇā||4||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">लोहगन्धेन तच्चात्र न पिवेत्किन्तु निक्षिपेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Lohagandhena taccātra na pivetkintu nikṣipet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एवं समभ्यसेत्तावद्यावत्तत्स्वादु जायते॥५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ samabhyasettāvadyāvattatsvādu jāyate||5||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जराव्याधिविनिर्मुक्तो जायते तत्पिवंस्ततः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jarāvyādhivinirmukto jāyate tatpivaṁstataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पड्भिर्मासैरनायासाद्वत्सरान्मृत्युजिद्भवेत्॥६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Paḍbhirmāsairanāyāsādvatsarānmṛtyujidbhavet||6||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्र स्वादुनि सञ्जाते तदाप्रभृति तत्रगम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatra svāduni sañjāte tadāprabhṛti tatragam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यदेव चिन्तयेद्द्रव्यं तेनास्यापूर्यते मुखम्॥७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yadeva cintayeddravyaṁ tenāsyāpūryate mukham||7||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">रुधिरं मदिरां वाथ वसां वा क्षीरमेव वा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Rudhiraṁ madirāṁ vātha vasāṁ vā kṣīrameva vā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">घृततैलादिकं वाथ द्रवद्द्रव्यमनन्यधीः॥८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ghṛtatailādikaṁ vātha dravaddravyamananyadhīḥ||8||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथान्यं सम्प्रवक्ष्यामि सङ्क्रान्तिविधिमुत्तमम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Athānyaṁ sampravakṣyāmi saṅkrāntividhimuttamam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मृते जीवच्छरीरे तु प्रविशेद्योगविद्यया॥९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mṛte jīvaccharīre tu praviśedyogavidyayā||9||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">निवातस्थो जितप्राणो जितासनविधिक्रमः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nivātastho jitaprāṇo jitāsanavidhikramaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कुर्वीत वायुनावेशमर्कतूले शनैः शनैः॥१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kurvīta vāyunāveśamarkatūle śanaiḥ śanaiḥ||10||</span></p>

## Строфы 11–20 {#s2}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s2-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वादाकृष्टिविधिं यावद्गुडे निम्बे च कारयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svādākṛṣṭividhiṁ yāvadguḍe nimbe ca kārayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">श्रीखण्डगुडकर्पूरैस्ततः कृत्वाकृतिं शुभाम्॥११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śrīkhaṇḍaguḍakarpūraistataḥ kṛtvākṛtiṁ śubhām||11||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रगुणामगुण … … … न्यङ्गेषु सन्दधत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Praguṇāmaguṇa … … … nyaṅgeṣu sandadhat|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न्यासं कृत्वापि तत्रापि वेधं कुर्याच्छनैः शनैः॥१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nyāsaṁ kṛtvāpi tatrāpi vedhaṁ kuryācchanaiḥ śanaiḥ||12||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">निरोधं तत्र कुर्वीत घट्टनं तदनन्तरम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nirodhaṁ tatra kurvīta ghaṭṭanaṁ tadanantaram|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">घट्टनं नाम विज्ञेयमङ्गप्रत्यङ्गचालनम्॥१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ghaṭṭanaṁ nāma vijñeyamaṅgapratyaṅgacālanam||13||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एवमभ्यसतस्तस्य योगयुक्तस्य योगिनः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evamabhyasatastasya yogayuktasya yoginaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चलते प्रतिमा सा तु धावते चापि संमुखी॥१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Calate pratimā sā tu dhāvate cāpi saṁmukhī||14||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पुनस्तां प्रेरयेत्तावद्यावत्स्वस्थानमागताम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Punastāṁ prerayettāvadyāvatsvasthānamāgatām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पतितां चालयेद्भूय उत्तानां पार्श्वतः स्थिताम्॥१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Patitāṁ cālayedbhūya uttānāṁ pārśvataḥ sthitām||15||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एवं सर्वात्मनस्तावद्यावत्स्ववशतां गता।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ sarvātmanastāvadyāvatsvavaśatāṁ gatā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततः प्रभृत्यसौ योगी प्रविशेद्यत्र रोचते॥१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tataḥ prabhṛtyasau yogī praviśedyatra rocate||16||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मृते जीवच्छरीरे वा सङ्क्रान्त्याक्रान्तिभेदतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mṛte jīvaccharīre vā saṅkrāntyākrāntibhedataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रक्षिप्य जलवच्छक्तिजालं सर्वाङ्गसन्धिषु॥१७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prakṣipya jalavacchaktijālaṁ sarvāṅgasandhiṣu||17||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रत्यङ्गमङ्गतस्तस्य शक्तिं तेनाक्रमेद्बुधः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pratyaṅgamaṅgatastasya śaktiṁ tenākramedbudhaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वकीयं रक्षयेद्देहहमाक्रान्तावन्यथा त्यजेत्॥१८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svakīyaṁ rakṣayeddehahamākrāntāvanyathā tyajet||18||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बहून्यपि शरीराणि दृढलक्ष्यो यदा भवेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bahūnyapi śarīrāṇi dṛḍhalakṣyo yadā bhavet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदा गृह्णात्यसन्देहं युगपत्सन्त्यजन्नपि॥१९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadā gṛhṇātyasandehaṁ yugapatsantyajannapi||19||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथापरं प्रवक्ष्यामि सद्यः प्रत्ययकारकम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Athāparaṁ pravakṣyāmi sadyaḥ pratyayakārakam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">समाधानामृतं दिव्यं योगिनां मृत्युनाशनम्॥२०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Samādhānāmṛtaṁ divyaṁ yogināṁ mṛtyunāśanam||20||</span></p>

## Строфы 21–30 {#s3}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s3-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चन्द्राकृष्टिकरं नाम मासाद्वा योगभोगदम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Candrākṛṣṭikaraṁ nāma māsādvā yogabhogadam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शुक्लपक्षे द्वितीयायां मेषस्थे तिग्मरोचिषि॥२१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śuklapakṣe dvitīyāyāṁ meṣasthe tigmarociṣi||21||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v22" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्नातः शुचिर्निराहारः कृतपूजाविधिर्बुधः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Snātaḥ śucirnirāhāraḥ kṛtapūjāvidhirbudhaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न्यसेच्चन्द्रे कलाजालं परया समधिष्ठितम्॥२२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nyaseccandre kalājālaṁ parayā samadhiṣṭhitam||22||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v23" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वबाधापरित्यक्ते प्रदेशे संस्थितो बुधः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvabādhāparityakte pradeśe saṁsthito budhaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एकचित्तः प्रशान्तात्मा शिवसद्भावभावितः॥२३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ekacittaḥ praśāntātmā śivasadbhāvabhāvitaḥ||23||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v24" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तावदालोकयेच्चन्द्रं यावदस्तमुपागतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tāvadālokayeccandraṁ yāvadastamupāgataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततो भुञ्जीत दुग्धेन चन्द्रध्यानसमन्वितः॥२४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tato bhuñjīta dugdhena candradhyānasamanvitaḥ||24||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v25" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एवं दिने दिने कुर्याद्यावत्पञ्चदशी भवेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ dine dine kuryādyāvatpañcadaśī bhavet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शेषां रात्रिं स्वपेद्ध्यायंश्चन्द्रबिम्बगतां पराम्॥२५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śeṣāṁ rātriṁ svapeddhyāyaṁścandrabimbagatāṁ parām||25||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v26" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पौर्णमास्यां तथा योगी अर्धरात्र उपस्थितः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Paurṇamāsyāṁ tathā yogī ardharātra upasthitaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जने निःशब्दतां याते प्रसुप्ते सर्वजन्तुभिः॥२६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jane niḥśabdatāṁ yāte prasupte sarvajantubhiḥ||26||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v27" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चन्द्रकोटिकरप्रख्यां तारहारविभूषणाम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Candrakoṭikaraprakhyāṁ tārahāravibhūṣaṇām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सिताम्बरपरीधानां सितचन्दनचर्चिताम्॥२७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sitāmbaraparīdhānāṁ sitacandanacarcitām||27||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v28" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मौक्तिकाभरणोपेतां सुरूपां नवयौवनाम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mauktikābharaṇopetāṁ surūpāṁ navayauvanām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आप्यायनकरीं देवीं समन्तादमृतस्रवाम्॥२८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Āpyāyanakarīṁ devīṁ samantādamṛtasravām||28||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v29" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">राजीवासनसंस्थां च योगनिद्रामवस्थिताम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Rājīvāsanasaṁsthāṁ ca yoganidrāmavasthitām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चन्द्रबिम्बे परां देवीमीक्षते नात्र संशयः॥२९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Candrabimbe parāṁ devīmīkṣate nātra saṁśayaḥ||29||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v30" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततस्तां चेतसा व्याप्य तावदाकर्षयेत्सुधीः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatastāṁ cetasā vyāpya tāvadākarṣayetsudhīḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यावन्मुखाग्रमायाता तत्र कुर्यात्स्थिरं मनः॥३०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yāvanmukhāgramāyātā tatra kuryātsthiraṁ manaḥ||30||</span></p>

## Строфы 31–36 {#s4}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s4-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततः प्रसार्य वदनं ध्यानासक्तेन चेतसा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tataḥ prasārya vadanaṁ dhyānāsaktena cetasā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">निगिरेत्तां समाकृष्य भूयो हृदि विचिन्तयेत्॥३१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nigirettāṁ samākṛṣya bhūyo hṛdi vicintayet||31||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v32" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तया प्रविष्टया देहं योगी दुःखविवर्जितः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tayā praviṣṭayā dehaṁ yogī duḥkhavivarjitaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शक्तितुल्यबलो भूत्वा जीवेदाचन्द्रतारकम्॥३२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śaktitulyabalo bhūtvā jīvedācandratārakam||32||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v33" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एकोऽप्यनेकधात्मानं संविभज्य निजेच्छया।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Eko'pyanekadhātmānaṁ saṁvibhajya nijecchayā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्रैलोक्यं यौगपद्येन भुनक्ति वशतां गतम्॥३३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Trailokyaṁ yaugapadyena bhunakti vaśatāṁ gatam||33||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v34" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आसाद्य विपुलान्भोगान्प्रलये समुपस्थिते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Āsādya vipulānbhogānpralaye samupasthite|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">परमभ्येति निर्वाणं दुष्पापमकृतामनाम्॥३४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Paramabhyeti nirvāṇaṁ duṣpāpamakṛtāmanām||34||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v35" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथवा तन्न शक्नोति गगने परिचिन्तितुम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Athavā tanna śaknoti gagane paricintitum|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रतिबिम्बे तथा ध्यायेदुदकादिषु पूर्ववत्॥३५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pratibimbe tathā dhyāyedudakādiṣu pūrvavat||35||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v36" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्पीत्वा मनसा शेषां स्वपेद्रात्रिमनुस्मरन्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatpītvā manasā śeṣāṁ svapedrātrimanusmaran|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूरोक्तं समवाप्नोति षड्भिर्मासैरखण्डितम्॥३६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūroktaṁ samavāpnoti ṣaḍbhirmāsairakhaṇḍitam||36||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इति श्रीमालिनीविजयोत्तरे तन्त्रे चन्द्राकृष्ट्यधिकार एकविंशतितमः॥२१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Iti śrīmālinīvijayottare tantre candrākṛṣṭyadhikāra ekaviṁśatitamaḥ||21||</span></p>
