## Строфы 1–10 {#s1}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथ एकोनविंशोऽधिकारः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Atha Ekonaviṁśo'dhikāraḥ|</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथैनं परमं योगविधिमाकर्ण्य शाङ्करी।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Athainaṁ paramaṁ yogavidhimākarṇya śāṅkarī|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पुनराह प्रसन्नास्या प्रणिपत्य जगद्गुरुम्॥१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Punarāha prasannāsyā praṇipatya jagadgurum||1||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">साध्यत्वेन श्रुता देव भिन्नयोनिस्तु मालिनी।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sādhyatvena śrutā deva bhinnayonistu mālinī|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विद्यात्रयं सविद्याङ्गं विधिवच्चावधारितम्॥२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vidyātrayaṁ savidyāṅgaṁ vidhivaccāvadhāritam||2||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अधुना श्रोतुमिच्छामि ह्यभिन्ना साध्यते कथम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Adhunā śrotumicchāmi hyabhinnā sādhyate katham|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हिताय साधकेन्द्राणां प्रसादाद्वक्तुमर्हसि॥३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Hitāya sādhakendrāṇāṁ prasādādvaktumarhasi||3||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एवमुक्तो महेशान्या जगतां पतिरादरात्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evamukto maheśānyā jagatāṁ patirādarāt|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विकसद्वदनाम्भोजः प्रत्युवाच वचोऽमृतम्॥४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vikasadvadanāmbhojaḥ pratyuvāca vaco'mṛtam||4||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आरिराधयिषुः शम्भुं कुलोक्तविधिना बुधः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ārirādhayiṣuḥ śambhuṁ kuloktavidhinā budhaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कुलचक्रं यजेदादौ बुधो दीक्षोक्तवर्त्मना॥५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kulacakraṁ yajedādau budho dīkṣoktavartmanā||5||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततो जपेत्परां शक्तिं लक्षमेकमखण्डितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tato japetparāṁ śaktiṁ lakṣamekamakhaṇḍitam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पराबीजपुटान्तःस्थां न द्रुतां न विलम्बिताम्॥६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Parābījapuṭāntaḥsthāṁ na drutāṁ na vilambitām||6||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तद्वत्खण्डाष्टकं चास्या लक्षं लक्षमखण्डितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadvatkhaṇḍāṣṭakaṁ cāsyā lakṣaṁ lakṣamakhaṇḍitam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जपेत्कुलेश्वरस्यापि लक्षषट्कमनन्यधीः॥७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Japetkuleśvarasyāpi lakṣaṣaṭkamananyadhīḥ||7||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">होमयित्वा दशांशेन द्रव्यं पूर्वोदितं बुधः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Homayitvā daśāṁśena dravyaṁ pūrvoditaṁ budhaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नित्यानुस्मृतिशीलस्य वाक्सिद्धिः सम्प्रजायते॥८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nityānusmṛtiśīlasya vāksiddhiḥ samprajāyate||8||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वकूले जपयुक्तस्य अशक्तस्यापि साधने।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svakūle japayuktasya aśaktasyāpi sādhane|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवन्ति कन्यसा देवि संसारे भोगसम्पदः॥९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavanti kanyasā devi saṁsāre bhogasampadaḥ||9||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शक्तस्तु साधयेत्सिद्धिं मध्यमामुत्तमामपि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śaktastu sādhayetsiddhiṁ madhyamāmuttamāmapi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कृतसेवाविधिः पृथ्वीं भ्रमेदुद्भ्रान्तपत्त्रिवत्॥१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kṛtasevāvidhiḥ pṛthvīṁ bhramedudbhrāntapattrivat||10||</span></p>

## Строфы 11–20 {#s2}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s2-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नगरे पञ्चरात्रं तु त्रिरात्रं पत्तने तु वै।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nagare pañcarātraṁ tu trirātraṁ pattane tu vai|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ग्रामेऽपि चैकरात्रं तु स्थित्वैनं विधिमाचरेत्॥११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Grāme'pi caikarātraṁ tu sthitvainaṁ vidhimācaret||11||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यन्नामाद्यक्षरं यत्र वर्गे तत्तस्य वस्तुनः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yannāmādyakṣaraṁ yatra varge tattasya vastunaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कुलमुक्तं विधानज्ञैर्नगरादेर्न संशयः॥१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kulamuktaṁ vidhānajñairnagarāderna saṁśayaḥ||12||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">या यत्र देवता वर्गे वाच्यत्वे संव्यवस्थिता।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yā yatra devatā varge vācyatve saṁvyavasthitā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सैव तस्य पतित्वेन ध्येया पूज्या च साधकैः॥१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Saiva tasya patitvena dhyeyā pūjyā ca sādhakaiḥ||13||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तस्य किञ्चित्समासाद्य नगरादिकमादरात्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tasya kiñcitsamāsādya nagarādikamādarāt|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वदिग्वर्गस्थितो भूत्वा चक्रं योज्य निजोदये॥१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svadigvargasthito bhūtvā cakraṁ yojya nijodaye||14||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भ्रमित्वा पुनरायाति पूर्वकालक्रमेण च।</span><br />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ददेद्भक्ष्यादिकं किञ्चिद्दापयेद्वाथ केनचित्।</span><br />

## Строфы 21–30 {#s3}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s3-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">योगिनीमेलकं प्राप्य षण्मासेनैव सिद्ध्यति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yoginīmelakaṁ prāpya ṣaṇmāsenaiva siddhyati|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दुष्करोऽयं विधिर्देवि सत्त्वहीनैर्नराधमैः॥२१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Duṣkaro'yaṁ vidhirdevi sattvahīnairnarādhamaiḥ||21||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v22" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वसिद्धिकरो मुख्यः कुलशास्त्रेषु सर्वतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvasiddhikaro mukhyaḥ kulaśāstreṣu sarvataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथैकस्मिन्नपि ग्रामे पत्तने नगरेऽपि वा॥२२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Athaikasminnapi grāme pattane nagare'pi vā||22||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v23" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तद्दिग्भागं समाश्रित्य तदेवं जपते कुलम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Taddigbhāgaṁ samāśritya tadevaṁ japate kulam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्रिभिरब्दैरनायासात्साधयेदुत्तमं फलम्॥२३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tribhirabdairanāyāsātsādhayeduttamaṁ phalam||23||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v24" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">लोकयात्रापरित्यक्तो ग्रासमात्रपरिग्रहः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Lokayātrāparityakto grāsamātraparigrahaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथवा नाभिचक्रे तु ध्यानचक्रं कुलात्मकम्॥२४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Athavā nābhicakre tu dhyānacakraṁ kulātmakam||24||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v25" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चेतसा भ्रमणं कुर्यात्सर्वकालक्रमेण तु।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Cetasā bhramaṇaṁ kuryātsarvakālakrameṇa tu|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततोऽस्य वत्सरार्धेन देहान्तं योगिनीकुलम्॥२५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tato'sya vatsarārdhena dehāntaṁ yoginīkulam||25||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v26" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आविर्भवत्यसन्देहात्स्वविज्ञानप्रकाशकम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Āvirbhavatyasandehātsvavijñānaprakāśakam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तेनाविर्भूतमात्रेण योगी योगिकुले कुली॥२६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tenāvirbhūtamātreṇa yogī yogikule kulī||26||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v27" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवेदपि पतिर्देवि योगिनां परमेश्वरि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavedapi patirdevi yogināṁ parameśvari|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथवा चिन्तयेद्देवि यकारादिकमष्टकम्॥२७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Athavā cintayeddevi yakārādikamaṣṭakam||27||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v28" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वरूपेण प्रभाकारकरालाकुलविग्रहम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svarūpeṇa prabhākārakarālākulavigraham|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तस्य मध्ये कुलेशानं स्वबोधकमनुस्मरन्॥२८॥</span><br />

<span class="pv-iast">Maṇḍalaṁ pṛthivī vāpi brahmāṇḍaṁ vā samastakam||90||</span></p>

## Строфы 91–101 {#s10}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s10-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विस्तीर्णं वा जनानीकमेकैकमुत्तमोत्तमम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vistīrṇaṁ vā janānīkamekaikamuttamottamam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स बाह्याभ्यन्तरं व्याप्य पुनः प्रतिनिवृत्त्य च॥९१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sa bāhyābhyantaraṁ vyāpya punaḥ pratinivṛttya ca||91||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v92" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रविशन्तीं स्वकं देहं पूर्वाकारामनुस्मरेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Praviśantīṁ svakaṁ dehaṁ pūrvākārāmanusmaret|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एवं दिने दिने कुर्यात्तद्गतेनान्तरात्मना॥९२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ dine dine kuryāttadgatenāntarātmanā||92||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v93" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततोऽस्य मासमात्रेण जनास्तत्र निवासिनः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tato'sya māsamātreṇa janāstatra nivāsinaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आगच्छन्ति यथा तीर्थं शक्तितेजोपबृंहिताः॥९३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Āgacchanti yathā tīrthaṁ śaktitejopabṛṁhitāḥ||93||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v94" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">रूपिण्यो विविधाकारा ललनाद्यास्तथा पराः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Rūpiṇyo vividhākārā lalanādyāstathā parāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भूचर्यः क्षोभमायान्ति षण्मासान्नात्र संशयः॥९४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhūcaryaḥ kṣobhamāyānti ṣaṇmāsānnātra saṁśayaḥ||94||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v95" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">योनिजा द्वा मजा क्षेत्रजाताः पीठसमुद्भवाः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yonijā dvā majā kṣetrajātāḥ pīṭhasamudbhavāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नायिकाश्च महादेवि क्रमात्क्षुभ्यन्ति वत्सरात्॥९५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nāyikāśca mahādevi kramātkṣubhyanti vatsarāt||95||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v96" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अन्तरिक्षगता दिव्या ब्रह्मलोकगताश्च याः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Antarikṣagatā divyā brahmalokagatāśca yāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ब्रह्माण्डान्तर्गताः सर्वाः क्षोभं यान्ति समात्रयात्॥९६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Brahmāṇḍāntargatāḥ sarvāḥ kṣobhaṁ yānti samātrayāt||96||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v97" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वं स्वं ददति विज्ञानं साधकेन समीहितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svaṁ svaṁ dadati vijñānaṁ sādhakena samīhitam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यदि ता न प्रयच्छन्ति साधकेन समीहितम्॥९७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yadi tā na prayacchanti sādhakena samīhitam||97||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v98" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नश्यते दिव्यविज्ञानं त्रुट्यते कुलसन्ततिः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Naśyate divyavijñānaṁ truṭyate kulasantatiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दत्त्वा तु साधकेन्द्रेण प्रार्थितं फलमादरात्॥९८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dattvā tu sādhakendreṇa prārthitaṁ phalamādarāt||98||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v99" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अनुपालितगुर्वाज्ञाः सिद्धिं प्राप्स्यन्त्यनुत्तमाम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anupālitagurvājñāḥ siddhiṁ prāpsyantyanuttamām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ताभ्यो विज्ञानमासाद्य योगी योगिकुले कुली॥९९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tābhyo vijñānamāsādya yogī yogikule kulī||99||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v100" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भुक्त्वा यथेप्सितान्भोगान्यात्यन्ते परमं पदम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhuktvā yathepsitānbhogānyātyante paramaṁ padam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इत्ययं कथितो लेशात्कौलिको विधिरुत्तमः॥१००॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ityayaṁ kathito leśātkauliko vidhiruttamaḥ||100||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v101" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">योगिनां सर्वसिद्ध्यर्थं कुलमार्गानुसारिणाम्॥१०१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yogināṁ sarvasiddhyarthaṁ kulamārgānusāriṇām||101||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इति श्रीमालिनीविजयोत्तरे तन्त्रे कुलचक्राधिकार एकोनविंशः॥१९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Iti śrīmālinīvijayottare tantre kulacakrādhikāra ekonaviṁśaḥ||19||</span></p>
