## Строфы 1–10 {#s1}

<p class="pv-copy nosearch pv-anchor" id="s1-sa"><button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-sa">Копировать санскрит</button> <button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-iast">Копировать транслитерацию</button></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथ अष्टादशोऽधिकारः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Atha Aṣṭādaśo'dhikāraḥ|</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शृणु देवि परं गुह्यमप्राप्यमकृतात्मनाम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śṛṇu devi paraṁ guhyamaprāpyamakṛtātmanām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यन्न कस्यचिदाख्यातं तदद्य कथयामि ते॥१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yanna kasyacidākhyātaṁ tadadya kathayāmi te||1||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वमन्यत्परित्यज्य चित्तमत्र निवेशयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvamanyatparityajya cittamatra niveśayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मृछैलधातुरत्नादिभवं लिङ्गं न पूजयेत्॥२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mṛchailadhāturatnādibhavaṁ liṅgaṁ na pūjayet||2||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यजेदाध्यात्मिकं लिङ्गं यत्र लीनं चराचरम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yajedādhyātmikaṁ liṅgaṁ yatra līnaṁ carācaram|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बहिर्लिङ्गस्य लिङ्गत्वमनेनाधिष्ठितं यतः॥३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bahirliṅgasya liṅgatvamanenādhiṣṭhitaṁ yataḥ||3||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अतः प्रपूजयेदेतत्परमाद्वैतमाश्रितः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ataḥ prapūjayedetatparamādvaitamāśritaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अनुध्यानेन देवेशि परेण परमाणुना॥४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anudhyānena deveśi pareṇa paramāṇunā||4||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">योऽनुध्यातः स एवैतल्लिङ्गं पश्यति नापरः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yo'nudhyātaḥ sa evaitalliṅgaṁ paśyati nāparaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यदेतत्स्पन्दनं नाम हृदये समवस्थितम्॥५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yadetatspandanaṁ nāma hṛdaye samavasthitam||5||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्र चित्तं समाधाय कम्प उद्भव एव च।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatra cittaṁ samādhāya kampa udbhava eva ca|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्र प्रशान्तिमापन्ने मासेनैकेन योगवित्॥६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatra praśāntimāpanne māsenaikena yogavit||6||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हृदयादुत्थितं लिङ्गं ब्रह्मरन्ध्रान्तमीश्वरि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Hṛdayādutthitaṁ liṅgaṁ brahmarandhrāntamīśvari|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वप्रमोद्द्योतिताशेषदेहान्तममलद्युति॥७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svapramoddyotitāśeṣadehāntamamaladyuti||7||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्रैव पश्यते सर्वं मन्त्रजालं महामतिः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatraiva paśyate sarvaṁ mantrajālaṁ mahāmatiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तन्मस्तकं समारुह्य मासमात्रमनन्यधीः॥८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tanmastakaṁ samāruhya māsamātramananyadhīḥ||8||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततस्तत्र सुनिष्पन्ने षण्मासात्सर्वसिद्धयः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatastatra suniṣpanne ṣaṇmāsātsarvasiddhayaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एतल्लिङ्गमविज्ञाय यो लिङ्गी लिङ्गमाश्रयेत्॥९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Etalliṅgamavijñāya yo liṅgī liṅgamāśrayet||9||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वृथा परिश्रमस्तस्य न लिङ्गफलमश्नुते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vṛthā pariśramastasya na liṅgaphalamaśnute|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शैवमेतन्महालिङ्गमात्मलिङ्गे [न] सिद्ध्यति॥१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śaivametanmahāliṅgamātmaliṅge [na] siddhyati||10||</span></p>

## Строфы 11–20 {#s2}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s2-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सिद्धेऽत्र लिङ्गवल्लिङ्गी लिङ्गस्थो लिङ्गवर्जितः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Siddhe'tra liṅgavalliṅgī liṅgastho liṅgavarjitaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवतीति किमाश्चर्यमेतस्माल्लिङ्गलिङ्गितः॥११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavatīti kimāścaryametasmālliṅgaliṅgitaḥ||11||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अनेन लिङ्गलिङ्गेन यदा योगी बहिर्व्रजेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anena liṅgaliṅgena yadā yogī bahirvrajet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदा लिङ्गीति विज्ञेयः पुरान्तं लिङ्गमिष्यते॥१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadā liṅgīti vijñeyaḥ purāntaṁ liṅgamiṣyate||12||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एतस्माल्लिङ्गविज्ञानाद्योगिनो लिङ्गिताः स्मृताः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Etasmālliṅgavijñānādyogino liṅgitāḥ smṛtāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अनेनाधिष्ठिताः मन्त्राः शान्तरौद्रादिभेदतः॥१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anenādhiṣṭhitāḥ mantrāḥ śāntaraudrādibhedataḥ||13||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवन्तीति किमाश्चर्यं तद्भावगतचेतसः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavantīti kimāścaryaṁ tadbhāvagatacetasaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">रौद्रं भावं समाश्रित्य यदि योगं समभ्यसेत्॥१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Raudraṁ bhāvaṁ samāśritya yadi yogaṁ samabhyaset||14||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दुर्निरीक्ष्यो भवेत्सर्वैः सदेवासुरमानुषैः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Durnirīkṣyo bhavetsarvaiḥ sadevāsuramānuṣaiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">गमागमविनिर्मुक्तः सर्वदृष्टिरकातरः॥१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Gamāgamavinirmuktaḥ sarvadṛṣṭirakātaraḥ||15||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मुहूर्तं तिष्ठते यावत्तावदेवेशमाप्नुयात्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Muhūrtaṁ tiṣṭhate yāvattāvadeveśamāpnuyāt|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आविष्टः पश्यते सर्वं सूर्यकोटिसमद्युति॥१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Āviṣṭaḥ paśyate sarvaṁ sūryakoṭisamadyuti||16||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यत्तदक्षरमव्यक्तं शैवं भैरवमित्यपि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yattadakṣaramavyaktaṁ śaivaṁ bhairavamityapi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तं दृष्ट्वा वत्सरार्धेन योगी सर्वज्ञतामियात्॥१७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Taṁ dṛṣṭvā vatsarārdhena yogī sarvajñatāmiyāt||17||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">य एवैनं समासाद्य यस्तृप्तिमधिगच्छति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ya evainaṁ samāsādya yastṛptimadhigacchati|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न च कृत्त्रिमयोगेषु स मुक्तः सर्वबन्धनैः॥१८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na ca kṛttrimayogeṣu sa muktaḥ sarvabandhanaiḥ||18||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्राणायामादिकैर्लिङ्गैर्योगाः स्युः कृत्रिमा मताः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prāṇāyāmādikairliṅgairyogāḥ syuḥ kṛtrimā matāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तेन तेऽकृतकस्यास्य कलां नार्हन्ति षोडशीम्॥१९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tena te'kṛtakasyāsya kalāṁ nārhanti ṣoḍaśīm||19||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एतत्समभ्यसन्योगी दिव्यचिह्नानि पश्यति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Etatsamabhyasanyogī divyacihnāni paśyati|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उपविष्ट ऋजुर्योगी न किञ्चिदपि चिन्तयेत्॥२०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Upaviṣṭa ṛjuryogī na kiñcidapi cintayet||20||</span></p>

## Строфы 21–30 {#s3}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s3-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मुहूर्तान्निर्दहेत्सर्वं देहस्थमकृतं कृतम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Muhūrtānnirdahetsarvaṁ dehasthamakṛtaṁ kṛtam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दह्यमानस्य तस्येह प्रकम्पानुभवो भवेत्॥२१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dahyamānasya tasyeha prakampānubhavo bhavet||21||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v22" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततस्तत्र स्थिरीभूते ज्योतिरन्तः प्रकाशते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatastatra sthirībhūte jyotirantaḥ prakāśate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तां दृष्ट्वा परमां दीप्तिं दिव्यज्ञानं प्रवर्तते॥२२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tāṁ dṛṣṭvā paramāṁ dīptiṁ divyajñānaṁ pravartate||22||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v23" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वतन्त्रशिवतामेति भुञ्जानो विषयानपि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svatantraśivatāmeti bhuñjāno viṣayānapi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अनिमीलितदिव्याक्षो यावदास्ते मुहूर्तकम्॥२३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Animīlitadivyākṣo yāvadāste muhūrtakam||23||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v24" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तस्मात्सर्वगतं भावमात्मनः प्रतिपद्यते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tasmātsarvagataṁ bhāvamātmanaḥ pratipadyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तमेव भावयेद्यत्नात्सर्वसिद्धिफलेप्सया॥२४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tameva bhāvayedyatnātsarvasiddhiphalepsayā||24||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v25" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततस्तं भावयेद्योगी कम्पमानोऽत्यनुल्वणम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatastaṁ bhāvayedyogī kampamāno'tyanulvaṇam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततः प्रपश्यते तेजो ललाटाग्रे समन्ततः॥२५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tataḥ prapaśyate tejo lalāṭāgre samantataḥ||25||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v26" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दृष्ट्वा तत्परमं तेजो दिव्यज्ञानमवाप्नुयात्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dṛṣṭvā tatparamaṁ tejo divyajñānamavāpnuyāt|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">षड्भिर्मासैरनायासाद्वत्सरेण प्रसिद्ध्यति॥२६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ṣaḍbhirmāsairanāyāsādvatsareṇa prasiddhyati||26||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v27" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शिवतुल्यबलो भूत्वा यत्रेष्टं तत्र गच्छति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śivatulyabalo bhūtvā yatreṣṭaṁ tatra gacchati|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चेतः सर्वगतं कृत्वा मुहूर्तादेव योगवित्॥२७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Cetaḥ sarvagataṁ kṛtvā muhūrtādeva yogavit||27||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v28" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शक्त्यावेशमवाप्नोति प्रकम्पानुभवात्मकम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śaktyāveśamavāpnoti prakampānubhavātmakam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततस्तत्र स्थिरीभूते मासमात्रेण योगवित्॥२८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatastatra sthirībhūte māsamātreṇa yogavit||28||</span></p>

<span class="pv-iast">Ṣaḍvidhaṁ vinyasenmārgaṁ tasya dehe puroktavat|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततस्तं दीपमालोक्य तदङ्गुष्ठाग्रतः क्रमात्॥५९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatastaṁ dīpamālokya tadaṅguṣṭhāgrataḥ kramāt||59||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v60" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नयेत्तेजः समाहृत्य द्वादशान्तमनन्यधीः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nayettejaḥ samāhṛtya dvādaśāntamananyadhīḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततस्तं तत्र सञ्चिन्त्य शिवेनैकत्वमागतम्॥६०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatastaṁ tatra sañcintya śivenaikatvamāgatam||60||</span></p>

## Строфы 61–70 {#s7}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s7-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्र ध्यायेत्तमोरूपं तिरोभावनशीलनम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatra dhyāyettamorūpaṁ tirobhāvanaśīlanam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पतन्तीं तेन मार्गेण ह्यङ्गुष्ठाग्रान्तमागताम्॥६१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Patantīṁ tena mārgeṇa hyaṅguṣṭhāgrāntamāgatām||61||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v62" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सबाह्याभ्यन्तरं ध्यायेन्निविडाञ्जन सप्रभाम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sabāhyābhyantaraṁ dhyāyenniviḍāñjana saprabhām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अनेन विधिना तस्य मूढबुद्धर्दुरात्मनः॥६२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anena vidhinā tasya mūḍhabuddhardurātmanaḥ||62||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v63" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विज्ञानमन्त्रविद्याद्या न कुर्वन्त्युपकारिताम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vijñānamantravidyādyā na kurvantyupakāritām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चित्ताभिसन्धिमात्रेण ह्यदृष्टस्यापि जायते॥६३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Cittābhisandhimātreṇa hyadṛṣṭasyāpi jāyate||63||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v64" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कथञ्चिदुपलब्धस्य नित्यमेवापकारिणः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kathañcidupalabdhasya nityamevāpakāriṇaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथवा सूर्यबिम्बाभं ध्यात्वा विच्छेद्यमग्रतः॥६४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Athavā sūryabimbābhaṁ dhyātvā vicchedyamagrataḥ||64||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v65" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वर्भानुरूपया शक्त्या ग्रस्तं तमनुचिन्तयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svarbhānurūpayā śaktyā grastaṁ tamanucintayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अपराधसहस्रैस्तु कोपेन महतान्वितः॥६५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aparādhasahasraistu kopena mahatānvitaḥ||65||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v66" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विधिमेनं प्रकुर्वीत क्रीडार्थं न तु जातुचित्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vidhimenaṁ prakurvīta krīḍārthaṁ na tu jātucit|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अनेन विधिना भ्रष्टो विज्ञानादपरेण च॥६६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anena vidhinā bhraṣṭo vijñānādapareṇa ca||66||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v67" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न शक्यो योजितुं भूयो यावत्तेनैव नोद्धृतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na śakyo yojituṁ bhūyo yāvattenaiva noddhṛtaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">करुणाकृष्टचित्तस्तु तस्य कृत्वा विशोधनम्॥६७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Karuṇākṛṣṭacittastu tasya kṛtvā viśodhanam||67||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v68" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्राणायामादिभिस्तीथैः प्रायश्चित्तैर्विधिश्रुतैः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prāṇāyāmādibhistīthaiḥ prāyaścittairvidhiśrutaiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततस्तस्य प्रकुर्वीत दीक्षां पूर्वोक्तवर्त्मना॥६८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatastasya prakurvīta dīkṣāṁ pūrvoktavartmanā||68||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v69" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततः सर्वमवाप्नोति फलं तस्मादनन्यधीः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tataḥ sarvamavāpnoti phalaṁ tasmādananyadhīḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एवं ज्ञात्वा प्रयत्नेन गरुमासादयेत्सुधीः॥६९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ jñātvā prayatnena garumāsādayetsudhīḥ||69||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v70" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यतः सन्तोष उत्पन्नः शिवज्ञानामृतात्मकः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yataḥ santoṣa utpannaḥ śivajñānāmṛtātmakaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न तस्यान्वेषयेद्वृत्तं शुभं वा यदि वाशुभम्॥७०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na tasyānveṣayedvṛttaṁ śubhaṁ vā yadi vāśubham||70||</span></p>

## Строфы 71–81.5 {#s8}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s8-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स एव तद्विजानाति युक्तं वायुक्तमेव वा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sa eva tadvijānāti yuktaṁ vāyuktameva vā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अकार्येषु यदा सक्तः प्राणद्रव्यापहारिषु॥७१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Akāryeṣu yadā saktaḥ prāṇadravyāpahāriṣu||71||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v72" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदा निवारणीयोऽसौ प्रणतेन विपश्चिता।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadā nivāraṇīyo'sau praṇatena vipaścitā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तेनातिवार्यमाणोऽपि यद्यसौ न निवर्तते॥७२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tenātivāryamāṇo'pi yadyasau na nivartate||72||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v73" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदान्यत्र क्वचिद्गत्वा शिवमेवानुचिन्तयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadānyatra kvacidgatvā śivamevānucintayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एष योगविधिः प्रोक्तः समासाद्योगिनां हितः॥७३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Eṣa yogavidhiḥ proktaḥ samāsādyogināṁ hitaḥ||73||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v74" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नात्र शुद्धिर्नचाशुद्धिर्न भक्ष्यादिविचारणम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nātra śuddhirnacāśuddhirna bhakṣyādivicāraṇam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न द्वैतं नापि चाद्वैतं लिङ्गपूजादिकं न च॥७४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na dvaitaṁ nāpi cādvaitaṁ liṅgapūjādikaṁ na ca||74||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v75" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न चापि तत्परित्यागो निष्परिग्रहतापि वा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na cāpi tatparityāgo niṣparigrahatāpi vā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सपरिग्रहता वापि जटाभस्मादिसङ्ग्रहः॥७५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Saparigrahatā vāpi jaṭābhasmādisaṅgrahaḥ||75||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v76" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्त्यागो न व्रतादीनां चरणाचरणं च यत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tattyāgo na vratādīnāṁ caraṇācaraṇaṁ ca yat|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">क्षेत्रादिसम्प्रवेशश्च समयादिप्रपालनम्॥७६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kṣetrādisampraveśaśca samayādiprapālanam||76||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v77" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">परस्वरूपलिङ्गादि नामगोत्रादिकं च यत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Parasvarūpaliṅgādi nāmagotrādikaṁ ca yat|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नास्मिन्विधीयते किञ्चिन्न चापि प्रतिषिध्यते॥७७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nāsminvidhīyate kiñcinna cāpi pratiṣidhyate||77||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v78" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विहितं सर्वमेवात्र प्रतिषिद्धमथापि वा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vihitaṁ sarvamevātra pratiṣiddhamathāpi vā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">किन्त्वेतदत्र देवेशि नियमेन विधीयते॥७८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kintvetadatra deveśi niyamena vidhīyate||78||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v79" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्त्वे चेतः स्थिरीकार्यं सुप्रयत्नेन योगिना।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tattve cetaḥ sthirīkāryaṁ suprayatnena yoginā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तच्च यस्य यथैव स्यात्स तथैव समाचरेत्॥७९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tacca yasya yathaiva syātsa tathaiva samācaret||79||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v80" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्त्वे निश्चलचित्तस्तु भुञ्जानो विषयानपि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tattve niścalacittastu bhuñjāno viṣayānapi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न संस्पृश्येत दोषैः स पद्मपत्रमिवाम्भसा॥८०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na saṁspṛśyeta doṣaiḥ sa padmapatramivāmbhasā||80||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v81" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विषापहारिमन्त्रादिसन्नद्धो भक्षयन्नपि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Viṣāpahārimantrādisannaddho bhakṣayannapi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विषं न मुह्यते तेन तद्वद्योगी महामतिः॥८१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Viṣaṁ na muhyate tena tadvadyogī mahāmatiḥ||81||</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इत्येतत्कथितं देवि किमन्यत्परिपृच्छसिं।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ityetatkathitaṁ devi kimanyatparipṛcchasiṁ|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इति श्रीमालिनीविजयोत्तरे तन्त्रे परमविद्याधिकारो नामाष्टादशोऽधिकारः॥१८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Iti śrīmālinīvijayottare tantre paramavidyādhikāro nāmāṣṭādaśo'dhikāraḥ||18||</span></p>
