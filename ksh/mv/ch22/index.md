## Строфы 1–10 {#s1}

<p class="pv-copy nosearch pv-anchor" id="s1-sa"><button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-sa">Копировать санскрит</button> <button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-iast">Копировать транслитерацию</button></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथ द्वाविंशतितमोऽधिकारः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Atha Dvāviṁśatitamo'dhikāraḥ|</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथान्यत्परमं गुह्यं कथयामि तव प्रिये।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Athānyatparamaṁ guhyaṁ kathayāmi tava priye|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यन्न कस्यचिदाख्यातं योगामृतमनुत्तमम्॥१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yanna kasyacidākhyātaṁ yogāmṛtamanuttamam||1||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सूर्याकृष्टिकरं नाम योगिनां योगसिद्धिदम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sūryākṛṣṭikaraṁ nāma yogināṁ yogasiddhidam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सम्यङ्मासचतुष्केण दिनाष्टाभ्यधिकेन तु॥२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Samyaṅmāsacatuṣkeṇa dināṣṭābhyadhikena tu||2||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रहरस्याष्टमो भागो नाडीकेत्यभिधीयते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Praharasyāṣṭamo bhāgo nāḍīketyabhidhīyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्पादक्रमवृद्ध्या तु प्रतिवासरमभ्यसेत्॥३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatpādakramavṛddhyā tu prativāsaramabhyaset||3||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उदयास्तमयं यावद्यत्र सूर्यः प्रदृश्यते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Udayāstamayaṁ yāvadyatra sūryaḥ pradṛśyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रदेशे तत्र विजने सर्वबाधाविवर्जिते॥४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pradeśe tatra vijane sarvabādhāvivarjite||4||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अहोरात्रोषितो योगी मकरस्थे दिवाकरे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ahorātroṣito yogī makarasthe divākare|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शूचिर्भूत्वा कृतन्यासः कृतशीतप्रतिक्रियः॥५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śūcirbhūtvā kṛtanyāsaḥ kṛtaśītapratikriyaḥ||5||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भानुबिम्बे न्यसेच्चक्रमष्टषट्द्वादशारकम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhānubimbe nyaseccakramaṣṭaṣaṭdvādaśārakam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शिवशक्तिगणोपेतं भैरवाष्टकसंयुतम्॥६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śivaśaktigaṇopetaṁ bhairavāṣṭakasaṁyutam||6||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वर्षादिऋतुसंयुक्तं मासैरृक्षादिभिर्युतम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Varṣādiṛtusaṁyuktaṁ māsairṛkṣādibhiryutam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अष्टारं चिन्तयेद्बिम्बे शेषं रश्मिषु चिन्तयेत्॥७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aṣṭāraṁ cintayedbimbe śeṣaṁ raśmiṣu cintayet||7||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्र चित्तं समाधाय प्रोक्तकालं विवक्षणः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatra cittaṁ samādhāya proktakālaṁ vivakṣaṇaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अनिमीलितनेत्रस्तु भानुबिम्बं निरीक्षयेत्॥८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Animīlitanetrastu bhānubimbaṁ nirīkṣayet||8||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततः काले व्यतिक्रान्ते सुनिमीलितलोचनः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tataḥ kāle vyatikrānte sunimīlitalocanaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रविशेदन्धकारान्तर्भुवनं निरुपद्रवम्॥९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Praviśedandhakārāntarbhuvanaṁ nirupadravam||9||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्रोन्मीलितनेत्रस्तु बिम्बाकारं प्रपश्यति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatronmīlitanetrastu bimbākāraṁ prapaśyati|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सन्धाय तत्र चैतन्यं तिष्ठेद्यावन्न पश्यति॥१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sandhāya tatra caitanyaṁ tiṣṭhedyāvanna paśyati||10||</span></p>

## Строфы 11–20 {#s2}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s2-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नष्टेऽपि चेतसा शेषं तिष्ठेत्कालमनुस्मरन्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Naṣṭe'pi cetasā śeṣaṁ tiṣṭhetkālamanusmaran|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एवं पासेन देवेशि स्थिरं तदुपजायते॥११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ pāsena deveśi sthiraṁ tadupajāyate||11||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मासद्वयेन सर्वत्र प्रेक्षते नात्र संशयः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Māsadvayena sarvatra prekṣate nātra saṁśayaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्रिभिः समीक्षते सर्वं रविबिम्बसमाकुलम्॥१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tribhiḥ samīkṣate sarvaṁ ravibimbasamākulam||12||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रोक्तकालावसानेन वृषस्थे तिग्मरोचिषि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Proktakālāvasānena vṛṣasthe tigmarociṣi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रेक्षते सूर्यबिम्बान्तः सचक्रं परमेश्वरम्॥१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prekṣate sūryabimbāntaḥ sacakraṁ parameśvaram||13||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उपलब्धं समाकृष्य मुखाग्ने स्थिरतां नयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Upalabdhaṁ samākṛṣya mukhāgne sthiratāṁ nayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आपीय पूर्ववत्पश्चाद्वृत्तिं निश्चलतां नयेत्॥१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Āpīya pūrvavatpaścādvṛttiṁ niścalatāṁ nayet||14||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्र तेन सहात्मानमेकीकृत्य मुहूर्तकम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatra tena sahātmānamekīkṛtya muhūrtakam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यावत्तिष्ठति देवेशि तावत्सन्त्यजति क्षितिम्॥१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yāvattiṣṭhati deveśi tāvatsantyajati kṣitim||15||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पश्यतो जनवृन्दस्य याति सूर्येण चैकतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Paśyato janavṛndasya yāti sūryeṇa caikataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अनेन विधिना देवि सिद्धयोगीश्वरेश्वरः॥१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anena vidhinā devi siddhayogīśvareśvaraḥ||16||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शिवाद्यवनिपर्यन्तं न क्वचित्प्रतिहन्यते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śivādyavaniparyantaṁ na kvacitpratihanyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भुक्त्वा तु विपुलान्भोगान्निष्कले लीयते परे॥१७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhuktvā tu vipulānbhogānniṣkale līyate pare||17||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदेतत् खेचरीचक्रं यत्र खेचरतां व्रजेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadetat khecarīcakraṁ yatra khecaratāṁ vrajet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सिद्धयोगीश्वरीतन्त्रे सरहस्यमुदाहृतम्॥१८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Siddhayogīśvarītantre sarahasyamudāhṛtam||18||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथवा चक्ररूपेण सबाह्याभ्यन्तरं स्वकम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Athavā cakrarūpeṇa sabāhyābhyantaraṁ svakam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">देहं चिन्तयतः पूर्वं फलं स्यान्निश्चितात्मनः॥१९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dehaṁ cintayataḥ pūrvaṁ phalaṁ syānniścitātmanaḥ||19||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उच्चरन्फादिनान्ता वा ध्वनिज्योतिर्मरुद्युताम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Uccaranphādināntā vā dhvanijyotirmarudyutām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विश्राम्य मस्तके चित्तं क्षणमेकं विचक्षणः॥२०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Viśrāmya mastake cittaṁ kṣaṇamekaṁ vicakṣaṇaḥ||20||</span></p>

## Строфы 21–30 {#s3}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s3-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्रीशूलेन प्रयोगेन सद्यस्त्यजति मेदिनीम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Trīśūlena prayogena sadyastyajati medinīm|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एवं समभ्यसन्मासाच्चक्रवद्भ्रमति क्षितौ॥२१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ samabhyasanmāsāccakravadbhramati kṣitau||21||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v22" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मुहूर्तं स्पृशते भूमिं मुहूर्ताच्च नभस्तलम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Muhūrtaṁ spṛśate bhūmiṁ muhūrtācca nabhastalam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शिवारावादि कुरुते वलनास्फोटनानि च॥२२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śivārāvādi kurute valanāsphoṭanāni ca||22||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v23" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मुद्राबन्धादिकं वाथ भाषा वा वक्त्यनेकधा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mudrābandhādikaṁ vātha bhāṣā vā vaktyanekadhā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">षण्मासान्मेदिनी त्यक्त्वा समाधिस्थो दृढेन्द्रियः॥२३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ṣaṇmāsānmedinī tyaktvā samādhistho dṛḍhendriyaḥ||23||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v24" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तिष्ठते हस्तमात्रेण गगने योगचिन्तकः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tiṣṭhate hastamātreṇa gagane yogacintakaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पश्यते योगिनीवृन्दमनेकाकारलक्षणम्॥२४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Paśyate yoginīvṛndamanekākāralakṣaṇam||24||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v25" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">संवत्सरेण युक्तात्मा तत्समानः प्रजायते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Saṁvatsareṇa yuktātmā tatsamānaḥ prajāyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पश्यतामेव लोकानां तेजोभिर्भासयन्दिशः॥२५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Paśyatāmeva lokānāṁ tejobhirbhāsayandiśaḥ||25||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v26" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यात्युत्कृष्य महीपृष्ठात् खेचरीणां पतिर्भवेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yātyutkṛṣya mahīpṛṣṭhāt khecarīṇāṁ patirbhavet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मुद्रा खगेश्वरी नाम कथिता योगिनीमते॥२६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mudrā khageśvarī nāma kathitā yoginīmate||26||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v27" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जागरित्वाथ वा योगी त्र्यहोरात्रमतन्द्रितः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jāgaritvātha vā yogī tryahorātramatandritaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चतुर्थेऽह्नि निशारम्भे पूजयित्वा महेश्वरम्॥२७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Caturthe'hni niśārambhe pūjayitvā maheśvaram||27||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v28" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततोऽन्धकारे बहुले कृतरक्षाविधिर्बुधः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tato'ndhakāre bahule kṛtarakṣāvidhirbudhaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भ्रुवोर्मध्ये समाधाय क्षणं चेतः प्रपश्यति॥२८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhruvormadhye samādhāya kṣaṇaṁ cetaḥ prapaśyati||28||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v29" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तेजो रूपप्रतीकाशं पर्यङ्कासनमास्थितः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tejo rūpapratīkāśaṁ paryaṅkāsanamāsthitaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रयोगं त्वेव सततं योगयुक्तः समभ्यसेतु॥२९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prayogaṁ tveva satataṁ yogayuktaḥ samabhyasetu||29||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v30" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पश्यते मासमात्रेण गृहान्तर्वस्तु यत् स्थितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Paśyate māsamātreṇa gṛhāntarvastu yat sthitam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">द्वाभ्यां बहिः स्थितं सर्वं त्रिभिः पत्तनसंस्थितम्॥३०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dvābhyāṁ bahiḥ sthitaṁ sarvaṁ tribhiḥ pattanasaṁsthitam||30||</span></p>

## Строфы 31–34 {#s4}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s4-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चतुर्भिर्विपयान्तःस्थं पञ्चभिर्मण्डलावधि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Caturbhirvipayāntaḥsthaṁ pañcabhirmaṇḍalāvadhi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">षड्भिर्मासैर्महायोगी च्छिद्रां पश्यति मेदिनीम्॥३१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ṣaḍbhirmāsairmahāyogī cchidrāṁ paśyati medinīm||31||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v32" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वज्ञत्वमवाप्नोति वत्सरान्नात्र संशयः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvajñatvamavāpnoti vatsarānnātra saṁśayaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">योगिनीसिद्धसङ्घस्य सद्भावव्याप्तिसंस्थितम्॥३२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yoginīsiddhasaṅghasya sadbhāvavyāptisaṁsthitam||32||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v33" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पश्यते योगयुक्तात्मा तत्समानश्च जायते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Paśyate yogayuktātmā tatsamānaśca jāyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अनेनैव विधानेन स्वस्तिकासनसंस्थितः॥३३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anenaiva vidhānena svastikāsanasaṁsthitaḥ||33||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v34" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बिन्दुं नानाविधं त्यक्त्वा शुद्धरूपमनुस्मरेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Binduṁ nānāvidhaṁ tyaktvā śuddharūpamanusmaret|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तेनापि सर्वं पूर्वोक्तं व्याप्नोति फलमुत्तमम्॥३४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tenāpi sarvaṁ pūrvoktaṁ vyāpnoti phalamuttamam||34||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इति श्रीमालिनीविजयोत्तरे तन्त्रे सूर्याकृष्ट्यधिकारो द्वाविंशतितमः॥२२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Iti śrīmālinīvijayottare tantre sūryākṛṣṭyadhikāro dvāviṁśatitamaḥ||22||</span></p>
