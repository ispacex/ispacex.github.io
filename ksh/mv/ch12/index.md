## Строфы 1–10 {#s1}

<p class="pv-copy nosearch pv-anchor" id="s1-sa"><button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-sa">Копировать санскрит</button> <button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-iast">Копировать транслитерацию</button></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथ द्वादशोऽधिकारः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Atha Dvādaśo'dhikāraḥ|</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथैतां देवदेअस्य श्रुत्वा वाचमतिस्फुटाम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Athaitāṁ devadeasya śrutvā vācamatisphuṭām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रहर्षोत्फुल्लनयना जगदानन्दकारिणी॥१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Praharṣotphullanayanā jagadānandakāriṇī||1||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सन्तोषामृतसन्तृप्ता देवी देवगणार्चिता।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Santoṣāmṛtasantṛptā devī devagaṇārcitā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रणम्यान्धकहन्तारं पुनराहेति भारतीम्॥२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Praṇamyāndhakahantāraṁ punarāheti bhāratīm||2||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूर्वमेव त्वया प्रोक्तं योगी योगं समभ्यसेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūrvameva tvayā proktaṁ yogī yogaṁ samabhyaset|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तस्याभ्यासः कथं कार्यः कथ्यतां त्रिपुरान्तक॥३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tasyābhyāsaḥ kathaṁ kāryaḥ kathyatāṁ tripurāntaka||3||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एवमुक्तो जगद्धात्र्या भैरवो भयनाशनः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evamukto jagaddhātryā bhairavo bhayanāśanaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्राह प्रसन्नगम्भीरां गिरमेतामुदारधीः॥४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prāha prasannagambhīrāṁ girametāmudāradhīḥ||4||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">योगाभ्यासविधिं देवि कथ्यमानं मया शृणु।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yogābhyāsavidhiṁ devi kathyamānaṁ mayā śṛṇu|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्थिरीभूतेन येनेह योगी सिद्धिमवाप्स्यति॥५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sthirībhūtena yeneha yogī siddhimavāpsyati||5||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">गुहायां भूगृहे वापि निःशब्दे सुमनोरमे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Guhāyāṁ bhūgṛhe vāpi niḥśabde sumanorame|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वाबाधाविनिर्मुक्ते योगी योगं समभ्यसेत्॥६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvābādhāvinirmukte yogī yogaṁ samabhyaset||6||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जितासनो जितमना जितप्राणो जितेन्द्रियः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jitāsano jitamanā jitaprāṇo jitendriyaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जितमिद्रो जितक्रोधो जितोद्वेगो गतव्यथः॥७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jitamidro jitakrodho jitodvego gatavyathaḥ||7||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">लक्ष्यभेदेन वा सर्वमथवा चित्तभेदतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Lakṣyabhedena vā sarvamathavā cittabhedataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">धरादिशक्तिपर्यन्तं योगीशस्तु प्रसाधयेत्॥८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dharādiśaktiparyantaṁ yogīśastu prasādhayet||8||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">व्योमविग्रहविन्द्वर्णभुवनध्वनिभेदतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vyomavigrahavindvarṇabhuvanadhvanibhedataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">लक्ष्यभेदः स्मृतः षोढा यथावदुपदिश्यते॥९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Lakṣyabhedaḥ smṛtaḥ ṣoḍhā yathāvadupadiśyate||9||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बाह्याभ्यन्तरभेदेन समुच्चयकृतेन च।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bāhyābhyantarabhedena samuccayakṛtena ca|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्रिविधं कीर्तितं व्योम दशधा विन्दुरिष्यते॥१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Trividhaṁ kīrtitaṁ vyoma daśadhā vinduriṣyate||10||</span></p>

## Строфы 11–20 {#s2}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s2-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कदम्बगोलकाकारः स्फुरत्तारकसन्निभः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kadambagolakākāraḥ sphurattārakasannibhaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शुक्लादिभेदभेदेन एकोऽपि दशधा मतः॥११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śuklādibhedabhedena eko'pi daśadhā mataḥ||11||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चिञ्चिनीचीरवाकादिप्रभेदाद्दशधा ध्वनिः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ciñcinīcīravākādiprabhedāddaśadhā dhvaniḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विग्रहः स्वाणुभेदाच्च द्विधा भिन्नोप्यनेकधा॥१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vigrahaḥ svāṇubhedācca dvidhā bhinnopyanekadhā||12||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भुवनानां न सङ्ख्यास्ति वर्णानां सा शतार्धिका।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhuvanānāṁ na saṅkhyāsti varṇānāṁ sā śatārdhikā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एकस्मिन्नपि साध्ये वै लक्षेदत्रानुषङ्गतः॥१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ekasminnapi sādhye vai lakṣedatrānuṣaṅgataḥ||13||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अन्यान्यपि फलानि स्युर्लक्ष्यभेदः स उच्यते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anyānyapi phalāni syurlakṣyabhedaḥ sa ucyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एकमेव फलं यत्र चित्तभेदस्त्वसौ मतः॥१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ekameva phalaṁ yatra cittabhedastvasau mataḥ||14||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v15" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">होमदीक्षाविशुद्धात्मा समावेशोपदेशवान्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Homadīkṣāviśuddhātmā samāveśopadeśavān|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यं सिषाधयिषुर्योगमादावेव समाचरेत्॥१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yaṁ siṣādhayiṣuryogamādāveva samācaret||15||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हस्तयोस्तु पराबीजं न्यस्य शक्तिमनुस्मरेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Hastayostu parābījaṁ nyasya śaktimanusmaret|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">महामुद्राप्रयोगेन विपरीतविधौ बुधः॥१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mahāmudrāprayogena viparītavidhau budhaḥ||16||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ज्वलद्वह्निप्रतीकाशं पादाग्रान्मस्तकान्तिकम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jvaladvahnipratīkāśaṁ pādāgrānmastakāntikam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नमस्कारं ततः पश्चाद्बद्धा हृदि धृतानिलः॥१७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Namaskāraṁ tataḥ paścādbaddhā hṛdi dhṛtānilaḥ||17||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v18" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वरूपेण पराबीजमतिदीप्तमनुस्मरेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svarūpeṇa parābījamatidīptamanusmaret|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तस्य मात्रात्रयं ध्यायेत्कखत्रयविनिर्गतम्॥१८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tasya mātrātrayaṁ dhyāyetkakhatrayavinirgatam||18||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततस्तालशताद्योगी समावेशमवाप्नुयात्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatastālaśatādyogī samāveśamavāpnuyāt|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ब्रह्मघ्नोऽपि हि सप्ताहात्प्रतिवासरमभ्यसेत्॥१९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Brahmaghno'pi hi saptāhātprativāsaramabhyaset||19||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एवमाविष्टदेहस्तु यथोक्तं विधिमाचरेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evamāviṣṭadehastu yathoktaṁ vidhimācaret|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यः पुनर्गुरुणैवादौ कृतावेशविधिक्रमः॥२०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yaḥ punarguruṇaivādau kṛtāveśavidhikramaḥ||20||</span></p>

## Строфы 21–30 {#s3}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s3-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स वासनानुभावेन भूमिकाजयमारभेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sa vāsanānubhāvena bhūmikājayamārabhet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">गणनाथं नमस्कृत्य संस्मृत्य त्रिगुरुक्रमम्॥२१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Gaṇanāthaṁ namaskṛtya saṁsmṛtya trigurukramam||21||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v22" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सम्यगाविष्टदेहः स्यादिति ध्यायेदनन्यधीः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Samyagāviṣṭadehaḥ syāditi dhyāyedananyadhīḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वदेहं हेमसङ्काशं तुर्याश्रं वज्रलाञ्छितम्॥२२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svadehaṁ hemasaṅkāśaṁ turyāśraṁ vajralāñchitam||22||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v23" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ततो गुरुत्वमायाति सप्तविंशतिभिर्दिनैः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tato gurutvamāyāti saptaviṁśatibhirdinaiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दिवसात्सप्तमादूर्ध्वं जडता चास्य जायते॥२३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Divasātsaptamādūrdhvaṁ jaḍatā cāsya jāyate||23||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v24" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">षड्भिर्मासैर्जितव्याधिर्द्रुतहेमनिभो भवेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ṣaḍbhirmāsairjitavyādhirdrutahemanibho bhavet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वज्रदेहस्त्रिभिश्चाब्दैर्नवनागपराक्रमः॥२४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vajradehastribhiścābdairnavanāgaparākramaḥ||24||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v25" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एषा ते पार्थिवी शुद्धा धारणा परिकीर्तिता।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Eṣā te pārthivī śuddhā dhāraṇā parikīrtitā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आद्या पूर्वोदिते देवि भेदे पञ्चदशात्मके॥२५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ādyā pūrvodite devi bhede pañcadaśātmake||25||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v26" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सव्यापारं स्मरेद्देहं दुर्तहेमसमप्रभम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Savyāpāraṁ smareddehaṁ durtahemasamaprabham|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उपविष्टं च तुर्याश्रे मण्डले वज्रभूषिते॥२६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Upaviṣṭaṁ ca turyāśre maṇḍale vajrabhūṣite||26||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v27" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सप्ताहाद्गुरुतामेति मासाद्व्याधिविवर्जितः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Saptāhādgurutāmeti māsādvyādhivivarjitaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">षड्भिर्मासैर्धरान्तःस्थं सर्वं जानाति तत्त्वतः॥२७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ṣaḍbhirmāsairdharāntaḥsthaṁ sarvaṁ jānāti tattvataḥ||27||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v28" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्रिभिरब्दैर्महीं भुङ्क्ते सप्ताम्भोनिधिमेखलाम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tribhirabdairmahīṁ bhuṅkte saptāmbhonidhimekhalām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">द्वितीयः कथितो भेदस्तृतीयमधुना शृणु॥२८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dvitīyaḥ kathito bhedastṛtīyamadhunā śṛṇu||28||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v29" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तद्वदेव स्मरेद्देहं किन्तु व्यापारवर्जितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadvadeva smareddehaṁ kintu vyāpāravarjitam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूर्वोक्तं फलमाप्नोति तद्वत्पातालसंयुतम्॥२९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūrvoktaṁ phalamāpnoti tadvatpātālasaṁyutam||29||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v30" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चतुर्थे हृद्गतं ध्यायेद्द्वादशाङ्गुलमायतम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Caturthe hṛdgataṁ dhyāyeddvādaśāṅgulamāyatam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूर्ववर्णस्वरूपेण सव्याअपारमतन्द्रितः॥३०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūrvavarṇasvarūpeṇa savyāapāramatandritaḥ||30||</span></p>

## Строфы 31–40 {#s4}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s4-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्राप्य पूर्वोदितं सर्वं पातालाधिपतिर्भवेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prāpya pūrvoditaṁ sarvaṁ pātālādhipatirbhavet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदेव स्थिरमाप्नोति निर्व्यापारे तु पञ्चमे॥३१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadeva sthiramāpnoti nirvyāpāre tu pañcame||31||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v32" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्फुरत्सूर्यनिभं पीतं षष्ठे कृष्णं घनावृतम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sphuratsūryanibhaṁ pītaṁ ṣaṣṭhe kṛṣṇaṁ ghanāvṛtam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">निस्तरङ्गं स्मरेत्तद्वत्सप्तमेऽपि विचक्षणः॥३२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nistaraṅgaṁ smarettadvatsaptame'pi vicakṣaṇaḥ||32||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v33" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">द्वयेऽप्यत्र स्थिरीभूते भुर्भुवःस्वरिति त्रयम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dvaye'pyatra sthirībhūte bhurbhuvaḥsvariti trayam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वेत्ति भुङ्क्ते च लोकानां पुरोक्तैरेव वत्सरैः॥३३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vetti bhuṅkte ca lokānāṁ puroktaireva vatsaraiḥ||33||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v34" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सकलं हृदयान्तस्थमात्मानं कनकप्रभम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sakalaṁ hṛdayāntasthamātmānaṁ kanakaprabham|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वप्रभाद्योतिताशेषदेहान्तमनुचिन्तयेत्॥३४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svaprabhādyotitāśeṣadehāntamanucintayet||34||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v35" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सव्यापारादिभेदेन सप्तलोकीं तु पूर्ववत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Savyāpārādibhedena saptalokīṁ tu pūrvavat|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वेत्ति भुङ्क्ते स्थिरीभूते भेदेऽस्मिन्नवमे बुधः॥३५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vetti bhuṅkte sthirībhūte bhede'sminnavame budhaḥ||35||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v36" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">रविबिम्बनिभं पीतं पूर्ववद्द्वितयं स्मरेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ravibimbanibhaṁ pītaṁ pūrvavaddvitayaṁ smaret|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ब्रह्मलोकमवाप्नोति पूर्वोक्तेनैव वर्त्मना॥३६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Brahmalokamavāpnoti pūrvoktenaiva vartmanā||36||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v37" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अधः प्रकाशितं पीतं द्विरूपं पूर्ववन्महत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Adhaḥ prakāśitaṁ pītaṁ dvirūpaṁ pūrvavanmahat|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चिन्तयेन्मत्समो भूत्वा मल्लोकमनुगच्छति॥३७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Cintayenmatsamo bhūtvā mallokamanugacchati||37||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v38" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सबाह्याभ्यन्तरं पीतं तेजः सर्वप्रकाशकम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sabāhyābhyantaraṁ pītaṁ tejaḥ sarvaprakāśakam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चिन्तयेच्छतरुद्राणामधिपत्वमवाप्नुयात्॥३८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Cintayecchatarudrāṇāmadhipatvamavāpnuyāt||38||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v39" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इत्येवं पृथिवीतत्त्वमभ्यस्यं दशपञ्चधा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ityevaṁ pṛthivītattvamabhyasyaṁ daśapañcadhā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">योगिभिर्योगसिद्ध्यर्थं तत्फलानां बुभुक्षया॥३९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yogibhiryogasiddhyarthaṁ tatphalānāṁ bubhukṣayā||39||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v40" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">योग्यतावशसञ्जाता यस्य यत्रैव वासना।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yogyatāvaśasañjātā yasya yatraiva vāsanā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स तत्रैव नियोक्तत्र्यो दीक्षाकाले विचक्षणैः॥४०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sa tatraiva niyoktatryo dīkṣākāle vicakṣaṇaiḥ||40||</span></p>

## Строфы 41–42 {#s5}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s5-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यो यत्र योजितस्तत्त्वे स तस्मान्न निवर्तते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yo yatra yojitastattve sa tasmānna nivartate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्फलं सर्वमासाद्य शिवयुक्तोऽपवृज्यते॥४१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatphalaṁ sarvamāsādya śivayukto'pavṛjyate||41||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v42" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अयुक्तोऽप्यूर्ध्वसंशुद्धिसम्प्राप्तभुवनेशतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ayukto'pyūrdhvasaṁśuddhisamprāptabhuvaneśataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शुद्धाच्छिवत्वमायाति दग्धसंसारबन्धनः॥४२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śuddhācchivatvamāyāti dagdhasaṁsārabandhanaḥ||42||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इति श्रीमालिनीविजयोत्तरे तन्त्रे प्रथमधारणाधिकारो द्वादशः॥१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Iti śrīmālinīvijayottare tantre prathamadhāraṇādhikāro dvādaśaḥ||12||</span></p>
