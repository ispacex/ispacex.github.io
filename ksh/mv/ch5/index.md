## Строфы 1–10 {#s1}

<p class="pv-copy nosearch pv-anchor" id="s1-sa"><button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-sa">Копировать санскрит</button> <button type="button" data-pv-copy="w1" data-pv-what="pv-iast">Копировать транслитерацию</button></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथ पञ्चमोऽधिकारः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Atha Pañcamo'dhikāraḥ|</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अथातः सम्प्रवक्ष्यामि भुवनाध्वानमीश्वरि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Athātaḥ sampravakṣyāmi bhuvanādhvānamīśvari|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आदौ कालाग्निभुवनं शोधितव्यं प्रयत्नतः॥१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ādau kālāgnibhuvanaṁ śodhitavyaṁ prayatnataḥ||1||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अवीचिः कुम्भीपाकश्च रौरवश्च तृतीयकः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Avīciḥ kumbhīpākaśca rauravaśca tṛtīyakaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कूष्माण्डभुवने शुद्धे सर्वे शुद्धा न संशयः॥२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kūṣmāṇḍabhuvane śuddhe sarve śuddhā na saṁśayaḥ||2||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पातालानि ततः सप्त तेषामादौ महातलम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pātālāni tataḥ sapta teṣāmādau mahātalam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">रसातलं ततश्चान्यत्तलातलमतः परम्॥३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Rasātalaṁ tataścānyattalātalamataḥ param||3||</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सुतलं नितलं चेति वितलं तलमेव च।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sutalaṁ nitalaṁ ceti vitalaṁ talameva ca|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हाटकेन विशुद्धेन सर्वेषां शुद्धिरिष्यते॥४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Hāṭakena viśuddhena sarveṣāṁ śuddhiriṣyate||4||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदूर्ध्वं पृथिवी ज्ञेया सप्तद्वीपार्णवान्विता।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadūrdhvaṁ pṛthivī jñeyā saptadvīpārṇavānvitā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">देवानामाश्रयो मेरुस्तन्मध्ये संव्यवस्थितः॥५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Devānāmāśrayo merustanmadhye saṁvyavasthitaḥ||5||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भुवोलोकस्तदूर्ध्वे च स्वर्लोकस्तस्य चोपरि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhuvolokastadūrdhve ca svarlokastasya copari|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">महो जनस्तपः सत्यमित्येतल्लोकसप्तकम्॥६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Maho janastapaḥ satyamityetallokasaptakam||6||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चतुर्दशविधो यत्र भूतग्रामः प्रवर्तते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Caturdaśavidho yatra bhūtagrāmaḥ pravartate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्थावरः सर्पजातिश्च पक्षिजातिस्तथापरा॥७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sthāvaraḥ sarpajātiśca pakṣijātistathāparā||7||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मृगसञ्ज्ञश्च पश्चाख्यः पञ्चमोऽन्यश्च मानुषः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mṛgasañjñaśca paścākhyaḥ pañcamo'nyaśca mānuṣaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पैशाचो राक्षसो याक्षो गान्धर्वश्चैन्द्र एव च॥८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Paiśāco rākṣaso yākṣo gāndharvaścaindra eva ca||8||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सौम्यश्च प्राजापत्यश्च ब्राह्मश्चात्र चतुर्दश।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Saumyaśca prājāpatyaśca brāhmaścātra caturdaśa|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वस्यैवास्य संशुद्धिर्ब्राह्मे संशोधिते सति॥९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvasyaivāsya saṁśuddhirbrāhme saṁśodhite sati||9||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भुवनं वैष्णवं तस्मान्मदीयं तदनन्तरम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhuvanaṁ vaiṣṇavaṁ tasmānmadīyaṁ tadanantaram|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्र शुद्धे भवेच्छुद्धं सर्वमेतन्न संशयः॥१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatra śuddhe bhavecchuddhaṁ sarvametanna saṁśayaḥ||10||</span></p>

## Строфы 11–22 {#s2}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s2-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कालाग्निपूर्वकैरेभिर्भुवनैः पञ्चभिः प्रिये।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kālāgnipūrvakairebhirbhuvanaiḥ pañcabhiḥ priye|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शुद्धैः सर्वमिदं शुद्धं ब्रह्माण्डान्तर्व्यवस्थितम्॥११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śuddhaiḥ sarvamidaṁ śuddhaṁ brahmāṇḍāntarvyavasthitam||11||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तद्बहिः शतरुद्राणां भुवनानि पृथक् पृथक्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadbahiḥ śatarudrāṇāṁ bhuvanāni pṛthak pṛthak|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दश संशोधयेत्पश्चादेकं तन्नायकावृतम्॥१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Daśa saṁśodhayetpaścādekaṁ tannāyakāvṛtam||12||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अनन्तः प्रथमस्तेषां कपालीशस्तथापरः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anantaḥ prathamasteṣāṁ kapālīśastathāparaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अग्निरुद्रो यमश्चैव नैर्ऋतो बल एव च॥१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Agnirudro yamaścaiva nairṛto bala eva ca||13||</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शीघ्रो निधीश्वरश्चैव सर्वविद्याधिपोऽपरः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śīghro nidhīśvaraścaiva sarvavidyādhipo'paraḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शम्भुश्च वीरभद्रश्च विधूमज्वलनप्रभः॥१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śambhuśca vīrabhadraśca vidhūmajvalanaprabhaḥ||14||</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एभिर्दशैकसङ्ख्यातैः शुद्धैं शुद्धं शतं मतम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ebhirdaśaikasaṅkhyātaiḥ śuddhaiṁ śuddhaṁ śataṁ matam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उपरिष्टात्पुरस्तेषामष्टकाः पञ्च संस्थिताः॥१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Upariṣṭātpurasteṣāmaṣṭakāḥ pañca saṁsthitāḥ||15||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v16" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">लकुली भारभूतिश्च दिण्ढ्याषाढी सपुष्करौ।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Lakulī bhārabhūtiśca diṇḍhyāṣāḍhī sapuṣkarau|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नैमिषं च प्रभासं च अमरेशमथाष्टकम्॥१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Naimiṣaṁ ca prabhāsaṁ ca amareśamathāṣṭakam||16||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एतत्पत्यष्टकं प्रोक्तमतो गुह्यातिगुह्यकम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Etatpatyaṣṭakaṁ proktamato guhyātiguhyakam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्र भैरवकेदारमहाकालाः समध्यमाः॥१७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatra bhairavakedāramahākālāḥ samadhyamāḥ||17||</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आम्रातिकेशजल्पेशश्रीशैलाः सहरीन्दवः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Āmrātikeśajalpeśaśrīśailāḥ saharīndavaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भीमेश्वरमहेन्द्रादृहासाः सविमलेश्वराः॥१८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhīmeśvaramahendrādṛhāsāḥ savimaleśvarāḥ||18||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v19" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कनखलं नाखलं च कुरुक्षेत्रं गया तथा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kanakhalaṁ nākhalaṁ ca kurukṣetraṁ gayā tathā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">गुह्यमेतत्तृतीयं तु पवित्रमधुनोच्यते॥१९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Guhyametattṛtīyaṁ tu pavitramadhunocyate||19||</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्थाणुस्वर्णाक्षकावाद्यौ भद्रगोकर्णकौ परौ।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sthāṇusvarṇākṣakāvādyau bhadragokarṇakau parau|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">महाकालाविमुक्तेशरुद्रकोट्यम्बरापदाः॥२०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mahākālāvimukteśarudrakoṭyambarāpadāḥ||20||</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्थूलः स्थूलेश्वरः शङ्कुकर्णकालञ्जरावपि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sthūlaḥ sthūleśvaraḥ śaṅkukarṇakālañjarāvapi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मण्डलेश्वरमाकोटद्विरण्डछगलाण्डकौ॥२१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Maṇḍaleśvaramākoṭadviraṇḍachagalāṇḍakau||21||</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्थाण्वष्टकमिति प्रोक्तमहङ्कारावधि स्थितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sthāṇvaṣṭakamiti proktamahaṅkārāvadhi sthitam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">देवयोन्यष्टकं बुधौ कथ्यमानं मया शृणु॥२२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Devayonyaṣṭakaṁ budhau kathyamānaṁ mayā śṛṇu||22||</span></p>

## Строфы 23–32 {#s3}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s3-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पैशाचं राक्षसं याक्षं गान्धर्वं चैन्द्रमेव च।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Paiśācaṁ rākṣasaṁ yākṣaṁ gāndharvaṁ caindrameva ca|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तथा सौम्यं सप्राजेशं ब्राह्ममष्टममिष्यते॥२३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tathā saumyaṁ saprājeśaṁ brāhmamaṣṭamamiṣyate||23||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v24" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">योगाष्टकं प्रधाने तु तत्रादावकृतं भवेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yogāṣṭakaṁ pradhāne tu tatrādāvakṛtaṁ bhavet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कृतं च वैभवं ब्राह्मं वैष्णवं तदनन्तरम्॥२४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kṛtaṁ ca vaibhavaṁ brāhmaṁ vaiṣṇavaṁ tadanantaram||24||</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कौमारमौमं श्रैकण्ठमिति योगाष्टकं तथा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kaumāramaumaṁ śraikaṇṭhamiti yogāṣṭakaṁ tathā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पुरुषे वामभीमोग्रभवेशानैकवीरकाः॥२५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Puruṣe vāmabhīmograbhaveśānaikavīrakāḥ||25||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v26" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रचण्डोमाधवाजाश्च अनन्तैकशिवावथ।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pracaṇḍomādhavājāśca anantaikaśivāvatha|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">क्रोधेशचण्डौ विद्यायां संवर्तो ज्योतिरेव च॥२६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Krodheśacaṇḍau vidyāyāṁ saṁvarto jyotireva ca||26||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v27" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कलातत्त्वे परिज्ञेयौ सुरपञ्चान्तकौ परे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kalātattve parijñeyau surapañcāntakau pare|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एकवीरशिखण्डीशश्रीकण्ठाः कालमाश्रिताः॥२७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ekavīraśikhaṇḍīśaśrīkaṇṭhāḥ kālamāśritāḥ||27||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v28" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">महातेजः प्रभृतयो मण्डलेशानसञ्ज्ञकाः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mahātejaḥ prabhṛtayo maṇḍaleśānasañjñakāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मायातत्त्वे स्थितास्तत्र वामदेवभवोद्भवौ॥२८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Māyātattve sthitāstatra vāmadevabhavodbhavau||28||</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एकपिङ्गेक्षणेशानभुवनेशपुरः सराः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ekapiṅgekṣaṇeśānabhuvaneśapuraḥ sarāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अङ्गुष्ठमात्रसहिताः कालानलसमत्विषः॥२९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aṅguṣṭhamātrasahitāḥ kālānalasamatviṣaḥ||29||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v30" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विद्यातत्त्वेऽपि पञ्चाहुर्भुवनानि मनीषिणः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vidyātattve'pi pañcāhurbhuvanāni manīṣiṇaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्र हालाहलः पूर्वो रुद्रः क्रोधस्तथापरः॥३०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatra hālāhalaḥ pūrvo rudraḥ krodhastathāparaḥ||30||</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अम्बिका च अघोरा च वामदेवी च कीर्त्यते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ambikā ca aghorā ca vāmadevī ca kīrtyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ईश्वरे पिवनाद्याः स्युरघोरान्ता महेश्वराः॥३१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Īśvare pivanādyāḥ syuraghorāntā maheśvarāḥ||31||</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">रौद्री ज्येष्ठा च वामा च तथा शक्तिसदाशिवौ।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Raudrī jyeṣṭhā ca vāmā ca tathā śaktisadāśivau|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एतानि सकले पञ्च भुवनानि विदुर्बुधाः॥३२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Etāni sakale pañca bhuvanāni vidurbudhāḥ||32||</span></p>

## Строфы 33–35.5 {#s4}

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="s4-sa" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एवं तु सर्वतत्त्वेषु शतमष्टादशोत्तरम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ tu sarvatattveṣu śatamaṣṭādaśottaram|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भुवनानां परिज्ञेयं सङ्क्षेपान्न तु विस्तरात्॥३३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhuvanānāṁ parijñeyaṁ saṅkṣepānna tu vistarāt||33||</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शुद्धेनानेन शुद्ध्यन्ति सर्वाण्यपि न शंशयः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śuddhenānena śuddhyanti sarvāṇyapi na śaṁśayaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वमार्गविशुद्धौ तु कर्तव्यायां महामतिः॥३४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvamārgaviśuddhau tu kartavyāyāṁ mahāmatiḥ||34||</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सकलावधि संशोध्य शिवे योगं प्रकल्पयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sakalāvadhi saṁśodhya śive yogaṁ prakalpayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बुभुक्षोः सकलं ध्यात्वा योगं कुर्वीत योगवित्॥३५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bubhukṣoḥ sakalaṁ dhyātvā yogaṁ kurvīta yogavit||35||</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इत्येष कीर्तितो मार्गो भुवनाख्यस्य मे मतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ityeṣa kīrtito mārgo bhuvanākhyasya me mataḥ|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इति श्रीमालिनीविजयोत्तरे तन्त्रे भुवनाध्वाधिकारः पञ्चमः॥५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Iti śrīmālinīvijayottare tantre bhuvanādhvādhikāraḥ pañcamaḥ||5||</span></p>
