<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">यदा पुनः करणेश्वरीप्रसरसङ्कोचं सम्पाद्य सर्गसंहारक्रमपरिशीलनयुक्तिमाविशति तदा</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yadā punaḥ karaṇeśvarīprasarasaṅkocaṁ sampādya sargasaṁhārakramapariśīlanayuktimāviśati tadā</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बललाभे विश्वमात्मसात्करोति॥१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Balalābhe viśvamātmasātkaroti||15||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">चितिरेव देहप्राणाद्याच्छादननिमज्जनेन स्वरूपमुन्मग्नत्वेन स्फारयन्ती बलं यथोक्तम्</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Citireva dehaprāṇādyācchādananimajjanena svarūpamunmagnatvena sphārayantī balaṁ yathoktam</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">तदाक्रम्य बलं मन्त्राः...।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadākramya balaṁ mantrāḥ...|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति। एवं च बललाभे - उन्मग्नस्वरूपाश्रयणे क्षित्यादिसदाशिवान्तं विश्वमात्मसात्करोति - स्वस्वरूपाभेदेन निर्भासयति। तदुक्तं पूर्वगुरुभिः स्वभाषामयेषु क्रमसूत्रेषु</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti| Evaṁ ca balalābhe - unmagnasvarūpāśrayaṇe kṣityādisadāśivāntaṁ viśvamātmasātkaroti - Svasvarūpābhedena nirbhāsayati| Taduktaṁ pūrvagurubhiḥ svabhāṣāmayeṣu kramasūtreṣu</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">यथा वह्निरुद्बोधितो दाह्यं दहति तथा विषयपाशान्भक्षयेत्</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yathā vahnirudbodhito dāhyaṁ dahati tathā viṣayapāśānbhakṣayet</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">न चैवं वक्तव्यम् - विश्वात्मसात्काररूपा समावेशभूः कादाचित्की कथमुपादेया इयं स्यादिति यतो देहाद्युन्मज्जननिमज्जनवशेनेदमस्याः कादाचित्कत्वमिवाभाति। वस्तुतस्तु चितिस्वातन्त्र्यावभासितदेहाद्युन्मज्जनादेव कादाचित्कत्वम्। एषा तु सदैव प्रकाशमानान्यथा तद्देहाद्यपि न प्रकाशेत। अत एव देहादिप्रमातृताभिमाननिमज्जनायाभ्यासो न तु सदा प्रथमानतासारप्रमातृताप्राप्त्यर्थम्</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na caivaṁ vaktavyam - viśvātmasātkārarūpā samāveśabhūḥ kādācitkī kathamupādeyā iyaṁ syāditi yato dehādyunmajjananimajjanavaśenedamasyāḥ kādācitkatvamivābhāti| Vastutastu citisvātantryāvabhāsitadehādyunmajjanādeva kādācitkatvam| Eṣā tu sadaiva prakāśamānānyathā taddehādyapi na prakāśeta| Ata eva dehādipramātṛtābhimānanimajjanāyābhyāso na tu sadā prathamānatāsārapramātṛtāprāptyartham</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति श्रीप्रत्यभिज्ञाकाराः॥१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti śrīpratyabhijñākārāḥ||15||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>However<rp> (</rp><rt>punar</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>when<rp> (</rp><rt>yadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(one oneself,)_ <ruby>after carrying out<rp> (</rp><rt>sampādya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> the expansion <ruby>and contraction of the goddesses of the senses<rp> (</rp><rt>karaṇa-īśvarī-prasara-saṅkocam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>enters<rp> (</rp><rt>āviśati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> into the practice of contacting <ruby>the succession of manifestation and withdrawal<rp> (</rp><rt>sarga-saṁhāra-krama-pariśīlana-yuktim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>then<rp> (</rp><rt>tadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>:</p>

<p class="pv-tr pv-anchor" id="s1-ru" markdown="1">Когда _(человек)_ <ruby>обретает<rp> (</rp><rt>lābhe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Силу Citi, или Универсального Сознания<rp> (</rp><rt>bala</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>он делает<rp> (</rp><rt>karoti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>вселенную<rp> (</rp><rt>viśvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>подобной себе<rp> (</rp><rt>ātmasāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(т.е. он уподобляет вселенную самому себе)_||15||</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>Citi Herself<rp> (</rp><rt>citiḥ eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(becomes <span class="pv-note">— although, paradoxically, She always is —</span>)_ <ruby>Bala or Power<rp> (</rp><rt>balam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> by submerging <ruby>the covering/cloak of body, vital energy, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>deha-prāṇa-ādi-ācchādana-nimajjanena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(and)_ <ruby>expanding<rp> (</rp><rt>sphārayantī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(Her)_ <ruby>essential nature<rp> (</rp><rt>svarūpam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>so that it emerges<rp> (</rp><rt>unmagnatvena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>As has been said<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā uktam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(in Spandakārikā-s 2.1:)_</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«Grabbing hold of<rp> (</rp><rt>ākramya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> that Force (tad... balam), <ruby>the Mantra-s<rp> (</rp><rt>mantrāḥ... iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>...»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>In this manner<rp> (</rp><rt>evam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, when one obtains the Power _(of Citi or Universal Consciousness)_ <span class="pv-w">(bala-lābhe)</span> —when one resorts to the emerged essential nature <span class="pv-note">— when one takes refuge in such essential nature —</span> <span class="pv-w">(unmagna-svarūpa-āśrayaṇe)</span>—, <ruby>he makes<rp> (</rp><rt>karoti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the universe<rp> (</rp><rt>viśvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>from the earth element up to Sadāśiva<rp> (</rp><rt>kṣiti-ādi-sadāśiva-antam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>equal to himself<rp> (</rp><rt>ātmasāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(that is,)_ he manifests _(it)_ <span class="pv-w">(nirbhāsayati)</span> <ruby>in unity with his own essential nature<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-svarūpa-abhedena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>The first/ancient Guru-s have said that<rp> (</rp><rt>tad uktam pūrva-gurubhiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the Kramasūtra-s<rp> (</rp><rt>krama-sūtreṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, which consist of their own speeches <span class="pv-note">— in the speeches of those Guru-s —</span> <span class="pv-w">(sva-bhāṣā-mayeṣu)</span>:</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«Just as<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>a fire<rp> (</rp><rt>vahniḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>ignited<rp> (</rp><rt>udbodhitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>consumes/burns<rp> (</rp><rt>dahati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the fuel<rp> (</rp><rt>dāhyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>so<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>he should consume<rp> (</rp><rt>bhakṣayet</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> the fetters _(called)_ <ruby>objects of the senses<rp> (</rp><rt>viṣaya-pāśān... iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">The author <span class="pv-note">— lit. authors —</span> <ruby>of the venerable Pratyabhijñā<rp> (</rp><rt>śrī-pratyabhijñā-kārāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(has said something about this:)_</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">«It should not be said (na ca... vaktavyam) <ruby>in this way<rp> (</rp><rt>evam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: _(The role of Citi,)_ <ruby>which is inclusion<rp> (</rp><rt>samāveśa-bhūḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(and)_ which is <ruby>that which makes the universe equal to Herself<rp> (</rp><rt>viśva-ātmasāt-kāra-rūpā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>is occasional/incidental<rp> (</rp><rt>kādācitkī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>How<rp> (</rp><rt>katham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> is this _(kind of role)_ <span class="pv-w">(upādeyā iyam syāt iti)</span> to be accepted? _(Why?)_ <ruby>Because<rp> (</rp><rt>yatas</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> this _(nature)_ <span class="pv-w">(idam)</span> of Hers <span class="pv-note">— of Citi —</span> <span class="pv-w">(asyāḥ)</span> <ruby>appears<rp> (</rp><rt>ābhāti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>as an occasional or incidental state<rp> (</rp><rt>kādācitkatvam iva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> due to <ruby>the immersion and emergence of the body, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>deha-ādi-unmajjana-nimajjana-vaśena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. In reality (vastutas tu), _(this)_ occasional or incidental _(apparent)_ <ruby>state<rp> (</rp><rt>kādācitkatvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> is due to the emergence of the body, etc. <ruby>manifested by the Absolute Freedom of Citi<rp> (</rp><rt>citi-svātantrya-avabhāsita-deha-ādi-unmajjanāt eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>But<rp> (</rp><rt>tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> She <span class="pv-note">— or "Her role" —</span> <span class="pv-w">(eṣā)</span> <ruby>is always manifesting Herself<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā eva prakāśamānā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Otherwise<rp> (</rp><rt>anyathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, even that, _(namely,)_ <ruby>the body, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>tad deha-ādi api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>would not be manifested<rp> (</rp><rt>na prakāśeta</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. For this very reason (atas eva), _(there exists)_ the practice _(of Yoga)_ <span class="pv-w">(abhyāsaḥ)</span> for submerging the erroneous conception that the state of the knower/perceiver <span class="pv-note">— of the subject —</span> is the body, etc. <span class="pv-w">(deha-ādi-pramātṛtā-abhimāna-nimajjanāya)</span>, and not (na tu) for attaining the state of the knower/perceiver <ruby>whose essence is always to expand and emerge<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā prathamānatā-sāra-pramātṛtā-prāpti-artham... iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>
