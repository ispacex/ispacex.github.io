<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">उक्तसूत्रार्थप्रातिपक्ष्येण तत्त्वदृष्टिं दर्शयितुमाह</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Uktasūtrārthaprātipakṣyeṇa tattvadṛṣṭiṁ darśayitumāha</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्परिज्ञाने चित्तमेवान्तर्मुखीभावेन चेतनपदाध्यारोहाच्चितिः॥१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatparijñāne cittamevāntarmukhībhāvena cetanapadādhyārohāccitiḥ||13||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">पूर्वसूत्रव्याख्याप्रसङ्गेन प्रमेयदृष्ट्या वितत्य व्याख्यातप्रायमेतत्सूत्रं शब्दसङ्गत्या त्वधुना व्याख्यायते। तस्यात्मीयस्य पञ्चकृत्यकारित्वस्य परिज्ञाने सत्यपरिज्ञानलक्षणकारणापगमात्स्वशक्तिव्यामोहिततानिवृत्तौ स्वातन्त्र्यलाभात्प्राग्व्याख्यातं यच्चित्तं तदेव सङ्कोचिनीं बहिर्मुखतां जहदन्तर्मुखीभावेन चेतनपदाध्यारोहात् - ग्राहकभूमिकाक्रमणक्रमेण सङ्कोचकलाया अपि विगलनेन स्वरूपापत्त्या चितिर्भवति स्वां चिन्मयीं परां भूमिमाविशतीत्यर्थः॥१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūrvasūtravyākhyāprasaṅgena prameyadṛṣṭyā vitatya vyākhyātaprāyametatsūtraṁ śabdasaṅgatyā tvadhunā vyākhyāyate| Tasyātmīyasya pañcakṛtyakāritvasya parijñāne satyaparijñānalakṣaṇakāraṇāpagamātsvaśaktivyāmohitatānivṛttau svātantryalābhātprāgvyākhyātaṁ yaccittaṁ tadeva saṅkocinīṁ bahirmukhatāṁ jahadantarmukhībhāvena cetanapadādhyārohāt - grāhakabhūmikākramaṇakrameṇa saṅkocakalāyā api vigalanena svarūpāpattyā citirbhavati svāṁ cinmayīṁ parāṁ bhūmimāviśatītyarthaḥ||13||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>To show<rp> (</rp><rt>darśayitum</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> the viewpoint of the _(Supreme)_ Principle <span class="pv-note">— or «the viewpoint of Truth» —</span> <span class="pv-w">(tattva-dṛṣṭim)</span> in contrast <ruby>with the meaning of the mentioned aphorism<rp> (</rp><rt>ukta-sūtra-artha-prātipakṣyeṇa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(the author)_ <ruby>said<rp> (</rp><rt>āha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>:</p>

<p class="pv-tr pv-anchor" id="s1-ru" markdown="1"><ruby>Когда имеется полное знание<rp> (</rp><rt>parijñāne</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> об этом <span class="pv-note">— т.e. "об авторстве пяти функций, или пятикратного акта" —</span> <span class="pv-w">(tad)</span>, <ruby>сам<rp> (</rp><rt>eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>ум<rp> (</rp><rt>cittam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>благодаря<rp> (</rp><rt>bhāvena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>интроверсии<rp> (</rp><rt>antarmukhī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(становится)_ <ruby>Высшей Силой<rp> (</rp><rt>citiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>путём возвышения<rp> (</rp><rt>adhyārohāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(к)_ <ruby>состоянию<rp> (</rp><rt>pada</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Чистого Сознания<rp> (</rp><rt>cetana</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||13||</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>From the viewpoint of the knowable object<rp> (</rp><rt>prameya-dṛṣṭyā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>etad</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>aphorism<rp> (</rp><rt>sūtram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>has been mostly explained<rp> (</rp><rt>vyākhyāta-prāyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>at length<rp> (</rp><rt>vitatya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> in connection <ruby>with the explanation of the previous aphorism<rp> (</rp><rt>pūrva-sūtra-vyākhyā-prasaṅgena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>But<rp> (</rp><rt>tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>now<rp> (</rp><rt>adhunā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>it is explained<rp> (</rp><rt>vyākhyāyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> according <ruby>to the conjunction and connection of the words<rp> (</rp><rt>śabda-saṅgatyā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>When there is complete knowledge<rp> (</rp><rt>parijñāne sati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of that<rp> (</rp><rt>tasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —_(that is,)_ of <ruby>one's own authorship of the five functions/acts<rp> (</rp><rt>ātmīyasya pañca-kṛtya-kāritvasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>—, when there is cessation of the condition of being <ruby>confused or infatuated by one's own powers<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-śakti-vyāmohitatā-nivṛttau</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> since the cause <ruby>characterized by ignorance has disappeared<rp> (</rp><rt>aparijñāna-lakṣaṇa-kāraṇa-apagamāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(then)_ the mind itself (cittam tad eva) —<ruby>which<rp> (</rp><rt>yad</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> has been explained before <span class="pv-note">— in aphorism 5 —</span> <span class="pv-w">(prāk-vyākhyātam)</span>—, <ruby>through the attainment of Absolute Freedom<rp> (</rp><rt>svātantrya-lābhāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>abandoning<rp> (</rp><rt>jahat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(its)_ <ruby>limiting extroversion<rp> (</rp><rt>saṅkocinīm bahirmukhatām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>by becoming introverted<rp> (</rp><rt>antarmukhī-bhāvena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>becomes<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> Citi <span class="pv-note">— the Supreme Power —</span> <span class="pv-w">(citiḥ)</span> by ascending _(up to)_ <ruby>the state of Pure Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>cetana-pada-adhyārohāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(that is,)_ through an entry into _(its)_ <ruby>essential nature<rp> (</rp><rt>svarūpa-āpattyā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by means of the disappearance<rp> (</rp><rt>vigalanena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> of even the aspect _(called)_ <ruby>contraction<rp> (</rp><rt>saṅkoca-kalāyāḥ api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(and)_ gradually <ruby>ascending to the stage of knower or perceiver<rp> (</rp><rt>grāhaka-bhūmikā-ākramaṇa-krameṇa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. _(In sum,)_ <ruby>he enters<rp> (</rp><rt>āviśati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the supreme<rp> (</rp><rt>parām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>stage<rp> (</rp><rt>bhūmim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>consisting of Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>cit-mayīm</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>This is the meaning<rp> (</rp><rt>iti arthaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||13||</p>
