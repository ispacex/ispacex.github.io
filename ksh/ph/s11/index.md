<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">न चायमेव प्रकारः पञ्चविधकृत्यकारित्वे यावदन्योऽपि कश्चिद्रहस्यरूपोऽस्ति - इत्याह</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na cāyameva prakāraḥ pañcavidhakṛtyakāritve yāvadanyo'pi kaścidrahasyarūpo'sti - Ityāha</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आभासनरक्तिविमर्शनबीजावस्थापनविलापनतस्तानि॥११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ābhāsanaraktivimarśanabījāvasthāpanavilāpanatastāni||11||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">पञ्चविधकृत्यानि करोति - इति पूर्वतः सम्बध्यते। श्रीमन्महार्थदृष्ट्या दृगादिदेवीप्रसरणक्रमेण यद्यदाभाति तत्तत्सृज्यते तथा सृष्टे पदे तत्र यदा प्रशान्तनिमेषं कञ्चित्कालं रज्यति तदा स्थितिदेव्या तत्स्थाप्यते चमत्कारापरपर्यायविमर्शनसमये तु संह्रियते। यथोक्तं श्रीरामेण</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pañcavidhakṛtyāni karoti - iti pūrvataḥ sambadhyate| Śrīmanmahārthadṛṣṭyā dṛgādidevīprasaraṇakrameṇa yadyadābhāti tattatsṛjyate tathā sṛṣṭe pade tatra yadā praśāntanimeṣaṁ kañcitkālaṁ rajyati tadā sthitidevyā tatsthāpyate camatkārāparaparyāyavimarśanasamaye tu saṁhriyate| Yathoktaṁ śrīrāmeṇa</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">समाधिवज्रेणाप्यन्यैरभेद्यो भेदभूधरः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Samādhivajreṇāpyanyairabhedyo bhedabhūdharaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">परामृष्टश्च नष्टश्च त्वद्भक्तिबलशालिभिः॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Parāmṛṣṭaśca naṣṭaśca tvadbhaktibalaśālibhiḥ||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति। यदा तु संह्रियमाणमपि एतदन्तर्विचित्राशङ्कादिसंस्कारमाधत्ते तदा तत्पुनरुद्भविष्यत्संसारबीजभावमापन्नं विलयपदमध्यारोपितम्। यदा पुनस्तत्तथान्तः स्थापितमन्यद्वानुभूयमानमेव हठपाकक्रमेणालङ्ग्रासयुक्त्या चिदग्निसाद्भावमापद्यते तदा पूर्णतापादनेनानुगृह्यत एव। ईदृशं च पञ्चविधकृत्यकारित्वं सर्वस्य सदा सन्निहितमपि सद्गुरूपदेशं विना न प्रकाशत इति सद्गुरुसपर्यैवैतत्प्रथार्थमनुसर्तव्या॥११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti| Yadā tu saṁhriyamāṇamapi etadantarvicitrāśaṅkādisaṁskāramādhatte tadā tatpunarudbhaviṣyatsaṁsārabījabhāvamāpannaṁ vilayapadamadhyāropitam| Yadā punastattathāntaḥ sthāpitamanyadvānubhūyamānameva haṭhapākakrameṇālaṅgrāsayuktyā cidagnisādbhāvamāpadyate tadā pūrṇatāpādanenānugṛhyata eva| Īdṛśaṁ ca pañcavidhakṛtyakāritvaṁ sarvasya sadā sannihitamapi sadgurūpadeśaṁ vinā na prakāśata iti sadgurusaparyaivaitatprathārthamanusartavyā||11||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">This (ca ayam) is not the only (na... eva) <ruby>way<rp> (</rp><rt>prakāraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> with regard <ruby>to the authorship of the fivefold function/act<rp> (</rp><rt>pañcavidha-kṛtya-kāritve</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>since<rp> (</rp><rt>yāvat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>there is<rp> (</rp><rt>asti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>also<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>another one<rp> (</rp><rt>anyaḥ... kaścid</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>which is secret<rp> (</rp><rt>rahasya-rūpaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>Thus<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(the author)_ <ruby>said<rp> (</rp><rt>āha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>:</p>

<p class="pv-tr pv-anchor" id="s1-ru" markdown="1">Те _(пять Актов)_ <span class="pv-w">(tāni)</span> _(могут быть объяснены)_ <ruby>с помощью<rp> (</rp><rt>taḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(этих пяти терминов)_: Ābhāsana <span class="pv-note">— т.e. "акт проявления" —</span> <span class="pv-w">(ābhāsana)</span>, Rakti <span class="pv-note">— т.e. "наслаждение" —</span> <span class="pv-w">(rakti)</span>, Vimarśana <span class="pv-note">— т.e. "акт переживания себя как Самости" —</span> <span class="pv-w">(vimarśana)</span>, Bījāvasthāpana <span class="pv-note">— т.e. "акт посева семян" —</span> <span class="pv-w">(bīja-avasthāpana)</span> _(и)_ Vilāpana <span class="pv-note">— т.e. "растворение покрова Māyā" —</span> <span class="pv-w">(vilāpana)</span>||11||</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>He performs<rp> (</rp><rt>karoti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> the five functions/acts <span class="pv-note">— lit. «the fivefold functions/acts», this is very convoluted Sanskrit, since it should have been written «pañcakṛtyāni» or else «pañcavidhakṛtyam» —</span> <span class="pv-w">(pañcavidha-kṛtyāni)</span> — This is connected (iti... sambadhyate) with what precedes <span class="pv-note">— lit. «before, in front of», strange Sanskrit here —</span> <span class="pv-w">(pūrvatas)</span>. According to the viewpoint of the venerable Mahārtha <span class="pv-note">— of the Krama school —</span> <span class="pv-w">(śrīmat-mahārtha-dṛṣṭyā)</span>, whatever (yad yad) <ruby>appears<rp> (</rp><rt>ābhāti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> through the process <ruby>of expansion of the goddess of sight, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>dṛk-ādi-devī-prasaraṇa-krameṇa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, all that (tad tad) <ruby>is emitted<rp> (</rp><rt>sṛjyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>When there thus exists an emitted entity<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā sṛṣṭe pade</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(and)_ <ruby>when<rp> (</rp><rt>yadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>he delights<rp> (</rp><rt>rajyati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in that<rp> (</rp><rt>tatra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for some time<rp> (</rp><rt>kañcid kālam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>without closing the eyes<rp> (</rp><rt>praśānta-nimeṣam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>then<rp> (</rp><rt>tadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>tad</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is maintained<rp> (</rp><rt>sthāpyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by the goddess of maintenance<rp> (</rp><rt>sthiti-devyā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>But<rp> (</rp><rt>tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(that very thing)_ <ruby>is withdrawn<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁhriyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> at the time of vimarśana —or of the act of experiencing oneself as the Self—, which is another way of saying «Camatkāra» <span class="pv-note">— lit. «Astonishment»; that is, the Great Astonishment of discovering that one's own Self is immutable, intact —</span> <span class="pv-w">(camatkāra-apara-paryāya-vimarśana-samaye)</span>. <ruby>As<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the venerable Rāma has said<rp> (</rp><rt>uktam śrī-rāmeṇa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>:</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«The mountain of duality<rp> (</rp><rt>bheda-bhūdharaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which cannot be divided<rp> (</rp><rt>abhedyaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> by others _(means)_ <span class="pv-w">(anyaiḥ)</span> —<ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> by <ruby>means of the thunderbolt of samādhi or trance<rp> (</rp><rt>samādhi-vajreṇa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>—, is perceived as one's own Self <span class="pv-note">— lit. touched —</span> <span class="pv-w">(parāmṛṣṭaḥ)</span> and (ca... ca) <ruby>destroyed<rp> (</rp><rt>naṣṭaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> by those who <ruby>are full of the strength of devotion toward You<rp> (</rp><rt>tvat-bhakti-bala-śālibhiḥ... iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>But<rp> (</rp><rt>tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>when<rp> (</rp><rt>yadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> this <span class="pv-note">— the knowable object —</span> <span class="pv-w">(etad)</span>, <ruby>which is being withdrawn<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁhriyamāṇam api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>implants<rp> (</rp><rt>ādhatte</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>internally<rp> (</rp><rt>antar</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>various impressions of doubt, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>vicitra-āśaṅkā-ādi-saṁskāram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>then<rp> (</rp><rt>tadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>tad</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> acquires the condition <ruby>of seed of the Saṁsāra that will arise<rp> (</rp><rt>udbhaviṣyat-saṁsāra-bīja-bhāvam āpannam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>again<rp> (</rp><rt>punar</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(and)_ the state of vilaya <span class="pv-note">— concealment or obscuration —</span> <span class="pv-w">(vilaya-padam)</span> is produced <span class="pv-note">— the translation as «is falsely attributed» is not valid here, since that applies to Vedānta —</span> <span class="pv-w">(adhyāropitam)</span>. <ruby>On the other hand<rp> (</rp><rt>punar</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>when<rp> (</rp><rt>yadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>tad</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>which has been maintained<rp> (</rp><rt>sthāpitam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>thus<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>internally<rp> (</rp><rt>antar</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —<ruby>or<rp> (</rp><rt>vā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>some other thing that was being experienced<rp> (</rp><rt>anyat... anubhūyamānam eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>— <ruby>is reduced<rp> (</rp><rt>āpadyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to equality with the Fire of Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>cit-agni-sādbhāvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>through the process of «haṭhapāka»<rp> (</rp><rt>haṭhapāka-krameṇa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(and)_ <ruby>the device of «alaṅgrāsa»<rp> (</rp><rt>alaṅgrāsa-yuktyā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>then<rp> (</rp><rt>tadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>he is favored/graced<rp> (</rp><rt>anugṛhyate eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by the State of Fullness<rp> (</rp><rt>pūrṇatāpādanena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>And<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>although<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>such<rp> (</rp><rt>īdṛśam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>authorship of the fivefold function/act<rp> (</rp><rt>pañcavidha-kṛtya-kāritvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(is)_ <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>near<rp> (</rp><rt>sannihitam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to everyone<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>it does not become evident or manifest<rp> (</rp><rt>na prakāśate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>without<rp> (</rp><rt>vinā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the teaching of a true Guru<rp> (</rp><rt>sat-guru-upadeśam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Thus<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the worship of a true Guru<rp> (</rp><rt>sat-guru-saparyā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>must be performed<rp> (</rp><rt>anusartavyā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to attain this expansion<rp> (</rp><rt>etad-prathā-artham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||11||</p>
