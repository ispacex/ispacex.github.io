<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">यद्येवम्भूतस्यात्मनो विभूतिस्तत्कथमयं मलावृतोऽणुः कलादिवलितः संसार्यभिधीयते - इत्याह</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yadyevambhūtasyātmano vibhūtistatkathamayaṁ malāvṛto'ṇuḥ kalādivalitaḥ saṁsāryabhidhīyate - Ityāha</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चिद्वत्तच्छक्तिसङ्कोचान्मलावृतः संसारी॥९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Cidvattacchaktisaṅkocānmalāvṛtaḥ saṁsārī||9||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">यदा चिदात्मा परमेश्वरः स्वस्वातन्त्र्यादभेदव्याप्तिं निमज्ज्य भेदव्याप्तिमवलम्बते तदा तदीया इच्छादिशक्तयोऽसङ्कुचिता अपि सङ्कोचवत्यो भान्ति तदानीमेव चायं मलावृतः संसारी भवति। तथा चाप्रतिहतस्वातन्त्र्यरूपेच्छाशक्तिः सङ्कुचिता सत्यपूर्णम्मन्यतारूपमाणवं मलं ज्ञानशक्तिः क्रमेण सङ्कोचाद्भेदे सर्वज्ञत्वस्य किञ्चिज्ज्ञत्वाप्तेरन्तःकरणबुद्धीन्द्रियतापत्तिपूर्वमत्यन्तं सङ्कोचग्रहणेन भिन्नवेद्यप्रथारूपं मायीयं मलं क्रियाशक्तिः क्रमेण भेदे सर्वकर्तृत्वस्य किञ्चित्कर्तृत्वाप्तेः कर्मेन्द्रियरूपसङ्कोचग्रहणपूर्वमत्यन्तं परिमिततां प्राप्ता शुभाशुभानुष्ठानमयं कार्मं मलम्। तथा सर्वकर्तृत्वसर्वज्ञत्वपूर्णत्वनित्यत्वव्यापकत्वशक्तयः सङ्कोचं गृह्णाना यथाक्रमम् कलाविद्यारागकालनियतिरूपतया भान्ति। तथाविधश्चायं शक्तिदरिद्रः संसार्युच्यते स्वशक्तिविकासे तु शिव एव॥९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yadā cidātmā parameśvaraḥ svasvātantryādabhedavyāptiṁ nimajjya bhedavyāptimavalambate tadā tadīyā icchādiśaktayo'saṅkucitā api saṅkocavatyo bhānti tadānīmeva cāyaṁ malāvṛtaḥ saṁsārī bhavati| Tathā cāpratihatasvātantryarūpecchāśaktiḥ saṅkucitā satyapūrṇammanyatārūpamāṇavaṁ malaṁ jñānaśaktiḥ krameṇa saṅkocādbhede sarvajñatvasya kiñcijjñatvāpterantaḥkaraṇabuddhīndriyatāpattipūrvamatyantaṁ saṅkocagrahaṇena bhinnavedyaprathārūpaṁ māyīyaṁ malaṁ kriyāśaktiḥ krameṇa bhede sarvakartṛtvasya kiñcitkartṛtvāpteḥ karmendriyarūpasaṅkocagrahaṇapūrvamatyantaṁ parimitatāṁ prāptā śubhāśubhānuṣṭhānamayaṁ kārmaṁ malam| Tathā sarvakartṛtvasarvajñatvapūrṇatvanityatvavyāpakatvaśaktayaḥ saṅkocaṁ gṛhṇānā yathākramam kalāvidyārāgakālaniyatirūpatayā bhānti| Tathāvidhaścāyaṁ śaktidaridraḥ saṁsāryucyate svaśaktivikāse tu śiva eva||9||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>If<rp> (</rp><rt>yadi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(such is)_ <ruby>the Glory<rp> (</rp><rt>vibhūtiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of the Self that is of this kind<rp> (</rp><rt>evambhūtasya ātmanaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>then<rp> (</rp><rt>tad</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>how<rp> (</rp><rt>katham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(is it that)_ <ruby>He<rp> (</rp><rt>ayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is called<rp> (</rp><rt>abhidhīyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the transmigratory soul<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁsārī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(who)_ is an atom <span class="pv-note">— a limited individual —</span> <span class="pv-w">(aṇuḥ)</span> <ruby>covered by mala or impurity<rp> (</rp><rt>mala-āvṛtaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(and)_ <ruby>enveloped by kalā, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>kalā-ādi-valitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>? — <ruby>In this manner<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(the author)_ <ruby>said<rp> (</rp><rt>āha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>:</p>

<p class="pv-tr pv-anchor" id="s1-ru" markdown="1">Тот <span class="pv-note">— т.e. "Самость" —</span> <span class="pv-w">(tad)</span>, <ruby>кто наполнен Сознанием<rp> (</rp><rt>cit-vat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(становится)_ <ruby>трансмиграционной душой<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁsārī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>покрытой<rp> (</rp><rt>avṛtaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Mala<rp> (</rp><rt>mala</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, из-за _(Его)_ <ruby>сжатия<rp> (</rp><rt>saṅkocāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Śakti, или Силы<rp> (</rp><rt>śakti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||9||</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>When<rp> (</rp><rt>yadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the Supreme Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>parama-īśvaraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>who is Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>cit-ātmā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>clings to<rp> (</rp><rt>avalambate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the penetration of dualism<rp> (</rp><rt>bheda-vyāptim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>after submerging<rp> (</rp><rt>nimajjya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the penetration of non-dualism<rp> (</rp><rt>abheda-vyāptim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>through His Absolute Freedom<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-svātantryāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>at that time<rp> (</rp><rt>tadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>His<rp> (</rp><rt>tadīyāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Powers of Will, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>icchā-ādi-śaktayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>although<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>not contracted<rp> (</rp><rt>asaṅkucitāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>appear<rp> (</rp><rt>bhānti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to be contracted<rp> (</rp><rt>saṅkocavatyaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Then<rp> (</rp><rt>tadānīm eva ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>He<rp> (</rp><rt>ayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>becomes<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the transmigratory soul<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁsārī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>covered by mala or impurity<rp> (</rp><rt>mala-āvṛtaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Accordingly<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, the Power <ruby>of Will whose nature is Irresistible Freedom<rp> (</rp><rt>apratihata-svātantrya-rūpa-icchā-śaktiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>upon becoming contracted<rp> (</rp><rt>saṅkucitā satī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(becomes)_ <ruby>the Āṇavamala<rp> (</rp><rt>āṇavam malam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>whose form is the notion of lack of Fullness<rp> (</rp><rt>apūrṇam-manyatā-rūpam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>The Power of Knowledge<rp> (</rp><rt>jñāna-śaktiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>gradually<rp> (</rp><rt>krameṇa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(assumes limitation)_ <ruby>through a contraction<rp> (</rp><rt>saṅkocāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in differentiation<rp> (</rp><rt>bhede</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>since Omniscience becomes limited knowledge<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvajñatvasya kiñcid-jñatva-āpteḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. _(Thereafter,)_ <ruby>assuming total contraction<rp> (</rp><rt>atyantam saṅkoca-grahaṇena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, preceded by the acquisition of an <ruby>internal psychic organ and powers of perception<rp> (</rp><rt>antaḥkaraṇa-buddhīndriyatā-āpatti-pūrvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(this Power of Knowledge becomes)_ <ruby>the Māyīyamala<rp> (</rp><rt>māyīyam malam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>whose form is an expansion of different objects<rp> (</rp><rt>bhinna-vedya-prathā-rūpam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. _(In turn,)_ <ruby>the Power of Action<rp> (</rp><rt>kriyā-śaktiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>gradually<rp> (</rp><rt>krameṇa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(assumes limitation)_ <ruby>in differentiation<rp> (</rp><rt>bhede</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>since Omnipotence becomes limited agency<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvakartṛtvasya kiñcid-kartṛtva-āpteḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. _(Subsequently,)_ <ruby>having acquired complete limitation<rp> (</rp><rt>atyantam parimitatām prāptā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(all)_ preceded by the assumption of <ruby>contraction in the form of the powers of action<rp> (</rp><rt>karmendriya-rūpa-saṅkoca-grahaṇa-pūrvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(this Power of Knowledge becomes)_ <ruby>the Kārmamala<rp> (</rp><rt>kārmam malam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> which consists <ruby>of the performance of good and bad actions<rp> (</rp><rt>śubha-aśubha-anuṣṭhāna-mayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>In this way<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>assuming<rp> (</rp><rt>gṛhṇānāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>contraction<rp> (</rp><rt>saṅkocam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, the Powers of Omnipotence, Omniscience, <ruby>Fullness, Eternity and Omnipresence<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvakartṛtva-sarvajñatva-pūrṇatva-nityatva-vyāpakatva-śaktayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>appear<rp> (</rp><rt>bhānti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>respectively<rp> (</rp><rt>yathākramam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> in the form of _(the Kañcuka-s or Sheaths called)_ <ruby>Kalā, Vidyā, Rāga, Kāla and Niyati<rp> (</rp><rt>kalā-vidyā-rāga-kāla-niyati-rūpatayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. This _(Self)_ <span class="pv-w">(ayam)</span> <ruby>which has such qualities<rp> (</rp><rt>tathāvidhaḥ ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who is poor in Śakti<rp> (</rp><rt>śakti-daridraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>is said to be<rp> (</rp><rt>ucyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the transmigratory soul<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁsārī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>But<rp> (</rp><rt>tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>when there is an expansion of His Powers<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-śakti-vikāse</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(this Self is)_ <ruby>Śiva Himself<rp> (</rp><rt>śivaḥ eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||9||</p>
