<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">ननु संसार्यवस्थायामस्य किञ्चिच्छिवतोचितमभिज्ञानमस्ति येन शिव एव तथावस्थितः - इत्युद्घोष्यते। अस्ति। इत्याह</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nanu saṁsāryavasthāyāmasya kiñcicchivatocitamabhijñānamasti yena śiva eva tathāvasthitaḥ - Ityudghoṣyate| Asti| Ityāha</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तथापि तद्वत्पञ्चकृत्यानि करोति॥१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tathāpi tadvatpañcakṛtyāni karoti||10||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इहेश्वराद्वयदर्शनस्य ब्रह्मवादिभ्योऽयमेव विशेषो यत्</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Iheśvarādvayadarśanasya brahmavādibhyo'yameva viśeṣo yat</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">सृष्टिसंहारकर्तारं विलयस्थितिकारकम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sṛṣṭisaṁhārakartāraṁ vilayasthitikārakam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">अनुग्रहकरं देवं प्रणतार्तिविनाशनम्॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anugrahakaraṁ devaṁ praṇatārtivināśanam||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति श्रीमत्स्वच्छन्दादिशासनोक्तनीत्या सदा पञ्चविधकृत्यकारित्वं चिदात्मनो भगवतः। यथा च भगवाञ्छुद्धेतराध्वस्फारणक्रमेण स्वरूपविकासरूपाणि सृष्ट्यादीनि करोति तथा सङ्कुचितचिच्छक्तितया संसारभूमिकायामपि पञ्चकृत्यानि विधत्ते। तथा हि</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti śrīmatsvacchandādiśāsanoktanītyā sadā pañcavidhakṛtyakāritvaṁ cidātmano bhagavataḥ| Yathā ca bhagavāñchuddhetarādhvasphāraṇakrameṇa svarūpavikāsarūpāṇi sṛṣṭyādīni karoti tathā saṅkucitacicchaktitayā saṁsārabhūmikāyāmapi pañcakṛtyāni vidhatte| Tathā hi</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">तदेवं व्यवहारेऽपि प्रभुर्देहादिमाविशन्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadevaṁ vyavahāre'pi prabhurdehādimāviśan|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">भान्तमेवान्तरर्थौघमिच्छया भासयेद्बहिः॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhāntamevāntararthaughamicchayā bhāsayedbahiḥ||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति प्रत्यभिज्ञाकारिकोक्तार्थदृष्ट्या देहप्राणादिपदमाविशंश्चिद्रूपो महेश्वरो बहिर्मुखीभावावसरे नीलादिकमर्थं नियतदेशकालादितया यदाभासयति तदा नियतदेशकालाद्याभासांशेऽस्य स्रष्टृतान्यदेशकालाद्याभासांशेऽस्य संहर्तृता नीलाद्याभासांशे स्थापकता भेदेनाभासांशे विलयकारिता प्रकाशैक्येन प्रकाशनेऽनुग्रहीतृता। यथा च सदा पञ्चविधकृत्यकारित्वं भगवतस्तथा मया वितत्य स्पन्दसन्दोहे निर्णीतम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti pratyabhijñākārikoktārthadṛṣṭyā dehaprāṇādipadamāviśaṁścidrūpo maheśvaro bahirmukhībhāvāvasare nīlādikamarthaṁ niyatadeśakālāditayā yadābhāsayati tadā niyatadeśakālādyābhāsāṁśe'sya sraṣṭṛtānyadeśakālādyābhāsāṁśe'sya saṁhartṛtā nīlādyābhāsāṁśe sthāpakatā bhedenābhāsāṁśe vilayakāritā prakāśaikyena prakāśane'nugrahītṛtā| Yathā ca sadā pañcavidhakṛtyakāritvaṁ bhagavatastathā mayā vitatya spandasandohe nirṇītam|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">एवमिदं पञ्चविधकृत्यकारित्वमात्मीयं सदा दृढप्रतिपत्त्या परिशील्यमानं माहेश्वर्यमुन्मीलयत्येव भक्तिभाजाम्। अत एव ये सदैतत्परिशीलयन्ति ते स्वरूपविकासमयं विश्वं जानाना जीवन्मुक्ता - इत्याम्नाताः। ये तु न तथा ते सर्वतो विभिन्नं मेयजातं पश्यन्तो बद्धात्मानः॥१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evamidaṁ pañcavidhakṛtyakāritvamātmīyaṁ sadā dṛḍhapratipattyā pariśīlyamānaṁ māheśvaryamunmīlayatyeva bhaktibhājām| Ata eva ye sadaitatpariśīlayanti te svarūpavikāsamayaṁ viśvaṁ jānānā jīvanmuktā - ityāmnātāḥ| Ye tu na tathā te sarvato vibhinnaṁ meyajātaṁ paśyanto baddhātmānaḥ||10||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>An objection<rp> (</rp><rt>nanu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>Is there<rp> (</rp><rt>asti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>any<rp> (</rp><rt>kiñcid</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>sign<rp> (</rp><rt>abhijñānam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>appropriate for the State of Śiva<rp> (</rp><rt>śivatā-ucitam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in his condition as a transmigratory soul<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁsāri-avasthāyām asya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by means of which<rp> (</rp><rt>yena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Śiva Himself<rp> (</rp><rt>śivaḥ eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> remains thus <span class="pv-note">— that is, just as Śiva is —</span> <span class="pv-w">(tathā-avasthitaḥ... iti)</span>? <ruby>It is proclaimed aloud<rp> (</rp><rt>udghoṣyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>There is<rp> (</rp><rt>asti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. _(Accordingly, the author)_ <ruby>said<rp> (</rp><rt>āha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>.</p>

<p class="pv-tr pv-anchor" id="s1-ru" markdown="1"><ruby>Даже<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā-api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(в этом ограниченном состоянии индивидуальная душа)_ <ruby>выполняет<rp> (</rp><rt>karoti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>пять<rp> (</rp><rt>pañca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>функций<rp> (</rp><rt>kṛtyāni</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>подобно<rp> (</rp><rt>vat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> тому, _(как это делает Высшая Самость)_ <span class="pv-w">(tad)</span>||10||</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>Here<rp> (</rp><rt>iha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, this (ayam eva) _(is)_ <ruby>the difference<rp> (</rp><rt>viśeṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> between the philosophy of Īśvarādvaya <span class="pv-note">— that is, Trika Shaivism —</span> <span class="pv-w">(īśvara-advaya-darśanasya)</span> and the followers of the doctrine of Brahma <span class="pv-note">— especially the followers of Advaitavedānta —</span> <span class="pv-w">(brahma-vādibhyaḥ)</span>: <ruby>That<rp> (</rp><rt>yad</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> the authorship of the fivefold function <span class="pv-note">— or act —</span> <span class="pv-w">(pañcavidha-kṛtya-kāritvam)</span> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>belongs to the Lord consisting of Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>cit-ātmanaḥ bhagavataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, according to the precept expressed in the teaching of the venerable Svacchandatantra _(1.3)_, etc. <span class="pv-w">(śrīmat-svacchanda-ādi-śāsana-ukta-nītyā)</span>:</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">«_(I bow before)_ <ruby>the God<rp> (</rp><rt>devam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> who performs the manifestation and the withdrawal _(of the universe)_ <span class="pv-w">(sṛṣṭi-saṁhāra-kartāram)</span>, who produces the concealment _(of essential nature)_ and the maintenance _(of the universe)_ <span class="pv-w">(vilaya-sthiti-kārakam)</span>, who carries out the revelation _(of essential nature)_ <span class="pv-w">(anugraha-karam)</span>, _(and)_ who destroys the sorrow of those who have bowed down _(before Him)_ <span class="pv-w">(praṇata-arti-vināśanam... iti)</span>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>And<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>just as<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, through the <ruby>process of the expansion of the impure course<rp> (</rp><rt>śuddha-itara-adhva-sphāraṇa-krameṇa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>produces<rp> (</rp><rt>karoti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>manifestation, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>sṛṣṭi-ādīni</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —all of <ruby>which are a unfolding of His essential nature<rp> (</rp><rt>svarūpa-vikāsa-rūpāṇi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>—, <ruby>in the same way<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>He carries out<rp> (</rp><rt>vidhatte</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the five functions/acts<rp> (</rp><rt>pañca-kṛtyāni</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>at the stage of Saṁsāra<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁsāra-bhūmikāyām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> through the Power of Consciousness _(but)_ <ruby>contracted<rp> (</rp><rt>saṅkucita-cit-śaktitayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Thus<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā hi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(it has also been said in the Īśvarapratyabhijñākārikā-s 1.6.7:)_</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">«Therefore (tad evam), <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the ordinary life<rp> (</rp><rt>vyavahāre</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>prabhuḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>entering into<rp> (</rp><rt>āviśan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the body, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>deha-ādim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>manifests<rp> (</rp><rt>bhāsayet</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>outside<rp> (</rp><rt>bahis</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, by _(His own)_ <ruby>Will<rp> (</rp><rt>icchayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the multitude of things/objects<rp> (</rp><rt>artha-ogham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> which shines inside _(Himself)_ <span class="pv-w">(bhāntam eva antar... iti)</span>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">According to the viewpoint of the meaning <ruby>expressed in the Īśvarapratyabhijñākārikā-s<rp> (</rp><rt>pratyabhijñā-kārikā-ukta-artha-dṛṣṭyā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>when<rp> (</rp><rt>yadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the Great Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>mahā-īśvaraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who is Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>cit-rūpaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>entering into<rp> (</rp><rt>āviśan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the condition of body, vital energy, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>deha-prāṇa-ādi-padam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>at the moment where the mind is exteriorized<rp> (</rp><rt>bahirmukhī-bhāva-avasare</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>manifests<rp> (</rp><rt>ābhāsayati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>an object such as blue, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>nīla-ādikam artham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>through defined space, time, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>niyata-deśa-kāla-āditayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>then<rp> (</rp><rt>tadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, with respect to the portion _(known as)_ <ruby>manifestation in a defined space, time, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>niyata-deśa-kāla-ādi-ābhāsa-aṁśe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(all that is)_ <ruby>His<rp> (</rp><rt>asya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>state as the Manifestor<rp> (</rp><rt>sraṣṭṛtā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. With respect to the portion _(known as)_ manifestation _(of that object)_ <ruby>in another space, time, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>anya-deśa-kāla-ādi-ābhāsa-aṁśe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(all that is)_ <ruby>His<rp> (</rp><rt>asya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>state as the Withdrawer<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁhartṛtā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. With respect to the portion _(known as continuity of the)_ <ruby>manifestation of blue, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>nīla-ādi-ābhāsa-aṁśe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(all that is His)_ <ruby>state as the Sustainer<rp> (</rp><rt>sthāpakatā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. With respect to the portion _(called)_ <ruby>manifestation<rp> (</rp><rt>ābhāsa-aṁśe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>as different<rp> (</rp><rt>bhedena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(all that is His)_ state <ruby>as the Producer of Concealment/Obscuration<rp> (</rp><rt>vilaya-kāritā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. _(And,)_ <ruby>with respect to the revelation<rp> (</rp><rt>prakāśane</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(that such an object)_ <ruby>is in unity with the Light<rp> (</rp><rt>prakāśa-aikyena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(all that is His)_ <ruby>state as the Bestower of Grace or Favor<rp> (</rp><rt>anugrahītṛtā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Since<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the authorship of the fivefold function/act<rp> (</rp><rt>pañcavidha-kṛtya-kāritvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>belongs to the Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>therefore<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(this)_ <ruby>has been extensively investigated<rp> (</rp><rt>vitatya... nirṇītam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by me<rp> (</rp><rt>mayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> in _(my)_ <ruby>Spandasandoha<rp> (</rp><rt>spanda-sandohe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>Thus<rp> (</rp><rt>evam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>idam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>authorship of the fivefold function/act<rp> (</rp><rt>pañcavidha-kṛtya-kāritvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>which is always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> one's own <span class="pv-note">— of oneself —</span> <span class="pv-w">(ātmīyam)</span>, <ruby>if it is perceived again and again<rp> (</rp><rt>pariśīlyamānam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with firm resolve<rp> (</rp><rt>dṛḍha-pratipattyā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>reveals<rp> (</rp><rt>unmīlayati eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> the Great Lordship <span class="pv-note">— it should be «mahaiśvaryam» and not «māheśvaryam», since, as far as I know, this term does not exist —</span> <span class="pv-w">(māheśvaryam)</span> <ruby>to the devotees<rp> (</rp><rt>bhakti-bhājām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. For this reason (atas eva), those who (ye... te) <ruby>always<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> perceive <span class="pv-note">— lit. practice —</span> <span class="pv-w">(pariśīlayanti)</span> this <span class="pv-note">— the authorship of the fivefold function/act —</span> <span class="pv-w">(etad)</span>, <ruby>knowing<rp> (</rp><rt>jānānāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that the universe<rp> (</rp><rt>viśvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is the unfolding of their essential nature<rp> (</rp><rt>svarūpa-vikāsa-mayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(become)_ <ruby>liberated in life<rp> (</rp><rt>jīvat-muktāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — This is what has been passed down _(to us)_ <ruby>in the written tradition<rp> (</rp><rt>iti āmnātāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>But<rp> (</rp><rt>tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> those who (ye... te) do not _(do)_ <ruby>thus<rp> (</rp><rt>na tathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>seeing<rp> (</rp><rt>paśyantaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the multitude of objects<rp> (</rp><rt>meya-jātam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>as different<rp> (</rp><rt>vibhinnam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, remain bound <span class="pv-note">— remain in bondage —</span> <span class="pv-w">(baddha-ātmānaḥ)</span>||10||</p>
