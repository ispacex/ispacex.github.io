<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">एवं च</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ ca</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चिदानन्दलाभे देहादिषु चेत्यमानेष्वपि चिदैकात्म्यप्रतिपत्तिदार्ढ्यं जीवन्मुक्तिः॥१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Cidānandalābhe dehādiṣu cetyamāneṣvapi cidaikātmyapratipattidārḍhyaṁ jīvanmuktiḥ||16||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">विश्वात्मसात्कारात्मनि समावेशरूपे चिदानन्दे लब्धे व्युत्थानदशायां दलकल्पतया देहप्राणनीलसुखादिष्वाभासमानेष्वपि यत्समावेशसंस्कारबलात्प्रतिपादयिष्यमाणयुक्तिक्रमोपबृंहिताच्चिदैकात्म्यप्रतिपत्तिदार्ढ्यम् - अविचला चिदेकत्वप्रथा सैव जीवन्मुक्तिः - जीवतः प्राणानपि धारयतो मुक्तिः प्रत्यभिज्ञातनिजस्वरूपविद्राविताशेषपाशराशित्वात्। यथोक्तं स्पन्दशास्त्रे</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Viśvātmasātkārātmani samāveśarūpe cidānande labdhe vyutthānadaśāyāṁ dalakalpatayā dehaprāṇanīlasukhādiṣvābhāsamāneṣvapi yatsamāveśasaṁskārabalātpratipādayiṣyamāṇayuktikramopabṛṁhitāccidaikātmyapratipattidārḍhyam - avicalā cidekatvaprathā saiva jīvanmuktiḥ - jīvataḥ prāṇānapi dhārayato muktiḥ pratyabhijñātanijasvarūpavidrāvitāśeṣapāśarāśitvāt| Yathoktaṁ spandaśāstre</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति वा यस्य संवित्तिः क्रीडात्वेनाखिलं जगत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Iti vā yasya saṁvittiḥ krīḍātvenākhilaṁ jagat|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">स पश्यन्सततं युक्तो जीवन्मुक्तो न संशयः॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sa paśyansatataṁ yukto jīvanmukto na saṁśayaḥ||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति॥१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti||16||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>And<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>thus<rp> (</rp><rt>evam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>:</p>

<p class="pv-tr pv-anchor" id="s1-ru" markdown="1">Когда _(человек)_ <ruby>достигает<rp> (</rp><rt>lābhe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Блаженства<rp> (</rp><rt>ānanda</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Абсолютного Сознания<rp> (</rp><rt>cit</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(обретается)_ <ruby>устойчивость, или твёрдость<rp> (</rp><rt>dārḍhyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>в осознании<rp> (</rp><rt>pratipatti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>идентичности<rp> (</rp><rt>aikātmya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> с _(этим)_ <ruby>Абсолютным Сознанием<rp> (</rp><rt>cit</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>даже несмотря на то<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>что переживается<rp> (</rp><rt>cetyamāneṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>тело<rp> (</rp><rt>deha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> и пр. <span class="pv-note">— ādi —</span> <span class="pv-w">(ādiṣu)</span>. _(Это состояние известно как)_ Jīvanmukti <span class="pv-note">— т.e. "Освобождение, или Mukti при жизни" —</span> <span class="pv-w">(jīvanmuktiḥ)</span>||16||</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">When <ruby>the Bliss of Absolute Consciousness is obtained<rp> (</rp><rt>cit-ānande labdhe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, which produces <ruby>equality between oneself and the universe<rp> (</rp><rt>viśva-ātmasāt-kāra-ātmani</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(and)_ whose form is samāveśa <span class="pv-note">— absorption —</span> <span class="pv-w">(samāveśarūpe)</span>, _(then,)_ <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> in the condition of vyutthāna <span class="pv-note">— all states except samāveśa —</span> <span class="pv-w">(vyutthāna-daśāyām)</span> —in <ruby>which body, vital energy, blue, pleasure, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>deha-prāṇa-nīla-sukha-ādiṣu ābhāsamāneṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>are being manifested as if they were fragments<rp> (</rp><rt>dala-kalpatayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>—, _(there is)_ stability or firmness of the awareness of identity with _(that)_ <ruby>Absolute Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>cit-aikātmya-pratipatti-dārḍhyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —_(there is)_ <ruby>a firm<rp> (</rp><rt>avicalā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>expansion of unity with Absolute Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>cit-ekatva-prathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>— <ruby>which<rp> (</rp><rt>yad</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(occurs)_ due to the force of the impressions of samāveśa <span class="pv-note">— of absorption in Śiva —</span> <span class="pv-w">(samāveśa-saṁskāra-balāt)</span>, _(all of it)_ magnified by <ruby>the succession of means that will be explained<rp> (</rp><rt>pratipādayiṣyamāṇa-yukti-krama-upabṛṁhitāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. That _(firm expansion of unity with Absolute Consciousness)_ <span class="pv-w">(sā eva)</span> _(is)_ <ruby>Liberation while living<rp> (</rp><rt>jīvat-muktiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(that is,)_ <ruby>Liberation<rp> (</rp><rt>muktiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of someone who remains alive<rp> (</rp><rt>jīvataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(and)_ who preserves _(his)_ <ruby>vital energies<rp> (</rp><rt>prāṇān api dhārayataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. _(Why?)_ Because the mass of all fetters has been dispersed <span class="pv-note">— also "dissolved" —</span> since <ruby>one's own essential nature has been recognized<rp> (</rp><rt>pratyabhijñāta-nija-svarūpa-vidrāvita-aśeṣa-pāśa-rāśitvāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>As<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>it has been said<rp> (</rp><rt>uktam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> in the scripture on Spanda <span class="pv-note">— in Spandakārikā-s 2.5 —</span> <span class="pv-w">(spanda-śāstre)</span>:</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«Or<rp> (</rp><rt>iti vā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>he<rp> (</rp><rt>saḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>who has that<rp> (</rp><rt>yasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>knowledge or realization<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁvittiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(and)_ <ruby>is constantly<rp> (</rp><rt>satatam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> united _(with the Supreme Self)_ <span class="pv-w">(yuktaḥ)</span> <ruby>views<rp> (</rp><rt>paśyan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the whole<rp> (</rp><rt>akhilam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>world<rp> (</rp><rt>jagat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> as a _(divine)_ <ruby>play<rp> (</rp><rt>krīḍātvena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>He is liberated while living<rp> (</rp><rt>jīvat-muktaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>there is no<rp> (</rp><rt>na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>doubt<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁśayaḥ... iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(about it)_»</p>
