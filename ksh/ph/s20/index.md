<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदा प्रकाशानन्दसारमहामन्त्रवीर्यात्मकपूर्णाहन्तावेशात्सदा सर्वसर्गसंहारकारिनिजसंविद्देवताचक्रेश्वरताप्राप्तिर्भवतीति शिवम्॥२०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadā prakāśānandasāramahāmantravīryātmakapūrṇāhantāveśātsadā sarvasargasaṁhārakārinijasaṁviddevatācakreśvaratāprāptirbhavatīti śivam||20||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">नित्योदिते समाधौ लब्धे सति प्रकाशानन्दसारा - चिदाह्लादैकघना महती मन्त्रवीर्यात्मिका - सर्वमन्त्रजीवितभूता पूर्णा पराभट्टारिकारूपा येयमहन्ता - अकृत्रिमः स्वात्मचमत्कारस्तत्रावेशात्सदा कालाग्न्यादेश्चरमकलापर्यन्तस्य विश्वस्य यौ सर्गसंहारौ - विचित्रौ सृष्टिप्रलयौ तत्कारि यन्निजं संविद्देवताचक्रं तदैश्वर्यस्य प्राप्तिः - आसादनं भवति प्राकरणिकस्य परमयोगिन इत्यर्थ इत्येतत्सर्वं शिवस्वरूपमेवेत्युपसंहारः - इति सङ्गतिः। तत्र यावदिदं किञ्चित्संवेद्यते तस्य संवेदनमेव स्वरूपं तस्याप्यन्तर्मुखविमर्शमयाः प्रमातारस्तत्त्वं तेषामपि विगलितदेहाद्युपाधिसङ्कोचाभिमानाशेषशरीरा सदाशिवेश्वरतैव सारमस्या अपि प्रकाशैकसद्भावापादिताशेषविश्वचमत्कारमयः श्रीमान्महेश्वर एव परमार्थः - नहि पारमार्थिकप्रकाशावेशं विना कस्यापि प्रकाशमानता घटते - स च परमेश्वरः स्वातन्त्र्यसारत्वादादिक्षान्तामायीयशब्दराशिपरामर्शमयत्वेनैवैतत्स्वीकृतसमस्तवाच्यवाचकमयाशेषजगदानन्दसद्भावापादनात्परं परिपूर्णत्वात्सर्वाकाङ्क्षाशून्यतयानन्दप्रसरनिर्भरोऽत एवानुत्तराकुलस्वरूपादकारादारभ्य शक्तिस्फाररूपहकलापर्यन्तं यद्विश्वं प्रसृतं क्षकारस्य प्रसरशमनरूपत्वात्तदकारहकाराभ्यामेव सम्पुटीकारयुक्त्या प्रत्याहारन्यायेनान्तः स्वीकृतं सदविभागवेदनात्मकबिन्दुरूपतया स्फुरितमनुत्तर एव विश्राम्यति - इति शब्दराशिस्वरूप एवायमकृतको विमर्शः। यथोक्तम्</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nityodite samādhau labdhe sati prakāśānandasārā - cidāhlādaikaghanā mahatī mantravīryātmikā - sarvamantrajīvitabhūtā pūrṇā parābhaṭṭārikārūpā yeyamahantā - akṛtrimaḥ svātmacamatkārastatrāveśātsadā kālāgnyādeścaramakalāparyantasya viśvasya yau sargasaṁhārau - vicitrau sṛṣṭipralayau tatkāri yannijaṁ saṁviddevatācakraṁ tadaiśvaryasya prāptiḥ - āsādanaṁ bhavati prākaraṇikasya paramayogina ityartha ityetatsarvaṁ śivasvarūpamevetyupasaṁhāraḥ - iti saṅgatiḥ| Tatra yāvadidaṁ kiñcitsaṁvedyate tasya saṁvedanameva svarūpaṁ tasyāpyantarmukhavimarśamayāḥ pramātārastattvaṁ teṣāmapi vigalitadehādyupādhisaṅkocābhimānāśeṣaśarīrā sadāśiveśvarataiva sāramasyā api prakāśaikasadbhāvāpāditāśeṣaviśvacamatkāramayaḥ śrīmānmaheśvara eva paramārthaḥ - nahi pāramārthikaprakāśāveśaṁ vinā kasyāpi prakāśamānatā ghaṭate - sa ca parameśvaraḥ svātantryasāratvādādikṣāntāmāyīyaśabdarāśiparāmarśamayatvenaivaitatsvīkṛtasamastavācyavācakamayāśeṣajagadānandasadbhāvāpādanātparaṁ paripūrṇatvātsarvākāṅkṣāśūnyatayānandaprasaranirbharo'ta evānuttarākulasvarūpādakārādārabhya śaktisphārarūpahakalāparyantaṁ yadviśvaṁ prasṛtaṁ kṣakārasya prasaraśamanarūpatvāttadakārahakārābhyāmeva sampuṭīkārayuktyā pratyāhāranyāyenāntaḥ svīkṛtaṁ sadavibhāgavedanātmakabindurūpatayā sphuritamanuttara eva viśrāmyati - iti śabdarāśisvarūpa evāyamakṛtako vimarśaḥ| Yathoktam</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">प्रकाशस्यात्मविश्रान्तिरहंभावो हि कीर्तितः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prakāśasyātmaviśrāntirahaṁbhāvo hi kīrtitaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">उक्ता च सैव विश्रान्तिः सर्वापेक्षानिरोधतः॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Uktā ca saiva viśrāntiḥ sarvāpekṣānirodhataḥ||</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">स्वातन्त्र्यमथ कर्तृत्वं मुख्यमीश्वरतापि च।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svātantryamatha kartṛtvaṁ mukhyamīśvaratāpi ca|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति। एषैव चाहन्ता सर्वमन्त्राणामुदयविश्रान्तिस्थानत्वादेतद्बलेनैव च तत्तदर्थक्रियाकारित्वान्महती वीर्यभूमिः। तदुक्तम्</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti| Eṣaiva cāhantā sarvamantrāṇāmudayaviśrāntisthānatvādetadbalenaiva ca tattadarthakriyākāritvānmahatī vīryabhūmiḥ| Taduktam</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">तदाक्रम्य बलं मन्त्रा...।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadākramya balaṁ mantrā...|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इत्यादि</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">ityādi</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">... तेनैते शिवधर्मिणः॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">... tenaite śivadharmiṇaḥ||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इत्यन्तं श्रीस्पन्दे। शिवसूत्रेष्वपि</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">ityantaṁ śrīspande| Śivasūtreṣvapi</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">महाह्रदानुसन्धानान्मन्त्रवीर्यानुभवः</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mahāhradānusandhānānmantravīryānubhavaḥ</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति। तदत्र महामन्त्रवीर्यात्मिकायां पूर्णाहन्तायामावेशो - देहप्राणादिनिमज्जनात्तत्पदावाप्त्यवष्टम्भेन देहादीनां नीलादीनामपि तद्रसाप्लावनेन तन्मयीकरणम्। तथा हि - देहसुखनीलादि यत्किञ्चित्प्रथतेऽध्यवसीयते स्मर्यते सङ्कल्प्यते वा तत्र सर्वत्रैव भगवती चितिशक्तिमयी प्रथा भित्तिभूतैव स्फुरति - तदस्फुरणे कस्याप्यस्फुरणादित्युक्तत्वात्। केवलं तथा स्फुरन्त्यपि सा तन्मायाशक्त्यावभासितदेहनीलाद्युपरागदत्ताभिमानवशाद्भिन्नभिन्नस्वभावेव भान्ती ज्ञानसङ्कल्पाध्यवसायादिरूपतया मायाप्रमातृभिरभिमन्यते वस्तुतस्त्वेकैवासौ चितिशक्तिः। यथोक्तम्</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti| Tadatra mahāmantravīryātmikāyāṁ pūrṇāhantāyāmāveśo - dehaprāṇādinimajjanāttatpadāvāptyavaṣṭambhena dehādīnāṁ nīlādīnāmapi tadrasāplāvanena tanmayīkaraṇam| Tathā hi - dehasukhanīlādi yatkiñcitprathate'dhyavasīyate smaryate saṅkalpyate vā tatra sarvatraiva bhagavatī citiśaktimayī prathā bhittibhūtaiva sphurati - tadasphuraṇe kasyāpyasphuraṇādityuktatvāt| Kevalaṁ tathā sphurantyapi sā tanmāyāśaktyāvabhāsitadehanīlādyuparāgadattābhimānavaśādbhinnabhinnasvabhāveva bhāntī jñānasaṅkalpādhyavasāyādirūpatayā māyāpramātṛbhirabhimanyate vastutastvekaivāsau citiśaktiḥ| Yathoktam</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">या चैषा प्रतिभा तत्तत्पदार्थक्रमरूषिता।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yā caiṣā pratibhā tattatpadārthakramarūṣitā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">अक्रमानन्तचिद्रूपः प्रमाता स महेश्वरः॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Akramānantacidrūpaḥ pramātā sa maheśvaraḥ||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति। तथा</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti| Tathā</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">मायाशक्त्या विभोः सैव भिन्नसंवेद्यगोचरा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Māyāśaktyā vibhoḥ saiva bhinnasaṁvedyagocarā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">कथिता ज्ञानसङ्कल्पाध्यवसायादिनामभिः॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kathitā jñānasaṅkalpādhyavasāyādināmabhiḥ||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति। एवमेषा सर्वदशास्वेकैव चितिशक्तिर्विजृम्भमाणा यदि तदनुप्रवेशतदवष्टम्भयुक्त्या समासाद्यते तत्तदावेशात्पूर्वोक्तयुक्त्या करणोन्मीलननिमीलनक्रमेण सर्वस्य सर्वमयत्वात्तत्तत्संहारादावपि सदा सर्वसर्गसंहारकारि यत्सहजसंवित्तिदेवताचक्रम् - अमायीयान्तर्बहिष्करणमरीचिपुञ्जस्तत्रेश्वरता - साम्राज्यं परभैरवात्मता तत्प्राप्तिर्भवति परमयोगिनः। यथोक्तम्</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti| Evameṣā sarvadaśāsvekaiva citiśaktirvijṛmbhamāṇā yadi tadanupraveśatadavaṣṭambhayuktyā samāsādyate tattadāveśātpūrvoktayuktyā karaṇonmīlananimīlanakrameṇa sarvasya sarvamayatvāttattatsaṁhārādāvapi sadā sarvasargasaṁhārakāri yatsahajasaṁvittidevatācakram - amāyīyāntarbahiṣkaraṇamarīcipuñjastatreśvaratā - sāmrājyaṁ parabhairavātmatā tatprāptirbhavati paramayoginaḥ| Yathoktam</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">यदा त्वेकत्र संरूढस्तदा तस्य लयोद्भवौ।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yadā tvekatra saṁrūḍhastadā tasya layodbhavau|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">नियच्छन्भोक्तृतामेति ततश्चक्रेश्वरो भवेत्॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Niyacchanbhoktṛtāmeti tataścakreśvaro bhavet||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति। अत्रैकत्रेति</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti| Atraikatreti</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">एकत्रारोपयेत्सर्वम्...।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ekatrāropayetsarvam...|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति। चित्सामान्यस्पन्दभूरुन्मेषात्मा व्याख्यातव्या। तस्येत्यनेन</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti| Citsāmānyaspandabhūrunmeṣātmā vyākhyātavyā| Tasyetyanena</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">पुर्यष्टकेन संरुद्ध...।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Puryaṣṭakena saṁruddha...|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति। उपक्रान्तं पुर्यष्टकमेव पराम्रष्टव्यं न तु यथा विवरणकृत एकत्र सूक्ष्मे स्थूले शरीरे वेति व्याकृतवन्तः। स्तुतं च मया</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti| Upakrāntaṁ puryaṣṭakameva parāmraṣṭavyaṁ na tu yathā vivaraṇakṛta ekatra sūkṣme sthūle śarīre veti vyākṛtavantaḥ| Stutaṁ ca mayā</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">स्वतन्त्रश्चितिचक्राणां चक्रवर्ती महेश्वरः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svatantraściticakrāṇāṁ cakravartī maheśvaraḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">संवित्तिदेवताचक्रजुष्टः कोऽपि जयत्यसौ॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Saṁvittidevatācakrajuṣṭaḥ ko'pi jayatyasau||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति। इतिशब्द उपसंहारे यदेतावदुक्तप्रकरणशरीरं तत्सर्वं शिवम् - शिवप्राप्तिहेतुत्वाच्छिवात्प्रसृतत्वाच्छिवस्वरूपाभिन्नत्वाच्च शिवमयमेवेति शिवम्॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti| Itiśabda upasaṁhāre yadetāvaduktaprakaraṇaśarīraṁ tatsarvaṁ śivam - śivaprāptihetutvācchivātprasṛtatvācchivasvarūpābhinnatvācca śivamayameveti śivam||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">देहप्राणसुखादिभिः प्रतिकिलं संरुध्यमानो जनः पूर्णानन्दघनामिमां न चिनुते माहेश्वरीं स्वां चितिम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dehaprāṇasukhādibhiḥ pratikilaṁ saṁrudhyamāno janaḥ pūrṇānandaghanāmimāṁ na cinute māheśvarīṁ svāṁ citim|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">मध्ये बोधसुधाब्धि विश्वमभितस्तत्फेनपिण्डोपमं यः पश्येदुपदेशतस्तु कथितः साक्षात्स एकः शिवः॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Madhye bodhasudhābdhi viśvamabhitastatphenapiṇḍopamaṁ yaḥ paśyedupadeśatastu kathitaḥ sākṣātsa ekaḥ śivaḥ||</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">येषां वृत्तः शाङ्करः शक्तिपातो येऽनभ्यासात्तीक्ष्णयुक्तिष्वयोग्याः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yeṣāṁ vṛttaḥ śāṅkaraḥ śaktipāto ye'nabhyāsāttīkṣṇayuktiṣvayogyāḥ|</span><br />

that presents itself as the calamity of manifested body, blue, etc. <span class="pv-note">— «uparāga», besides «calamity», also means «color», that is, it is a calamity that Pure Consciousness considers Itself as the body, the color blue, etc.; yes, Kṣemarāja is often very confusing —</span> <span class="pv-w">(avabhāsita-deha-nīla-ādi-uparāga-datta-abhimāna-vaśāt)</span>, is considered _(also)_ <ruby>erroneously<rp> (</rp><rt>abhimanyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> by the māyic knowers <span class="pv-note">— the Sakala-s and Pralayākala-s —</span> <span class="pv-w">(māyā-pramātṛbhiḥ)</span> <ruby>as knowledge, imagination, determination, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>jñāna-saṅkalpa-adhyavasāya-ādi-rūpatayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>But<rp> (</rp><rt>tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in reality<rp> (</rp><rt>vastutas</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>asau</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Power of Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>citi-śaktiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is only one<rp> (</rp><rt>ekā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>As<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>it has been said<rp> (</rp><rt>uktam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(in the Īśvarapratyabhijñākārikā-s 1.7.1:)_</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«And<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>eṣā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Pratibhā or ever creative Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>pratibhā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>which<rp> (</rp><rt>yā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> is adorned/perfumed with a succession <ruby>of various categories/objects of experience<rp> (</rp><rt>tad-tad-padārtha-krama-rūṣitā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(is)_ <ruby>the Knower<rp> (</rp><rt>pramātā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the Great Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>saḥ mahā-īśvaraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> whose nature/form <ruby>is infinite Consciousness devoid of succession<rp> (</rp><rt>akrama-ananta-cit-rūpaḥ... iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>Similarly<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(in the Īśvarapratyabhijñākārikā-s 1.5.18:)_</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«By the power of Māyā<rp> (</rp><rt>māyā-śaktyā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, to the Omnipresent One <span class="pv-note">— i.e. to the Master —</span> <span class="pv-w">(vibhoḥ)</span> that _(manifestation)_ <span class="pv-w">(sā eva)</span> <ruby>whose sphere of action is different objects<rp> (</rp><rt>bhinna-saṁvedya-gocarā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is called<rp> (</rp><rt>kathitā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> by the names <ruby>of knowledge, imagination, determination, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>jñāna-saṅkalpa-adhyavasāya-ādi-nāmabhiḥ... iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>Thus<rp> (</rp><rt>evam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>eṣā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Power of Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>citi-śaktiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>flourishes/arises<rp> (</rp><rt>vijṛmbhamāṇā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>as only one<rp> (</rp><rt>ekā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in all conditions<rp> (</rp><rt>sarva-daśāsu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>If<rp> (</rp><rt>yadi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> by means of an entering into and firm grasp of That <span class="pv-note">— of the Power of Consciousness —</span> <span class="pv-w">(tad-anupraveśa-tad-avaṣṭambha-yuktyā)</span>, It is attained <span class="pv-note">— the Power of Consciousness is achieved —</span> <span class="pv-w">(samāsādyate)</span>, <ruby>then<rp> (</rp><rt>tad</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>through a penetration into That<rp> (</rp><rt>tad-āveśāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by means of the aforementioned method<rp> (</rp><rt>pūrva-ukta-yuktyā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —through the process/succession of opening and closing the senses <span class="pv-note">— Kramamudrā —</span> <span class="pv-w">(karaṇa-unmīlana-nimīlana-krameṇa)</span>—, <ruby>since everything consists of everything<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvasya sarva-mayatvāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the various withdrawals, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>tad-tad-saṁhāra-ādu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>there is<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for the supreme Yogī<rp> (</rp><rt>parama-yoginaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the acquisition/obtainment of that<rp> (</rp><rt>tad-prāptiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. _(Of what? Of the)_ <ruby>State of Parabhairava<rp> (</rp><rt>parabhairava-ātmatā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(which is)_ <ruby>īśvaratā<rp> (</rp><rt>īśvaratā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(or)_ <ruby>sovereignty<rp> (</rp><rt>sāmrājyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>over that<rp> (</rp><rt>tatra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. _(Sovereignty over what? Over)_ <ruby>the group of deities of natural Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>sahaja-saṁvitti-devatā-cakram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —the non-māyic mass of rays of the internal and external instruments <span class="pv-note">— the goddesses of mind, ego, intellect and indriya-s —</span> <span class="pv-w">(a-māyīya-antar-bahis-karaṇa-marīci-puñjaḥ)</span>— <ruby>which<rp> (</rp><rt>yad</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> produces all manifestations _(and)_ withdrawals _(of the universe)_ <span class="pv-w">(sarva-sarga-saṁhārakāri)</span>. <ruby>As<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>it has been said<rp> (</rp><rt>uktam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(in the Spandakārikā-s 3.19:)_</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«But<rp> (</rp><rt>tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>when<rp> (</rp><rt>yadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>taking root firmly<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁrūḍhaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> in one place <span class="pv-note">— i.e. in the principle of Spanda —</span> <span class="pv-w">(ekatra)</span>, <ruby>then<rp> (</rp><rt>tadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>by controlling<rp> (</rp><rt>niyacchan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the disappearance and emergence<rp> (</rp><rt>laya-udbhavau</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> of that _(Puryaṣṭaka <span class="pv-note">— subtle body —</span>)_ <span class="pv-w">(tasya)</span>, _(the ex-limited being)_ becomes the _(real)_ <ruby>enjoyer<rp> (</rp><rt>bhoktṛtām eti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(and,)_ <ruby>consequently<rp> (</rp><rt>tatas</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, he _(also)_ <ruby>becomes<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavet</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> the Lord of the group _(of śakti-s or powers)_ <span class="pv-w">(cakra-īśvaraḥ... iti)</span>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>Here<rp> (</rp><rt>atra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(the term)_ «ekatra» <span class="pv-note">— in one place —</span> (ekatra iti) _(from the previous stanza, is explained by Vasugupta through this portion of the Spandakārikā-s 3.12:)_</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">«... <ruby>one should put<rp> (</rp><rt>āropayet</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>everything<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> in one place (ekatra... iti)...»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>The stage of the generic Spanda of Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>cit-sāmānya-spanda-bhūḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>should be explained<rp> (</rp><rt>vyākhyātavyā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>as possessing the nature of Unmeṣa<rp> (</rp><rt>unmeṣa-ātmā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>With this<rp> (</rp><rt>anena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> «tasya» <span class="pv-note">— see above in the quotation from the Spandakārikā-s 3.19 —</span> (tasya iti) _(Vasugupta referred to the Puryaṣṭaka or subtle body, which he mentioned in the Spandakārikā-s 3.17:)_</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«Completely stopped and besieged<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁruddhaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by the Puryaṣṭaka<rp> (</rp><rt>puryaṣṭakena... iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>...»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">_(Thus, as I said before, «tasya» <span class="pv-note">— lit. of that —</span>)_ <ruby>should be considered<rp> (</rp><rt>parāmraṣṭavyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> as _(relating to)_ the Puryaṣṭaka <span class="pv-note">— subtle body —</span> <span class="pv-w">(puryaṣṭakam eva)</span> <ruby>which was mentioned before<rp> (</rp><rt>upakrāntam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, and not (na tu) <ruby>as<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>did interpret<rp> (</rp><rt>vyākṛtavantaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> the author of the Vivaraṇa <span class="pv-note">— Kṣemarāja refers to Kallaṭa, and uses the plural number respectfully —</span> <span class="pv-w">(vivaraṇa-kṛtaḥ)</span>: <ruby>«In one place<rp> (</rp><rt>ekatra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(that is,)_ <ruby>in the subtle or gross body<rp> (</rp><rt>sūkṣme sthūle śarīre vā iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>». <ruby>I have praised<rp> (</rp><rt>stutam ca mayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(thus by means of this stanza:)_</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">«Triumphs (jayati asau) <ruby>someone<rp> (</rp><rt>kaḥ api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>who is the Free<rp> (</rp><rt>svatantraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Emperor<rp> (</rp><rt>cakravartī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> of the cakra-s or groups of Citi <span class="pv-note">— of Śakti —</span> <span class="pv-w">(citi-cakrāṇām)</span> _(and)_ <ruby>the Great Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>mahā-īśvaraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> served and worshipped <ruby>by the group of deities of Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁvitti-devatā-cakra-juṣṭaḥ... iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">_(Now Kṣemarāja analyses the expression «iti śivam» at the end of this aphorism 20:)_ <ruby>The word «iti»<rp> (</rp><rt>iti-śabdaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>implies conclusion<rp> (</rp><rt>upasaṁhāre</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>All<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>tad</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>which<rp> (</rp><rt>yad</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> is the body of the aforesaid book <span class="pv-note">— lit. «of the aforesaid book whose extent is this», that is, of this commentary on the Pratyabhijñāhṛdayam —</span> <span class="pv-w">(etāvat ukta-prakaraṇa-śarīram)</span> _(is)_ <ruby>Śiva<rp> (</rp><rt>śivam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. _(Why?)_ _**(1)**_ Because <ruby>it is the cause of the attainment of Śiva<rp> (</rp><rt>śiva-prāpti-hetutvāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _**(2)**_ <ruby>because it has gotten unfolded<rp> (</rp><rt>prasṛtatvāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from Śiva<rp> (</rp><rt>śivāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _**(3)**_ because it is <ruby>not different from the essential nature of Śiva<rp> (</rp><rt>śiva-svarūpa-abhinnatvāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Iti śivam<rp> (</rp><rt>iti śivam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — _(Thus, everything)_ <ruby>consists solely of Śiva<rp> (</rp><rt>śiva-mayam eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — _(Here ended the commentary on aphorism 20. What follows closes the book appropriately)_.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«The person<rp> (</rp><rt>janaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>being constantly blocked/obstructed<rp> (</rp><rt>pratikalam saṁrudhyamānaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by the body, the vital energy, pleasure, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>deha-prāṇa-sukha-ādibhiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, does not perceive (na cinute) this Citi <span class="pv-note">— Śakti —</span> <ruby>of his<rp> (</rp><rt>imām... svām citim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, which is _(the Power)_ <ruby>of the Great Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>māheśvarīm</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(and)_ which <ruby>is a compact mass of Full and Perfect Bliss<rp> (</rp><rt>pūrṇa-ānanda-ghanām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>But<rp> (</rp><rt>tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>he who<rp> (</rp><rt>yaḥ... saḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, in the <ruby>midst of the Ocean of Nectar of Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>madhye bodha-sudhā-abdhi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>perceives<rp> (</rp><rt>paśyet</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <span class="pv-note">— <ruby>through the teaching<rp> (</rp><rt>upadeśatas</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —</span> <ruby>that the universe<rp> (</rp><rt>viśvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> is similar to a mass of Its foam <span class="pv-note">— of said Ocean of Nectar of Consciousness —</span> <span class="pv-w">(tad-phena-piṇḍa-upamam)</span> <ruby>on all sides<rp> (</rp><rt>abhitas</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>he is said to be<rp> (</rp><rt>kathitaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the one<rp> (</rp><rt>ekaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Śiva<rp> (</rp><rt>śivaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in person<rp> (</rp><rt>sākṣāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«This<rp> (</rp><rt>eṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> teaching about the _(Supreme)_ Principle <span class="pv-note">— about Truth —</span> <span class="pv-w">(tattva-upadeśaḥ)</span> <ruby>has been proclaimed<rp> (</rp><rt>uktaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>for those<rp> (</rp><rt>teṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>who<rp> (</rp><rt>ye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>due to lack of practice<rp> (</rp><rt>anabhyāsāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>are not fit<rp> (</rp><rt>ayogyāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with regard to keen/sharp reasonings<rp> (</rp><rt>tīkṣṇa-yuktiṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(and)_ <ruby>who cannot know/understand<rp> (</rp><rt>śaktāḥ jñātum na</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> the Īśvarapratyabhijñā _(of Utpaladeva)_ <span class="pv-w">(īśvara-pratyabhijñām)</span>, _(but)_ <ruby>for whom<rp> (</rp><rt>yeṣām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(nevertheless,)_ <ruby>a descent of the Power<rp> (</rp><rt>śakti-pātaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> of Śaṅkara <span class="pv-note">— of Śiva —</span> <span class="pv-w">(śāṅkaraḥ)</span> <ruby>has occurred<rp> (</rp><rt>vṛttaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>This<rp> (</rp><rt>idam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Pratyabhijñāhṛdayam<rp> (</rp><rt>pratyabhijñā-hṛdayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is finished<rp> (</rp><rt>samāptam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">_(This is)_ <ruby>the work<rp> (</rp><rt>kṛtiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> of the venerable Ācārya _(called)_ Rājānaka Kṣemarāja <span class="pv-w">(śrīmataḥ rājānaka-kṣemarāja-ācāryasya)</span>, who depends on the lotus feet of His Holiness, the adorable Abhinavagupta, the best of the <ruby>Ācārya-s and Great Devotee of the Great Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>tatrabhavat-mahā-māheśvara-ācārya-varya-śrīmat-abhinavagupta-pāda-padma-upajīvinaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>May there be<rp> (</rp><rt>astu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>auspiciousness and well-being<rp> (</rp><rt>śubham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>!</p>
