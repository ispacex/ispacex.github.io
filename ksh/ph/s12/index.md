<span class="pv-iast">Yasya punaḥ sadgurūpadeśaṁ vinaitatparijñānaṁ nāsti tasyāvacchāditasvasvarūpābhirnijābhiḥ śaktibhirvyāmohitatvaṁ bhavati - Ityāha</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदपरिज्ञाने स्वशक्तिभिर्व्यामोहितता संसारित्वम्॥१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadaparijñāne svaśaktibhirvyāmohitatā saṁsāritvam||12||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">तस्यैतस्य सदा सम्भवतः पञ्चविधकृत्यकारित्वस्यापरिज्ञाने - शक्तिपातहेतुकस्वबलोन्मीलनाभावादप्रकाशने स्वाभिः शक्तिभिर्व्यामोहितत्वं - विविधलौकिकशास्त्रीयशङ्काशङ्कुकीलितत्वं यदिदमेव संसारित्वम्। तदुक्तं श्रीसर्ववीरभट्टारके</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tasyaitasya sadā sambhavataḥ pañcavidhakṛtyakāritvasyāparijñāne - śaktipātahetukasvabalonmīlanābhāvādaprakāśane svābhiḥ śaktibhirvyāmohitatvaṁ - vividhalaukikaśāstrīyaśaṅkāśaṅkukīlitatvaṁ yadidameva saṁsāritvam| Taduktaṁ śrīsarvavīrabhaṭṭārake</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">अज्ञानाच्छङ्कते लोकस्ततः सृष्टिश्च संहृतिः॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ajñānācchaṅkate lokastataḥ sṛṣṭiśca saṁhṛtiḥ||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">मन्त्रा वर्णात्मकाः सर्वे सर्वे वर्णाः शिवात्मकाः॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mantrā varṇātmakāḥ sarve sarve varṇāḥ śivātmakāḥ||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति च। तथा हि - चित्प्रकाशादव्यतिरिक्ता नित्योदितमहामन्त्ररूपा पूर्णाहंविमर्शमयी येयं परा वाक्शक्तिरादिक्षान्तरूपाशेषशक्तिचक्रगर्भिणी सा तावत्पश्यन्तीमध्यमादिक्रमेण ग्राहकभूमिकां भासयति। तत्र च परारूपत्वेन स्वरूपमप्रथयन्ती मायाप्रमातुरस्फुटासाधारणार्थावभासरूपां प्रतिक्षणं नवनवां विकल्पक्रियामुल्लासयति शुद्धामपि चाविकल्पभूमिं तदाच्छादितामेव दर्शयति। तत्र च ब्राह्म्यादिदेवताधिष्ठितककारादिविचित्रशक्तिभिर्व्यामोहितो देहप्राणादिमेव परिमितमवशमात्मानं मन्यते मूढजनः। ब्राह्म्यादिदेव्यः पशुदशायां भेदविषये सृष्टिस्थित्यभेदविषये च संहारं प्रथयन्त्यः परिमितविकल्पपात्रतामेव सम्पादयन्ति पतिदशायां तु भेदे संहारमभेदे च सर्गस्थिती प्रकटयन्त्यः क्रमात्क्रमं विकल्पनिर्ह्रासनेन श्रीमद्भैरवमुद्रानुप्रवेशमयीं महतीमविकल्पभूमिमेवोन्मीलयन्ति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti ca| Tathā hi - citprakāśādavyatiriktā nityoditamahāmantrarūpā pūrṇāhaṁvimarśamayī yeyaṁ parā vākśaktirādikṣāntarūpāśeṣaśakticakragarbhiṇī sā tāvatpaśyantīmadhyamādikrameṇa grāhakabhūmikāṁ bhāsayati| Tatra ca parārūpatvena svarūpamaprathayantī māyāpramāturasphuṭāsādhāraṇārthāvabhāsarūpāṁ pratikṣaṇaṁ navanavāṁ vikalpakriyāmullāsayati śuddhāmapi cāvikalpabhūmiṁ tadācchāditāmeva darśayati| Tatra ca brāhmyādidevatādhiṣṭhitakakārādivicitraśaktibhirvyāmohito dehaprāṇādimeva parimitamavaśamātmānaṁ manyate mūḍhajanaḥ| Brāhmyādidevyaḥ paśudaśāyāṁ bhedaviṣaye sṛṣṭisthityabhedaviṣaye ca saṁhāraṁ prathayantyaḥ parimitavikalpapātratāmeva sampādayanti patidaśāyāṁ tu bhede saṁhāramabhede ca sargasthitī prakaṭayantyaḥ kramātkramaṁ vikalpanirhrāsanena śrīmadbhairavamudrānupraveśamayīṁ mahatīmavikalpabhūmimevonmīlayanti|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">सर्वो ममायं विभव इत्येवं परिजानतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvo mamāyaṁ vibhava ityevaṁ parijānataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">विश्वात्मनो विकल्पानां प्रसरेऽपि महेशता॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Viśvātmano vikalpānāṁ prasare'pi maheśatā||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इत्यादिरूपां चिदानन्दावेशमग्नां शुद्धविकल्पशक्तिमुल्लासयन्ति। तत उक्तनीत्या स्वशक्तिव्यामोहिततैव संसारित्वम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">ityādirūpāṁ cidānandāveśamagnāṁ śuddhavikalpaśaktimullāsayanti| Tata uktanītyā svaśaktivyāmohitataiva saṁsāritvam|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">किञ्च चितिशक्तिरेव भगवती विश्ववमनात्संसारवामाचारत्वाच्च वामेश्वर्याख्या सती खेचरीगोचरीदिक्चरीभूचरीरूपैरशेषैः प्रमात्रन्तःकरणबहिष्करणभावस्वभावैः परिस्फुरन्ती पशुभूमिकाभ्यां शून्यपदविश्रान्ता किञ्चित्कर्तृत्वाद्यात्मककलादिशक्त्यात्मना खेचरीचक्रेण गोपितपारमार्थिकचिद्गगनचरीत्वस्वरूपेण चकास्ति भेदनिश्चयाभिमानविकल्पनप्रधानान्तःकरणदेवीरूपेण गोचरीचक्रेण गोपिताभेदनिश्चयाद्यात्मकपारमार्थिकस्वरूपेण प्रकाशते भेदालोचनादिप्रधानबहिष्करणदेवतात्मना च दिक्चरीचक्रेण गोपिताभेदप्रथात्मकपारमार्थिकस्वरूपेण स्फुरति सर्वतो व्यवच्छिन्नाभासस्वभावप्रमेयात्मना च भूचरीचक्रेण गोपितसार्वात्म्यस्वरूपेण पशुहृदयव्यामोहिना भाति। पतिभूमिकायां तु सर्वकर्तृत्वादिशक्त्यात्मकचिद्गगनचरीत्वेनाभेदनिश्चयाद्यात्मना गोचरीत्वेनाभेदालोचनाद्यात्मना दिक्चरीत्वेन स्वाङ्गकल्पाद्वयप्रथासारप्रमेयात्मना च भूचरीत्वेन पतिहृदयविकासिना स्फुरति। तथा चोक्तं सहजचमत्कारपरिजनिताकृतकादरेण भट्टदामोदरेण विमुक्तकेषु</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kiñca citiśaktireva bhagavatī viśvavamanātsaṁsāravāmācāratvācca vāmeśvaryākhyā satī khecarīgocarīdikcarībhūcarīrūpairaśeṣaiḥ pramātrantaḥkaraṇabahiṣkaraṇabhāvasvabhāvaiḥ parisphurantī paśubhūmikābhyāṁ śūnyapadaviśrāntā kiñcitkartṛtvādyātmakakalādiśaktyātmanā khecarīcakreṇa gopitapāramārthikacidgaganacarītvasvarūpeṇa cakāsti bhedaniścayābhimānavikalpanapradhānāntaḥkaraṇadevīrūpeṇa gocarīcakreṇa gopitābhedaniścayādyātmakapāramārthikasvarūpeṇa prakāśate bhedālocanādipradhānabahiṣkaraṇadevatātmanā ca dikcarīcakreṇa gopitābhedaprathātmakapāramārthikasvarūpeṇa sphurati sarvato vyavacchinnābhāsasvabhāvaprameyātmanā ca bhūcarīcakreṇa gopitasārvātmyasvarūpeṇa paśuhṛdayavyāmohinā bhāti| Patibhūmikāyāṁ tu sarvakartṛtvādiśaktyātmakacidgaganacarītvenābhedaniścayādyātmanā gocarītvenābhedālocanādyātmanā dikcarītvena svāṅgakalpādvayaprathāsāraprameyātmanā ca bhūcarītvena patihṛdayavikāsinā sphurati| Tathā coktaṁ sahajacamatkāraparijanitākṛtakādareṇa bhaṭṭadāmodareṇa vimuktakeṣu</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">पूर्णावच्छिन्नमात्रन्तर्बहिष्करणभावगाः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūrṇāvacchinnamātrantarbahiṣkaraṇabhāvagāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">वामेशाद्याः परिज्ञानाज्ञानात्स्युर्मुक्तिबन्धदाः॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vāmeśādyāḥ parijñānājñānātsyurmuktibandhadāḥ||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति। एवं च निजशक्तिव्यामोहिततैव संसारित्वम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti| Evaṁ ca nijaśaktivyāmohitataiva saṁsāritvam|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">अपि च चिदात्मनः परमेश्वरस्य स्वा अनपायिन्येकैव स्फुरत्तासारकर्तृतात्मैश्वर्यशक्तिः। सा यदा स्वरूपं गोपयित्वा पाशवे पदे प्राणापानसमानशक्तिदशाभिर्जाग्रत्स्वप्नसुषुप्तभूमिभिर्देहप्राणपुर्यष्टककलाभिश्च व्यामोहयति तदा तद्व्यामोहितता संसारित्वं यदा तु मध्यधामोल्लासामुदानशक्तिं विश्वव्याप्तिसारां च व्यानशक्तिं तुर्यदशारूपां तुर्यातीतदशारूपां च चिदानन्दघनामुन्मीलयति तदा देहाद्यवस्थायामपि पतिदशात्मा जीवन्मुक्तिर्भवति। एवं त्रिधा स्वशक्तिव्यामोहितता व्याख्याता। चिद्वदिति (९) सूत्रे चित्प्रकाशो गृहीतसङ्कोचः संसारी इत्युक्तमिह तु स्वशक्तिव्यामोहितत्वेनास्य संसारित्वं भवति - इति भङ्ग्यन्तरेणोक्तम्। एवं सङ्कुचितशक्तिः प्राणादिमानपि यदा स्वशक्तिव्यामोहितो न भवति तदायम्</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Api ca cidātmanaḥ parameśvarasya svā anapāyinyekaiva sphurattāsārakartṛtātmaiśvaryaśaktiḥ| Sā yadā svarūpaṁ gopayitvā pāśave pade prāṇāpānasamānaśaktidaśābhirjāgratsvapnasuṣuptabhūmibhirdehaprāṇapuryaṣṭakakalābhiśca vyāmohayati tadā tadvyāmohitatā saṁsāritvaṁ yadā tu madhyadhāmollāsāmudānaśaktiṁ viśvavyāptisārāṁ ca vyānaśaktiṁ turyadaśārūpāṁ turyātītadaśārūpāṁ ca cidānandaghanāmunmīlayati tadā dehādyavasthāyāmapi patidaśātmā jīvanmuktirbhavati| Evaṁ tridhā svaśaktivyāmohitatā vyākhyātā| Cidvaditi (9) sūtre citprakāśo gṛhītasaṅkocaḥ saṁsārī ityuktamiha tu svaśaktivyāmohitatvenāsya saṁsāritvaṁ bhavati - Iti bhaṅgyantareṇoktam| Evaṁ saṅkucitaśaktiḥ prāṇādimānapi yadā svaśaktivyāmohito na bhavati tadāyam</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इत्याम्नायस्थित्या शिवभट्टारक एव - इति भङ्ग्या निरूपितं भवति। यदागमः</span><br />

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">This (idam eva) <ruby>which<rp> (</rp><rt>yad</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(is)_ <ruby>the condition of a transmigratory soul<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁsāritvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(means)_ <ruby>being bewildered or infatuated<rp> (</rp><rt>vyāmohitatvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by one's own powers<rp> (</rp><rt>svābhiḥ śaktibhiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —_(that is,)_ being <ruby>nailed by various worldly or scriptural doubts<rp> (</rp><rt>vividha-laukika-śāstrīya-śaṅkā-śaṅku-kīlitatvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>— <ruby>when there is total ignorance<rp> (</rp><rt>aparijñāne</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —_(i.e.,)_ <ruby>when there is no revelation<rp> (</rp><rt>aprakāśane</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> because of the absence of the unfoldment <ruby>of one's own Force caused by a descent of Power<rp> (</rp><rt>śaktipāta-hetuka-sva-bala-unmīlana-abhāvāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>— <ruby>about that very same thing which occurs always<rp> (</rp><rt>tasya etasya sadā sambhavataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —_(in short,)_ about <ruby>the authorship of the fivefold function/act<rp> (</rp><rt>pañcavidha-kṛtya-kāritvasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>—. <ruby>That<rp> (</rp><rt>tad</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>has been stated<rp> (</rp><rt>uktam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the most venerable Sarvavīra<rp> (</rp><rt>śrī-sarvavīra-bhaṭṭārake</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>:</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«The people<rp> (</rp><rt>lokaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>doubt<rp> (</rp><rt>śaṅkate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>due to ignorance<rp> (</rp><rt>ajñānāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>therefore<rp> (</rp><rt>tatas</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(there is)_ <ruby>manifestation<rp> (</rp><rt>sṛṣṭiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> withdrawal _(of the universe)_ <span class="pv-w">(saṁhṛtiḥ... iti)</span>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>Also<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>:</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«All<rp> (</rp><rt>sarve</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Mantras<rp> (</rp><rt>mantrāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>consist of letters<rp> (</rp><rt>varṇa-ātmakāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(and)_ <ruby>all<rp> (</rp><rt>sarve</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>letters<rp> (</rp><rt>varṇāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>consist of Śiva<rp> (</rp><rt>śiva-ātmakāḥ... iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>Thus<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā hi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>This very<rp> (</rp><rt>iyam... sā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> power of Parāvāk <span class="pv-note">— of the <p class="pv-en" lang="en">«For him who is the universe <span class="pv-note">— i.e. for the ex-limited individual who abandoned individuality and attained universality —</span> <span class="pv-w">(viśva-ātmanaḥ)</span> _(and)_ <ruby>is aware/knows<rp> (</rp><rt>parijānataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in this way<rp> (</rp><rt>evam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>"All<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>ayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Glory<rp> (</rp><rt>vibhavaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(is)_ mine (mama... iti)<ruby>", even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the spreading out<rp> (</rp><rt>prasare</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of vikalpa-s or thoughts<rp> (</rp><rt>vikalpānām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(there is)_ <ruby>the State of the Great Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>mahā-īśatā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>Hence<rp> (</rp><rt>tatas</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>according to the mentioned precept<rp> (</rp><rt>ukta-nītyā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the state of transmigratory soul<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁsāritvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> is the condition of being <ruby>bewildered or infatuated by one's own powers<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-śakti-vyāmohitatā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>Moreover<rp> (</rp><rt>kiñca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the Lady Citiśakti Herself<rp> (</rp><rt>citi-śaktiḥ eva bhagavatī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is called Vāmeśvarī<rp> (</rp><rt>vāma-īśvarī-ākhyā satī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>because She emits the universe<rp> (</rp><rt>viśva-vamanāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> because <ruby>She relates to the opposite course of Saṁsāra<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁsāra-vāma-ācāratvāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>She appears sparklingly<rp> (</rp><rt>parisphurantī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> as all the dispositions of limited knower/perceiver, internal _(psychic)_ organ, external organ, and objective entities, whose natures are khecarī, gocarī, dikcarī and bhūcarī, _(respectively)_ <span class="pv-w">(khecarī-gocarī-dikcarī-bhūcarī-rūpaiḥ aśeṣaiḥ pramātṛ-antaḥkaraṇa-bahiṣkaraṇa-bhāva-svabhāvaiḥ)</span>. <p class="pv-en" lang="en">Thus (evam ca), <ruby>to be a transmigratory soul<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁsāritvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(implies)_ being <ruby>confounded or infatuated by one's own powers<rp> (</rp><rt>nija-śakti-vyāmohitatā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>.</p>

(</rp><rt>turya-atīta-daśā-rūpām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>—, which <span class="pv-note">— both powers —</span> <ruby>form a compact mass of Consciousness and Bliss<rp> (</rp><rt>cit-ānanda-ghanām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>then<rp> (</rp><rt>tadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the state of body, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>deha-ādi-avasthāyām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>there exists<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Liberation while living<rp> (</rp><rt>jīvat-muktiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> whose essence is the State of Pati or Lord <span class="pv-note">— the State of Śiva —</span> <span class="pv-w">(pati-daśā-ātmā)</span>. <ruby>Thus<rp> (</rp><rt>evam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, the condition of being <ruby>bewildered or infatuated by one's own powers<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-śakti-vyāmohitatā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>has been explained<rp> (</rp><rt>vyākhyātā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in three ways<rp> (</rp><rt>tridhā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>In aphorism 9<rp> (</rp><rt>9 sūtre</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(which begins with)_ «Cidvat» (cit-vat iti), <ruby>it has been said<rp> (</rp><rt>uktam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the Light of Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>cit-prakāśaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>having assumed contraction<rp> (</rp><rt>gṛhīta-saṅkocaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(is)_ <ruby>the transmigratory soul<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁsārī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>But<rp> (</rp><rt>tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>here<rp> (</rp><rt>iha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>it has been expressed<rp> (</rp><rt>uktam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in another manner<rp> (</rp><rt>bhaṅgī-antareṇa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>his<rp> (</rp><rt>asya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>state as a transmigratory soul<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁsāritvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>occurs<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> because it becomes <ruby>bewildered or infatuated by his own powers<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-śakti-vyāmohitatvena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>In this (</rp><rt>nirūpitam bhavati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>indirectly<rp> (</rp><rt>bhaṅgyā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(as)_ <ruby>«the adorable Śiva Himself<rp> (</rp><rt>śiva-bhaṭṭārakaḥ eva... iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>» <ruby>according to the axiom of the written tradition<rp> (</rp><rt>āmnāya-sthityā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(which says:)_</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«The Supreme Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>parama-īśvaraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>endowed with a body<rp> (</rp><rt>śarīrī... iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">Just as the revealed scripture _(expresses)_ <span class="pv-w">(yad āgamaḥ)</span>:</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«After assuming<rp> (</rp><rt>āsthāya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>a human body<rp> (</rp><rt>manuṣya-deham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>they<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>are the Supreme Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>parama-īśvarāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>hidden/secret<rp> (</rp><rt>channāḥ... iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">«They also succeed (te api sidhyanti) <ruby>who<rp> (</rp><rt>ye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>perceive<rp> (</rp><rt>paśyanti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the body<rp> (</rp><rt>śarīram eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>or<rp> (</rp><rt>vā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even a pot, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>ghaṭa-ādi api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —all of which consists <ruby>of the thirty-six tattva-s or principles<rp> (</rp><rt>ṣaṭtriṁśat-tattva-mayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>— as having the form of Śiva <span class="pv-note">— or simply, as being Śiva —</span> <span class="pv-w">(śiva-rūpatayā... iti)</span>»</p>
