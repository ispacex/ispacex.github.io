<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">अथ कथं चिदानन्दलाभो भवति - इत्याह</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Atha kathaṁ cidānandalābho bhavati - Ityāha</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मध्यविकासाच्चिदानन्दलाभः॥१७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Madhyavikāsāccidānandalābhaḥ||17||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">सर्वान्तरतमत्वेन वर्तमानत्वात्तद्भित्तिलग्नतां विना च कस्यचिदपि स्वरूपानुपपत्तेः संविदेव भगवती मध्यम्। सा तु मायादशायां तथाभूतापि स्वरूपं गूहयित्वा</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvāntaratamatvena vartamānatvāttadbhittilagnatāṁ vinā ca kasyacidapi svarūpānupapatteḥ saṁvideva bhagavatī madhyam| Sā tu māyādaśāyāṁ tathābhūtāpi svarūpaṁ gūhayitvā</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">प्राक्संवित्प्राणे परिणता</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prāksaṁvitprāṇe pariṇatā</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति नीत्या प्राणशक्तिभूमिं स्वीकृत्यावरोहक्रमेण बुद्धिदेहादिभुवमधिशयाना नाडीसहस्रसरणिमनुसृता। तत्रापि च पलाशपर्णमध्यशाखान्यायेन आ ब्रह्मरन्ध्रादधोवक्त्रपर्यन्तं प्राणशक्तिब्रह्माश्रयमध्यमनाडीरूपतया प्राधान्येन स्थिता तत एव सर्ववृत्तीनामुदयात्तत्रैव च विश्रामात्। एवम्भूताप्येषा पशूनां निमीलितस्वरूपैव स्थिता। यदा तूक्तयुक्तिक्रमेण सर्वान्तरतमत्वे मध्यभूता संविद्भगवती विकसति यदि वा वक्ष्यमाणक्रमेण मध्यभूता ब्रह्मनाडी विकसति तदा तद्विकासाच्चिदानन्दस्योक्तरूपस्य लाभः - प्राप्तिर्भवति। ततश्च प्रागुक्ता जीवन्मुक्तिः॥१७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti nītyā prāṇaśaktibhūmiṁ svīkṛtyāvarohakrameṇa buddhidehādibhuvamadhiśayānā nāḍīsahasrasaraṇimanusṛtā| Tatrāpi ca palāśaparṇamadhyaśākhānyāyena ā brahmarandhrādadhovaktraparyantaṁ prāṇaśaktibrahmāśrayamadhyamanāḍīrūpatayā prādhānyena sthitā tata eva sarvavṛttīnāmudayāttatraiva ca viśrāmāt| Evambhūtāpyeṣā paśūnāṁ nimīlitasvarūpaiva sthitā| Yadā tūktayuktikrameṇa sarvāntaratamatve madhyabhūtā saṁvidbhagavatī vikasati yadi vā vakṣyamāṇakrameṇa madhyabhūtā brahmanāḍī vikasati tadā tadvikāsāccidānandasyoktarūpasya lābhaḥ - prāptirbhavati| Tataśca prāguktā jīvanmuktiḥ||17||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>Now<rp> (</rp><rt>atha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>how<rp> (</rp><rt>katham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> does the acquisition <ruby>of the Bliss of Absolute Consciousness occur<rp> (</rp><rt>cit-ānanda-lābhaḥ bhavati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. _(The author)_ <ruby>said<rp> (</rp><rt>āha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>so<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>:</p>

<p class="pv-tr pv-anchor" id="s1-ru" markdown="1"><ruby>Через раскрытие или развитие<rp> (</rp><rt>vikāsāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> среднего _(состояния)_ <span class="pv-w">(madhya)</span> _(происходит)_ <ruby>обретение<rp> (</rp><rt>lābhaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Блаженства<rp> (</rp><rt>ānanda</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Абсолютного Сознания<rp> (</rp><rt>cit</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||17||</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>The Lady<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavatī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> Saṁvid Herself <span class="pv-note">— the Pure Consciousness also called Paramaśiva or Parabhairava —</span> <span class="pv-w">(saṁvid eva)</span> _(is)_ <ruby>the center<rp> (</rp><rt>madhyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. _(Why?)_ <ruby>Because it exists<rp> (</rp><rt>vartamānatvāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>as the innermost Reality in everything<rp> (</rp><rt>sarva-antaratamatvena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>because the nature<rp> (</rp><rt>svarūpa-anupapatteḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> of "something" (kasyacid api) is not possible <span class="pv-note">— lit. is not applicable —</span> unless it adheres to that Screen <span class="pv-note">— lit. Wall; Saṁvid is the Screen on which the entire universe is reflected —</span> <span class="pv-w">(tad-bhitti-lagnatām vinā)</span>. <ruby>But<rp> (</rp><rt>tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>although She is thus<rp> (</rp><rt>sā... tathābhūtā api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>after concealing<rp> (</rp><rt>gūhayitvā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(Her)_ <ruby>essential nature<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-rūpam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the state of Māyā<rp> (</rp><rt>māyā-daśāyām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>according to the precept<rp> (</rp><rt>nītyā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>:</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«In the beginning<rp> (</rp><rt>prāk</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>Saṁvid<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁvid</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>transformed<rp> (</rp><rt>pariṇatā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> into the vital energy <span class="pv-note">— it should have been written "prāṇena", in the instrumental case —</span> <span class="pv-w">(prāṇe)</span>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>After accepting<rp> (</rp><rt>svīkṛtya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the role of the prāṇaśakti<rp> (</rp><rt>prāṇa-śakti-bhūmim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>She rests<rp> (</rp><rt>adhiśayānā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the states of intellect, body, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>buddhi-deha-ādi-bhuvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in descending order<rp> (</rp><rt>avaroha-krameṇa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(and)_ follows <span class="pv-note">— lit. has followed —</span> <span class="pv-w">(anusṛtā)</span> <ruby>the path of the thousand subtle channels<rp> (</rp><rt>nāḍī-sahasra-saraṇim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>And<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> there <span class="pv-note">— at the level of the sakala who dwells in a body —</span> <span class="pv-w">(tatra)</span>, <ruby>She remains<rp> (</rp><rt>sthitā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>predominantly<rp> (</rp><rt>prādhānyena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> in the form of the central subtle channel <span class="pv-note">— the Suṣumṇā —</span> <ruby>whose seat is Brahma as prāṇaśakti<rp> (</rp><rt>prāṇa-śakti-brahma-āśraya-madhyama-nāḍī-rūpatayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, from brahmarandhra <span class="pv-note">— at the crown —</span> <span class="pv-w">(ā brahma-randhrāt)</span> to the adhovaktra <span class="pv-note">— located below the cakra called Mūlādhāra —</span> <span class="pv-w">(adhas-vaktra-paryantam)</span> as the central midrib in the leaf of a palāśa tree <span class="pv-note">— butea frondosa —</span> <span class="pv-w">(palāśa-parṇa-madhya-śākhā-nyāyena)</span>. _(She is the central channel called Suṣumṇā,)_ <ruby>since all activities arise<rp> (</rp><rt>sarva-vṛttīnām udayāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> from That <span class="pv-note">— from the Suṣumṇā —</span> (tatas eva) <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>rest<rp> (</rp><rt>viśrāmāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> right there <span class="pv-note">— in the Suṣumṇā —</span> (tatra eva). <ruby>Although<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>She<rp> (</rp><rt>eṣā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is thus<rp> (</rp><rt>evambhūtā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>She remains<rp> (</rp><rt>sthitā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with Her essential nature closed<rp> (</rp><rt>nimīlita-svarūpā eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to the paśu-s <span class="pv-note">— to the limited beings who have not yet awakened —</span> <span class="pv-w">(paśūnām)</span>. <ruby>But<rp> (</rp><rt>tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>when<rp> (</rp><rt>yadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the Lady<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavatī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Saṁvid<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁvid</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who is the center<rp> (</rp><rt>madhya-bhūtā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(that appears)_ <ruby>as the innermost Reality in everything<rp> (</rp><rt>sarva-antaratamatve</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>unfolds<rp> (</rp><rt>vikasati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> through the process concerning the aforementioned means <span class="pv-note">— it refers to the authorship of the fivefold function/act —</span> <span class="pv-w">(ukta-yukti-krameṇa)</span> —<ruby>or<rp> (</rp><rt>vā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>if<rp> (</rp><rt>yadi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> the brahmanāḍī <span class="pv-note">— Suṣumṇā consists of three concentric channels called tāmasasuṣumnā, vajriṇī and brahmanāḍī (the latter being the innermost) —</span> <span class="pv-w">(brahmanāḍī)</span>, <ruby>which is the center<rp> (</rp><rt>madhya-bhūtā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>unfolds<rp> (</rp><rt>vikasati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> through <ruby>the process that will be discussed later<rp> (</rp><rt>vakṣyamāṇa-krameṇa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>—, <ruby>then<rp> (</rp><rt>tadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>due to the unfolding of that<rp> (</rp><rt>tad-vikāsāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(there occurs)_ <ruby>the acquisition<rp> (</rp><rt>lābhaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> of <ruby>the aforesaid Bliss of Absolute Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>cit-ānandasya ukta-rūpasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —_(that is,)_ <ruby>there exists<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(such)_ <ruby>attainment<rp> (</rp><rt>prāptiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>—. <ruby>And<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>after that<rp> (</rp><rt>tatas</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(ensues)_ <ruby>Liberation while living<rp> (</rp><rt>jīvat-muktiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which has been previously mentioned<rp> (</rp><rt>prāk-uktā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||17||</p>
