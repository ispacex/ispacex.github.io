<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">मध्यविकासे युक्तिमाह</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Madhyavikāse yuktimāha</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विकल्पक्षयशक्तिसङ्कोचविकासवाहच्छेदाद्यन्तकोटिनिभालनादय इहोपायाः॥१८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vikalpakṣayaśaktisaṅkocavikāsavāhacchedādyantakoṭinibhālanādaya ihopāyāḥ||18||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इह मध्यशक्तिविकासे विकल्पक्षयादय उपायाः। प्रागुपदिष्टपञ्चविधकृत्यकारित्वाद्यनुसरणेन सर्वमध्यभूतायाः संविदो विकासो जायते - इत्यभिहितप्रायम्। उपायान्तरमपि तूच्यते - प्राणायाममुद्राबन्धादिसमस्तयन्त्रणातन्त्रत्रोटनेन सुखोपायमेव हृदये निहितचित्त उक्तयुक्त्या स्वस्थितिप्रतिबन्धकं विकल्पमकिञ्चिच्चिन्तकत्वेन प्रशमयन्नविकल्पपरामर्शेन देहाद्यकलुषस्वचित्प्रमातृतानिभालनप्रवणोऽचिरादेवोन्मिषद्विकासां तुर्यतुर्यातीतसमावेशदशामासादयति। यथोक्तम्</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Iha madhyaśaktivikāse vikalpakṣayādaya upāyāḥ| Prāgupadiṣṭapañcavidhakṛtyakāritvādyanusaraṇena sarvamadhyabhūtāyāḥ saṁvido vikāso jāyate - ityabhihitaprāyam| Upāyāntaramapi tūcyate - prāṇāyāmamudrābandhādisamastayantraṇātantratroṭanena sukhopāyameva hṛdaye nihitacitta uktayuktyā svasthitipratibandhakaṁ vikalpamakiñciccintakatvena praśamayannavikalpaparāmarśena dehādyakaluṣasvacitpramātṛtānibhālanapravaṇo'cirādevonmiṣadvikāsāṁ turyaturyātītasamāveśadaśāmāsādayati| Yathoktam</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">विकल्पहानेनैकाग्र्यात्क्रमेणेश्वरतापदम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vikalpahānenaikāgryātkrameṇeśvaratāpadam|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति श्रीप्रत्यभिज्ञायाम्। श्रीस्पन्देऽपि</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti śrīpratyabhijñāyām| Śrīspande'pi</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">यदा क्षोभः प्रलीयेत तदा स्यात्परमं पदम्॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yadā kṣobhaḥ pralīyeta tadā syātparamaṁ padam||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति। श्रीज्ञानगर्भेऽपि</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti| Śrījñānagarbhe'pi</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">विहाय सकलाः क्रिया जननि मानसीः सर्वतो विमुक्तकरणक्रियानुसृतिपारतन्त्र्योज्ज्वलम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vihāya sakalāḥ kriyā janani mānasīḥ sarvato vimuktakaraṇakriyānusṛtipāratantryojjvalam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">स्थितैस्त्वदनुभावतः सपदि वेद्यते सा परा दशा नृभिरतन्द्रितासमसुखामृतस्यन्दिनी॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sthitaistvadanubhāvataḥ sapadi vedyate sā parā daśā nṛbhiratandritāsamasukhāmṛtasyandinī||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति। अयं चोपायो मूर्धन्यत्वात्प्रत्यभिज्ञायां प्रतिपादितत्वादादावुक्तः। शक्तिसङ्कोचादयस्तु यद्यपि प्रत्यभिज्ञायां न प्रतिपादितास्तथाप्याम्नायिकत्वादस्माभिः प्रसङ्गात्प्रदर्श्यन्ते बहुषु हि प्रदर्शितेषु कश्चित्केनचित्प्रवेक्ष्यतीति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti| Ayaṁ copāyo mūrdhanyatvātpratyabhijñāyāṁ pratipāditatvādādāvuktaḥ| Śaktisaṅkocādayastu yadyapi pratyabhijñāyāṁ na pratipāditāstathāpyāmnāyikatvādasmābhiḥ prasaṅgātpradarśyante bahuṣu hi pradarśiteṣu kaścitkenacitpravekṣyatīti|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">शक्तेः सङ्कोच - इन्द्रियद्वारेण प्रसरन्त्या एवाकुञ्चनक्रमेणोन्मुखीकरणम्। यथोक्तमाथर्वणिकोपनिषत्सु कठवल्ल्यां चतुर्थवल्लिप्रथममन्त्रे</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śakteḥ saṅkoca - indriyadvāreṇa prasarantyā evākuñcanakrameṇonmukhīkaraṇam| Yathoktamātharvaṇikopaniṣatsu kaṭhavallyāṁ caturthavalliprathamamantre</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">पराञ्चि खानि व्यतृणत्स्वयम्भूस्तस्मात्पराङ्पश्यति नान्तरात्मन्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Parāñci khāni vyatṛṇatsvayambhūstasmātparāṅpaśyati nāntarātman|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">कश्चिद्धीरः प्रत्यगात्मानमैक्षदावृत्तचक्षुरमृतत्वमश्नन्॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kaściddhīraḥ pratyagātmānamaikṣadāvṛttacakṣuramṛtatvamaśnan||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति। प्रसृताया अपि वा कूर्माङ्गसङ्कोचवत्त्राससमये हृत्प्रवेशवच्च सर्वतो निवर्तनम्। यथोक्तम्</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti| Prasṛtāyā api vā kūrmāṅgasaṅkocavattrāsasamaye hṛtpraveśavacca sarvato nivartanam| Yathoktam</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">तदपोद्धृते नित्योदितस्थितिः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadapoddhṛte nityoditasthitiḥ|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">शक्तेर्विकासोऽन्तर्निगूढाया अक्रममेव सकलकरणचक्रविस्फारणेन</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śaktervikāso'ntarnigūḍhāyā akramameva sakalakaraṇacakravisphāraṇena</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">अन्तर्लक्ष्यो बहिर्दृष्टिर्निमेषोन्मेषवर्जितः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Antarlakṣyo bahirdṛṣṭirnimeṣonmeṣavarjitaḥ|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति। भैरवीयमुद्रानुप्रवेशयुक्त्या बहिः प्रसरणम्। यथोक्तं कक्ष्यास्तोत्रे</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti| Bhairavīyamudrānupraveśayuktyā bahiḥ prasaraṇam| Yathoktaṁ kakṣyāstotre</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">सर्वाः शक्तीश्चेतसा दर्शनाद्याः स्वे स्वे वेद्ये यौगपद्येन विष्वक्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvāḥ śaktīścetasā darśanādyāḥ sve sve vedye yaugapadyena viṣvak|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">क्षिप्त्वा मध्ये हाटकस्तम्भभूतस्तिष्ठन्विश्वाधार एकोऽवभासि॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kṣiptvā madhye hāṭakastambhabhūtastiṣṭhanviśvādhāra eko'vabhāsi||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति। श्रीभट्टकल्लटेनाप्युक्तम्</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti| Śrībhaṭṭakallaṭenāpyuktam</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">रूपादिषु परिणामात्तत्सिद्धिः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Rūpādiṣu pariṇāmāttatsiddhiḥ|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति। शक्तेश्च सङ्कोचविकासौ नासापुटस्पन्दनक्रमोन्मिषत्सूक्ष्मप्राणशक्त्या भ्रूभेदनेन क्रमासादितोर्ध्वकुण्डलिनीपदे प्रसरविश्रान्तिदशापरिशीलनमधःकुण्डलिन्यां च षष्ठवक्त्ररूपायां प्रगुणीकृत्य शक्तिं तन्मूलतदग्रतन्मध्यभूमिस्पर्शावेशः। यथोक्तं विज्ञानभट्टारके</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti| Śakteśca saṅkocavikāsau nāsāpuṭaspandanakramonmiṣatsūkṣmaprāṇaśaktyā bhrūbhedanena kramāsāditordhvakuṇḍalinīpade prasaraviśrāntidaśāpariśīlanamadhaḥkuṇḍalinyāṁ ca ṣaṣṭhavaktrarūpāyāṁ praguṇīkṛtya śaktiṁ tanmūlatadagratanmadhyabhūmisparśāveśaḥ| Yathoktaṁ vijñānabhaṭṭārake</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">वह्नेर्विषस्य मध्ये तु चित्तं सुखमयं क्षिपेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vahnerviṣasya madhye tu cittaṁ sukhamayaṁ kṣipet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">केवलं वायुपूर्णं वा स्मरानन्देन युज्यते॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kevalaṁ vāyupūrṇaṁ vā smarānandena yujyate||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति। अत्र वह्निरनुप्रवेशक्रमेण सङ्कोचभूर्विषस्थानं प्रसरयुक्त्या विकासपदं विषॢ व्याप्तावित्यर्थानुगमात्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti| Atra vahniranupraveśakrameṇa saṅkocabhūrviṣasthānaṁ prasarayuktyā vikāsapadaṁ viṣḷ vyāptāvityarthānugamāt|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">वाहयोः - वामदक्षिणगतयोः प्राणापानयोश्छेदो - हृदयविश्रान्तिपुरःसरमन्तः ककारहकारादिप्रायानच्कवर्णोच्चारेण विच्छेदनम्। यथोक्तं ज्ञानगर्भे</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vāhayoḥ - vāmadakṣiṇagatayoḥ prāṇāpānayośchedo - hṛdayaviśrāntipuraḥsaramantaḥ kakārahakārādiprāyānackavarṇoccāreṇa vicchedanam| Yathoktaṁ jñānagarbhe</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">अनच्कककृतायतिप्रसृतपार्श्वनाडीद्वयच्छिदो विधृतचेतसो हृदयपङ्कजस्योदरे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anackakakṛtāyatiprasṛtapārśvanāḍīdvayacchido vidhṛtacetaso hṛdayapaṅkajasyodare|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">उदेति तव दारितान्धतमसः स विद्याङ्कुरो य एष परमेशतां जनयितुं पशोरप्यलम्॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Udeti tava dāritāndhatamasaḥ sa vidyāṅkuro ya eṣa parameśatāṁ janayituṁ paśorapyalam||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">आदिकोटिर्हृदयमन्तकोटिर्द्वादशान्तस्तयोः प्राणोल्लासविश्रान्त्यवसरे निभालनं - चित्तनिवेशनेन परिशीलनम्। यथोक्तं विज्ञानभैरवे</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ādikoṭirhṛdayamantakoṭirdvādaśāntastayoḥ prāṇollāsaviśrāntyavasare nibhālanaṁ - cittaniveśanena pariśīlanam| Yathoktaṁ vijñānabhairave</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">हृद्याकाशे निलीनाक्षः पद्मसम्पुटमध्यगः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Hṛdyākāśe nilīnākṣaḥ padmasampuṭamadhyagaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">अनन्यचेताः सुभगे परं सौभाग्यमाप्नुयात्॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ananyacetāḥ subhage paraṁ saubhāgyamāpnuyāt||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति। तथा</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti| Tathā</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">यथा तथा यत्र तत्र द्वादशान्ते मनः क्षिपेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yathā tathā yatra tatra dvādaśānte manaḥ kṣipet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">प्रतिक्षणं क्षीणवृत्तेर्वैलक्षण्यं दिनैर्भवेत्॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pratikṣaṇaṁ kṣīṇavṛttervailakṣaṇyaṁ dinairbhavet||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति। आदिपदादुन्मेषदशानिषेवणम्। यथोक्तम्</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti| Ādipadādunmeṣadaśāniṣevaṇam| Yathoktam</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">उन्मेषः स तु विज्ञेयः स्वयं तमुपलक्षयेत्॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Unmeṣaḥ sa tu vijñeyaḥ svayaṁ tamupalakṣayet||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति स्पन्दे। तथा रमणीयविषयचर्वणादयश्च सङ्गृहीताः। यथोक्तं श्रीविज्ञानभैरव एव</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti spande| Tathā ramaṇīyaviṣayacarvaṇādayaśca saṅgṛhītāḥ| Yathoktaṁ śrīvijñānabhairava eva</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">जग्धिपानकृतोल्लासरसानन्दविजृम्भणात्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jagdhipānakṛtollāsarasānandavijṛmbhaṇāt|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">भावयेद्भरितावस्थां महानन्दमयो भवेत्॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhāvayedbharitāvasthāṁ mahānandamayo bhavet||</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">गीतादिविषयास्वादासमसौख्यैकतात्मनः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Gītādiviṣayāsvādāsamasaukhyaikatātmanaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">योगिनस्तन्मयत्वेन मनोरूढेस्तदात्मता॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yoginastanmayatvena manorūḍhestadātmatā||</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">यत्र यत्र मनस्तुष्टिर्मनस्तत्रैव धारयेत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yatra yatra manastuṣṭirmanastatraiva dhārayet|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">तत्र तत्र परानन्दस्वरूपं सम्प्रकाशते॥</span><br />

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">This (ayam ca) <ruby>means<rp> (</rp><rt>upāyaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>has been described<rp> (</rp><rt>uktaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the first place<rp> (</rp><rt>ādau</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>because it is the most elevated<rp> (</rp><rt>mūrdhanyatvāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(and also)_ <ruby>because it has been explained<rp> (</rp><rt>pratipāditatvāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> in the Pratyabhijñā _(school)_ <span class="pv-w">(pratyabhijñāyām)</span>. <ruby>But<rp> (</rp><rt>tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> although (yadi api) <ruby>the saṅkoca of Śakti, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>śakti-saṅkoca-ādayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>have not been explained<rp> (</rp><rt>na pratipāditāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> in the _(school of)_ <ruby>Pratyabhijñā<rp> (</rp><rt>pratyabhijñāyām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>nevertheless<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>we have shown them<rp> (</rp><rt>asmābhiḥ... pradarśyante</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>incidentally<rp> (</rp><rt>prasaṅgāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> since <ruby>they belong to the sacred written tradition<rp> (</rp><rt>āmnāyikatvāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>Bhairavamudrā<rp> (</rp><rt>bhairavīya-mudrā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(is)_ <ruby>an expansion<rp> (</rp><rt>prasaraṇam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>outward<rp> (</rp><rt>bahis</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in conjunction with a penetration<rp> (</rp><rt>anupraveśa-yuktyā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>As<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>has been said<rp> (</rp><rt>uktam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the Kakṣyāstotra<rp> (</rp><rt>kakṣyāstotre</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>:</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>The most venerable Kallaṭa has also said<rp> (</rp><rt>śrī-bhaṭṭa-kallaṭena api uktam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>also<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>:</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">«Perfection with regard to that <span class="pv-note">— with regard to the development of the madhyaśakti —</span> <span class="pv-w">(tad)</span> _(is carried out)_ through a transformation _(of one's own consciousness)_ <span class="pv-w">(pariṇāmāt)</span> _(even)_ <ruby>in the presence of forms, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>rūpa-ādiṣu... iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«He should cast<rp> (</rp><rt>kṣipet</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(his)_ <ruby>mind<rp> (</rp><rt>cittam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>filled with delight<rp> (</rp><rt>sukha-mayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> into the center (madhye tu) of vahni <span class="pv-note">— that is, saṅkoca or contraction —</span> and viṣa <span class="pv-note">— also called vikāsa or expansion —</span> <span class="pv-w">(vahneḥ viṣasya)</span>, _(either)_ alone <span class="pv-note">— that is, his mind alone —</span> <span class="pv-w">(kevalam)</span> <ruby>or<rp> (</rp><rt>vā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>filled with vital air<rp> (</rp><rt>vāyu-pūrṇam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. _(As a consequence,)_ <ruby>he becomes endowed<rp> (</rp><rt>yujyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>with sexual bliss<rp> (</rp><rt>smara-ānandena... iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>Here<rp> (</rp><rt>atra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> vahni <span class="pv-note">— lit. fire —</span> <span class="pv-w">(vahniḥ)</span> _(is)_ <ruby>the stage of saṅkoca<rp> (</rp><rt>saṅkoca-bhūḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> through the process of entry _(of the prāṇaśakti into the sixth mouth mentioned earlier)_ <span class="pv-w">(anupraveśa-krameṇa)</span>. The site of viṣa <span class="pv-note">— lit. poison —</span> <span class="pv-w">(viṣa-sthānam)</span> _(is)_ <ruby>the stage of vikāsa<rp> (</rp><rt>vikāsa-padam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> through the mechanism of propagation <span class="pv-note">— prasara —</span> <span class="pv-w">(prasara-yuktyā)</span> <ruby>following the meaning<rp> (</rp><rt>artha-anugamāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(of the root)_ <ruby>"viṣ - to penetrate"<rp> (</rp><rt>viṣḷ vyāptau</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">_(«Vāhaccheda» in the aphorism means:)_ <ruby>Cessation<rp> (</rp><rt>chedaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —_(or)_ <ruby>interruption<rp> (</rp><rt>vicchedanam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> through the internal pronunciation of «anacka» sounds <span class="pv-note">— without vowels —</span> <ruby>consisting mainly of the sounds ka, ha, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>antar ka-kāra-ha-kāra-ādi-prāya-anacka-varṇa-uccāreṇa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>together with a repose in the heart<rp> (</rp><rt>hṛdaya-viśrānti-puraḥsaram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>— <ruby>of the two flows<rp> (</rp><rt>vāhayoḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —of <ruby>prāṇa and apāna located on the right and left<rp> (</rp><rt>vāma-dakṣiṇa-gatayoḥ prāṇa-apānayoḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>—. <ruby>As<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>it has been said<rp> (</rp><rt>uktam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the Jñānagarbha<rp> (</rp><rt>jñāna-garbhe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>:</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«Within the interior<rp> (</rp><rt>udare</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of the lotus of the heart<rp> (</rp><rt>hṛdaya-paṅkajasya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of one whose mind is under control<rp> (</rp><rt>vidhṛta-cetasaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, who has performed an interruption _(of the flow of vital energy)_ through the two subtle channels extending on _(both)_ sides by means of a restriction <ruby>produced by the vowel-less sound "ka"<rp> (</rp><rt>anacka-ka-kṛta-āyati-prasṛta-pārśva-nāḍī-dvaya-chidaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>there arises<rp> (</rp><rt>udeti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that very sprout of Yours of Knowledge<rp> (</rp><rt>tava... saḥ vidyā-aṅkuraḥ... eṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —<ruby>whose thick darkness has been dispelled<rp> (</rp><rt>dārita-andhatamasaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>— <ruby>which<rp> (</rp><rt>yaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is capable<rp> (</rp><rt>alam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of generating<rp> (</rp><rt>janayitum</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the State of the Supreme Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>parama-īśatām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> for a paśu <span class="pv-note">— for a limited being —</span> <span class="pv-w">(paśoḥ... iti)</span>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>The initial point<rp> (</rp><rt>ādi-koṭiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(is)_ <ruby>the heart<rp> (</rp><rt>hṛdayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the final point<rp> (</rp><rt>anta-koṭiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(is)_ <ruby>the dvādaśānta<rp> (</rp><rt>dvādaśāntaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. Nibhālana <span class="pv-note">— lit. perception —</span> <span class="pv-w">(nibhālanam)</span> _(is)_ <ruby>the practice<rp> (</rp><rt>pariśīlanam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by fixing the mind<rp> (</rp><rt>citta-niveśanena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> at the moment when there is an appearance <ruby>of the vital energy and a repose of it<rp> (</rp><rt>prāṇa-ullāsa-viśrānti-avasare</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> between the two <span class="pv-note">— between the heart and dvādaśānta —</span> <span class="pv-w">(tayoḥ)</span>. <ruby>As<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>it has been said<rp> (</rp><rt>uktam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the Vijñānabhairava<rp> (</rp><rt>vijñāna-bhairave</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>:</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«O beautiful One<rp> (</rp><rt>subhage</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>he whose organs of sense are merged<rp> (</rp><rt>nilīna-akṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>into the ether<rp> (</rp><rt>ākāśe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the heart<rp> (</rp><rt>hṛdi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, who resides in the middle of the hemispheres of _(that)_ lotus <span class="pv-note">— i.e. he resides in Pramātā or Knower, while in the upper hemisphere is Pramāṇa (means of knowledge), <ruby>and in the lower hemisphere is Prameya<rp> (</rp><rt>knowable</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —</span> <span class="pv-w">(padma-sampuṭa-madhyagaḥ)</span>, _(and)_ who is totally one-pointed <span class="pv-note">— viz. his mind is concentrated on nothing which is different from Consciousness or Bhairava —</span> <span class="pv-w">(ananya-cetāḥ)</span>, <ruby>obtains<rp> (</rp><rt>āpnuyāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the Supreme<rp> (</rp><rt>param</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> Fortune/Welfare <span class="pv-note">— i.e. he gets lordship over the universe, Supreme Bliss and charm —</span> <span class="pv-w">(saubhāgyam... iti)</span>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>Likewise<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>:</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«Howsoever<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā tathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(and)_ wheresoever (yatra tatra), <ruby>he should throw<rp> (</rp><rt>kṣipet</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(his)_ <ruby>mind<rp> (</rp><rt>manas</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>into dvādaśānta<rp> (</rp><rt>dvādaśānte</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>at every moment<rp> (</rp><rt>pratikṣaṇam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. For the one whose mental fluctuation has decreased _(in that way)_ <span class="pv-w">(kṣīṇa-vṛtteḥ)</span>, <ruby>there is<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavet</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(the manifestation of)_ the extraordinary nature of Parabhairava <span class="pv-note">— lit. indescribableness —</span> <span class="pv-w">(vailakṣaṇyam)</span> <ruby>in a few days<rp> (</rp><rt>dinaiḥ... iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">From the word «ādi» <span class="pv-note">— lit. etc. —</span> <span class="pv-w">(ādi-padāt)</span> _(is suggested)_ the practice of the state of Unmeṣa <span class="pv-note">— taught by Vasugupta in his Spandakārikā-s —</span> <span class="pv-w">(unmeṣa-daśā-niṣevaṇam)</span>. <ruby>As<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>it has been said<rp> (</rp><rt>uktam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> in the Spandakārikā-s _(3.9)_ <span class="pv-w">(spande)</span>:</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«That<rp> (</rp><rt>saḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is to be certainly known<rp> (</rp><rt>tu vijñeyaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>as Unmeṣa<rp> (</rp><rt>unmeṣaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>... <ruby>One should perceive<rp> (</rp><rt>upalakṣayet</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> that _(Unmeṣa)_ <span class="pv-w">(tam)</span> by himself (svayam... iti)»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>Similarly<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the tasting/enjoyment of pleasant things, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>ramaṇīya-viṣaya-carvaṇa-ādayaḥ ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>have been summarized<rp> (</rp><rt>saṅgṛhītāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>As<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>it has been said<rp> (</rp><rt>uktam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> in the venerable Vijñānabhairava _(72-74)_ <span class="pv-w">(śrī-vijñānabhairave eva)</span>:</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">«From the expansion of the delight of the taste that <ruby>appears from the acts of eating and drinking<rp> (</rp><rt>jagdhi-pāna-kṛta-ullāsa-rasa-ānanda-vijṛmbhaṇāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>he should contemplate<rp> (</rp><rt>bhāvayet</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the full state<rp> (</rp><rt>bharita-avasthā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>After that<rp> (</rp><rt>tatas</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>there occurs<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavet</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>a great delight<rp> (</rp><rt>mahā-ānandaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">«When the yogī becomes one with the incomparable bliss because <ruby>of the enjoyment in the spheres of songs, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>gīta-ādi-viṣaya-āsvāda-asama-saukhya-ekatā-ātmanaḥ... yoginaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, due to identification with it <span class="pv-note">— that is, "with the incomparable bliss" —</span> <span class="pv-w">(tad-mayatvena)</span>, _(there occurs)_ identification with that _(Supreme Brahma)_ <span class="pv-w">(tad-ātmatā)</span> through <ruby>the development or expansion of the mind<rp> (</rp><rt>manas-rūḍheḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">«Wherever (yatra yatra) _(there is)_ <ruby>mental satisfaction<rp> (</rp><rt>manas-tuṣṭiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the mind<rp> (</rp><rt>manas</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>should be fixed<rp> (</rp><rt>dhārayet</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> right there (tatra eva). In those circumstances (tatra tatra), <ruby>the essential nature of the Highest Bliss<rp> (</rp><rt>para-ānanda-svarūpam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>becomes manifest<rp> (</rp><rt>samprakāśate... iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>Thus<rp> (</rp><rt>evam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>other<rp> (</rp><rt>anyat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(practices such as)_ the contemplation <ruby>of one's own Self full of Bliss, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>ānanda-pūrṇa-sva-ātma-bhāvanā-ādikam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> must also be inferred (api... anumantavyam). In this way (iti evam), <ruby>here<rp> (</rp><rt>atra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(the term)_ <ruby>etcetera<rp> (</rp><rt>ādayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(refers to)_ <ruby>means<rp> (</rp><rt>upāyāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>relating to the unfoldment of the center<rp> (</rp><rt>madhya-vikāse</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||18||</p>
