<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">एवं च</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ ca</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तद्भूमिकाः सर्वदर्शनस्थितयः॥८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadbhūmikāḥ sarvadarśanasthitayaḥ||8||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">सर्वेषां चार्वाकादिदर्शनानां स्थितयः - सिद्धान्तास्तस्यैतस्यात्मनो नटस्यैव स्वेच्छावगृहीताः कृत्रिमा भूमिकाः। तथा च</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarveṣāṁ cārvākādidarśanānāṁ sthitayaḥ - siddhāntāstasyaitasyātmano naṭasyaiva svecchāvagṛhītāḥ kṛtrimā bhūmikāḥ| Tathā ca</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">चैतन्यविशिष्टं शरीरमात्मा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Caitanyaviśiṣṭaṁ śarīramātmā|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति चार्वाकाः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti cārvākāḥ|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">नैयायिकादयो ज्ञानादिगुणगणाश्रयं बुद्धितत्त्वप्रायमेवात्मानं संसृतौ मन्यन्तेऽपवर्गे तु तदुच्छेदे शून्यप्रायम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Naiyāyikādayo jñānādiguṇagaṇāśrayaṁ buddhitattvaprāyamevātmānaṁ saṁsṛtau manyante'pavarge tu taducchede śūnyaprāyam|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">अहंप्रतीतिप्रत्येयः सुखदुःखाद्युपाधिभिस्तिरस्कृत आत्मा - इति मन्वाना मीमांसका अपि बुद्धावेव निविष्टाः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ahaṁpratītipratyeyaḥ sukhaduḥkhādyupādhibhistiraskṛta ātmā - Iti manvānā mīmāṁsakā api buddhāveva niviṣṭāḥ|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">ज्ञानसन्तान एव तत्त्वम् - इति सौगता बुद्धिवृत्तिष्वेव पर्यवसिताः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jñānasantāna eva tattvam - Iti saugatā buddhivṛttiṣveva paryavasitāḥ|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">प्राण एवात्मा - इति केचिच्छ्रुत्यन्तविदः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prāṇa evātmā - Iti kecicchrutyantavidaḥ|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">असदेवेदमासीत् - इत्यभावब्रह्मवादिनः शून्यभुवमवगाह्य स्थिताः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Asadevedamāsīt - Ityabhāvabrahmavādinaḥ śūnyabhuvamavagāhya sthitāḥ|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">माध्यमिका अप्येवमेव।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mādhyamikā apyevameva|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">परा प्रकृतिर्भगवान्वासुदेवस्तद्विस्फुलिङ्गप्राया एव जीवाः - इति पाञ्चरात्राः परस्याः प्रकृतेः परिणामाभ्युपगमादव्यक्त एवाभिनिविष्टाः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Parā prakṛtirbhagavānvāsudevastadvisphuliṅgaprāyā eva jīvāḥ - Iti pāñcarātrāḥ parasyāḥ prakṛteḥ pariṇāmābhyupagamādavyakta evābhiniviṣṭāḥ|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">साङ्ख्यादयस्तु विज्ञानाकलप्रायां भूमिमवलम्बन्ते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sāṅkhyādayastu vijñānākalaprāyāṁ bhūmimavalambante|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">सदेवेदमग्र आसीत् - इतीश्वरतत्त्वपदमाश्रिता अपरे श्रुत्यन्तविदः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sadevedamagra āsīt - Itīśvaratattvapadamāśritā apare śrutyantavidaḥ|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">शब्दब्रह्ममयं पश्यन्तीरूपमात्मतत्त्वम् - इति वैयाकरणाः श्रीसदाशिवपदमध्यासिताः। एवमन्यदप्यनुमन्तव्यम्। एतच्चागमेषु</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śabdabrahmamayaṁ paśyantīrūpamātmatattvam - Iti vaiyākaraṇāḥ śrīsadāśivapadamadhyāsitāḥ| Evamanyadapyanumantavyam| Etaccāgameṣu</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">बुद्धितत्त्वे स्थिता बौद्धा गुणेष्वेवार्हताः स्थिताः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Buddhitattve sthitā bauddhā guṇeṣvevārhatāḥ sthitāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">स्थिता वेदविदः पुंस्यव्यक्ते पाञ्चरात्रिकाः॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sthitā vedavidaḥ puṁsyavyakte pāñcarātrikāḥ||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इत्यादिना निरूपितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">ityādinā nirūpitam|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">विश्वोत्तीर्णमात्मतत्त्वम् - इति तान्त्रिकाः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Viśvottīrṇamātmatattvam - Iti tāntrikāḥ|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">विश्वमयम् - इति कुलाद्याम्नायनिविष्टाः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Viśvamayam - Iti kulādyāmnāyaniviṣṭāḥ|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">विश्वोत्तीर्णं विश्वमयं च - इति त्रिकादिदर्शनविदः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Viśvottīrṇaṁ viśvamayaṁ ca - Iti trikādidarśanavidaḥ|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">एवमेकस्यैव चिदात्मनो भगवतः स्वातन्त्र्यावभासिताः सर्वा इमा भूमिकाः स्वातन्त्र्यप्रच्छादनोन्मीलनतारतम्यभेदिता अत एक एवैतावद्व्याप्तिक आत्मा। मितदृष्टयस्त्वंशांशिकासु तदिच्छयैवाभिमानं ग्राहिता येन देहादिषु भूमिषु पूर्वपूर्वप्रमातृव्याप्तिसारताप्रथायामप्युक्तरूपां महाव्याप्तिं परशक्तिपातं विना न लभन्ते। यथोक्तम्</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evamekasyaiva cidātmano bhagavataḥ svātantryāvabhāsitāḥ sarvā imā bhūmikāḥ svātantryapracchādanonmīlanatāratamyabheditā ata eka evaitāvadvyāptika ātmā| Mitadṛṣṭayastvaṁśāṁśikāsu tadicchayaivābhimānaṁ grāhitā yena dehādiṣu bhūmiṣu pūrvapūrvapramātṛvyāptisāratāprathāyāmapyuktarūpāṁ mahāvyāptiṁ paraśaktipātaṁ vinā na labhante| Yathoktam</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">वैष्णवाद्यास्तु ये केचिद्विद्यारागेण रञ्जिताः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vaiṣṇavādyāstu ye kecidvidyārāgeṇa rañjitāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">न विदन्ति परं देवं सर्वज्ञं ज्ञानशालिनम्॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na vidanti paraṁ devaṁ sarvajñaṁ jñānaśālinam||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति। तथा</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti| Tathā</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">भ्रमत्येव तान्माया ह्यमोक्षे मोक्षलिप्सया।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhramatyeva tānmāyā hyamokṣe mokṣalipsayā|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">त आत्मोपासकाः शैवं न गच्छन्ति परं पदम्॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ta ātmopāsakāḥ śaivaṁ na gacchanti paraṁ padam||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति च।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti ca|</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">अपि च सर्वेषां दर्शनानां - समस्तानां नीलसुखादिज्ञानानां याः स्थितयः - अन्तर्मुखरूपा विश्रान्तयस्तास्तद्भूमिकाः - चिदानन्दघनस्वात्मस्वरूपाभिव्यक्त्युपायाः। तथा हि यदा यदा बहिर्मुखं रूपं स्वरूपे विश्राम्यति तदा तदा बाह्यवस्तूपसंहारोऽन्तःप्रशान्तपदावस्थितिस्तत्तदुदेष्यत्संवित्सन्तत्यासूत्रणम् - इति सृष्टिस्थितिसंहारमेलनारूपा इयं तुरीया संविद्भट्टारिका तत्तत्सृष्ट्यादिभेदानुद्वमन्ती संहरन्ती च सदा पूर्णा च कृशा चोभयरूपा चानुभयात्मा चाक्रममेव स्फुरन्ती स्थिता। उक्तं च श्रीप्रत्यभिज्ञाटीकायाम्</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Api ca sarveṣāṁ darśanānāṁ - samastānāṁ nīlasukhādijñānānāṁ yāḥ sthitayaḥ - antarmukharūpā viśrāntayastāstadbhūmikāḥ - cidānandaghanasvātmasvarūpābhivyaktyupāyāḥ| Tathā hi yadā yadā bahirmukhaṁ rūpaṁ svarūpe viśrāmyati tadā tadā bāhyavastūpasaṁhāro'ntaḥpraśāntapadāvasthitistattadudeṣyatsaṁvitsantatyāsūtraṇam - Iti sṛṣṭisthitisaṁhāramelanārūpā iyaṁ turīyā saṁvidbhaṭṭārikā tattatsṛṣṭyādibhedānudvamantī saṁharantī ca sadā pūrṇā ca kṛśā cobhayarūpā cānubhayātmā cākramameva sphurantī sthitā| Uktaṁ ca śrīpratyabhijñāṭīkāyām</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">तावदर्थावलेहेनोत्तिष्ठति पूर्णा च भवति</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tāvadarthāvalehenottiṣṭhati pūrṇā ca bhavati</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति। एषा च भट्टारिका क्रमात्क्रममधिकमनुशील्यमाना स्वात्मसात्करोत्येव भक्तजनम्॥८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti| Eṣā ca bhaṭṭārikā kramātkramamadhikamanuśīlyamānā svātmasātkarotyeva bhaktajanam||8||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>And<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>thus<rp> (</rp><rt>evam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>:</p>

<p class="pv-tr pv-anchor" id="s1-ru" markdown="1"><ruby>Позиции<rp> (</rp><rt>sthitayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>всех<rp> (</rp><rt>sarva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>философских систем<rp> (</rp><rt>darśana</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(являются лишь различными)_ <ruby>ролями<rp> (</rp><rt>bhūmikāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(играемыми)_ этим _(Сознанием, или Самостью)_ <span class="pv-w">(tad)</span>||8||</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>The positions<rp> (</rp><rt>sthitayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> of all <ruby>philosophical systems such as the Cārvāka, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>sarveṣām cārvāka-ādi-darśanānāṁ sthitayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(are)_ <ruby>established doctrines<rp> (</rp><rt>siddhāntāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(or)_ <ruby>fictitious roles<rp> (</rp><rt>bhūmikāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <span class="pv-w">(kṛtrimāḥ)</span> <span class="pv-note">— <ruby>voluntarily assumed<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-icchā-avagṛhītāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —</span> <ruby>of the actor who is that very Self<rp> (</rp><rt>tasya etasya ātmanaḥ naṭasya eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Accordingly<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the followers of the Cārvāka system<rp> (</rp><rt>cārvākāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(postulate)_ <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>:</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«The body<rp> (</rp><rt>śarīram</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>characterized by Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>caitanya-viśiṣṭam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(is)_ <ruby>the Self<rp> (</rp><rt>ātmā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>The followers of the Nyāya system, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>naiyāyika-ādayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>think<rp> (</rp><rt>manyante</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that the Self<rp> (</rp><rt>ātmānam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> in Saṁsāra <span class="pv-note">— in the Transmigration full of ignorance —</span> <span class="pv-w">(saṁsṛtau)</span> _(is)_ <ruby>mostly the principle of intellect<rp> (</rp><rt>buddhi-tattva-prāyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <span class="pv-note">— which is the receptacle of <ruby>the group of qualities such as knowledge, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>jñāna-ādi-guṇa-gaṇa-āśrayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —</span>; <ruby>but that<rp> (</rp><rt>tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>during Liberation<rp> (</rp><rt>apavarge</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, upon the cessation of that <span class="pv-note">— upon the cessation of the intellect —</span> <span class="pv-w">(tad-ucchede)</span>, _(the Self)_ <ruby>is mostly a void<rp> (</rp><rt>śūnya-prāyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">The followers of the Mīmāṁsā <span class="pv-note">— according to my understanding, this would be Pūrvamīmāṁsā —</span> <span class="pv-w">(mīmāṁsakāḥ)</span> <ruby>are also seated<rp> (</rp><rt>api... niviṣṭāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> in the intellect (buddhau eva) <ruby>on their thinking<rp> (</rp><rt>manvānāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the Self<rp> (</rp><rt>ātmā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(is)_ <ruby>what is known/recognized in I-consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>ahaṁ-pratīti-pratyeyaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(but that)_ <ruby>it is hidden/eclipsed<rp> (</rp><rt>tiraskṛtaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> by <ruby>the limiting conditions of pleasure, pain, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>sukha-duḥkha-ādi-upādhibhiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>The Buddhists<rp> (</rp><rt>saugatāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>conclude<rp> (</rp><rt>paryavasitāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the activities of the intellect<rp> (</rp><rt>buddhi-vṛttiṣu eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(believing)_ <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> the _(Supreme)_ <ruby>Principle<rp> (</rp><rt>tattvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is solely a continuous flow of cognitions<rp> (</rp><rt>jñāna-santānaḥ eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>Some<rp> (</rp><rt>kecid</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>knowers of Vedānta<rp> (</rp><rt>śruti-anta-vidaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(establish)_ <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the vital energy itself<rp> (</rp><rt>prāṇaḥ eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(is)_ <ruby>the Self<rp> (</rp><rt>ātmā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">Those who <ruby>follow the doctrine of Brahma as nonexistence<rp> (</rp><rt>abhāva-brahma-vādinaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(based on the Upaniṣad-s' quotation which says)_ <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>«This<rp> (</rp><rt>idam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>was<rp> (</rp><rt>āsīt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> the non-Self <span class="pv-note">— nonexistence —</span> (asat eva)», <ruby>being absorbed<rp> (</rp><rt>avagāhya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the stage of the void<rp> (</rp><rt>śūnya-bhuvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, remain _(there)_ <span class="pv-note">— in that void —</span> <span class="pv-w">(sthitāḥ)</span>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>Even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the followers of Mādhyamika Buddhism<rp> (</rp><rt>mādhyamikā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(think)_ in this way (evam eva).</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">_(Thinking)_ <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the Lord<rp> (</rp><rt>bhagavān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Vāsudeva<rp> (</rp><rt>vāsudevaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(is)_ the Superior Prakṛti <span class="pv-note">— here the term Prakṛti means «Original Source» and not the tattva 13 of Trika Shaivism —</span> <span class="pv-w">(parā prakṛtiḥ)</span>, _(and that)_ <ruby>the individual souls<rp> (</rp><rt>jīvāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>are mostly His sparks<rp> (</rp><rt>tad-visphuliṅga-prāyāḥ eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, the followers <ruby>of the doctrine based on the Pañcarātra-s<rp> (</rp><rt>pāñca-rātrāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, by admitting that _(the individual souls)_ <ruby>are a transformation<rp> (</rp><rt>pariṇāma-abhyupagamāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of the Superior Prakṛti<rp> (</rp><rt>parasyāḥ prakṛteḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>cling<rp> (</rp><rt>abhiniviṣṭāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> to the Unmanifest (avyakte eva).</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>But<rp> (</rp><rt>tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the followers of Sāṅkhya, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>sāṅkhya-ādayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>cling<rp> (</rp><rt>avalambante</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to the stage of mostly the Vijñānākala-s<rp> (</rp><rt>vijñānākala-prāyām bhūmim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">_(Following the teaching of the Upaniṣad-s, concerning)_ <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>«In the beginning<rp> (</rp><rt>agre</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>idam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>was<rp> (</rp><rt>āsīt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> only Existence (sat eva)», <ruby>other<rp> (</rp><rt>apare</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>knowers of Vedānta<rp> (</rp><rt>śruti-anta-vidaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> have taken the <ruby>state of the principle Īśvara as their refuge<rp> (</rp><rt>īśvara-tattva-padam āśritāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">_(Believing)_ <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the Principle of the Self<rp> (</rp><rt>ātma-tattvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>consists of Śabdabrahma<rp> (</rp><rt>śabda-brahma-mayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>whose form is Paśyantī<rp> (</rp><rt>paśyantī-rūpam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the followers of Vyākaraṇa<rp> (</rp><rt>vaiyākaraṇāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>are seated<rp> (</rp><rt>adhyāsitāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the state of the venerable Sadāśiva<rp> (</rp><rt>śrī-sadāśiva-padam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>In this way<rp> (</rp><rt>evam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(some)_ other _(philosophical system)_ <span class="pv-w">(anyat)</span> could be inferred <span class="pv-note">— lit. must be inferred —</span> <span class="pv-w">(anumantavyam)</span>. <ruby>And<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>etad</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> has been _(likewise)_ <ruby>described<rp> (</rp><rt>nirūpitam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the Āgama-s or Revealed Scriptures<rp> (</rp><rt>āgameṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> through _(this and)_ other _(stanzas)_ <span class="pv-w">(iti-ādinā)</span>:</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«The Buddhists<rp> (</rp><rt>bauddhāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>remain<rp> (</rp><rt>sthitāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the principle of the intellect<rp> (</rp><rt>buddhi-tattve</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the Jains<rp> (</rp><rt>ārhatāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>remain<rp> (</rp><rt>sthitāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the guṇa-s<rp> (</rp><rt>guṇeṣu eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the knowers of the Veda-s<rp> (</rp><rt>veda-vidaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>remain<rp> (</rp><rt>sthitāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in Puruṣa<rp> (</rp><rt>puṁsi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(and)_ <ruby>the followers of the Pañcarātra-s<rp> (</rp><rt>pāñcarātrikāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the Unmanifest<rp> (</rp><rt>avyakte</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>The Tāntrikas<rp> (</rp><rt>tāntrikāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(hold)_ <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the Principle of the Self<rp> (</rp><rt>ātma-tattvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>transcends the universe<rp> (</rp><rt>viśva-uttīrṇam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">Those who are established in the written tradition of Kula, etc. <span class="pv-note">— the author refers to the śākta-s or followers of Śakti —</span> <span class="pv-w">(kula-ādi-āmnāya-niviṣṭāḥ)</span> _(postulate)_ <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(the Principle of the Self)_ <ruby>is immanent with regard to the universe<rp> (</rp><rt>viśva-mayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">_(On the other hand,)_ <ruby>the knowers of the Trika philosophy, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>trika-ādi-darśana-vidaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(argue)_ <ruby>that<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(the Principle of the Self)_ is both transcendent <ruby>and immanent with reference to the universe<rp> (</rp><rt>viśva-uttīrṇam viśva-mayam ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>In this way<rp> (</rp><rt>evam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>all<rp> (</rp><rt>sarvāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>these<rp> (</rp><rt>imāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>roles<rp> (</rp><rt>bhūmikāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>manifested by the Absolute Freedom<rp> (</rp><rt>svātantrya-avabhāsitāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of the one Lord who consists of Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>ekasya eva cit-ātmanaḥ bhagavataḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> are divided into a gradation _(produced)_ by the appearance and concealment of _(that)_ Absolute Freedom <span class="pv-w">(svātantrya-pracchādana-unmīlana-tāratamya-bheditāḥ)</span>. <ruby>For this reason<rp> (</rp><rt>atas</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>there is only<rp> (</rp><rt>ekaḥ eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>one Self<rp> (</rp><rt>ātmā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> that pervades to such an extent <span class="pv-note">— that is, «that pervades all those roles» —</span> <span class="pv-w">(etāvat-vyāptikaḥ)</span>. <ruby>But<rp> (</rp><rt>tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>those who have a limited perception<rp> (</rp><rt>mita-dṛṣṭayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>in various parts<rp> (</rp><rt>aṁśāṁśikāsu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>are forced to accept<rp> (</rp><rt>grāhitāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, through the Will of that _(Absolute Freedom)_ <span class="pv-w">(tad-icchayā eva)</span>, <ruby>an erroneous conception<rp> (</rp><rt>abhimānam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>by which<rp> (</rp><rt>yena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>in the stages of body, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>deha-ādiṣu bhūmiṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, although there is an expansion whose essence is <ruby>an entering into each of the previous knowers<rp> (</rp><rt>pūrva-pūrva-pramātṛ-vyāpti-sāratā-prathāyām api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, they do not attain (na labhante) <ruby>the great aforementioned pervasion<rp> (</rp><rt>ukta-rūpām mahā-vyāptim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>without<rp> (</rp><rt>vinā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>a supreme descent of Power<rp> (</rp><rt>para-śakti-pātam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>As it has been said<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā uktam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(somewhere:)_</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«However<rp> (</rp><rt>tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>some<rp> (</rp><rt>kecid</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Vaiṣṇava-s, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>vaiṣṇava-ādyāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>who<rp> (</rp><rt>ye</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>are tinged<rp> (</rp><rt>rañjitāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> by the dye of Vidyā <span class="pv-note">— the tattva 8 according to Trika —</span> <span class="pv-w">(vidyā-rāgeṇa)</span>, do not know (na vidanti) the Supreme <ruby>and Omniscient God who is full of Knowledge<rp> (</rp><rt>param devam sarvajñam jñāna-śālinam... iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>Similarly<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>:</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«Māyā<rp> (</rp><rt>māyā hi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>makes them wander around<rp> (</rp><rt>bhramayati eva tān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in something that is not Liberation<rp> (</rp><rt>amokṣe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> due to _(their)_ <ruby>desire to attain Liberation<rp> (</rp><rt>mokṣa-lipsayā... iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>And also<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>:</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«Those<rp> (</rp><rt>te</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> worshippers of the Self _(as if He were the body, the vital energy, etc.)_ <span class="pv-w">(ātma-upāsakāḥ)</span> do not attain (na gacchanti) <ruby>the Supreme<rp> (</rp><rt>param</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>State<rp> (</rp><rt>padam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of Śiva<rp> (</rp><rt>śaivam... iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>And<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(there exists)_ <ruby>also<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(another way of interpreting this aphorism 8:)_ <ruby>The inward<rp> (</rp><rt>antarmukha-rūpāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>«sthiti-s»<rp> (</rp><rt>yāḥ sthitayaḥ... tāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(or)_ <ruby>reposes<rp> (</rp><rt>viśrāntayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>of all the «darśana-s»<rp> (</rp><rt>sarveṣām darśanānām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, that is, <ruby>of all cognitions such as blue, pleasure, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>samastānām nīla-sukha-ādi-jñānānām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(are)_ «bhūmikā-s» <ruby>for that<rp> (</rp><rt>tad-bhūmikāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —_(are)_ means for the manifestation of the essential nature of one's own Self which <ruby>is a compact mass of Consciousness and Bliss<rp> (</rp><rt>cit-ānanda-ghana-sva-ātma-svarūpa-abhivyakti-upāyāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>—. <ruby>For example<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā hi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>whenever<rp> (</rp><rt>yadā yadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the outward<rp> (</rp><rt>bahirmukham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>form<rp> (</rp><rt>rūpam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>reposes<rp> (</rp><rt>viśrāmyati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the essential nature<rp> (</rp><rt>svarūpe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>at that time<rp> (</rp><rt>tadā tadā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(there occurs)_ <ruby>a withdrawal of the external thing<rp> (</rp><rt>bāhya-vastu-upasaṁhāraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. _(After that withdrawal, there ensues)_ <ruby>the abiding in a peaceful internal state<rp> (</rp><rt>antar-praśānta-pada-avasthitiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. _(Finally,)_ something like various garlands, _(figuratively speaking,)_ <ruby>formed by continuous series of experiences<rp> (</rp><rt>tad-tad... saṁvid-santati-āsūtraṇam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>arises<rp> (</rp><rt>udeṣyat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>In this way<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>iyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>most venerable<rp> (</rp><rt>bhaṭṭārikā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Fourth<rp> (</rp><rt>turīyā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁvid</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, whose nature is to unite manifestation, maintenance, and withdrawal _(of the universe)_ <span class="pv-w">(sṛṣṭi-sthiti-saṁhāra-melanā-rūpā)</span>, <ruby>emits<rp> (</rp><rt>udvamantī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>withdraws<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁharantī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>varieties of various manifestations, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>tad-tad-sṛṣṭi-ādi-bhedān</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(and also)_ <ruby>keeps shining/appearing<rp> (</rp><rt>sphurantī sthitā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> simultaneously (akramam eva) _(as)_ <ruby>always full<rp> (</rp><rt>sadā pūrṇā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> and (ca... ca) <ruby>emaciated<rp> (</rp><rt>kṛśā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(as)_ both things _(at once)_ <span class="pv-w">(ubhaya-rūpā)</span> and (ca... ca) _(as)_ <ruby>neither of these two things<rp> (</rp><rt>an-ubhaya-ātmā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. It has been said (uktam ca) <ruby>in the venerable Pratyabhijñāṭīkā<rp> (</rp><rt>śrī-pratyabhijñā-ṭīkāyām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>:</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>«Certainly<rp> (</rp><rt>tāvad</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, licking the objects <span class="pv-note">— devouring them —</span> <span class="pv-w">(artha-avalehena)</span>, <ruby>She arises<rp> (</rp><rt>uttiṣṭhati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>becomes<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>full<rp> (</rp><rt>pūrṇā... iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>She<rp> (</rp><rt>eṣā ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the Adorable One<rp> (</rp><rt>bhaṭṭārikā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>being served and worshipped<rp> (</rp><rt>anuśīlyamānā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> more _(and more)_ <span class="pv-w">(adhikam)</span>, <ruby>makes the devotee Her own<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-ātma-sāt karoti eva bhakta-janam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>very gradually<rp> (</rp><rt>kramāt kramam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||8||</p>
