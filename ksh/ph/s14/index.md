<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">ननु यदि पारमार्थिकं चिच्छक्तिपदं सकलभेदकवलनस्वभावं तदस्य मायापदेऽपि तथारूपेण भवितव्यं यथा जलदाच्छादितस्यापि भानोर्भावावभासकत्वम् - इत्याशङ्क्याह</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nanu yadi pāramārthikaṁ cicchaktipadaṁ sakalabhedakavalanasvabhāvaṁ tadasya māyāpade'pi tathārūpeṇa bhavitavyaṁ yathā jaladācchāditasyāpi bhānorbhāvāvabhāsakatvam - Ityāśaṅkyāha</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चितिवह्निरवरोहपदे छन्नोऽपि मात्रया मेयेन्धनं प्लुष्यति॥१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Citivahniravarohapade channo'pi mātrayā meyendhanaṁ pluṣyati||14||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">चितिरेव विश्वग्रसनशीलत्वाद्वह्निरसावेवावरोहपदे - मायाप्रमातृतायां छन्नोऽपि - स्वातन्त्र्यादाच्छादितस्वभावोऽपि भूरिभूतिच्छन्नाग्निवन्मात्रया - अंशेन नीलपीतादिप्रमेयेन्धनं प्लुष्यति - स्वात्मसात्करोति। मात्रापदस्येदमाकूतम् - यत्कवलयनपि सार्वात्म्येन न ग्रसतेऽपि त्वंशेन संस्कारात्मनोत्थापयति। ग्रासकत्वं च सर्वप्रमातॄणां स्वानुभवत एव सिद्धम्। यदुक्तं श्रीमदुत्पलदेवपादैर्निजस्तोत्रेषु</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Citireva viśvagrasanaśīlatvādvahnirasāvevāvarohapade - māyāpramātṛtāyāṁ channo'pi - svātantryādācchāditasvabhāvo'pi bhūribhūticchannāgnivanmātrayā - aṁśena nīlapītādiprameyendhanaṁ pluṣyati - svātmasātkaroti| Mātrāpadasyedamākūtam - yatkavalayanapi sārvātmyena na grasate'pi tvaṁśena saṁskārātmanotthāpayati| Grāsakatvaṁ ca sarvapramātṝṇāṁ svānubhavata eva siddham| Yaduktaṁ śrīmadutpaladevapādairnijastotreṣu</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">वर्तन्ते जन्तवोऽशेषा अपि ब्रह्मेन्द्रविष्णवः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vartante jantavo'śeṣā api brahmendraviṣṇavaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">ग्रसमानास्ततो वन्दे देव विश्वं भवन्मयम्॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Grasamānāstato vande deva viśvaṁ bhavanmayam||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति॥१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti||14||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>An objection<rp> (</rp><rt>nanu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: <ruby>If<rp> (</rp><rt>yadi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> the essential/real stage of Cicchakti <span class="pv-note">— lit. of the Power of Consciousness —</span> <span class="pv-w">(pāramārthikaṁ cit-śakti-padam)</span> has for its essential nature <ruby>the act of swallowing all differences/dualities<rp> (</rp><rt>sakala-bheda-kavalana-svabhāvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>then<rp> (</rp><rt>tad</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>It must remain the same<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā-rūpeṇa bhavitavyam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in Its stage of Māyā<rp> (</rp><rt>māyā-pade</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>just as<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(there is)_ <ruby>manifestation of objects<rp> (</rp><rt>bhāva-avabhāsakatvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> by the <ruby>sun, even though it remains covered by clouds<rp> (</rp><rt>jalada-ācchāditasya api bhānoḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — <ruby>Having doubted/objected<rp> (</rp><rt>āśaṅkya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in that way<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(the author)_ <ruby>said<rp> (</rp><rt>āha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>:</p>

<p class="pv-tr pv-anchor" id="s1-ru" markdown="1"><ruby>Огонь<rp> (</rp><rt>vahniḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Высшей Силы<rp> (</rp><rt>citi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>несмотря на то, что<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> скрыт _(Māyā, или Заблуждением)_ <span class="pv-w">(channaḥ)</span> <ruby>на низшей<rp> (</rp><rt>avaroha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>стадии<rp> (</rp><rt>pade</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>частично<rp> (</rp><rt>mātrayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>расходует<rp> (</rp><rt>pluṣyati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>топливо<rp> (</rp><rt>indhanam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>познаваемых<rp> (</rp><rt>meya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||14||</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">Citi Herself <span class="pv-note">— the Supreme Power Herself —</span> <span class="pv-w">(citiḥ eva)</span> _(is)_ <ruby>a fire<rp> (</rp><rt>vahniḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> because Her tendency/disposition <ruby>consists in devouring the universe<rp> (</rp><rt>viśva-grasana-śīlatvāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. Herself <span class="pv-note">— Citi Herself —</span> (asau eva), <ruby>at the stage of descent<rp> (</rp><rt>avaroha-pade</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —in the condition of the Māyāpramātā <span class="pv-note">— of the knower under the sway of Māyā —</span> <span class="pv-w">(māyā-pramātṛtāyām)</span>—, <ruby>although<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>covered or concealed<rp> (</rp><rt>channaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —<ruby>although<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>having Her essential nature covered<rp> (</rp><rt>ācchādita-svabhāvaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>voluntarily<rp> (</rp><rt>svātantryāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>—, <ruby>partially<rp> (</rp><rt>mātrayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —<ruby>only a portion<rp> (</rp><rt>aṁśena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>—, <ruby>like a fire covered by abundant ashes<rp> (</rp><rt>bhūri-bhūti-channa-agni-vat</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>consumes<rp> (</rp><rt>pluṣyati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —makes equal to Herself <span class="pv-note">— that is, assimilates, makes it equal to Citi —</span> <span class="pv-w">(sva-ātma-sāt karoti)</span>— the fuel <ruby>of knowable objects such as blue, yellow, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>nīla-pīta-ādi-prameya-indhanam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>This<rp> (</rp><rt>idam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(is)_ <ruby>the intention<rp> (</rp><rt>ākūtam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> of the word «mātrā» _(present in the aphorism)_ <span class="pv-note">— lit. a small portion —</span> <span class="pv-w">(mātrā-padasya)</span>: <ruby>That<rp> (</rp><rt>yad</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>although<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(the Fire of Citi)_ is swallowing _(the knowable object)_ <span class="pv-w">(kavalayan)</span>, It does not devour (na grasate) <ruby>it totally<rp> (</rp><rt>sārvātmyena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> but rather (api tu) <ruby>partially<rp> (</rp><rt>aṁśena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(since)_ It makes it arise _(again)_ <span class="pv-w">(utthāpayati)</span> by means <ruby>of the saṁskāra-s or accumulated impressions<rp> (</rp><rt>saṁskāra-ātmanā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>And<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the state of being a devourer<rp> (</rp><rt>grāsakatvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the case of all knowers/perceivers<rp> (</rp><rt>sarva-pramātṝṇām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>is proved<rp> (</rp><rt>siddham</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by one's own experience<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-anubhavatas</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. As <ruby>has been said by the most venerable Utpaladeva<rp> (</rp><rt>yad uktam śrīmat-utpaladeva-pādaiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in his own hymns<rp> (</rp><rt>nija-stotreṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —more precisely, in Śivastotrāvalī 20.17—:</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">«_(Since)_ <ruby>all<rp> (</rp><rt>aśeṣāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>creatures<rp> (</rp><rt>jantavaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Brahmā, Indra and Viṣṇu<rp> (</rp><rt>brahma-indra-viṣṇavaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>continue devouring<rp> (</rp><rt>vartante... grasamānāḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>therefore<rp> (</rp><rt>tatas</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>O God<rp> (</rp><rt>deva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>!, <ruby>I pay homage<rp> (</rp><rt>vande</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to the universe<rp> (</rp><rt>viśvam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> that consists of You (bhavat-mayam... iti)»</p>
