<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">मध्यविकासाच्चिदानन्दलाभः स एव च परमयोगिनः समावेशसमापत्त्यादिपर्यायः समाधिस्तस्य नित्योदितत्वे युक्तिमाह</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Madhyavikāsāccidānandalābhaḥ sa eva ca paramayoginaḥ samāveśasamāpattyādiparyāyaḥ samādhistasya nityoditatve yuktimāha</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">समाधिसंस्कारवति व्युत्थाने भूयो भूयश्चिदैक्यामर्शान्नित्योदितसमाधिलाभः॥१९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Samādhisaṁskāravati vyutthāne bhūyo bhūyaścidaikyāmarśānnityoditasamādhilābhaḥ||19||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">आसादितसमावेशो योगिवरो व्युत्थानेऽपि समाधिरससंस्कारेण क्षीव इव सानन्दं घूर्णमानो भावराशिं शरदभ्रलवमिव चिद्गगन एव लीयमानं पश्यन्भूयो भूयोऽन्तर्मुखतामेव समवलम्बमानो निमीलनसमाधिक्रमेण चिदैक्यमेव विमृशन्व्युत्थानाभिमतावसरेऽपि समाध्येकरस एव भवति। यथोक्तं क्रमसूत्रेषु</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Āsāditasamāveśo yogivaro vyutthāne'pi samādhirasasaṁskāreṇa kṣīva iva sānandaṁ ghūrṇamāno bhāvarāśiṁ śaradabhralavamiva cidgagana eva līyamānaṁ paśyanbhūyo bhūyo'ntarmukhatāmeva samavalambamāno nimīlanasamādhikrameṇa cidaikyameva vimṛśanvyutthānābhimatāvasare'pi samādhyekarasa eva bhavati| Yathoktaṁ kramasūtreṣu</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">क्रममुद्रयान्तःस्वरूपया बहिर्मुखः समाविष्टो भवति साधकः। तत्रादौ बाह्यादन्तः प्रवेश आभ्यन्तराद्बाह्यस्वरूपे प्रवेश आवेशवशाज्जायत - इति सबाह्याभ्यन्तरोऽयं मुद्राक्रमः</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kramamudrayāntaḥsvarūpayā bahirmukhaḥ samāviṣṭo bhavati sādhakaḥ| Tatrādau bāhyādantaḥ praveśa ābhyantarādbāhyasvarūpe praveśa āveśavaśājjāyata - iti sabāhyābhyantaro'yaṁ mudrākramaḥ</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इति। अत्रायमर्थः सृष्टिस्थितिसंहृतिसंविच्चक्रात्मकं क्रमं मुद्रयति स्वाधिष्ठितमात्मसात्करोति येयं तुरीया चितिशक्तिस्तया क्रममुद्रयान्तरिति - पूर्णाहन्तास्वरूपया बहिर्मुख - इति विषयेषु व्यापृतोऽपि समाविष्टः - साक्षात्कृतपरशक्तिस्फारः साधकः - परमयोगी भवति। तत्र च बाह्याद्ग्रस्यमानाद्विषयग्रामादन्तः - परस्यां चिति भूमौ ग्रसनक्रमेणैव प्रवेशः - समावेशो भवति। आभ्यन्तराच्चितिशक्तिस्वरूपाच्च साक्षात्कृतादावेशवशात् - समावेशसामर्थ्यादेव बाह्यस्वरूपे - इदन्तानिर्भासे विषयग्रामे वमनयुक्त्या प्रवेशः - चिद्रसाश्यानताप्रथनात्मा समावेशो जायत - इति। सबाह्याभ्यन्तरोऽयं नित्योदितसमावेशात्मा मुदो - हर्षस्य वितरणात्परमानन्दस्वरूपत्वात्पाशद्रावणाद्विश्वस्यान्तस्तुरीयसत्तायां मुद्रणाच्च मुद्रात्मा क्रमोऽपि सृष्ट्यादिक्रमाभासकत्वात्तत्क्रमाभासरूपत्वाच्च क्रम इत्यभिधीयत इति॥१९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">iti| Atrāyamarthaḥ sṛṣṭisthitisaṁhṛtisaṁviccakrātmakaṁ kramaṁ mudrayati svādhiṣṭhitamātmasātkaroti yeyaṁ turīyā citiśaktistayā kramamudrayāntariti - pūrṇāhantāsvarūpayā bahirmukha - iti viṣayeṣu vyāpṛto'pi samāviṣṭaḥ - sākṣātkṛtaparaśaktisphāraḥ sādhakaḥ - paramayogī bhavati| Tatra ca bāhyādgrasyamānādviṣayagrāmādantaḥ - parasyāṁ citi bhūmau grasanakrameṇaiva praveśaḥ - samāveśo bhavati| Ābhyantarāccitiśaktisvarūpācca sākṣātkṛtādāveśavaśāt - samāveśasāmarthyādeva bāhyasvarūpe - idantānirbhāse viṣayagrāme vamanayuktyā praveśaḥ - cidrasāśyānatāprathanātmā samāveśo jāyata - iti| Sabāhyābhyantaro'yaṁ nityoditasamāveśātmā mudo - harṣasya vitaraṇātparamānandasvarūpatvātpāśadrāvaṇādviśvasyāntasturīyasattāyāṁ mudraṇācca mudrātmā kramo'pi sṛṣṭyādikramābhāsakatvāttatkramābhāsarūpatvācca krama ityabhidhīyata iti||19||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>From the unfoldment of the center<rp> (</rp><rt>madhya-vikāsāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(there is)_ acquisition <ruby>of the Bliss of Absolute Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>cit-ānanda-lābhaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. That very same thing <span class="pv-note">— the aforementioned acquisition —</span> <span class="pv-w">(saḥ eva)</span> _(is)_ <ruby>the samādhi or trance<rp> (</rp><rt>samādhiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —<ruby>whose synonyms are samāveśa, samāpatti, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>samāveśa-samāpatti-ādi-paryāyaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>— <ruby>of the Supreme Yogī<rp> (</rp><rt>parama-yoginaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. With reference to its ever-active state <span class="pv-note">— to the permanent state of such samādhi or trance —</span> (tasya nitya-uditatve), _(the author)_ <ruby>said<rp> (</rp><rt>āha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the means<rp> (</rp><rt>yuktim</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>:</p>

<p class="pv-tr pv-anchor" id="s1-ru" markdown="1">В состоянии, наступающем <ruby>после Samādhi, или Совершенной Концентрации<rp> (</rp><rt>vyutthāne</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, которое наполнено saṁskāra-s <span class="pv-note">— saṁskāravat —</span> <span class="pv-w">(saṁskāravati)</span> _(того)_ <ruby>Samādhi<rp> (</rp><rt>samādhi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(происходит)_ <ruby>обретение<rp> (</rp><rt>lābhaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>постоянного<rp> (</rp><rt>nitya-udita</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Samādhi<rp> (</rp><rt>samādhi</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>через размышление<rp> (</rp><rt>āmarśāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>снова и снова<rp> (</rp><rt>bhūyas bhūyas</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>о собственной идентичности<rp> (</rp><rt>aikya</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>с Абсолютным Сознанием<rp> (</rp><rt>cit</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||19||</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>The best of Yogī-s<rp> (</rp><rt>yogi-varaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> who has attained samāveśa <span class="pv-note">— absorption, that is, trance —</span> <span class="pv-w">(āsādita-samāveśaḥ)</span>, _(and who)_ <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> in vyutthāna <span class="pv-note">— in any state other than trance —</span> <span class="pv-w">(vyutthāne)</span> _(is)_ <ruby>as if<rp> (</rp><rt>iva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>intoxicated<rp> (</rp><rt>kṣīvaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> by the saṁskāra <span class="pv-note">— by the impression —</span> <ruby>of the sap and vigor of samādhi or trance<rp> (</rp><rt>samādhi-rasa-saṁskāreṇa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(and)_ <ruby>moves blissfully to and fro<rp> (</rp><rt>sa-ānandam ghūrṇamānaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>sees/perceives<rp> (</rp><rt>paśyan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> the mass of entities <span class="pv-note">— beings and objects —</span> <span class="pv-w">(bhāva-rāśim)</span> —_(which looks)_ <ruby>like<rp> (</rp><rt>iva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>a small portion of an autumnal cloud<rp> (</rp><rt>śarad-abhra-lavam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>— <ruby>dissolving<rp> (</rp><rt>līyamānam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> into the Firmament of Absolute Consciousness (cit-gagane eva). <ruby>Resorting<rp> (</rp><rt>samavalambamānaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>to introversion<rp> (</rp><rt>antarmukhatām eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>again and again<rp> (</rp><rt>bhūyas bhūyas</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(and)_ <ruby>reflecting<rp> (</rp><rt>vimṛśan</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> upon unity with Absolute Consciousness (cit-aikyam eva) <ruby>through the process of trance with closed eyes<rp> (</rp><rt>nimīlana-samādhi-krameṇa</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(such a) Yogī_ <span class="pv-w">(bhavati)</span> <ruby>is one whose only pleasure is samādhi<rp> (</rp><rt>samādhi-eka-rasaḥ eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>on occasion of what is regarded as vyutthāna<rp> (</rp><rt>vyutthāna-abhimata-avasare</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>As<rp> (</rp><rt>yathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>has been said<rp> (</rp><rt>uktam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>in the Kramasūtra-s<rp> (</rp><rt>krama-sūtreṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>:</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">«The sādhaka <span class="pv-note">— the spiritual aspirant —</span> <span class="pv-w">(sādhakaḥ)</span>, _(even though appearing to be)_ <ruby>externalized<rp> (</rp><rt>bahirmukhaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, is absorbed <span class="pv-note">— is in trance —</span> <span class="pv-w">(samāviṣṭaḥ bhavati)</span> by means of the Kramamudrā whose nature is internal <span class="pv-note">— that is, it pertains to introversion —</span> <span class="pv-w">(krama-mudrayā antar-svarūpayā)</span>. <ruby>There<rp> (</rp><rt>tatra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>by the force of absorption<rp> (</rp><rt>āveśa-vaśāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>there arises<rp> (</rp><rt>jāyate</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>at the beginning<rp> (</rp><rt>ādau</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>an entry<rp> (</rp><rt>praveśaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from outside<rp> (</rp><rt>bāhyāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>inward<rp> (</rp><rt>antar</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(and then,)_ <ruby>an entry<rp> (</rp><rt>praveśaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>from inside<rp> (</rp><rt>ābhyantarāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> toward that which has an external nature <span class="pv-note">— outward —</span> <span class="pv-w">(bāhya-svarūpe)</span>. <ruby>Thus<rp> (</rp><rt>iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>ayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Kramamudrā<rp> (</rp><rt>mudrā-kramaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>contains both the external and the internal<rp> (</rp><rt>sa-bāhya-ābhyantaraḥ... iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>»</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>Here<rp> (</rp><rt>atra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>this<rp> (</rp><rt>ayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(is)_ <ruby>the meaning<rp> (</rp><rt>arthaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>: _(The expression)_ «krama-mudrayā» _(which literally means «by means of the Kramamudrā»,)_ <span class="pv-w">(krama-mudrayā)</span> _(is explained thus:)_ <ruby>By means of this fourth Power of Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>iyam turīyā citi-śaktiḥ tayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> which «mudrayati» <span class="pv-note">— 3rd P. singular of the nominal verb «mudraya» —</span> <span class="pv-w">(mudrayati... yā)</span> krama <span class="pv-note">— lit. succession —</span> <span class="pv-w">(kramam)</span>, _(that is, which)_ <ruby>appropriates<rp> (</rp><rt>ātmasāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(the succession)_ which is the group of the consciousnesses of manifestation, maintenance and withdrawal _(of the universe)_ <span class="pv-w">(sṛṣṭi-sthitisaṁhṛti-saṁvid-cakra-ātmakam)</span>, which <span class="pv-note">— such succession —</span> _(already)_ <ruby>is presided over by the Self<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-adhiṣṭhitam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. «Antaḥ_(svarūpayā)_» (antar iti) _(means)_ «by <ruby>Her whose nature is perfect Self-consciousness»<rp> (</rp><rt>pūrṇa-ahantā-svarūpayā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>«Bahirmukhaḥ»<rp> (</rp><rt>bahirmukhaḥ... iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(means that the sādhaka)_ <ruby>is absorbed in trance<rp> (</rp><rt>samāviṣṭaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>even though<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>he is occupied<rp> (</rp><rt>vyāpṛtaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> with _(external)_ <ruby>things<rp> (</rp><rt>viṣayeṣu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. _(And)_ <ruby>the sādhaka or spiritual aspirant<rp> (</rp><rt>sādhakaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> who <ruby>has realized the expansion of the Highest Power<rp> (</rp><rt>sākṣāt-kṛta-para-śakti-sphāraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(under all those circumstances,)_ <ruby>becomes<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>a Supreme Yogī<rp> (</rp><rt>parama-yogī</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>And<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>there<rp> (</rp><rt>tatra</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>devouring the multitude of objects from outside<rp> (</rp><rt>bāhyāt grasyamānāt viṣaya-grāmāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>inward<rp> (</rp><rt>antar</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —<ruby>toward the stage of the Highest Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>parasyām citi bhūmau</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>—, <ruby>there is<rp> (</rp><rt>bhavati</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>absorption in trance<rp> (</rp><rt>samāveśaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(that is, there is)_ <ruby>an entry<rp> (</rp><rt>praveśaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by means of the very process of devouring<rp> (</rp><rt>grasana-krameṇa eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>From inside<rp> (</rp><rt>ābhyantarāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —from having realized the <ruby>essential nature of the Power of Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>citi-śakti-svarūpāt ca sākṣātkṛtāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>— <ruby>«there takes place»<rp> (</rp><rt>jāyate... iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>by the force of āveśa<rp> (</rp><rt>āveśa-vaśāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —<ruby>by the power of absorption<rp> (</rp><rt>samāveśa-sāmarthyāt eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>—, <ruby>an entry<rp> (</rp><rt>praveśaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, by means of the device of vamana <span class="pv-note">— lit. «the act of vomiting», that is, manifesting —</span> <span class="pv-w">(vamana-yuktyā)</span>, <ruby>into that which has an external nature<rp> (</rp><rt>bāhya-svarūpe</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> —into the mass <ruby>of objects that shines as the condition of This<rp> (</rp><rt>idantā-nirbhāse viṣaya-grāme</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>—. _(Such an entry is)_ <ruby>an absorption<rp> (</rp><rt>samāveśaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> whose nature consists of an unfolding of the solidification <ruby>of the sap of Absolute Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>cit-rasa-āśyānatā-prathana-ātmā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>This<rp> (</rp><rt>ayam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(Mudrākrama <span class="pv-note">— Kramamudrā —</span>)_ which <ruby>contains both the external and the internal<rp> (</rp><rt>sa-bāhya-ābhyantaraḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(and)_ <ruby>is the ever-active absorption<rp> (</rp><rt>nitya-udita-samāveśa-ātmā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(is)_ <ruby>mudrā<rp> (</rp><rt>mudrā-ātmā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(for the following three reasons:)_ _**(1)**_ <ruby>Because it bestows mudā or bliss<rp> (</rp><rt>mudaḥ... harṣasya vitaraṇāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>since its nature is Supreme Bliss<rp> (</rp><rt>parama-ānanda-svarūpatvāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; _**(2)**_ <ruby>because it dissolves the bonds<rp> (</rp><rt>pāśa-drāvaṇāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; _**(3)**_ <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>because it seals the universe<rp> (</rp><rt>viśvasya... mudraṇāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> into the being <ruby>of the inner fourth state of consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>antar turīya-sattāyā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. _(And this Mudrākrama is)_ <ruby>also<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>krama<rp> (</rp><rt>kramaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. _(Why?)_ <ruby>It is called<rp> (</rp><rt>abhidhīyate ti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>«krama»<rp> (</rp><rt>kramaḥ iti</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(for these two reasons:)_ _**(1)**_ Because it makes manifestation, etc. <ruby>appear as a succession<rp> (</rp><rt>sṛṣṭi-ādi-krama-ābhāsakatvāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _**(2)**_ because _(the Mudrākrama)_ <ruby>itself is the appearance of that succession<rp> (</rp><rt>tad-krama-ābhāsa-rūpatvāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||19||</p>
