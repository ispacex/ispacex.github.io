<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">अस्यैव सम्यक्स्वरूपज्ञानाद्यतो मुक्तिरसम्यक्तु संसारस्ततस्तिलश एतत्स्वरूपं निर्भङ्क्तुमाह</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Asyaiva samyaksvarūpajñānādyato muktirasamyaktu saṁsārastatastilaśa etatsvarūpaṁ nirbhaṅktumāha</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स चैको द्विरूपस्त्रिमयश्चतुरात्मा सप्तपञ्चकस्वभावः॥७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sa caiko dvirūpastrimayaścaturātmā saptapañcakasvabhāvaḥ||7||</span></p>

<p class="pv-pair" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa" lang="sa">निर्णीतदृशा चिदात्मा शिवभट्टारक एवैक आत्मा न त्वन्यः कश्चित्प्रकाशस्य देशकालादिभिर्भेदायोगाज्जडस्य तु ग्राहकत्वानुपपत्तेः। प्रकाश एव यतः स्वातन्त्र्याद्गृहीतप्राणादिसङ्कोचः सङ्कुचितार्थग्राहकतामश्नुते ततोऽसौ प्रकाशरूपत्वसङ्कोचावभासवत्त्वाभ्यां द्विरूपः। आणवमायीयकार्ममलावृतत्वात्त्रिमयः। शून्यप्राणपुर्यष्टकशरीरस्वभावत्वाच्चतुरात्मा। सप्तपञ्चकानि - शिवादिपृथिव्यन्तानि पञ्चत्रिंशत्तत्त्वानि तत्स्वभावः। तथा शिवादिसकलान्तप्रमातृसप्तकस्वरूपश्चिदानन्देच्छाज्ञानक्रियाशक्तिरूपत्वेऽप्यख्यातिवशात्कलाविद्यारागकालनियतिकञ्चुकवलितत्वात्पञ्चकस्वरूपः। एवं च शिवैकरूपत्वेन पञ्चत्रिंशत्तत्त्वमयत्वेन प्रमातृसप्तकस्वभावत्वेन चिदादिशक्तिपञ्चकात्मकत्वेन चायं प्रत्यभिज्ञायमानो मुक्तिदोऽन्यथा तु संसारहेतुः॥७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nirṇītadṛśā cidātmā śivabhaṭṭāraka evaika ātmā na tvanyaḥ kaścitprakāśasya deśakālādibhirbhedāyogājjaḍasya tu grāhakatvānupapatteḥ| Prakāśa eva yataḥ svātantryādgṛhītaprāṇādisaṅkocaḥ saṅkucitārthagrāhakatāmaśnute tato'sau prakāśarūpatvasaṅkocāvabhāsavattvābhyāṁ dvirūpaḥ| Āṇavamāyīyakārmamalāvṛtatvāttrimayaḥ| Śūnyaprāṇapuryaṣṭakaśarīrasvabhāvatvāccaturātmā| Saptapañcakāni - Śivādipṛthivyantāni pañcatriṁśattattvāni tatsvabhāvaḥ| Tathā śivādisakalāntapramātṛsaptakasvarūpaścidānandecchājñānakriyāśaktirūpatve'pyakhyātivaśātkalāvidyārāgakālaniyatikañcukavalitatvātpañcakasvarūpaḥ| Evaṁ ca śivaikarūpatvena pañcatriṁśattattvamayatvena pramātṛsaptakasvabhāvatvena cidādiśaktipañcakātmakatvena cāyaṁ pratyabhijñāyamāno muktido'nyathā tu saṁsārahetuḥ||7||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-en" lang="en"><ruby>Because<rp> (</rp><rt>yatas</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>Liberation<rp> (</rp><rt>muktiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(occurs)_ through a correct knowledge of His essential nature <span class="pv-note">— that is, of the essential nature of the Self —</span> <span class="pv-w">(asya eva samyak-svarūpa-jñānāt)</span>, _(and because)_ Saṁsāra <span class="pv-note">— Transmigration full of suffering —</span> <span class="pv-w">(saṁsāraḥ)</span> _(occurs through an)_ incorrect _(knowledge of His essential nature)_ <span class="pv-w">(asamyak)</span>, <ruby>therefore<rp> (</rp><rt>tatas</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>in order to break<rp> (</rp><rt>nirbhaṅktum</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>His essential nature<rp> (</rp><rt>etad-svarūpam</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>into small fragments<rp> (</rp><rt>tilaśas</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(and in this way be able to analyze them better, the author)_ <ruby>said<rp> (</rp><rt>āha</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>:</p>

<p class="pv-tr pv-anchor" id="s1-ru" markdown="1"><ruby>И<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(хотя)_ Он <span class="pv-note">— т.e. "Высшая Самость" —</span> <span class="pv-w">(saḥ)</span> <ruby>Один<rp> (</rp><rt>ekaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(Он становится)_ <ruby>двойственным<rp> (</rp><rt>dvirūpaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>тройственным<rp> (</rp><rt>trimayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>четверным<rp> (</rp><rt>catur-ātmā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(и имеющим)_ <ruby>природу<rp> (</rp><rt>sva-bhāvaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>семи<rp> (</rp><rt>sapta</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>групп по пять<rp> (</rp><rt>pañcaka</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>||7||</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">From the <ruby>standpoint of what has already been determined<rp> (</rp><rt>nirṇīta-dṛśā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>the Adorable Śiva Himself<rp> (</rp><rt>śiva-bhaṭṭārakaḥ eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>consisting of Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>cit-ātmā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(is)_ <ruby>a single Self<rp> (</rp><rt>ekaḥ ātmā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(and)_ no one else <span class="pv-note">— or «no other» —</span> <span class="pv-w">(na tu anyaḥ kaścid)</span>. _(Why?)_ <ruby>Since it is impossible to divide the Light<rp> (</rp><rt>prakāśasya... bheda-ayogāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>through space, time, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>deśa-kāla-ādibhiḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>; _(and also)_ since what is inert fails to be the state of a knowing subject <span class="pv-note">— or «it is impossible for it to be the state of a knowing subject» —</span> <span class="pv-w">(jaḍasya tu grāhakatva-anupapatteḥ)</span>. <ruby>Since<rp> (</rp><rt>yatas</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>the very Light<rp> (</rp><rt>prakāśaḥ eva</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, through _(Its)_ <ruby>Absolute Freedom<rp> (</rp><rt>svātantryāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, assumes the <ruby>contraction/limitation of vital energy, etc.<rp> (</rp><rt>gṛhīta-prāṇa-ādi-saṅkocaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(and)_ <ruby>attains<rp> (</rp><rt>aśnute</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> the condition <ruby>of knower of contracted/limited objects<rp> (</rp><rt>saṅkucita-artha-grāhakatām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>therefore<rp> (</rp><rt>tatas</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, It <span class="pv-note">— that very Light —</span> <span class="pv-w">(asau)</span> _(becomes)_ <ruby>twofold<rp> (</rp><rt>dvi-rūpaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> as the nature of Light and as the condition <ruby>of manifestor of contraction/limitation<rp> (</rp><rt>prakāśa-rūpatva-saṅkoca-avabhāsavattvābhyām</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. _(And,)_ since _(that very Light)_ is <ruby>covered by Āṇavamala, Māyīyamala and Kārmamala<rp> (</rp><rt>āṇava-māyīya-kārma-mala-āvṛtatvāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(It becomes)_ <ruby>threefold<rp> (</rp><rt>tri-mayaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. _(Likewise,)_ because It has as Its nature the void, the vital <ruby>energy, the subtle body and the physical body<rp> (</rp><rt>śūnya-prāṇa-puryaṣṭaka-śarīra-svabhāvatvāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(this Light becomes)_ <ruby>fourfold<rp> (</rp><rt>catur-ātmā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. _(Finally,)_ <ruby>the seven groups of five<rp> (</rp><rt>sapta-pañcakāni</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> — _(Because that is)_ Its nature <span class="pv-note">— of the Light —</span> <span class="pv-w">(tad-svabhāvaḥ)</span>, _(namely,)_ <ruby>thirty-five principles<rp> (</rp><rt>pañca-triṁśat-tattvāni</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, from Śiva to the earth element <span class="pv-note">— in reality, this sums up to thirty-six principles —</span> <span class="pv-w">(śiva-ādi-pṛthivī-antāni)</span>. <ruby>Thus<rp> (</rp><rt>tathā</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(Its)_ nature is <ruby>a group of seven knowers, from Śiva to Sakala<rp> (</rp><rt>śiva-ādi-sakala-anta-pramātṛ-saptaka-svarūpaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(and)_ <ruby>although<rp> (</rp><rt>api</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> _(Its)_ form consists of the powers of Consciousness, <ruby>Bliss, Will, Knowledge and Action<rp> (</rp><rt>cit-ānanda-icchā-jñāna-kriyā-śakti-rūpatve</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(this Light)_ <ruby>has a fivefold nature<rp> (</rp><rt>pañcaka-svarūpaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> because of «akhyāti» <span class="pv-note">— lit. «because of the absence of an awareness», but here it means «because of the primordial ignorance [also called Āṇavamala]» —</span> <span class="pv-w">(akhyāti-vaśāt)</span> since It remains surrounded by the <ruby>sheaths of Kalā, Vidyā, Rāga, Kāla and Niyati<rp> (</rp><rt>kalā-vidyā-rāga-kāla-niyati-kañcuka-valitatvāt</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. In this way (evam ca), It <span class="pv-note">— the Light of Consciousness —</span> <span class="pv-w">(ayam)</span> <ruby>grants Liberation<rp> (</rp><rt>mukti-daḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> <ruby>by being recognized as<rp> (</rp><rt>pratyabhijñāyamānaḥ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> having a single form _(called)_ <ruby>Śiva<rp> (</rp><rt>śiva-eka-rūpatvena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>which is composed of thirty-five principles<rp> (</rp><rt>pañca-triṁśat-tattva-mayatvena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>whose nature is seven knowers<rp> (</rp><rt>pramātṛ-saptaka-svabhāvatvena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, <ruby>and<rp> (</rp><rt>ca</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby> which consists of five <ruby>powers beginning with that of Consciousness<rp> (</rp><rt>cit-ādi-śakti-pañcaka-ātmakatvena</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>. <ruby>Otherwise<rp> (</rp><rt>anyathā tu</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>, _(It is only)_ a cause of Saṁsāra <span class="pv-note">— Transmigration full of sufferings —</span> <span class="pv-w">(saṁsāra-hetuḥ)</span>||7||</p>
