# Глава 11 — Kalādyadhvā

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">118 строф · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [перевод главы у источника](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/ru/node/873)</p>

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Глава 10 — Tattvabhedanam](/ksh/ta/ch10/) · [Глава 12 — Adhvopayogaḥ →](/ksh/ta/ch12/)</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.1">11.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloka ekādaśamāhnikam|<br />
Kalādhvā vakṣyate śrīmacchāmbhavājñānusārataḥ||11.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.2">11.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathā pūrvoktabhuvanamadhye nijanijaṁ gaṇam|<br />
Anuyatparato bhinnaṁ tattvaṁ nāmeti bhaṇyate||11.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.3">11.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathā teṣvapi tattveṣu svavarge'nugamātmakam|<br />
Vyāvṛttaṁ paravargācca kaleti śivaśāsane||11.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.4">11.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kecidāhuḥ punaryāsau śaktirantaḥ susūkṣmikā|<br />
Tattvānāṁ sā kaletyuktā dharaṇyāṁ dhārikā yathā||11.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.5">11.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atra pakṣadvaye vastu na bhinnaṁ bhāsate yataḥ|<br />
Anugāmi na sāmānyamiṣṭaṁ naiyāyikādivat||11.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.6">11.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anye vadanti dīkṣādau sukhasaṅgrahaṇārthataḥ|<br />
Śivena kalpito vargaḥ kaleti samayāśrayaḥ||11.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.7">11.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kṛtaśca devadevena samayo'paramārthatām|<br />
Na gacchatīti nāsatyo na cānyasamayodayaḥ||11.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.8">11.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nivṛttiḥ pṛthivītattve pratiṣṭhāvyaktagocare|<br />
Vidyā niśānte śāntā ca śaktyante'ṇḍamidaṁ catuḥ||11.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.9">11.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śāntātītā śive tattve kalātītaḥ paraḥ śivaḥ|<br />
Nahyatra vargīkaraṇaṁ samayaḥ kalanāpi vā||11.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.10">11.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yujyate sarvatodikkaṁ svātantryollāsadhāmani|<br />
Svātantryāttu nijaṁ rūpaṁ boddhṛdharmādavicyutam||11.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.11">11.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Upadeśatadāveśaparamārthatvasiddhaye|<br />
Bodhyatāmānayandevaḥ sphuṭameva vibhāvyate||11.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.12">11.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yato'taḥ śivatattve'pi kalāsaṅgatirucyate|<br />
Aṇḍaṁ ca nāma bhuvanavibhāgasthitikāraṇam||11.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.13">11.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prāhurāvaraṇaṁ tacca śaktyantaṁ yāvadasti hi|<br />
Yadyapi prākśivākhye'pi tattve bhuvanapaddhatiḥ||11.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.14">11.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktā tathāpyapratighe nāsminnāvṛtisambhavaḥ|<br />
Nanvevaṁ dharaṇīṁ muktvā śaktau prakṛtimāyayoḥ||11.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.15">11.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Api cāpratighatve'pi kathamaṇḍasya sambhavaḥ|<br />
Atrāsmadguravaḥ prāhuryatpṛthivyādipañcakam||11.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.16">11.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pratyakṣamidamābhāti tato'nyannāsti kiñcana|<br />
Meyatve sthūlasūkṣmatvānmānatve karaṇatvataḥ||11.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.17">11.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kartṛtollāsataḥ kartṛbhāve sphuṭatayoditam|<br />
Triṁśattattvaṁ vibhedātma tadabhedo niśā matā||11.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.18">11.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kāryatvakaraṇatvādivibhāgagalane sati|<br />
Vikāsotkasvatantratve śivāntaṁ pañcakaṁ jaguḥ||11.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.19">11.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīmatkālottarādau ca kathitaṁ bhūyasā tathā|<br />
Pañcaitāni tu tattvāni yairvyāptamakhilaṁ jagat||11.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.20">11.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pañcamantratanau tena sadyojātādi bhaṇyate|<br />
Īśānāntaṁ tatra tatra dharādigaganāntakam||11.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.21">11.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śivatattvamataḥ śūnyātiśūnyaṁ syādanāvṛtam|<br />
Yattu sarvāvibhāgātma svatantraṁ bodhasundaram||11.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.22">11.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saptatriṁśaṁ tu tatprāhustattvaṁ paraśivābhidham|<br />
Tasyāpyuktanayādvedyabhāve'tra parikalpite||11.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.23">11.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yadāste hyanavacchinnaṁ tadaṣṭātriṁśamucyate|<br />
Na cānavasthā hyevaṁ syāddṛśyatāṁ hi mahātmabhiḥ||11.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.24">11.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yadvedyaṁ kiñcidābhāti tatkṣaye yatprakāśate|<br />
Tattattvamiti nirṇītaṁ ṣaṭtriṁśaṁ hṛdi bhāsate||11.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.25">11.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatkiṁ na kiñcidvā kiñcidityākāṅkṣāvaśe vapuḥ|<br />
Cidānandasvatantraikarūpaṁ taditi deśane||11.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.26">11.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saptatriṁśaṁ samābhāti tatrākāṅkṣā ca nāparā|<br />
Taccāpi kḷptavedyatvaṁ yatra bhāti sa cinmayaḥ||11.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.27">11.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aṣṭātriṁśattamaḥ so'pi bhāvanāyopadiśyate|<br />
Yadi nāma tataḥ saptatriṁśa eva punarbhavet||11.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.28">11.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Avibhāgasvatantratvacinmayatvādidharmatā|<br />
Samaiva vedyīkaraṇaṁ kevalaṁ tvadhikaṁ yataḥ||11.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.29">11.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dharāyāṁ guṇatattvānte māyānte kramaśaḥ sthitāḥ|<br />
Gandho raso rūpamantaḥ sūkṣmabhāvakrameṇa tu||11.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.30">11.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti sthite naye śaktitattvānte'pyasti saukṣmyabhāk|<br />
Sparśaḥ ko'pi sadā yasmai yoginaḥ spṛhayālavaḥ||11.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.31">11.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatsparśānte tu saṁvittiḥ śuddhacidvyomarūpiṇī|<br />
Yasyāṁ rūḍhaḥ samabhyeti svaprakāśātmikāṁ parām||11.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.32">11.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ato vindurato nādo rūpamasmādato rasaḥ|<br />
Ityuktaṁ kṣobhakatvena spande sparśastu no tathā||11.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.33">11.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mataṁ caitanmaheśasya śrīpūrve yadabhāṣata|<br />
Dhārikāpyāyinī boddhrī pavitrī cāvakāśadā||11.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.34">11.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ebhiḥ śabdairvyavaharannivṛttyādernijaṁ vapuḥ|<br />
Pañcatattvavidhiḥ proktastritattvamadhunocyate||11.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.35">11.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vijñānākalaparyantamātmā vidyeśvarāntakam|<br />
Śeṣe śivastritattve syādekatattve śivaḥ param||11.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.36">11.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Imau bhedāvubhau tattvabhedamātrakṛtāviti|<br />
Tattvādhvaivāyamitthaṁ ca na ṣaḍadhvasthiteḥ kṣatiḥ||11.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.37">11.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prakṛt pumānyatiḥ kālo māyā vidyeśasauśivau|<br />
Śivaśca navatattve'pi vidhau tattvādhvarūpatā||11.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.38">11.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Evamaṣṭādaśākhye'pi vidhau nyāyaṁ vadetsudhīḥ|<br />
Yatra yatra hi bhogecchā tatprādhānyopayogataḥ||11.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.39">11.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anyāntarbhāvanātaśca dīkṣānantavibhedabhāk|<br />
Tena ṣaṭtriṁśato yāvadekatattvavidhirbhavet||11.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.40">11.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tattvādhvaiva sa devena prokto vyāsasamāsataḥ|<br />
Ekatattvavidhiścaiṣa suprabuddhaṁ guruṁ prati||11.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.41">11.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śiṣyaṁ ca gatabhogāśamuditaḥ śambhunā yataḥ|<br />
Bhedaṁ visphārya visphārya śaktyā svacchandarūpayā||11.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.42">11.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svātmanyabhinne bhagavānnityaṁ viśramayansthitaḥ|<br />
Itthaṁ tryātmādhvano bhedaḥ sthūlasūkṣmaparatvataḥ||11.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.43">11.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Meyabhāgagataḥ proktaḥ puratattvakalātmakaḥ|<br />
Adhunā mātṛbhāgasthaṁ rūpaṁ tredhā nirūpyate||11.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.44">11.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yatpramāṇātmakaṁ rūpamadhvano mātṛbhāgagam|<br />
Padaṁ hyavagamātmatvasamāveśāttaducyate||11.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.45">11.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadeva ca padaṁ mantraḥ prakṣobhātpracyutaṁ yadā|<br />
Guptabhāṣī yato mātā tūṣṇīmbhūto vyavasthitaḥ||11.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.46">11.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathāpi na vimarśātmarūpaṁ tyajati tena saḥ|<br />
Pramāṇātmavimarśātmā mānavatkṣobhabhāṅna tu||11.46||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.47">11.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mantrāṇāṁ ca padānāṁ ca tenoktaṁ trikaśāsane|<br />
Abhinnameva svaṁ rūpaṁ niḥspandākṣobhitaṁ param||11.47||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.48">11.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Audāsīnyaparityāge prakṣobhānavarohaṇe|<br />
Varṇādhvā mātṛbhāge syātpūrvaṁ yā kathitā pramā||11.48||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.49">11.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sā tu pūrṇasvarūpatvādavibhāgamayī yataḥ|<br />
Tata ekaikavarṇatvaṁ tattve tattve kṣamāditaḥ||11.49||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.50"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.50">11.50</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kṛtvā śaive pare proktāḥ ṣoḍaśārṇā visargataḥ|<br />
Tatra śaktiparispandastāvānprākca nirūpitaḥ||11.50||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.51"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.51">11.51</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṅkalayyocyate sarvamadhunā sukhasaṁvide|<br />
Padamantravarṇamekaṁ puraṣoḍaśakaṁ dhareti ca nivṛttiḥ|<br />
Tattvārṇamagninayanaṁ rasaśarapuramastramantrapadamanyā||11.51||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.52"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.52">11.52</a> <span class="pv-iast">Munitattvārṇaṁ dvikapadamantraṁ vasvakṣibhuvanamaparakalā|<br />
Agnyarṇatattvamekakapadamantraṁ sainyabhuvanamiti turyā||11.52||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.53"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.53">11.53</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ṣoḍaśa varṇāḥ padamantratattvamekaṁ ca śāntyatīteyam|<br />
Abhinavaguptenāryātrayamuktaṁ saṅgrahāya śiṣyebhyaḥ||11.53||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.54"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.54">11.54</a> <span class="pv-iast">So'yaṁ samasta evādhvā bhairavābhedavṛttimān|<br />
Tatsvātantryātsvatantratvamaśnuvāno'vabhāsate||11.54||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.55"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.55">11.55</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathāhi mātṛrūpastho mantrādhveti nirūpitaḥ|<br />
Tathāhi cidvimarśena grastā vācyadaśā yadā||11.55||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.56"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.56">11.56</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śivajñānakriyāyattamananatrāṇatatparā|<br />
Aśeṣaśaktipaṭalīlīlālāmpaṭyapāṭavāt||11.56||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.57"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.57">11.57</a> <span class="pv-iast">Cyutā mānamayādrūpātsaṁvinmantrādhvatāṁ gatā|<br />
Pramāṇarūpatāmetya prayātyadhvā padātmatām||11.57||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.58"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.58">11.58</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathā hi māturviśrāntirvarṇānsaṅghaṭya tānbahūn|<br />
Saṅghaṭṭanaṁ ca kramikaṁ sañjalpātmakameva tat||11.58||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.59"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.59">11.59</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vikalpasya svakaṁ rūpaṁ bhogāveśamayaṁ sphuṭam|<br />
Ataḥ pramāṇatārūpaṁ padamasmadgururjagau||11.59||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.60"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.60">11.60</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pramāṇarūpatāveśamaparityajya meyatām||11.60||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.61"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.61">11.61</a> <span class="pv-iast">Gacchankalanayā yogādadhvā proktaḥ kalātmakaḥ|<br />
Śuddhe prameyatāyoge sūkṣmasthūlatvabhāgini||11.61||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.62"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.62">11.62</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tattvādhvabhuvanādhvatve krameṇānusaredguruḥ|<br />
Prameyamānamātṝṇāṁ yadrūpamupari sthitam||11.62||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.63"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.63">11.63</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pramātmātra sthito'dhvāyaṁ varṇātmā dṛśyatāṁ kila|<br />
Ucchalatsaṁvidāmātraviśrāntyāsvādayoginaḥ||11.63||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.64"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.64">11.64</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sarvābhidhānasāmarthyādaniyantritaśaktayaḥ|<br />
Sṛṣṭāḥ svātmasahotthe'rthe dharāparyantabhāgini||11.64||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.65"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.65">11.65</a> <span class="pv-iast">Āmṛśantaḥ svacidbhūmau tāvato'rthānabhedataḥ|<br />
Varṇaughāste pramārūpāṁ satyāṁ bibhrati saṁvidam||11.65||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.66"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.66">11.66</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bālāstiryakpramātāro ye'pyasaṅketabhāginaḥ|<br />
Te'pyakṛtrimasaṁskārasārāmenāṁ svasaṁvidam||11.66||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.67"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.67">11.67</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhinnabhinnāmupāśritya yānti citrāṁ pramātṛtām|<br />
Asyāṁ cākṛtrimānantavarṇasaṁvidi rūḍhatām||11.67||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.68"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.68">11.68</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṅketā yānti cette'pi yāntyasaṅketavṛttitām|<br />
Anayā tu vinā sarve saṅketā bahuśaḥ kṛtāḥ||11.68||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.69"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.69">11.69</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aviśrāntatayā kuryuranavasthāṁ duruttarām|<br />
Bālo vyutpādyate yena tatra saṅketamārgaṇāt||11.69||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.70"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.70">11.70</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aṅgulyādeśane'pyasya nāvikalpā tathā matiḥ|<br />
Vikalpaḥ śabdamūlaśca śabdaḥ saṅketajīvitaḥ||11.70||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.71"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.71">11.71</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tenānanto hyamāyīyo yo varṇagrāma īdṛśaḥ|<br />
Saṁvidvimarśasacivaḥ sadaiva sa hi jṛmbhate||11.71||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.72"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.72">11.72</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yata eva ca māyīyā varṇāḥ sūtiṁ vitenire|<br />
Ye ca māyīyavarṇeṣu vīryatvena nirūpitāḥ||11.72||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.73"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.73">11.73</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṅketanirapekṣāste prameti parigṛhyatām|<br />
Tathā hi paravākyeṣu śruteṣvāvriyate nijā||11.73||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.74"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.74">11.74</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pramā yasya jaḍo'sau no tatrārthe'bhyeti mātṛtām|<br />
Śukavatsa paṭhatyeva paraṁ tatkramitaikabhāk||11.74||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.75"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.75">11.75</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svātantryalābhataḥ svākyapramālābhe tu boddhṛtā|<br />
Yasya hi svapramābodho vipakṣodbhedanigrahāt||11.75||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.76"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.76">11.76</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vākyādivarṇapuñje sve sa pramātā vaśībhavet|<br />
Yathā yathā cākṛtakaṁ tadrūpamatiricyate||11.76||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.77"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.77">11.77</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathā tathā camatkāratāratamyaṁ vibhāvyate|<br />
Ādyāmāyīyavarṇāntarnimagne cottarottare||11.77||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.78"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.78">11.78</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṅkete pūrvapūrvāṁśamajjane pratibhābhidaḥ|<br />
Ādyodrekamahattve'pi pratibhātmani niṣṭhitāḥ||11.78||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.79"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.79">11.79</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dhruvaṁ kavitvavaktṛtvaśālitāṁ yānti sarvataḥ|<br />
Yāvaddhāmani saṅketanikārakalanojjhite||11.79||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.80"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.80">11.80</a> <span class="pv-iast">Viśrāntaścinmaye kiṁ kiṁ na vetti kurute na vā|<br />
Ata eva hi vāksiddhau varṇānāṁ samupāsyatā||11.80||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.81"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.81">11.81</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sarvajñatvādisiddhau vā kā siddhiryā na tanmayī|<br />
Taduktaṁ varadena śrīsiddhayogīśvarīmate||11.81||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.82"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.82">11.82</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tena guptena guptāste śeṣā varṇāstviti sphuṭam|<br />
Evaṁ māmātṛmānatvameyatvairyo'vabhāsate||11.82||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.83"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.83">11.83</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ṣaḍvidhaḥ svavapuḥśuddhau śuddhiṁ so'dhvādhigacchati|<br />
Ekena vapuṣā śuddhau tatraivānyaprakāratām||11.83||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.84"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.84">11.84</a> <span class="pv-iast">Antarbhāvyācarecchuddhimanusandhānavānguruḥ|<br />
Anantarbhāvaśaktau tu sūkṣmaṁ sūkṣmaṁ tu śodhayet||11.84||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.85"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.85">11.85</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadviśuddhaṁ bījabhāvātsūte nottarasantatim|<br />
Śodhanaṁ bahudhā tattadbhogaprāptyekatānatā||11.85||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.86"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.86">11.86</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadādhipatyaṁ tattyāgastacchivātmatvavedanam|<br />
Tallīnatā tannirāsaḥ sarvaṁ caitatkramākramāt||11.86||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.87"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.87">11.87</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ata eva ca te mantrāḥ śodhakāścitrarūpiṇaḥ|<br />
Siddhāntavāmadakṣādau citrāṁ śuddhiṁ vitanvate||11.87||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.88"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.88">11.88</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anuttaratrikānāmakramamantrāstu ye kila|<br />
Te sarve sarvadāḥ kintu kasyacitkvāpi mukhyatā||11.88||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.89"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.89">11.89</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ataḥ śodhakabhāvena śāstre śrīpūrvasañjñite|<br />
Parāparādimantrāṇāmadhvanyuktā vyavasthitiḥ||11.89||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.90"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.90">11.90</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śodhakatvaṁ ca mālinyā devīnāṁ tritayasya ca|<br />
Devatrayasya vaktrāṇāmaṅgānāmaṣṭakasya ca||11.90||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.91"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.91">11.91</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kiṁ vātibahunā dvāravāstvādhāragurukrame|<br />
Lokapāstravidhau mantrānmuktvā sarvaṁ viśodhakam||11.91||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.92"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.92">11.92</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yaccaitadadhvanaḥ proktaṁ śodhyatvaṁ śoddhṛtā ca yā|<br />
Sā svātantryācchivābhede yuktetyuktaṁ ca śāsane||11.92||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.93"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.93">11.93</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sarvametadvibhātyeva parameśitari dhruve|<br />
Pratibimbasvarūpeṇa na tu bāhyatayā yataḥ||11.93||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.94"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.94">11.94</a> <span class="pv-iast">Cidvyomnyeva śive tattaddehādimatirīdṛśī|<br />
Bhinnā saṁsāriṇāṁ rajjau sarpasragvīcibuddhivat||11.94||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.95"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.95">11.95</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yataḥ prāgdehamaraṇasiddhāntaḥ svapnagocaraḥ|<br />
Dehāntarādirmaraṇe kīdṛgvā dehasambhavaḥ||11.95||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.96"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.96">11.96</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svapne'pi pratibhāmātrasāmānyaprathanābalāt|<br />
Viśeṣāḥ pratibhāsante na bhāvyante'pi te yathā||11.96||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.97"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.97">11.97</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śālagrāmopalāḥ keciccitrākṛtibhṛto yathā|<br />
Tathā māyādibhūmyantalekhācitrahṛdaścitaḥ||11.97||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.98"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.98">11.98</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nagarārṇavaśailādyāstadicchānuvidhāyinaḥ|<br />
Na svayaṁ sadasanto no kāraṇākāraṇātmakāḥ||11.98||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.99"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.99">11.99</a> <span class="pv-iast">Niyateścirarūḍhāyāḥ samucchedātpravartanāt|<br />
Arūḍhāyāḥ svatantro'yaṁ sthitaścidvyomabhairavaḥ||11.99||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.100"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.100">11.100</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ekacinmātrasampūrṇabhairavābhedabhāgini|<br />
Evamasmītyanāmarśo bhedako bhāvamaṇḍale||11.100||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.101"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.101">11.101</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sarvapramāṇairno siddhaṁ svapne kartrantaraṁ yathā|<br />
Svasaṁvidaḥ svasiddhāyāstathā sarvatra buddhyatām||11.101||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.102"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.102">11.102</a> <span class="pv-iast">Cittacitrapurodyāne krīḍanevaṁ hi vetti yaḥ|<br />
Ahameva sthito bhūtabhāvatattvapurairiti||11.102||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.103"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.103">11.103</a> <span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ jāto mṛto'smīti janmamṛtyuvicitratāḥ|<br />
Ajanmanyamṛtau bhānti cittabhittau svanirmitāḥ||11.103||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.104"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.104">11.104</a> <span class="pv-iast">Parehasaṁvidāmātraṁ paralokeha lokate|<br />
Vastutaḥ saṁvido deśaḥ kālo vā naiva kiñcana||11.104||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.105"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.105">11.105</a> <span class="pv-iast">Abhaviṣyadayaṁ sargo mūrtaścenna tu cinmayaḥ|<br />
Tadavekṣyata tanmadhyātkenaiko'pi dharādharaḥ||11.105||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.106"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.106">11.106</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhūtatanmātravargāderādhārādheyatākrame|<br />
Ante saṁvinmayī śaktiḥ śivarūpaiva dhāriṇī||11.106||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.107"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.107">11.107</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasmātpratītirevetthaṁ kartrī dhartrī ca sā śivaḥ|<br />
Tato bhāvāstatra bhāvāḥ śaktirādhārikā tataḥ||11.107||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.108"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.108">11.108</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sāṅkalpikaṁ nirādhāramapi naiva patatyadhaḥ|<br />
Svādhāraśaktau viśrāntaṁ viśvamitthaṁ vimṛśyatām||11.108||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.109"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.109">11.109</a> <span class="pv-iast">Asyā ghanāhamityādi rūḍhireva dharāditā|<br />
Yāvadante cidasmīti nirvṛttā bhairavātmatā||11.109||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.110"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.110">11.110</a> <span class="pv-iast">Maṇāvindrāyudhe bhāsa iva nīlādayaḥ śive|<br />
Paramārthata eṣāṁ tu nodayo na vyayaḥ kvacit||11.110||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.111"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.111">11.111</a> <span class="pv-iast">Deśe kāle'tra vā sṛṣṭirityetadasamañjasam|<br />
Cidātmanā hi devena sṛṣṭirdikkālayorapi||11.111||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.112"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.112">11.112</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jāgarābhimate sārdhahastatritayagocare|<br />
Prahare ca pṛthaksvapnāścitradikkālamāninaḥ||11.112||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.113"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.113">11.113</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ata eva kṣaṇaṁ nāma na kiñcidapi manmahe|<br />
Kriyākṣaṇe vāpyekasminbahvyaḥ saṁsyurdrutāḥ kriyāḥ||11.113||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.114"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.114">11.114</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tena ye bhāvasaṅkocaṁ kṣaṇāntaṁ pratipedire|<br />
Te nūnamenayā nāḍyā śūnyadṛṣṭyavalambinaḥ||11.114||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.115"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.115">11.115</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadya eṣa sato bhāvāñ śūnyīkartuṁ tathāsataḥ|<br />
Sphuṭīkartuṁ svatantratvādīśaḥ so'smatprabhuḥ śivaḥ||11.115||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.116"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.116">11.116</a> <span class="pv-iast">Taditthaṁ parameśāno viśvarūpaḥ pragīyate|<br />
Na tu bhinnasya kasyāpi dharāderupapannatā||11.116||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.117"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.117">11.117</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktaṁ caitatpuraiveti na bhūyaḥ pravivicyate|<br />
Bhūyobhiścāpi bāhyārthadūṣaṇaiḥ pravyaramyata||11.117||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v11.118"><a class="ta-num" href="#v11.118">11.118</a> <span class="pv-iast">Taditthameṣa nirṇītaḥ kalādervistaro'dhvanaḥ||11.118||</span></p>
