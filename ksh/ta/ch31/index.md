# Глава 31 — Maṇḍalam

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">163 строфы · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [английский перевод у источника — русского там нет](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/en/node/902)</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.1">31.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloka ekatriṁśamāhnikam|<br />
Atha maṇḍalasadbhāvaḥ saṅkṣepeṇābhidhīyate|<br />
Sādhayitvā diśaṁ pūrvāṁ sūtramāsphālayetsamam||31.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.2">31.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadardhayitvā madhyaprākpratīcīṣvaṅkayetpunaḥ|<br />
Tato'pyardhatadardhārdhamānataḥ pūrvapaścimau||31.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.3">31.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aṅkayettāvatā dadyātsūtreṇa bhramayugmakam|<br />
Matsyasandhidvayaṁ tvevaṁ dakṣiṇottarayorbhavet||31.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.4">31.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tanmadhye pātayetsūtraṁ dakṣiṇottarasiddhaye|<br />
Yadi vā prākparāktulyasūtreṇottaradakṣiṇe||31.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.5">31.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aṅkayedaparādaṅkātpūrvādapi tathaiva te|<br />
Matsyamadhye kṣipetsūtramāyataṁ dakṣiṇottare||31.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.6">31.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Matakṣetrārdhamānena madhyāddikṣvaṅkayettataḥ|<br />
Sūtrābhyāṁ digdvayotthābhyāṁ matsyaḥ syātpratikoṇagaḥ||31.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.7">31.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Matsyeṣu vedāḥ sūtrāṇītyevaṁ syāccaturasrakam|<br />
Ekasmātprabhṛti proktaṁ śatāntaṁ maṇḍalaṁ yataḥ||31.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.8">31.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Siddhātantre maṇḍalānāṁ śataṁ tatpīṭha ucyate|<br />
Yattanmadhyagataṁ mukhyaṁ maṇḍalānāṁ trayaṁ smṛtam||31.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.9">31.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Madhyaśūlaṁ tritriśūlaṁ navaśūlamiti sphuṭam|<br />
Tatra śūlavidhānaṁ yaduktaṁ bhedairanantakaiḥ||31.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.10">31.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadyoni maṇḍalaṁ brūmaḥ sadbhāvakramadarśitam|<br />
Vedāśrite caturhaste tribhāgaṁ sarvatastyajet||31.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.11">31.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhāgaiḥ ṣoḍaśabhiḥ sarvaṁ tattatkṣetraṁ vibhājayet|<br />
Brahmasūtradvayasyātha madhyaṁ brahmapadaṁ sphuṭam||31.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.12">31.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kṛtvāvadhiṁ tato lakṣyaṁ caturthaṁ sūtramāditaḥ|<br />
Tatastiryagvrajetsūtraṁ caturthaṁ tadanantare||31.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.13">31.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Koṣṭhe cendudvayaṁ kuryādbahirbhāgārdhabhāgataḥ|<br />
Tayorlagnaṁ brahmasūtrāttṛtīye marmaṇi sthitam||31.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.14">31.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Koṣṭhakārdhe'paraṁ ceti yugmamantarmukhaṁ bhavet|<br />
Brahmasūtrāddvitīyasminhastaṁ marmaṇi niścalam||31.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.15">31.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kṛtvā pūrṇenduyugalaṁ vartayeta vicakṣaṇaḥ|<br />
Brahmasūtragatātṣaṣṭhāttiryagbhāgāttṛtīyake||31.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.16">31.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kṛtvārdhakoṣṭhake sūtraṁ pūrṇacandrāgralambitam|<br />
Bhrāmayedunmukhaṁ khaṇḍacandrayugvahnibhāgagam||31.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.17">31.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tiryagbhāgadvayaṁ tyaktvā khaṇḍendoḥ paścimāttataḥ|<br />
Koṇaṁ yāvattathā syācca kuryātkhaṇḍaṁ bhramadvayam||31.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.18">31.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sutīkṣṇakuṭilāgraṁ tadekaṁ śṛṅgaṁ prajāyate|<br />
Dvitīyasminnapi proktaḥ śṛṅga eṣa vidhiḥ sphuṭaḥ||31.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.19">31.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Madhyaśṛṅge'tha kartavye tṛtīya ūrdhvakoṣṭhake|<br />
Caturthārdhe ca candrārdhadvayamantarmukhaṁ bhavet||31.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.20">31.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tacca pūrṇendumekaṁ prāgvartitaṁ prāpnuyādyathā|<br />
Anyonyagranthiyogena baddhāratvaṁ prajāyate||31.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.21">31.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ dvitīyapārśve'sya khaṇḍendudvayavartanāt|<br />
Madhyābhyāṁ gaṇḍikā śliṣṭā parābhyāmagrato nayet||31.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.22">31.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sūtraṁ pārśvadvaye yena tīkṣṇaṁ syānmadhyaśṛṅgagam|<br />
Pārśvadvayādhare paścādbrahmasūtraṁ dvitīyakam||31.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.23">31.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Avadhānena saṅgrāhyamācāryeṇohavedinā|<br />
Bhavetpaścānmukho mantrī tasmiṁśca brahmasūtrake||31.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.24">31.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Madhyaśṛṅgaṁ varjayitvā sarvaḥ pūrvodito vidhiḥ|<br />
Tato yadunmukhaṁ khaṇḍacandrayugmaṁ puroditam||31.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.25">31.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tato dvayena kartavyā gaṇḍikāntaḥsusaṅgatā|<br />
Dvayenāgragasūtrābhyāṁ madhyaśṛṅgadvayaṁ bhavet||31.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.26">31.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Adho bhāgavivṛddhyāsya padmaṁ vṛttacatuṣṭayam|<br />
Tataścakraṁ ṣoḍaśāraṁ dvādaśāraṁ dvidhātha tat||31.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.27">31.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Madhye kuleśvarīsthānaṁ vyoma vā tilakaṁ ca vā|<br />
Padmaṁ vātha ṣaḍaraṁ vā viyaddvādaśakaṁ ca vā||31.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.28">31.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tritriśūle'tra saptāre śliṣṭamātreṇa madhyataḥ|<br />
Padmānāmatha cakrāṇāṁ vyomnāṁ vā saptakaṁ bhavet||31.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.29">31.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Miśritaṁ vātha saṅkīrṇaṁ samāsavyāsabhedataḥ|<br />
Tataḥ kṣetrārdhamānena kṣetraṁ tatrādhikaṁ kṣipet||31.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.30">31.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatra daṇḍaḥ smṛto bhāgaḥ ṣaḍarāmalasārakaḥ|<br />
Sutīkṣṇāgraḥ suraktābhaḥ kṣaṇādāveśakārakaḥ||31.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.31">31.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yā sā kuṇḍalinī devī taraṅgākhyā mahormiṇī|<br />
Sā ṣaḍaśreṇa kandākhye sthitā ṣaḍdevatātmikā||31.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.32">31.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aṣṭabhāgaiśca vistīrṇo dīrghaścāpi tadardhataḥ|<br />
Tato dvārāṇi kāryāṇi citravartanayā kramāt||31.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.33">31.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vedāśrāyatarūpāṇi yadi vā vṛttamātrataḥ|<br />
Spaṣṭaśṛṅgamatho kuryādyadi vā vaiparītyataḥ||31.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.34">31.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Unmukhaṁ candrayugmaṁ vā bhaṅktvā kuryāccatuṣṭayam|<br />
Kuṭilo madhyataḥ spaṣṭo'dhomukhaḥ pārśvagaḥ sthitaḥ||31.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.35">31.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uttāno'rdho'samaḥ pūrṇaḥ śliṣṭo granthigatastathā|<br />
Candrasyetthaṁ dvādaśadhā vartanā bhramabhedinī||31.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.36">31.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Antarbahirmukhatvena sā punardvividhā matā|<br />
Tadbhedānmaṇḍalānāṁ syādasaṅkhyo bhedavistaraḥ||31.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.37">31.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pīṭhavīthībahirbhūmikaṇṭhakarṇakapolataḥ|<br />
Śobhopaśobhāsambhedādguṇarekhāvikalpataḥ||31.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.38">31.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svastikadvitayādyaṣṭatayā paryantabhedataḥ|<br />
Bhāvābhāvavikalpena maṇḍalānāmanantatā||31.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.39">31.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tato rajāṁsi deyāni yathāśobhānusārataḥ|<br />
Sindūraṁ rājavartaṁ ca khaṭikā ca sitottamā||31.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.40">31.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uttamāni rajāṁsīha devatātrayayogataḥ|<br />
Parā candrasamaprakhyā raktā devī parāparā||31.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.41">31.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aparā sā parā kālī bhīṣaṇā caṇḍayoginī|<br />
Dṛṣṭvaitanmaṇḍalaṁ devyaḥ sarvā nṛtyanti sarvadā||31.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.42">31.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anarcite'pyadīkṣeṇa dṛṣṭe dīkṣyeta mātṛbhiḥ|<br />
Kiṁvātibahunoktena tritriśūlārasaptakāḥ||31.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.43">31.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śūlayāgāḥ ṣaṭ sahasrāṇyevaṁ sārdhaśatadvayam|<br />
Yā sā devī parā śaktiḥ prāṇavāhā vyavasthitā||31.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.44">31.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Viśvāntaḥ kuṇḍalākārā sā sākṣādatra vartitā|<br />
Tattvāni tattvadevyaśca viśvamasminpratiṣṭhitam||31.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.45">31.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atrordhve tantumātreṇa tisraḥ śūlāragāḥ sthitāḥ|<br />
Āsanatvena cecchādyā bhogamokṣaprasādhikāḥ||31.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.46">31.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tāstu mokṣaikakāmasya śūlāraviddhamadhyakāḥ|<br />
Tasmādenaṁ mahāyāgaṁ mahāvibhavavistaraiḥ||31.46||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.47">31.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pūjayedbhūtikāmo vā mokṣakāmo'pi vā budhaḥ|<br />
Asya darśanamātreṇa bhūtavetālaguhyakāḥ||31.47||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.48">31.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Palāyante daśa diśaḥ śivaḥ sākṣātprasīdati|<br />
Mandaśaktibalāviddho'pyetanmaṇḍalapūjanāt||31.48||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.49">31.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Satataṁ māsaṣaṭkena trikajñānaṁ samaśnute|<br />
Yatprāpya heyopādeyaṁ svayameva vicārya saḥ||31.49||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.50"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.50">31.50</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dehānte syādbhairavātmā siddhikāmo'tha sidhyati|<br />
Maṇḍalasyāsya yo vyāptiṁ devatānyāsameva ca||31.50||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.51"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.51">31.51</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vartanāṁ ca vijānāti sa gurustrikaśāsane|<br />
Tasya pādarajo mūrdhni dhāryaṁ śivasamīhinā||31.51||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.52"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.52">31.52</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atra sṛṣṭisthitidhvaṁsānkramāttrīnapi pūjayet|<br />
Turyaṁ tu madhyato yadvā sarveṣu paripūrakam||31.52||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.53"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.53">31.53</a> <span class="pv-iast">Catustriśūlaṁ vā guptadaṇḍaṁ yāgaṁ samācaret|<br />
Tatra tat pūjayetsamyaksphuṭaṁ kramacatuṣṭayam||31.53||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.54"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.54">31.54</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ityetatkathitaṁ gupte ṣaḍardhahṛdaye pare|<br />
Ṣaṭke proktaṁ sūcitaṁ śrīsiddhayogīśvarīmate||31.54||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.55"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.55">31.55</a> <span class="pv-iast">Agrataḥ sūtrayitvā tu maṇḍalaṁ sarvakāmadam|<br />
Mahāśūlasamopetaṁ padmacakrādibhūṣitam||31.55||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.56"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.56">31.56</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dvāre dvāre likhecchūlaṁ varjayitvā tu paścimam|<br />
Koṇeṣvapi ca vā kāryaṁ mahāśūlaṁ drumānvitam||31.56||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.57"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.57">31.57</a> <span class="pv-iast">Amṛtāmbhodbhavārīṇāṁ śūlāgre tu trikaṁ trikam|<br />
Śūla itthaṁ prakartavyamaṣṭadhā tat tridhāpi vā||31.57||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.58"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.58">31.58</a> <span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ saṁsūcitaṁ divyaṁ khecarīṇāṁ puraṁ tviti|<br />
Sthānāntare'pi kathitaṁ śrīsiddhātantraśāsane||31.58||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.59"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.59">31.59</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kajaṁ madhye tadardhena śūlaśṛṅgāṇi tāni tu|<br />
Śūlāṅkaṁ maṇḍalaṁ kalpyaṁ kamalāṅkaṁ ca pūraṇe||31.59||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.60"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.60">31.60</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha śūlābjavinyāsaḥ śrīpūrve triśiromate|<br />
Siddhātantre trikakule devyāyāmalamālayoḥ||31.60||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.61"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.61">31.61</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathoktaḥ sāraśāstre ca tantrasadbhāvaguhyayoḥ|<br />
Tathā pradarśyate spaṣṭaṁ yadyapyuktakramātkṛtaḥ||31.61||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.62"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.62">31.62</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vedāśrite trihaste prākpūrvamardhaṁ vibhājayet|<br />
Hastārdhaṁ sarvatastyaktvā pūrvodagyāmyadiggatam||31.62||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.63"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.63">31.63</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tryaṅgulaiḥ koṣṭhakairūrdhvaistiryakcāṣṭadvidhātmakaiḥ|<br />
Dvau dvau bhāgau parityajya punardakṣiṇasaumyagau||31.63||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.64"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.64">31.64</a> <span class="pv-iast">Brahmaṇaḥ pārśvayorjīvāccaturthātpūrvatastathā|<br />
Bhāgārdhabhāgamānaṁ tu khaṇḍacandradvayaṁ dvayam||31.64||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.65"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.65">31.65</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tayorantastṛtīye tu dakṣiṇottarapārśvayoḥ|<br />
Jīve khaṇḍenduyugalaṁ kuryādantarbhramādbudhaḥ||31.65||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.66"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.66">31.66</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tayoraparamarmasthaṁ khaṇḍendudvayakoṭigam|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.137"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.137">31.137</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vahnibhūtamunivyomabāhyagarbhe purīṣu ca|<br />
Lopayeccaiva marmāṇyantarnāḍivivarjitān||31.137||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.138"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.138">31.138</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dvāraprākārakoṇeṣu netrānalaśarānṛtūn|<br />
Nāḍayo brahmavaṁśasya lopyā netrādrasasthitāḥ||31.138||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.139"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.139">31.139</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vahnernetrānalau lopyau vedānnetrayugaṁ rasāt|<br />
Netraṁ saumyagataṁ lopyaṁ pūrvādvedānalau rasāt||31.139||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.140"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.140">31.140</a> <span class="pv-iast">Lokasthānnāḍikānhitvā netrādvedāgnayaḥ kramāt|<br />
Śarairvahniyutaṁ caiva yugaṁ netrāgnayo kramāt||31.140||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.141"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.141">31.141</a> <span class="pv-iast">Netrātpūrvagatāccaiva sumerurdvārasañjñitaḥ|<br />
Svastikā ca purī ramyā caturdikṣu sthitāvubhau||31.141||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.142"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.142">31.142</a> <span class="pv-iast">Marmaṇāṁ ca śate dve ca ṛṣibhirguṇitā diśaḥ|<br />
Netrādikāṁśca sammārjya mārgamadhyātsuśobhane||31.142||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.143"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.143">31.143</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ṛṣitrayakṛte madhye viṣayaiḥ karṇikā bhavet|<br />
Netrīkṛtānvasūnpatraṁ netraṁ sakṛdvibhājitam||31.143||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.144"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.144">31.144</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vahniṁ vasugataṁ kṛtvā śaśāṅkasthāṁśca lopayet|<br />
Vahnīṣuṛṣimadhyācca lopyaṁ pīṭhendukāvadhi||31.144||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.145"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.145">31.145</a> <span class="pv-iast">Brahmaṇo netraviṣayānnetrādvedānalau haret|<br />
Sāgare netrakaṁ lopyaṁ nāḍayaḥ pūrvadiggatāḥ||31.145||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.146"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.146">31.146</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhūtanetragatānmūrdhnā netrāddvivahnidṛktrikāt|<br />
Saumyagātpīṭhakoṇeṣu lopayeta caturṣvapi||31.146||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.147"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.147">31.147</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dalāni kāryāṇi sitaiḥ kesaraṁ raktapītalaiḥ|<br />
Karṇikā kanakaprakhyā pallavāntāśca lohitāḥ||31.147||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.148"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.148">31.148</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vyomarekhā tu susitā vartulābjāstu nīlabhāḥ|<br />
Pīṭhaṁ rekhātrayopetaṁ sitalohitapītalam||31.148||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.149"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.149">31.149</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svastikāśca caturvarṇā agnerīśānagocarāḥ|<br />
Vīthī vidrumasaṅkāśā svadikṣvastrāṇi bāhyataḥ||31.149||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.150"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.150">31.150</a> <span class="pv-iast">Indranīlanibhaṁ vajraṁ śaktiṁ padmamaṇiprabhām|<br />
Daṇḍaṁ hāṭakasaṅkāśaṁ vaktraṁ tasyātilohitam||31.150||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.151"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.151">31.151</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nīladyutisamaṁ khaḍgaṁ pāśaṁ vatsakasaprabham|<br />
Dhvajaṁ puṣpaphalopetaṁ pañcaraṅgaiśca śobhitam||31.151||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.152"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.152">31.152</a> <span class="pv-iast">Gadā hemanibhātyugrā nānāratnavibhūṣitā|<br />
Śūlaṁ nīlāmbujasamaṁ jvaladvahnyugraśekharam||31.152||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.153"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.153">31.153</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasyopari sitaṁ padmamīṣatpītāruṇaprabham|<br />
Cakraṁ hemanibhaṁ dīptamarā vaiḍūryasannibhāḥ||31.153||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.154"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.154">31.154</a> <span class="pv-iast">Arāmadhyaṁ supītaṁ ca bāhyaṁ jvālāruṇaṁ bhavet|<br />
Mandiraṁ devadevasya sarvakāmaphalapradam||31.154||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.155"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.155">31.155</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīsiddhāyāṁ śūlavidhiḥ prākkṣetre caturaśrite|<br />
Hastamātraṁ tridhā sūryānnavakhaṇḍaṁ yathā bhavet||31.155||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.156"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.156">31.156</a> <span class="pv-iast">Madhye śūlaṁ ca tatretthaṁ madhyabhāgaṁ tridhā bhajet|<br />
Navabhiḥ koṣṭhakairyuktaṁ tato'yaṁ vidhirucyate||31.156||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.157"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.157">31.157</a> <span class="pv-iast">Madhyabhāgatrayaṁ tyaktvā madhye bhāgadvayasya tu|<br />
Adhastādbhrāmayetsūtraṁ śaśāṅkaśakalākṛti||31.157||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.158"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.158">31.158</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ubhayato bhrāmayettatra yathāgre hākṛtirbhavet|<br />
Koṭyāṁ tatra kṛtaṁ sūtraṁ nayedrekhāṁ tu pūrvikām||31.158||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.159"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.159">31.159</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aparadvārapūrveṇa tyaktvāṅgulacatuṣṭayam|<br />
Rekhāṁ vināśayetprājño yathā śūlākṛtirbhavet||31.159||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.160"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.160">31.160</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śūlāgre tvardhahastena tyaktvā padmāni kārayet|<br />
Adhaḥ śṛṅgatrayaṁ hastamadhye padmaṁ sakarṇikam||31.160||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.161"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.161">31.161</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mukhāgre dhārayetsūtraṁ tribhirhastaistu pātayet|<br />
Madhye cordhvaṁ tataḥ kuryādadhastādaṅguladvayam||31.161||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.162"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.162">31.162</a> <span class="pv-iast">Rekhādvayaṁ pātayeta yathā śūlaṁ bhavatyapi|<br />
Adhobhāgādibhiścordhvaṁ tatra rekhā prapadyate||31.162||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v31.163"><a class="ta-num" href="#v31.163">31.163</a> <span class="pv-iast">Samīkṛtya tataḥ sūtra ūrdhve dve evameva tu|<br />
Madhyaṁ padmaṁ pratiṣṭhāpyaṁ śūlādhastādyaśasvini||31.163||</span></p>
