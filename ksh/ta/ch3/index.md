# Глава 3 — Paropāyaḥ

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">294 строфы · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [перевод главы у источника](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/ru/node/858)</p>

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Глава 2 — Gatopāyaḥ](/ksh/ta/ch2/) · [Глава 4 — Śāktopāyaḥ →](/ksh/ta/ch4/)</p>

<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloke tṛtīyamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.1">3.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha paraupayikaṁ praṇigadyate padamanuttarameva maheśituḥ|<br />
Prakāśamātraṁ yatproktaṁ bhairavīyaṁ paraṁ mahaḥ|<br />
Tatra svatantratāmātramadhikaṁ pravivicyate||3.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.2">3.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yaḥ prakāśaḥ sa sarvasya prakāśatvaṁ prayacchati|<br />
Na ca tadvyatirekyasti viśvaṁ sadvāvabhāsate||3.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.3">3.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ato'sau parameśānaḥ svātmavyomanyanargalaḥ|<br />
Iyataḥ sṛṣṭisaṁhārāḍambarasya pradarśakaḥ||3.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.4">3.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nirmale makure yadvadbhānti bhūmijalādayaḥ|<br />
Amiśrāstadvadekasmiṁścinnāthe viśvavṛttayaḥ||3.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.5">3.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sadṛśaṁ bhāti nayanadarpaṇāmbaravāriṣu|<br />
Tathā hi nirmale rūpe rūpamevāvabhāsate||3.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.6">3.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pracchannarāgiṇī kāntapratibimbitasundaram|<br />
Darpaṇaṁ kucakumbhābhyāṁ spṛśatyapi na tṛpyati||3.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.7">3.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na hi sparśo'sya vimalo rūpameva tathā yataḥ|<br />
Nairmalyaṁ cātiniviḍasajātīyaikasaṅgatiḥ||3.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.8">3.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svasminnabhedādbhinnasya darśanakṣamataiva yā|<br />
Atyaktasvaprakāśasya nairmalyaṁ tadgurūditam||3.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.9">3.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nairmalyaṁ mukhyamekasya saṁvinnāthasya sarvataḥ|<br />
Aṁśāṁśikātaḥ kvāpyanyadvimalaṁ tattadicchayā||3.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.10">3.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhāvānāṁ yatpratīghāti vapurmāyātmakaṁ hi tat|<br />
Teṣāmevāsti sadvidyāmayaṁ tvapratighātakam||3.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.11">3.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadevamubhayākāramavabhāsaṁ prakāśayan|<br />
Vibhāti varado bimbapratibimbadṛśākhile||3.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.12">3.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yastvāha netratejāṁsi svacchātpratiphalantyalam|<br />
Viparyasya svakaṁ vaktraṁ gṛhṇantīti sa pṛcchyate||3.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.13">3.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dehādanyatra yattejastadadhiṣṭhāturātmanaḥ|<br />
Tenaiva tejasā jñatve ko'rthaḥ syāddarpaṇena tu||3.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.14">3.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Viparyastaistu tejobhirgrāhakātmatvamāgataiḥ|<br />
Rūpaṁ dṛśyeta vadane nije na makurāntare||3.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.15">3.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svamukhe sparśavaccaitadrūpaṁ bhāyānmametyalam|<br />
Na tvasya spṛśyabhinnasya vedyaikāntasvarūpiṇaḥ||3.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.16">3.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Rūpasaṁsthānamātraṁ tatsparśagandharasādibhiḥ|<br />
Nyagbhūtaireva tadyuktaṁ vastu tatpratibimbitam||3.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.17">3.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nyagbhāvo grāhyatābhāvāttadabhāvo'pramāṇataḥ|<br />
Sa cārthasaṅgamābhāvātso'pyādarśe'navasthiteḥ||3.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.18">3.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ata eva gurutvādirdharmo naitasya lakṣyate|<br />
Nahyādarśe saṁsthito'sau taddṛṣṭau sa upāyakaḥ||3.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.19">3.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasmāttu naiṣa bhedena yadbhāti tata ucyate|<br />
Ādhārastatra tūpāyā dīpadṛksaṁvidaḥ kramāt||3.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.20">3.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dīpacakṣurvibodhānāṁ kāṭhinyābhāvataḥ param|<br />
Sarvataścāpi nairmalyānna vibhādarśavatpṛthak||3.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.21">3.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Etacca devadevena darśitaṁ bodhavṛddhaye|<br />
Mūḍhānāṁ vastu bhavati tato'pyanyatra nāpyalam||3.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.22">3.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pratīghāti svatantraṁ no na sthāyyasthāyi cāpi na|<br />
Svacchasyaivaiṣa kasyāpi mahimeti kṛpālunā||3.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.23">3.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na deśo no rūpaṁ na ca samayayogo na parimā na cānyonyāsaṅgo na ca tadapahānirna ghanatā|<br />
Na cāvastutvaṁ syānna ca kimapi sāraṁ nijamiti dhruvaṁ mohaḥ śāmyediti niradiśaddarpaṇavidhiḥ||3.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.24">3.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Itthaṁ pradarśite'mutra pratibimbanavartmani|<br />
Śabdasya pratibimbaṁ yat pratiśrutketi bhaṇyate||3.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.25">3.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na cāsau śabdajaḥ śabda āgacchattvena saṁśravāt|<br />
Tenaiva vaktrā dūrasthaiḥ śabdasyāśravaṇādapi||3.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.26">3.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Piṭhirādipidhānāṁśaviśiṣṭachidrasaṅgatau|<br />
Citratvāccāsya śabdasya pratibimbaṁ mukhādivat||3.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.27">3.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Idamanyasya vedyasya rūpamityavabhāsate|<br />
Yathādarśe tathā kenāpyuktamākarṇaye tviti||3.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.28">3.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Niyamādbimbasāmmukhyaṁ pratibimbasya yattataḥ|<br />
Tanmadhyagāḥ pramātāraḥ śṛṇvanti pratiśabdakam||3.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.29">3.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mukhyagrahaṁ tvapi vinā pratibimbagraho bhavet|<br />
Svapaścātsthaṁ priyaṁ paśyeṭṭaṅkitaṁ mukure vapuḥ||3.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.30">3.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sāmmukhyaṁ cocyate tādṛgdarpaṇābhedasaṁsthiteḥ||3.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.31">3.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ataḥ kūpādipiṭhirākāśe tatpratibimbitam|<br />
Vaktrākāśaṁ saśabdaṁ sadbhāti tatparavaktṛvat||3.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.32">3.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathā cādarśapāścātyabhāgastho vetti no mukham|<br />
Tathā tathāvidhākāśapaścātstho vetti na dhvanim||3.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.33">3.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śabdo na cānabhivyaktaḥ pratibimbati taddhruvam|<br />
Abhivyaktiśrutī tasya samakālaṁ dvitīyake||3.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.34">3.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kṣaṇe tu pratibimbatvaṁ śrutiśca samakālikā|<br />
Tulyakālaṁ hi no hastatacchāyārūpaniścayaḥ||3.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.35">3.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Itthaṁ pradarśite'mutra pratibimbasatattvake|<br />
Prakṛtaṁ brūmahe tatra pratibimbanamarhati||3.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.36">3.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śabdo nabhasi sānande sparśadhāmani sundaraḥ|<br />
Sparśo'nyo'pi dṛḍhāghātaśūlaśītādikodbhavaḥ|<br />
Parasthaḥ pratibimbatvātsvadehoddhūlanākaraḥ||3.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.37">3.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na caiṣa mukhyastatkāryapāramparyāprakāśanāt||3.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.38">3.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ ghrāṇāntare gandho raso dantodake sphuṭaḥ||3.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.39">3.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathā ca rūpaṁ pratibimbitaṁ dṛśorna cakṣuṣānyena vinā hi lakṣyate|<br />
Tathā rasasparśanasaurabhādikaṁ na lakṣyate'kṣeṇa vinā sthitaṁ tvapi||3.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.40">3.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na cāntare sparśanadhāmani sthitaṁ bahiḥspṛśo'nyākṣadhiyaḥ sa gocaraḥ||3.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.41">3.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ato'ntikasthasvakatādṛgindriyaprayojanāntaḥkaraṇairyadā kṛtā|<br />
Tadā tadāttaṁ pratibimbamindriye svakāṁ kriyāṁ sūyata eva tādṛśīm||3.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.42">3.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na tu smṛtānmānasagocarādṛtā bhavetkriyā sā kila vartamānataḥ|<br />
Ataḥ sthitaḥ sparśavarastadindriye samāgataḥ sanviditastathākriyaḥ||3.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.43">3.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Asambhave bāhyagatasya tādṛśaḥ sva eva tasminpratibimbitastathā|<br />
Karoti tāṁ sparśavaraḥ sukhātmikāṁ sa cāpi kasyāmapi nāḍisantatau||3.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.44">3.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tena saṁvittimakure viśvamātmānamarpayat|<br />
Nāthasya vadate'muṣya vimalāṁ viśvarūpatām||3.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.45">3.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathā ca gandharūpaspṛgrasādyāḥ pratibimbitāḥ|<br />
Tadādhāroparāgeṇa bhānti khaḍge mukhādivat||3.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.46">3.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathā viśvamidaṁ bodhe pratibimbitamāśrayet|<br />
Prakāśatvasvatantratvaprabhṛtiṁ dharmavistaram||3.46||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.47">3.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathā ca sarvataḥ svacche sphaṭike sarvato bhavet|<br />
Pratibimbaṁ tathā bodhe sarvataḥ svacchatājuṣi||3.47||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.48">3.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atyantasvacchatā sā yatsvākṛtyanavabhāsanam|<br />
Ataḥ svacchatamo bodho na ratnaṁ tvākṛtigrahāt||3.48||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.49">3.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pratibimbaṁ ca bimbena bāhyasthena samarpyate|<br />
Tasyaiva pratibimbatve kiṁ bimbamavaśiṣyatām||3.49||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.50"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.50">3.50</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yadvāpi kāraṇaṁ kiñcidbimbatvenābhiṣicyate|<br />
Tadapi pratibimbatvameti bodhe'nyathā tvasat||3.50||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.51"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.51">3.51</a> <span class="pv-iast">Itthametatsvasaṁvittidṛḍhanyāyāstrarakṣitam|<br />
Sāmrājyameva viśvatra pratibimbasya jṛmbhate||3.51||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.52"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.52">3.52</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nanu bimbasya virahe pratibimbaṁ kathaṁ bhavet|<br />
Kiṁ kurmo dṛśyate taddhi nanu tadbimbamucyatām||3.52||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.53"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.53">3.53</a> <span class="pv-iast">Naivaṁ tallakṣaṇābhāvādbimbaṁ kila kimucyate|<br />
Anyāmiśraṁ svatantraṁ sadbhāsamānaṁ mukhaṁ yathā||3.53||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.54"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.54">3.54</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svarūpānapahānena pararūpasadṛkṣatām|<br />
Pratibimbātmatāmāhuḥ khaḍgādarśatalādivat||3.54||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.55"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.55">3.55</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktaṁ ca sati bāhye'pi dhīrekānekavedanāt|<br />
Anekasadṛśākārā na tvaneketi saugataiḥ||3.55||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.56"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.56">3.56</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nanvitthaṁ pratibimbasya lakṣaṇaṁ kiṁ taducyate|<br />
Anyavyāmiśraṇāyogāttadbhedāśakyabhāsanam|<br />
Pratibimbamiti prāhurdarpaṇe vadanaṁ yathā||3.56||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.57"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.57">3.57</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bodhamiśramidaṁ bodhādbhedenāśakyabhāsanam|<br />
Paratattvādi bodhe kiṁ pratibimbaṁ na bhaṇyate||3.57||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.58"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.58">3.58</a> <span class="pv-iast">Lakṣaṇasya vyavasthaiṣākasmāccedbimbamucyatām|<br />
Prājñā vastuni yujyante na tu sāmayike dhvanau||3.58||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.59"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.59">3.59</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nanu na pratibimbasya vinā bimbaṁ bhavetsthitiḥ|<br />
Kiṁ tataḥ pratibimbe hi bimbaṁ tādātmyavṛtti na||3.59||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.60"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.60">3.60</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ataśca lakṣaṇasyāsya proktasya tadasambhave|<br />
Na hānirhetumātre tu praśno'yaṁ paryavasyati||3.60||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.61"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.61">3.61</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatrāpi ca nimittākhye nopādāne kathañcana|<br />
Nimittakāraṇānāṁ ca kadācitkvāpi sambhavaḥ||3.61||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.62"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.62">3.62</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ata eva purovartinyāloke smaraṇādinā|<br />
Nimittena ghanenāstu saṅkrāntadayitākṛtiḥ||3.62||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.63"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.63">3.63</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anyathā saṁvidārūḍhā kāntā vicchedayoginī|<br />
Kasmādbhāti na vai saṁvid vicchedaṁ purato gatā||3.63||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.64"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.64">3.64</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ata evāntaraṁ kiñciddhīsañjñaṁ bhavatu sphuṭam|<br />
Yatrāsya vicchidā bhānaṁ saṅkalpasvapnadarśane||3.64||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.65"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.65">3.65</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ato nimittaṁ devasya śaktayaḥ santu tādṛśe|<br />
Itthaṁ viśvamidaṁ nāthe bhairavīyacidambare|<br />
Pratibimbamalaṁ svacche na khalvanyaprasādataḥ||3.65||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.66"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.66">3.66</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ananyāpekṣitā yāsya viśvātmatvaṁ prati prabhoḥ|<br />
Tāṁ parāṁ pratibhāṁ devīṁ saṅgirante hyanuttarām||3.66||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.67"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.67">3.67</a> <span class="pv-iast">Akulasyāsya devasya kulaprathanaśālinī|<br />
Kaulikī sā parā śaktiraviyukto yayā prabhuḥ||3.67||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.68"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.68">3.68</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tayoryadyāmalaṁ rūpaṁ sa saṅghaṭṭa iti smṛtaḥ|<br />
Ānandaśaktiḥ saivoktā yato viśvaṁ visṛjyate||3.68||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.285"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.285">3.285</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sadoditamahābodhajvālājaṭilatātmani|<br />
Viśvaṁ dravati mayyetaditi paśyanpraśāmyati||3.285||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.286"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.286">3.286</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anantacitrasadgarbhasaṁsārasvapnasadmanaḥ|<br />
Ploṣakaḥ śiva evāhamityullāsī hutāśanaḥ||3.286||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.287"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.287">3.287</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jagatsarvaṁ mattaḥ prabhavati vibhedena bahudhā tathāpyetadrūḍhaṁ mayi vigalite tvatra na paraḥ|<br />
Taditthaṁ yaḥ sṛṣṭisthitivilayamekīkṛtivaśādanaṁśaṁ paśyetsa sphurati hi turīyaṁ padamitaḥ||3.287||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.288"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.288">3.288</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadasminparamopāye śāmbhavādvaitaśālini|<br />
Ke'pyeva yānti viśvāsaṁ parameśena bhāvitāḥ||3.288||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.289"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.289">3.289</a> <span class="pv-iast">Snānaṁ vrataṁ dehaśuddhirdhāraṇā mantrayojanā|<br />
Adhvakḷptiryāgavidhirhomajapyasamādhayaḥ||3.289||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.290"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.290">3.290</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ityādikalpanā kāpi nātra bhedena yujyate|<br />
Parānugrahakāritvamatrasthasya sphuṭaṁ sthitam||3.290||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.291"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.291">3.291</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yadi tādṛganugrāhyo daiśikasyopasarpati|<br />
Athāsau tādṛśo na syādbhavabhaktyā ca bhāvitaḥ||3.291||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.292"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.292">3.292</a> <span class="pv-iast">Taṁ cārādhayate bhāvitādṛśānugraheritaḥ|<br />
Tadā vicitraṁ dīkṣādividhiṁ śikṣeta kovidaḥ||3.292||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.293"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.293">3.293</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhāvinyo'pi hyupāsāstā atraivāyānti niṣṭhitim|<br />
Etanmayatvaṁ paramaṁ prāpyaṁ nirvarṇyate śivam||3.293||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v3.294"><a class="ta-num" href="#v3.294">3.294</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti kathitamidaṁ suvistaraṁ paramaṁ śāmbhavamātmavedanam||3.294||</span></p>
