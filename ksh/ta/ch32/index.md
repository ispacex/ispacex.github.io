# Глава 32 — Mudrikāvidhiḥ

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">67 строф · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [английский перевод у источника — русского там нет](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/en/node/903)</p>

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Глава 31 — Maṇḍalam](/ksh/ta/ch31/) · [Глава 33 — Ekīkāraḥ →](/ksh/ta/ch33/)</p>

<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloke dvātriṁśamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.1">32.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha kathaye mudrāṇāṁ gurvāgamagītamatra vidhim|<br />
Mudrā ca pratibimbātmā śrīmaddevyākhyayāmale|<br />
Uktā bimbodayaśrutyā vācyadvayavivecanāt||32.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.2">32.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bimbātsamudayo yasyā ityuktā pratibimbatā|<br />
Bimbasya yasyā udaya ityuktā tadupāyatā||32.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.3">32.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mudaṁ svarūpalābhākhyaṁ dehadvāreṇa cātmanām|<br />
Rātyarpayati yattena mudrā śāstreṣu varṇitā||32.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.4">32.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatra pradhānabhūtā śrīkhecarī devatātmikā|<br />
Niṣkalatvena vikhyātā sākalyena triśūlinī||32.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.5">32.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Karaṅkiṇī krodhanā ca bhairavī lelihānikā|<br />
Mahāpretā yogamudrā jvālinī kṣobhiṇī dhruvā||32.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.6">32.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ityevaṁ bahubhedeyaṁ śrīkhecaryeva gīyate|<br />
Anyāstadaṅgabhūtāstu padmādyā mālinīmate||32.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.7">32.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tāsāṁ bahutvāmukhyatvayogābhyāṁ neha varṇanam|<br />
Śrīkhecarīsamāviṣṭo yadyatsthānaṁ samāśrayet||32.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.8">32.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Devīsannidhaye tatsyādalaṁ kiṁ ḍambarairvṛthā|<br />
Kāmye karmaṇi tāśca syurmukhyāḥ kasyāpi jātucit||32.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.9">32.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tacca nāsmābhiruditaṁ tatkiṁ tadupayoginā|<br />
Mudrā caturvidhā kāyakaravākcittabhedataḥ||32.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.10">32.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatra pūrṇena rūpeṇa khecarīmeva varṇaye|<br />
Baddhvā padmāsanaṁ yogī nābhāvakṣeśvaraṁ kṣipet||32.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.11">32.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Daṇḍākāraṁ tu taṁ tāvannayedyāvatkakhatrayam|<br />
Nigṛhya tatra tattūrṇaṁ prerayetkhatrayeṇa tu||32.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.12">32.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Etāṁ baddhvā khe gatiḥ syāditi śrīpūrvaśāsane|<br />
Dhvanijyotirmarudyuktaṁ cittaṁ viśramya copari||32.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.13">32.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anenābhyāsayogena śivaṁ bhittvā paraṁ vrajet|<br />
Jatrvadhastātkarau kṛtvā vāmapādaṁ ca dakṣiṇe||32.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.14">32.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vidāryāsyaṁ kaniṣṭhābhyāṁ madhyamābhyāṁ tu nāsikām|<br />
Anāme kuñcayetprājño bhrūbhaṅgaṁ tarjanīdvayam||32.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.15">32.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jihvāṁ ca cālayenmantrī hāhākāraṁ ca kārayet|<br />
Triśūlena prayogeṇa brahmarandhramupasthitaḥ||32.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.16">32.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Padaṁ santyajya tanmātraṁ sadyastyajati medinīm|<br />
Śūnyāśūnyalaye kṛtvā ekadaṇḍe'nilānalau||32.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.17">32.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śaktitritayasambaddhe'dhiṣṭhātṛtridaivate|<br />
Triśūlaṁ tadvijānīyādyena vyomotpatedbudhaḥ||32.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.18">32.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ākāśabhāvaṁ santyajya sattāmātramupasthitaḥ|<br />
Śūlaṁ samarasaṁ kṛtvā rase rasa iva sthitaḥ||32.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.19">32.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ekadaṇḍaṁ sa vijñāya triśūlaṁ khacaraṁ priye|<br />
Baddhvā tu khecarīṁ mudrāṁ dhyātvātmānaṁ ca bhairavam||32.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.20">32.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Khecarīcakrasañjuṣṭaṁ sadyastyajati medinīm|<br />
Tyaktāṁśako nirācāro niḥśaṅko lokavarjitaḥ||32.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.21">32.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Avadhūto nirācāro nāhamasmīti bhāvayan|<br />
Mantraikaniṣṭhaḥ sampaśyandehasthāḥ sarvadevatāḥ||32.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.22">32.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Hlādodvegāsmitākruṣṭanidrāmaithunamatsare|<br />
Rūpādau vā kartṛkarmakaraṇeṣu ca sarvaśaḥ||32.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.23">32.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nāhamasmīti manvāna ekībhūtaṁ vicintayan|<br />
Karṇākṣimukhanāsādicakrasthaṁ devatāgaṇam||32.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.24">32.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Grahītāraṁ sadā paśyankhecaryā sidhyati sphuṭam|<br />
Vidyāśaṅkī malāśaṅkī śāstraśaṅkī na sidhyati||32.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.25">32.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śivo raviḥ śivo vahniḥ paktṛtvātsa purohitaḥ|<br />
Tatrasthā devatāḥ sarvā dyotayantyo'khilaṁ jagat||32.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.26">32.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kaniṣṭhābhyāṁ vidāryāsyaṁ tarjanībhyāṁ bhruvau tathā|<br />
Anāme madhyame vaktre jihvayā tālukaṁ spṛśet||32.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.27">32.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Eṣā karaṅkiṇī devī jvālinīṁ śṛṇu sāmpratam|<br />
Hanurlalāṭagau hastau prasāryāṅgulitaḥ sphuṭau||32.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.28">32.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Cālayedvāyuvegena kṛtvāntarbhrukuṭīṁ budhaḥ|<br />
Vidāryāsyaṁ sajihvaṁ ca hāhākāraṁ tu kārayet||32.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.29">32.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Eṣā jvālinyagnicakre tayā cāṣṭottaraṁ śatam|<br />
Japedyadi tataḥ siddhyettrailokyaṁ sacarācaram||32.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.30">32.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Paradeheṣu cātmānaṁ paraṁ cātmaśarīrataḥ|<br />
Paśyeccarantaṁ hānādādgamāgamapadasthitam||32.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.31">32.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Navacchidragataṁ caikaṁ nadantaṁ vyāpakaṁ dhruvam|<br />
Anayā hi khacārī śrīyogasañcāra ucyate||32.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.32">32.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kulakuṇḍalikāṁ buddhvāṇorantaravedinīm|<br />
Vāmo yo'yaṁ jagatyasmiṁstasya saṁharaṇodyatām||32.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.33">32.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svasthāne nirvṛtiṁ labdhvā jñānāmṛtarasātmakam|<br />
Vrajetkandapadaṁ madhye rāvaṁ kṛtvā hyarāvakam||32.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.34">32.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yāvajjīvaṁ catuṣkoṇaṁ piṇḍādhāraṁ ca kāmikam|<br />
Tatra tāṁ bodhayitvā tu gatiṁ buddhvā kramāgatām||32.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.35">32.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Cakrobhayanibaddhāṁ tu śākhāprāntāvalambinīm|<br />
Mūlasthānādyathā yogī tamogranthiṁ vidārayet||32.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.36">32.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vajrākhyāṁ jñānajenaiva tathā śākhobhayāntataḥ|<br />
Koṇamadhyaviniṣkrāntaṁ liṅgamūlaṁ vibhedayet||32.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.37">32.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatra saṅghaṭṭitaṁ cakrayugmamaikyena bhāsate|<br />
Vaiparītyāttu nikṣipya dvidhābhāvaṁ vrajatyataḥ||32.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.38">32.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ūrvādyaṅguṣṭhakālāgniparyante sā vinikṣipet|<br />
Gamāgamanasañcāre caretsā liṅgaliṅginī||32.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.39">32.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatra tatpadasaṁyogādunmīlanavidhāyinī|<br />
Yo jānāti sa sidhyettu rasādānavisargayoḥ||32.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.40">32.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sasaṅgamamidaṁ sthānamūrmiṇyunmīlanaṁ param|<br />
Eṣa kramastato'nyo'pi vyutkramaḥ khecarī parā||32.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.41">32.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yonyādhāreti vikhyātā śūlamūleti śabdyate|<br />
Varṇāstatra layaṁ yānti hyavarṇe varṇarūpiṇi||32.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.42">32.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nādiphāntaṁ samuccārya kauleśaṁ dehasannibham|<br />
Ākramya prathamaṁ cakraṁ khe yantre pādapīḍitam||32.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.43">32.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nādaṁ vai śaktisadgarbhaṁ sadgarbhātkaulinīpadam|<br />
Bījapañcakacāreṇa śūlabhedakrameṇa tu||32.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.44">32.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Hṛcchūlagranthibhedaiścidrudraśaktiṁ prabodhayet|<br />
Vāyucakrāntanilayaṁ bindvākhyaṁ nābhimaṇḍalam||32.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.45">32.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Āgacchellambikāsthānaṁ sūtradvādaśanirgatam|<br />
Candracakravilomena praviśedbhūtapañjare||32.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.46">32.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhūyastu kurute līlāṁ māyāpañjaravartinīm|<br />
Punaḥ sṛṣṭiḥ saṁhṛtiśca khecaryā kriyate budhaiḥ||32.46||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.47">32.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīmadvīrāvalīyoga eṣa syātkhecarīvidhiḥ|<br />
Cumbākāreṇa vaktreṇa yattattvaṁ śrūyate param||32.47||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.48">32.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Grasamānamidaṁ viśvaṁ candrārkapuṭasampuṭe|<br />
Tenaiva syātkhagāmīti śrīmatkāmika ucyate||32.48||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.49">32.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhavānmuktvā drāvayanti pāśānmudrā hi śaktayaḥ|<br />
Mukhyāsāṁ khecarī sā ca tridhoccāreṇa vācikī||32.49||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.50"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.50">32.50</a> <span class="pv-iast">Triśiromudgaro devi kāyikī paripaṭhyate|<br />
Nāsāṁ netradvayaṁ cāpi hṛtstanadvayameva ca||32.50||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.51"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.51">32.51</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vṛṣaṇadvayaliṅgaṁ ca prāpya kāyaṁ gatā tviyam|<br />
Bhavasthānābhavasthānamuccāreṇāvadhārayet||32.51||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.52"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.52">32.52</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mānasīyamitastvanyāḥ padmādyā aṣṭa mudrikāḥ|<br />
Mātṛvyūhakule tāḥ syurasyāstu parivāragāḥ||32.52||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.53"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.53">32.53</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śarīraṁ tu samastaṁ yatkūṭākṣarasamākṛti|<br />
Eṣā mudrā mahāmudrā bhairavasyeti gahvare||32.53||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.54"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.54">32.54</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sūpaviṣṭaḥ padmake tu hastāgrāṅguliraśmibhiḥ|<br />
Parāṅmukhairjhaṭityudyadraśmibhiḥ pṛṣṭhasaṁsthitaiḥ||32.54||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.55"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.55">32.55</a> <span class="pv-iast">Antaḥsthitiḥ khecarīyaṁ saṅkocākhyā śaśāṅkinī|<br />
Tasmādeva samuttamya bāhū caivāvakuñcitau||32.55||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.56"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.56">32.56</a> <span class="pv-iast">Samyagvyomasu saṁsthānādvyomākhyā khecarī matā|<br />
Muṣṭidvitayasaṅghaṭṭāddhṛdi sā hṛdayāhvayā||32.56||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.57"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.57">32.57</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śāntākhyā sā hastayugmamūrdhvādhaḥ sthitamudgatam|<br />
Samadṛṣṭyāvalokyaṁ ca bahiryojitapāṇikam||32.57||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.58"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.58">32.58</a> <span class="pv-iast">Eṣaiva śaktimudrā cedadhodhāvitapāṇikā|<br />
Daśānāmaṅgulīnāṁ tu muṣṭibandhādanantaram||32.58||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.59"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.59">32.59</a> <span class="pv-iast">Drākkṣepātkhecarī devī pañcakuṇḍalinī matā|<br />
Saṁhāramudrā caiṣaiva yadyūrdhvaṁ kṣipyate kila||32.59||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.60"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.60">32.60</a> <span class="pv-iast">Utkrāmaṇī jhagityeva paśūnāṁ pāśakartarī|<br />
Śvabhre sudūre jhaṭiti svātmānaṁ pātayanniva||32.60||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.61"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.61">32.61</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sāhasānupraveśena kuñcitaṁ hastayugmakam|<br />
Adhovīkṣaṇaśīlaṁ ca samyagdṛṣṭisamanvitam||32.61||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.62"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.62">32.62</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vīrabhairavasañjñeyaṁ khecarī bodhavardhinī|<br />
Aṣṭadhetthaṁ varṇitā śrībhargāṣṭakaśikhākule||32.62||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.63"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.63">32.63</a> <span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ nānāvidhānbhedānāśrityaikaiva yā sthitā|<br />
Śrīkhecarī tayāviṣṭaḥ paraṁ bījaṁ prapadyate||32.63||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.64"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.64">32.64</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ekaṁ sṛṣṭimayaṁ bījaṁ yadvīryaṁ sarvamantragam|<br />
Ekā mudrā khecarī ca mudraughaḥ prāṇito yayā||32.64||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.65"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.65">32.65</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadevaṁ khecarīcakrarūḍhau yadrūpamullaset|<br />
Tadeva mudrā mantavyā śeṣaḥ syāddehavikriyā||32.65||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.66"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.66">32.66</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yāgādau tanmadhye tadavasitau jñānayogaparimarśe|<br />
Vighnapraśame pāśacchede mudrāvidheḥ samayaḥ||32.66||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v32.67"><a class="ta-num" href="#v32.67">32.67</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bodhāveśaḥ sannidhiraikyena visarjanaṁ svarūpagatiḥ|<br />
Śaṅkādalanaṁ cakrodayadīptiriti kramātkṛtyam||32.67||</span></p>
