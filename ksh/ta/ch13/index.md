# Глава 13 — Śaktipātatirohitī

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">361 строфа · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [перевод главы у источника](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/ru/node/875)</p>

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Глава 12 — Adhvopayogaḥ](/ksh/ta/ch12/) · [Глава 14 — Dīkṣopakramaṇam →](/ksh/ta/ch14/)</p>

<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloke trayodaśamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.1">13.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Athādhikṛtibhājanaṁ ka iha vā kathaṁ vetyalaṁ vivecayitumucyate vividhaśaktipātakramaḥ||13.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.2">13.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatra kecidiha prāhuḥ śaktipāta imaṁ vidhim|<br />
Taṁ pradarśya nirākṛtya svamataṁ darśayiṣyate||13.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.3">13.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatredaṁ dṛśyamānaṁ satsukhaduḥkhavimohabhāk|<br />
Viṣamaṁ sattathābhūtaṁ samaṁ hetuṁ prakalpayet||13.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.4">13.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">So'vyaktaṁ tacca sattvādinānārūpamacetanam|<br />
Ghaṭādivatkāryamiti hetureko'sya sā niśā||13.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.5">13.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sā jaḍā kāryatādrūpyātkāryaṁ cāsyāṁ sadeva hi|<br />
Kalādidharaṇīprāntaṁ jāḍyātsā sūtaye'kṣamā||13.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.6">13.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Teneśaḥ kṣobhayedenāṁ kṣobho'syāḥ sūtiyogyatā|<br />
Puṁsaḥ prati ca sā bhogyaṁ sūte'nādīnpṛthagvidhān||13.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.7">13.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Puṁsaśca nirviśeṣatve muktāṇūnprati kiṁ na tat|<br />
Nimittaṁ karmasaṁskāraḥ sa ca teṣu na vidyate||13.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.8">13.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti cetkarmasaṁskārābhāvasteṣāṁ kutaḥ kila|<br />
Na bhogādanyakarmāṁśaprasaṅgo hi duratyayaḥ||13.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.9">13.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yugapatkarmaṇāṁ bhogo na ca yuktaḥ krameṇa hi|<br />
Phaledyatkarma tatkasmātsvaṁ rūpaṁ santyajetkvacit||13.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.10">13.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jñānātkarmakṣayaścettatkuta īśvaracoditāt|<br />
Dharmādyadi kutaḥ so'pi karmataścettaducyatām||13.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.11">13.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nahi karmāsti tādṛkṣaṁ yena jñānaṁ pravartate|<br />
Karmajatve ca tajjñānaṁ phalarāśau pateddhruvam||13.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.12">13.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anyakarmaphalaṁ prācyaṁ karmarāśiṁ ca kiṁ dahet|<br />
Īśasya dveṣarāgādiśūnyasyāpi kathaṁ kvacit||13.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.13">13.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathābhisandhirnānyatra bhedahetorabhāvataḥ|<br />
Nanvitthaṁ pradahejjñānaṁ karmajālāni karma hi||13.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.14">13.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ajñānasahakārīdaṁ sūte svargādikaṁ phalam|<br />
Ajñānaṁ jñānato naśyedanyakarmaphalādapi||13.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.15">13.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Upavāsādikaṁ cānyadduṣṭakarmaphalaṁ bhavet|<br />
Niṣphalīkurute duṣṭaṁ karmetyaṅgīkṛtaṁ kila||13.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.16">13.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ajñānamiti yatproktaṁ jñānābhāvaḥ sa cetsa kim|<br />
Prāgabhāvo'thavā dhvaṁsa ādye kiṁ sarvasaṁvidām||13.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.17">13.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kasyāpi vātha jñānasya prācyaḥ pakṣastvasambhavī|<br />
Na kiñcidyasya vijñānamudapādi tathāvidhaḥ||13.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.18">13.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nāṇurasti bhave hyasminnanādau ko'nvayaṁ kramaḥ|<br />
Bhāvinaḥ prāgabhāvaśca jñānasyeti sthite sati||13.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.19">13.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Muktāṇorapi so'styeva janmataḥ prāgasau na ca|<br />
Jñānaṁ bhāvi vimukte'sminniti ceccarcyatāmidam||13.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.20">13.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kasmājjñānaṁ na bhāvyatra nanu dehādyajanmataḥ|<br />
Tatkasmātkarmaṇaḥ kṣaiṇyāttatkuto'jñānahānitaḥ||13.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.21">13.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ajñānasya kathaṁ hāniḥ prāgabhāve hi saṁvidaḥ|<br />
Ajñānaṁ prāgabhāvo'sau na bhāvyutpattyasambhavāt||13.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.22">13.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kasmānna bhāvi tajjñānaṁ nanu dehādyajanmataḥ|<br />
Ityeṣa sarvapakṣaghno niśitaścakrakabhramaḥ||13.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.23">13.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha pradhvaṁsa evedamajñānaṁ tatsadā sthitam|<br />
Muktāṇuṣviti teṣvastu māyākāryavijṛmbhitam||13.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.24">13.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Athājñānaṁ nahyabhāvo mithyājñānaṁ tu tanmatam|<br />
Tadeva karmaṇāṁ svasminkartavye sahakāraṇam||13.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.25">13.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vaktavyaṁ tarhi kiṁ karma yadā sūte svakaṁ phalam|<br />
Tadaiva mithyājñānena satā hetutvamāpyate||13.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.26">13.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha yasminkṣaṇe karma kṛtaṁ tatra svarūpasat|<br />
Mithyājñānaṁ yadi tatastādṛśātkarmaṇaḥ phalam||13.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.27">13.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prākpakṣe pralaye vṛtte prācyasṛṣṭipravartane|<br />
Dehādyabhāvānno mithyājñānasya kvāpi sambhavaḥ||13.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.28">13.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uttarasminpunaḥ pakṣe yadā yadyena yatra vā|<br />
Kriyate karma tatsarvamajñānasacivaṁ tadā||13.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.29">13.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Avaśyamiti kasyāpi na karmaprakṣayo bhavet|<br />
Yadyapi jñānavānbhūtvā vidhatte karma kiñcana||13.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.30">13.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Viphalaṁ syāttu tatpūrvakarmarāśau tu kā gatiḥ|<br />
Atha pralayakāle'pi citsvabhāvatvayogataḥ||13.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.31">13.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aṇūnāṁ sambhavatyeva jñānaṁ mithyeti tatkutaḥ|<br />
Svabhāvāditi cenmukte śive vā kiṁ tathā na hi||13.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.32">13.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yaccādarśanamākhyātaṁ nimittaṁ pariṇāmini|<br />
Pradhāne taddhi saṅkīrṇavaiviktyobhayayogataḥ||13.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.33">13.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Darśanāya pumarthaikayogyatāsacivaṁ dhiyaḥ|<br />
Ārabhya sūte dharaṇīparyantaṁ tatra yaccitaḥ||13.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.34">13.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Buddhivṛttyaviśiṣṭatvaṁ puṁsprakāśaprasādataḥ|<br />
Prakāśanāddhiyo'rthena saha bhogaḥ sa bhaṇyate||13.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.35">13.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Buddhirevāsmi vikṛtidharmikānyastu ko'pyasau|<br />
Madvilakṣaṇa ekātmetyevaṁ vaiviktyasaṁvidi||13.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.36">13.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pumarthasya kṛtatvena sahakāriviyogataḥ|<br />
Taṁ pumāṁsaṁ prati naiva sūte kintvanyameva hi||13.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.37">13.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atra puṁso'tha mūlasya dharmo'darśanatā dvayoḥ|<br />
Athaveti vikalpo'yamāstāmetattu bhaṇyatām||13.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.38">13.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhogo vivekaparyanta iti yattatra ko'vadhiḥ|<br />
Vivekalābhe nikhilasūtidṛgyadi sāpi kim||13.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.39">13.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sāmānyena viśeṣairvā prācye syādekajanmataḥ|<br />
Uttare na kadācitsyādbhāvikālasya yogataḥ||13.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.40">13.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kaiścideva viśeṣaiścetsarveṣāṁ yugapadbhavet|<br />
Viveko'nādisaṁyogātkā hyanyonyavicitratā||13.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.41">13.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasmātsāṅkhyadṛśāpīdamajñānaṁ naiva yujyate|<br />
Ajñānena vinā bandhamokṣau naiva vyavasthayā||13.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.42">13.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yujyete tacca kathitayuktibhirnopapadyate|<br />
Māyākarmāṇudevecchāsadbhāve'pi sthite tataḥ||13.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.43">13.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na bandhamokṣayoryogo bhedahetorasambhavāt|<br />
Tasmādajñānaśabdena jñatvakartṛtvadharmāṇām||13.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.44">13.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Cidaṇūnāmāvaraṇaṁ kiñcidvācyaṁ vipaścitā|<br />
Āvāraṇātmanā siddhaṁ tatsvarūpādabhedavat||13.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.45">13.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhede pramāṇābhāvācca tadekaṁ nikhilātmasu|<br />
Tacca kasmātprasūtaṁ syānmāyātaścetkathaṁ nu sā||13.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.46">13.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kvacideva suvītaitanna tu muktātmanītyayam|<br />
Prācyaḥ paryanuyogaḥ syānnimittaṁ cenna labhyate||13.46||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.47">13.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Utpattyabhāvatastena nityaṁ na ca vinaśyati|<br />
Tata evaikatāyāṁ cānyātmasādhāraṇatvataḥ||13.47||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.48">13.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na vāvastvarthakāritvānna cittatsaṁvṛtitvataḥ|<br />
Na caitenātmanāṁ yogo hetumāṁstadasambhavāt||13.48||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.49">13.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tenaikaṁ vastu sannityaṁ nityasambaddhamātmabhiḥ|<br />
Jaḍaṁ malaṁ tadajñānaṁ saṁsārāṅkurakāraṇam||13.49||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.50"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.50">13.50</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasya roddhrī yadā śaktirudāste śivaraśmibhiḥ|<br />
Tadāṇuḥ spṛśyate spṛṣṭaḥ svake jñānakriye sphuṭe||13.50||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.51"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.51">13.51</a> <span class="pv-iast">Samāviśedayaṁ sūryakānto'rkeṇeva coditaḥ|<br />
Roddhryāśca śaktermādhyasthyatāratamyavaśakramāt||13.51||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.52"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.52">13.52</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vicitratvamataḥ prāhurabhivyaktau svasaṁvidaḥ|<br />
Sa eṣa śaktipātākhyaḥ śāstreṣu paribhāṣyate||13.52||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.53"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.53">13.53</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atrocyate malastāvaditthameṣa na yujyate|<br />
Iti pūrvāhnike proktaṁ punaruktau tu kiṁ phalam||13.53||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.54"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.54">13.54</a> <span class="pv-iast">Malasya pākaḥ ko'yaṁ syānnāśaśceditarātmanām|<br />
Sa eko mala ityukternairmalyamanuṣajyate||13.54||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.55"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.55">13.55</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha pratyātmaniyato'nādiśca prāgabhāvavat|<br />
Malo naśyettathāpyeṣa nāśo yadi sahetukaḥ||13.55||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.56"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.56">13.56</a> <span class="pv-iast">Hetuḥ karmeśvarecchā vā karma tāvanna tādṛśam|<br />
Īśvarecchā svatantrā ca kvacideva tathaiva kim||13.56||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.57"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.57">13.57</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ahetuko'sya nāśaścetprāgevaiṣa vinaśyatu|<br />
Kṣaṇāntaraṁ sadṛksūte iti cetsthirataiva sā||13.57||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.58"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.58">13.58</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na ca nityasya bhāvasya hetvanāyattajanmanaḥ|<br />
Nāśo dṛṣṭaḥ prāgabhāvastvavastviti tathāstu saḥ||13.58||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.59"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.59">13.59</a> <span class="pv-iast">Athāsya pāko nāmaiṣa svaśaktipratibaddhatā|<br />
Sarvānprati tathaiṣa syādruddhaśaktirviṣāgnivat||13.59||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.60"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.60">13.60</a> <span class="pv-iast">Punarudbhūtaśaktau ca svakāryaṁ syādviṣāgnivat|<br />
Muktā api na muktāḥ syuḥ śaktiṁ cāsya na manmahe||13.60||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.61"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.61">13.61</a> <span class="pv-iast">Roddhrīti cetkasya nṛṇāṁ jñatvakartṛtvayoryadi|<br />
Sadbhāvamātrādroddhṛtve śivamuktāṇvasambhavaḥ||13.61||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.62"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.62">13.62</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sannidhānātiriktaṁ ca na kiñcitkurute malaḥ|<br />
Ātmanāṁ pariṇāmitvādanityatvaprasaṅgataḥ||13.62||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.63"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.63">13.63</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jñatvakartṛtvamātraṁ ca pudgalā na tadāśrayāḥ|<br />
Taccedāvāritaṁ hanta rūpanāśaḥ prasajyate||13.63||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.64"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.64">13.64</a> <span class="pv-iast">Āvāraṇaṁ cādṛśyatvaṁ na ca tadvastuno'nyatām|<br />
Karoti ghaṭavajjñānaṁ nāvarītuṁ ca śakyate||13.64||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.65"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.65">13.65</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jñānenāvaraṇīyena tadevāvaraṇaṁ katham|<br />
Na jñāyate tathā ca syādāvṛtirnāmamātrataḥ||13.65||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.66"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.66">13.66</a> <span class="pv-iast">Roddhryāśca śakteḥ kastasya pratibandhaka īśvaraḥ|<br />
Yadyapekṣāvirahitastatra prāgdattamuttaram||13.66||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.67"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.67">13.67</a> <span class="pv-iast">Karmasāmyamapekṣyātha tasyecchā sampravartate|<br />
Tasyāpi rūpaṁ vaktavyaṁ samatā karmaṇāṁ hi kā||13.67||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.68"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.68">13.68</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhogaparyāyamāhātmyātkāle kvāpi phalaṁ prati|<br />
Virodhātkarmaṇī ruddhe tiṣṭhataḥ sāmyamīdṛśam||13.68||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v13.69"><a class="ta-num" href="#v13.69">13.69</a> <span class="pv-iast">Taṁ ca kālāṁśakaṁ devaḥ sarvajño vīkṣya taṁ malam|<br />
Runddhe lakṣyaḥ sa kālaśca sukhaduḥkhādivarjanaiḥ||13.69||</span></p>
