# Глава 15 — Dīkṣā sāmayī

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">615 строф · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [перевод главы у источника](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/ru/node/879)</p>

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Глава 14 — Dīkṣopakramaṇam](/ksh/ta/ch14/) · [Глава 16 — Pautrikavidhiḥ →](/ksh/ta/ch16/)</p>

<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloke pañcadaśamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.1">15.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Athaitadupayogāya yāgastāvannirūpyate|<br />
Tatra dīkṣaiva bhoge ca muktau cāyātyupāyatām||15.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.2">15.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svayaṁ saṁskārayogādvā tadaṅgaṁ tatpradarśyate|<br />
Yo yatrābhilaṣedbhogānsa tatraiva niyojitaḥ||15.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.3">15.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Siddhibhāṅmantraśaktyeti śrīmatsvāyambhuve vibhuḥ|<br />
Yogyatāvaśato yatra vāsanā yasya tatra saḥ||15.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.4">15.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yojyo na cyavate tasmāditi śrīmālinīmate|<br />
Vadanbhogādyupāyatvaṁ dīkṣāyāḥ prāha no guruḥ||15.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.5">15.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na cādhikāritā dīkṣāṁ vinā yoge'sti śāṅkare|<br />
Na ca yogādhikāritvamekamevānayā bhavet||15.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.6">15.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Api mantrādhikāritvaṁ muktiśca śivadīkṣayā|<br />
Ityasminmālinīvākye sākṣānmokṣābhyupāyatā||15.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.7">15.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dīkṣāyāḥ kathitā prācyagranthena punarucyate|<br />
Pāramparyeṇa saṁskṛtyā mokṣabhogābhyupāyatā||15.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.8">15.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yeṣāmadhyavasāyo'sti na vidyāṁ pratyaśaktitaḥ|<br />
Sukhopāyamidaṁ teṣāṁ vidhānamuditaṁ guroḥ||15.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.9">15.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti śrīmanmataṅgākhye hyuktā mokṣābhyupāyatā|<br />
Samyagjñānasvabhāvā hi vidyā sākṣādvimocikā||15.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.10">15.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktaṁ tatraiva tattvānāṁ kāryakāraṇabhāvataḥ|<br />
Heyādeyatvakathane vidyāpāda iti sphuṭam||15.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.11">15.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatrāśaktāstu ye teṣāṁ dīkṣācaryāsamādhayaḥ|<br />
Te vidyāpūrvakā yasmāttasmājjñānyuttamottamaḥ||15.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.12">15.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jñānaṁ ca śāstrāttaccāpi śrāvyo nādīkṣito yataḥ|<br />
Ato'sya saṁskriyāmātropayogo dīkṣayā kṛtaḥ||15.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.13">15.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yatra tatrāstu guruṇā yojito'sau phalaṁ punaḥ|<br />
Svavijñānocitaṁ yāti jñānītyuktaṁ purā kila||15.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.14">15.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yasya tvīśaprasādena divyā kācana yogyatā|<br />
Guroḥ śiśośca tau naiva prati dīkṣopayogitā||15.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.15">15.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jñānameva tadā dīkṣā śrītraiśikanirūpaṇāt|<br />
Sarvaśāstrārthavettṛtvamakasmāccāsya jāyate||15.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.16">15.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti śrīmālinīnītyā yaḥ sāṁsiddhikasaṁvidaḥ|<br />
Sa uttamādhikārī syājjñānavānhi gururmataḥ||15.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.17">15.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ātmane vā parebhyo vā hitārthī cetayedidam|<br />
Ityuktyā mālinīśāstre tatsarvaṁ prakaṭīkṛtam||15.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.18">15.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jñānayogyāstathā keciccaryāyogyāstathāpare|<br />
Dīkṣāyogyā yogayogyā iti śrīkairaṇe vidhau||15.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.19">15.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatroktalakṣaṇaḥ karmayogajñānaviśāradaḥ|<br />
Uttarottaratābhūmyutkṛṣṭo gururudīritaḥ||15.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.20">15.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sa ca prāguktaśaktyanyatamapātapavitritam|<br />
Parīkṣya pṛṣṭvā vā śiṣyaṁ dīkṣākarma samācaret||15.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.21">15.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktaṁ svacchandaśāstre ca śiṣyaṁ pṛcchedguruḥ svayam|<br />
Phalaṁ prārthayase yādṛktādṛksādhanamārabhe||15.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.22">15.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vāsanābhedataḥ sādhyaprāptirmantrapracoditā|<br />
Mantramudrādhvadravyāṇāṁ home sādhāraṇā sthitiḥ||15.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.23">15.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vāsanābhedato bhinnaṁ śiṣyāṇāṁ ca guroḥ phalam|<br />
Sādhako dvividhaḥ śaivadharmī lokojjhitasthitiḥ||15.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.24">15.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Lokadharmī phalākāṅkṣī śubhasthaścāśubhojjhitaḥ|<br />
Dvidhā mumukṣurnirbījaḥ samayādivivarjitaḥ||15.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.25">15.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bālabāliśavṛddhastrībhogabhugvyādhitādikaḥ|<br />
Anyaḥ sabījo yasyetthaṁ dīkṣoktā śivaśāsane||15.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.26">15.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vidvaddvandvasahānāṁ tu sabījā samayātmikā|<br />
Dīkṣānugrāhikā pālyā viśeṣasamayāstu taiḥ||15.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.27">15.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Abhāvaṁ bhāvayetsamyakkarmaṇāṁ prācyabhāvinām|<br />
Mumukṣornirapekṣasya prārabdhrekaṁ na śodhayet||15.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.28">15.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sādhakasya tu bhūtyarthamitthameva viśodhayet|<br />
Śivadharmiṇyasau dīkṣā lokadharmāpahāriṇī||15.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.29">15.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Adharmarūpiṇāmeva na śubhānāṁ tu śodhanam|<br />
Lokadharmiṇyasau dīkṣā mantrārādhanavarjitā||15.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.30">15.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prārabdhadehabhede tu bhuṅkte'sāvaṇimādikān|<br />
Bhuktvordhvaṁ yāti yatraiṣa yukto'tha sakale'kale||15.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.31">15.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Samayācārapāśaṁ tu nirbījāyāṁ viśodhayet|<br />
Dīkṣāmātreṇa muktiḥ syādbhaktyā deve gurau sadā||15.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.32">15.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sadyonirvāṇadā seyaṁ nirbījā yeti bhaṇyate|<br />
Atītānāgatārabdhapāśatrayaviyojikā||15.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.33">15.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dīkṣāvasāne śuddhasya dehatyāge paraṁ padam|<br />
Dehatyāge sabījāyāṁ karmābhāvādvipadyate||15.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.34">15.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Samayācārapāśaṁ tu dīkṣitaḥ pālayetsadā|<br />
Evaṁ pṛṣṭvā parijñāya vicārya ca guruḥ svayam||15.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.35">15.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ucitāṁ saṁvidhitsustāṁ vāsanāṁ tādṛśīṁ śrayet|<br />
Āyātaśaktipātasya dīkṣāṁ prati na daiśikaḥ||15.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.36">15.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Avajñāṁ vidadhīteti śambhunājñā nirūpitā|<br />
Svadhanena daridrasya kuryāddīkṣāṁ guruḥ svayam||15.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.37">15.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Api dūrvāmbubhiryadvā dīkṣāyai bhikṣate śiśuḥ|<br />
Bhikṣopāttaṁ nijaṁ vātha dhanaṁ prāggurave śiśuḥ||15.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.38">15.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dadyādyena viśuddhaṁ tadyāgayogyatvamaśnute|<br />
Tatrādau śivatāpattisvātantryāveśa eva yaḥ||15.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.39">15.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sa eva hi guruḥ kāryastato'sau dīkṣaṇe kṣamaḥ|<br />
Śivatāveśitā cāsya bahūpāyā pradarśitā||15.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.40">15.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kramikā bāhyarūpā tu snānanyāsārcanādibhiḥ|<br />
Bahvīṣu tāsu tāsveṣa kriyāsu śivatāṁ hṛdi||15.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.41">15.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sandadhaddṛḍhamabhyeti śivabhāvaṁ prasannadhīḥ|<br />
Śivībhūto yadyadicchettattatkartuṁ samīhate||15.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.42">15.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śivābhimānitopāyo bāhyo heturna mokṣadaḥ|<br />
Śivo'yaṁ śiva evāsmītyevamācāryaśiṣyayoḥ||15.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.43">15.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Hetutadvattayā dārḍhyābhimāno mocako hyaṇoḥ|<br />
Nādhyātmena vinā bāhyaṁ nādhyātmaṁ bāhyavarjitam||15.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.44">15.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Siddhyejjñānakriyābhyāṁ taddvitīyaṁ samprakāśate|<br />
Śrībrahmayāmale deva iti tena nyarūpayat||15.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.45">15.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīmadānandaśāstre ca nāśuddhiḥ syādvipaścitaḥ|<br />
Kintu snānaṁ suvastratvaṁ tuṣṭisañjananaṁ bhavet||15.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.46">15.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatra prasiddhadehādimātṛnirmalatākramāt|<br />
Ayatnato'ntarantaḥ syānnairmalyaṁ snāyatāṁ tataḥ||15.46||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.47">15.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Snānaṁ ca devadevasya yanmūrtyaṣṭakamucyate|<br />
Tatraivaṁ mantradīpte'ntarmaladāhe nimajjanam||15.47||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.48">15.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatreṣṭamantrahṛdayo gorajo'ntaḥ padatrayam|<br />
Gatvāgatya bhajetsnānaṁ pārthivaṁ dhṛtidāyakam||15.48||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.49">15.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Astramantritamṛddhūtamalaḥ pañcāṅgamantritaiḥ|<br />
Jalairmūrdhādipādāntaṁ kramādākṣālayettataḥ||15.49||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.50"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.50">15.50</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nimajjetsāṅgamūlākhyaṁ japannā tanmayatvataḥ|<br />
Utthāyāśeṣasajjyotirdevatāgarbhamambare||15.50||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.51"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.51">15.51</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sūryaṁ jalena mālinyā tarpayedviśvatarpakam|<br />
Devānpitṝnmunīnyakṣānrakṣāṁsyanyacca bhautikam||15.51||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.52"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.52">15.52</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sarvaṁ santarpayetprāṇo vīryātmā sa ca bhāskaraḥ|<br />
Tato japetparāmekāṁ prāguktoccārayogataḥ||15.52||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.53"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.53">15.53</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ā tanmayatvasaṁvitterjalasnānamidaṁ matam|<br />
Agnyutthaṁ bhasma śastreṇa japtvā malanibarhaṇam||15.53||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.54"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.54">15.54</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kavaktrahṛdguhyapade pañcāṅgairbhasma mantritam|<br />
Bhasmamuṣṭiṁ sāṅgamūlajaptāṁ mūrdhni kṣipettataḥ||15.54||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.55"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.55">15.55</a> <span class="pv-iast">Hastapādau jalenaiva prakṣālyācamanādikam|<br />
Tarpaṇaṁ japa ityevaṁ bhasmasnānaṁ hi taijasam||15.55||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.56"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.56">15.56</a> <span class="pv-iast">Gorajovatyanudrikte vāyau hlādini mantravāk|<br />
Gatyāgatiprayoge vā vāyavyaṁ snānamācaret||15.56||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.57"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.57">15.57</a> <span class="pv-iast">Amale gagane vyāpinyekāgrībhūtadṛṣṭikaḥ|<br />
Smaranmantraṁ yadāsīta kānyā nirmalatā tataḥ||15.57||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.58"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.58">15.58</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yadi vā nirmalādvyomnaḥ patatā vāriṇā tanum|<br />
Sparśayenmantrajapayuṅ nābhasaṁ snānamīdṛśam||15.58||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.59"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.59">15.59</a> <span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ somārkatejaḥsu śivabhāvena bhāvanāt|<br />
Nimajjandhautamālinyaḥ kva vā yogyo na jāyate||15.59||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.60"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.60">15.60</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ātmaiva parameśāno nirācāramahāhradaḥ|<br />
Viśvaṁ nimajjya tatraiva tiṣṭhecchuddhaśca śodhakaḥ||15.60||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.61"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.61">15.61</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti snānāṣṭakaṁ śuddhāvuttarottaramuttamam|<br />
Sarvatra paścāttaṁ mantramekībhūtamupāharet||15.61||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.62"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.62">15.62</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dhṛtyāpyāyamalaploṣavīryavyāptisṛjisthitīḥ|<br />
Abhedaṁ ca kramādeti snānāṣṭakaparo muniḥ||15.62||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.63"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.63">15.63</a> <span class="pv-iast">Etā hyanugrahātmāno mūrtayo'ṣṭau śivātmikāḥ|<br />
Svarūpaśivarūpābhyāṁ dhyānāttattatphalapradāḥ||15.63||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.64"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.64">15.64</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anena vidhinārcāyāṁ kandādhārādiyojanām|<br />
Kurvanvyāsasamāsābhyāṁ dharādestatphalaṁ bhajet||15.64||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.65"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.65">15.65</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathā hi yogasañcāre mantrāḥ syurbhuvi pārthivāḥ|<br />
Āpye āpyā yāvadamī śive śivamayā iti||15.65||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.66"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.66">15.66</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīnirmaryādaśāstre'pi taditthaṁ sunirūpitam|<br />
Dharādeśca viśeṣo'sti vīrasādhakasammataḥ||15.66||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.67"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.67">15.67</a> <span class="pv-iast">Raṇareṇurvīrajalaṁ vīrabhasma mahāmarut|<br />
Śmaśānāraṇyagaganaṁ candrārkau tadupāhitau||15.67||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.68"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.68">15.68</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ātmā nirdhūtaniḥśeṣavikalpātaṅkasusthitaḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.69"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.69">15.69</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīmattriśirasi proktaṁ madyaśīdhusurādinā|<br />

Āgatasya ca mantrasya na kuryāttarpaṇaṁ yadi||15.564||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.565"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.565">15.565</a> <span class="pv-iast">Haratyardhaśarīraṁ tadityūce bhagavānyataḥ|<br />
Śrīmadūrmau ca devīnāṁ vīrāṇāṁ ceṣṭitaṁ na vai||15.565||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.566"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.566">15.566</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prathayenna jugupseta vadennādravyapāṇikaḥ|<br />
Śrīpūrvaṁ nāma vaktavyaṁ gurordravyakareṇa ca||15.566||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.567"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.567">15.567</a> <span class="pv-iast">Gurvādīnāṁ na laṅghyā ca chāyā na dvaitibhiḥ saha|<br />
Jalpaṁ kurvansvaśāstrārthaṁ vadennāpi ca sūcayet||15.567||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.568"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.568">15.568</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nityāṁ viśeṣapūjāṁ ca kuryānnaimittike vidhau|<br />
Tathāpi madhye varṣasya tathāpi hi pavitrake||15.568||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.569"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.569">15.569</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anyastamantro nāsīta sevyaṁ śāstrāntaraṁ ca no|<br />
Aprarūḍhaṁ hi vijñānaṁ kampetetarabhāvanāt||15.569||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.570"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.570">15.570</a> <span class="pv-iast">Gṛhopaskaraṇāstrāṇi devatāyāgayogataḥ|<br />
Arcyānīti na padbhyāṁ vai spṛśennāpi vilaṅghayet||15.570||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.571"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.571">15.571</a> <span class="pv-iast">Guruvarge gṛhāyāte viśeṣaṁ kañcidācaret|<br />
Dīkṣitānāṁ na nindādi kuryādvidveṣapūrvakam||15.571||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.572"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.572">15.572</a> <span class="pv-iast">Upadeśāya no doṣaḥ sa hyavidveṣapūrvakaḥ|<br />
Na vaiṣṇavādikādhaḥsthadṛṣṭibhiḥ saṁvasedalam||15.572||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.573"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.573">15.573</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sahabhojanaśayyādyairnaiṣāṁ prakaṭayetsthitim|<br />
Uktaṁ śrīmādhavakule śāsanāntarasaṁsthitān||15.573||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.574"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.574">15.574</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vedoktiṁ vaiṣṇavoktiṁ ca tairuktaṁ varjayetsadā|<br />
Akulīneṣu samparkāttatkulātpatanādbhayam||15.574||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.575"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.575">15.575</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ekapātre kulāmnāye tasmāttānparivarjayet|<br />
Pramādācca kṛte sakhye goṣṭhyāṁ cakraṁ tu pūjayet||15.575||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.576"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.576">15.576</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīmadūrmau ca kathitamāgamāntarasevake|<br />
Gurvantararate mūḍhe devadravyopajīvake||15.576||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.577"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.577">15.577</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śaktihiṁsākare duṣṭe samparkaṁ naiva kārayet|<br />
Na vikalpena dīkṣādau vrajedāyatanādikam||15.577||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.578"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.578">15.578</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktāsthāśithilatve yannimittaṁ naiva taccaret|<br />
Śāsanasthānpurājātyā na paśyennāpyudīrayet||15.578||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.579"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.579">15.579</a> <span class="pv-iast">Naca vyavaharetsarvāñchivābhedena kevalam|<br />
Sadvidyaiḥ sākamāsīta jñānadīptyai yateta ca||15.579||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.580"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.580">15.580</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nāsaṁskṛtāṁ vrajettajjaṁ viphalatvaṁ na cācaret|<br />
Melakārdhaniśācaryā janavarjaṁ ca tatra hi||15.580||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.581"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.581">15.581</a> <span class="pv-iast">Māṁsādidāhagandhaṁ ca jighreddevīpriyo hyasau|<br />
Gurvājñāṁ pālayansarvaṁ tyajenmantramayo bhavet||15.581||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.582"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.582">15.582</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śāstrapūjājapadhyānavivekatadupakriyāḥ|<br />
Akurvanniṣphalāṁ naiva ceṣṭeta trividhāṁ kriyām||15.582||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.583"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.583">15.583</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mantratantrairna vādaṁ ca kuryānno bhakṣayedviṣam|<br />
Samayānāṁ vilope ca guruṁ pṛcchedasannidhau||15.583||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.584"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.584">15.584</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadvargaṁ nijasantānamanyaṁ tasyāpyasannidhau|<br />
Tenoktamanutiṣṭhecca nirvikalpaṁ prayatnataḥ||15.584||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.585"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.585">15.585</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yataḥ śāstrādisambodhatanmayīkṛtamānasaḥ|<br />
Śiva eva gururnāsya vāgasatyā viniḥsaret||15.585||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.586"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.586">15.586</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śivasya svātmasaṁskṛtyai prahvībhāvo guroḥ punaḥ|<br />
Hlādāyetyubhayārthāya tattuṣṭiḥ phaladā śiśoḥ||15.586||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.587"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.587">15.587</a> <span class="pv-iast">Gurvāyattaikasiddhirhi samayyapi vibodhabhāk|<br />
Tadbodhabahumānena vidyādgurutamaṁ gurum||15.587||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.588"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.588">15.588</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ataḥ samprāpya vijñānaṁ yo gurau bahumānavān|<br />
Nāsau vijñānaviśvasto nāsaktaṁ bhraṣṭa eva saḥ||15.588||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.589"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.589">15.589</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jñānānāśvastacittaṁ taṁ vacomātreṇa śāstrajñam|<br />
Uktaṁ ca nārcayejjātu hṛdā vijñānadūṣakam||15.589||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.590"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.590">15.590</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tādṛkca na guruḥ kāryastaṁ kṛtvāpi parityajet|<br />
Mukhyabuddhyā na sampaśyedvaiṣṇavādigatāngurūn||15.590||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.591"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.591">15.591</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathā ca śrīmadūrmyākhye guroruktaṁ viśeṣaṇam|<br />
Gurvājñā prāṇasandehe nopekṣyā no vikalpyate||15.591||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.592"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.592">15.592</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kauladīkṣā kaulaśāstraṁ tattvajñānaṁ prakāśitam|<br />
Yenāsau gururityukto hyanye vai nāmadhāriṇaḥ||15.592||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.593"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.593">15.593</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīmadānandaśāstre ca tathaivoktaṁ viśeṣaṇam|<br />
Yasmāddīkṣā mantraśāstraṁ tattvajñānaṁ sa vai guruḥ||15.593||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.594"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.594">15.594</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tiṣṭhedavyaktaliṅgaśca na liṅgaṁ dhārayetkvacit|<br />
Na liṅgibhiḥ samaṁ kaiścitkuryādācāramelanam||15.594||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.595"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.595">15.595</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kevalaṁ liṅginaḥ pālyā na bībhatsyā virūpakāḥ|<br />
Śrīmadrātrikule coktaṁ mokṣaḥ śaṅkāpahānitaḥ||15.595||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.596"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.596">15.596</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aśuddhavāsanasyaiṣā mokṣavārtāpi durlabhā|<br />
Na likhenmantrahṛdayaṁ śrīmanmāloditaṁ kila||15.596||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.597"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.597">15.597</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadaṅgāduddharenmantraṁ na tu lekhe vilekhayet|<br />
Atattve'bhiniveśaṁ ca na kuryātpakṣapātataḥ||15.597||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.598"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.598">15.598</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jātividyākulācāradehadeśaguṇārthajān|<br />
Grahāngrahānivāṣṭau drāktyajedgahvaradarśitān||15.598||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.599"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.599">15.599</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathā śrīniśicārādau hayatvenopadarśitān|<br />
Brāhmaṇo'haṁ mayā vedaśāstroktādaparaṁ katham||15.599||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.600"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.600">15.600</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anuṣṭheyamayaṁ jātigrahaḥ paranirodhakaḥ|<br />
Evamanye'pyudāhāryāḥ kulagahvaravartmanā||15.600||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.601"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.601">15.601</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atatsvabhāve tādrūpyaṁ darśayannavaśe'pi yaḥ|<br />
Svarūpācchādakaḥ so'tra graho graha ivoditaḥ||15.601||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.602"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.602">15.602</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṁvitsvabhāve no jātiprabhṛtiḥ kāpi kalpanā|<br />
Rūpasya sā tvasvarūpeṇa tadrūpaṁ chādayatyalam||15.602||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.603"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.603">15.603</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yā kācitkalpanā saṁvittattvasyākhaṇḍitātmanaḥ|<br />
Saṅkocakāriṇī sarvaḥ sa grahastāṁ parityajet||15.603||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.604"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.604">15.604</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīmadānandaśāstre ca kathitaṁ parameṣṭhinā|<br />
Nirapekṣaḥ prabhurvāmo na śuddhistatra kāraṇam||15.604||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.605"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.605">15.605</a> <span class="pv-iast">Devītṛptirmakhe raktamāṁsairno śaucayojanāt|<br />
Dvijāntyajaiḥ samaṁ kāryā carvante'pi marīcayaḥ||15.605||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.606"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.606">15.606</a> <span class="pv-iast">Avikārakṛtastena vikalpānnirayo bhavet|<br />
Sarvadevamayaḥ kāyaḥ sarvaprāṇiṣviti sphuṭam||15.606||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.607"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.607">15.607</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīmadbhirnakuleśādyairapyetatsunirūpitam|<br />
Śarīramevāyatanaṁ nānyadāyatanaṁ vrajet||15.607||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.608"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.608">15.608</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tīrthamekaṁ smarenmantramanyatīrthāni varjayet|<br />
Vidhimenaṁ sukhaṁ jñātvā vidhijālaṁ parityajet||15.608||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.609"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.609">15.609</a> <span class="pv-iast">Samādhirniścayaṁ muktvā na cānyenopalabhyate|<br />
Iti matvā vidhānajñaḥ sammohaṁ parivarjayet||15.609||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.610"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.610">15.610</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mantrasya hṛdayaṁ muktvā na cānyatparamaṁ kvacit|<br />
Iti matvā vidhānajño mantrajālaṁ parityajet||15.610||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.611"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.611">15.611</a> <span class="pv-iast">Naivedyaṁ prāśayennadyāstaccheṣaṁ ca jale kṣipet|<br />
Tairbhukte na bhaveddoṣo jalajaiḥ pūrvadīkṣitaiḥ||15.611||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.612"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.612">15.612</a> <span class="pv-iast">Avaśyapālanīyatvātparatattvena saṅgamāt|<br />
Jñānaprāptyabhyupāyatvātsamayāste prakīrtitāḥ||15.612||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.613"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.613">15.613</a> <span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ saṁśrāvya samayāndevaṁ sampūjya daiśikaḥ|<br />
Visarjayetsvacidvyomni śānte mūrtivilāpanāt||15.613||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.614"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.614">15.614</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yadi putrakadīkṣāsya na kāryā samanantaram|<br />
Tadābhiṣiñcetsāstreṇa śivakumbhena taṁ śiśum||15.614||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v15.615"><a class="ta-num" href="#v15.615">15.615</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ātmānaṁ ca tato yasmājjalamūrtirmaheśvaraḥ|<br />
Mantrayuṅnikhilāpyāyī kāryaṁ tadabhiṣecanam||15.615||</span></p>
