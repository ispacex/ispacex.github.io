# Глава 21 — Pārokṣī

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">61 строфа · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [английский перевод у источника — русского там нет](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/en/node/889)</p>

<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloka ekaviṁśatitamamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.1">21.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Parokṣasaṁsthitasyātha dīkṣākarma nigadyate||21.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.2">21.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhuktimuktiprasiddhyarthaṁ nīyate sadguruṁ prati|<br />
Ityasminmālinīvākye pratiḥ sāmmukhyavācakaḥ||21.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.3">21.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sāmmukhyaṁ cāsya śiṣyasya tatkṛpāspadatātmakam|<br />
Tamārādhyeti vacanaṁ kṛpāhetūpalakṣaṇam||21.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.4">21.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatsambandhāttataḥ kaścittatkṣaṇādapavṛjyate|<br />
Ityasyāyamapi hyartho mālinīvākyasanmaṇeḥ||21.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.5">21.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatkṣaṇāditi nāsyāsti yiyāsādikṣaṇāntaram|<br />
Kintvevameva karuṇānighnastaṁ gururuddharet||21.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.6">21.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Gurusevākṣīṇatanordīkṣāmaprāpya pañcatām|<br />
Gatasyātha svayaṁ mṛtyukṣaṇoditatathāruceḥ||21.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.7">21.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Athavādharatantrādidīkṣāsaṁskārabhāginaḥ|<br />
Prāptasāmayikasyātha parāṁ dīkṣāmavindataḥ||21.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.8">21.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ḍimbāhatasya yogeśībhakṣitasyābhicārataḥ|<br />
Mṛtasya guruṇā yantratantrādinihatasya vā||21.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.9">21.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhraṣṭasvasamayasyātha dīkṣāṁ prāptavato'pyalam|<br />
Bandhubhāryāsuhṛtputragāḍhābhyarthanayogataḥ||21.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.10">21.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svayaṁ tadviṣayotpannakaruṇābalato'pi vā|<br />
Vijñātatanmukhāyātaśaktipātāṁśadharmiṇaḥ||21.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.11">21.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Gururdīkṣāṁ mṛtoddhārīṁ kurvīta śivadāyinīm|<br />
Śrīmṛtyuñjayasiddhādau yaduktaṁ parameśinā||21.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.12">21.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Adīkṣite nṛpatyādāvalase patite mṛte|<br />
Bālāturastrīvṛddhe ca mṛtoddhāraṁ prakalpayet||21.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.13">21.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vidhiḥ sarvaḥ pūrvamuktaḥ sa tu saṅkṣipta iṣyate|<br />
Gurvādipūjārahito bāhye bhogāya sā yataḥ||21.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.14">21.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Adhivāsacarukṣetraśayyāmaṇḍalakalpanam|<br />
Nopayogyatra tacchiṣyasaṁskriyāsvapnadṛṣṭaye||21.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.15">21.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mantrasannidhisantṛptiyogyāyātra tu maṇḍalam|<br />
Bhūyo dine ca devārcā sākṣānnāsyopakāri tat||21.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.16">21.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kriyopakaraṇasthānamaṇḍalākṛtimantrataḥ|<br />
Dhyānayogaikatadbhaktijñānatanmayabhāvataḥ||21.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.17">21.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatpraviṣṭasya kasyāpi śiṣyāṇāṁ ca gurostathā|<br />
Ekādaśaite kathitāḥ sannidhānāya hetavaḥ||21.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.18">21.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uttarottaramutkṛṣṭāstathā vyāmiśraṇāvaśāt|<br />
Kriyātibhūyasī puṣpādyuttamalakṣaṇānvitam||21.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.19">21.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ekaliṅgādi ca sthānaṁ yatrātmā samprasīdati|<br />
Maṇḍalaṁ tritriśūlābjacakraṁ yanmantramaṇḍale||21.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.20">21.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anāhūte'pi dṛṣṭaṁ satsamayitvaprasādhanam|<br />
Yaduktaṁ mālinītantre siddhaṁ samayamaṇḍalam||21.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.21">21.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yena sandṛṣṭamātreti siddhamātrapadadvayāt|<br />
Ākṛtirdīptarūpā yā mantrastadvatsudīptikaḥ||21.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.22">21.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śiṣṭaṁ spaṣṭamato neha kathitaṁ vistarātpunaḥ|<br />
Kṛtvā maṇḍalamabhyarcya tatra devaṁ kuśairatha||21.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.23">21.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Gomayenākṛtiṁ kuryācchiṣyavattāṁ nidhāpayet|<br />
Tatastasyāṁ śodhyamekamadhvānaṁ vyāptibhāvanāt||21.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.24">21.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prakṛtyantaṁ vinikṣipya punarenaṁ vidhiṁ caret|<br />
Mahājālaprayogeṇa sarvasmādadhvamadhyataḥ||21.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.25">21.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Cittamākṛṣya tatrasthaṁ kuryāttadvidhirucyate|<br />
Mūlādhārādudetya prasṛtasuvitatānantanāḍyadhvadaṇḍaṁ vīryeṇākramya nāsāgaganaparigataṁ vikṣipanvyāptumīṣṭe|<br />
Yāvaddhūmābhirāmapracitataraśikhājālakenādhvacakraṁ sañchādyābhīṣṭajīvānayanamiti mahājālanāmā prayogaḥ||21.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.26">21.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Etenācchādanīyaṁ vrajati paravaśaṁ sammukhīnatvamādau pañcādānīyate cetsakalamatha tato'pyadhvamadhyādyatheṣṭam|<br />
Ākṛṣṭāvuddhṛtau vā mṛtajanaviṣaye karṣaṇīye'tha jīve yogaḥ śrīśambhunāthāgamaparigamito jālanāmā mayoktaḥ||21.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.27">21.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ciravighaṭite senāyugme yathāmilite punarhayagajanaraṁ svāṁ svāṁ jātiṁ rasādabhidhāvati|<br />
Karaṇapavanairnāḍīcakraistathaiva samāgatairnijanijarasādekībhāvyaṁ svajālavaśīkṛtaiḥ||21.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.28">21.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mahājālasamākṛṣṭo jīvo vijñānaśālinā|<br />
Svaḥpretatiryaṅnirayāṁstadaivaiṣa vimuñcati||21.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.29">21.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tajjñānamantrayogāptaḥ puruṣaścaiṣa kṛtrimam|<br />
Yogīva sādhyahṛdayāttadā tādātmyamujjhati||21.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.30">21.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sthāvarādidaśāścitrāstatsalokasamīpatāḥ|<br />
Tyajecceti na citraṁ sa evaṁ yaḥ karmaṇāpi vā||21.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.31">21.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Adhikāriśarīratvānmānuṣye tu śarīragaḥ|<br />
Na tadā mucyate dehāddehānte tu śivaṁ vrajet||21.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.32">21.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasmindehe tu kāpyasya jāyate śāṅkarī parā|<br />
Bhaktirūhācca vijñānādācāryādvāpyasevitāt||21.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.33">21.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Taddehasaṁsthito'pyeṣa jīvo jālabalādimam|<br />
Dārbhādidehaṁ vyāpnoti svādhiṣṭhityāpyacetayan||21.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.34">21.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yogamantrakriyājñānabhūyobalavaśātpunaḥ|<br />
Manuṣyadehamapyeṣa tadaivāśu vimuñcati||21.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.35">21.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Suptakalpo'pyadeho'pi yo jīvaḥ so'pi jālataḥ|<br />
Ākṛṣṭo dārbhamāyāti dehaṁ phalamayaṁ ca vā||21.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.36">21.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jātīphalādi yatkiñcittena vā dehakalpanā|<br />
Antarbahirdvayaucityāttadatrotkṛṣṭamucyate||21.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.37">21.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tato jālakramānītaḥ sa jīvaḥ suptavatsthitaḥ|<br />
Manoviśiṣṭadehādisāmagrīprāptyabhāvataḥ||21.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.38">21.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na spandate na jānāti na vakti na kilecchati|<br />
Tādṛśasyaiva saṁskārānsarvānprāgvatprakalpayet||21.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.39">21.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nirbījadīkṣāyogena sarvaṁ kṛtvā puroditam|<br />
Vidhiṁ yojanikāṁ pūrṇāhutyā sākaṁ kṣipecca tam||21.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.40">21.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dārbhādidehe mantrāgnāvarpite pūrṇayā saha|<br />
Muktapāśaḥ śivaṁ yāti punarāvṛttivarjitaḥ||21.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.41">21.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sapratyayā tviyaṁ yatra spandate darbhajā tanuḥ|<br />
Tatra prāṇamanomantrārpaṇayogāttathā bhavet||21.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.42">21.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sābhyāsasya tadapyuktaṁ balāśvāsi na tatkṛte|<br />
Mṛtoddhāroditaireva yathāsambhūti hetubhiḥ||21.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.43">21.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jīvatparokṣadīkṣāpi kāryā nirbījikā tu sā|<br />
Tasyāṁ darbhākṛtiprāyakalpane jālayogataḥ||21.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.44">21.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṅkalpamātreṇākarṣo jīvasya mṛtibhītitaḥ|<br />
Śiṣṭaṁ prāgvatkuśādyutthākāraviploṣavarjitam||21.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.45">21.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pārimityādanaiśvaryātsādhye niyatiyantraṇāt|<br />
Jālākṛṣṭirvinābhyāsaṁ rāgadveṣānna jāyate||21.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.46">21.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Parokṣa evātulyābhirdīkṣābhiryadi dīkṣitaḥ|<br />
Tatrottaraṁ syādbalavatsaṁskārāya tvadhastanam||21.46||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.47">21.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhuktiyojanikāyāṁ tu bhūyobhirgurubhistathā|<br />
Kṛtāyāṁ bhogavaicitryaṁ hetuvaicitryayogataḥ||21.47||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.48">21.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Parokṣadīkṣaṇe'māyottīrṇe bhogāya yojayet|<br />
Bhogānīpsā durlabhā hi satī vā bhogahānaye||21.48||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.49">21.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktaṁ hi svānyasaṁvittyoḥ svasaṁvidbalavattarā|<br />
Bādhakatve bādhikāsau sāmyaudāsīnyayostathā||21.49||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.50"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.50">21.50</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīmāndharmaśivo'pyāha pārokṣyāṁ karmapaddhatau|<br />
Parokṣadīkṣaṇe samyakpūrṇāhutividhau yadi||21.50||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.51"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.51">21.51</a> <span class="pv-iast">Agniściṭiciṭāśabdaṁ sadhūmaṁ pratimuñcati|<br />
Dhatte nīlāmbudacchāyāṁ muhurjvalati śāmyati||21.51||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.52"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.52">21.52</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vistaro ghorarūpaśca mahīṁ dhāvati cāpyadhaḥ|<br />
Dhvāṅkṣādyaśravyaśabdo vā tadā taṁ lakṣayedguruḥ||21.52||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.53"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.53">21.53</a> <span class="pv-iast">Brahmahatyādibhiḥ pāpaistatsaṅgaiścopapātakaiḥ|<br />
Tadā tasya na kartavyā dīkṣāsminnakṛte vidhau||21.53||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.54"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.54">21.54</a> <span class="pv-iast">Navātmā phaṭpuṭāntaḥsthaḥ punaḥ pañcaphaḍanvitaḥ|<br />
Amukasyeti pāpāni dahāmyanu phaḍaṣṭakam||21.54||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.55"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.55">21.55</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti sāhasriko homaḥ kartavyastilataṇḍulaiḥ|<br />
Ante pūrṇā ca dātavyā tato'smai dīkṣayā guruḥ||21.55||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.56"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.56">21.56</a> <span class="pv-iast">Parayojanaparyantaṁ kuryāttattvaviśodhanam|<br />
Pratyakṣe'pi sthitasyāṇoḥ pāpino bhagavanmayīm||21.56||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.57"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.57">21.57</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śaktiṁ prāptavato jyeṣṭhāmevameva vidhiṁ caret|<br />
Yadi vā daiśikaḥ samyaṅna dīptastasya tatpurā||21.57||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.58"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.58">21.58</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prāyaścittaistathā dānaiḥ prāṇāyāmaiśca śodhanam|<br />
Kṛtvā vidhimimāṁ cāpi dīkṣāṁ kuryādaśaṅkitaḥ||21.58||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.59"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.59">21.59</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sarvathāvartamāno'pi tattvavinmocayetpaśūn|<br />
Icchayaiva śivaḥ sākṣāttasmāttaṁ pūjayetsadā||21.59||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.60"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.60">21.60</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śāṭhyaṁ tatra na kāryaṁ ca tatkṛtvādho vrajecchiśuḥ|<br />
Na punaḥ kīrtayettasya pāpaṁ kīrtayitā vrajet||21.60||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v21.61"><a class="ta-num" href="#v21.61">21.61</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nirayaṁ varjayettasmāditi dīkṣottare vidhiḥ|<br />
Eṣā parokṣadīkṣā dvidhoditā jīvaditarabhedena||21.61||</span></p>
