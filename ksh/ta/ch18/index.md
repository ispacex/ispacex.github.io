# Глава 18 — Sūkṣmā dīkṣā

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">11 строф · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [английский перевод у источника — русского там нет](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/en/node/886)</p>

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Глава 17 — Prameyaprakriyā](/ksh/ta/ch17/) · [Глава 19 — Sadyaḥsamutkrama →](/ksh/ta/ch19/)</p>

<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloke'ṣṭādaśamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v18.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v18.1">18.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha saṅkṣiptadīkṣeyaṁ śivatāpattidocyate|<br />
Na rajo nādhivāso'tra na bhūkṣetraparigrahaḥ|<br />
Yatra tatra pradeśe tu pūjayitvā guruḥ śivam||18.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v18.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v18.2">18.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Adhvānaṁ manasā dhyātvā dīkṣayettattvapāragaḥ|<br />
Jananādivihīnāṁ tu yena yenādhvanā guruḥ||18.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v18.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v18.3">18.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kuryātsa ekatattvāntāṁ śivabhāvaikabhāvitaḥ|<br />
Parāmantrastato'syeti tattvaṁ saṁśodhayāmyatha||18.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v18.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v18.4">18.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svāheti pratitattvaṁ syācchuddhe pūrṇāhutiṁ kṣipet|<br />
Evaṁ mantrāntaraiḥ kuryātsamastairathavoktavat||18.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v18.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v18.5">18.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Parāsampuṭitaṁ nāma svāhāntaṁ prathamāntakam|<br />
Śataṁ sahasraṁ sāṣṭaṁ vā tena śaktyaiva homayet||18.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v18.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v18.6">18.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tataḥ pūrṇeti saṁśodhyahīnamuttamamīdṛśam|<br />
Dīkṣākarmoditaṁ tatra tatra śāstre maheśinā||18.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v18.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v18.7">18.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pratyekaṁ mātṛkāyugmavarṇaistattvāni śodhayet|<br />
Yadi vā piṇḍamantreṇa sarvamantreṣvayaṁ vidhiḥ||18.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v18.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v18.8">18.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathā yathā ca svabhyastajñānastanmayatātmakaḥ|<br />
Gurustathā tathā kuryātsaṅkṣiptaṁ karma nānyathā||18.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v18.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v18.9">18.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrībrahmayāmale coktaṁ saṅkṣipte'pi hi bhāvayet|<br />
Vyāptiṁ sarvādhvasāmānyāṁ kintu yāge savistaraḥ||18.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v18.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v18.10">18.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atanmayībhūtamiti vikṣiptaṁ karma sandadhat|<br />
Kramāttādātmyametīti vikṣiptaṁ vidhimācaret||18.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v18.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v18.11">18.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṅkṣipto vidhirukto'yaṁ kṛpayā yaḥ śivoditaḥ|<br />
Dīkṣottare kairaṇe ca tatra tatrāpi śāsane||18.11||</span></p>
