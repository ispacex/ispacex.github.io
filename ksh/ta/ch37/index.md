# Глава 37 — Śāstropādeyatvanirūpaṇam

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">85 строф · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [английский перевод у источника — русского там нет](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/en/node/908)</p>

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Глава 36 — Āyātikathanam](/ksh/ta/ch36/)</p>

<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloke saptatriṁśamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.1">37.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktanītyaiva sarvatra vyavahāre pravartite|<br />
Prasiddhāvupajīvyāyāmavaśyagrāhya āgamaḥ||37.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.2">37.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathā laukikadṛṣṭyānyaphalabhāktatprasiddhitaḥ|<br />
Samyagvyavaharaṁstadvacchivabhāktatprasiddhitaḥ||37.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.3">37.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadavaśyagrahītavye śāstre svāṁśopadeśini|<br />
Manākphale'bhyupādeyatamaṁ tadviparītakam||37.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.4">37.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">[Yathā khageśvarībhāvaniḥśaṅkatvādviṣaṁ vrajet|<br />
Kṣayaṁ karmasthitistadvadaśaṅkādbhairavatvataḥ||37.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.5">37.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yadārṣe pātahetūktaṁ tadasminvāmaśāsane|<br />
Āśusiddhyai yataḥ sarvamārṣaṁ māyodarasthitam||37.5||]</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.6">37.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tacca yatsarvasarvajñadṛṣṭaṁ taccāpi kiṁ bhavet|<br />
Yadaśeṣopadeśena sūyate'nuttaraṁ phalam||37.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.7">37.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathādharādharaproktavastutattvānuvādataḥ|<br />
Uttaraṁ kathitaṁ saṁvitsiddhaṁ taddhi tathā bhavet||37.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.8">37.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yaduktādhikasaṁvittisiddhavastunirūpaṇāt|<br />
Apūrṇasarvavitproktirjñāyate'dharaśāsane||37.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.9">37.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ūrdhvaśāsanavastvaṁśe dṛṣṭvāpi ca samujjhite|<br />
Adhaḥśāstreṣu māyātvaṁ lakṣyate sargarakṣaṇāt||37.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.10">37.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīmadānandaśāstrādau proktaṁ ca parameśinā|<br />
Ṛṣivākyaṁ bahukleśamadhruvālpaphalaṁ mitam||37.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.11">37.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Naiva pramāṇayedvidvānśaivamevāgamaṁ śrayet|<br />
Yadārṣe pātahetūktaṁ tadasminvāmaśāsane||37.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.12">37.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Āśusiddhyai yataḥ sarvamārṣaṁ māyodarasthitam|<br />
Yathā khageśvarībhāvaniḥśaṅkatvādviṣaṁ vrajet||37.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.13">37.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kṣayaṁ karmasthitistadvadaśaṅkādbhairavatvataḥ|<br />
Ajñatvānupadeṣṭṛtvasandaṣṭe'dharaśāsane||37.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.14">37.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Etadviparyayādgrāhyamavaśyaṁ śivaśāsanam|<br />
Dvāvāptau tatra ca śrīmacchrīkaṇṭhalakuleśvarau||37.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.15">37.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dvipravāhamidaṁ śāstraṁ samyaṅniḥśreyasapradam|<br />
Prācyasya tu yathābhīṣṭabhogadatvamapi sthitam||37.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.16">37.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tacca pañcavidhaṁ proktaṁ śaktivaicitryacitritam|<br />
Pañcasrota iti proktaṁ śrīmacchrīkaṇṭhaśāsanam||37.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.17">37.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Daśāṣṭādaśadhā srotaḥpañcakaṁ yattato'pyalam|<br />
Utkṛṣṭaṁ bhairavābhikhyaṁ catuḥṣaṣṭivibheditam||37.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.18">37.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīmadānandaśāstrādau proktaṁ bhagavatā kila|<br />
Samūhaḥ pīṭhametacca dvidhā dakṣiṇavāmataḥ||37.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.19">37.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mantro vidyeti tasmācca mudrāmaṇḍalagaṁ dvayam|<br />
Mananatrāṇadaṁ yattu mantrākhyaṁ tatra vidyayā||37.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.20">37.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Upodbalanamāpyāyaḥ sā hi vedyārthabhāsinī|<br />
Mantrapratikṛtirmudrā tadāpyāyanakārakam||37.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.21">37.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Maṇḍalaṁ sāramuktaṁ hi maṇḍaśrutyā śivāhvayam|<br />
Evamanyonyasambhedavṛtti pīṭhacatuṣṭayam||37.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.22">37.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yatastasmādbhavetsarvaṁ pīṭhe pīṭhe'pi vastutaḥ|<br />
Pradhānatvāttasya tasya vastuno bhinnatā punaḥ||37.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.23">37.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kathitā sādhakendrāṇāṁ tattadvastuprasiddhaye|<br />
Pratyekaṁ taccaturdhaivaṁ maṇḍalaṁ mudrikā tathā||37.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.24">37.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mantro vidyeti ca pīṭhamutkṛṣṭaṁ cottarottam|<br />
Vidyāpīṭhapradhānaṁ ca siddhayogīśvarīmatam||37.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.25">37.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasyāpi paramaṁ sāraṁ mālinīvijayottaram|<br />
Uktaṁ śrīratnamālāyāmetacca parameśinā||37.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.26">37.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aśeṣatantrasāraṁ tu vāmadakṣiṇamāśritam|<br />
Ekatra militaṁ kaulaṁ śrīṣaḍardhakaśāsane||37.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.27">37.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Siddhānte karma bahulaṁ malamāyādirūṣitam|<br />
Dakṣiṇaṁ raudrakarmāḍhyaṁ vāmaṁ siddhisamākulam||37.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.28">37.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svalpapuṇyaṁ bahukleśaṁ svapratītivivarjitam|<br />
Mokṣavidyāvihīnaṁ ca vinayaṁ tyaja dūrataḥ||37.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.29">37.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yasminkāle ca guruṇā nirvikalpaṁ prakāśitam|<br />
Muktastenaiva kālena yantraṁ tiṣṭhati kevalam||37.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.30">37.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mayaitatsrotasāṁ rūpamanuttarapadāddhruvāt|<br />
Ārabhya vistareṇoktaṁ mālinīślokavārtike||37.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.31">37.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jijñāsustata evedamavadhārayituṁ kṣamaḥ|<br />
Vayaṁ tūktānuvacanamaphalaṁ nādriyāmahe||37.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.32">37.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Itthaṁ dadadanāyāsājjīvanmuktimahāphalam|<br />
Yathepsitamahābhogadātṛtvena vyavasthitam||37.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.33">37.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ṣaḍardhasāraṁ sacchāstramupādeyamidaṁ sphuṭam|<br />
Ṣaṭtriṁśatā tattvabalena sūtā yadyapyanantā bhuvanāvalīyam|<br />
Brahmāṇḍamatyantamanoharaṁ tu vaicitryavarjaṁ na hi ramyabhāvaḥ||37.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.34">37.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhūrādisaptapurapūrṇatame'pi tasminmanye dvitīyabhuvanaṁ bhavanaṁ sukhasya|<br />
Kvānyatra citragatisūryaśaśāṅkaśobhirātrindivaprasarabhogavibhāgabhūṣā||37.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.35">37.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatrāpi ca tridivabhogamahārghavarṣadvīpāntarādadhikameva kumārikāhvam|<br />
Yatrādharādharapadātparamaṁ śivāntamāroḍhumapyadhikṛtiḥ kṛtināmanarghā||37.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.36">37.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prākkarmabhogipaśutocitabhogabhājā kiṁ janmanā nanu sukhaikapade'pi dhāmni|<br />
Sarvo hi bhāvini paraṁ paritoṣameti sambhāvite na tu nimeṣiṇi vartamāne||37.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.37">37.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kanyāhvaye'pi bhuvane'tra paraṁ mahīyāndeśaḥ sa yatra kila śāstravarāṇi cakṣuḥ|<br />
Jātyandhasadmani na janma na ko'bhinindedbhinnāñjanāyitaravipramukhaprakāśe||37.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.38">37.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Niḥśeṣaśāstrasadanaṁ kila madhyadeśastasminnajāyata guṇābhyadhiko dvijanmā|<br />
Ko'pyatrigupta iti nāmaniruktagotraḥ śāstrābdhicarvaṇakalodyadagastyagotraḥ||37.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.39">37.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tamatha lalitādityo rājā nijaṁ puramānayatpraṇayarabhasātkaśmīrākhyaṁ himālayamūrdhagam|<br />
Adhivasati yadgaurīkāntaḥ karairvijayādibhiryugapadakhilaṁ bhoge sāraṁ rasātparicarvitum||37.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.40">37.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sthāne sthāne munibhirakhilaiścakrire yannivāsā yaccādhyāste pratipadamidaṁ sa svayaṁ candracūḍaḥ|<br />
Tanmanye'haṁ samabhilaṣitāśeṣasiddherna siddhyai kaśmīrebhyaḥ paramatha puraṁ pūrṇavṛtterna tuṣṭyai||37.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.41">37.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yatra svayaṁ śāradacandraśubhrā śrīśāradeti prathitā janeṣu|<br />
Śāṇḍilyasevārasasuprasannā sarvaṁ janaṁ svairvibhavairyunakti||37.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.42">37.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nāraṅgāruṇakānti pāṇḍuvikacadballāvadātacchavi prodbhinnāmalamātuluṅgakanakacchāyābhirāmaprabham|<br />
Kerīkuntalakandalīpratikṛtiśyāmaprabhābhāsvaraṁ yasmiñchakticatuṣṭayojjvalamalaṁ madyaṁ mahābhairavam||37.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.43">37.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Trinayanamahākopajvālāvilīna iha sthito madanaviśikhavrāto madyacchalena vijṛmbhate|<br />
Kathamitarathā rāgaṁ mohaṁ madaṁ madanajvaraṁ vidadhadaniśaṁ kāmātaṅkairvaśīkurute jagat||37.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.44">37.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yatkāntānāṁ praṇayavacasi prauḍhimānaṁ vidhatte yannirvighnaṁ nidhuvanavidhau sādhvasaṁ sandhunoti|<br />
Yasminviśvāḥ kalitarucayo devatāścakracaryastanmārdvīkaṁ sapadi tanute yatra bhogāpavargau||37.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.45">37.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Udyadgaurāṅkuravikasitaiḥ śyāmaraktaiḥ palāśairantargāḍhāruṇarucilasatkesarālīvicitraiḥ|<br />
Ākīrṇā bhūḥ pratipadamasau yatra kāśmīrapuṣpaiḥ samyagdevītritayayajanodyānamāviṣkaroti||37.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.46">37.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sarvo lokaḥ kaviratha budho yatra śūro'pi vāgmī candroddyotā masṛṇagatayaḥ pauranāryaśca yatra|<br />
Yatrāṅgārojjvalavikasitānantasauṣumṇamārgagrastārkendurgaganavimalo yoginīnāṁ ca vargaḥ||37.46||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.47">37.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīmatparaṁ pravaranāma puraṁ ca tatra yannirmame pravarasena iti kṣitīśaḥ|<br />
Yaḥ svapratiṣṭhitamaheśvarapūjanānte vyomotpatannudasṛjatkila dhūpaghaṇṭām||37.47||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.48">37.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Āndolanoditamanoharavīranādaiḥ sā cāsya tatsucaritaṁ prathayāmbabhūva|<br />
Sadvṛttasāragurutaijasamūrtayo hi tyaktā api prabhuguṇānadhikaṁ dhvananti||37.48||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.49">37.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sampūrṇacandravimaladyutivīrakāntāgāḍhāṅgarāgaghanakuṅkumapiñjaraśrīḥ|<br />
Proddhūtavetasalatāsitacāmaraughairājyābhiṣekamaniśaṁ dadatī smarasya||37.49||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.50"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.50">37.50</a> <span class="pv-iast">Rodhaḥpratiṣṭhitamaheśvarasiddhaliṅgasvāyambhuvārcanavilepanagandhapuṣpaiḥ|<br />
Āvarjyamānatanuvīcinimajjanaughavidhvastapāpmamunisiddhamanuṣyavandyā||37.50||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.51"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.51">37.51</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhogāpavargaparipūraṇakalpavallī bhogaikadānarasikāṁ surasiddhasindhum|<br />
Nyakkurvatī harapinākakalāvatīrṇā yadbhūṣayatyavirataṁ taṭinī vitastā||37.51||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.52"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.52">37.52</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasminkuverapuracārisitāṁśumaulisāmmukhyadarśanavirūḍhapavitrabhāve|<br />
Vaitastarodhasi nivāsamamuṣya cakre rājā dvijasya parikalpitabhūrisampat||37.52||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.53"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.53">37.53</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasyānvaye mahati ko'pi varāhaguptanāmā babhūva bhagavānsvayamantakāle|<br />
Gīrvāṇasindhulaharīkalitāgramūrdhā yasyākarotparamanugrahamāgraheṇa||37.53||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.54"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.54">37.54</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasyātmajaścukhalaketi jane prasiddhaścandrāvadātadhiṣaṇo narasiṁhaguptaḥ|<br />
Yaṁ sarvaśāstrarasamajjanaśubhracittaṁ māheśvarī paramalaṅkurute sma bhaktiḥ||37.54||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.55"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.55">37.55</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tāruṇyasāgarataraṅgabharānapohya vairāgyapotamadhiruhya dṛḍhaṁ haṭhena|<br />
Yo bhaktirohaṇamavāpya maheśacintāratnairalaṁ dalayati sma bhavāpadastāḥ||37.55||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.56"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.56">37.56</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasyātmajo'bhinavagupta iti prasiddhaḥ śrīcandracūḍacaraṇābjaparāgapūtaḥ|<br />
Mātā vyayūyujadamuṁ kila bālya eva daivaṁ hi bhāviparikarmaṇi saṁskaroti||37.56||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.57"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.57">37.57</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mātā paraṁ bandhuriti pravādaḥ sneho'tigāḍhīkurute hi pāśān|<br />
Tanmūlabandhe galite kilāsya manye sthitā jīvata eva muktiḥ||37.57||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.58"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.58">37.58</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pitrā sa śabdagahane kṛtasampraveśastarkārṇavormipṛṣatāmalapūtacittaḥ|<br />
Sāhityasāndrarasabhogaparo maheśabhaktyā svayaṅgrahaṇadurmadayā gṛhītaḥ||37.58||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.59"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.59">37.59</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sa tanmayībhūya na lokavartanīmajīgaṇatkāmapi kevalaṁ punaḥ|<br />
Tadīyasambhogavivṛddhaye purā karoti dāsyaṁ guruveśmasu svayam||37.59||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.60"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.60">37.60</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ānandasantatimahārṇavakarṇadhāraḥ saddaiśikairakavarātmajavāmanāthaḥ|<br />
Śrīnāthasantatimahāmbaragharmakāntiḥ śrībhūtirājatanayaḥ svapitṛprasādaḥ||37.60||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.61"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.61">37.61</a> <span class="pv-iast">Traiyambakaprasarasāgaraśāyisomānandātmajotpalajalakṣmaṇaguptanāthaḥ|<br />
Turyākhyasantatimahodadhipūrṇacandraḥ śrīsaumataḥ sakalavitkila śambhunāthaḥ||37.61||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.62"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.62">37.62</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīcandraśarmabhavabhaktivilāsayogānandābhinandaśivaśaktivicitranāthāḥ|<br />
Anye'pi dharmaśivavāmanakodbhaṭaśrībhūteśabhāskaramukhapramukhā mahāntaḥ||37.62||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.63"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.63">37.63</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ete sevārasaviracitānugrahāḥ śāstrasāraprauḍhādeśaprakaṭasubhagaṁ svādhikāraṁ kilāsmai|<br />
Yat samprāduryadapi ca janānnaikṣatākṣetrabhūtānsvātmārāmastadayamaniśaṁ tattvasevāraso'bhūt||37.63||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.64"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.64">37.64</a> <span class="pv-iast">So'nugrahītumatha śāmbhavabhaktibhājaṁ svaṁ bhrātaramakhilaśāstravimarśapūrṇam|<br />
Yāvanmanaḥ praṇidadhāti manorathākhyaṁ tāvajjanaḥ katipayastamupāsasāda||37.64||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.65"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.65">37.65</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīśaurisañjñatanayaḥ kila karṇanāmā yo yauvane viditaśāmbhavatattvasāraḥ|<br />
Dehaṁ tyajanprathayati sma janasya satyaṁ yogacyutaṁ prati mahāmunikṛṣṇavākyam||37.65||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.66"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.66">37.66</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadbālamitramatha mantrisutaḥ prasiddhaḥ śrīmandra ityakhilasāraguṇābhirāmaḥ|<br />
Lakṣmīsarasvati samaṁ yamalañcakāra sāpatnakaṁ tirayate subhagaprabhāvaḥ||37.66||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.67"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.67">37.67</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anye pitṛvyatanayāḥ śivaśaktiśubhrāḥ kṣemotpalābhinavacakrakapadmaguptāḥ|<br />
Ye sampadaṁ tṛṇamamaṁsata śambhusevāsampūritaṁ svahṛdayaṁ hṛdi bhāvayantaḥ||37.67||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.68"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.68">37.68</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ṣaḍardhaśāstreṣu samastameva yenādhijagme vidhimaṇḍalādi|<br />
Sa rāmagupto guruśambhuśāstrasevāvidhivyagrasamagramārgaḥ||37.68||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.69"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.69">37.69</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anyo'pi kaścana janaḥ śivaśaktipātasampreraṇāparavaśasvakaśaktisārthaḥ|<br />
Abhyarthanāvimukhabhāvamaśikṣitena tenāpyanugrahapadaḥ kṛta eṣa vargaḥ||37.69||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.70"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.70">37.70</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ācāryamabhyarthayate sma gāḍhaṁ sampūrṇatantrādhigamāya samyak|<br />
Jāyeta daivānugṛhītabuddheḥ sampatprabandhaikarasaiva sampat||37.70||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.71"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.71">37.71</a> <span class="pv-iast">So'pyabhyupāgamadabhīpsitamasya yadvā svātodyameva hi ninartiṣato'vatīrṇam|<br />
So'nugrahapravaṇa eva hi sadgurūṇāmājñāvaśena śubhasūtimahāṅkureṇa||37.71||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.72"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.72">37.72</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vikṣiptabhāvaparihāramatho cikīrṣanmandraḥ svake puravare sthitimasya vavre|<br />
Ābālagopamapi yatra maheśvarasya dāsyaṁ janaścarati pīṭhanivāsakalpe||37.72||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.73"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.73">37.73</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasyābhavatkila pitṛvyavadhūrvidhātrā yā nirmame galitasaṁsṛticitracintā|<br />
Śītāṁśumaulicaraṇābjaparāgamātrabhūṣāvidhirvihitavatsalikocitākhyā||37.73||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.74"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.74">37.74</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mūrtā kṣameva karuṇeva gṛhītadehā dhāreva vigrahavatī śubhaśīlatāyāḥ|<br />
Vairāgyasāraparipākadaśeva pūrṇā tattvārtharatnarucirasthitirohaṇorvī||37.74||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.75"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.75">37.75</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhartāpi tasyāḥ śaśiśubhramaulerbhaktyā paraṁ pāvitacittavṛttiḥ|<br />
Sa śaurirātteśvaramantribhāvastatyāja yo bhūpatimantribhāvam||37.75||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.76"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.76">37.76</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasya snuṣā karṇavadhūrvidhūtasaṁsāravṛttiḥ sutamekameva|<br />
Yāsūta yogeśvaradattasañjñaṁ nāmānurūpasphuradarthatattvam||37.76||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.77"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.77">37.77</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yāmagrage vayasi bhartṛviyogadīnāmanvagrahīttrinayanaḥ svayameva bhaktyā|<br />
Bhāviprabhāvarabhaseṣu janeṣvanarthaḥ sārthaṁ samākṛṣati so'rthaparamparāṇām||37.77||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.78"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.78">37.78</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhaktyullasatpulakatāṁ sphuṭamaṅgabhūṣāṁ śrīśambhunāthanatimeva lalāṭikāṁ ca|<br />
Śaivaśrutiṁ śravaṇabhūṣaṇamapyavāpya saubhāgyamabhyadhikamudvahati sma yāntaḥ||37.78||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.79"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.79">37.79</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ambābhidhānā kila sā guruṁ taṁ svaṁ bhrātaraṁ śambhudṛśābhyapaśyat|<br />
Bhāviprabhāvojjvalabhavyabuddhiḥ sato'vajānāti na bandhubuddhyā||37.79||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.80"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.80">37.80</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhrātā tadīyo'bhinavaśca nāmnā na kevalaṁ saccaritairapi svaiḥ|<br />
Pītena vijñānarasena yasya tatraiva tṛṣṇā vavṛdhe nikāmam||37.80||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.81"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.81">37.81</a> <span class="pv-iast">So'nyaśca śāmbhavamarīcicayapraṇaśyatsaṅkocahārdanalinīghaṭitojjvalaśrīḥ|<br />
Taṁ lumpakaḥ paricacāra samudyameṣu sādhuḥ samāvahati hanta karāvalambam||37.81||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.82"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.82">37.82</a> <span class="pv-iast">Itthaṁ gṛhe vatsalikāvitīrṇe sthitaḥ samādhāya matiṁ bahūni|<br />
Pūrvaśrutānyākalayansvabuddhyā śāstrāṇi tebhyaḥ samavāpa sāram||37.82||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.83"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.83">37.83</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sa tannibandhaṁ vidadhe mahārthaṁ yuktyāgamodīritatantratattvam|<br />
Ālokamāsādya yadīyameṣa lokaḥ sukhaṁ sañcaritā kriyāsu||37.83||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.84"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.84">37.84</a> <span class="pv-iast">Santo'nugṛhṇīta kṛtiṁ tadīyāṁ gṛhṇīta pūrvaṁ vidhireṣa tāvat|<br />
Tadapi gṛhṇātu bhavanmatiṁ sā sadyo'nugṛhṇātu ca tattvadṛṣṭyā||37.84||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v37.85"><a class="ta-num" href="#v37.85">37.85</a> <span class="pv-iast">Idamabhinavaguptaprombhitaṁ śāstrasāraṁ śiva niśamaya tāvatsarvataḥśrotratantraḥ|<br />
Tava kila nutireṣā sā hi tvadrūpacarcetyabhinavaparituṣṭo lokamātmīkuruṣva||37.85||</span></p>
