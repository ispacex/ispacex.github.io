# Глава 29 — Rahasyacaryā

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">291 строфа · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [английский перевод у источника — русского там нет](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/en/node/899)</p>

<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloka ekonatriṁśamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.1">29.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha samucitādhikāriṇa uddiśya rahasya ucyate'tra vidhiḥ|<br />
Atha sarvāpyupāseyaṁ kulaprakriyayocyate||29.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.2">29.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathā dhārādhirūḍheṣu guruśiṣyeṣu yocitā|<br />
Uktaṁ ca parameśena sāratvaṁ kramapūjane||29.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.3">29.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Siddhakramaniyuktasya māsenaikena yadbhavet|<br />
Na tadvarṣasahasraiḥ syānmantraughairvividhairiti||29.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.4">29.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kulaṁ ca parameśasya śaktiḥ sāmarthyamūrdhvatā|<br />
Svātantryamojo vīryaṁ ca piṇḍaḥ saṁviccharīrakam||29.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.5">29.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathātvena samastāni bhāvajātāni paśyataḥ|<br />
Dhvastaśaṅkāsamūhasya yāgastādṛśa eva saḥ||29.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.6">29.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tādṛgrūpanirūḍhyarthaṁ manovākkāyavartmanā|<br />
Yadyatsamācaredvīraḥ kulayāgaḥ sa sa smṛtaḥ||29.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.7">29.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bahiḥ śaktau yāmale ca dehe prāṇapathe matau|<br />
Iti ṣoḍhā kulejyā syātpratibhedaṁ vibhedinī||29.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.8">29.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Snānamaṇḍalakuṇḍādi ṣoḍhānyāsādi yanna tat|<br />
Kiñcidatropayujyeta kṛtaṁ vā khaṇḍanāya no||29.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.9">29.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ṣaṇmaṇḍalavinirmuktaṁ sarvāvaraṇavarjitam|<br />
Jñānajñeyamayaṁ kaulaṁ proktaṁ traiśirase mate||29.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.10">29.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atra yāge ca yaddravyaṁ niṣiddhaṁ śāstrasantatau|<br />
Tadeva yojayeddhīmānvāmāmṛtapariplutam||29.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.11">29.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrībrahmayāmale'pyuktaṁ surā śivaraso bahiḥ|<br />
Tāṁ vinā bhuktimuktī no piṣṭakṣaudraguḍaistu sā||29.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.12">29.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Strīnapuṁsakapuṁrūpā tu pūrvāparabhogadā|<br />
Drākṣotthaṁ tu paraṁ tejo bhairavaṁ kalpanojjhitam||29.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.13">29.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Etatsvayaṁ rasaḥ śuddhaḥ prakāśānandacinmayaḥ|<br />
Devatānāṁ priyaṁ nityaṁ tasmādetatpivetsadā||29.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.14">29.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīmatkramarahasye ca nyarūpi parameśinā|<br />
Arghapātraṁ yāgadhāma dīpa ityucyate trayam||29.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.15">29.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Rahasyaṁ kaulike yāge tatrārghaḥ śaktisaṅgamāt|<br />
Bhūvastrakāyapīṭhākhyaṁ dhāma cotkarṣabhākkramāt||29.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.16">29.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dīpā ghṛtotthā gāvo hi bhūcaryo devatāḥ smṛtāḥ|<br />
Iti jñātvā traye'muṣminyatnavānkauliko bhavet||29.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.17">29.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tenārghapātraprādhānyaṁ jñātvā dravyāṇi śambhunā|<br />
Yānyuktānyaviśaṅko'tra bhavecchaṅkā hi dūṣikā||29.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.18">29.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yāgauko gandhadhūpāḍhyaṁ praviśya prāgudaṅmukhaḥ|<br />
Parayā vā'tha mālinyā vilomāccānulomataḥ||29.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.19">29.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dāhāpyāyamayīṁ śuddhiṁ dīptasaumyavibhedataḥ|<br />
Krameṇa kuryādathavā mātṛsadbhāvamantrataḥ||29.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.20">29.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dīkṣāṁ cetpracikīrṣustacchodhyādhvanyāsakalpanam|<br />
Tataḥ saṁśodhyavastūni śaktyaivāmṛtatāṁ nayet||29.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.21">29.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Parāsampuṭagā yadvā mātṛsampuṭagāpyatho|<br />
Kevalā mālinī yadvā tāḥ samasteṣu karmasu||29.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.22">29.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nandahetuphalairdravyairarghapātraṁ prapūrayet|<br />
Tatroktamantratādātmyādbhairavātmatvamānayet||29.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.23">29.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tena nirbharamātmānaṁ bahiścakrānucakragam|<br />
Vipruḍbhirūrdhvādharayorantaḥ pītyā ca tarpayet||29.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.24">29.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathā pūrṇasvaraśmyoghaḥ procchaladvṛttitāvaśāt|<br />
Bahistādṛśamātmānaṁ didṛkṣurbahirarcayet||29.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.25">29.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Arkāṅgule'tha taddvitriguṇe raktapaṭe śubhe|<br />
Vyomni sindūrasubhage rājavarttabhṛte'thavā||29.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.26">29.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nārikelātmake kādye madyapūrṇe'tha bhājane|<br />
Yadvā samudite rūpe maṇḍalasthe ca tadṛśi||29.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.27">29.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yāgaṁ kurvīta matimāṁstatrāyaṁ krama ucyate|<br />
Diśyudīcyāṁ rudrakoṇādvāyavyantaṁ gaṇeśvaram||29.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.28">29.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vaṭukaṁ trīngurūnsiddhānyoginīḥ pīṭhamarcayet|<br />
Prācyāṁ diśi gaṇeśādha ārabhyābhyarcayettataḥ||29.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.29">29.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Siddhacakraṁ dikcatuṣke gaṇeśādhastanāntakam|<br />
Khagendraḥ sahavijjāmba illāīambayā saha||29.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.30">29.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vaktaṣṭirvimalo'nantamekhalāmbāyutaḥ purā|<br />
Śaktyā maṅgalayā kūrma illāīambayā saha||29.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.31">29.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jaitro yāmye hyavijitastathā sānandamekhalaḥ|<br />
Kāmamaṅgalayā meṣaḥ kullāīambayā saha||29.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.32">29.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vindhyo'jito'pyajarayā saha mekhalayā pare|<br />
Macchandaḥ kuṅkuṇāmbā ca ṣaḍyugmaṁ sādhikārakam||29.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.33">29.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saumye marutta īśāntaṁ dvitīyā paṅktirīdṛśī|<br />
Amaravaradevacitrālivindhyaguḍikā iti kramātṣaḍamī||29.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.34">29.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sillāī eruṇayā tathā kumārī ca bodhāī|<br />
Samahālacchī cāparamekhalayā śaktayaḥ ṣaḍimāḥ||29.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.35">29.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ete hi sādhikārāḥ pūjyā yeṣāmiyaṁ bahuvibhedā|<br />
Santatiranavacchinnā citrā śiṣyapraśiṣyamayī||29.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.36">29.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ānandāvalibodhiprabhupādāntātha yogiśabdāntā|<br />
Etā ovallyaḥ syurmudrāṣaṭkaṃ kramāttvetat||29.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.37">29.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dakṣāṅguṣṭhādikaniṣṭhikāntamatha sā kanīyasī vāmāt|<br />
Dvidaśāntordhvagakuṇḍalibaindavahṛnnābhikandamiti chummāḥ||29.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.38">29.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śavarāḍabillapaṭṭillāḥ karabillāmbiśarabillāḥ|<br />
Aḍabīḍombīdakṣiṇabillāḥ kumbhārikākṣarākhyā ca||29.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.39">29.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Devīkoṭṭakulādritripurīkāmākhyamaṭṭahāsaśca|<br />
Dakṣiṇapīṭhaṃ caitatṣaṭkaṃ gharapallipīṭhagaṃ kramaśaḥ||29.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.40">29.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti saṅketābhijño bhramate pīṭheṣu yadi sa siddhīpsuḥ|<br />
Acirāllabhate tattatprāpyaṁ yadyoginīvadanāt||29.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.41">29.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhaṭṭendravalkalāhīndragajendrāḥ samahīdharāḥ|<br />
Ūrdhvaretasa ete ṣaḍadhikārapadojjhitāḥ||29.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.42">29.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Adhikāro hi vīryasya prasaraḥ kulavartmani|<br />
Tadaprasarayogena te proktā ūrdhvaretasaḥ||29.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.43">29.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anyāśca gurutatpatnyaḥ śrīmatkālīkuloditāḥ|<br />
Anāttadehāḥ krīḍanti taistairdehairaśaṅkitāḥ||29.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.44">29.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prabodhitatathecchākaistajje kaulaṁ prakāśate|<br />
Tathārūpatayā tatra gurutvaṁ paribhāṣitam||29.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.45">29.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Te viśeṣānna sampūjyāḥ smartavyā eva kevalam|<br />
Tato'bhyantarato vāyuvahnyormātṛkayā saha||29.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.46">29.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mālinī kramaśaḥ pūjyā tato'ntarmantracakrakam|<br />
Mantrasiddhaprāṇasaṁvitkaraṇātmani yā kule||29.46||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.47">29.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Cakrātmake citiḥ prabhvī proktā seha kuleśvarī|<br />
Sā madhye śrīparā devī mātṛsadbhāvarūpiṇī||29.47||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.48">29.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pūjyātha tatsamāropādaparātha parāparā|<br />
Ekavīrā ca sā pūjyā yadivā sakuleśvarā||29.48||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.49">29.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prasarecchaktirucchūnā sollāso bhairavaḥ punaḥ|<br />
Saṅghaṭṭānandaviśrāntyā yugmamitthaṁ prapūjayet||29.49||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.50"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.50">29.50</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mahāprakāśarūpāyāḥ saṁvido visphuliṅgavat|<br />
Yo raśmyoghastamevātra pūjayeddevatāgaṇam||29.50||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.51"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.51">29.51</a> <span class="pv-iast">Antardvādaśakaṁ pūjyaṁ tato'ṣṭāṣṭākameva ca|<br />
Catuṣkaṁ vā yathecchaṁ vā kā saṅkhyā kila raśmiṣu||29.51||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.52"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.52">29.52</a> <span class="pv-iast">Māheśī vairiñcī kaumārī vaiṣṇavī caturdikkam|<br />
Aindrī yāmyā muṇḍā yogeśīrīśatastu koṇeṣu||29.52||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.53"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.53">29.53</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pavanāntamaghorādikamaṣṭakamasminnathāṣṭake kramaśaḥ|<br />
Saṅghaṭṭānandadṛśā sampūjyaṁ yāmalībhūtam||29.53||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.54"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.54">29.54</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aṣṭāṣṭake'pi hi vidhau nānānāmaprapañcite bahudhā|<br />
Vidhireṣa eva vihitastatsaṅkhyā dīpamālā syāt||29.54||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.55"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.55">29.55</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīratnamālāśāstre tu varṇasaṅkhyāḥ pradīpakāḥ|<br />
Varṇāṁśca mukhyapūjyāyā vidyāyā gaṇayetsudhīḥ||29.55||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.56"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.56">29.56</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pīṭhakṣetrādibhiḥ sākaṁ kuryādvā kulapūjanam|<br />
Yathā śrīmādhavakule parameśena bhāṣitam||29.56||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.57"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.57">29.57</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sṛṣṭisaṁsthitisaṁhārānāmakramacatuṣṭayam|<br />
Pīṭhaśmaśānasahitaṁ pūjayedbhogamokṣayoḥ||29.57||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.58"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.58">29.58</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ātmano vāthavā śakteścakrasyātha smaredimam|<br />
Nyasyatvena vidhiṁ dehe pīṭhākhye pārameśvaram||29.58||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.59"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.59">29.59</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aṭṭahāsaṁ śikhāsthāne caritraṁ ca karandhrake|<br />
Śrutyoḥ kaulagiriṁ nāsārandhrayośca jayantikām||29.59||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.60"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.60">29.60</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhruvorujjayinīṁ vaktre prayāgaṁ hṛdaye punaḥ|<br />
Vārāṇasīṁ skandhayuge śrīpīṭhaṁ virajaṁ gale||29.60||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.61"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.61">29.61</a> <span class="pv-iast">Eḍābhīmudare hālāṁ nābhau kande tu gośrutim|<br />
Upasthe marukośaṁ ca nagaraṁ pauṇḍravardhanam||29.61||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.62"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.62">29.62</a> <span class="pv-iast">Elāpuraṁ purastīraṁ sakthyūrvordakṣiṇāditaḥ|<br />
Kuḍyākeśīṁ ca sopānaṁ māyāpūkṣīrake tathā||29.62||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.63"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.63">29.63</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jānujaṅghe gulphayugme tvāmrātanṛpasadmanī|<br />
Pādādhāre tu vairiñcīṁ kālāgnyavadhidhārikām||29.63||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.64"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.64">29.64</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nāhamasmi na cānyo'sti kevalāḥ śaktayastvaham|<br />
Ityevaṁvāsanāṁ kuryātsarvadā smṛtimātrataḥ||29.64||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.65"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.65">29.65</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na tithirna ca nakṣatraṁ nopavāso vidhīyate|<br />
Grāmyadharmarataḥ siddhyetsarvadā smaraṇena hi||29.65||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.66"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.66">29.66</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mātaṅgakṛṣṇasaunikakārmukacārmikavikoṣidhātuvibhedāḥ|<br />
Mātsyikacākrikadayitāsteṣāṁ patnyo navātra navayāge||29.66||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.67"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.67">29.67</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṅgamavaruṇākulagiryaṭṭahāsajayantīcaritrakāmrakakoṭṭam|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.280"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.280">29.280</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṅghaṭṭotpāṭayogena vedhayedgranthipañcakam|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.281"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.281">29.281</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nātmavyomabahirmantradehasandhānamācaret|<br />
Dīkṣeyaṁ sarvajantūnāṁ śivatāpattidāyikā||29.281||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.282"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.282">29.282</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dīkṣānte dīpakānpaktvā samastaiḥ sādhakaiḥ saha|<br />
Caruḥ prāśyaḥ kulācāryairmahāpātakanāśanaḥ||29.282||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.283"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.283">29.283</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti śrīratnamālāyāmūnādhikavidhistu yaḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.284"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.284">29.284</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pare'hani guroḥ kāryo yāgastena vinā yataḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.285"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.285">29.285</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yena yena gurustuṣyettattadasmai nivedayet|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.286"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.286">29.286</a> <span class="pv-iast">Alipātraṁ susampūrṇaṁ vīrendrakarasaṁsthitam|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.287"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.287">29.287</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tarpayitvā tu bhūtāni gurave vinivedayet|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.288"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.288">29.288</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svaṁ mantraṁ tacca vanditvā dūtīṁ gaṇapatiṁ gurūn|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.289"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.289">29.289</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vīraspṛṣṭaṁ svayaṁ dravyaṁ pivennaivānyathā kvacit|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.290"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.290">29.290</a> <span class="pv-iast">Lobhamohamadakrodharāgamāyājuṣaśca ye|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v29.291"><a class="ta-num" href="#v29.291">29.291</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yāgādau yāgamadhye ca yāgānte gurupūjane|<br />
