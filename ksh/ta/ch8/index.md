<p class="ta-st" id="v8.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.8">8.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatsamastaṁ svasaṁvittau sā saṁvidbharitātmikā|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.9">8.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīmahīkṣottare caitānadhveśān gururabravīt|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.10">8.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Rudro granthau ca māyāyāmīśaḥ sādākhyagocare|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.11">8.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ śivatvamāpannamiti matvā nyarūpyata|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.13">8.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Avyāhatavibhāgo'smibhāvo mūlaṁ tu bodhagam|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.14">8.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bodhamadhyaṁ bhavetkiñcidādhārādheyalakṣaṇam|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.15">8.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bodhāgraṁ tattu cidbodhaṁ nistaraṅgaṁ bṛhatsukham|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.16">8.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Avyavacchinnasaṁvittirbhairavaḥ parameśvaraḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.17">8.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Adhvānaṁ ṣaḍvidhaṁ dhyāyansadyaḥ śivamayo bhavet|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.18">8.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pūrṇasyordhvādimadhyāntavyavasthā nāsti vāstavī|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.19">8.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svasvarūpānusāreṇa madhyāditvādikalpanāḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.20">8.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tattvaṁ sarvāntarālasthaṁ yatsarvāvaraṇairvṛtam|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.21">8.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Netā kaṭāharudrāṇāmanantaḥ kāmasevinām|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.22">8.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sa devaṁ bhairavaṁ dhyāyan nāgaiśca parivāritaḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.23">8.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Lokānāṁ bhasmasādbhāvabhayānnordhvaṁ sa vīkṣate|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.24">8.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Narakebhyaḥ purā vyaktastenāsau tadadho mataḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.25">8.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadūrdhve narakādhīśāḥ kramādduḥkhaikavedanāḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.26">8.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Avīcikumbhīpākākhyarauravāsteṣvanukramāt|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.27">8.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pratyekameṣāmekonā koṭirucchritirantaram|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.28">8.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kūṣmāṇḍa ūrdhve lakṣonakoṭisthānastadīśitā|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.29">8.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatra bhīmairlokapuruṣaiḥ pīḍyante bhogaparyantam|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.30">8.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yānti na te narakayujaḥ kṛṣṇaṁ teṣāṁ sukhālpatādāyi|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.31">8.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pātālāṣṭakamekaikamaṣṭame hāṭakaḥ prabhuḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.32">8.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Siddhīrdadātyasāvevaṁ śrīmadrauravaśāsane|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.33">8.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dīkṣitā api ye luptasamayā naca kurvate|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.34">8.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Devāgnidravyavṛttyaṁśajīvinaścottamasthitāḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.35">8.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Te hāṭakavibhoragre kiṅkarā vividhātmakāḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.36">8.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadīśatattve līyante kramācca parame śive|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.37">8.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Te kālavahnisantāpadīnākrandaparāyaṇāḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.38">8.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pātyante mātṛbhirghorayātanaughapurassaram|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.39">8.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mānuṣānteṣu tatrāpi kecinmantravidaḥ kramāt|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.40">8.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ityeṣa gaṇavṛttānto nāmnā hulahulādinā|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.41">8.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pātālordhve sahasrāṇi viṁśatirbhūkaṭāhakaḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.42">8.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhadrakālyāḥ puraṁ yatra tābhiḥ krīḍanti sādhakāḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.43">8.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Etāni yātanāsthānaṁ gurumantrādidūṣiṇām|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.45">8.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Madhyordhvādhaḥ samudvṛttaśarāvacaturaśrakaḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.47">8.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jyotiṣkaśikharaṁ śambhoḥ śrīkaṇṭhāṁśaśca sa prabhuḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.48">8.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Cakravāṭaścaturdikko meruratra tu lokapāḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.49">8.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Apsaraḥsiddhasādhyāstāmuttareṇa vināyakāḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.58"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.58">8.58</a> <span class="pv-iast">Te merugāḥ sakṛcchambhuṁ ye vārcanti yathocitam|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.59"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.59">8.59</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mandaro gandhamādaśca vipulo'tha supārśvakaḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.60"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.60">8.60</a> <span class="pv-iast">Etairbhuvamavaṣṭabhya merustiṣṭhati niścalaḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.416"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.416">8.416</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadatha mahādevāṣṭakamiti buddhau saptadaśa saṅkhyā|<br />
Guṇatattve paṅktitrayamiti ṣaṭpañcāśataṁ purāṇi viduḥ||8.416||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.417"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.417">8.417</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yadyapi guṇasāmyātmani mūle krodheśvarāṣṭakaṁ tathāpi dhiyi|<br />
Tacchodhitamiti gaṇanāṁ na punaḥ prāptaṁ pratiṣṭhāyām||8.417||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.418"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.418">8.418</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti jalatattvānmūlaṁ tattvacaturviṁśatiḥ pratiṣṭhāyām|<br />
Ambādituṣṭivargastārādyāḥ siddhayo'ṇimādigaṇaḥ||8.418||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.419"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.419">8.419</a> <span class="pv-iast">Guravo guruśiṣyā ṛṣivarga iḍādiśca vigrahāṣṭakayuk|<br />
Gandhādivikārapuraṁ buddhiguṇāṣṭakamahaṅkriyā viṣayaguṇāḥ||8.419||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.420"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.420">8.420</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kāmādisaptaviṁśakamāgantu tathā gaṇeśavidyeśamayau|<br />
Iti pāśeṣu puratrayamitthaṁ puruṣe'tra bhuvanaṣoḍaśakam||8.420||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.421"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.421">8.421</a> <span class="pv-iast">Niyatau śaṅkaradaśakaṁ kāle śivadaśakamiti puradvitayam|<br />
Rāge suhṛṣṭabhuvanaṁ guruśiṣyapuraṁ ca vitkalāyugale||8.421||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.422"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.422">8.422</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhuvanaṁ bhuvanaṁ niśi puṭapuratrayaṁ vākpuraṁ pramāṇapuram|<br />
Iti saptaviṁśatipurā vidyā puruṣāditattvasaptakayuk||8.422||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.423"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.423">8.423</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vāmeśarūpasūkṣmaṁ śuddhaṁ vidyātha śaktitejasvimitiḥ|<br />
Suviśuddhiśivau mokṣaṁ dhruveṣisambuddhasamayasauśivasañjñāḥ||8.423||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.424"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.424">8.424</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saptadaśapurā śāntā vidyeśasadāśivapuratritayayuktā|<br />
Bindvardhendunirodhyaḥ parasauśivamindhikādipurasauṣumne||8.424||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.425"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.425">8.425</a> <span class="pv-iast">Paranādo brahmabilaṁ sūkṣmādiyutordhvakuṇḍalī śaktiḥ|<br />
Vyāpivyomānantānāthānāśritapurāṇi pañca tataḥ||8.425||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.426"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.426">8.426</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ṣaṣṭhaṁ ca paramamanāśritamatha samanābhuvanaṣoḍaśī yadi vā|<br />
Bindvāvaraṇaṁ parasauśivaṁ ca pañcendhikādibhuvanāni||8.426||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.427"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.427">8.427</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sauṣumnaṁ brahmabilaṁ kuṇḍalinī vyāpipañcakaṁ samanā|<br />
Iti ṣoḍaśabhuvaneyaṁ tattvayugaṁ śāntyatītā syāt||8.427||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.428"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.428">8.428</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīmanmataṅgaśāstre ca kramo'yaṁ purapūgagaḥ|<br />
Kālāgnirnarakāḥ khābdhiyutaṁ mukhyatayā śatam||8.428||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.429"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.429">8.429</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kūṣmāṇḍaḥ saptapātālī saptalokī maheśvaraḥ|<br />
Ityaṇḍamadhyaṁ tadbāhye śataṁ rudrā iti sthitāḥ||8.429||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.430"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.430">8.430</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sthānānāṁ dviśatī bhūmiḥ saptapañcāśatā yutā|<br />
Pañcāṣṭakasya madhyāddvātriṁśadbhūtacatuṣṭaye||8.430||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.431"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.431">8.431</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tanmātreṣu ca pañca syurviśvedevāstato'ṣṭakam|<br />
Pañcamaṁ sendriye garve buddhau devāṣṭakaṁ guṇe||8.431||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.432"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.432">8.432</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yogāṣṭakaṁ krodhasañjñaṁ mūle kāle sanaiyate|<br />
Patadrugādyāścāṅguṣṭhamātrādyā rāgatattvagāḥ||8.432||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.433"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.433">8.433</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dvādaśaikaśivādyāḥ syurvidyāyāṁ kalane daśa|<br />
Vāmādyāstriśatī seyaṁ triparvaṇyabdhirasyayuk||8.433||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.434"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.434">8.434</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śaivāḥ kecidihānantāḥ śraikaṇṭhā iti saṅgrahaḥ|<br />
Yatra yadā parabhogān bubhukṣate tatra yojanaṁ kāryam||8.434||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.435"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.435">8.435</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śodhanamatha taddhānau śeṣaṁ tvantargataṁ kāryam|<br />
Ityāgamaṁ prathayituṁ darśitametadvikalpitaṁ tena||8.435||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.436"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.436">8.436</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anye'pi bahuvikalpāḥ svadhiyācāryaiḥ samabhyūhyāḥ|<br />
Śrīpūrvaśāsane punaraṣṭādaśādhikaṁ śataṁ kathitam||8.436||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.437"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.437">8.437</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadiha pradhānamadhikaṁ saṅkṣepeṇocyate śodhyam|<br />
Kālāgniḥ kūṣmāṇḍo narakeśo hāṭako'tha bhūtalapaḥ||8.437||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.438"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.438">8.438</a> <span class="pv-iast">Brahmā munilokeśo rudrāḥ pañcāntarālasthāḥ|<br />
Adhare'nantaḥ prācyāḥ kapālivahnyantanirṛtibalākhyāḥ||8.438||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.439"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.439">8.439</a> <span class="pv-iast">Laghunidhipatividyādhipaśambhūrdhvāntaṁ savīrabhadrapati|<br />
Ekādaśabhirbāhye brahmāṇḍaṁ pañcabhistathāntarikaiḥ||8.439||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.440"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.440">8.440</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti ṣoḍaśapurametannivṛttikalayeha kalanīyam|<br />
Lakulīśabhārabhūtī diṇḍyāṣāḍhī ca puṣkaranimeṣau||8.440||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.441"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.441">8.441</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prabhāsasureśāviti salile pratyātmakaṁ saparivāre|<br />
Bhairavakedāramahākālā madhyāmrajalpākhyāḥ||8.441||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.442"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.442">8.442</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīśailahariścandrāviti guhyāṣṭakamidaṁ mahasi|<br />
Bhīmendrāṭṭahāsavimalakanakhalanākhalakurusthitigayākhyāḥ||8.442||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.443"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.443">8.443</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atiguhyāṣṭakametanmaruti ca satanmātrake ca sākṣe ca|<br />
Sthāṇusuvarṇākhyau kila bhadro gokarṇako mahālayakaḥ||8.443||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.444"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.444">8.444</a> <span class="pv-iast">Avimuktarudrakoṭī vastrāpada ityadaḥ pavitraṁ khe|<br />
Sthūlasthūleśaśaṅkuśrutikālañjarāśca maṇḍalabhṛt||8.444||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.445"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.445">8.445</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mākoṭāṇḍadvitayacchagalāṇḍā aṣṭakaṁ hyahaṅkāre|<br />
Anye'haṅkārāntastanmātrāṇīndriyāṇi cāpyāhuḥ||8.445||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.446"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.446">8.446</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dhiyi yonyaṣṭakamuktaṁ prakṛtau yogāṣṭakaṁ kilākṛtaprabhṛti|<br />
Iti saptāṣṭakabhuvanā pratiṣṭhitiḥ salilato hi mūlāntā||8.446||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.447"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.447">8.447</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nari vāmo bhīmograu bhaveśavīrāḥ pracaṇḍagaurīśau|<br />
Ajasānantaikaśivau vidyāyāṁ krodhacaṇḍayugmaṁ syāt||8.447||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.448"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.448">8.448</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṁvarto jyotiratho kalāniyatyāṁ ca sūrapañcāntau|<br />
Vīraśikhīśaśrīkaṇṭhasañjñametattrayaṁ ca kāle syāt||8.448||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.449"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.449">8.449</a> <span class="pv-iast">Samahātejā vāmo bhavodbhavaścaikapiṅgaleśānau|<br />
Bhuvaneśapuraḥsarakāvaṅguṣṭha ime niśi sthitā hyaṣṭau||8.449||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.450"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.450">8.450</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aṣṭāviṁśatibhuvanā vidyā puruṣānniśāntamiyam|<br />
Hālāhalarudrakrudambikāghorikāḥ savāmāḥ syuḥ||8.450||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.451"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.451">8.451</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vidyāyāṁ vidyeśāstvaṣṭāvīśe sadāśive pañca|<br />
Vāmā jyeṣṭhā raudrī śaktiḥ sakalā ca śāntāyām||8.451||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v8.452"><a class="ta-num" href="#v8.452">8.452</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aṣṭādaśa bhuvanā syāt śāntyatītā tvabhuvanaiva|<br />
Iti deśādhvavibhāgaḥ kathitaḥ śrīśambhunā samādiṣṭaḥ||8.452||</span></p>
