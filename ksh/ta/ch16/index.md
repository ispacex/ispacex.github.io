<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloke ṣoḍaśamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.1">16.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha putrakatvasiddhyai nirūpyate śivanirūpito'tra vidhiḥ|<br />
Yadā tu samayasthasya putrakatve niyojanam|<br />
Gurutve sādhakatve vā kartumicchati daiśikaḥ||16.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.2">16.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadādhivāsaṁ kṛtvāhni dvitīye maṇḍalaṁ likhet|<br />
Sāmudāyikayāge'tha tathānyatra yathoditam||16.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.3">16.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ṣaḍaṣṭataddviguṇitacaturviṁśatisaṅkhyayā|<br />
Cakrapañcakamākhyātaṁ śāstre śrīpūrvasañjñite||16.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.4">16.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dvātriṁśattaddviguṇitaṁ śrīmattraiśirase mate|<br />
Asaṅkhyacakrasambandhaḥ śrīsiddhādau nirūpitaḥ||16.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.5">16.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasmādyathā tathā yāgaṁ yāvaccakreṇa sammatam|<br />
Pūjayedyena tenātra triśūlatrayamālikhet||16.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.6">16.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Triśūlatritaye devītrayaṁ paryāyavṛttitaḥ|<br />
Madhyasavyānyabhedena pūrṇaṁ sampūjitaṁ bhavet||16.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.7">16.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vartanā maṇḍalasyāgre saṅkṣepādupadekṣyate|<br />
Ālikhya maṇḍalaṁ gandhavastreṇaivāsya mārjanam||16.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.8">16.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kṛtvā snāto guruḥ prāgvanmaṇḍalāgre'tra devatāḥ|<br />
Bāhyagāḥ pūjayeddvāradeśe ca dvāradevatāḥ||16.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.9">16.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Maṇḍalasya purobhāge tadaiśānadiśaḥ kramāt|<br />
Āgneyyantaṁ gaṇeśādīnkṣetrapāntānprapūjayet||16.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.10">16.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Gaṇapatiguruparamākhyāḥ parameṣṭhī pūrvasiddhavākkṣetrapatiḥ|<br />
Iti saptakamākhyātaṁ gurupaṅktividhau prapūjyamasmadgurubhiḥ||16.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.11">16.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tata ājñāṁ gṛhītvā tu puṣpadhūpādipūjitam|<br />
Pūjyamādhāraśaktyādi śūlamūlātprabhṛtyalam||16.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.12">16.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śivāntaṁ sitapadmānte triśūlānāṁ traye kramāt|<br />
Madhyaśūle madhyagaḥ syātsadbhāvaḥ parayā saha||16.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.13">16.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vāme cāparayā sākaṁ navātmā dakṣagaṁ param|<br />
Triśūle dakṣiṇe madhyaśṛṅgastho ratiśekharaḥ||16.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.14">16.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Syātparāparayā sākaṁ dakṣe bhairavasatpare|<br />
Vāme triśūle madhyastho navātmāparayā saha||16.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.15">16.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Syātpare parayā sākaṁ vāmāre saṁśca bhairavaḥ|<br />
Itthaṁ sarvagatatve śrīparādevyāḥ sthite sati||16.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.16">16.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yāgo bhavetsusampūrṇastadadhiṣṭhānamātrataḥ|<br />
Ekaśūle'pyato yāge cintayettadadhiṣṭhitam||16.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.17">16.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Avidhijño vidhānajña ityevaṁ trīśikoditam|<br />
Tato madhye tathā dakṣe vāme śṛṅge ca sarvataḥ||16.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.18">16.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Lokapālāstraparyantamekātmatvena pūjayet|<br />
Paratvena ca sarvāsāṁ devatānāṁ prapūjayet||16.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.19">16.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīmantaṁ mātṛsadbhāvabhaṭṭārakamanāmayam|<br />
Tato'pi bhogayāgena vidyāṅgaṁ bhairavāṣṭakam||16.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.20">16.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yāmalaṁ cakradevīśca svasthāne pūjayedbahiḥ|<br />
Lokapālānastrayutāngandhapuṣpāsavādibhiḥ||16.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.21">16.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pūjayetparayā bhaktyā vittaśāṭhyavivarjitaḥ|<br />
Tataḥ kumbhāstrakalaśīmaṇḍalasthānalātmanām||16.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.22">16.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pañcānāmanusandhānaṁ kuryādadvayabhāvanāt|<br />
Ye tu tāmadvayavyāptiṁ na vindanti śivātmikām||16.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.23">16.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mantranāḍīprayogeṇa te viśantyadvaye pathi|<br />
Svadakṣiṇena niḥsṛtya maṇḍalasthasya vāmataḥ||16.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.24">16.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Praviśyānyena niḥsṛtya kumbhasthe karkarīgate|<br />
Vahnisthe ca krameṇetthaṁ yāvatsvasminsvavāmataḥ||16.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.25">16.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mūlānusandhānabalātprāṇatantūmbhane sati|<br />
Itthamaikyasphurattātmā vyāptisaṁvitprakāśate||16.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.26">16.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tato viśeṣapūjāṁ ca kuryādadvayabhāvitām|<br />
Yacchivādvayapīyūṣasaṁsiktaṁ paramaṁ hi tat||16.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.27">16.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tenārghapuṣpagandhāderāsavasya paśoratha|<br />
Yā śivādvayatādṛṣṭiḥ sā śuddhiḥ paramīkṛtiḥ||16.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.28">16.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nivedayedvibhoragre jīvāndhātūṁstadutthitān|<br />
Siddhānasiddhānvyāmiśrānyadvā kiñciccarācaram||16.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.29">16.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dṛṣṭaprokṣitasandraṣṭṛprālabdhopāttayojitāḥ|<br />
Nirvāpito vīrapaśuḥ so'ṣṭadhottaratottamāḥ||16.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.30">16.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathottaraṁ na dātavyamayogyebhyaḥ kadācana|<br />
Śivopayuktaṁ hi havirna sarvo bhoktumarhati||16.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.31">16.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yastu dīkṣāvihīno'pi śivecchāvidhicoditaḥ|<br />
Bhaktyāśnāti sa sampūrṇaḥ samayī syātsubhāvitaḥ||16.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.32">16.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dṛṣṭo'valokitaścaiva kiraṇeddhadṛgarpaṇāt|<br />
Prokṣitaḥ kevalaṁ hyarghapātravipruḍbhirukṣitaḥ||16.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.33">16.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sandraṣṭā darśitāśeṣasamyakpūjitamaṇḍalaḥ|<br />
Prālabdha uktatritayasaṁskṛtaḥ so'pi dhūnayet||16.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.34">16.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kampeta prasravetstabdhaḥ pralīno vā yathottaram|<br />
Upātto yāgasānnidhye śamitaḥ śastramārutaiḥ||16.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.35">16.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yojitaḥ kāraṇatyāgakrameṇa śivayojanāt|<br />
Nirvāpitaḥ kṛtābhyāsaguruprāṇamano'rpaṇāt||16.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.36">16.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dakṣiṇenāgninā saumyakalājālavilāpanāt|<br />
Tathā hyādau paraṁ rūpamekībhāvena saṁśrayet||16.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.37">16.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasmādāgneyacāreṇa jvālāmālāmupāviśet|<br />
Paśorvāmena candrāṁśujālaṁ tāpena gālayet||16.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.38">16.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nābhicakre'tha viśrāmyetprāṇaraśmigaṇaiḥ saha|<br />
Paro bhūtvā svaśaktyātra jīvaṁ jīvena veṣṭayet||16.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.39">16.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svacitsūryeṇa santāpya drāvayetkalāṁ kalām|<br />
Tato drutaṁ kalājālaṁ prāpayyaikatvamātmani||16.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.40">16.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Samastatattvasampūrṇamāpyāyanavidhāyinam|<br />
Unmūlayeta saṁrambhātkarmabandhādamuṁ rasāt||16.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.41">16.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tata unmūlanodveṣṭayogādvāmaṁ paribhraman|<br />
Kuṇḍalyamṛtasampūrṇasvakaprāṇaprasevakaḥ||16.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.42">16.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vāmāvartakramopāttahṛtpadmāmṛtakesaraḥ|<br />
Hṛtkarṇikārūḍhilābhādojodhātuṁ vilāpitam||16.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.43">16.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śuddhasomātmakaṁ sāramīṣallohitapītalam|<br />
Ādāya karihastāgrasadṛśe prāṇavigrahe||16.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.44">16.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Niḥsṛtya jhaṭiti svātmavāmamārgeṇa saṁviśet|<br />
Āpyāyayannapānākhyacandracakraṁ hṛdambuje||16.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.45">16.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sthitaṁ taddevatācakraṁ tena sāreṇa tarpayet|<br />
Anena vidhinā sarvānrasaraktādikāṁstathā||16.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.46">16.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dhātūnsamāharetsaṅghakramādekaikaśo'thavā|<br />
Kevalaṁ tvathavāgnīnduravisaṅghaṭṭamadhyagam||16.46||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.47">16.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jyotīrūpamatha prāṇaśaktyākhyaṁ jīvamāharet|<br />
Jīvaṁ samarasīkuryāddevīcakreṇa bhāvanāt||16.47||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.48">16.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadeva tarpaṇaṁ mukhyaṁ bhogyabhoktrātmataiva sā|<br />
Agnisampuṭaphullārṇatryaśrakālātmako mahān||16.48||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.49">16.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Piṇḍo raktādisāraughacālanākarṣaṇādiṣu|<br />
Itthaṁ viśrāntiyogena ghaṭikārdhakrame sati||16.49||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.50"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.50">16.50</a> <span class="pv-iast">Āvṛttiśatayogena paśornirvāpaṇaṁ bhavet|<br />
Kṛtvā katipayaṁ kālaṁ tatrābhyāsamananyadhīḥ||16.50||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.51"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.51">16.51</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathā cintāmaṇau proktaṁ tena rūpeṇa yogavit|<br />
Niḥśaṅkaḥ siddhimāpnoti gopyaṁ tatprāṇavatsphuṭam||16.51||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.52"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.52">16.52</a> <span class="pv-iast">Parokṣe'pi paśāvevaṁ vidhiḥ syādyojanaṁ prati|<br />
Praveśito yāgabhuvi hatastatraiva sādhitaḥ||16.52||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.53"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.53">16.53</a> <span class="pv-iast">Cakrajuṣṭaśca tatraiva sa vīrapaśurucyate|<br />
Yastvanyatrāpi nihataḥ sāmastyenāṁśato'pi vā||16.53||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.54"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.54">16.54</a> <span class="pv-iast">Devāya vinivedyeta sa vai bāhyapaśurmataḥ|<br />
Rājyaṁ lābho'tha tatsthairyaṁ śive bhaktistadātmatā||16.54||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.55"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.55">16.55</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śivajñānaṁ mantralokaprāptistatparivāratā|<br />
Tatsāyujyaṁ paśoḥ sāmyādbāhyādervīradharmaṇaḥ||16.55||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.56"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.56">16.56</a> <span class="pv-iast">Puṣpādayo'pi tallābhabhāginaḥ śivapūjayā|<br />
Ekopāyena deveśo viśvānugrahaṇātmakaḥ||16.56||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.57"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.57">16.57</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yāgenaivānugṛhṇāti kiṁ kiṁ yanna carācaram|<br />
Tenāvīro'pi śaṅkādiyuktaḥ kāruṇiko'pi ca||16.57||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.58"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.58">16.58</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na hiṁsābuddhimādadhyātpaśukarmaṇi jātucit|<br />
Paśormahopakāro'yaṁ tadātve'pyapriyaṁ bhavet||16.58||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.59"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.59">16.59</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vyādhicchedauṣadhatapoyojanātra nidarśanam|<br />
Śrīmanmṛtyuñjaye proktaṁ pāśacchede kṛte paśoḥ||16.59||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.60"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.60">16.60</a> <span class="pv-iast">Malatrayaviyogena śarīraṁ na prarohati|<br />
Dharmādharmaughavicchedāccharīraṁ cyavate kila||16.60||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.61"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.61">16.61</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tenaitanmāraṇaṁ noktaṁ dīkṣeyaṁ citrarūpiṇī|<br />
Rūḍhapāśasya yaḥ prāṇairviyogo māraṇaṁ hi tat||16.61||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.62"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.62">16.62</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iyaṁ tu yojanaiva syātpaśordevāya tarpaṇe|<br />
Tasmāddevoktimāśritya paśūndadyādbahūniti||16.62||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.63"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.63">16.63</a> <span class="pv-iast">Niveditaḥ punaḥ prāptadeho bhūyo niveditaḥ|<br />
Ṣaṭkṛtva itthaṁ yaḥ so'tra ṣaḍjanmā paśuruttamaḥ||16.63||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.64"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.64">16.64</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathā pākakramācchuddhaṁ hema tadvatsa kīrtitaḥ|<br />
Kāṁ siddhiṁ naiva vitaretsvayaṁ kiṁ vā na mucyate||16.64||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.65"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.65">16.65</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktaṁ tvānandaśāstre yo mantrasaṁskāravāṁstyajet|<br />
Samayānkutsayeddevīrdadyānmantrānvinā nayāt||16.65||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.66"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.66">16.66</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dīkṣāmantrādikaṁ prāpya tyajetputrādimohitaḥ|<br />
Tato manuṣyatāmetya punarevaṁ karotyapi||16.66||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.67"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.67">16.67</a> <span class="pv-iast">Itthamekādisaptāntajanmāsau dvividho dvipāt|<br />
Catuṣpādvā paśurdevīcarukārthaṁ prajāyate||16.67||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.68"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.68">16.68</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dātrarpito'sau taddvārā yāti sāyujyataḥ śivam|<br />

Bhāvyavastusphuṭībhāvaḥ sañjalpahrāsayogataḥ||16.280||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.281"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.281">16.281</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vastveva bhāvayatyeṣa na sañjalpamimaṁ punaḥ|<br />
Gṛhṇāti bhāsanopāyaṁ bhāte tatra tu tena kim||16.281||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.282"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.282">16.282</a> <span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ sañjalpanirhrāse suparisphuṭatātmakam|<br />
Akṛtrimavimarśātma sphuredvastvavikalpakam||16.282||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.283"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.283">16.283</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nirvikalpā ca sā saṁvidyadyathā paśyati sphuṭam|<br />
Tattathaiva tathātmatvādvastuno'pi bahiḥsthiteḥ||16.283||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.284"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.284">16.284</a> <span class="pv-iast">Viśeṣatastvamāyīyaśivatābhedaśālinaḥ|<br />
Mokṣe'bhyupāyaḥ sañjalpo bandhamokṣau tataḥ kila||16.284||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.285"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.285">16.285</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vikalpe'pi guroḥ samyagabhinnaśivatājuṣaḥ|<br />
Avikalpakaparyantaparīkṣā nopayujyate||16.285||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.286"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.286">16.286</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadvimarśasvabhāvā hi sā vācyā mantradevatā|<br />
Mahāsaṁvitsamāsannetyuktaṁ śrīgamaśāsane||16.286||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.287"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.287">16.287</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nikaṭasthā yathā rājñāmanyeṣāṁ sādhayantyalam|<br />
Siddhiṁ rājopagāṁ śīghramevaṁ mantrādayaḥ parām||16.287||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.288"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.288">16.288</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktābhiprāyagarbhaṁ taduktaṁ śrīmālinīmate|<br />
Mantrāṇāṁ lakṣaṇaṁ kasmādityukte munibhiḥ kila||16.288||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.289"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.289">16.289</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yogamekatvamicchanti vastuno'nyena vastunā|<br />
Yadvastu jñeyamityuktaṁ heyatvādiprasiddhaye||16.289||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.290"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.290">16.290</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatprasiddhyai śivenoktaṁ jñānaṁ yadupavarṇitam|<br />
Sabījayogasaṁsiddhyai mantralakṣaṇamapyalam||16.290||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.291"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.291">16.291</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na cādhikāritā dīkṣāṁ vinā yoge'sti śāṅkare|<br />
Kriyājñānavibhedena sā ca dvedhā nigadyate||16.291||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.292"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.292">16.292</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dvividhā sā prakartavyā tena caitadudāhṛtam|<br />
Na ca yogādhikāritvamekamevānayā bhavet||16.292||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.293"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.293">16.293</a> <span class="pv-iast">Api mantrādhikāritvaṁ muktiśca śivadīkṣayā|<br />
Anenaitadapi proktaṁ yogī tattvaikyasiddhaye||16.293||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.294"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.294">16.294</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mantramevāśrayenmūlaṁ nirvikalpāntamādṛtaḥ|<br />
Mantrābhyāsena bhogaṁ vā mokṣaṁ vāpi prasādhayan||16.294||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.295"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.295">16.295</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatrādhikāritālabdhyai dīkṣāṁ gṛhṇīta daiśikāt|<br />
Tena mantrajñānayogabalāttattatprasādhayet||16.295||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.296"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.296">16.296</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yatsyādasyānyatattve'pi yuktasya guruṇā śiśoḥ|<br />
Dīkṣā hyasyopayujyeta saṁskriyāyāṁ sa saṁskṛtaḥ||16.296||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.297"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.297">16.297</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svabalenaiva bhogaṁ vā mokṣaṁ vā labhate budhaḥ|<br />
Tena vijñānayogādibalī prāksamayī bhavan||16.297||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.298"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.298">16.298</a> <span class="pv-iast">Putrako vā na tāvānsyādapitu svabalocitaḥ|<br />
Yastu vijñānayogādivandhyaḥ so'ndho yathā pathi||16.298||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.299"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.299">16.299</a> <span class="pv-iast">Daiśikāyatta eva syādbhoge muktau ca sarvathā|<br />
Dīkṣā ca kevalā jñānaṁ vināpi nijamāntaram||16.299||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.300"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.300">16.300</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mocikaiveti kathitaṁ yuktyā cāgamataḥ purā|<br />
Yastu dīkṣākṛtāmevāpekṣya yojanikāṁ śiśuḥ||16.300||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.301"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.301">16.301</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sphuṭībhūtyai taducitaṁ jñānaṁ yogamathāśritaḥ|<br />
So'pi yatraiva yuktaḥ syāttanmayatvaṁ prapadyate||16.301||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.302"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.302">16.302</a> <span class="pv-iast">Gurudīkṣāmantraśāstrādhīnasarvasthitistataḥ|<br />
Duṣṭānāmeva sarveṣāṁ bhūtabhavyabhaviṣyatām||16.302||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.303"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.303">16.303</a> <span class="pv-iast">Karmaṇāṁ śodhanaṁ kāryaṁ bubhukṣorna śubhātmanām|<br />
Yaḥ punarlaukikaṁ bhogaṁ rājyasvargādikaṁ śiśuḥ||16.303||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.304"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.304">16.304</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tyaktvā lokottaraṁ bhogamīpsustasya śubheṣvapi|<br />
Tatra dravyamayīṁ dīkṣāṁ kurvannājyatilādikaiḥ||16.304||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.305"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.305">16.305</a> <span class="pv-iast">Karmāsya śodhayāmīti juhuyāddaiśikottamaḥ|<br />
Jñānamayyāṁ tu dīkṣāyāṁ tadviśuddhyati sandhitaḥ||16.305||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.306"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.306">16.306</a> <span class="pv-iast">Guroḥ svasaṁvidrūḍhasya balāttatprakṣayo bhavet|<br />
Yadāsyāśubhakarmāṇi śuddhāni syustadā śubham||16.306||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.307"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.307">16.307</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svatāratamyāśrayaṇādadhvamadhye prasūtidam|<br />
Śubhapākakramopāttaphalabhogasamāptitaḥ||16.307||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.308"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.308">16.308</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yatraiṣa yojitastatstho bhāvikarmakṣaye kṛte|<br />
Bhāvināṁ cādyadehasthadehāntaravibhedinām||16.308||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.309"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.309">16.309</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aśubhāṁśaviśuddhau syādbhogasyaivānupakṣayaḥ|<br />
Bhuñjānasyāsya satataṁ bhogānmāyālayāntataḥ||16.309||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.310"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.310">16.310</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na duḥkhaphaladaṁ dehādyadhvamadhye'pi kiñcana|<br />
Tato māyālaye bhuktasamastasukhabhogakaḥ||16.310||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v16.311"><a class="ta-num" href="#v16.311">16.311</a> <span class="pv-iast">Niṣkale sakale vaiti layaṁ yojanikābalāt|<br />
Iti prameyaṁ kathitaṁ dīkṣā kāle guroryathā||16.311||</span></p>
