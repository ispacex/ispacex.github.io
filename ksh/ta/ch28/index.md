<p class="ta-st" id="v28.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.1">28.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti nityavidhiḥ prokto naimittikamathocyate||28.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.2">28.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Niyataṁ bhāvi yannityaṁ tadityasminvidhau sthite|<br />
Mukhyatvaṁ tanmayībhūtaṁ sarvaṁ naimittikaṁ tataḥ||28.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.3">28.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dinādikalpanotthe tu naiyatye sarvanityatā|<br />
Dinamāsarkṣavarṣādinaiyatyāducyate tadā||28.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.4">28.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aśaṅkitavyāvaśyantāsattākaṁ jātucidbhavam|<br />
Pramātraniyataṁ prāhurnaimittikamidaṁ budhāḥ||28.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.5">28.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sandhyādi parvasampūjā pavitrakamidaṁ sadā|<br />
Nityaṁ niyatarūpatvātsarvasminśāsanāśrite||28.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.6">28.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jñānaśāstragurubhrātṛtadvargaprāptayastathā|<br />
Tajjanmasaṁskriyābhedāḥ svajanmotsavasaṅgatiḥ||28.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.7">28.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrāddhaṁ vipatpratīkāraḥ pramodo'dbhutadarśanam|<br />
Yoginīmelakaḥ svāṁśasantānādyaiśca melanam||28.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.8">28.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śāstravyākhyāpurāmadhyāvasānāni kramodayaḥ|<br />
Devatādarśanaṁ svāpnamājñā samayaniṣkṛtiḥ||28.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.9">28.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti naimittikaṁ śrīmattantrasāre nirūpitam|<br />
Trayoviṁśatibhedena viśeṣārcānibandhanam||28.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.10">28.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatra parvavidhiṁ brūmo dvidhā parva kulākulam|<br />
Kulāṣṭakakṛtaṁ pūrvaṁ proktaṁ śrīyogasañcare||28.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.11">28.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Abdhīndu munirityetanmāheśyā brahmasantateḥ|<br />
Pratipatpañcadaśyau dve kaumāryā rasavahniyuk||28.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.12">28.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Abdhirakṣīndu vaiṣṇavyā aindryāstvastraṁ trayodaśī|<br />
Vārāhyā randhrarudrau dve caṇḍyā vasvakṣiyugmakam||28.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.13">28.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dve dve tithī tu sarvāsāṁ yogeśyā daśamī punaḥ|<br />
Tasyā apyaṣṭamī yasmāddvitithiḥ sā prakīrtitā||28.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.14">28.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anyaiścākulaparvāpi vaiparītyena lakṣitam|<br />
Kulaparveti tadbrūmo yathoktaṁ bhairave kule||28.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.15">28.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Haiḍare trikasadbhāve trike kālīkulādike|<br />
Yo'yaṁ prāṇāśritaḥ pūrvaṁ kālaḥ proktaḥ suvistarāt||28.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.16">28.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sa cakrabhedasañcāre kāñcitsūte svasaṁvidam|<br />
Svasaṁvitpūrṇatālābhasamayaḥ parva bhaṇyate||28.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.17">28.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Parva pūraṇa ityeva yadvā pṝ pūraṇārthakaḥ|<br />
Parvaśabdo niruktaśca parva tatpūraṇāditi||28.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.18">28.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Haiḍare'tra ca śabdo'yaṁ dvidhā nāntetaraḥ śrutaḥ|<br />
Taccakracāraniṣṇātā ye kecitpūrṇasaṁvidaḥ||28.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.19">28.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tanmelakasamāyuktāste tatpūjāparāḥ sadā|<br />
Yo'pyatanmaya eṣo'pi tatkāle svakramārcanāt||28.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.20">28.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadyoginīsiddhasaṅghamelakāttanmayībhavet|<br />
Yathā prekṣaṇake tattaddraṣṭṛsaṁvidabheditām||28.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.21">28.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kramoditāṁ sadya eva labhate tatpraveśanāt|<br />
Yogābhyāsakramopāttāṁ tathā pūrṇāṁ svasaṁvidam||28.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.22">28.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Labhate sadya evaitatsaṁvidaikyapraveśanāt|<br />
Tatkālaṁ cāpi saṁvitteḥ pūrṇatvātkāmadogdhṛtā||28.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.23">28.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tena tattatphalaṁ tatra kāle sampūjayācirāt|<br />
Yathā ciropāttadhanaḥ kurvannutsavamādarāt||28.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.24">28.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atithiṁ so'nugṛhṇāti tatkālābhijñamāgatam|<br />
Tathā suphalasaṁsiddhyai yoginīsiddhanāyakāḥ||28.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.25">28.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yatnavanto'pi tatkālābhijñaṁ tamanugṛhṇate|<br />
Uktaṁ ca tatra teneha kule sāmānyatetyalam||28.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.26">28.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yasya yaddhṛdaye devi vartate daiśikājñayā|<br />
Mantro yogaḥ kramaścaiva pūjanātsiddhido bhavet||28.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.27">28.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kulācāreṇa deveśi pūjyaṁ siddhivimuktaye|<br />
Ye parvasveṣu deveśi tarpaṇaṁ tu viśeṣataḥ||28.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.28">28.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Gurūṇāṁ devatānāṁ ca na kurvanti pramādataḥ|<br />
Durācārā hi te duṣṭāḥ paśutulyā varānane||28.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.29">28.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Abhāvānnityapūjāyā avaśyaṁ hyeṣu pūjayet|<br />
Aṭanaṁ jñānaśaktyādilābhārthaṁ yatprakīrtitam||28.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.30">28.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śaktiyāgaśca yaḥ prokto vaśyākarṣaṇamāraṇam|<br />
Tatsarvaṁ parvadivaseṣvayatnenaiva siddhyati||28.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.31">28.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatsāmānyaviśeṣābhyāṁ ṣoḍhā parva nirūpitam|<br />
Māsasyādyaṁ pañcamaṁ ca śrīdinaṁ paribhāṣyate||28.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.32">28.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Utkṛṣṭatvātparvadinaṁ śrīpūrvatvena bhāṣyate|<br />
Samayo hyeṣa yadguptaṁ tannānupapadaṁ vadet||28.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.33">28.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Turyāṣṭamānyabhuvanacaramāṇi dvayorapi|<br />
Pakṣayoriha sāmānyasāmānyaṁ parva kīrtitam||28.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.34">28.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yadeteṣu dineṣveva bhaviṣyadgrahabhātmakaḥ|<br />
Ubhayātmā viśeṣaḥ syāttatsāmānyaviśeṣatā||28.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.35">28.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sā caikādaśadhaikasminnekasminvibhunoditā|<br />
Sajātīyā tu sotkṛṣṭetyevaṁ śambhurnyarūpayat||28.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.36">28.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kṛṣṇayugaṁ vahnisitaṁ śrutikṛṣṇaṁ vahnisitamiti pakṣāḥ|<br />
Arkendujīvacandrā budhayugmendvarkakaviguruvidhu syāt||28.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.37">28.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Paraphalguścaitramaghe tiṣyaḥ prākphalgukarṇaśatabhiṣajaḥ|<br />
Mūlaprājāpatye viśākhikā śravaṇasañjñayā bhāni||28.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.38">28.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Randhre tithyarkapare vasurandhre śaśivṛṣāṅkarasarandhrayugam|<br />
Prathamaniśāmadhyaniśe madhyāhnaśarā dinodayo madhyadinam||28.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.39">28.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prathamaniśeti ca samayo mārgaśiraḥprabhṛtimāseṣu|<br />
Kanyāntyajātha veśyā rāgavatī tattvavedinī dūtī||28.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.40">28.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vyāsasamāsātkramaśaḥ pūjyāścakre'nuyāgākhye|<br />
Sarvatra ca parvadine kuryādanuyāgacakramatiśayataḥ||28.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.41">28.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Guptāguptavidhānādiyāgacaryākrameṇa sampūrṇam|<br />
Anuyāgaḥ kila mukhyaḥ sarvasminneva karmaviniyoge||28.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.42">28.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anuyāgakālalābhe tasmātprayateta tatparataḥ|<br />
Bhagrahasamayaviśeṣo nāśvayuje ko'pi tena tadvarjam||28.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.43">28.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Velābhagrahakalanā kathitaikādaśasu māseṣu|<br />
Phālgunamāse śuklaṁ yatproktaṁ dvādaśīdinaṁ parva||28.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.44">28.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Agratithivedhayogo mukhyatamo'sau viśeṣo'tra|<br />
Divasaniśe kila kṛtvā tribhāgaśaḥ prathamamadhyamāparavibhāgaḥ||28.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.45">28.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pūjākālastatra tribhāgite mukhyatamaḥ kālaḥ|<br />
Yadi saṅghaṭeta velā mukhyatamā bhagrahau tathā cakram||28.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.46">28.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadyāga ādiyāgastatkāmyaṁ pūjayaiva parvasu siddhyet|<br />
Dinavelābhagrahakalpanena tatrāpi saumyaraudratvam||28.46||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.47">28.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jñātvā sādhakamukhyastattatkāryaṁ tadā tadā kuryāt|<br />
Ukto yo'rcākālastaṁ cedullaṅghya bhagrahatithiḥ syāt||28.47||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.48">28.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tāmanādṛtya viśeṣaṁ pradhānayetsāmayamiti kecit|<br />
Neti tvasmadguravo viśeṣarūpā hi tithiriha na velā||28.48||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.49">28.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṁvedyarūpaśaśadharabhāgaḥ saṁvedanārkakaranikaraiḥ|<br />
Yāvānyāvati pūrṇaḥ sā hi tithirbhagrahaiḥ sphuṭībhavati||28.49||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.50"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.50">28.50</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasmānmukhyātra tithiḥ sā ca viśeṣyā graharkṣayogena|<br />
Velātra na pradhānaṁ yuktaṁ caitattathāhi parameśaḥ||28.50||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.51"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.51">28.51</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrītrikabhairavakulaśāstreṣūce na parvadivaseṣu|<br />
Velāyogaṁ kañcana tithibhagrahayogato hyanyam||28.51||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.52"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.52">28.52</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhagrahayogābhāve tithistu pūjyā pradhānarūpatvāt|<br />
Śvetābhāve kṛṣṇacchāgālambhaṁ hi kathayanti||28.52||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.53"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.53">28.53</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yatpunarūrmiprabhṛtini śāstre veloditāpi tatkāmyam|<br />
Mukhyatayoddiśya vidhiṁ tathā ca tatra pauṣaparvadine||28.53||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.54"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.54">28.54</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kṛtvārcanamardhaniśi dhyātvā japtvā bahirgatasya yathā|<br />
Ādeśaḥ phalati tathā māghe cakrādvacaḥ phalati||28.54||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.55"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.55">28.55</a> <span class="pv-iast">Acirādabhīṣṭasiddhiḥ pañcasu maitrī dhanaṁ ca melāpaḥ|<br />
Cakrasthāne krodhātpāṣāṇasphoṭanena ripunāśaḥ||28.55||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.56"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.56">28.56</a> <span class="pv-iast">Siddhādeśaprāptirmārgāntaṁ kathyate vibhunā|<br />
Bhagrahayogābhāve velāṁ tu titheravaśyamīkṣeta||28.56||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.57"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.57">28.57</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sā hi tathā sphuṭarūpā titheḥ svabhāvodayaṁ dadyāt|<br />
Bhagrahatithivelāṁśānuyāyi sarvāṅgasundaraṁ tu dinam||28.57||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.58"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.58">28.58</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yadi labhyeta tadāsminviśeṣatamapūjanaṁ racayet|<br />
Na ca kāmyameva kevalametatparivarjane yataḥ kathitaḥ||28.58||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.59"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.59">28.59</a> <span class="pv-iast">Samayavilopaḥ śrīmadbhairavakula ūrmiśāstre ca|<br />
Duṣṭā hi durācārāḥ paśutulyāḥ parva ye na viduḥ||28.59||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.60"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.60">28.60</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na ca kāmyasyākaraṇe syājjātu pratyavāyitvam|<br />
Tatrānuyāgasiddhyarthaṁ cakrayāgo nirūpyate||28.60||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.61"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.61">28.61</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mūrtiyāga iti prokto yaḥ śrīyogīśvarīmate|<br />
Nityaṁ naimittikaṁ karma yadatroktaṁ maheśinā||28.61||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.62"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.62">28.62</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sarvatra cakrayāgo'tra mukhyaḥ kāmye viśeṣataḥ|<br />
Jñānī yogī ca puruṣaḥ strī vāsminmūrtisañjñake||28.62||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.63"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.63">28.63</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yoge prayatnato yojyastaddhi pātramanuttaram|<br />
Tatsamparkātpūrṇatā syāditi traiśirasādiṣu||28.63||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.64"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.64">28.64</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tena sarvaṁ hutaṁ ceṣṭaṁ trailokyaṁ sacarācaram|<br />
Jñānine yogine vāpi yo dadāti karoti vā||28.64||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.65"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.65">28.65</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dīkṣottare'pi ca proktamannaṁ brahmā raso hariḥ|<br />
Bhoktā śiva iti jñānī śvapacānapyathoddharet||28.65||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.66"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.66">28.66</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sarvatattvamayo bhūtvā yadi bhuṅkte sa sādhakaḥ|<br />
Tena bhojitamātreṇa sakṛtkoṭistu bhojitāḥ||28.66||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.67"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.67">28.67</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha tattvavidetasminyadi bhuñjīta tatpriye|<br />
Parisaṅkhyā na vidyeta tadāha bhagavāñchivaḥ||28.67||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.68"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.68">28.68</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhojyaṁ māyātmakaṁ sarvaṁ śivo bhoktā sa cāpyaham|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.428"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.428">28.428</a> <span class="pv-iast">Āśāntaṁ pūjayitvainaṁ dakṣiṇābhiryajecchiśuḥ|<br />
Sarvasvamasmai sandadyādātmānamapi bhāvitaḥ||28.428||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.429"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.429">28.429</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atoṣayitvā tu guruṁ dakṣiṇābhiḥ samantataḥ|<br />
Tattvajño'pyṛṇabandhena tena yātyadhikāritām||28.429||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.430"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.430">28.430</a> <span class="pv-iast">Gurupūjāmakurvāṇaḥ śataṁ janmāni jāyate|<br />
Adhikārī tato muktiṁ yātīti skandayāmale||28.430||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.431"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.431">28.431</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasmādavaśyaṁ dātavyā gurave dakṣiṇā punaḥ|<br />
Pūrvaṁ hi yāgāṅgatayā proktaṁ tattuṣṭaye tvidam||28.431||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.432"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.432">28.432</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tajjuṣṭamatha tasyājñāṁ prāpyāśnīyātsvayaṁ śiśuḥ|<br />
Tataḥ prapūjayeccakraṁ yathāvibhavasambhavam||28.432||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.433"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.433">28.433</a> <span class="pv-iast">Akṛtvā guruyāgaṁ tu kṛtamapyakṛtaṁ yataḥ|<br />
Tasmātprayatnataḥ kāryo guruyāgo yathābalam||28.433||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v28.434"><a class="ta-num" href="#v28.434">28.434</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atatrastho'pi hi guruḥ pūjyaḥ saṅkalpya pūrvavat|<br />
Taddravyaṁ devatākṛtye kuryādbhaktajaneṣvatha||28.434||</span></p>
