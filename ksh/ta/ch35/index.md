# Глава 35 — Śāstramelanam

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">44 строфы · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [английский перевод у источника — русского там нет](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/en/node/906)</p>

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Глава 34 — Svasvarūpe praveśaḥ](/ksh/ta/ch34/) · [Глава 36 — Āyātikathanam →](/ksh/ta/ch36/)</p>

<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloke pañcatriṁśamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.1">35.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Athocyate samastānāṁ śāstrāṇāmiha melanam|<br />
Iha tāvatsamasto'yaṁ vyavahāraḥ purātanaḥ||35.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.2">35.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prasiddhimanusandhāya saiva cāgama ucyate|<br />
Anvayavyatirekau hi prasiddherupajīvakau||35.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.3">35.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svāyattatve tayorvyaktipūge kiṁ syāttayorgatiḥ|<br />
Pratyakṣamapi netrātmadīpārthādiviśeṣajam||35.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.4">35.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Apekṣate tatra mūle prasiddhiṁ tāṁ tathātmikām|<br />
Abhitaḥsaṁvṛte jāta ekākī kṣudhitaḥ śiśuḥ||35.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.5">35.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kiṁ karotu kimādattāṁ kena paśyatu kiṁ vrajet|<br />
Nanu vastuśatākīrṇe sthāne'pyasya yadeva hi||35.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.6">35.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Paśyato jighrato vāpi spṛśataḥ samprasīdati|<br />
Cetastadevādāya drākso'nvayavyatirekabhāk||35.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.7">35.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Hanta cetaḥprasādo'pi yo'sāvarthaviśeṣagaḥ|<br />
So'pi prāgvāsanārūpavimarśaparikalpitaḥ||35.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.8">35.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na pratyakṣānumānādibāhyamānaprasādajaḥ|<br />
Prāgvāsanopajīvyetatpratibhāmātrameva na||35.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.9">35.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na mṛdabhyavahārecchā puṁso bālasya jāyate|<br />
Prāgvāsanopajīvī cedvimarśaḥ sā ca vāsanā||35.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.10">35.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prācyā cedāgatā seyaṁ prasiddhiḥ paurvakālikī|<br />
Na ca cetaḥprasattyaiva sarvo vyavahṛtikramaḥ||35.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.11">35.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mūlaṁ prasiddhistanmānaṁ sarvatraiveti gṛhyatām|<br />
Pūrvapūrvopajīvitvamārgaṇe sā kvacitsvayam||35.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.12">35.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sarvajñarūpe hyekasminniḥśaṅkaṁ bhāsate purā|<br />
Vyavahāro hi naikatra samastaḥ ko'pi mātari||35.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.13">35.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tenāsarvajñapūrvatvamātreṇaiṣā na sidhyati|<br />
Bahusarvajñapūrvatve na mānaṁ cāsti kiñcana||35.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.14">35.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhogāpavargataddhetuprasiddhiśataśobhitaḥ|<br />
Tadvimarśasvabhāvo'sau bhairavaḥ parameśvaraḥ||35.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.15">35.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tataścāṁśāṁśikāyogātsā prasiddhiḥ paramparām|<br />
Śāstraṁ vāśritya vitatā lokānsaṁvyavahārayet||35.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.16">35.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tayaivāśaiśavātsarve vyavahāradharājuṣaḥ|<br />
Santaḥ samupajīvanti śaivamevādyamāgamam||35.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.17">35.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Apūrṇāstu pare tena na mokṣaphalabhāginaḥ|<br />
Upajīvanti yāvattu tāvattatphalabhāginaḥ||35.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.18">35.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bālyāpāye'pi yadbhoktumannameva pravartate|<br />
Tatprasiddhyaiva nādhyakṣānnānumānādasambhavāt||35.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.19">35.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na ca kāpyatra doṣāśā śaṅkāyāśca nivṛttitaḥ|<br />
Prasiddhiścāvigānotthā pratītiḥ śabdanātmikā||35.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.20">35.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mātuḥ svabhāvo yattasyāṁ śaṅkate naiṣa jātucit|<br />
Svakṛtatvavaśādeva sarvavitsa hi śaṅkaraḥ||35.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.21">35.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yāvattu śivatā nāsya tāvatsvātmānusāriṇīm|<br />
Tāvatīmeva tāmeṣa prasiddhiṁ nābhiśaṅkate||35.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.22">35.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anyasyāmabhiśaṅkī syādbhūyastāṁ bahu manyate|<br />
Evaṁ bhāviśivatvo'mūṁ prasiddhiṁ manyate dhruvam||35.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.23">35.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Eka evāgamaścāyaṁ vibhunā sarvadarśinā|<br />
Darśito yaḥ pravṛtte ca nivṛtte ca pathi sthitaḥ||35.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.24">35.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dharmārthakāmamokṣeṣu pūrṇāpūrṇādibhedataḥ|<br />
Vicitreṣu phaleṣveka upāyaḥ śāmbhavāgamaḥ||35.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.25">35.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasminviṣayavaicitryādvicitraphaladāyini|<br />
Citropāyopadeśo'pi na virodhāvaho bhavet||35.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.26">35.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Laukikaṁ vaidikaṁ sāṅkhyaṁ yogādi pāñcarātrakam|<br />
Bauddhārhatanyāyaśāstraṁ padārthakramatantraṇam||35.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.27">35.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Siddhāntatantraśāktādi sarvaṁ brahmodbhavaṁ yataḥ|<br />
Śrīsvacchandādiṣu proktaṁ sadyojātādibhedataḥ||35.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.28">35.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathaikatrāpi vedādau tattadāśramagāminaḥ|<br />
Saṁskārāntaramatrāpi tathā liṅgoddhṛtādikam||35.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.29">35.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathāca tatra pūrvasminnāśrame nottarāśramāt|<br />
Phalameti tathā pāñcarātrādau na śivātmatām||35.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.30">35.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Eka evāgamastasmāttatra laukikaśāstrataḥ|<br />
Prabhṛtyā vaiṣṇavādbauddhācchaivātsarvaṁ hi niṣṭhitam||35.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.31">35.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasya yattat paraṁ prāpyaṁ dhāma tat trikaśabditam|<br />
Sarvāvibhedānucchedāttadeva kulamucyate||35.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.32">35.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathordhvādharatābhāksu dehāṅgeṣu vibhediṣu|<br />
Ekaṁ prāṇitamevaṁ syāttrikaṁ sarveṣu śāstrataḥ||35.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.33">35.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīmatkālīkule coktaṁ pañcasrotovivarjitam|<br />
Daśāṣṭādaśabhedasya sārametatprakīrtitam||35.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.34">35.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Puṣpe gandhastile tailaṁ dehe jīvo jale'mṛtam|<br />
Yathā tathaiva śāstrāṇāṁ kulamantaḥ pratiṣṭhitam||35.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.35">35.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadeka evāgamo'yaṁ citraścitre'dhikāriṇi|<br />
Tathaiva sā prasiddhirhi svayūthyaparayūthyagā||35.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.36">35.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sāṅkhyaṁ yogaṁ pāñcarātraṁ vedāṁścaiva na nindayet|<br />
Yataḥ śivodbhavāḥ sarva iti svacchandaśāsane||35.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.37">35.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ekasmādāgamāccaite khaṇḍakhaṇḍā vyapoddhṛtāḥ|<br />
Loke syurāgamāstaiśca jano bhrāmyati mohitaḥ||35.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.38">35.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anekāgamapakṣe'pi vācyā viṣayabheditā|<br />
Avaśyamūrdhvādharatāsthityā prāmāṇyasiddhaye||35.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.39">35.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anyathā naiva kasyāpi prāmāṇyaṁ siddhyati dhruvam|<br />
Nityatvamavisaṁvāda iti no mānakāraṇam||35.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.40">35.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Asminnaṁśe'pyamuṣyaiva prāmāṇyaṁ syāttathoditeḥ|<br />
Anyathāvyākṛtau kḷptāvasatyatve prarocane||35.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.41">35.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atiprasaṅga sarvasyāpyāgamasyāpabādhakaḥ|<br />
Avaśyopetya ityasminmāna āgamanāmani||35.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.42">35.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Avaśyopetyamevaitacchāstraniṣṭhānirūpaṇam|<br />
Pradhāne'ṅge kṛto yatnaḥ phalavānvastuto yataḥ||35.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.43">35.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ato'sminyatnavānko'pi bhavecchambhupracoditaḥ|<br />
Tatra tatra ca śāstreṣu nyarūpyata maheśinā||35.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v35.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v35.44">35.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Etāvatyadhikārī yaḥ sa durlabha iti sphuṭam|<br />
Itthaṁ śrīśambhunāthena mamoktaṁ śāstramelanam||35.44||</span></p>
