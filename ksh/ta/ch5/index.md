<p class="ta-st" id="v5.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.1">5.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Āṇavena vidhinā paradhāma prepsatāmatha nirūpyata etat||5.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.2">5.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vikalpasyaiva saṁskāre jāte niṣpratiyogini|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.3">5.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vikalpaḥ kasyacitsvātmasvātantryādeva susthiraḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.4">5.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kasyacittu vikalpo'sau svātmasaṁskaraṇaṁ prati|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.5">5.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vikalpo nāma cinmātrasvabhāvo yadyapi sthitaḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.6">5.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Niścayo bahudhā caiṣa tatropāyāśca bhedinaḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.7">5.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatra buddhau tathā prāṇe dehe cāpi pramātari|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.8">5.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yataḥ prakāśāccinmātrāt prāṇādyavyatirekavat|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.9">5.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktaṁ traiśirase caitaddevyai candrārdhamaulinā|<br />
Jīvaḥ śaktiḥ śivasyaiva sarvatraiva sthitāpi sā||5.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.10">5.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svarūpapratyaye rūḍhā jñānasyonmīlanātparā|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.11">5.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Paśyañjaḍātmatābhāgaṁ tirodhāyādvayo bhavet|<br />
Tatra svātantryadṛṣṭyā vā darpaṇe mukhabimbavat||5.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.12">5.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Viśuddhaṁ nijacaitanyaṁ niścinotyatadātmakam|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.13">5.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Satyatastadabhinnaṁ syāttasyānyonyavibhedataḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.14">5.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Niṣṭhitaikasphuranmūrtermūrtyantaravirodhataḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.15">5.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prāṇe dehe'thavā kasmātsaṅkrāmetkena vā katham|<br />
Tathāpi nirvikalpe'sminvikalpo nāsti taṁ vinā||5.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.16">5.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dṛṣṭe'pyadṛṣṭakalpatvaṁ vikalpena tu niścayaḥ|<br />
Buddhiprāṇaśarīreṣu pārameśvaryamañjasā||5.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.17">5.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vikalpyaṁ śūnyarūpe na pramātari vikalpanam|<br />
Buddhirdhyānamayī tatra prāṇa uccāraṇātmakaḥ||5.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.18">5.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uccāraṇaṁ ca prāṇādyā vyānāntāḥ pañca vṛttayaḥ|<br />
Ādyā tu prāṇanābhikhyāparoccārātmikā bhavet||5.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.19">5.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śarīrasyākṣaviṣayaitatpiṇḍatvena saṁsthitiḥ|<br />
Tatra dhyānamayaṁ tāvadanuttaramihocyate||5.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.20">5.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yaḥ prakāśaḥ svatantro'yaṁ citsvabhāvo hṛdi sthitaḥ|<br />
Sarvatattvamayaḥ proktametacca triśiromate||5.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.21">5.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kadalīsampuṭākāraṁ sabāhyābhyantarāntaram|<br />
Īkṣate hṛdayāntaḥsthaṁ tat puṣpamiva tattvavit||5.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.22">5.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Somasūryāgnisaṅghaṭṭaṁ tatra dhyāyedananyadhīḥ|<br />
Taddhyānāraṇisaṅkṣobhānmahābhairavahavyabhuk||5.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.23">5.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Hṛdayākhye mahākuṇḍe jājvalan sphītatāṁ vrajet|<br />
Tasya śaktimataḥ sphītaśakterbhairavatejasaḥ||5.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.24">5.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mātṛmānaprameyākhyaṁ dhāmābhedena bhāvayet|<br />
Vahnyarkasomaśaktīnāṁ tadeva tritayaṁ bhavet||5.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.25">5.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Parā parāparā ceyamaparā ca sadoditā|<br />
Sṛṣṭisaṁsthitisaṁhāraistāsāṁ pratyekatastridhā||5.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.26">5.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Caturthaṁ cānavacchinnaṁ rūpamāsāmakalpitam|<br />
Evaṁ dvādaśa tā devyaḥ sūryabimbavadāsthitāḥ||5.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.27">5.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ekaikamāsāṁ vahnyarkasomatacchāntibhāsanam|<br />
Etadānuttaraṁ cakraṁ hṛdayāccakṣurādibhiḥ||5.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.28">5.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vyomabhirniḥsaratyeva tattadviṣayagocare|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.29">5.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Somasūryāgnibhāsātma rūpaṁ samavatiṣṭhate|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.30">5.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Cakreṇānena patatā tādātmyaṁ paribhāvayet|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.31">5.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Cakraṁ sarvātmakaṁ tattatsārvabhaumamahīśavat|<br />
Itthaṁ viśvādhvapaṭalamayatnenaiva līyate||5.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.32">5.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhairavīyamahācakre saṁvittiparivārite|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.33">5.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svātmocchalattayā bhrāmyaccakraṁ sañcintayenmahat|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.34">5.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Praśāmyadbhāvayeccakraṁ tataḥ śāntaṁ tataḥ śamam|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.35">5.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatsaṁvidi tataḥ saṁvidvilīnārthaiva bhāsate|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.36">5.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ pratikṣaṇaṁ viśvaṁ svasaṁvidi vilāpayan|<br />
Visṛjaṁśca tato bhūyaḥ śaśvadbhairavatāṁ vrajet||5.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.37">5.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ triśūlāt prabhṛti catuṣpañcārakakramāt|<br />
Pañcāśadaraparyantaṁ cakraṁ yogī vibhāvayet||5.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.38">5.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Catuṣṣaṣṭiśatāraṁ vā sahasrāramathāpi vā|<br />
Asaṅkhyārasahasraṁ vā cakraṁ dhyāyedananyadhīḥ||5.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.39">5.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṁvinnāthasya mahato devasyollāsisaṁvidaḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.40">5.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śaktayo'sya jagat kṛtsnaṁ śaktimāṁstu maheśvaraḥ|<br />
Iti māṅgalaśāstre tu śrīśrīkaṇṭho nyarūpayat||5.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.41">5.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ityetat prathamopāyarūpaṁ dhyānaṁ nyarūpayat|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.42">5.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anayaiva diśānyāni dhyānānyapi samāśrayet|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.43">5.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha prāṇasya yā vṛttiḥ prāṇanādyā nirūpitā|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.44">5.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nijānande pramātraṁśamātre hṛdi purā sthitaḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.45">5.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prāṇodaye prameye tu parānandaṁ vibhāvayet|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.46">5.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Parānandagatastiṣṭhedapānaśaśiśobhitaḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.47">5.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Samānabhūmimāgatya brahmānandamayo bhavet|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.48">5.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Udānavahnau viśrānto mahānandaṁ vibhāvayet|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.49">5.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nirupādhirmahāvyāptirvyānākhyopādhivarjitā|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.50"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.50">5.50</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nahyatra saṁsthitiḥ kāpi vibhaktā jaḍarūpiṇaḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.51"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.51">5.51</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yadanāhatasaṁvitti paramāmṛtabṛṁhitam|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.52"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.52">5.52</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadeva jagadānandamasmabhyaṁ śambhurūcivān|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.53"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.53">5.53</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yā tatra samyagviśrāntiḥ sānuttaramayī sthitiḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.54"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.54">5.54</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ṣaṭprāṇoccārajaṁ rūpamatha vyāptyā taducyate|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.55"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.55">5.55</a> <span class="pv-iast">Catuṣkikāmbujālambilambikāsaudhamāśrayet|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.56"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.56">5.56</a> <span class="pv-iast">Icchājñānakriyāśaktisamatve praviśet sudhīḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.57"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.57">5.57</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrayedbhrūbindunādāntaśaktisopānamālikām|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.58"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.58">5.58</a> <span class="pv-iast">Visargastatra viśrāmyenmatsyodaradaśājuṣi|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.59"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.59">5.59</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vikāsasaṅkocamayaṁ praviśya hṛdi hṛṣyati|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.60"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.60">5.60</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrayedvikāsasaṅkocarūḍhabhairavayāmalām|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.61"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.61">5.61</a> <span class="pv-iast">Parasminneti viśrāntiṁ sarvāpūraṇayogataḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.62"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.62">5.62</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prakāśasyātmaviśrāntāvahamityeva dṛśyatām|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.63"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.63">5.63</a> <span class="pv-iast">Cidvimarśaparāhaṅkṛt prathamollāsinī sphuret|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.64"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.64">5.64</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sūryeṇābhāsayedbhāvaṁ pūrayedatha carcayet|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.65"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.65">5.65</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sañjīvanyamṛtaṁ bodhavahnau visṛjati sphuran|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.66"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.66">5.66</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadevamamṛtaṁ divyaṁ saṁviddevīṣu tarpakam|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.67"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.67">5.67</a> <span class="pv-iast">Visṛṣṭaṁ cedbhavetsarvaṁ hutaṁ ṣoḍhādhvamaṇḍalam|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.68"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.68">5.68</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aṁa iti kuleśvaryā sahito hi kuleśitā|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v5.69"><a class="ta-num" href="#v5.69">5.69</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vitprāṇaguṇadehāntarbahirdravyamayīmimām|<br />
