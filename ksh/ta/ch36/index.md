# Глава 36 — Āyātikathanam

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">15 строф · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [английский перевод у источника — русского там нет](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/en/node/907)</p>

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Глава 35 — Śāstramelanam](/ksh/ta/ch35/) · [Глава 37 — Śāstropādeyatvanirūpaṇam →](/ksh/ta/ch37/)</p>

<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloke ṣaṭtriṁśamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v36.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v36.1">36.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Āyātiratha śāstrasya kathyate'vasarāgatā|<br />
Śrīsiddhādivinirdiṣṭā gurubhiśca nirūpitā|<br />
Bhairavo bhairavī devī svacchando lākulo'ṇurāṭ||36.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v36.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v36.2">36.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Gahaneśo'bjajaḥ śakro guruḥ koṭyapakarṣataḥ|<br />
Navabhiḥ kramaśo'dhītaṁ navakoṭipravistaram||36.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v36.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v36.3">36.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Etaistato guruḥ koṭimātrātpādaṁ vitīrṇavān|<br />
Dakṣādibhya ubhau pādau saṁvartādibhya eva ca||36.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v36.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v36.4">36.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pādaṁ ca vāmanādibhyaḥ pādārdhaṁ bhārgavāya ca|<br />
Pādapādaṁ tu balaye pādapādastu yo'paraḥ||36.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v36.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v36.5">36.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Siṁhāyārdhaṁ tataḥ śiṣṭāddvau bhāgau vinatābhuve|<br />
Pādaṁ vāsukināgāya khaṇḍāḥ saptadaśa tvamī||36.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v36.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v36.6">36.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svargādardhaṁ rāvaṇo'tha jahre rāmo'rdhamapyataḥ|<br />
Vibhīṣaṇamukhādāpa guruśiṣyavidhikramāt||36.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v36.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v36.7">36.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Khaṇḍairekānnaviṁśatyā vibhaktaṁ tadabhūttataḥ|<br />
Khaṇḍaṁ khaṇḍaṁ cāṣṭakhaṇḍaṁ proktapādādibhedataḥ||36.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v36.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v36.8">36.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pādo mūloddhārāvuttarabṛhaduttare tathā kalpaḥ|<br />
Sāṁhitakalpaskandāvanuttaraṁ vyāpakaṁ tridhā tisraḥ||36.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v36.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v36.9">36.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Devyo'tra nirūpyante kramaśo vistāriṇaiva rūpeṇa|<br />
Navame pade tu gaṇanā na kāciduktā vyavacchidāhīne||36.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v36.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v36.10">36.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Rāmācca lakṣmaṇastasmātsiddhāstebhyo'pi dānavāḥ|<br />
Guhyakāśca tatastebhyo yogino nṛvarāstataḥ||36.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v36.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v36.11">36.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Teṣāṁ krameṇa yatprāptaṁ bhraṣṭaṁ kālāntarādyadā|<br />
Tadā śrīkaṇṭhanāthājñāvaśātsiddhā avātaran||36.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v36.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v36.12">36.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tryambakāmardakābhikhyaśrīnāthā advaye dvaye|<br />
Dvayādvaye ca nipuṇāḥ krameṇa śivaśāsane||36.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v36.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v36.13">36.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ādyasya cānvayo jajñe dvitīyo duhitṛkramāt|<br />
Sa cārdhatryambakābhikhyaḥ santānaḥ supratiṣṭhitaḥ||36.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v36.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v36.14">36.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ataścārdhacatasro'tra maṭhikāḥ santatikramāt|<br />
Śiṣyapraśiṣyairvistīrṇāḥ śataśākhaṁ vyavasthitaiḥ||36.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v36.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v36.15">36.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Adhyuṣṭasantatisrotaḥsārabhūtarasāhṛtim|<br />
Vidhāya tantrāloko'yaṁ syandate sakalānrasān||36.15||</span></p>
