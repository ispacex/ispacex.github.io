# Глава 22 — Liṅgoddhāraḥ

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">48 строф · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [английский перевод у источника — русского там нет](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/en/node/890)</p>

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Глава 21 — Pārokṣī](/ksh/ta/ch21/) · [Глава 23 — Abhiṣecanam →](/ksh/ta/ch23/)</p>

<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloke dvāviṁśatitamamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.1">22.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Liṅgoddhārākhyāmatha vacmaḥ śivaśāsanaikanirdiṣṭām||22.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.2">22.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktaṁ śrīmālinītantre kila pārthivadhāraṇām|<br />
Uktvā yo yojito yatra sa tasmānna nivartate||22.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.3">22.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yogyatāvaśasañjātā yasya yatraiva vāsanā|<br />
Sa tatraiva niyoktavyo dīkṣākāle tatastvasau||22.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.4">22.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Phalaṁ sarvaṁ samāsādya śive yukto'pavṛjyate|<br />
Ayukto'pyūrdhvasaṁśuddhiṁ samprāpya bhuvaneśataḥ||22.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.5">22.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śuddhaḥ śivatvamāyāti dagdhasaṁsārabandhanaḥ|<br />
Uktvā pundhāraṇāṁ coktametadvaidāntikaṁ mayā||22.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.6">22.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kapilāya purā proktaṁ prathame paṭale tathā|<br />
Anena kramayogena samprāptaṁ paramaṁ padam||22.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.7">22.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na bhūyaḥ paśutāmeti śuddhe svātmani tiṣṭhati|<br />
Ato hi dhvanyate'rtho'yaṁ śivatattvādhareṣvapi||22.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.8">22.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tattveṣu yojitasyāsti punaruddharaṇīyatā|<br />
Samastaśāstrakathitavastuvaiviktyadāyinaḥ||22.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.9">22.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śivāgamasya sarvebhyo'pyāgamebhyo viśiṣṭatā|<br />
Śivajñānena ca vinā bhūyo'pi paśutodbhavaḥ||22.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.10">22.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kramaśca śaktisampāto malahāniryiyāsutā|<br />
Dīkṣā bodho heyahānirupādeyalayātmatā||22.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.11">22.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhogyatvapāśavatyāgaḥ patikartṛtvasaṅkṣayaḥ|<br />
Svātmasthitiścetyevaṁ hi darśanāntarasaṁsthiteḥ||22.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.12">22.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Proktamuddharaṇīyatvaṁ śivaśaktīritasya hi|<br />
Atha vaiṣṇavabauddhāditantrāntādharavartinām||22.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.13">22.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yadā śivārkaraśmyoghairvikāsi hṛdayāmbujam|<br />
Liṅgoddhṛtistadā pūrvaṁ dīkṣākarma tataḥ param||22.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.14">22.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prāgliṅgāntarasaṁstho'pi dīkṣātaḥ śivatāṁ vrajet|<br />
Tatropavāsya taṁ cānyadine sādhāramantrataḥ||22.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.15">22.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sthaṇḍile pūjayitveśaṁ śrāvayettasya vartanīm|<br />
Eṣa prāgabhavalliṅgī coditastvadhunā tvayā||22.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.16">22.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prasannena tadetasmai kuru samyaganugraham|<br />
Svaliṅgatyāgaśaṅkotthaṁ prāyaścittaṁ ca māsya bhūt||22.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.17">22.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Acirāttvanmayībhūya bhogaṁ mokṣaṁ prapadyatām|<br />
Evamastvityathājñāṁ ca gṛhītvā vratamasya tat||22.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.18">22.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Apāsyāmbhasi nikṣipya snapayedanurūpataḥ|<br />
Snātaṁ samprokṣayedarghapātrāmbhobhiranantaram||22.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.19">22.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pañcagavyaṁ dantakāṣṭhaṁ tatastasmai samarpayet|<br />
Tatastaṁ baddhanetraṁ ca praveśya praṇipātayet||22.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.20">22.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Praṇavo mātṛkā māyā vyomavyāpī ṣaḍakṣaraḥ|<br />
Bahurūpo'tha netrākhyaḥ sapta sādhāraṇā amī||22.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.21">22.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Teṣāṁ madhyādekatamaṁ mantramasmai samarpayet|<br />
So'pyahorātramevainaṁ japedalpabhugapyabhuk||22.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.22">22.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mantramasmai samarpyātha sādhāravidhisaṁskṛte|<br />
Vahnau tarpitatanmantre vrataśuddhiṁ samācaret||22.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.23">22.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pūjitenaiva mantreṇa kṛtvā nāmāsya sampuṭam|<br />
Prāyaścittaṁ śodhayāmi phaṭsvāhetyūhayogataḥ||22.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.24">22.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śataṁ sahasraṁ vā hutvā punaḥ pūrṇāhutiṁ tathā|<br />
Prayogādvauṣaḍantāṁ ca kṣiptvāhūya vrateśvaram||22.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.25">22.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tāro vrateśvarāyeti namaścetyenamarcayet|<br />
Śrāvayecca tvayā nāsya kāryaṁ kiñcicchivājñayā||22.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.26">22.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tato vrateśvarastarpyaḥ svāhāntena tataśca saḥ|<br />
Kṣamayitvā visṛjyaḥ syāttato'gneśca visarjanam||22.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.27">22.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tacchrāvaṇaṁ ca devāya kṣamasveti visarjanam|<br />
Tatastṛtīyadivase prāgvatsarvo vidhiḥ smṛtaḥ||22.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.28">22.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Adhivāsādikaḥ sveṣṭadīkṣākarmāvasānakaḥ|<br />
Prāgliṅgināṁ mokṣadīkṣā sādhikāravivarjitā||22.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.29">22.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sādhakācāryatāmārge na yogyāste punarbhuvaḥ|<br />
Punarbhuvo'pi jñāneddhā bhavanti gurutāspadam||22.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.30">22.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mokṣāyaiva na bhogāya bhogāyāpyabhyupāyataḥ|<br />
Ityuktavānsvapaddhatyāmīśānaśivadaiśikaḥ||22.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.31">22.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīdevyāyāmalīyoktitattvasamyakpravādakaḥ|<br />
Gurvantasyāpyadhodṛṣṭiśāyinaḥ saṁskriyāmimām||22.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.32">22.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kṛtvā rahasyaṁ kathayennānyathā kāmike kila|<br />
Anyatantrābhiṣikte'pi rahasyaṁ na prakāśayet||22.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.33">22.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svatantrastho'pi gurvanto gurumajñamupāśritaḥ|<br />
Tatra paścādanāśvastastatrāpi vidhimācaret||22.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.34">22.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ajñācāryamukhāyātaṁ nirvīryaṁ mantrameṣa yat|<br />
Japtavānsa guruścātra nādhikāryuktadūṣaṇāt||22.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.35">22.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tato'sya śuddhiṁ prākkṛtvā tato dīkṣāṁ samācaret|<br />
Adhodarśanasaṁsthena guruṇā dīkṣitaḥ purā||22.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.36">22.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tīvraśaktivaśātpaścādyadā gacchetsa sadgurum|<br />
Tadāpyasya śiśorevaṁ śuddhiṁ kṛtvā sa sadguruḥ||22.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.37">22.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dīkṣādikarma nikhilaṁ kuryāduktavidhānataḥ|<br />
Prāpto'pi sadgururyogyabhāvamasya na vetti cet||22.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.38">22.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vijñānadāne tacchiṣyo yogyatāṁ darśayennijām|<br />
Sarvathā tvabruvanneṣa bruvāṇo vā viparyayam||22.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.39">22.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ajño vastuta eveti tattyaktvetthaṁ vidhiṁ caret|<br />
Na tirobhāvaśaṅkātra kartavyā buddhiśālinā||22.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.40">22.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Adhaḥspṛktvaṁ tirobhūtirnordhvopāyavivecanam|<br />
Siddhānte dīkṣitāstantre daśāṣṭādaśabhedini||22.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.41">22.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhairavīye catuḥṣaṣṭau tānpaśūndīkṣayettrike|<br />
Siddhavīrāvalīsāre bhairavīye kule'pi ca||22.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.42">22.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pañcadīkṣākramopāntā dīkṣānuttarasañjñitā|<br />
Tena sarvo'dharastho'pi liṅgoddhṛtyānugṛhyate||22.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.43">22.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yo'pi hṛtsthamaheśānacodanātaḥ suvistṛtam|<br />
Śāstrajñānaṁ samanvicchetso'pi yāyādbahūngurūn||22.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.44">22.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Taddīkṣāścāpi gṛhṇīyādabhiṣecanapaścimāḥ|<br />
Jñānopodbalikāstā hi tattajjñānavatā kṛtāḥ||22.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.45">22.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktaṁ ca śrīmate śāstre tatra tatra ca bhūyasā|<br />
Āmodārthī yathā bhṛṅgaḥ puṣpātpuṣpāntaraṁ vrajet||22.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.46">22.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vijñānārthī tathā śiṣyo gurorgurvantaraṁ tviti|<br />
Gurūṇāṁ bhūyasāṁ madhye yato vijñānamuttamam||22.46||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.47">22.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prāptaṁ so'sya gururdīkṣā cātra mukhyā hi saṁvidi|<br />
Sarvajñānanidhānaṁ tu guruṁ samprāpya susthitaḥ||22.47||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v22.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v22.48">22.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tamevārādhayeddhīmāṁstattajjijñāsanonmukhaḥ|<br />
Iti dīkṣāvidhiḥ prokto liṅgoddharaṇapaścimaḥ||22.48||</span></p>
