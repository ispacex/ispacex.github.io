# Глава 24 — Antyeṣṭiḥ

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">24 строфы · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [английский перевод у источника — русского там нет](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/en/node/892)</p>

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Глава 23 — Abhiṣecanam](/ksh/ta/ch23/) · [Глава 25 — Śrāddhakḷptiḥ →](/ksh/ta/ch25/)</p>

<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloke caturviṁśatitamamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v24.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v24.1">24.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha śāmbhavaśāsanoditāṁ sarahasyāṁ śṛṇutāntyasaṁskriyām||24.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v24.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v24.2">24.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sarveṣāmadharasthānāṁ gurvantānāmapi sphuṭam|<br />
Śaktipātātpurāproktātkuryādantyeṣṭidīkṣaṇam||24.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v24.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v24.3">24.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ūrdhvaśāsanagānāṁ ca samayopahatātmanām|<br />
Antyeṣṭidīkṣā kartavyā guruṇā tattvavedinā||24.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v24.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v24.4">24.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Samayācāradoṣeṣu pramādātskhalitasya hi|<br />
Antyeṣṭidīkṣā kāryeti śrīdīkṣottaraśāsane||24.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v24.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v24.5">24.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yatkiñcitkathitaṁ pūrvaṁ mṛtoddhārābhidhe vidhau|<br />
Pratimāyāṁ tadevātra sarvaṁ śavatanau caret||24.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v24.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v24.6">24.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīsiddhātantrakathito vidhireṣa nirūpyate|<br />
Antimaṁ yadbhavetpūrvaṁ tatkṛtvāntimamādimam||24.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v24.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v24.7">24.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṁhṛtyaikaikamiṣṭiryā sāntyeṣṭirdvitayī matā|<br />
Pūjādhyānajapapluṣṭasamaye na tu sādhake||24.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v24.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v24.8">24.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Piṇḍapātādayaṁ muktaḥ khecaro vā bhavetpriye|<br />
Ācārye tattvasampanne yatra tatra mṛte sati||24.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v24.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v24.9">24.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Antyeṣṭirnaiva vidyeta śuddhacetasyamūrdhani|<br />
Mantrayogādibhirye ca māritā narake tu te||24.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v24.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v24.10">24.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kāryā teṣāmihāntyeṣṭirguruṇātikṛpālunā|<br />
Na maṇḍalādikaṁ tvatra bhavecchmāśānike vidhau||24.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v24.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v24.11">24.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kecittadapi kartavyamūcire pretasadmani|<br />
Pūjayitvā vibhuṁ sarvaṁ nyāsaṁ pūrvavadācaret||24.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v24.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v24.12">24.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṁhārakramayogena caraṇānmūrdhapaścimam|<br />
Tathaiva bodhayedenaṁ kriyājñānasamādhibhiḥ||24.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v24.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v24.13">24.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bindunā rodhayettattvaṁ śaktibījena vedhayet|<br />
Ghaṭṭayennādadeśe tu triśūlena tu tāḍayet||24.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v24.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v24.14">24.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Suṣumnāntargatenaiva visargeṇa punaḥ punaḥ|<br />
Tāḍayeta kalāḥ sarvāḥ kampate'sau tataḥ paśuḥ||24.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v24.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v24.15">24.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Utkṣipedvāmahastaṁ vā tatastaṁ yojayetpare|<br />
Pratyayena vinā mokṣo hyaśraddheyo vimohitaiḥ||24.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v24.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v24.16">24.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadarthametaduditaṁ na tu mokṣopayogadaḥ|<br />
Ityūce parameśaḥ śrīkulagahvaraśāsane||24.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v24.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v24.17">24.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sādhyo'numeyo mokṣādiḥ pratyayairyadatīndriyaḥ|<br />
Dīkṣottare ca puryaṣṭavargārpaṇamihoditam||24.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v24.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v24.18">24.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadvidhiḥ śrutipatre'bje madhye devaṁ sadāśivam|<br />
Īśarudraharibrahmacatuṣkaṁ prāgdigāditaḥ||24.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v24.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v24.19">24.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pūjayitvā śrutisparśau rasaṁ gandhaṁ vapurdvayam|<br />
Dhyahaṅkṛtī manaśceti brahmādiṣvarpayetkramāt||24.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v24.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v24.20">24.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Eteṣāṁ tarpaṇaṁ kṛtvā śatahomena daiśikaḥ|<br />
Eṣā sānnyāsikī dīkṣā puryaṣṭakaviśodhanī||24.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v24.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v24.21">24.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Puryaṣṭakasyābhāve ca na svarganarakādayaḥ|<br />
Tathā kṛtvā na kartavyaṁ laukikaṁ kiñcanāpi hi||24.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v24.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v24.22">24.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktaṁ śrīmādhavakule śāsanastho mṛteṣvapi|<br />
Piṇḍapātodakāsravādi laukikaṁ parivarjayet||24.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v24.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v24.23">24.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śivaṁ sampūjya cakrārcāṁ yathāśakti samācaret|<br />
Kramāttridaśamaviṁśatriṁśavatsaravāsare||24.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v24.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v24.24">24.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ityukto'ntyeṣṭiyāgo'yaṁ parameśvarabhāṣitaḥ||24.24||</span></p>
