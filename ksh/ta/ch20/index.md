# Глава 20 — Tulādīkṣā

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">15 строф · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [английский перевод у источника — русского там нет](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/en/node/888)</p>

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Глава 19 — Sadyaḥsamutkrama](/ksh/ta/ch19/) · [Глава 21 — Pārokṣī →](/ksh/ta/ch21/)</p>

<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloke viṁśatitamamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v20.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v20.1">20.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha dīkṣāṁ bruve mūḍhajanāśvāsapradāyinīm||20.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v20.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v20.2">20.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Trikoṇe vahnisadane vahnivarṇojjvale'bhitaḥ|<br />
Vāyavyapuranirdhūte kare savye sujājvale||20.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v20.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v20.3">20.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bījaṁ kiñcidgṛhītvaitattathaiva hṛdayāntare|<br />
Kare ca dahyamānaṁ saccintayettajjapaikayuk||20.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v20.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v20.4">20.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vahnidīpitaphaṭkāradhoraṇīdāhapīḍitam|<br />
Bījaṁ nirbījatāmeti svasūtikaraṇākṣamam||20.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v20.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v20.5">20.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Taptaṁ naitatprarohāya tenaiva pratyayena tu|<br />
Malamāyākhyakarmāṇi mantradhyānakriyābalāt||20.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v20.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v20.6">20.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dagdhāni na svakāryāya nirbījapratyayaṁ tvimam|<br />
Sa śrīmānsuprasanno me śambhunātho nyarūpayat||20.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v20.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v20.7">20.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bījasyāpyatra kāryā ca yojanā kṛpayā guroḥ|<br />
Yato dīkṣā sudīptatvātsthāvarāṇyapi mocayet||20.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v20.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v20.8">20.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yo gururjapahomārcādhyānasiddhatvamātmani|<br />
Jñātvā dīkṣāṁ carettasya dīkṣā sapratyayā smṛtā||20.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v20.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v20.9">20.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Avadhūte nirācāre tattvajñe natvayaṁ vidhiḥ|<br />
Sācāraiḥ kriyate dīkṣā yā dṛṣṭapratyayānvitā||20.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v20.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v20.10">20.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nirācāreṇa dīkṣāyāṁ pratyayastu na gadyate|<br />
Jñānaṁ svapratyayaṁ yasmānna phalāntaramarhati||20.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v20.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v20.11">20.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dhyānādi tatphalātsādhyamiti siddhāmatoditam|<br />
Tulāśuddhiparīkṣāṁ vā kuryātpratyayayoginīm||20.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v20.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v20.12">20.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathā śrītantrasadbhāve kathitā parameśinā|<br />
Śrīpūrvaśāstre'pyeṣā ca sūcitā parameśinā||20.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v20.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v20.13">20.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ānanda udbhavaḥ kampo nidrā ghūrṇiśca pañcamī|<br />
Ityevaṁ vadatā śaktitāratamyābhidhāyinā||20.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v20.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v20.14">20.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Udbhavo laghubhāvena dehagrahatirohiteḥ|<br />
Deho hi pārthivo mukhyastadā mukhyatvamujjhati||20.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v20.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v20.15">20.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhāvilāghavamantreṇa śiṣyaṁ dhyātvā samutplutam|<br />
Karmāṇi tatrāśeṣāṇi pūrvoktānyācaredguruḥ||20.15||</span></p>
