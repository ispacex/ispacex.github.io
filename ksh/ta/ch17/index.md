# Глава 17 — Prameyaprakriyā

<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloke saptadaśamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.1">17.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha bhairavatādātmyadāyinīṁ prakriyāṁ bruve|<br />
Evaṁ maṇḍalakumbhāgniśiṣyasvātmasu pañcasu||17.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.2">17.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Gṛhītvā vyāptimaikyena nyasyādhvānaṁ ca śiṣyagam|<br />
Karmamāyāṇumalinatrayaṁ bāhau gale tathā||17.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.3">17.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śikhāyāṁ ca kṣipetsūtragranthiyogena daiśikaḥ|<br />
Tasyātadrūpatābhānaṁ malo granthiḥ sa kīrtyate||17.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.4">17.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Itipratītidārḍhyārthaṁ bahirgranthyupakalpanam|<br />
Bāhū karmāspadaṁ viṣṇurmāyātmā galasaṁśritaḥ||17.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.5">17.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Adhovahā śikhāṇutvaṁ tenetthaṁ kalpanā kṛtā|<br />
Naraśaktiśivākhyasya trayasya bahubhedatām||17.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.6">17.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vaktuṁ tristriguṇaṁ sūtraṁ granthaye parikalpayet|<br />
Tejojalānnatritayaṁ tredhā pratyekamapyadaḥ||17.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.7">17.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrutyante ke'pyataḥ śuklakṛṣṇaraktaṁ prapedire|<br />
Tato'gnau tarpitāśeṣamantre cidvyomamātrake||17.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.8">17.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sāmānyarūpe tattvānāṁ kramācchuddhiṁ samācaret|<br />
Tatra svamantrayogena dharāmāvāhayetpurā||17.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.9">17.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iṣṭvā puṣpādibhiḥ sarpistilādyairatha tarpayet|<br />
Tattattvavyāpikāṁ paścānmāyātattvādhidevatām||17.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.10">17.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Māyāśaktiṁ svamantreṇāvāhyābhyarcya pratarpayet|<br />
Āvāhane mātṛkārṇaṁ mālinyarṇaṁ ca pūjane||17.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.11">17.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kuryāditi guruḥ prāha svarūpāpyāyanadvayāt|<br />
Tāro varṇo'tha sambuddhipadaṁ tvāmityataḥ param||17.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.12">17.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uttamaikayutaṁ karmapadaṁ dīpakamapyataḥ|<br />
Tubhyaṁ nāma caturthyantaṁ tato'pyucitadīpakam||17.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.13">17.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ityūhamantrayogena tattatkarma pravartayet|<br />
Āvāhanānantaraṁ hi karma sarvaṁ nigadyate||17.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.14">17.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Āvāhanaṁ ca sambodhaḥ svasvabhāvavyavasthiteḥ|<br />
Bhāvasyāhammayasvātmatādātmyāveśyamānatā||17.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.15">17.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śāktī bhūmiśca saivoktā yasyāṁ mukhyāsti pūjyatā|<br />
Abhātatvādabhedācca nahyasau nṛśivātmanoḥ||17.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.16">17.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jaḍābhāseṣu tattveṣu saṁvitsthityai tato guruḥ|<br />
Āvāhanavibhaktiṁ prākkṛtvā turyavibhaktitaḥ||17.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.17">17.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Namaskārāntatāyogātpūrṇāṁ sattāṁ prakalpayet|<br />
Tataḥ pūrṇasvabhāvatvaṁ tadrūpodrekayogataḥ||17.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.18">17.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dhyeyodreko bhaveddhyātṛprahvībhāvavaśādyataḥ|<br />
Āvāhyeṣṭvā pratarpyeti śrīsvacchande nirūpitam||17.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.19">17.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anenaiva pathā neyamityasmadguravo jaguḥ|<br />
Paratvena tu yatpūjyaṁ tatsvatantracidātmakam||17.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.20">17.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anavacchitprakāśatvānna prakāśyaṁ tu kutracit|<br />
Tasya hyetatprapūjyatvadhyeyatvādi yadullaset||17.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.21">17.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasyaiva tatsvatantratvaṁ yātidurghaṭakāritā|<br />
Sambodharūpe tattasminkathaṁ sambodhanā bhavet||17.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.22">17.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prakāśanāyāṁ vai na syātprakāśasya prakāśatā|<br />
Sambodhanavibhaktyaiva vinā karmādiśaktitām||17.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.23">17.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svātantryāttaṁ darśayituṁ tatrohamimamācaret|<br />
Devamāvāhayāmīti tato devāya dīpakam||17.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.24">17.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prāgyuktyā pūrṇatādāyi namaḥsvāhādikaṁ bhavet|<br />
Nutiḥ pūrṇatvamagnīndusaṅghaṭṭāpyāyatā param||17.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.25">17.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Āpyāyakaṁ ca procchālaṁ vauṣaḍādi pradīpayet|<br />
Tatra bāhye'pi tādātmyaprasiddhaṁ karma codyate||17.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.26">17.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yadi karmapadaṁ tanno gururabhyūhayetkvacit|<br />
Anābhāsitatadvastubhāsanāya niyujyate||17.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.27">17.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mantraḥ kiṁ tena tatra syātsphuṭaṁ yatrāvabhāsi tat|<br />
Tena prokṣaṇasaṁsekajapādividhiṣu dhruvam||17.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.28">17.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatkarmābhyūhanaṁ kuryātpratyuta vyavadhātṛtām|<br />
Bahistathātmatābhāve kāryaṁ karmapadohanam||17.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.29">17.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tṛptāvāhutihutabhukpāśaploṣacchidādiṣu|<br />
Yatroddiṣṭe vidhau paścāttadanantaiḥ kriyātmakaiḥ||17.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.30">17.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aṁśaiḥ sādhyaṁ na tatroho dīkṣaṇādividhiṣviva|<br />
Tataḥ śiṣyasya tattattvasthāne'streṇa pratāḍanam||17.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.31">17.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kṛtvātha śivahastena hṛdayaṁ parimarśayet|<br />
Tataḥ svanāḍīmārgeṇa hṛdayaṁ prāpya vai śiśoḥ||17.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.32">17.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śiṣyātmanā sahaikatvaṁ gatvādāya ca taṁ hṛdā|<br />
Puṭitaṁ haṁsarūpākhyaṁ tatra saṁhāramudrayā||17.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.33">17.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kuryādātmīyahṛdayasthitamapyavabhāsakam|<br />
Śiṣyadehasya tejobhī raśmimātrāviyogataḥ||17.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.34">17.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svabandhasthānacalanātsvatantrasthānalābhataḥ|<br />
Svakarmāparatantratvātsarvatrotpattimarhati||17.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.35">17.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tenātmahṛdayānītaṁ prākkṛtvā pudgalaṁ tataḥ|<br />
Māyāyāṁ taddharātattvaśarīrāṇyasya saṁsṛjet||17.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.36">17.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatrāsya garbhādhānaṁ ca yuktaṁ puṁsavanādibhiḥ|<br />
Garbhaniṣkrāmaparyantairekāṁ kurvīta saṁskriyām||17.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.37">17.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jananaṁ bhogabhoktṛtvaṁ militvaikātha saṁskriyā|<br />
Tato'sya teṣu bhogeṣu kuryāttanmayatālayam||17.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.38">17.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatastattattvapāśānāṁ vicchedaṁ samupācaret|<br />
Saṁskārāṇāṁ catuṣke'sminnaparāṁ ca parāparām||17.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.39">17.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mantrāṇāṁ pañcadaśakaṁ parāṁ vā yojayetkramāt|<br />
Pibanyādyaṣṭakaṁ śastrādikaṁ ṣaṭkaṁ parā tathā||17.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.40">17.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti pañcadaśaite syuḥ kramāllīnatvasaṁskṛtau|<br />
Aparāmantramuktvā prāgamukātmana ityatha||17.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.41">17.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Garbhādhānaṁ karomīti punarmantraṁ tameva ca|<br />
Svāhāntamuccarandadyādāhutitritayaṁ guruḥ||17.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.42">17.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Paraṁ parāparāmantramamukātmana ityatha|<br />
Jātasya bhogabhoktṛtvaṁ karomyatha parāparām||17.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.43">17.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ante svāheti proccārya vitarettisra āhutīḥ|<br />
Uccārya pibanīmantramamukātmana ityatha||17.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.44">17.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhoge layaṁ karomīti punarmantraṁ tameva ca|<br />
Svāhāntamāhutīstisro dadyādājyatilādibhiḥ||17.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.45">17.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Eṣa eva vamanyādau vidhiḥ pañcadaśāntake|<br />
Pūrvaṁ parātmakaṁ mantramamukātmana ityatha||17.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.46">17.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pāśacchedaṁ karomīti parāmantraḥ punastataḥ|<br />
Huṁ svāhā phaṭ samuccārya dadyāttisro'pyathāhutīḥ||17.46||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.47">17.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṁskārāṇāṁ catuṣke'sminye mantrāḥ kathitā mayā|<br />
Teṣu karmapadātpūrvaṁ dharātattvapadaṁ vadet||17.47||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.48">17.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tato dharātattvapatimāmantryeṣṭvā pratarpya ca|<br />
Śivābhimānasaṁrabdho gururevaṁ samādiśet||17.48||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.49">17.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tattveśvara tvayā nāsya putrakasya śivājñayā|<br />
Pratibandhaḥ prakartavyo yātuḥ padamanāmayam||17.49||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.50"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.50">17.50</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tato yadi samīheta dharātattvāntarālagam|<br />
Pṛthakśodhayituṁ mantrī bhuvanādyadhvapañcakam||17.50||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.51"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.51">17.51</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aparāmantrataḥ prāgvattisrastisrastadāhutīḥ|<br />
Dadyātpuraṁ śodhayāmītyūhayuktaṁ prasannadhīḥ||17.51||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.52"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.52">17.52</a> <span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ kalāmantrapadavarṇeṣvapi vicakṣaṇaḥ|<br />
Tisrastisro hutīrdadyātpṛthaksāmastyato'pi vā||17.52||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.53"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.53">17.53</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tataḥ pūrṇāhutiṁ dattvā parayā vauṣaḍantayā|<br />
Aparāmantrataḥ śiṣyamuddhṛtyātmahṛdaṁ nayet||17.53||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.54"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.54">17.54</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yadā tvekena śuddhena tadantarbhāvacintanāt|<br />
Na pṛthakśodhayettattvanāthasaṁśravaṇātparam||17.54||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.55"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.55">17.55</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadā pūrṇāṁ vitīryāṇumutkṣipyātmani yojayet|<br />
Tātsthyātmasaṁsthayogāya tayaivāparayāhutīḥ||17.55||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.56"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.56">17.56</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sakarmapadayā dadyāditi kecittu manvate|<br />
Anye tu guravaḥ prāhurbhāvanāmayamīdṛśam||17.56||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.57"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.57">17.57</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nātra bāhyāhutirdeyā daiśikasya pṛthakpunaḥ|<br />
Dadyādvā yadi no doṣaḥ syādupāyaḥ sa bhāvane||17.57||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.58"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.58">17.58</a> <span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ prāktanatātsthyātmasaṁsthatve yojayedguruḥ|<br />
Tataḥ śiṣyahṛdaṁ neyaḥ sa ātmā tāvato'dhvanaḥ||17.58||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.59"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.59">17.59</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śuddhastaddārḍhyasiddhyai ca pūrṇā syātparayā punaḥ|<br />
Mahāpāśupataṁ pūrvaṁ vilomasya viśuddhaye||17.59||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.60"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.60">17.60</a> <span class="pv-iast">Juhomi punarastreṇa vauṣaḍantā iti kṣipet|<br />
Punaḥ pūrṇāṁ tato māyāmabhyarcyātha visarjayet||17.60||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.61"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.61">17.61</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dharātattvaṁ viśuddhaṁ sajjalena śuddharūpiṇā|<br />
Bhāvayenmiśritaṁ vāri śuddhiyogyaṁ tato bhavet||17.61||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.62"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.62">17.62</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathā tattatpurātattvamiśraṇāduttarottaram|<br />
Sarvā śivībhavettattvāvalī śuddhānyathā pṛthak||17.62||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.63"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.63">17.63</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pṛthaktvaṁ ca malo māyābhidhānastasya sambhave|<br />
Karmakṣaye'pi no muktirbhavedvidyeśvarādivat||17.63||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.64"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.64">17.64</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tato'pi jalatattvasya vahnau vyomni cidātmake|<br />
Āhvānādyakhilaṁ yāvattejasyasya vimaśraṇam||17.64||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.65"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.65">17.65</a> <span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ kramātkalātattve śuddhe pāśaṁ bhujāśritam|<br />
Chindyātkalā hi sā kiñcitkartṛtvonmīlanātmikā||17.65||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.66"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.66">17.66</a> <span class="pv-iast">Karmākhyamalajṛmbhātmā taṁ ca granthiṁ srugagragam|<br />
Pūrṇāhutyā samaṁ vahnimantratejasi nirdahet||17.66||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.67"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.67">17.67</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mantro hi viśvarūpaḥ sannupāśrayavaśāttathā|<br />
Vyaktarūpastato vahnau pāśaploṣavidhāyakaḥ||17.67||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.68"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.68">17.68</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pluṣṭo līnasvabhāvo'sau pāśastaṁ prati śambhuvat|<br />
Parameśamahātejaḥśeṣamātratvamaśnute||17.68||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.69"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.69">17.69</a> <span class="pv-iast">Karmapāśe'tra hotavye pūrṇayāsya śubhāśubham|<br />
Aśubhaṁ vā bhavadbhūtaṁ bhāvi vātha samastakam||17.69||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.70"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.70">17.70</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dahāmi phaṭtrayaṁ vauṣaḍiti pūrṇāṁ vinikṣipet|<br />
Evaṁ māyāntasaṁśuddhau kaṇṭhapāśaṁ ca homayet||17.70||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.71"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.71">17.71</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pūrṇayā tasya māyākhyaṁ pāśaṁ bhedaprathātmakam|<br />
Dahāmi phaṭtrayaṁ vauṣaḍiti pūrṇāṁ kṣipedguruḥ||17.71||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.72"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.72">17.72</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nirbījā yadi kāryā tu tadātraivāparāṁ kṣipet|<br />
Pūrṇāṁ samayapāśākhyabījadāhapadānvitām||17.72||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.73"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.73">17.73</a> <span class="pv-iast">Gurau deve tathā śāstre bhaktiḥ kāryāsya nahyasau|<br />
Samayaḥ śaktipātasya svabhāvo hyeṣa no pṛthak||17.73||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.74"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.74">17.74</a> <span class="pv-iast">Māyānte śuddhimāyāte vāgīśī yā purābhavat|<br />
Māyāśaktimayī saiva vidyāśaktitvamaśnute||17.74||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.75"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.75">17.75</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tacchuddhavidyāmāhūya vidyāśaktiṁ niyojayet|<br />
Evaṁ krameṇa saṁśuddhe sadāśivapade'pyalam||17.75||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.76"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.76">17.76</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śikhāṁ granthiyutāṁ chittvā malamāṇavakaṁ dahet|<br />
Yato'dhikārabhogākhyau dvau pāśau tu sadāśive||17.76||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.77"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.77">17.77</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ityuktyāṇavapāśo'tra māyīyastu niśāvadhiḥ|<br />
Śiṣyo yathocitaṁ snāyādācāmeddaiśikaḥ svayam||17.77||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.78"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.78">17.78</a> <span class="pv-iast">Āṇavākhye vinirdagdhe hyadhovāhiśikhāmale|<br />
Tataḥ prāguktasakalaprameyaṁ paricintayan||17.78||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.79"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.79">17.79</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śiṣyadehādimātmīyadehaprāṇādiyojitam|<br />
Kṛtvātmadehaprāṇāderviśvamantaranusmaret||17.79||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.80"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.80">17.80</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktaprakriyayā caivaṁ dṛḍhabuddhirananyadhīḥ|<br />
Prāṇasthaṁ deśakālādhvayugaṁ prāṇaṁ ca śaktigam||17.80||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.81"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.81">17.81</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tāṁ ca saṁvidgatāṁ śuddhāṁ saṁvidaṁ śivarūpiṇīm|<br />
Śiṣyasaṁvidabhinnāṁ ca mantravahnyādyabhedinīm||17.81||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.82"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.82">17.82</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dhyāyanprāgvatprayogeṇa śivaṁ sakalaniṣkalam|<br />
Dvyātmakaṁ vā kṣipetpūrṇāṁ praśāntakaraṇena tu||17.82||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.83"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.83">17.83</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktaṁ traiśirase tantre sarvasampūraṇātmakam|<br />
Mūlādudayagatyā tu śivenduparisamplutam||17.83||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.84"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.84">17.84</a> <span class="pv-iast">Janmāntamadhyakuharamūlasrotaḥsamutthitam|<br />
Śivārkaraśmibhistīvraiḥ kṣubdhaṁ jñānāmṛtaṁ tu yat||17.84||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.85"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.85">17.85</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tena santarpayetsamyakpraśāntakaraṇena tu|<br />
Śūnyadhāmābjamadhyasthaprabhākiraṇabhāsvaraḥ||17.85||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.86"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.86">17.86</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ādheyādhāraniḥspandabodhaśāstraparigrahaḥ|<br />
Janmādheyaprapañcaikasphoṭasaṅghaṭṭaghaṭṭanaḥ||17.86||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.87"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.87">17.87</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mūlasthānātsamārabhya kṛtvā someśamantagam|<br />
Khamiva tiṣṭhate yāvatpraśāntaṁ tāvaducyate||17.87||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.88"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.88">17.88</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktaṁ śrīpūrvaśāstre ca srucamāpūrya sarpiṣā|<br />
Kṛtvā śiṣyaṁ tathātmasthaṁ mūlamantramanusmaran||17.88||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.89"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.89">17.89</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śivaṁ śaktiṁ tathātmānaṁ śiṣyaṁ sarpistathānalam|<br />
Ekīkurvañchanairgaccheddvādaśāntamananyadhīḥ||17.89||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.90"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.90">17.90</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatra kumbhakamāsthāya dhyāyansakalaniṣkalam|<br />
Tiṣṭhettāvadanudvigno yāvadājyakṣayo bhavet||17.90||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.91"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.91">17.91</a> <span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ yuktaḥ pare tattve guruṇā śivamūrtinā|<br />
Na bhūyaḥ paśutāmeti dagdhamāyānibandhanaḥ||17.91||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.92"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.92">17.92</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dehapāte punaḥ prepsedyadi tattveṣu kutracit|<br />
Bhogānsamastavyastatvabhedairante paraṁ padam||17.92||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.93"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.93">17.93</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadā tattattvabhūmau tu tatsaṅkhyāyāmananyadhīḥ|<br />
Punaryojanikāṁ kuryātpūrṇāhutyantareṇa tu||17.93||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.94"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.94">17.94</a> <span class="pv-iast">Muktipradā bhogamokṣapradā vā yā prakīrtitā|<br />
Dīkṣā sā syātsabījatvanirbījātmatayā dvidhā||17.94||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.95"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.95">17.95</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bāle nirjñātamaraṇe tvaśakte vā jarādibhiḥ|<br />
Kāryā nirbījikā dīkṣā śaktipātabalodaye||17.95||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.96"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.96">17.96</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nirbījāyāṁ sāmayāṁstu pāśānapi viśodhayet|<br />
Kṛtanirbījadīkṣastu devāgnigurubhaktibhāk||17.96||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.97"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.97">17.97</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iyataiva śivaṁ yāyātsadyo bhogānvibhujya vā|<br />
Śrīmaddīkṣottare coktaṁ cāre ṣaṭtriṁśadaṅgule||17.97||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.98"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.98">17.98</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tattvānyāpādamūrdhāntaṁ bhuvanāni tyajetkramāt|<br />
Tuṭimātraṁ niṣkalaṁ yadadehaṁ tadahaṁ param||17.98||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.99"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.99">17.99</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śaktyā tatra kṣipāmyenamiti dhyāyaṁstu dīkṣayet|<br />
Sabījāyāṁ tu dīkṣāyāṁ samayānna viśodhayet||17.99||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.100"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.100">17.100</a> <span class="pv-iast">Viśeṣastvayametasyāṁ yāvajjīvaṁ śiśorguruḥ|<br />
Śeṣavṛttyai śuddhatattvasṛṣṭiṁ kurvīta pūrṇayā||17.100||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.101"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.101">17.101</a> <span class="pv-iast">Abhinnācchivasambodhajaladheryugapatsphurat|<br />
Pūrṇāṁ kṣipaṁstattvajālaṁ dhyāyedbhārūpakaṁ sṛtam||17.101||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.102"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.102">17.102</a> <span class="pv-iast">Viśuddhatattvasṛṣṭiṁ vā kuryātkumbhābhiṣecanāt|<br />
Tathā dhyānabalādeva yadvā pūrṇābhiṣecanaiḥ||17.102||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.103"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.103">17.103</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pṛthivī sthirarūpāsya śivarūpeṇa bhāvitā|<br />
Sthirīkaroti tāmeva bhāvanāmiti śuddhyati||17.103||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.104"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.104">17.104</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jalamāpyāyayatyenāṁ tejo bhāsvaratāṁ nayet|<br />
Marudānandasaṁsparśaṁ vyoma vaitatyamāvahet||17.104||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.105"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.105">17.105</a> <span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ tanmātravargo'pi śivatāmaya iṣyate|<br />
Parānandamahāvyāptiraśeṣamalavicyutiḥ||17.105||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.106"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.106">17.106</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śive gantṛtvamādānamupādeyaśivastutiḥ|<br />
Śivāmodabharāsvādadarśanasparśanānyalam||17.106||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.107"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.107">17.107</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadākarṇanamityevamindriyāṇāṁ viśuddhatā|<br />
Saṅkalpādhyavasāmānāḥ prakāśo raktisaṁsthitī||17.107||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.108"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.108">17.108</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śivātmatvena yatseyaṁ śuddhatā mānasādike|<br />
Niyamo rañjanaṁ kartṛbhāvaḥ kalanayā saha||17.108||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.109"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.109">17.109</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vedanaṁ heyavastvaṁśaviṣaye suptakalpatā|<br />
Itthaṁ śivaikyarūḍhasya ṣaṭkañcukagaṇo'pyayam||17.109||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.110"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.110">17.110</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śuddha eva pumānprāptaśivabhāvo viśuddhyati|<br />
Vidyeśādiṣu tattveṣu naiva kācidaśuddhatā||17.110||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.111"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.111">17.111</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ityevaṁ śuddhatattvānāṁ sṛṣṭyā śiṣyo'pi tanmayaḥ|<br />
Bhaveddhyetatsūcitaṁ śrīmālinīvijayottare||17.111||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.112"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.112">17.112</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bandhamokṣāvubhāvetāvindriyāṇāṁ jagurbudhāḥ|<br />
Nigṛhītāni bandhāya vimuktāni vimuktaye||17.112||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.113"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.113">17.113</a> <span class="pv-iast">Etāni vyāpake bhāve yadā syurmanasā saha|<br />
Muktāni kvāpi viṣaye rodhādbandhāya tāni tu||17.113||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.114"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.114">17.114</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ityevaṁ dvividho bhāvaḥ śuddhāśuddhaprabhedataḥ|<br />
Indriyāṇāṁ samākhyātaḥ siddhayogīśvarīmate||17.114||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.115"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.115">17.115</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīmānvidyāgurustvāha pramāṇastutidarśane|<br />
Samastamantrairdīkṣāyāṁ niyamastveṣa kathyate||17.115||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.116"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.116">17.116</a> <span class="pv-iast">Māyāntaśuddhau sarvāḥ syuḥ kriyā hyaparayā sadā|<br />
Dvyātmayā sakalānte tu niṣkale parayaiva tu||17.116||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.117"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.117">17.117</a> <span class="pv-iast">Īśānte ca pibanyādi sakalānte'ṅgapañcakam|<br />
Ityevaṁvidhimālocya karma kuryādgurūttamaḥ||17.117||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.118"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.118">17.118</a> <span class="pv-iast">Purādhvani hutīnāṁ yā saṅkhyeyaṁ tattvavarṇayoḥ|<br />
Tāmeva dviguṇīkuryātpadādhvani caturguṇām||17.118||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.119"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.119">17.119</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kramānmantrakalāmārge dviguṇā dviguṇā kramāt|<br />
Yāvattritattvasaṁśuddhau syādviṁśatiguṇā tataḥ||17.119||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.120"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.120">17.120</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pratikarma bhavetṣaṣṭirāhutīnāṁ tritattvake|<br />
Ekatattve śataṁ prāhurāhutīnāṁ tu sāṣṭakam||17.120||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.121"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.121">17.121</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vilomakarmaṇā sākaṁ yāḥ pūrṇāhutayaḥ smṛtāḥ|<br />
Tāsāṁ sarvādhvasaṁśuddhau saṅkhyānyatvaṁ na kiñcana||17.121||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v17.122"><a class="ta-num" href="#v17.122">17.122</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ityeṣā kathitā dīkṣā jananādisamanvitā||17.122||</span></p>
