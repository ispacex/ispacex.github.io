<p class="ta-st" id="v6.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.20">6.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atadrūḍhānyajanatākartavyaparilopanāt|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.29">6.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Amūrtāyāḥ sarvagatvānniṣkriyāyāśca saṁvidaḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.39">6.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kriyāśaktiḥ samastānāṁ tattvānāṁ ca paraṁ vapuḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.40">6.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Udriktābhogakāryātmaviśvaikātmyamidaṁ yataḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.211"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.211">6.211</a> <span class="pv-iast">Antaḥsaṅkrāntigaṁ grāhyaṁ tanmukhyaṁ tatphaloditeḥ|<br />
Uktaḥ samānagaḥ kāla udāne tu nirūpyate||6.211||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.212"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.212">6.212</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prāṇavyāptau yaduktaṁ tadudāne'pyatra kevalam|<br />
Nāsāśaktyantayoḥ sthāne brahmarandhrordhvadhāmanī||6.212||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.213"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.213">6.213</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tenodāne'tra hṛdayānmūrdhanyadvādaśāntagam|<br />
Tuṭyādiṣaṣṭivarṣāntaṁ viśvaṁ kālaṁ vicārayet||6.213||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.214"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.214">6.214</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vyāne tu viśvātmamaye vyāpake kramavarjite|<br />
Sūkṣmasūkṣmocchaladrūpamātraḥ kālo vyavasthitaḥ||6.214||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.215"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.215">6.215</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sṛṣṭiḥ pravilayaḥ sthemā saṁhāro'nugraho yataḥ|<br />
Kramātprāṇādike kāle taṁ taṁ tatrāśrayettataḥ||6.215||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.216"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.216">6.216</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prāṇacāre'tra yo varṇapadamantrodayaḥ sthitaḥ|<br />
Yatnajo'yatnajaḥ sūkṣmaḥ paraḥ sthūlaḥ sa kathyate||6.216||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.217"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.217">6.217</a> <span class="pv-iast">Eko nādātmako varṇaḥ sarvavarṇāvibhāgavān|<br />
So'nastamitarūpatvādanāhata ihoditaḥ||6.217||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.218"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.218">6.218</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sa tu bhairavasadbhāvo mātṛsadbhāva eṣa saḥ|<br />
Parā saikākṣarā devī yatra līnaṁ carācaram||6.218||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.219"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.219">6.219</a> <span class="pv-iast">Hrasvārṇatrayamekaikaṁ ravyaṅgulamathetarat|<br />
Praveśa iti ṣaḍvarṇāḥ sūryendupathagāḥ kramāt||6.219||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.220"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.220">6.220</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ikārokārayorādisandhau sandhyakṣaradvayam|<br />
E o iti praveśe tu ai au iti dvayaṁ viduḥ||6.220||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.221"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.221">6.221</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ṣaṇṭhārṇāni praveśe tu dvādaśāntalalāṭayoḥ|<br />
Gale hṛdi ca bindvarṇavisargau paritaḥsthitau||6.221||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.222"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.222">6.222</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kādipañcakamādyasya varṇasyāntaḥ sadoditam|<br />
Evaṁ sasthānavarṇānāmantaḥ sā sārṇasantatiḥ||6.222||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.223"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.223">6.223</a> <span class="pv-iast">Hṛdyeṣa prāṇarūpastu sakāro jīvanātmakaḥ|<br />
Binduḥ prakāśo hārṇaśca pūraṇātmatayā sthitaḥ||6.223||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.224"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.224">6.224</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktaḥ paro'yamudayo varṇānāṁ sūkṣma ucyate|<br />
Praveśe ṣoḍaśaunmukhye ravayaḥ ṣaṇṭhavarjitāḥ||6.224||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.225"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.225">6.225</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadevendvarkamatrānye varṇāḥ sūkṣmodayastvayam|<br />
Kālo'rdhamātraḥ kādīnāṁ trayastriṁśata ucyate||6.225||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.226"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.226">6.226</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mātrā hrasvāḥ pañca dīrghāṣṭakaṁ dvistriḥ plutaṁ tu ḹ|<br />
Ekāśītimimāmardhamātrāṇāmāha no guruḥ||6.226||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.227"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.227">6.227</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yadvaśādbhagavānekāśītikaṁ mantramabhyadhāt|<br />
Ekāśītipadā devī śaktiḥ proktā śivātmikā||6.227||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.228"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.228">6.228</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīmātaṅge tathā dharmasaṅghātātmā śivo yataḥ|<br />
Tathā tathā parāmarśaśakticakreśvaraḥ prabhuḥ||6.228||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.229"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.229">6.229</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sthūlaikāśītipadajaparāmarśairvibhāvyate|<br />
Tata eva parāmarśo yāvatyekaḥ samāpyate||6.229||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.230"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.230">6.230</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tāvattatpadamuktaṁ no suptiṅniyamayantritam|<br />
Ekāśītipadodāravimarśakramabṛṁhitaḥ||6.230||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.231"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.231">6.231</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sthūlopāyaḥ paropāyastveṣa mātrākṛto layaḥ|<br />
Ardhamātrā nava nava syuścaturṣu caturṣu yat||6.231||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.232"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.232">6.232</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aṅguleṣviti ṣaṭtriṁśatyekāśītipadodayaḥ|<br />
Aṅgule navabhāgena vibhakte navamāśakāḥ||6.232||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.233"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.233">6.233</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vedā mātrārdhamanyattu dvicatuḥṣaḍguṇaṁ trayam|<br />
Evamaṅgularandhrāṁśacatuṣkadvayagaṁ laghu||6.233||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.234"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.234">6.234</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dīrghaṁ plutaṁ kramāddvitriguṇamardhaṁ tato'pi hal|<br />
Kṣakārastryardhamātrātmā mātrikaḥ sa tathāntarā||6.234||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.235"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.235">6.235</a> <span class="pv-iast">Viśrāntāvardhamātrāsya tasmiṁstu kalite sati|<br />
Aṅgulārdhe'dribhāgena tvardhamātrā purā punaḥ||6.235||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.236"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.236">6.236</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kṣakāraḥ sarvasaṁyogagrahaṇātmā tu sarvagaḥ|<br />
Sarvavarṇodayādyantasandhiṣūdayabhāgvibhuḥ||6.236||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.237"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.237">6.237</a> <span class="pv-iast">Itthaṁ ṣaṭtriṁśake cāre varṇānāmudayaḥ phale|<br />
Krūre saumye vilomena hādi yāvadapaścimam||6.237||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.238"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.238">6.238</a> <span class="pv-iast">Hṛdyakāro dvādaśānte hakārastadidaṁ viduḥ|<br />
Ahamātmakamadvaitaṁ yaḥ prakāśātmaviśramaḥ||6.238||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.239"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.239">6.239</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śivaśaktyavibhāgena mātraikāśītikā tviyam|<br />
Dvāsaptatāvaṅguleṣu dviguṇatvena saṁsaret||6.239||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.240"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.240">6.240</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktaḥ sūkṣmodayastraidhaṁ dvidhoktastu parodayaḥ|<br />
Atha sthūlodayo'rṇānāṁ bhaṇyate guruṇoditaḥ||6.240||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.241"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.241">6.241</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ekaikamardhapraharaṁ dine vargāṣṭakodayaḥ|<br />
Rātrau ca hrāsavṛddhī atra kecidāhurna ke'pi tu||6.241||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.242"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.242">6.242</a> <span class="pv-iast">Eṣa vargodayo rātrau divā cāpyardhayāmagaḥ|<br />
Prāṇatrayodaśaśatī pañcāśadadhikā ca sā||6.242||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.243"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.243">6.243</a> <span class="pv-iast">Adhyardhā kila saṅkrāntirvarge varge divāniśoḥ|<br />
Tadaikye tūdayaścāraśatānāṁ saptaviṁśatiḥ||6.243||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.244"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.244">6.244</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nava vargāṁstu ye prāhusteṣāṁ prāṇaśatī ravīn[viḥ]|<br />
Satribhāgaiva saṅkrāntirvarge pratyekamucyate||6.244||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.245"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.245">6.245</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aharniśaṁ tadaikye tu śatānāṁ śruticakṣuṣī|<br />
Sthūlo vargodayaḥ so'yamathārṇodaya ucyate||6.245||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.246"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.246">6.246</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ekaikavarṇe prāṇānāṁ dviśataṁ ṣoḍaśādhikam|<br />
Bahiścaṣakaṣaṭtriṁśaddina itthaṁ tathā niśi||6.246||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.247"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.247">6.247</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śatamaṣṭottaraṁ tatra raudraṁ śāktamathottaram|<br />
Yāmalasthitiyoge tu rudraśaktyavibhāgitā||6.247||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.248"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.248">6.248</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dinarātryavibhāge tu dṛgvahnyabdhyasucāraṇāḥ|<br />
Sapañcamāṁśā nāḍī ca bahirvarṇodayaḥ smṛtaḥ||6.248||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.249"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.249">6.249</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti pañcāśikā seyaṁ varṇānāṁ paricarcitā|<br />
Ekonāṁ ye tu tāmāhustanmataṁ sampracakṣmahe||6.249||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.250"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.250">6.250</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vedāścārāḥ pañcamāṁśanyūnaṁ cārārdhamekaśaḥ|<br />
Varṇe'dhikaṁ taddviguṇamavibhāge divāniśoḥ||6.250||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.251"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.251">6.251</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sthūlo varṇodayaḥ so'yaṁ purā sūkṣmo nigadyate||6.251||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v6.252"><a class="ta-num" href="#v6.252">6.252</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti kālatattvamuditaṁ śāstramukhāgamanijānubhavasiddham||6.252||</span></p>
