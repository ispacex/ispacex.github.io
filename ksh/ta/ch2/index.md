# Глава 2 — Gatopāyaḥ

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">50 строф · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [перевод главы у источника](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/ru/node/857)</p>

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Глава 1 — Vijñānabhit](/ksh/ta/ch1/) · [Глава 3 — Paropāyaḥ →](/ksh/ta/ch3/)</p>

<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloke dvitīyamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.1">2.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yattatrādyaṁ padamaviratānuttarajñaptirūpaṁ|<br />
Tannirṇetuṁ prakaraṇamidamārabhe'haṁ dvitīyam||2.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.2">2.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anupāyaṁ hi yadrūpaṁ ko'rtho deśanayātra vai|<br />
Sakṛtsyāddeśanā paścādanupāyatvamucyate||2.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.3">2.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anupāyamidaṁ tattvamityupāyaṁ vinā kutaḥ|<br />
Svayaṁ tu teṣāṁ tattādṛk kiṁ brūmaḥ kila tānprati||2.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.4">2.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yaccaturdhoditaṁ rūpaṁ vijñānasya vibhorasau|<br />
Svabhāva eva mantavyaḥ sa hi nityodito vibhuḥ||2.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.5">2.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Etāvadbhirasaṅkhyātaiḥ svabhāvairyatprakāśate|<br />
Ke'pyaṁśāṁśikayā tena viśantyanye niraṁśataḥ||2.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.6">2.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatrāpi cābhyupāyādisāpekṣānyatvayogataḥ|<br />
Upāyasyāpi no vāryā tadanyatvādvicitratā||2.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.7">2.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatra ye nirmalātmāno bhairavīyāṁ svasaṁvidam|<br />
Nirupāyāmupāsīnāstadvidhiḥ praṇigadyate||2.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.8">2.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatra tāvatkriyāyogo nābhyupāyatvamarhati|<br />
Sa hi tasmātsamudbhūtaḥ pratyuta pravibhāvyate||2.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.9">2.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jñaptāvupāya eva syāditi cejjñaptirucyate|<br />
Prakāśatvaṁ svaprakāśe tacca tatrānyataḥ katham||2.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.10">2.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṁvittattvaṁ svaprakāśamityasminkaṁ nu yuktibhiḥ|<br />
Tadabhāve bhavedviśvaṁ jaḍatvādaprakāśakam||2.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.11">2.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yāvānupāyo bāhyaḥ syādāntaro vāpi kaścana|<br />
Sa sarvastanmukhaprekṣī tatropāyatvabhākkatham||2.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.12">2.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tyajāvadhānāni nanu kva nāma dhatse'vadhānaṁ vicinu svayaṁ tat|<br />
Pūrṇe'vadhānaṁ na hi nāma yuktaṁ nāpūrṇamabhyeti ca satyabhāvam||2.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.13">2.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tenāvadhānaprāṇasya bhāvanādeḥ pare pathi|<br />
Bhairavīye kathaṅkāraṁ bhavetsākṣādupāyatā||2.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.14">2.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ye'pi sākṣādupāyena tadrūpaṁ praviviñcate|<br />
Nūnaṁ te sūryasaṁvittyai khadyotādhitsavo jaḍāḥ||2.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.15">2.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kiṁ ca yāvadidaṁ bāhyamāntaropāyasammatam|<br />
Tatprakāśātmatāmātraṁ śivasyaiva nijaṁ vapuḥ||2.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.16">2.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nīlaṁ pītaṁ sukhamiti prakāśaḥ kevalaḥ śivaḥ|<br />
Amuṣminparamādvaite prakāśātmani ko'paraḥ||2.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.17">2.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Upāyopeyabhāvaḥ syātprakāśaḥ kevalaṁ hi saḥ||2.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.18">2.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Idaṁ dvaitamayaṁ bheda idamadvaitamityapi|<br />
Prakāśavapurevāyaṁ bhāsate parameśvaraḥ||2.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.19">2.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Asyāṁ bhūmau sukhaṁ duḥkhaṁ bandho mokṣaścitirjaḍaḥ|<br />
Ghaṭakumbhavadekārthāḥ śabdāste'pyekameva ca||2.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.20">2.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prakāśe hyaprakāśāṁśaḥ kathaṁ nāma prakāśatām|<br />
Prakāśamāne tasminvā taddvaitāstasya lopitāḥ||2.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.21">2.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aprakāśe'tha tasminvā vastutā kathamucyate|<br />
Na prakāśaviśeṣatvamata evopapadyate||2.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.22">2.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ata ekaprakāśo'yamiti vāde'tra susthite|<br />
Dūrādāvāritāḥ satyaṁ vibhinnajñānavādinaḥ||2.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.23">2.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prakāśamātramuditamaprakāśaniṣedhanāt|<br />
Ekaśabdasya na tvarthaḥ saṅkhyā cidvyaktibhedabhāk||2.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.24">2.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Naiṣa śaktirmahādevī na paratrāśrito yataḥ|<br />
Na caiṣa śaktimāndevo na kasyāpyāśrayo yataḥ||2.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.25">2.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Naiṣa dhyeyo dhyātrabhāvānna dhyātā dhyeyavarjanāt|<br />
Na pūjyaḥ pūjakābhāvātpūjyābhāvānna pūjakaḥ||2.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.26">2.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na mantro na ca mantryo'sau na ca mantrayitā prabhuḥ|<br />
Na dīkṣā dīkṣako vāpi na dīkṣāvānmaheśvaraḥ||2.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.27">2.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sthānāsananirodhārghasandhānāvāhanādikam|<br />
Visarjanāntaṁ nāstyatra kartṛkarmakriyojjhite||2.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.28">2.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na sanna cāsatsadasanna ca tannobhayojjhitam|<br />
Durvijñeyā hi sāvasthā kimapyetadanuttaram||2.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.29">2.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ayamityavabhāso hi yo bhāvo'vacchinnātmakaḥ|<br />
Sa eva ghaṭavalloke saṁstathā naiṣa bhairavaḥ||2.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.30">2.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Asattvaṁ cāprakāśatvaṁ na kutrāpyupayogitā|<br />
Viśvasya jīvitaṁ satyaṁ prakāśaikātmakaśca saḥ||2.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.31">2.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ābhyāmeva tu hetubhyāṁ na dvyātmā na dvayojjhitaḥ|<br />
Sarvātmanā hi bhātyeṣa kena rūpeṇa mantryatām||2.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.32">2.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīmattriśirasi proktaṁ parajñānasvarūpakam|<br />
Śaktyā garbhāntarvartinyā śaktigarbhaṁ paraṁ padam||2.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.33">2.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na bhāvo nāpyabhāvo na dvayaṁ vācāmagocarāt|<br />
Akathyapadavīrūḍhaṁ śaktisthaṁ śaktivarjitam||2.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.34">2.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti ye rūḍhasaṁvittiparamārthapavitritāḥ|<br />
Anuttarapathe rūḍhāste'bhyupāyāniyantritāḥ||2.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.35">2.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Teṣāmidaṁ samābhāti sarvato bhāvamaṇḍalam|<br />
Puraḥsthameva saṁvittibhairavāgnivilāpitam||2.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.36">2.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Eteṣāṁ sukhaduḥkhāṁśaśaṅkātaṅkavikalpanāḥ|<br />
Nirvikalpaparāveśamātraśeṣatvamāgatāḥ||2.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.37">2.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Eṣāṁ na mantro na dhyānaṁ na pūjā nāpi kalpanā|<br />
Na samayyādikācāryaparyantaḥ ko'pi vibhramaḥ||2.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.38">2.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Samastayantraṇātantratroṭanāṭaṅkadharmiṇaḥ|<br />
Nānugrahātparaṁ kiñciccheṣavṛttau prayojanam||2.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.39">2.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svaṁ kartavyaṁ kimapi kalayaṁlloka eṣa prayatnānno pārārthyaṁ prati ghaṭayate kāñcana svapravṛttim|<br />
Yastu dhvastākhilabhavamalo bhairavībhāvapūrṇaḥ kṛtyaṁ tasya sphuṭamidamiyallokakartavyamātram||2.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.40">2.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Taṁ ye paśyanti tādrūpyakrameṇāmalasaṁvidaḥ|<br />
Te'pi tadrūpiṇastāvatyevāsyānugrahātmatā||2.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.41">2.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Etattattvaparijñānaṁ mukhyaṁ yāgādi kathyate|<br />
Dīkṣāntaṁ vibhunā śrīmatsiddhayogīśvarīmate||2.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.42">2.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sthaṇḍilāduttaraṁ tūraṁ tūrāduttarataḥ paṭaḥ|<br />
Paṭāddhyānaṁ tato dhyeyaṁ tataḥ syāddhāraṇottarā||2.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.43">2.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tato'pi yogajaṁ rūpaṁ tato'pi jñānamuttaram|<br />
Jñānena hi mahāsiddho bhavedyogīśvarastviti||2.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.44">2.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">So'pi svātantryadhāmnā cedapyanirmalasaṁvidām|<br />
Anugrahaṁ cikīrṣustadbhāvinaṁ vidhimāśrayet||2.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.45">2.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anugrāhyānusāreṇa vicitraḥ sa ca kathyate|<br />
Parāparādyupāyaughasaṅkīrṇatvavibhedataḥ||2.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.46">2.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadarthameva cāsyāpi parameśvararūpiṇaḥ|<br />
Tadabhyupāyaśāstrādiśravaṇādhyayanādaraḥ||2.46||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.47">2.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nahi tasya svatantrasya kāpi kutrāpi khaṇḍanā|<br />
Nānirmalacitaḥ puṁso'nugrahastvanupāyakaḥ||2.47||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.48">2.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīmadūrmimahāśāstre siddhasantānarūpake|<br />
Idamuktaṁ tathā śrīmatsomānandādidaiśikaiḥ||2.48||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.49">2.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Gurorvākyādyuktipracayaracanonmārjanavaśāt samāśvāsācchāstraṁ prati samuditādvāpi kathitāt|<br />
Vilīne śaṅkābhre hṛdayagaganodbhāsimahasaḥ prabhoḥ sūryasyeva spṛśata caraṇāndhvāntajayinaḥ||2.49||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v2.50"><a class="ta-num" href="#v2.50">2.50</a> <span class="pv-iast">Idamanuttaradhāmavivecakaṁ vigalitaupayikaṁ kṛtamāhnikam||2.50||</span></p>
