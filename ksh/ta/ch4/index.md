# Глава 4 — Śāktopāyaḥ

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.1">4.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha śāktamupāyamaṇḍalaṁ kathayāmaḥ paramātmasaṁvide||4.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.2">4.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anantarāhnikokte'sminsvabhāve pārameśvare|<br />
Pravivikṣurvikalpasya kuryātsaṁskāramañjasā||4.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.3">4.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vikalpaḥ saṁskṛtaḥ sūte vikalpaṁ svātmasaṁskṛtam|<br />
Svatulyaṁ so'pi so'pyanyaṁ so'pyanyaṁ sadṛśātmakam||4.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.4">4.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Caturṣveva vikalpeṣu yaḥ saṁskāraḥ kramādasau|<br />
Asphuṭaḥ sphuṭatābhāvī prasphuṭansphuṭitātmakaḥ||4.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.5">4.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tataḥ sphuṭataro yāvadante sphuṭatamo bhavet|<br />
Asphuṭādau vikalpe ca bhedo'pyastyāntarālikaḥ||4.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.6">4.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tataḥ sphuṭatamodāratādrūpyaparivṛṁhitā|<br />
Saṁvidabhyeti vimalāmavikalpasvarūpatām||4.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.7">4.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ataśca bhairavīyaṁ yattejaḥ saṁvitsvabhāvakam|<br />
Bhūyo bhūyo vimṛśatāṁ jāyate tatsphuṭātmatā||4.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.8">4.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nanu saṁvitparāmraṣṭrī parāmarśamayī svataḥ|<br />
Parāmṛśyā kathaṁ tāthārūpyasṛṣṭau tu sā jaḍā||4.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.9">4.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ucyate svātmasaṁvittiḥ svabhāvādeva nirbharā|<br />
Nāsyāmapāsyaṁ nādheyaṁ kiñcidityuditaṁ purā||4.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.10">4.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kiṁ tu durghaṭakāritvātsvācchandyānnirmalādasau|<br />
Svātmapracchādanakrīḍāpaṇḍitaḥ parameśvaraḥ||4.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.11">4.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anāvṛtte svarūpe'pi yadātmācchādanaṁ vibhoḥ|<br />
Saiva māyā yato bheda etāvānviśvavṛttikaḥ||4.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.12">4.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathābhāsanamevāsya dvaitamuktaṁ maheśituḥ|<br />
Taddvayāpāsanenāyaṁ parāmarśo'bhidhīyate||4.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.13">4.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Durbhedapādapasyāsya mūlaṁ kṛntanti kovidāḥ|<br />
Dhārārūḍhena sattarkakuṭhāreṇeti niścayaḥ||4.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.14">4.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tāmenāṁ bhāvanāmāhuḥ sarvakāmadughāṁ budhāḥ|<br />
Sphuṭayedvastu yāpetaṁ manorathapadādapi||4.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.15">4.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīpūrvaśāstre tatproktaṁ tarko yogāṅgamuttamam|<br />
Heyādyālocanāttasmāttatra yatnaḥ praśasyate||4.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.16">4.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mārge cetaḥ sthirībhūtaṁ heye'pi viṣayecchayā|<br />
Prerya tena nayettāvadyāvatpadamanāmayam||4.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.17">4.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mārgo'tra mokṣopāyaḥ sa heyaḥ śāstrāntaroditaḥ|<br />
Viṣiṇoti nibadhnāti yecchā niyatisaṅgatam||4.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.18">4.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Rāgatattvaṁ tayoktaṁ yat tena tatrānurajyate|<br />
Yathā sāmrājyasambhogaṁ dṛṣṭvādṛṣṭvāthavādhame||4.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.19">4.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhoge rajyeta durbuddhistadvanmokṣe'pi rāgataḥ|<br />
Sa evāṁśaka ityuktaḥ svabhāvākhyaḥ sa tu sphuṭam||4.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.20">4.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Siddhyaṅgamiti mokṣāya pratyūha iti kovidāḥ|<br />
Śivaśāsanamāhātmyaṁ vidannapyata eva hi||4.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.21">4.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vaiṣṇavādyeṣu rajyeta mūḍho rāgeṇa rañjitaḥ|<br />
Yatastāvati sā tasya vāmākhyā śaktiraiśvarī||4.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.22">4.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pāñcarātrikavairiñcasaugatādervijṛmbhate|<br />
Dṛṣṭāḥ sāmrājyasambhogaṁ nindantaḥ ke'pi bāliśāḥ||4.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.23">4.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na tu santoṣataḥ sveṣu bhogeṣvāśīḥpravartanāt|<br />
Evañcidbhairavāveśanindātatparamānasāḥ||4.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.24">4.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhavantyatisughorābhiḥ śaktibhiḥ pātitā yataḥ|<br />
Tena śāmbhavamāhātmyaṁ jānanyaḥ śāsanāntare||4.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.25">4.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Āśvasto nottarītavyaṁ tena bhedamahārṇavāt|<br />
Śrīkāmikāyāṁ proktaṁ ca pāśaprakaraṇe sphuṭam||4.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.26">4.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vedasāṅkhyapurāṇajñāḥ pāñcarātraparāyaṇāḥ|<br />
Ye kecidṛṣayo dhīrāḥ śāstrāntaraparāyaṇāḥ||4.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.27">4.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bauddhārhatādyāḥ sarve te vidyārāgeṇa rañjitāḥ|<br />
Māyāpāśena baddhatvācchivadīkṣāṁ na vindate||4.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.28">4.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Rāgaśabdena ca proktaṁ rāgatattvaṁ niyāmakam|<br />
Māyīye tacca taṁ tasmiñchāstre niyamayediti||4.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.29">4.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mokṣo'pi vaiṣṇavāderyaḥ svasaṅkalpena bhāvitaḥ|<br />
Paraprakṛtisāyujyaṁ yadvāpyānandarūpatā||4.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.30">4.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Viśuddhacittamātraṁ vā dīpavatsantatikṣayaḥ|<br />
Sa savedyāpavedyātmapralayākalatāmayaḥ||4.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.31">4.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Taṁ prāpyāpi ciraṁ kālaṁ tadbhogābhogabhuktataḥ|<br />
Tattattvapralayānte tu tadūrdhvāṁ sṛṣṭimāgataḥ||4.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.32">4.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mantratvameti sambodhādananteśena kalpitāt|<br />
Etaccāgre taniṣyāma ityāstāṁ tāvadatra tat||4.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.33">4.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tenājñajanatākḷptapravādairyo viḍambitaḥ|<br />
Asadgurau rūḍhacitsa māyāpāśena rañjitaḥ||4.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.34">4.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">So'pi sattarkayogena nīyate sadguruṁ prati|<br />
Sattarkaḥ śuddhavidyaiva sā cecchā parameśituḥ||4.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.35">4.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīpūrvaśāstre tenoktaṁ sa yiyāsuḥ śivecchayā|<br />
Bhuktimuktiprasiddhyarthaṁ nīyate sadguruṁ prati||4.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.36">4.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śaktipātastu tatraiṣa kramikaḥ sampravartate|<br />
Sthitvā yo'sadgurau śāstrāntare vā satpathaṁ śritaḥ||4.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.37">4.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Guruśāstragate sattve'sattve cātra vibhedakam|<br />
Śaktipātasya vaicitryaṁ purastātpravivicyate||4.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.38">4.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktaṁ svacchandaśāstre tat vaiṣṇavādyānpravādinaḥ|<br />
Sarvānbhramayate māyā sāmokṣe mokṣalipsayā||4.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.39">4.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yastu rūḍho'pi tatrodyatparāmarśaviśāradaḥ|<br />
Sa śuddhavidyāmāhātmyācchaktipātapavitritaḥ||4.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.40">4.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ārohatyeva sanmārgaṁ pratyūhaparivarjitaḥ|<br />
Sa tāvatkasyacittarkaḥ svata eva pravartate||4.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.41">4.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sa ca sāṁsiddhikaḥ śāstre proktaḥ svapratyayātmakaḥ|<br />
Kiraṇāyāṁ yadapyuktaṁ gurutaḥ śāstrataḥ svataḥ||4.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.42">4.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatrottarottaraṁ mukhyaṁ pūrvapūrva upāyakaḥ|<br />
Yasya svato'yaṁ sattarkaḥ sarvatraivādhikāravān||4.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.43">4.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Abhiṣiktaḥ svasaṁvittidevībhirdīkṣitaśca saḥ|<br />
Sa eva sarvācāryāṇāṁ madhye mukhyaḥ prakīrtitaḥ||4.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.44">4.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatsannidhāne nānyeṣu kalpiteṣvadhikāritā|<br />
Sa samastaṁ ca śāstrārthaṁ sattarkādeva manyate||4.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.45">4.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śuddhavidyā hi tannāsti satyaṁ yadyanna bhāsayet|<br />
Sarvaśāstrārthavettṛtvamakasmāccāsya jāyate||4.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.46">4.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti śrīpūvavākye tadakasmāditi śabdataḥ|<br />
Lokāprasiddho yo hetuḥ so'kasmāditi kathyate||4.46||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.47">4.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sa caiṣa parameśānaśuddhavidyāvijṛmbhitam|<br />
Asya bhedāśca bahavo nirbhittiḥ sahabhittikaḥ||4.47||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.48">4.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sarvago'ṁśagataḥ so'pi mukhyāmukhyāṁśaniṣṭhitaḥ|<br />
Bhittiḥ paropajīvitvaṁ parā prajñātha tatkṛtiḥ||4.48||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.49">4.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Adṛṣṭamaṇḍalo'pyevaṁ yaḥ kaścidvetti tattvataḥ|<br />
Sa siddhibhāgbhavennityaṁ sa yogī sa ca dīkṣitaḥ||4.49||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.50"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.50">4.50</a> <span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ yo vetti tattvena tasya nirvāṇagāminī|<br />
Dīkṣā bhavediti proktaṁ tacchrītriṁśakaśāsane||4.50||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.51"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.51">4.51</a> <span class="pv-iast">Akalpito gururjñeyaḥ sāṁsiddhika iti smṛtaḥ|<br />
Yastu tadrūpabhāgātmabhāvanātaḥ paraṁ vinā||4.51||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.52"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.52">4.52</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śāstravitsa guruḥ śāstre prokto'kalpitakalpakaḥ|<br />
Tasyāpi bhedā utkṛṣṭamadhyamandādyupāyataḥ||4.52||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.53"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.53">4.53</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhāvanāto'tha vā dhyānājjapātsvapnādvratāddhuteḥ|<br />
Prāpnotyakalpitodāramabhiṣekaṁ mahāmatiḥ||4.53||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.54"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.54">4.54</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīmadvājasanīye śrīvīre śrībrahmayāmale|<br />
Śrīsiddhāyāmidaṁ dhātrā proktamanyatra ca sphuṭam||4.54||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.55"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.55">4.55</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasya svecchāpravṛttatvātkāraṇānantateṣyate|<br />
Kadācidbhaktiyogena karmaṇā vidyayāpi vā||4.55||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.56"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.56">4.56</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jñānadharmopadeśena mantrairvā dīkṣayāpi vā|<br />
Evamādyairanekaiśca prakāraiḥ parameśvaraḥ||4.56||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.57"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.57">4.57</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṁsāriṇo'nugṛhṇāti viśvasya jagataḥ patiḥ|<br />
Mātṛmaṇḍalasambodhātsaṁskārāttapasaḥ priye||4.57||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.58"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.58">4.58</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dhyānādyogājjapājjñānānmantrārādhanāto vratāt|<br />
Samprāpyaṁ kulasāmānyaṁ jñānaṁ kaulikasiddhidam||4.58||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.59"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.59">4.59</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tattvajñānātmakaṁ sādhyaṁ yatra yatraiva dṛśyate|<br />
Sa eva hi gurustatra hetujālaṁ prakalpyatām||4.59||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.60"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.60">4.60</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tattvajñānādṛte nānyallakṣaṇaṁ brahmayāmale|<br />
Tatraiva coktaṁ sevāyāṁ kṛtāyāmavikalpataḥ||4.60||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.61"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.61">4.61</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sādhakasya na cetsiddhiḥ kiṁ kāryamiti codite|<br />
Ātmīyamasya sañjñānakrameṇa svātmadīkṣaṇam||4.61||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.62"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.62">4.62</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sasphuratvaprasiddhyarthaṁ tataḥ sādhyaṁ prasidhyati|<br />
Anena svātmavijñānaṁ sasphuratvaprasādhakam||4.62||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.63"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.63">4.63</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktaṁ mukhyatayācāryo bhavedyadi na sasphuraḥ|<br />
Tatraiva ca punaḥ śrīmadraktārādhanakarmaṇi||4.63||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.64"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.64">4.64</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vidhiṁ proktaṁ sadā kurvanmāsenācārya ucyate|<br />
Pakṣeṇa sādhako'rdhārdhātputrakaḥ samayī tathā||4.64||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.65"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.65">4.65</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dīkṣayejjapayogena raktādevī kramādyataḥ|<br />
Guroralābhe proktasya vidhimetaṁ samācaret||4.65||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.66"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.66">4.66</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mate ca pustakādvidyādhyayane doṣa īdṛśaḥ|<br />
Ukto yastena taddoṣābhāve'sau na niṣiddhatā||4.66||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.67"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.67">4.67</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mantradravyādiguptatve phalaṁ kimiti codite|<br />
Pustakādhītavidyā ye dīkṣāsamayavarjitāḥ||4.67||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.68"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.68">4.68</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tāmasāḥ parahiṁsādi vaśyādi ca carantyalam|<br />
Na ca tattvaṁ vidustena doṣabhāja iti sphuṭam||4.68||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.216"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.216">4.216</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kṣetrādisampraveśaśca samayādiprapālanam|<br />
Parasvarūpaliṅgādi nāmagotrādikaṁ ca yat||4.216||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.217"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.217">4.217</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nāsminvidhīyate kiñcinna cāpi pratiṣidhyate|<br />
Vihitaṁ sarvamevātra pratiṣiddhamathāpi ca||4.217||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.218"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.218">4.218</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kiṁ tvetadatra deveśi niyamena vidhīyate|<br />
Tattve cetaḥ sthirīkāryaṁ suprasannena yoginā||4.218||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.219"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.219">4.219</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tacca yasya yathaiva syātsa tathaiva samācaret|<br />
Tattve niścalacittastu bhuñjāno viṣayānapi||4.219||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.220"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.220">4.220</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na saṁspṛśyeta doṣaiḥ sa padmapatramivāmbhasā|<br />
Viṣāpahārimantrādisannaddho bhakṣayannapi||4.220||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.221"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.221">4.221</a> <span class="pv-iast">Viṣaṁ na muhyate tena tadvadyogī mahāmatiḥ|<br />
Aśuddhaṁ hi kathaṁ nāma dehādyaṁ pāñcabhautikam||4.221||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.222"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.222">4.222</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prakāśatātirikte kiṁ śuddhyaśuddhī hi vastunaḥ|<br />
Aśuddhasya ca bhāvasya śuddhiḥ syāttādṛśaiva kim||4.222||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.223"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.223">4.223</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anyonyāśrayavaiyarthyānavasthā itthamatra hi|<br />
Pṛthivī jalataḥ śuddhyejjalaṁ dharaṇitastathā||4.223||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.224"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.224">4.224</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anyonyāśrayatā seyamaśuddhatve'pyayaṁ kramaḥ|<br />
Aśuddhājjalataḥ śuddhyeddhareti vyarthatā bhavet||4.224||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.225"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.225">4.225</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vāyuto vāriṇo vāyostejasastasya vānyataḥ|<br />
Bahurūpādikā mantrāḥ pāvanātteṣu śuddhatā||4.225||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.226"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.226">4.226</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mantrāḥ svabhāvataḥ śuddhā yadi te'pi na kiṁ tathā|<br />
Śivātmatā teṣu śuddhiryadi tatrāpi sā na kim||4.226||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.227"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.227">4.227</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śivātmatvāparijñānaṁ na mantreṣu dharādivat|<br />
Te tena śuddhā iti cettajjñaptistarhi śuddhatā||4.227||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.228"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.228">4.228</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yoginaṁ prati sā cāsti bhāveṣviti viśuddhatā|<br />
Nanu codanayā śuddhyaśuddhyādikaviniścayaḥ||4.228||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.229"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.229">4.229</a> <span class="pv-iast">Itthamastu tathāpyeṣā codanaiva śivoditā|<br />
Kā syātsatīti cedetadanyatra pravitānitam||4.229||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.230"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.230">4.230</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vaidikyā bādhiteyaṁ cedviparītaṁ na kiṁ bhavet|<br />
Samyakcenmanyase bādho viśiṣṭaviṣayatvataḥ||4.230||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.231"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.231">4.231</a> <span class="pv-iast">Apavādena kartavyaḥ sāmānyavihite vidhau|<br />
Śuddhyaśuddhī ca sāmānyavihite tattvabodhini||4.231||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.232"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.232">4.232</a> <span class="pv-iast">Puṁsi te bādhite eva tathā cātreti varṇitam|<br />
Nārthavādādiśaṅkā ca vākye māheśvare bhavet||4.232||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.233"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.233">4.233</a> <span class="pv-iast">Abuddhipūrvaṁ hi tathā saṁsthite satataṁ bhavet|<br />
Vyomādirūpe nigame śaṅkā mithyārthatāṁ prati||4.233||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.234"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.234">4.234</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anavacchinnavijñānavaiśvarūpyasunirbharaḥ|<br />
Śāstrātmanā sthito devo mithyātvaṁ kvāpi nārhati||4.234||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.235"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.235">4.235</a> <span class="pv-iast">Icchāvānbhāvarūpeṇa yathā tiṣṭhāsurīśvaraḥ|<br />
Tatsvarūpābhidhānena tiṣṭhāsuḥ sa tathā sthitaḥ||4.235||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.236"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.236">4.236</a> <span class="pv-iast">Arthavādo'pi yatrānyavidhyādimukhamīkṣate|<br />
Tatrāstvasatyaḥ svātantrye sa eva tu vidhāyakaḥ||4.236||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.237"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.237">4.237</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vidhivākyāntare gacchannaṅgabhāvamathāpi vā|<br />
Na nirarthaka evāyaṁ sannidhergajaḍādivat||4.237||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.238"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.238">4.238</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svārthapratyāyanaṁ cāsya svasaṁvittyaiva bhāsate|<br />
Tadapahnavanaṁ kartuṁ śakyaṁ vidhiniṣedhayoḥ||4.238||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.239"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.239">4.239</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yuktiścātrāsti vākyeṣu svasaṁviccāpyabādhitā|<br />
Yā samagrārthamāṇikyatattvaniścayakāriṇī||4.239||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.240"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.240">4.240</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mṛtadehe'tha dehotthe yā cāśuddhiḥ prakīrtitā|<br />
Anyatra neti buddhyantāmaśuddhaṁ saṁvidaścyutam||4.240||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.241"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.241">4.241</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṁvittādātmyamāpannaṁ sarvaṁ śuddhamataḥ sthitam|<br />
Śrīmadvīrāvalau coktaṁ śuddhyaśuddhinirūpaṇe||4.241||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.242"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.242">4.242</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sarveṣāṁ vāhako jīvo nāsti kiñcidajīvakam|<br />
Yatkiñcijjīvarahitamaśuddhaṁ tadvijānata||4.242||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.243"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.243">4.243</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasmādyatsaṁvido nātidūre tacchuddhimāvahet|<br />
Avikalpena bhāvena munayo'pi tathābhavan||4.243||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.244"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.244">4.244</a> <span class="pv-iast">Lokasaṁrakṣaṇārthaṁ tu tattattvaṁ taiḥ pragopitam|<br />
Bahiḥ satsvapi bhāveṣu śuddhyaśuddhī na nīlavat||4.244||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.245"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.245">4.245</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pramātṛdharma evāyaṁ cidaikyānaikyavedanāt|<br />
Yadi vā vastudharmo'pi mātrapekṣānibandhanaḥ||4.245||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.246"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.246">4.246</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sautrāmaṇyāṁ surā hotuḥ śuddhānyasya viparyayaḥ|<br />
Anena codanānāṁ ca svavākyairapi bādhanam||4.246||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.247"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.247">4.247</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kvacitsandarśitaṁ brahmahatyāvidhiniṣedhavat|<br />
Bhakṣyādividhayo'pyenaṁ nyāyamāśritya carcitāḥ||4.247||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.248"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.248">4.248</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sarvajñānottarādau ca bhāṣate sma maheśvaraḥ|<br />
Nararṣidevadruhiṇaviṣṇurudrādyudīritam||4.248||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.249"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.249">4.249</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uttarottaravaiśiṣṭyāt pūrvapūrvaprabādhakam|<br />
Na śaivaṁ vaiṣṇavairvākyairbādhanīyaṁ kadācana||4.249||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.250"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.250">4.250</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vaiṣṇavaṁ brahmasambhūtairnetyādi paricarcayet|<br />
Bādhate yo vaiparītyātsa mūḍhaḥ pāpabhāgbhavet||4.250||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.251"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.251">4.251</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasmānmukhyatayā skanda lokadharmānna cācaret|<br />
Nānyaśāstrasamuddiṣṭaṁ srotasyuktaṁ nije caret||4.251||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.252"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.252">4.252</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yato yadyapi devena vedādyapi nirūpitam|<br />
Tathāpi kila saṅkocabhāvābhāvavikalpataḥ||4.252||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.253"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.253">4.253</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṅkocatāratamyena pāśavaṁ jñānamīritam|<br />
Vikāsatāratamyena patijñānaṁ tu bādhakam||4.253||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.254"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.254">4.254</a> <span class="pv-iast">Idaṁ dvaitamidaṁ neti parasparaniṣedhataḥ|<br />
Māyīyabhedakḷptaṁ tatsyādakālpanike katham||4.254||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.255"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.255">4.255</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktaṁ bhargaśikhāyāṁ ca mṛtyukālakalādikam|<br />
Dvaitādvaitavikalpotthaṁ grasate kṛtadhīriti||4.255||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.256"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.256">4.256</a> <span class="pv-iast">Siddhānte liṅgapūjoktā viśvādhvamayatāvide|<br />
Kulādiṣu niṣiddhāsau dehe viśvātmatāvide||4.256||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.257"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.257">4.257</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iha sarvātmake kasmāttadvidhipratiṣedhane|<br />
Niyamānupraveśena tādātmyapratipattaye||4.257||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.258"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.258">4.258</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jaṭādi kaule tyāgo'sya sukhopāyopadeśataḥ|<br />
Vratacaryā ca mantrārthatādātmyapratipattaye||4.258||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.259"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.259">4.259</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tanniṣedhastu mantrārthasārvātmyapratipattaye|<br />
Kṣetrapīṭhopapīṭheṣu praveśo vighnaśāntaye||4.259||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.260"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.260">4.260</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mantrādyārādhakasyātha tallābhāyopadiśyate|<br />
Kṣetrādigamanābhāvavidhistu svātmanastathā||4.260||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.261"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.261">4.261</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vaiśvarūpyeṇa pūrṇatvaṁ jñātumityapi varṇitam|<br />
Samayācārasadbhāvaḥ pālyatvenopadiśyate||4.261||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.262"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.262">4.262</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhedaprāṇatayā tattattyāgāttattvaviśuddhaye|<br />
Samayādiniṣedhastu mataśāstreṣu kathyate||4.262||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.263"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.263">4.263</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nirmaryādaṁ svasambodhaṁ sampūrṇaṁ buddhyatāmiti|<br />
Parakīyamidaṁ rūpaṁ dhyeyametattu me nijam||4.263||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.264"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.264">4.264</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jvālādiliṅgaṁ cānyasya kapālādi tu me nijam|<br />
Ādiśabdāttapaścaryāvelātithyādi kathyate||4.264||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.265"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.265">4.265</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nāma śaktiśivādyantametasya mama nānyathā|<br />
Gotraṁ ca gurusantāno maṭhikākulaśabditaḥ||4.265||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.266"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.266">4.266</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīsantatistryambakākhyā tadardhāmardasañjñitā|<br />
Itthamardhacatasro'tra maṭhikāḥ śāṅkare krame||4.266||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.267"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.267">4.267</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yugakrameṇa kūrmādyā mīnāntā siddhasantatiḥ|<br />
Ādiśabdena ca gharaṁ pallī pīṭhopapīṭhakam||4.267||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.268"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.268">4.268</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mudrā chummeti teṣāṁ ca vidhānaṁ svaparasthitam|<br />
Tādātmyapratipattyai hi svaṁ santānaṁ samāśrayet||4.268||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.269"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.269">4.269</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhuñjīta pūjayeccakraṁ parasantāninā nahi|<br />
Etacca mataśāstreṣu niṣiddhaṁ khaṇḍanā yataḥ||4.269||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.270"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.270">4.270</a> <span class="pv-iast">Akhaṇḍe'pi pare tattve bhedenānena jāyate|<br />
Evaṁ kṣetrapraveśādi santānaniyamāntataḥ||4.270||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.271"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.271">4.271</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nāsminvidhīyate taddhi sākṣānnaupayikaṁ śive|<br />
Na tasya ca niṣodho yanna tattattvasya khaṇḍanam||4.271||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.272"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.272">4.272</a> <span class="pv-iast">Viśvātmano hi nāthasya svasminrūpe vikalpitau|<br />
Vidhirniṣedho vā śaktau na svarūpasya khaṇḍane||4.272||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.273"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.273">4.273</a> <span class="pv-iast">Paratattvapraveśe tu yameva nikaṭaṁ yadā|<br />
Upāyaṁ vetti sa grāhyastadā tyājyo'tha vā kvacit||4.273||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.274"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.274">4.274</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na yantraṇātra kāryeti proktaṁ śrītrikaśāsane|<br />
Samatā sarvadevānāmovallīmantravarṇayoḥ||4.274||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.275"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.275">4.275</a> <span class="pv-iast">Āgamanāṁ gatīnāṁ ca sarvaṁ śivamayaṁ yataḥ|<br />
Sa hyakhaṇḍitasadbhāvaṁ śivatattvaṁ prapaśyati||4.275||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.276"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.276">4.276</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yo hyakhaṇḍitasadbhāvamātmatattvaṁ prapadyate|<br />
Ketakīkusumasaurabhe bhṛśaṁ bhṛṅga eva rasiko na makṣikā|<br />
Bhairavīyaparamādvayārcane ko'pi rajyati maheśacoditaḥ||4.276||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.277"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.277">4.277</a> <span class="pv-iast">Asmiṁśca yāge viśrāntiṁ kurvatāṁ bhavaḍambaraḥ|<br />
Himānīva mahāgrīṣme svayameva vilīyate||4.277||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.278"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.278">4.278</a> <span class="pv-iast">Alaṁ vātiprasaṅgena bhūyasātiprapañcite|<br />
Yogyo'bhinavagupto'sminko'pi yāgavidhau budhaḥ||4.278||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v4.279"><a class="ta-num" href="#v4.279">4.279</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ityanuttarapadapravikāse śāktamaupayikamadya viviktam||4.279||</span></p>
