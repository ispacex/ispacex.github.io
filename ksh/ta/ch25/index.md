# Глава 25 — Śrāddhakḷptiḥ

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">29 строф · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [английский перевод у источника — русского там нет](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/en/node/893)</p>

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Глава 24 — Antyeṣṭiḥ](/ksh/ta/ch24/) · [Глава 26 — Śeṣavṛttinirūpaṇam →](/ksh/ta/ch26/)</p>

<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloke pañcaviṁśatitamamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.1">25.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha śrāddhavidhiḥ śrīmatṣaḍardhokto nigadyate||25.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.2">25.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Siddhātantre sūcito'sau mūrtiyāganirūpaṇe|<br />
Antyeṣṭyā suviśuddhānāmaśuddhānāṁ ca tadvidhiḥ||25.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.3">25.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tryahe turye'hni daśame māsi māsyādyavatsare|<br />
Varṣe varṣe sarvakālaṁ kāryastatsvaiḥ sa pūrvavat||25.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.4">25.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatra prāgvadyajeddevaṁ homayedanale tathā|<br />
Tato naivedyameva prāggṛhītvā hastagocare||25.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.5">25.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Gururannamayīṁ śaktiṁ vṛṁhikāṁ vīryarūpiṇīm|<br />
Dhyātvā tayā samāviṣṭaṁ taṁ sādhyaṁ cintayetsudhīḥ||25.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.6">25.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tato'sya yaḥ pāśavo'ṁśo bhogyarūpastamarpayet|<br />
Bhoktaryekātmabhāvena śiṣya itthaṁ śivībhavet||25.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.7">25.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhogyatānyā tanurdeha iti pāśātmakā matāḥ|<br />
Śrāddhe mṛtoddhṛtāvantayāge teṣāṁ śivīkṛtiḥ||25.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.8">25.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ekenaiva vidhānena yadyapi syātkṛtārthatā|<br />
Tathāpi tanmayībhāvasiddhyai sarvaṁ vidhiṁ caret||25.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.9">25.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bubhukṣostu kriyābhyāsabhūmānau phalabhūmani|<br />
Hetū tato mṛtoddhāraśrāddhādyasmai samācaret||25.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.10">25.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tattvajñānārkavidhvastadhvāntasya tu na ko'pyayam|<br />
Antyeṣṭiśrāddhavidhyādirupayogī kadācana||25.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.11">25.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Teṣāṁ tu guru tadvargavargyasabrahmacāriṇām|<br />
Tatsantānajuṣāmaikyadinaṁ parvadinaṁ bhavet||25.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.12">25.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yadā hi bodhasyodrekastadā parvāha pūraṇāt|<br />
Janmaikyadivasau tena parvaṇī bodhasiddhitaḥ||25.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.13">25.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Putrako'pi yadā kasmaicana syādupakārakaḥ|<br />
Tadā mātuḥ pituḥ śaktervāmadakṣāntarālagāḥ||25.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.14">25.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nāḍīḥ pravāhayeddevāyārpayeta niveditam|<br />
Śrīmadbharuṇatantre ca tacchivena nirūpitam||25.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.15">25.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadvāhakālāpekṣā ca kāryā tadrūpasiddhaye|<br />
Svācchandyenātha tatsiddhiṁ vidhinā bhāvinā caret||25.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.16">25.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yasya kasyāpi vā śrāddhe gurudevāgnitarpaṇam|<br />
Sacakreṣṭirbhavecchrauto na tu syātpāśavo vidhiḥ||25.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.17">25.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīmaukuṭe tathā coktaṁ śivaśāstre sthito'pi yaḥ|<br />
Pratyeti vaidike bhagnaghaṇṭāvanna sa kiñcana||25.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.18">25.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathoktadevapūjādicakrayāgāntakarmaṇā|<br />
Rudratvametyasau janturbhogāndivyānsamaśnute||25.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.19">25.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha vacmaḥ sphuṭaṁ śrīmatsiddhāyāṁ nāḍicāraṇam|<br />
Yā vāhayitumiṣyeta nāḍī tāmeva bhāvayet||25.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.20">25.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhāvanātanmayībhāve sā nāḍī vahati sphuṭam|<br />
Yadvā vāhayituṁ yeṣṭā tadaṅgaṁ tena pāṇinā||25.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.21">25.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Āpīḍya kukṣiṁ namayetsā vahennāḍikā kṣaṇāt|<br />
Evaṁ śrāddhamukhenāpi bhogamokṣobhayasthitim||25.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.22">25.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kuryāditi śivenoktaṁ tatra tatra kṛpālunā|<br />
Śaktipātodaye jantoryenopāyena daiśikaḥ||25.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.23">25.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Karotyuddharaṇaṁ tattannirvāṇāyāsya kalpate|<br />
Uddhartā devadevo hi sa cācintyaprabhāvakaḥ||25.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.24">25.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Upāyaṁ gurudīkṣādi dvāramātreṇa saṁśrayet|<br />
Uktaṁ śrīmanmataṅgākhye munipraśnādanantaram||25.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.25">25.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Muktirvivekāttattvānāṁ dīkṣāto yogato yadi|<br />
Caryāmātrātkathaṁ sā syādityataḥ samamuttaram||25.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.26">25.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prahasyoce vibhuḥ kasmādbhrāntiste parameśituḥ|<br />
Sarvānugrāhakatvaṁ hi saṁsiddhaṁ dṛśyatāṁ kila||25.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.27">25.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prāptamṛtyorviṣavyādhiśastrādi kila kāraṇam|<br />
Alpaṁ vā bahu vā tadvadanudhyā muktikāraṇam||25.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.28">25.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Muktyarthamupacaryante bāhyaliṅgānyamūni tu|<br />
Iti jñātvā na sandeha itthaṁ kāryo vipaścitā||25.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v25.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v25.29">25.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iyataiva kathaṁ muktiriti bhaktiṁ parāṁ śrayet|<br />
Uktaḥ śrāddhavidhirbhrāntigarātaṅkavimardanaḥ||25.29||</span></p>
