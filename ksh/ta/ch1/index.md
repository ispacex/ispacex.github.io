<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloke prathamamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.1">1.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vimalakalāśrayābhinavasṛṣṭimahā jananī bharitatanuśca pañcamukhaguptarucirjanakaḥ|<br />
Tadubhayayāmalasphuritabhāvavisargamayaṁ hṛdayamanuttarāmṛtakulaṁ mama saṁsphuratāt||1.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.2">1.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Naumi citpratibhāṁ devīṁ parāṁ bhairavayoginīm|<br />
Mātṛmānaprameyāṁśaśūlāmbujakṛtāspadāṁ||1.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.3">1.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Naumi devīṁ śarīrasthāṁ nṛtyato bhairavākṛteḥ|<br />
Prāvṛṇmeghaghanavyomavidyullekhāvilāsinīm||1.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.4">1.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dīptajyotiśchaṭāpluṣṭabhedabandhatrayaṁ sphurat|<br />
Stājjñānaśūlaṁ satpakṣavipakṣotkartanakṣamam||1.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.5">1.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svātantryaśaktiḥ kramasaṁsisṛkṣā kramātmatā ceti vibhorvibhūtiḥ|<br />
Tadeva devītrayamantarāstāmanuttaraṁ me prathayatsvarūpam||1.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.6">1.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Taddevatāvibhavabhāvimahāmarīcicakreśvarāyitanijasthitireka eva|<br />
Devīsuto gaṇapatiḥ sphuradindukāntiḥ samyaksamucchalayatānmama saṁvidabdhim||1.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.7">1.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Rāgāruṇaṁ granthibilāvakīrṇaṁ yo jālamātānavitānavṛtti|<br />
Kalombhitaṁ bāhyapathe cakāra stānme sa macchandavibhuḥ prasannaḥ||1.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.8">1.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Traiyambakābhihitasantatitāmraparṇīsanmauktikaprakarakāntiviśeṣabhājaḥ|<br />
Pūrve jayanti guravo guruśāstrasindhukallolakelikalanāmalakarṇadhārāḥ||1.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.9">1.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jayati gurureka eva śrīśrīkaṇṭho bhuvi prathitaḥ|<br />
Tadaparamūrtirbhagavānmaheśvaro bhūtirājaśca||1.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.10">1.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīsomānandabodhaśrīmadutpalaviniḥsṛtāḥ|<br />
Jayanti saṁvidāmodasandarbhā dikprasarpiṇaḥ||1.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.11">1.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadāsvādabharāveśabṛṁhitāṁ matiṣaṭpadīm|<br />
Gurorlakṣmaṇaguptasya nādasammohinīṁ numaḥ||1.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.12">1.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yaḥ pūrṇānandaviśrāntasarvaśāstrārthapāragaḥ|<br />
Sa śrīcukhulako diśyādiṣṭaṁ me gururuttamaḥ||1.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.13">1.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jayatājjagaduddhṛtikṣamo'sau bhagavatyā saha śambhunātha ekaḥ|<br />
Yadudīritaśāsanāṁśubhirme prakaṭo'yaṁ gahano'pi śāstramārgaḥ||1.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.14">1.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Santi paddhatayaścitrāḥ srotobhedeṣu bhūyasā|<br />
Anuttaraṣaḍardhārthakrame tvekāpi nekṣyate||1.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.15">1.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ityahaṁ bahuśaḥ sadbhiḥ śiṣyasabrahmacāribhiḥ|<br />
Arthito racaye spaṣṭāṁ pūrṇārthāṁ prakriyāmimām||1.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.16">1.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrībhaṭṭanāthacaraṇābjayugāttathā śrībhaṭṭārikāṅghriyugalādgurusantatiryā|<br />
Bodhānyapāśaviṣanuttadupāsanotthabodhojjvalo'bhinavagupta idaṁ karoti||1.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.17">1.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na tadastīha yanna śrīmālinīvijayottare|<br />
Devadevena nirdiṣṭaṁ svaśabdenātha liṅgataḥ||1.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.18">1.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Daśāṣṭādaśavasvaṣṭabhinnaṁ yacchāsanaṁ vibhoḥ|<br />
Tatsāraṁ trikaśāstraṁ hi tatsāraṁ mālinīmatam||1.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.19">1.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ato'trāntargataṁ sarvaṁ sampradāyojjhitairbudhaiḥ|<br />
Adṛṣṭaṁ prakaṭīkurmo gurunāthājñayā vayam||1.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.20">1.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Abhinavaguptasya kṛtiḥ seyaṁ yasyoditā gurubhirākhyā|<br />
Trinayanacaraṇasaroruhacintanalabdhaprasiddhiriti||1.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.21">1.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīśambhunāthabhāskaracaraṇanipātaprabhāpagatasaṅkocam|<br />
Abhinavaguptahṛdambujametadvicinuta maheśapūjanahetoḥ||1.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.22">1.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iha tāvatsamasteṣu śāstreṣu parigīyate|<br />
Ajñānaṁ saṁsṛterheturjñānaṁ mokṣaikakāraṇam||1.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.23">1.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Malamajñānamicchanti saṁsārāṅkurakāraṇam|<br />
Iti proktaṁ tathā ca śrīmālinīvijayottare||1.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.24">1.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Viśeṣaṇena buddhisthe saṁsārottarakālike|<br />
Sambhāvanāṁ nirasyaitadabhāve mokṣamabravīt||1.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.25">1.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ajñānamiti na jñānābhāvaścātiprasaṅgataḥ|<br />
Sa hi loṣṭādike'pyasti na ca tasyāsti saṁsṛtiḥ||1.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.26">1.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ato jñeyasya tattvasya sāmastyenāprathātmakam|<br />
Jñānameva tadajñānaṁ śivasūtreṣu bhāṣitam||1.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.27">1.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Caitanyamātmā jñānaṁ ca bandha ityatra sūtrayoḥ|<br />
Saṁśleṣetarayogābhyāmayamarthaḥ pradarśitaḥ||1.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.28">1.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Caitanyamiti bhāvāntaḥ śabdaḥ svātantryamātrakam|<br />
Anākṣiptaviśeṣaṁ sadāha sūtre purātane||1.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.29">1.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dvitīyena tu sūtreṇa kriyāṁ vā karaṇaṁ ca vā|<br />
Bruvatā tasya cinmātrarūpasya dvaitamucyate||1.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.30">1.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dvaitaprathā tadajñānaṁ tucchatvādbandha ucyate|<br />
Tata eva samucchedyamityāvṛttyā nirūpitam||1.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.31">1.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svatantrātmātiriktastu tuccho'tuccho'pi kaścana|<br />
Na mokṣo nāma tannāsya pṛthaṅnāmāpi gṛhyate||1.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.32">1.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yattu jñeyasatattvasya pūrṇapūrṇaprathātmakam|<br />
Taduttarottaraṁ jñānaṁ tattatsaṁsāraśāntidam||1.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.33">1.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Rāgādyakaluṣo'smyantaḥśūnyo'haṁ kartṛtojjhitaḥ|<br />
Itthaṁ samāsavyāsābhyāṁ jñānaṁ muñcati tāvataḥ||1.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.34">1.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasmānmukto'pyavacchedādavacchedāntarasthiteḥ|<br />
Amukta eva muktastu sarvāvacchedavarjitaḥ||1.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.35">1.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yattu jñeyasatattvasya jñānaṁ sarvātmanojjhitam|<br />
Avacchedairna tatkutrāpyajñānaṁ satyamuktidam||1.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.36">1.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jñānājñānasvarūpaṁ yaduktaṁ pratyekamapyadaḥ|<br />
Dvidhā pauruṣabauddhatvabhidoktaṁ śivaśāsane||1.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.37">1.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatra puṁso yadajñānaṁ malākhyaṁ tajjamapyatha|<br />
Svapūrṇacitkriyārūpaśivatāvaraṇātmakam||1.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.38">1.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṅkocidṛkkriyārūpaṁ tatpaśoravikalpitam|<br />
Tadajñānaṁ na buddhyaṁśo'dhyavasāyādyabhāvataḥ||1.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.39">1.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ahamitthamidaṁ vedmītyevamadhyavasāyinī|<br />
Ṣaṭkañcukābilāṇūtthapratibimbanato yadā||1.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.40">1.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dhīrjāyate tadā tādṛgjñānamajñānaśabditam|<br />
Bauddhaṁ tasya ca tatpauṁsnaṁ poṣaṇīyaṁ ca poṣṭṛ ca||1.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.41">1.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kṣīṇe tu paśusaṁskāre puṁsaḥ prāptaparasthiteḥ|<br />
Vikasvaraṁ tadvijñānaṁ pauruṣaṁ nirvikalpakam||1.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.42">1.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vikasvarāvikalpātmajñānaucityena yāvatā|<br />
Tadbauddhaṁ yasya tatpauṁsnaṁ prāgvatpoṣyaṁ ca poṣṭṛ ca||1.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.43">1.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatra dīkṣādinā pauṁsnamajñānaṁ dhvaṁsi yadyapi|<br />
Tathāpi taccharīrānte tajjñānaṁ vyajyate sphuṭam||1.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.44">1.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bauddhajñānena tu yadā bauddhamajñānajṛmbhitam|<br />
Vilīyate tadā jīvanmuktiḥ karatale sthitā||1.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.45">1.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dīkṣāpi bauddhavijñānapūrvā satyaṁ vimocikā|<br />
Tena tatrāpi bauddhasya jñānasyāsti pradhānatā||1.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.46">1.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jñānājñānagataṁ caitaddvitvaṁ svāyambhuve rurau|<br />
Mataṅgādau kṛtaṁ śrīmatkheṭapālādidaiśikaiḥ||1.46||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.47">1.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathāvidhāvasāyātmabauddhavijñānasampade|<br />
Śāstrameva pradhānaṁ yajjñeyatattvapradarśakam||1.47||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.48">1.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dīkṣayā galite'pyantarajñāne pauruṣātmani|<br />
Dhīgatasyānivṛttatvādvikalpo'pi hi sambhavet||1.48||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.49">1.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dehasadbhāvaparyantamātmabhāvo yato dhiyi|<br />
Dehānte'pi na mokṣaḥ syātpauruṣājñānahānitaḥ||1.49||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.50"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.50">1.50</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bauddhājñānanivṛttau tu vikalponmūlanāddhruvam|<br />
Tadaiva mokṣa ityuktaṁ dhātrā śrīmanniśāṭane||1.50||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.51"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.51">1.51</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vikalpayuktacittastu piṇḍapātācchivaṁ vrajet|<br />
Itarastu tadaiveti śāstrasyātra pradhānataḥ||1.51||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.52"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.52">1.52</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jñeyasya hi paraṁ tattvaṁ yaḥ prakāśātmakaḥ śivaḥ|<br />
Nahyaprakāśarūpasya prākāśyaṁ vastutāpi vā||1.52||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.53"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.53">1.53</a> <span class="pv-iast">Avastutāpi bhāvānāṁ camatkāraikagocarā|<br />
Yatkuḍyasadṛśī neyaṁ dhīravastvetadityapi||1.53||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.54"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.54">1.54</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prakāśo nāma yaścāyaṁ sarvatraiva prakāśate|<br />
Anapahnavanīyatvāt kiṁ tasminmānakalpanaiḥ||1.54||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.55"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.55">1.55</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pramāṇānyapi vastūnāṁ jīvitaṁ yāni tanvate|<br />
Teṣāmapi paro jīvaḥ sa eva parameśvaraḥ||1.55||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.56"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.56">1.56</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sarvāpahnavahevākadharmāpyevaṁ hi vartate|<br />
Jñānamātmārthamityetanneti māṁ prati bhāsate||1.56||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.57"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.57">1.57</a> <span class="pv-iast">Apahnutau sādhane vā vastūnāṁādyamīdṛśam|<br />
Yattatra ke pramāṇānāmupapattyupayogite||1.57||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.58"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.58">1.58</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kāmike tata evoktaṁ hetuvādavivarjitam|<br />
Tasya devātidevasya parāpekṣā na vidyate||1.58||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.59"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.59">1.59</a> <span class="pv-iast">Parasya tadapekṣatvātsvatantro'yamataḥ sthitaḥ|<br />
Anapekṣasya vaśino deśakālākṛtikramāḥ||1.59||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.60"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.60">1.60</a> <span class="pv-iast">Niyatā neti sa vibhurnityo viśvākṛitiḥ śivaḥ|<br />
Vibhutvātsarvago nityabhāvādādyantavarjitaḥ||1.60||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.61"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.61">1.61</a> <span class="pv-iast">Viśvākṛtitvāccidacittadvaicitryāvabhāsakaḥ|<br />
Tato'sya bahurūpatvamuktaṁ dīkṣottarādike||1.61||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.62"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.62">1.62</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhuvanaṁ vigraho jyotiḥ khaṁ śabdo mantra eva ca|<br />
Bindunādādisambhinnaḥ ṣaḍvidhaḥ śiva ucyate||1.62||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.63"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.63">1.63</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yo yadātmakatāniṣṭhastadbhāvaṁ sa prapadyate|<br />
Vyomādiśabdavijñānātparo mokṣo na saṁśayaḥ||1.63||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.64"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.64">1.64</a> <span class="pv-iast">Viśvākṛtitve devasya tadetaccopalakṣaṇam|<br />
Anavacchinnatārūḍhāvavacchedalaye'sya ca||1.64||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.65"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.65">1.65</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktaṁ ca kāmike devaḥ sarvākṛtirnirākṛtiḥ|<br />
Jaladarpaṇavattena sarvaṁ vyāptaṁ carācaram||1.65||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.66"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.66">1.66</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na cāsya vibhutādyo'yaṁ dharmo'nyonyaṁ vibhidyate|<br />
Eka evāsya dharmo'sau sarvākṣepeṇa vartate||1.66||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v1.67"><a class="ta-num" href="#v1.67">1.67</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tena svātantryaśaktyaiva yukta ityāñjaso vidhiḥ|<br />
