# Глава 10 — Tattvabhedanam

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">309 строф · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [перевод главы у источника](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/ru/node/871)</p>

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Глава 9 — Tattvādhvā](/ksh/ta/ch9/) · [Глава 11 — Kalādyadhvā →](/ksh/ta/ch11/)</p>

<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloke daśamamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.1">10.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ucyate trikaśāstraikarahasyaṁ tattvabhedanam||10.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.2">10.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Eṣāmamīṣāṁ tattvānāṁ svavargeṣvanugāminām|<br />
Bhedāntaramapi proktaṁ śāstre'tra śrītrikābhidhe||10.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.3">10.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śaktimacchaktibhedena dharādyaṁ mūlapaścimam|<br />
Bhidyate pañcadaśadhā svarūpeṇa sahānarāt||10.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.4">10.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kalāntaṁ bhedayugghīnaṁ rudravatpralayākalaḥ|<br />
Tadvanmāyā ca navadhā jñākalāḥ saptadhā punaḥ||10.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.5">10.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mantrāstadīśāḥ pāñcadhye mantreśapatayastridhā|<br />
Śivo na bhidyate svaikaprakāśaghanacinmayaḥ||10.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.6">10.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śivo mantramaheśeśamantrā akalayukkalī|<br />
Śaktimantaḥ sapta tathā śaktayastaccaturdaśa||10.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.7">10.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svaṁ svarūpaṁ pañcadaśaṁ tadbhūḥ pañcadaśātmikā|<br />
Tathāhi tisro devasya śaktayo varṇitāḥ purā||10.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.8">10.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tā eva mātṛmāmeyatrairūpyeṇa vyavasthitāḥ|<br />
Parāṁśo mātṛrūpo'tra pramāṇāṁśaḥ parāparaḥ||10.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.9">10.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Meyo'paraḥ śaktimāṁśca śaktiḥ svaṁ rūpamityadaḥ|<br />
Tatra svarūpaṁ bhūmeryatpṛthagjaḍamavasthitam||10.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.10">10.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mātṛmānādyupādhibhirasañjātoparāgakam|<br />
Sakalādiśivāntaistu mātṛbhirvedyatāsya yā||10.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.11">10.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śaktimadbhiranudbhūtaśaktibhiḥ sapta tadbhidaḥ|<br />
Sakalādiśivāntānāṁ śaktiṣūdrecitātmasu||10.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.12">10.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vedyatājanitāḥ sapta bhedā iti caturdaśa|<br />
Sakalasya pramāṇāṁśo yo'sau vidyākalātmakaḥ||10.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.13">10.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sāmānyātmā sa śaktitve gaṇito natu tadbhidaḥ|<br />
Layākalasya mānāṁśaḥ sa eva paramasphuṭaḥ||10.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.14">10.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jñānākalasya mānaṁ tu galadvidyākalāvṛti|<br />
Aśuddhavidyākalanādhvaṁsasaṁskārasaṅgatā||10.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.15">10.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prabubhutsuḥ śuddhavidyā mantrāṇāṁ karaṇaṁ bhavet|<br />
Prabuddhā śuddhavidyā tu tatsaṁskāreṇa saṅgatā||10.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.16">10.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mānaṁ mantreśvarāṇāṁ syāttatsaṁskāravivarjitā|<br />
Mānaṁ mantramaheśānāṁ karaṇaṁ śaktirucyate||10.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.17">10.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svātantryamātrasadbhāvā yā tvicchāśaktiraiśvarī|<br />
Śivasya saiva karaṇaṁ tayā vetti karoti ca||10.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.18">10.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ā śivātsakalāntaṁ ye mātāraḥ sapta te dvidhā|<br />
Nyagbhūtodriktaśaktitvāttadbhedo vedyabhedakaḥ||10.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.19">10.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathāhi vedyatā nāma bhāvasyaiva nijaṁ vapuḥ|<br />
Caitreṇa vedyaṁ vedmīti kiṁ hyatra pratibhāsatām||10.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.20">10.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nanu caitrīyavijñānamātramatra prakāśate|<br />
Vedyatākhyastu no dharmo bhāti bhāvasya nīlavat||10.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.21">10.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vedyatā ca svabhāvena dharmo bhāvasya cettataḥ|<br />
Sarvānpratyeva vedyaḥ syādghaṭanīlādidharmavat||10.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.22">10.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha vedakasaṁvittibalādvedyatvadharmabhāk|<br />
Bhāvastathāpi doṣo'sau kuvindakṛtavastravat||10.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.23">10.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vedyatākhyastu yo dharmaḥ so'vedyaścetkhapuṣpavat|<br />
Vedyaścedasti tatrāpi vedyatetyanavasthitiḥ||10.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.24">10.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tato na kiñcidvedyaṁ syānmūrchitaṁ tu jagadbhavet|<br />
Nanu vijñātrupādhyaṁśopaskṛtaṁ vapurucyatām||10.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.25">10.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhāvasyārthaprakāśātma yathā jñānamidaṁ tvasat|<br />
Ekavijñātṛvedyatve na jñātrantaravedyatā||10.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.26">10.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Samastajñātṛvedyatve naikavijñātṛvedyatā|<br />
Tasmānna vedyatā nāma bhāvadharmo'sti kaścana||10.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.27">10.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhāvasya vedyatā saiva saṁvido yaḥ samudbhavaḥ|<br />
Arthagrahaṇarūpaṁ hi yatra vijñānamātmani||10.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.28">10.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Samavaiti prakāśyo'rthastaṁ pratyeṣaiva vedyatā|<br />
Atra brūmaḥ padārthānāṁ na dharmo yadi vedyatā||10.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.29">10.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Avedyā eva te saṁsyurjñāne satyapi varṇite|<br />
Yathāhi pṛthubudhnādirūpe kumbhasya satyapi||10.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.30">10.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atadātmā paṭo naiti pṛthubudhnādirūpatām|<br />
Tathā satyapi vijñāne vijñātṛsamavāyini||10.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.31">10.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Avedyadharmakā bhāvāḥ kathaṁ vedyatvamāpnuyuḥ|<br />
Anarthaḥ sumahāṁścaiṣa dṛśyatāṁ vastu yatsvayam||10.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.32">10.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prakāśātma na tatsaṁviccāprakāśā tadāśrayaḥ|<br />
Aprakāśo manodīpacakṣurādi tathaiva tat||10.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.33">10.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kiṁ tatprakāśatāṁ nāma supte jagati sarvataḥ|<br />
Jñānasyārthaprakāśatvaṁ nanu rūpaṁ pradīpavat||10.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.34">10.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Apūrvamatra viditaṁ narīnṛtyāmahe tataḥ|<br />
Arthaprakāśo jñānasya yadrūpaṁ tannirūpyatām||10.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.35">10.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Arthaḥ prakāśaścedrūpamartho vā jñānameva vā|<br />
Athārthasya prakāśo yastadrūpamiti bhaṇyate||10.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.36">10.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ṣaṣṭhī kartari cedukto doṣa eva duruddharaḥ|<br />
Atha karmaṇi ṣaṣṭhyeṣā ṇyarthastatra hṛdi sthitaḥ||10.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.37">10.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathā cedaṁ darśayāmaḥ kiṁ prakāśaḥ prakāśate|<br />
Aprakāśo'pi naivāsau tathāpi ca na kiñcana||10.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.38">10.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tarhi loke kathaṁ ṇyartha ucyate cetanasthitau|<br />
Mukhyo ṇyarthasya viṣayo jaḍeṣu tvaupacārikaḥ||10.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.39">10.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathāhi gantuṁ śakto'pi caitro'nyāyattatāṁ gateḥ|<br />
Manvāna eva vaktyasmi gamitaḥ svāmineti hi||10.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.40">10.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svāmyapyasya gatau śaktiṁ buddhvā svādhīnatāṁ sphuṭam|<br />
Paśyannivṛttimāśaṅkya gamayāmīti bhāṣate||10.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.41">10.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Preryaprerakayorevaṁ maulikī ṇyarthasaṅgatiḥ|<br />
Tadabhiprāyato'nyo'pi loke vyavaharettathā||10.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.42">10.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śaraṁ gamayatītyatra punarvegākhyasaṁskriyām|<br />
Vidadhatprerakammanya upacāreṇa jāyate||10.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.43">10.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vāyuradriṁ pātayatītyatra dvāvapi tau jaḍau|<br />
Draṣṭṛbhiḥ prerakapreryavapuṣā parikalpitau||10.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.44">10.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Itthaṁ jaḍena sambandhe na mukhyā ṇyarthasaṅgatiḥ|<br />
Āstāmanyatra vitatametadvistarato mayā||10.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.45">10.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Arthe prakāśanā seyamupacārastato bhavet|<br />
Astu cedbhāsate tarhi sa eva patadadrivat||10.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.46">10.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Upacāre nimittena kenāpi kila bhūyate|<br />
Vāyuḥ pātayatītyatra nimittaṁ tatkṛtā kriyā||10.46||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.47">10.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Girau yenaiṣa saṁyoganāśādbhraṁśaṁ prapadyate|<br />
Iha tu jñānamarthasya na kiñcitkarameva tat||10.47||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.48">10.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Upacāraḥ kathaṁ nāma bhavetso'pi hyavastu san|<br />
Aprakāśita evārthaḥ prakāśatvopacārataḥ||10.48||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.49">10.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tādṛgeva śiśuḥ kiṁ hi dahatyagnyupacārataḥ|<br />
Śiśau vahnyupacāre yadbījaṁ taikṣṇyādi tacca sat||10.49||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.50"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.50">10.50</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prakāśatvopacāre tu kiṁ bījaṁ yatra satyatā|<br />
Siddhe hi cetane yukta upacāraḥ sa hi sphuṭam||10.50||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.51"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.51">10.51</a> <span class="pv-iast">Adhyāropātmakaḥ so'pi pratisandhānajīvitaḥ|<br />
Na cādyāpi kimapyasti cetanaṁ jñānamapyadaḥ||10.51||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.52"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.52">10.52</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aprakāśaṁ tadanyena tatprakāśe'pyayaṁ vidhiḥ|<br />
Nanu pradīpo rūpasya prakāśaḥ kathamīdṛśam||10.52||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.53"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.53">10.53</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atrāpi na vahantyetāḥ kiṁ nu yuktivikalpanāḥ|<br />
Yādṛśā svena rūpeṇa dīpo rūpaṁ prakāśayet||10.53||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.54"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.54">10.54</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tādṛśā svayamapyeṣa bhāti jñānaṁ tu no tathā|<br />
Pradīpaścaiṣa bhāvānāṁ prakāśatvaṁ dadhātyalam||10.54||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.55"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.55">10.55</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anyathā na prakāśeranna bhede cedṛśo vidhiḥ|<br />
Tasmātprakāśa evāyaṁ pūrvoktaḥ paramaḥ śivaḥ||10.55||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.56"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.56">10.56</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathā yathā prakāśeta tattadbhāvavapuḥ sphuṭam|<br />
Evaṁ ca nīlatā nāma yathā kācitprakāśate||10.56||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.57"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.57">10.57</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadvaccakāsti vedyatvaṁ tacca bhāvāṁśapṛṣṭhagam|<br />
Phalaṁ prakaṭatārthasya saṁvidveti dvayaṁ tataḥ||10.57||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.58"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.58">10.58</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vipakṣato rakṣitaṁ ca sandhānaṁ cāpi tanmithaḥ|<br />
Tathāhi nibhṛtaścauraścaitravedyamiti sphuṭam||10.58||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.59"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.59">10.59</a> <span class="pv-iast">Buddhvā nādatta evāśu parīpsāvivaśo'pi san|<br />
Seyaṁ paśyati māṁ netratribhāgeneti sādaram||10.59||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.60"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.60">10.60</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svaṁ dehamamṛteneva siktaṁ paśyati kāmukaḥ|<br />
Na caitajjñānasaṁvittimātraṁ bhāvāṁśapṛṣṭhagam||10.60||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.61"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.61">10.61</a> <span class="pv-iast">Arthakriyākaraṁ taccenna dharmaḥ ko nvasau bhavet|<br />
Yaccoktaṁ vedyatādharmo bhāvaḥ sarvānapi prati||10.61||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.62"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.62">10.62</a> <span class="pv-iast">Syādityetatsvapakṣaghnaṁ duṣprayogāstravattava|<br />
Asmākaṁ tu svaprakāśaśivatāmātravādinām||10.62||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.63"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.63">10.63</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anyaṁ prati cakāstīti vaca eva na vidyate|<br />
Sarvānprati ca tannīlaṁ sa ghaṭaśceti yadvacaḥ||10.63||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.64"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.64">10.64</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadapyaviditaprāyaṁ gṛhītaṁ mugdhabuddhibhiḥ|<br />
Nahi kālāgnirudrīyakāyāvagatanīlimā||10.64||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.65"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.65">10.65</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tava nīlaḥ kiṁ nu pīto maivaṁ bhūnnatu nīlakaḥ|<br />
Na kañcitprati nīlo'sau nīlo vā yaṁ prati sthitaḥ||10.65||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.66"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.66">10.66</a> <span class="pv-iast">Taṁ pratyeva sa vedyaḥ syātsaṅkalpadvārako'ntataḥ|<br />
Yathā cārthaprakāśātma jñānaṁ saṅgīryate tvayā||10.66||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.67"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.67">10.67</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathā tajjñātṛvedyatvaṁ bhāvīyaṁ rūpamucyatām|<br />
Na ca jñātātra niyataḥ kaścijjñāne yathā tava||10.67||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.68"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.68">10.68</a> <span class="pv-iast">Arthe jñātā yadā yo yastadvedyaṁ vapurucyatām|<br />
Tattadvijñātṛvedyatvaṁ sarvānpratyeva bhāsatām||10.68||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.69"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.69">10.69</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ityevaṁ codayanmanye vrajedbadhiradhuryatām|<br />
Nahyanyaṁ prati vai kañcidbhāti sā vedyatā tathā||10.69||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.70"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.70">10.70</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhāvasya rūpamityukte keyamasthānavaidhurī|<br />
Anena nītimārgeṇa nirmūlamapasāritā||10.70||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.287"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.287">10.287</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti śrīsumatiprajñācandrikāśāntatāmasaḥ|<br />
Śrīśambhunāthaḥ sadbhāvaṁ jāgradādau nyarūpayat||10.287||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.288"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.288">10.288</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anye tu kathayantyeṣāṁ bhaṅgīmanyādṛśīṁ śritāḥ|<br />
Yadrūpaṁ jāgradādīnāṁ tadidānīṁ nirūpyate||10.288||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.289"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.289">10.289</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatrākṣavṛttimāśritya bāhyākāragraho hi yaḥ|<br />
Tajjāgratsphuṭamāsīnamanubandhi punaḥ punaḥ||10.289||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.290"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.290">10.290</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ātmasaṅkalpanirmāṇaṁ svapno jāgradviparyayaḥ|<br />
Layākalasya bhogo'sau malakarmavaśānnatu||10.290||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.291"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.291">10.291</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sthiro bhavenniśābhāvātsuptaṁ saukhyādyavedane|<br />
Jñānākalasya malataḥ kevalādbhogamātrataḥ||10.291||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.292"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.292">10.292</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhedavantaḥ svato'bhinnāścikīrṣyante jaḍājaḍāḥ|<br />
Turye tatra sthitā mantratannāthādhīśvarāstrayaḥ||10.292||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.293"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.293">10.293</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yāvadbhairavabodhāntaḥpraveśanasahiṣṇavaḥ|<br />
Bhāvā vigaladātmīyasārāḥ svayamabhedinaḥ||10.293||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.294"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.294">10.294</a> <span class="pv-iast">Turyātītapade saṁsyuriti pañcadaśātmake|<br />
Yasya yadyatsphuṭaṁ rūpaṁ tajjāgraditi manyatām||10.294||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.295"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.295">10.295</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yadevāsthiramābhāti sa pūrvaṁ svapna īdṛśaḥ|<br />
Asphuṭaṁ tu yadābhāti suṣuptaṁ tatpuro'pi yat||10.295||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.296"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.296">10.296</a> <span class="pv-iast">Trayasyāsyānusandhistu yadvaśādupajāyate|<br />
Sraksūtrakalpaṁ tatturyaṁ sarvabhedeṣu gṛhyatām||10.296||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.297"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.297">10.297</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yattvadvaitabharollāsadrāvitāśeṣabhedakam|<br />
Turyātītaṁ tu tatprāhuritthaṁ sarvatra yojayet||10.297||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.298"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.298">10.298</a> <span class="pv-iast">Layākale tu svaṁ rūpaṁ jāgrattatpūrvavṛtti tu|<br />
Svapnādīti kramaṁ sarvaṁ sarvatrānusaredbudhaḥ||10.298||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.299"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.299">10.299</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ekatrāpi prabhau pūrṇe citturyātītamucyate|<br />
Ānandasturyamicchaiva bījabhūmiḥ suṣuptatā||10.299||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.300"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.300">10.300</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jñānaśaktiḥ svapna uktaḥ kriyāśaktistu jāgṛtiḥ|<br />
Na caivamupacāraḥ syātsarvaṁ tatraiva vastutaḥ||10.300||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.301"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.301">10.301</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na cenna kvāpi mukhyatvaṁ nopacāro'pi tatkvacit|<br />
Etacchrīpūrvaśāstre ca sphuṭamuktaṁ maheśinā||10.301||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.302"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.302">10.302</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatra svarūpaṁ śaktiśca sakalaśceti tattrayam|<br />
Iti jāgradavastheyaṁ bhede pañcadaśātmake||10.302||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.303"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.303">10.303</a> <span class="pv-iast">Akalau svapnasauṣupte turyaṁ mantrādivargabhāk|<br />
Turyātītaṁ śaktiśambhū trayodaśābhidhe punaḥ||10.303||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.304"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.304">10.304</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svarūpaṁ jāgradanyattu prāgvatpralayakevale|<br />
Svaṁ jāgratsvapnasupte dve turyādyatra ca pūrvavat||10.304||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.305"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.305">10.305</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vijñānākalabhede'pi svaṁ mantrā mantranāyakāḥ|<br />
Tadīśāḥ śaktiśambhvitthaṁ pañca syurjāgradādayaḥ||10.305||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.306"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.306">10.306</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saptabhede tu mantrākhye svaṁ mantreśā maheśvarāḥ|<br />
Śaktiḥ śambhuśca pañcoktā avasthā jāgradādayaḥ||10.306||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.307"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.307">10.307</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svarūpaṁ mantramāheśī śaktirmantramaheśvaraḥ|<br />
Śaktiḥ śambhurimāḥ pañca mantreśe pañcabhedake||10.307||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.308"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.308">10.308</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svaṁ kriyā jñānamicchā ca śambhuratra ca pañcamī|<br />
Maheśabhede trividhe jāgradādi nirūpitam||10.308||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v10.309"><a class="ta-num" href="#v10.309">10.309</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vyāpārādādhipatyācca taddhānyā prerakatvataḥ|<br />
Icchānivṛtteḥ svasthatvācchiva eko'pi pañcadhā||10.309||</span></p>
