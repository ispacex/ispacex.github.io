<p class="ta-st" id="v23.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.3">23.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svabhyastajñāninaṁ santaṁ bubhūṣumatha bhāvinam|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.4">23.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yo naivaṁ veda naivāsāvabhiṣikto'pi daiśikaḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.5">23.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yo na vedādhvasandhānaṁ ṣoḍhā bāhyāntarasthitam|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.6">23.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sarvalakṣaṇahīno'pi jñānavāngururiṣyate|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.7">23.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Padavākyapramāṇajñaḥ śivabhaktyekatatparaḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.8">23.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na svayambhūstasya coktaṁ lakṣaṇaṁ parameśinā|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.9">23.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Paśvātmanā svayambhūṣṇurnādhikārī sa kutracit|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.10">23.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kuṇḍo golaśca te duṣṭā uktaṁ devyākhyayāmale|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.11">23.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīpūrvaśāstre na tveṣa niyamaḥ ko'pi coditaḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.12">23.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yaḥ punaḥ sarvatattvāni vettītyādi ca lakṣaṇam|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.13">23.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathaiva siddhaye seyamājñeti kila varṇitam|<br />
Yastu karmitayācāryastatra kāṇādivarjanam||23.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.14">23.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yataḥ kārakasāmagryātkarmiṇo nādhikaḥ kvacit|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.15">23.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Taddṛṣṭadoṣātkrodhādeḥ samyakjñātaryasau kutaḥ|<br />
Guravastu svayambhvādi varjyaṁ yadyāmalādiṣu||23.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.16">23.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Karmyabhiprāyataḥ sarvaṁ taditi vyācacakṣire|<br />
Ato deśakulācāradehalakṣaṇakalpanām||23.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.17">23.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anādṛtyaiva sampūrṇajñānaṁ kuryādgururgurum|<br />
Prāgvatsampūjya hutvā ca śrāvayitvā cikīrṣitam||23.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.18">23.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tato'bhiṣiñcettaṁ śiṣyaṁ catuḥṣaṣṭyā tataḥ sakṛt|<br />
Tanmantrarasatoyena pūrvoktavidhinā guruḥ||23.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.19">23.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vibhavena suvistīrṇaṁ tatastasmai vadetsvakam|<br />
Sarvaṁ kartavyasāraṁ yacchāstrāṇāṁ paramaṁ rahaḥ||23.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.20">23.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anugrāhyāstvayā śiṣyāḥ śivaśaktipracoditāḥ|<br />
Uktaṁ jñānottare caitadbrāhmaṇāḥ kṣatriyā viśaḥ||23.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.21">23.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Napuṁsakāḥ striyaḥ śūdrā ye cānye'pi tadarthinaḥ|<br />
Te dīkṣāyāṁ na mīmāṁsyā jñānadāne vicārayet||23.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.22">23.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jñānamūlo guruḥ proktaḥ saptasatrīṁ pravartayet|<br />
Dīkṣā vyākhyā kṛpā maitrī śāstracintā śivaikatā||23.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.23">23.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Annādidānamityetatpālayetsaptasatrakam|<br />
Abhiṣekavidhau cāsmai karaṇīkhaṭikādikam||23.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.24">23.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sarvopakaraṇavrātamarpaṇīyaṁ vipaścite|<br />
So'bhiṣikto guruṁ paścāddakṣiṇābhiḥ prapūjayet||23.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.25">23.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jñānahīno guruḥ karmī svādhikāraṁ samarpya no|<br />
Dīkṣādyadhikṛtiṁ kuryādvinā tasyājñayā punaḥ||23.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.26">23.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ityevaṁ śrāvayetso'pi namaskṛtyābhinandayet|<br />
Tataḥ prabhṛtyasau pūrvo gurustyaktādhikārakaḥ||23.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.27">23.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathecchaṁ vicaredvyākhyādīkṣādau yantraṇojjhitaḥ|<br />
Kurvanna bādhyate yasmāddīpāddīpavadīdṛśaḥ||23.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.28">23.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Santāno nādhikārasya cyavo'kurvanna bādhyate|<br />
Prākcākurvanvihanyeta siddhātantre taducyate||23.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.29">23.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathārthamupadeśaṁ tu kurvannācārya ucyate|<br />
Na cāvajñā kriyākāle saṁsāroddharaṇaṁ prati||23.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.30">23.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na dīkṣeta guruḥ śiṣyaṁ tattvayogyaṁ tu garvataḥ|<br />
Yo'sya syānnarake vāsa iha ca vyādhito bhavet||23.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.31">23.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prāptābhiṣekaḥ sa guruḥ ṣaṇmāsānmantrapaddhatim|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.32">23.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yadaiva tanmayībhūtastadā vīryamupāgataḥ|<br />
Chindyātpāśāṁstato yatnaṁ kuryāttanmayatāsthitau||23.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.33">23.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Hṛccakrādutthitā sūkṣmā śaśisphaṭikasannibhā|<br />
Lekhākārā nādarūpā praśāntā cakrapaṅktigā||23.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.34">23.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dvādaśānte nirūḍhā sā sauṣumne tripathāntare|<br />
Tatra hṛccakramāpūrya japenmantraṁ jvalatprabham||23.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.35">23.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Cakṣurlomādirandhraughavahajjvālaurvasannibham|<br />
Yāvacchāntaśikhākīrṇaṁ viśvājyapravilāpakam||23.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.36">23.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadājyadhārāsantṛptamānābhikuharāntaram|<br />
Evaṁ mantrā mokṣadāḥ syurdīptā buddhāḥ sunirmalāḥ||23.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.37">23.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mūlakandanabhonābhihṛtkaṇṭhālikatālugam|<br />
Ardhendurodhikānādatadantavyāpiśaktigam||23.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.38">23.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Samanonmanaśuddhātmaparacakrasamāśritam|<br />
Yatra yatra japeccakre samastavyastabhedanāt||23.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.39">23.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatra tatra mahāmantra iti devyākhyayāmale|<br />
Vidyāvratamidaṁ proktaṁ mantravīryaprasiddhaye||23.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.40">23.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tacca tādātmyameveti yaduktaṁ spandaśāsane|<br />
Tadākramya balaṁ mantrāḥ sarvajñabalaśālinaḥ||23.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.41">23.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pravartante'dhikārāya karaṇānīva dehinām|<br />
Kṛtavidyāvrataḥ paścāddīkṣāvyākhyādi sarvataḥ||23.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.42">23.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kuryādyogyeṣu śiṣyeṣu nāyogyeṣu kadācana|<br />
Rahasye yojayedvipraṁ parīkṣya viparītataḥ||23.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.43">23.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ācārācchaktimapyevaṁ nānyathetyūrmiśāsane|<br />
Nityādyalpālpakaṁ kuryādyaduktaṁ brahmayāmale||23.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.44">23.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Cīrṇavidyāvrataḥ sarvaṁ manasā vā smaretpriye|<br />
Dehasambandhasañchannasārvajñyo dambhabhājanam||23.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.45">23.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Avidandīkṣamāṇo'pi na duṣyeddaiśikaḥ kvacit|<br />
Jñātvā tvayogyatāṁ nainaṁ dīkṣeta pratyavāyitām||23.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.46">23.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Buddhvā jñāne śāstrasiddhigurutvādau ca taṁ punaḥ|<br />
Bhūya eva parīkṣeta tattadaucityaśālinam||23.46||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.47">23.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatra tatra niyuñjīta na tu jātu viparyayāt|<br />
Nanu tadvastvayogyasya tatrecchā jāyate kutaḥ||23.47||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.48">23.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadīśādhiṣṭhitecchaiva yogyatāmasya sūcayet|<br />
Satyaṁ kāpi prabuddhāsāvicchā rūḍhiṁ na gacchati||23.48||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.49">23.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vidyudvatpāpaśīlasya yathā pāpāpavarjane|<br />
Rūḍhyarūḍhī tadicchāyā api śambhuprasādataḥ||23.49||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.50"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.50">23.50</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aprarūḍhatathecchākastata eva na bhājanam|<br />
Yaḥ samyagjñānamādāya guruviśvāsavarjitaḥ||23.50||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.51"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.51">23.51</a> <span class="pv-iast">Lokaṁ viplāvayennāsmiñjñāte vijñānamarpayet|<br />
Ajñāte'pi punarjñāte vijñānaharaṇaṁ caret||23.51||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.52"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.52">23.52</a> <span class="pv-iast">Punaḥpunaryadā jñāto viśvāsaparivarjitaḥ|<br />
Tadā samagrato dhyāyetsphurantaṁ candrasūryavat||23.52||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.53"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.53">23.53</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tato nijahṛdambhojabodhāmbarataloditām|<br />
Svarbhānumalināṁ dhyāyedvāmāṁ śaktiṁ vimohanīm||23.53||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.54"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.54">23.54</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vāmācārakrameṇaināṁ niḥsṛtāṁ sādhyagāminīm|<br />
Cintayitvā tayā grastaprakāśaṁ taṁ vicintayet||23.54||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.55"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.55">23.55</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anena kramayogena mūḍhabuddherdurātmanaḥ|<br />
Vijñānamantravidyādyāḥ prakurvantyapakāritām||23.55||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.56"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.56">23.56</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nanu vijñānamātmasthaṁ kathaṁ hartuṁ kṣamaṁ bhavet|<br />
Ato vijñānaharaṇaṁ kathaṁ śrīpūrva ucyate||23.56||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.57"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.57">23.57</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ucyate nāsya śiṣyasya vijñānaṁ rūḍhimāgatam|<br />
Tathātve haraṇaṁ kasmātpūrṇayogyatvaśālinaḥ||23.57||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.58"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.58">23.58</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kintveṣa vāmayā śaktyā mūḍho gāḍhaṁ vibhoḥ kṛtaḥ|<br />
Svabhāvādeva tenāsya vidyādyamapakārakam||23.58||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.59"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.59">23.59</a> <span class="pv-iast">Guruḥ punaḥ śivābhinnaḥ sanyaḥ pañcavidhāṁ kṛtim|<br />
Kuryādyadi tataḥ pūrṇamadhikāritvamasya tat||23.59||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.60"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.60">23.60</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ato yathā śuddhatattvasṛṣṭisthityormalātyaye|<br />
Yojanānugrahe kāryacatuṣke'dhikṛto guruḥ||23.60||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.61"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.61">23.61</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śivābhedena tatkuryāttadvatpañcamamapyayam|<br />
Tirobhāvābhidhaṁ kṛtyaṁ tathāsau śivatātmakaḥ||23.61||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.62"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.62">23.62</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ata eva śive śāstre jñāne cāśvāsabhājanam|<br />
Gurormūḍhatayā kopadhāmāpi na tirohitaḥ||23.62||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.63"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.63">23.63</a> <span class="pv-iast">Gururhi kupito yasya sa tirohita ucyate|<br />
Saṁsārī sa tu devo hi gururna ca mṛṣāvidaḥ||23.63||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.64"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.64">23.64</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tata eva ca śāstrādidūṣako yadyapi krudhā|<br />
Na dahyate'sau guruṇā tathāpyeṣa tirohitaḥ||23.64||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.65"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.65">23.65</a> <span class="pv-iast">Asmadgurvāgamastveṣa tirobhūte svayaṁ śiśau|<br />
Na kupyenna śapeddhīmānsa hyanugrāhakaḥ sadā||23.65||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.66"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.66">23.66</a> <span class="pv-iast">Īśecchācoditaḥ pāśaṁ yadi kaṇṭhe nipīḍayet|<br />
Kimācāryeṇa tatrāsya kāryā syātsahakāritā||23.66||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.67"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.67">23.67</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śivābhinno'pi hi gururanugrahamayīṁ vibhoḥ|<br />
Mukhyāṁ śaktimupāsīno'nugṛhṇīyātsa sarvathā||23.67||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.68"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.68">23.68</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svātantryamātrajñaptyai tu kathitaṁ śāstra īdṛśam|<br />
Na kāryaṁ patatāṁ hastālambaḥ sahyo na pātanam||23.68||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.69"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.69">23.69</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ata eva svatantratvādicchāyāḥ punarunmukham|<br />
Prāyaścittairviśodhyainaṁ dīkṣeta kṛpayā guruḥ||23.69||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.70"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.70">23.70</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ūrdhvadṛṣṭau prapannaḥ sannanāśvastastataḥ param|<br />
Adhaḥśāstraṁ prapadyāpi na śreyaḥpātratāmiyāt||23.70||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.71"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.71">23.71</a> <span class="pv-iast">Adhodṛṣṭau prapannastu tadanāśvastamānasaḥ|<br />
Ūrdhvaśāsanabhākpāpaṁ taccojjhecca śivībhavet||23.71||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.72"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.72">23.72</a> <span class="pv-iast">Rājñe druhyannamātyāṅgabhūto'pi hi vihanyate|<br />
Viparyayastu netyevamūrdhvāṁ dṛṣṭiṁ samāśrayet||23.72||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.73"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.73">23.73</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīpūrvaśāstre tenoktaṁ yāvattenaiva noddhṛtaḥ|<br />
Atra hyartho'yametāvatpūrvoktajñānavṛṁhitaḥ||23.73||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.74"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.74">23.74</a> <span class="pv-iast">Gurustāvatsa evātra tacchabdenāvamṛśyate|<br />
Tādṛksvabhyastavijñānabhājordhvapadaśālinā||23.74||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.75"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.75">23.75</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anuddhṛtasya na śreya etadanyagurūddhṛteḥ|<br />
Ata evāmbujanmārkadṛṣṭānto'tra nirūpitaḥ||23.75||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.76"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.76">23.76</a> <span class="pv-iast">Trijagajjyotiṣo hyanyattejo'nyacca niśākṛtaḥ|<br />
Jñānamanyattrikaguroranyattvadharavartinām||23.76||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.77"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.77">23.77</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ata eva purābhūtagurvabhāvo yadā tadā|<br />
Tadanyaṁ lakṣaṇopetamāśrayetpunarunmukhaḥ||23.77||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.78"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.78">23.78</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sati tasmiṁstūnmukhaḥ sankasmājjahyādyadi sphuṭam|<br />
Syādanyatarago doṣo yo'dhikārāpaghātakaḥ||23.78||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.79"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.79">23.79</a> <span class="pv-iast">Doṣaśceha na lokastho doṣatvena nirūpyate|<br />
Ajñānakhyāpanāyuktakhyāpanātmā tvasau mataḥ||23.79||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.80"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.80">23.80</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śiṣyasyāpi tathābhūtajñānānāśvastarūpatā|<br />
Mukhyo doṣastadanye hi doṣāstatprabhavā yataḥ||23.80||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.81"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.81">23.81</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na dhvastavyādhikaḥ ko hi bhiṣajaṁ bahu manyate|<br />
Asūyurnūnamadhvastavyādhiḥ svasthāyate balāt||23.81||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.82"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.82">23.82</a> <span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ jñānasamāśvastaḥ kiṁ kiṁ na gurave caret|<br />
No cennūnamaviśvasto viśvasta iva tiṣṭhati||23.82||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.83"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.83">23.83</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ajñānādaya evaite doṣā na laukikā guroḥ|<br />
Iti khyāpayituṁ proktaṁ mālinīvijayottare||23.83||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.84"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.84">23.84</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na tasyānveṣayedvṛttaṁ śubhaṁ vā yadi vāśubham|<br />
Sa eva tadvijānāti yuktaṁ cāyuktameva vā||23.84||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.85"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.85">23.85</a> <span class="pv-iast">Akāryeṣu yadā saktaḥ prāṇadravyāpahāriṣu|<br />
Tadā nivāraṇīyo'sau praṇatena vipaścitā||23.85||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.86"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.86">23.86</a> <span class="pv-iast">Viśeṣaṇamakāryāṇāmuktābhiprāyameva yat|<br />
Tenātivāryamāṇo'pi yadyasau na nivartate||23.86||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.87"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.87">23.87</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadānyatra kvacidgatvā śivamevānucintayet|<br />
Na hyasya sa gurutve syāddoṣo yenoṣare kṛṣim||23.87||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.88"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.88">23.88</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kuryādvrajenniśāyāṁ vā sa tvarthaprāṇahārakaḥ|<br />
Tadīyāpriyabhīrustu paraṁ tādṛśamācaret||23.88||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.89"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.89">23.89</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yatastadapriyaṁ naiṣa śṛṇuyāditi bhāṣitam|<br />
Śrīmātaṅge taduktaṁ ca nādhītaṁ bhūmabhītitaḥ||23.89||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.90"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.90">23.90</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yaccaitaduktametāvatkartavyamiti taddhruvam|<br />
Tīvraśaktigṛhītānāṁ svayameva hṛdi sphuret||23.90||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.91"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.91">23.91</a> <span class="pv-iast">Upadeśastvayaṁ mandamadhyaśakternijāṁ kramāt|<br />
Śaktiṁ jvalayituṁ proktaḥ sā hyevaṁ jājvalītyalam||23.91||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.92"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.92">23.92</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dṛḍhānurāgasubhagasaṁrambhābhogabhāginaḥ|<br />
Svollāsi smarasarvasvaṁ dārḍhyāyānyatra dṛśyate||23.92||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.93"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.93">23.93</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nanveṣa kasmāddṛṣṭāntaḥ kimetenāśubhaṁ kṛtam|<br />
Citspandaḥ sarvago bhinnādupādheḥ sa tathā tathā||23.93||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.94"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.94">23.94</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhavetko'pi tirobhūtaḥ punarunmukhito'pi san|<br />
Vināpi daiśikātprāgvatsvayameva vimucyate||23.94||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.95"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.95">23.95</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prakārastveṣa nātroktaḥ śaktipātabalādgataḥ|<br />
Asambhāvyatayā cātra dṛḍhakopaprasādavat||23.95||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.96"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.96">23.96</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ityeṣa yo guroḥ prokto vidhistaṁ pālayedguruḥ|<br />
Anyathā na śivaṁ yāyācchrīmatsāre ca varṇitam||23.96||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.97"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.97">23.97</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anyāyaṁ ye prakurvanti śāstrārthaṁ varjayantyalam|<br />
Te'rdhanārīśapuragā guravaḥ samayacyutāḥ||23.97||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.98"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.98">23.98</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anyatrāpyadhikāraṁ na kuryādvidyeśatāṁ vrajet|<br />
Anyatra samayatyāgātkravyādatvaṁ śataṁ samāḥ||23.98||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.99"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.99">23.99</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iyattatratyatātparyaṁ siddhāntagururunnayaḥ|<br />
Bhavetpiśācavidyeśaḥ śuddha eva tu tāntrikaḥ||23.99||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.100"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.100">23.100</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ṣaḍardhadaiśikaścārdhanārīśabhuvanasthitiḥ|<br />
Eṣā karmapradhānānāṁ gurūṇāṁ gatirucyate||23.100||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.101"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.101">23.101</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jñānināṁ caiṣa no bandha iti sarvatra varṇitam|<br />
Sādhakasyābhiṣeke'pi sarvo'yaṁ kathyate vidhiḥ||23.101||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.102"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.102">23.102</a> <span class="pv-iast">Adhikārārpaṇaṁ nātra na ca vidyāvrataṁ kila|<br />
Sādhyamantrārpaṇaṁ tvatra svopayogikriyākrame||23.102||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v23.103"><a class="ta-num" href="#v23.103">23.103</a> <span class="pv-iast">Samaste'pyupadeśaḥ syānnijopakaraṇārpaṇam|<br />
Abhiṣekavidhirnirūpitaḥ parameśena yathā nirūpitaḥ||23.103||</span></p>
