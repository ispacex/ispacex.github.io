# Глава 7 — Cakrodayaḥ

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">71 строфа · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [перевод главы у источника](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/ru/node/865)</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.1">7.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha paramarahasyo'yaṁ cakrāṇāṁ bhaṇyate'bhyudayaḥ||7.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.2">7.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ityayatnajamākhyātaṁ yatnajaṁ tu nigadyate|<br />
Bījapiṇḍātmakaṁ sarvaṁ saṁvidaḥ spandanātmatām||7.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.3">7.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vidadhatparasaṁvittāvupāya iti varṇitam|<br />
Yathāraghaṭṭacakrāgraghaṭīyantraughavāhanam||7.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.4">7.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ekānusandhiyatnena citraṁ yantrodayaṁ bhajet|<br />
Ekānusandhānabalājjāte mantrodaye'niśam||7.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.5">7.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tanmantradevatā yatnāttādātmyena prasīdati|<br />
Khe rasaikākṣi nityotthe tadardhaṁ dvikapiṇḍake||7.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.6">7.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Trike sapta sahasrāṇi dviśatītyudayo mataḥ|<br />
Catuṣke tu sahasrāṇi pañca caiva catuḥśatī||7.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.7">7.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pañcārṇe'bdhisahasrāṇi triśatī viṁśatistathā|<br />
Ṣaṭke sahasratritayaṁ ṣaṭśatī codayo bhavet||7.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.8">7.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saptake trisahasraṁ tu ṣaḍaśītyadhikaṁ smṛtam|<br />
Śataistu saptaviṁśatyā varṇāṣṭakavikalpite||7.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.9">7.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Caturviṁśatiśatyā tu navārṇeṣūdayo bhavet|<br />
Adhiṣaṣṭyekaviṁśatyā śatānāṁ daśavarṇake||7.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.10">7.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ekānnaviṁśatiśataṁ catuḥṣaṣṭiḥ śivārṇake|<br />
Aṣṭādaśa śatāni syurudayo dvādaśārṇake||7.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.11">7.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Trayodaśārṇe dvāṣaṣṭyā śatāni kila ṣoḍaśa|<br />
Tricatvāriṁśatā pañcadaśeti bhuvanārṇake||7.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.12">7.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Caturdaśaśatī khābdhiḥ syātpañcadaśavarṇake|<br />
Trayodaśaśatī sārdhā ṣoḍaśārṇe tu kathyate||7.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.13">7.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śatadvādaśikā saptadaśārṇe saikasaptatiḥ|<br />
Aṣṭādaśārṇe vijñeyā śatadvādaśikā budhaiḥ||7.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.14">7.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Caturviṁśatisaṅkhyāke cakre navaśatī bhavet|<br />
Saptaviṁśatisaṅkhyāte tūdayo'ṣṭaśatātmakaḥ||7.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.15">7.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dvātriṁśake mahācakre ṣaṭśatī pañcasaptatiḥ|<br />
Dvicaturviṁśake cakre sārdhāṁ śatacatuṣṭayīm||7.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.16">7.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Udayaṁ piṇḍayogajñaḥ piṇḍamantreṣu lakṣayet|<br />
Catuṣpañcāśake cakre śatānāṁ tu catuṣṭayam||7.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.17">7.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saptatriṁśatsahārdhena triśatyaṣṭāṣṭake bhavet|<br />
Ardhamardhatribhāgaśca ṣaṭṣaṣṭirdviśatī bhavet||7.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.18">7.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ekāśītipade cakre udayaḥ prāṇacāragaḥ|<br />
Cakre tu ṣaṇṇavatyākhye sapādā dviśatī bhavet||7.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.19">7.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aṣṭottaraśate cakre dviśatastūdayo bhavet|<br />
Krameṇetthamidaṁ cakraṁ ṣaṭkṛtvo dviguṇaṁ yadā||7.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.20">7.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tato'pi dviguṇe'ṣṭāṁśasyārdhamadhyardhamekakam|<br />
Tato'pi sūkṣmakuśalairardhārdhādiprakalpane||7.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.21">7.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhāgaṣoḍaśakasthityā sūkṣmaścāro'bhilakṣyate|<br />
Evaṁ prayatnasaṁruddhaprāṇacārasya yoginaḥ||7.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.22">7.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Krameṇa prāṇacārasya grāsa evopajāyate|<br />
Prāṇagrāsakramāvāptakālasaṅkarṣaṇasthitiḥ||7.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.23">7.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṁvidekaiva pūrṇā syājjñānabhedavyapohanāt|<br />
Tathā hi prāṇacārasya navasyānudaye sati||7.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.24">7.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na kālabhedajanito jñānabhedaḥ prakalpate|<br />
Saṁvedyabhedānna jñānaṁ bhinnaṁ śikharivṛttavat||7.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.25">7.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kālastu bhedakastasya sa tu sūkṣmaḥ kṣaṇo mataḥ|<br />
Saukṣmyasya cāvadhirjñānaṁ yāvattiṣṭhati sa kṣaṇaḥ||7.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.26">7.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anyathā na sa nirvaktuṁ nipuṇairapi pāryate|<br />
Jñānaṁ kiyadbhavettāvattadabhāvo na bhāsate||7.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.27">7.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadabhāvaśca no tāvadyāvattatrākṣavartmani|<br />
Arthe vātmapradeśe vā na saṁyogavibhāgitā||7.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.28">7.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sā cedudayate spandamayī tatprāṇagā dhruvam|<br />
Bhavedeva tataḥ prāṇaspandābhāve na sā bhavet||7.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.29">7.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadabhāvānna vijñānābhāvaḥ saivaṁ tu saiva dhīḥ|<br />
Na cāsau vastuto dīrghā kālabhedavyapohanāt||7.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.30">7.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vastuto hyata eveyaṁ kālaṁ saṁvinna saṁspṛśet|<br />
Ata ekaiva saṁvittirnānārūpe tathātathā||7.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.31">7.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vindānā nirvikalpāpi vikalpo bhāvagocare|<br />
Spandāntaraṁ na yāvattaduditaṁ tāvadeva saḥ||7.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.32">7.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tāvāneko vikalpaḥ syādvividhaṁ vastu kalpayan|<br />
Ye tvitthaṁ na vidusteṣāṁ vikalpo nopapadyate||7.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.33">7.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sa hyeko na bhavetkaścit trijagatyāpi jātucit|<br />
Śabdārūṣaṇayā jñānaṁ vikalpaḥ kila kathyate||7.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.34">7.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sā ca syātkramikaivetthaṁ kiṁ kathaṁ ko vikalpayet|<br />
Ghaṭa ityapi neyānsyādvikalpaḥ kā kathā sthitau||7.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.35">7.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na vikalpaśca ko'pyasti yo mātrāmātraniṣṭhitaḥ|<br />
Na ca jñānasamūho'sti teṣāmayugapatsthiteḥ||7.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.36">7.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tenāstaṅgata evaiṣa vyavahāro vikalpajaḥ|<br />
Tasmātspandāntaraṁ yāvannodiyāttāvadekakam||7.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.37">7.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vijñānaṁ tadvikalpātmadharmakoṭīrapi spṛśet|<br />
Ekāśītipadodāraśaktyāmarśātmakastataḥ||7.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.38">7.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vikalpaḥ śivatādāyī pūrvameva nirūpitaḥ|<br />
Yathā karṇau nartayāmītyevaṁ yatnāttathā bhavet||7.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.39">7.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Cakracāragatādyatnāttadvattaccakragaiva dhīḥ|<br />
Japahomārcanādīnāṁ prāṇasāmyamato vidhiḥ||7.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.40">7.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Siddhāmate kuṇḍalinīśaktiḥ prāṇasamonmanā|<br />
Uktaṁ ca yoginīkaule tadetatparameśinā||7.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.41">7.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Padamantrākṣare cakre vibhāgaṁ śaktitattvagam|<br />
Padeṣu kṛtvā mantrajño japādau phalabhāgbhavet||7.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.42">7.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dvitrisaptāṣṭasaṅkhyātaṁ lopayecchatikodayam|<br />
Iti śaktisthitā mantrā vidyā vā cakranāyakāḥ||7.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.43">7.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Padapiṇḍasvarūpeṇa jñātvā yojyāḥ sadā priye|<br />
Nityodaye mahātattve udayasthe sadāśive||7.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.44">7.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ayuktāḥ śaktimārge tu na japtāścodayena ye|<br />
Te na siddhyanti yatnena japtāḥ koṭiśatairapi||7.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.45">7.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mālāmantreṣu sarveṣu mānaso japa ucyate|<br />
Upāṁśurvā śaktyudayaṁ teṣāṁ na parikalpayet||7.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.46">7.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Padamantreṣu sarveṣu yāvattatpadaśaktigam|<br />
Śakyate satataṁ yuktaistāvajjapyaṁ tu sādhakaiḥ||7.46||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.47">7.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tāvatī teṣu vai saṅkhyā padeṣu padasañjñitā|<br />
Tāvantamudayaṁ kṛtvā tripadoktyāditaḥ kramāt||7.47||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.48">7.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dvādaśākhye dvādaśite cakre sārdhaṁ śataṁ bhavet|<br />
Udayastaddhi sacatuścatvāriṁśacchataṁ bhavet||7.48||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.49">7.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ṣoḍaśākhye dvādaśite dvānavatyadhike śate|<br />
Cārārdhena samaṁ proktaṁ śataṁ dvādaśakādhikam||7.49||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.50"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.50">7.50</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ṣoḍaśākhye ṣoḍaśite bhaveccaturaśītigaḥ|<br />
Udayo dviśataṁ taddhi ṣaṭpañcāśatsamuttaram||7.50||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.51"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.51">7.51</a> <span class="pv-iast">Cārāṣṭabhāgāṁstrīnatra kathayantyadhikānbudhāḥ|<br />
Aṣṭāṣṭake dvādaśite pādārdhaṁ viṁśatiṁ vasūn||7.51||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.52"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.52">7.52</a> <span class="pv-iast">Udayaḥ saptaśatikā sāṣṭā ṣaṣṭiryato hi saḥ|<br />
Eṣa cakrodayaḥ proktaḥ sādhakānāṁ hitāvahaḥ||7.52||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.53"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.53">7.53</a> <span class="pv-iast">Niruddhya mānasīrvṛttīścakre viśrāntimāgataḥ|<br />
Vyutthāya yāvadviśrāmyettāvaccārodayo hyayam||7.53||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.54"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.54">7.54</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pūrṇe samudaye tvatra praveśaikātmyanirgamāḥ|<br />
Traya ityata evoktaḥ siddhau madhyodayo varaḥ||7.54||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.55"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.55">7.55</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ādyantodayanirmuktā madhyamodayasaṁyutāḥ|<br />
Mantravidyācakragaṇāḥ siddhibhājo bhavanti hi||7.55||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.56"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.56">7.56</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mantracakrodayajñastu vidyācakrodayārthavit|<br />
Kṣipraṁ siddhyediti proktaṁ śrīmaddviṁśatike trike||7.56||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.57"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.57">7.57</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dvistriścaturvā mātrābhirvidyāṁ vā cakrameva vā|<br />
Tattvodayayutaṁ nityaṁ pṛthagbhūtaṁ japetsadā||7.57||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.58"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.58">7.58</a> <span class="pv-iast">Piṇḍākṣarapadairmantramekaikaṁ śaktitattvagam|<br />
Bahvakṣarastu yo mantro vidyā vā cakrameva vā||7.58||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.59"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.59">7.59</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śaktisthaṁ naiva taṁ tatra vibhāgastvoṁnamo'ntagaḥ|<br />
Asmiṁstattvodaye tasmādahorātrastriśastriśaḥ||7.59||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.60"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.60">7.60</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vibhajyate vibhāgaśca punareva triśastriśaḥ|<br />
Pūrvodaye tu viśramya dvitīyenollasedyadā||7.60||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.61"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.61">7.61</a> <span class="pv-iast">Viśeccārdhārdhikāyogāttadoktārdhodayo bhavet|<br />
Yadā pūrṇodayātmā tu samaḥ kālastrike sphuret||7.61||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.62"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.62">7.62</a> <span class="pv-iast">Praveśaviśrāntyullāse syātsvatryaṁśodayastadā|<br />
Ityeṣa kālavibhavaḥ prāṇa eva pratiṣṭhitaḥ||7.62||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.63"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.63">7.63</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sa spande khe sa taccityāṁ tenāsyāṁ viśvaniṣṭhitiḥ|<br />
Ataḥ saṁvitpratiṣṭhānau yato viśvalayodayau||7.63||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.64"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.64">7.64</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śaktyante'dhvani tatspandāsaṅkhyātā vāstavī tataḥ|<br />
Uktaṁ śrīmālinītantre gātre yatraiva kutracit||7.64||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.65"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.65">7.65</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vikāra upajāyeta tattattvaṁ tattvamuttamam|<br />
Prāṇe pratiṣṭhitaḥ kālastadāviṣṭā ca yattanuḥ||7.65||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.66"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.66">7.66</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dehe pratiṣṭhitasyāsya tato rūpaṁ nirūpyate|<br />
Citspandaprāṇavṛttīnāmantyā yā sthūlatā suṣiḥ||7.66||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.67"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.67">7.67</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sā nāḍīrūpatāmetya dehaṁ santānayedimam|<br />
Śrīsvacchande'ta evoktaṁ yathā parṇaṁ svatantubhiḥ||7.67||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v7.68"><a class="ta-num" href="#v7.68">7.68</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vyāptaṁ tadvattanurdvāradvāribhāvena nāḍibhiḥ|<br />
Pādāṅguṣṭhādikordhvasthabrahmakuṇḍalikāntagaḥ||7.68||</span></p>
