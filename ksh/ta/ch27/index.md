# Глава 27 — Liṅgārcā

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">59 строф · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [английский перевод у источника — русского там нет](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/en/node/895)</p>

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Глава 26 — Śeṣavṛttinirūpaṇam](/ksh/ta/ch26/) · [Глава 28 — Bahubhitparvapavitrādi nimittajam →](/ksh/ta/ch28/)</p>

<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloke saptaviṁśatitamamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.1">27.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Athocyate liṅgapūjā sūcitā mālinīmate||27.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.2">27.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Eteṣāmūrdhvaśāstroktamantrāṇāṁ na pratiṣṭhitam|<br />
Bahiṣkuryāttato hyete rahasyatvena siddhidāḥ||27.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.3">27.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svavīryānandamāhātmyapraveśāveśaśālinīm|<br />
Ye siddhiṁ dadate teṣāṁ bāhyatvaṁ rūpavicyutiḥ||27.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.4">27.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kiñca coktaṁ samāveśāpūrṇe bhoktrātmakaḥ śivaḥ|<br />
Bhogalāmpaṭyabhāgbhogavicchede nigrahātmakaḥ||27.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.5">27.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śāntatvanyakkriyodbhūtajighatsābṛṁhitaṁ vapuḥ|<br />
Svayaṁ pratiṣṭhitaṁ yena so'syābhoge vinaśyati||27.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.6">27.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktaṁ jñānottarāyāṁ ca tadetatparameśinā|<br />
Śivo yāgapriyo yasmādviśeṣānmātṛmadhyagaḥ||27.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.7">27.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasmādrahasyaśāstreṣu ye mantrāstānbudho bahiḥ|<br />
Na pratiṣṭhāpayejjātu viśeṣādvyaktarūpiṇaḥ||27.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.8">27.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ata eva mṛtasyārthe pratiṣṭhānyatra yoditā|<br />
Sātra śāstreṣu no kāryā kāryā sādhāraṇī punaḥ||27.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.9">27.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ā tanmayatvasaṁsiddherā cābhīṣṭaphalodayāt|<br />
Putrakaḥ sādhako vyaktamavyaktaṁ vā samāśrayet||27.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.10">27.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Putrakairgururabhyarthyaḥ sādhakastu svayaṁ vidan|<br />
Yadi tatsthāpayenno cettenāpyarthyo gururbhavet||27.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.11">27.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Guruścātra nirodhākhye kāla itthaṁ vibhau vadet|<br />
Jīvatyasminphalāntaṁ tvaṁ tiṣṭherjīvāvadhīti vā||27.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.12">27.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Liṅgaṁ ca bāṇaliṅgaṁ vā ratnajaṁ vātha mauktikam|<br />
Pauṣpamānnamatho vāstraṁ gandhadravyakṛtaṁ ca vā||27.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.13">27.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na tu pāṣāṇajaṁ liṅgaṁ śilpyutthaṁ parikalpayet|<br />
Dhātūtthaṁ ca suvarṇotthavarjamanyadvivarjayet||27.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.14">27.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na cātra liṅgamānādi kvacidapyupayujyate|<br />
Udāravīryairmantrairyadbhāsitaṁ phaladaṁ hi tat||27.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.15">27.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasyāpi sthaṇḍilādyuktavidhinā śuddhimācaret|<br />
Mantrārpaṇaṁ tathaiva syānnirodhastūktayuktitaḥ||27.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.16">27.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Agnau ca tarpaṇaṁ bhūriviśeṣāddakṣiṇā guroḥ|<br />
Dīnāditṛptirvibhavādyāga ityadhiko vidhiḥ||27.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.17">27.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sarveṣvavyaktaliṅgeṣu pradhānaṁ syādakalpitam|<br />
Tathā ca tatra tatroktaṁ lakṣaṇe pārameśvare||27.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.18">27.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sūtre pātre dhvaje vastre svayambhūbāṇapūjite|<br />
Nadīprasravaṇotthe ca nāhvānaṁ nāpi kalpanā||27.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.19">27.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pīṭhaprāsādamantrāṁśavelādiniyamo na ca|<br />
Vyaktaṁ vā citrapustādau devadārusuvarṇajam||27.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.20">27.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha dīkṣitasacchilpikṛtaṁ sthāpayate guruḥ|<br />
Athavā lakṣaṇopetamūrdhatatkarparāśritam||27.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.21">27.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Paṅkticakrakaśūlābjavidhinā tūramāśrayet|<br />
Tallakṣaṇaṁ bruve śrīmatpicuśāstre nirūpitam||27.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.22">27.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tūre yāgaḥ sadā śastaḥ siddhido doṣavarjite|<br />
Jālakairjarjarai randhrairdantairūnādhikai rujā||27.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.23">27.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yukte ca tūre hāniḥ syāttaddhīne yāga uttamaḥ|<br />
Kāmya eva bhavettūramiti kecitprapedire||27.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.24">27.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Guravastu vidhau kāmye yatnāddoṣāṁstyajediti|<br />
Vyācakṣate picuproktaṁ na nitye karmaṇītyadaḥ||27.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.25">27.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīsiddhātantra uktaṁ ca tūralakṣaṇamuttamam|<br />
Ekādikacatuṣkhaṇḍe gomukhe pūrṇacandrake||27.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.26">27.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Padmagorocanāmuktānīrasphaṭikasannibhe|<br />
Ekādipañcasadrandhravidyārekhānvite śubhe||27.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.27">27.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na rūkṣavakraśakaladīrghanimnasabinduke|<br />
Ślakṣṇayā vajrasūcyātra sphuṭaṁ devīgaṇānvitam||27.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.28">27.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sarvaṁ samālikhetpūjyaṁ sarvāvayavasundaram|<br />
Etadevānusartavyamarghapātre'pi lakṣaṇam||27.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.29">27.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrībrahmayāmale'pyuktaṁ pātraṁ gomukhamuttamam|<br />
Gajakūrmatalaṁ kumbhavṛttaśaktikajākṛti||27.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.30">27.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Akṣasūtramatho kuryāttatraivābhyarcayetkramam|<br />
Vīradhātujalodbhūtamuktāratnasuvarṇajam||27.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.31">27.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Akṣasūtraṁ kramotkṛṣṭaṁ raudrākṣaṁ vā viśeṣataḥ|<br />
Śataṁ tithyuttaraṁ yadvā sāṣṭaṁ yadvā tadardhakam||27.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.32">27.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadardhaṁ vātha pañcāśadyuktaṁ tatparikalpayet|<br />
Vaktrāṇi pañca citspandajñānecchākṛtisaṅgateḥ||27.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.33">27.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pañcadhādyantagaṁ caikyamityupāntyākṣago vidhiḥ|<br />
Śaktitadvatprabhedena tatra dvairūpyamucyate||27.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.34">27.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tato dviguṇamāne tu dvirūpaṁ nyāsamācaret|<br />
Tato'pi dviguṇe sṛṣṭisaṁhṛtidvitayena tam||27.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.35">27.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mātṛkāṁ mālinīṁ vātha nyasyetkhaśarasammite|<br />
Uttame tu dvayīṁ nyasyennyasya pūrvaṁ pracoditān||27.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.36">27.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dīkṣāyāṁ mukhyato mantrāṁstānpañcadaśa daiśikaḥ|<br />
Yadi vā tattvabhuvanakalāmantrapadārṇajaiḥ||27.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.37">27.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṅkhyābhedaiḥ kṛte sūtre taṁ taṁ nyāsaṁ guruścaret|<br />
Kṛtvākṣasūtraṁ tasyāpi sarvaṁ sthaṇḍilavadbhavet||27.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.38">27.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pūjitena ca tenaiva japaṁ kuryādatandritaḥ|<br />
Vidhiruktastvayaṁ śrīmanmālinīvijayottare||27.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.39">27.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Cakravadbhramayannetadyadvakti sa japo bhavet|<br />
Yadīkṣate juhotyetadbodhāgnau sampraveśanāt||27.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.40">27.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Athavārghamahāpātraṁ kuryāttaccottaraṁ param|<br />
Nārikelamatho bailvaṁ sauvarṇaṁ rājataṁ ca vā||27.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.41">27.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasyāpyeṣa vidhiḥ sarvaḥ pratiṣṭhādau prakīrtitaḥ|<br />
Tanniṣkamparasaiḥ pūrṇaṁ kṛtvāsminpūjayetkramam||27.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.42">27.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Adhomukhaṁ sadā sthāpyaṁ pūjitaṁ pūjane punaḥ|<br />
Tatpātramunmukhaṁ tacca riktaṁ kuryānna tādṛśam||27.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.43">27.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pūjānte tadrasāpūrṇamātmānaṁ pravidhāya tat|<br />
Adhomukhaṁ ca sampūjya sthāpayeta vicakṣaṇaḥ||27.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.44">27.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Khaḍgaṁ kṛpāṇikāṁ yadvā kartarīṁ makuraṁ ca vā|<br />
Vimalaṁ tattathā kuryācchrīmatkālīmukhoditam||27.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.45">27.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrībhairavakule'pyuktaṁ kulaparvaprapūjane|<br />
Sthaṇḍile'gnau paṭe liṅge pātre padme'tha maṇḍale||27.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.46">27.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mūrtau ghaṭe'strasaṅghāte dhaṭe sūtre'tha pūjayet|<br />
Svena svenopacāreṇa saṅkaraṁ varjayediti||27.46||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.47">27.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathāpsu śāntaye mantrāstadvadastrādiṣu dhruvam|<br />
Śatrucchedādikartāraḥ kāmyo'taḥ saṅkarojjhitaḥ||27.47||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.48">27.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Akāmasya tu te tattatsthānopādhivaśāddhruvam|<br />
Pāśakartanasaṁśuddhatattvāpyāyādikāriṇaḥ||27.48||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.49">27.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Athavā pustakaṁ tādṛgrahaḥśāstrakramombhitam|<br />
Suśuddhaṁ dīkṣitakṛtaṁ tatrāpyeṣa vidhiḥ smṛtaḥ||27.49||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.50"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.50">27.50</a> <span class="pv-iast">Itthaṁ svayampratiṣṭheṣu yāvadyāvatsthitirbhavet|<br />
Vibhavaistarpaṇaṁ śuddhistāvadvicchedavarjanam||27.50||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.51"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.51">27.51</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ata eva yadā bhūridinaṁ maṇḍalakalpanam|<br />
Tadā dine dine kuryādvibhavaistarpaṇaṁ bahu||27.51||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.52"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.52">27.52</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pratiṣṭhāyāṁ ca sarvatra guruḥ pūrvoditaṁ param|<br />
Satattvamanusandhāya sannidhiṁ sphuṭamācaret||27.52||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.53"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.53">27.53</a> <span class="pv-iast">Siddhe tu tanmayībhāve phale putrakasādhakaiḥ|<br />
Anyasmai taddvayādanyatarasmai tatsamarpyate||27.53||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.54"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.54">27.54</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasyāpyeṣa vidhiḥ sarvastadalābhe tu sarvathā|<br />
Agādhe'mbhasi tatkṣepyaṁ kṣamayitvā visṛjya ca||27.54||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.55"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.55">27.55</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ityeṣa svapratiṣṭhānavidhiḥ śivanirūpitaḥ|<br />
Parapratiṣṭhite liṅge bāṇīye'tha svayambhuvi||27.55||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.56"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.56">27.56</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sarvamāsanapakṣe prāṅnyasya sampūjayetkramam|<br />
Śuddhāśuddhādhvajāḥ sarve mantrāḥ sarvaḥ śivāntakaḥ||27.56||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.57"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.57">27.57</a> <span class="pv-iast">Adhvā cehāsane proktastatsarvatrārcayedidam|<br />
Āvāhanavisṛṣṭī tu tatra prāgvatsamācaret||27.57||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.58"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.58">27.58</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktaṁ tantre'pyaghoreśe svacchande vibhunā tathā|<br />
Athavā pratyahaṁ proktamānārdhārdhaniyogataḥ||27.58||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v27.59"><a class="ta-num" href="#v27.59">27.59</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kṛtveṣṭaṁ maṇḍalaṁ tatra samastaṁ kramamarcayet|<br />
Bahuprakārabhinnasya liṅgasyārcā nirūpitā||27.59||</span></p>
