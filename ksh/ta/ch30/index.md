<p class="ta-st" id="v30.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.1">30.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha yathocitamantrakadambakaṁ trikakulakramayogi nirūpyate|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.2">30.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pratibuddhā hi te mantrā vimarśaikasvabhāvakāḥ|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.3">30.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yamāviśanti cācāryaṁ taṁ tādātmyanirūḍhitaḥ|<br />
Svatantrīkurvate yānti karaṇānyapi kartṛtām||30.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.4">30.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ādhāraśaktau hrīṁ pṛthvīprabhṛtau tu catuṣṭaye|<br />
Kṣlāṁ kṣvīṁ vaṁ kṣamiti prāhuḥ kramādvarṇacatuṣṭayam||30.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.5">30.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Haṁ nāle yaṁ tathā raṁ laṁ vaṁ dharmādicatuṣṭaye|<br />
Ṛṁ ṝṁ ḷṁ ḹṁ catuṣke ca viparītakramādbhavet||30.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.6">30.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Oṁ auṁ hastrayamityetadvidyāmāyākalātraye|<br />
Anusvāravisargau ca vidyeśeśvaratattvayoḥ||30.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.7">30.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kādibhāntāḥ kesareṣu prāṇo'ṣṭasvarasaṁyutaḥ|<br />
Sabinduko daleṣvaṣṭasvatha svaṁ nāma dīpitam||30.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.8">30.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śaktīnāṁ navakasya syācchaṣasā maṇḍalatraye|<br />
Sabindukāḥ kṣmaṁ prete jraṁ śūlaśṛṅgeṣu kalpayet||30.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.9">30.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pṛthagāsanapūjāyāṁ kramānmantrā ime smṛtāḥ|<br />
Saṅkṣepapūjane tu prāgādyamantyaṁ ca bījakam||30.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.10">30.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ādāyādhāraśaktyādiśūlaśṛṅgāntamarcayet|<br />
Agnimārutapṛthvyambusaṣaṣṭhasvarabindukam||30.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.11">30.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ratiśekharamantro'sya vaktrāṅgaṁ hrasvadīrghakaiḥ|<br />
Agniprāṇāgnisaṁhārakālendrāmbusamīraṇāḥ||30.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.12">30.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṣaṣṭhasvarabindvardhacandrādyāḥ syurnavātmanaḥ|<br />
Bindunādādikā vyāptiḥ śrīmattraiśirase mate||30.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.13">30.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kṣepākrānticidudbodhadīpanasthāpanānyatha|<br />
Tatsaṁvittistadāpattiriti sañjñābhiśabditā||30.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.14">30.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Etāvatī mahāvyāptirmūrtitvenātra kīrtitā|<br />
Pariṇāmastallayaśca namaskāraḥ sa ucyate||30.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.15">30.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Eṣa tryarṇojjhito'dhastāddīrghaiḥ ṣaḍbhiḥ svarairyutaḥ|<br />
Ṣaḍaṅgāni hṛdādīni vaktrāṇyasya ca kalpayet||30.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.16">30.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kṣayaravalabījaistu dīptairbinduvibhūṣitaiḥ|<br />
Jhakārasaṁhṛtiprāṇāḥ saṣaṣṭhasvarabindukāḥ||30.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.17">30.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Eṣa bhairavasadbhāvaścandrārdhādivibhūṣitaḥ|<br />
Mātṛkāmālinīmantrau prāgeva samudāhṛtau||30.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.18">30.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Oṅkāro'tha caturthyantā sañjñā natiriti kramāt|<br />
Gaṇeśādiṣu mantraḥ syādbījaṁ yeṣu na coditam||30.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.19">30.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nāmādyakṣaramākārabinducandrādidīpitam|<br />
Sarveṣāmeva bījānāṁ taccaturdaśaṣaṣṭhayuk||30.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.20">30.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Āmantritānyaghoryāditritayasya kramoditaiḥ|<br />
Bījairvisargiṇī māyā huṁ hakāro visargavān||30.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.21">30.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Punardevītrayasyāpi kramādāmantraṇatrayam|<br />
Dvitīyasminpade'kāra ekārasyeha ca smṛtaḥ||30.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.22">30.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tataḥ śaktidvayāmantro luptaṁ tatrāntyamakṣaram|<br />
He'gnivarṇāvubhau pañcasvarayuktau parau pṛthak||30.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.23">30.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Akārayuktāvastraṁ huṁ ha visargī punaḥ śaraḥ|<br />
Tāreṇa saha vasvagnivarṇārdhārṇadvayādhikā||30.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.24">30.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Eṣā parāparādevyā vidyā śrītrikaśāsane|<br />
Pañcaṣaṭpañcavedākṣivahninetrākṣaraṁ padam||30.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.25">30.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aghoryādau saptake syātpibanyāḥ pariśiṣṭakam|<br />
Pratyekavarṇago'pyuktaḥ siddhayogīśvarīmate||30.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.26">30.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Devatācakravinyāsaḥ sa bahutvānna lipyate|<br />
Māyā visargiṇī hūṁ phaṭ ceti mantro'parātmakaḥ||30.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.27">30.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Parāyāstūktasadvyāptirjīvaḥ sahacaturdaśaḥ|<br />
Sānekabhedā triśiraḥśāstre proktā maheśinā||30.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.28">30.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svarūpato'vibhinnāpi racanānekasaṅkulā|<br />
Jīvaḥ prāṇastha evātra prāṇo vā jīvasaṁsthitaḥ||30.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.29">30.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ādhārādheyabhāvenāvinābhāvayogataḥ|<br />
Haṁsaṁ cāmṛtamadhyasthaṁ kālarudravibheditam||30.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.30">30.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhuvaneśaśiroyuktamanaṅgadvayayojitam|<br />
Dīptāddīptataraṁ jñeyaṁ ṣaṭcakrakramayojitam||30.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.31">30.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prāṇaṁ daṇḍāsanasthaṁ tu guhyaśaktīcchayā yutam|<br />
Pareyaṁ vācikoddiṣṭā mahājñānasvarūpataḥ||30.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.32">30.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sphuṭaṁ bhairavahṛjjñānamidaṁ tvekākṣaraṁ param|<br />
Amṛtaṁ kevalaṁ khasthaṁ yadvā sāvitrikāyutam||30.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.33">30.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śūnyadvayasamopetaṁ parāyā hṛdayaṁ param|<br />
Yugmayāge prasiddhaṁ tu kartavyaṁ tattvavedibhiḥ||30.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.34">30.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anye'pyekākṣarā ye tvekavīravidhānataḥ|<br />
Guptā guptatarāste tvaṅgābhijanavarjitāḥ||30.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.35">30.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yaṣṭavyāḥ sādhakendraistu kulasthāḥ siddhidāyakāḥ|<br />
Kulakramavidhānena sūkṣmavijñānayogataḥ||30.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.36">30.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anuṣṭheyāḥ sadā devi striyā vā puruṣeṇa vā|<br />
Sakāro dīrghaṣaṭkena yukto'ṅgānyānanāni tu||30.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.37">30.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Syātsa eva paraṁ hrasvapañcasvarakhasaṁyutaḥ|<br />
Oṅkāraiḥ pañcabhirmantro vidyāṅgahṛdayaṁ bhavet||30.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.38">30.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Praṇavaścāmṛte tejo mālini svāhayā saha|<br />
Ekādaśākṣaraṁ brahmaśirastanmālinīmate||30.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.39">30.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vedavedani hūṁ phaṭca praṇavādiyutā śikhā|<br />
Vajriṇe vajradharāya svāhetyoṅkārapūrvakam||30.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.40">30.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ekādaśākṣaraṁ varma puruṣṭutamiti smṛtam|<br />
Tāro dvijihvaḥ khaśarasvarayugjīva eva ca||30.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.41">30.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Netrametatprakāśātma sarvasādhāraṇaṁ smṛtam|<br />
Tāraḥ ślīṁ paśu hūṁ phaṭca tadastraṁ rasavarṇakam||30.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.42">30.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Laraṭakṣavayairdīrghaiḥ samayuktaiḥ sabindukaiḥ|<br />
Indrādayastadastrāṇi hrasvairviṣṇuprajāpatī||30.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.43">30.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Smṛtau turyadvitīyābhyāṁ hrasvābhyāṁ padmacakrake|<br />
Namaḥ svāhā tathā vauṣaṭ huṁ vaṣaṭ phaṭ ca jātayaḥ||30.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.44">30.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aṅgeṣu kramaśaḥ ṣaṭsu karmasvatha tadātmikāḥ|<br />
Jape home tathāpyāye samuccāṭe'tha śāntike||30.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.45">30.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Abhicāre ca mantrāṇāṁ namaskārādijātayaḥ|<br />
Akṣiṣaṇmunivargebhyo dvitīyāḥ saha bindunā||30.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.46">30.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yonyarṇena ca mātṝṇāṁ sadbhāvaḥ kālakarṣiṇī|<br />
Ādyojjhito vāpyantena varjito vātha sammataḥ||30.46||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.47">30.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jīvaḥ prāṇapuṭāntaḥsthaḥ kālānalasamadyutiḥ|<br />
Atidīptastu vāmāṅghrirbhūṣito mūrdhni bindunā||30.47||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.48">30.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dakṣajānugataścāyaṁ sarvamātṛgaṇārcitaḥ|<br />
Anena prāṇitāḥ sarve dadate vāñchitaṁ phalam||30.48||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.49">30.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sadbhāvaḥ paramo hyeṣa mātṝṇāṁ bhairavasya ca|<br />
Tasmādenaṁ japenmantrī ya icchetsiddhimuttamām||30.49||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.50"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.50">30.50</a> <span class="pv-iast">Rudraśaktisamāveśo nityamatra pratiṣṭhitaḥ|<br />
Yasmādeṣā parā śaktirbhedenānyena kīrtitā||30.50||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.51"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.51">30.51</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yāvatyaḥ siddhayastantre tāḥ sarvāḥ kurute tviyam|<br />
Aṅgavaktrāṇi cāpyasyāḥ prāgvatsvaraniyogataḥ||30.51||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.52"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.52">30.52</a> <span class="pv-iast">Daṇḍo jīvastriśūlaṁ ca dakṣāṅgulyaparastanau|<br />
Nābhikaṇṭhau marudrudrau visargaḥ satriśūlakaḥ||30.52||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.53"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.53">30.53</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sarvayoginicakrāṇāmadhipo'yamudāhṛtaḥ|<br />
Asyāpyuccāraṇādeva saṁvittiḥ syātparoditā||30.53||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.54"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.54">30.54</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mahācaṇḍeti tu yogeśvaṛ ityaṣṭavarṇakam|<br />
Navārṇeyaṁ guptatarā sadbhāvaḥ kālakarṣiṇī||30.54||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.55"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.55">30.55</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīḍāmare mahāyāge parātparataroditā|<br />
Sudhācchedakaṣaṇṭhādyairbījaṁ chedakamasvaram||30.55||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.56"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.56">30.56</a> <span class="pv-iast">Adhyardhārṇā kālarātriḥ kṣurikā mālinīmate|<br />
Śatāvartanayā hyasyā jāyate mūrdhni vedanā||30.56||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.57"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.57">30.57</a> <span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ pratyayamālocya mṛtyujiddhyānamāśrayet|<br />
Naināṁ samuccareddevi ya iccheddīrghajīvitam||30.57||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.58"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.58">30.58</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dvirdaṇḍāgnī śūlanabhaḥprāṇāśchettranalau tathā|<br />
Kūṭāgnī savisargāśca pañcāpyete'tha pañcasu||30.58||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.59"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.59">30.59</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vyomasviti śivenoktaṁ tantrasadbhāvaśāsane|<br />
Chedinī kṣurikeyaṁ syādyayā yojayate pare||30.59||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.60"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.60">30.60</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bindvindvanalakūṭāgnimarutṣaṣṭhasvarairyutam|<br />
Āpādatalamūrdhāntaṁ smaredastramidaṁ jvalat||30.60||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.61"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.61">30.61</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kuñcanaṁ cāṅgulīnāṁ tu kartavyaṁ codanaṁ tataḥ|<br />
Jānvādiparacakrāntaṁ cakrāccakraṁ tu kuñcayet||30.61||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.62"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.62">30.62</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kathitaṁ sarahasyaṁ tu sadyonirvāṇadaṁ param|<br />
Athocyate brahmavidyā sadyaḥpratyayadāyinī||30.62||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.63"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.63">30.63</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śivaḥ śrībhūtirājo yāmasmabhyaṁ pratyapādayat|<br />
Sarveṣāmeva bhūtānāṁ maraṇe samupasthite||30.63||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.64"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.64">30.64</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yayā paṭhitayotkramya jīvo yāti nirañjanam|<br />
Yā jñānino'pi sampūrṇakṛtyasyāpi śrutā satī||30.64||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.65"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.65">30.65</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prāṇādicchedajāṁ mṛtyuvyathāṁ sadyo vyapohati|<br />
Yāmākarṇya mahāmohavivaśo'pi kramādgataḥ||30.65||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.66"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.66">30.66</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prabodhaṁ vaktṛsāmmukhyamabhyeti rabhasātsvayam|<br />
Paramapadāttvamihāgāḥ sanātanastvaṁ jahīhi dehāntam||30.66||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.67"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.67">30.67</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pādāṅguṣṭhādi vibho nibandhanaṁ bandhanaṁ hyugram|<br />
Āryāvākyamidaṁ pūrvaṁ bhuvanākhyaiḥ padairbhavet||30.67||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.68"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.68">30.68</a> <span class="pv-iast">Gulphānte jānugataṁ jatrusthaṁ bandhanaṁ tathā meḍhre|<br />
Jahihi puramagryamadhyaṁ hṛtpadmāttvaṁ samuttiṣṭha||30.68||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.69"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.69">30.69</a> <span class="pv-iast">Etāvadbhiḥ padairetadāryāvākyaṁ dvitīyakam|<br />
Haṁsa hayagrīva vibho sadāśivastvaṁ paro'si jīvākhyaḥ||30.69||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.70"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.70">30.70</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ravisomavahnisaṅghaṭṭabindudeho hahaha samutkrāma|<br />
Tṛtīyamāryāvākyaṁ prāksaṅkhyairekādhikaiḥ padaiḥ||30.70||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.71"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.71">30.71</a> <span class="pv-iast">Haṁsamahāmantramayaḥ sanātanastvaṁ śubhāśubhāpekṣī|<br />
Maṇḍalamadhyaniviṣṭaḥ śaktimahāsetukāraṇamahārthaḥ||30.71||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.72"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.72">30.72</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kamalobhayaviniviṣṭaḥ prabodhamāyāhi devatādeha|<br />
Āryāvākyamidaṁ sārdhaṁ rudrasaṅkhyapaderitam||30.72||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.73"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.73">30.73</a> <span class="pv-iast">Niḥśvāse tvapaśabdasya sthāne'styupa iti dhvaniḥ|<br />
Ajñānāttvaṁ baddhaḥ prabodhitottiṣṭha devāde||30.73||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.74"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.74">30.74</a> <span class="pv-iast">Etatpañcamamāryārdhavākyaṁ syātsaptabhiḥ padaiḥ|<br />
Vraja tālumāhvayāntaṁ hyauḍambaraghaṭṭitaṁ mahādvāram||30.74||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.75"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.75">30.75</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prāpya prayāhi haṁho haṁho vā vāmadevapadam|<br />
Āryyāvākyamidaṁ ṣaṣṭhaṁ syāccaturdaśabhiḥ padaiḥ||30.75||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.76"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.76">30.76</a> <span class="pv-iast">Granthīśvara paramātmanśānta mahātālurandhramāsādya|<br />
Utkrama he deheśvara nirañjanaṁ śivapadaṁ prayāhyāśu||30.76||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.77"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.77">30.77</a> <span class="pv-iast">Āryāvākyaṁ saptamaṁ syāttaccaturdaśabhiḥ padaiḥ|<br />
Prabhañjanastvamityevaṁ pāṭho niḥśvāsaśāsane||30.77||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.78"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.78">30.78</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ākramya madhyamārgaṁ prāṇāpānau samāhṛtya|<br />
Dharmādharmau tyaktvā nārāyaṇa yāhi śāntāntam||30.78||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.79"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.79">30.79</a> <span class="pv-iast">Āryāvākyamidaṁ proktamaṣṭamaṁ navabhiḥ padaiḥ|<br />
He brahmanhe viṣṇo he rudra śivo'si vāsudevastvam||30.79||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.80"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.80">30.80</a> <span class="pv-iast">Agnīṣomasanātanamṛtpiṇḍaṁ jahihi he mahākāśa|<br />
Etadbhuvanasaṅkhyātairāryyāvākyaṁ prakīrtitam||30.80||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.81"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.81">30.81</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sanātma tripiṇḍamiti mahākośamiti sthitam|<br />
Padatrayaṁ tu niḥśvāsamukuṭottarakādiṣu||30.81||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.82"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.82">30.82</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aṅguṣṭhamātramamalamāvaraṇaṁ jahihi he mahāsūkṣma|<br />
Āryyāvākyamidaṁ ṣaḍbhiḥ padairdaśamamucyate||30.82||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.83"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.83">30.83</a> <span class="pv-iast">Alaṁ dviriti sūkṣmaṁ cetyevaṁ śrīmukuṭottare|<br />
Puruṣastvaṁ prakṛtimayairbaddho'haṅkāratantunā bandhaiḥ||30.83||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.84"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.84">30.84</a> <span class="pv-iast">Abhavābhava nityodita paramātmaṁstyaja sarāgamadhvānam|<br />
Etattrayodaśapadaṁ syādāryāvākyamuttamam||30.84||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.85"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.85">30.85</a> <span class="pv-iast">Hrīṁhūmmantraśarīramavilambamāśu tvamehi dehāntam|<br />
Āryārdhavākyametatsyāddvādaśaṁ ṣaṭpadaṁ param||30.85||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.86"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.86">30.86</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadidaṁ guṇabhūtamayaṁ tyaja sva ṣāṭkośikaṁ piṇḍam|<br />
Syāttrayodaśamāryārdhaṁ padaiḥ saptabhirīdṛśam||30.86||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.87"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.87">30.87</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mā dehaṁ bhūtamayaṁ pragṛhyatāṁ śāśvataṁ mahādeham|<br />
Āryārdhavākyaṁ tāvadbhiḥ padairetaccaturdaśam||30.87||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.88"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.88">30.88</a> <span class="pv-iast">Maṇḍalamamalamanantaṁ tridhā sthitaṁ gaccha bhittvaitat|<br />
Āryārdhavākyamaṣṭābhiḥ padaiḥ pañcadaśaṁ tvidam||30.88||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.89"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.89">30.89</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sakaleyaṁ brahmavidyā syātpañcadaśabhiḥ sphuṭaiḥ|<br />
Vākyaiḥ pañcākṣaraistvasyā niṣkalā parikīrtyate||30.89||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.90"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.90">30.90</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prativākyaṁ yayādyantayojitā paripaṭhyate|<br />
Tāro māyā vedakalo mātṛtāro navātmakaḥ||30.90||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.91"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.91">30.91</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti pañcākṣarāṇi syuḥ proktavyāptyanusārataḥ|<br />
Binduprāṇāmṛtajalaṁ marutṣaṣṭhasvarānvitam||30.91||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.92"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.92">30.92</a> <span class="pv-iast">Etena śaktyuccārasthabījenālabhyate paśuḥ|<br />
Kṛtadīkṣāvidhiḥ pūrvaṁ brahmaghno'pi viśuddhyati||30.92||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.93"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.93">30.93</a> <span class="pv-iast">Laghutvena tulāśuddhiḥ sadyaḥpratyayakāriṇī|<br />
Tāraḥ śamarayaiḥ piṇḍo natiśca caturarṇakam||30.93||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.94"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.94">30.94</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śākinīstobhanaṁ marma hṛdayaṁ jīvitaṁ tvidam|<br />
Ṣaṣṭhaprāṇatrikūṭordhvabāhuśūlākhyabindubhiḥ||30.94||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.95"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.95">30.95</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anackanāsādhovaktracandrakhaṇḍaiśca maṇḍitam|<br />
Hṛdayaṁ bhairavākhyaṁ tu sarvasaṁhārakārakam||30.95||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.96"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.96">30.96</a> <span class="pv-iast">Agnimaṇḍalamadhyasthabhairavānalatāpitāḥ|<br />
Vaśamāyānti śākinyaḥ sthānametena ceddahet||30.96||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.97"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.97">30.97</a> <span class="pv-iast">Visarjayettāḥ prathamamanyathā cchidrayanti tāḥ|<br />
Hrīṁ klīṁ vleṁ kleṁ ebhirvarṇairdvādaśasvarabhūṣitaiḥ||30.97||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.98"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.98">30.98</a> <span class="pv-iast">Priyamelāpanaṁ nāma hṛdayaṁ sampuṭaṁ japet|<br />
Pratyekamathavā dvābhyāṁ sarvairvā vidhiruttamaḥ||30.98||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.99"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.99">30.99</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tulāmelakayāgaḥ śrītantrasadbhāvaśāsane|<br />
Ya uktaḥ śambhunāthena sa mayā darśitaḥ kramāt||30.99||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.100"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.100">30.100</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha vittavihīnānāṁ prapannānāṁ ca tattvataḥ|<br />
Deśakālādidoṣeṇa na tathādhyavasāyinām||30.100||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.101"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.101">30.101</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prakartavyā yathā dīkṣā śrīsantatyāgamoditā|<br />
Kathyate hāṭakeśānapātālādhipacoditā||30.101||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.102"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.102">30.102</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīnātha ārya bhagavannetattritayaṁ hi kanda ādhāre|<br />
Varuṇo macchando bhagavatta iti trayamidaṁ hṛdaye||30.102||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.103"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.103">30.103</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dharmādivargasañjñāścatvāraḥ kaṇṭhadeśagāḥ pūjyāḥ|<br />
Hrīṁśrīmpūrvāḥ sarve sambodhajuṣaśca pādaśabdāntāḥ||30.103||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.104"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.104">30.104</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mūrdhatale vidyātrayamuktaṁ bhāvyatha mano'bhiyogena|<br />
Kusumairānandairvā bhāvanayā vāpi kevalayā||30.104||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.105"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.105">30.105</a> <span class="pv-iast">Guruṇā tattvavidā kila śiṣyo yadi mokṣamātrakṛtahṛdayaḥ|<br />
Mokṣaikadānacaturā dīkṣā seyaṁ paropaniṣaduktā||30.105||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.106"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.106">30.106</a> <span class="pv-iast">Etaddīkṣādīkṣita etadvidyātrayaṁ smaranhṛdaye|<br />
Bāhyārcādi vinaiva hi vrajati paraṁ dhāma dehānte||30.106||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.107"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.107">30.107</a> <span class="pv-iast">Praṇavo māyā bindurvarṇatrayamāditaḥ kuryāt|<br />
Padapañcakasya sambodhanayuktasyāgnidayitānte||30.107||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.108"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.108">30.108</a> <span class="pv-iast">Siddhasādhani tatpūrvaṁ śabdabrahmasvarūpiṇi|<br />
Samastabandhaśabdena sahitaṁ ca nikṛntani||30.108||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.109"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.109">30.109</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bodhani śivasadbhāvajananyāmantritaṁ ca tat|<br />
Pañcāṣṭarandhratryaṣṭārṇakrameṇa padapañcakam||30.109||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.110"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.110">30.110</a> <span class="pv-iast">Khapañcārṇā parabrahmavidyeyaṁ mokṣadā śivā|<br />
Anuttarecche ghāntaśca satrayodaśasusvaraḥ||30.110||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.111"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.111">30.111</a> <span class="pv-iast">Asya varṇatrayasyānte tvantaḥsthānāṁ catuṣṭayam|<br />
Vargādyaśvau tryasrabinduyukpānto'rṇatrayādataḥ||30.111||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.112"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.112">30.112</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mahāhāṭakaśabdādyamīśvarītyarṇasaptakam|<br />
Āmantritaṁ kṣamasveti tryarṇaṁ pāpāntakāriṇi||30.112||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.113"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.113">30.113</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ṣaḍarṇaṁ pāpaśabdādivimohanipadaṁ tataḥ|<br />
Pāpaṁ hana dhuna dvirdvirdaśārṇaṁ padamīdṛśam||30.113||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.114"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.114">30.114</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pañcamyantaṁ ṣaḍarṇaṁ syādrudraśaktivaśāditi|<br />
Tata ekākṣaraṁ yattadvisargabrahma kīrtitam||30.114||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.115"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.115">30.115</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadanackatakāreṇa sahaikībhāvataḥ paṭhet|<br />
Randhrābdhivarṇā vidyeyaṁ dīkṣāvidyeti kīrtitā||30.115||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.116"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.116">30.116</a> <span class="pv-iast">Māyārṇañca pare brahme caturvidye padatrayam|<br />
Aṣṭārṇamatha pañcārṇaṁ yogadhāriṇisañjñitam||30.116||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.117"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.117">30.117</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ātmāntarātmaparamātmarūpaṁ ca padatrayam|<br />
Ekārāntaṁ bodhanasthaṁ daśārṇaṁ parikīrtitam||30.117||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.118"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.118">30.118</a> <span class="pv-iast">Rudraśaktīti vedārṇaṁ syādrudradayite'tha me|<br />
Pāpaṁ dahadahetyeṣā dvādaśārṇā catuṣpadī||30.118||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.119"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.119">30.119</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saumye sadāśive yugmaṁ ṣaṭkaṁ bindviṣusāvahā|<br />
Sārdhavarṇacatuṣkaṁ tadityeṣā samayāpahā||30.119||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.120"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.120">30.120</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vidyā sārdhārṇakhaśarasaṅkhyā sā pārameśvarī|<br />
Etadvidyātrayaṁ śrīmadbhūtirājo nyarūpayat||30.120||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.121"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.121">30.121</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yaḥ sākṣādabhajacchrīmāñśrīkaṇṭho mānuṣīṁ tanum|<br />
Atra vīryaṁ puraivoktaṁ sarvatrānusaredguruḥ||30.121||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.122"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.122">30.122</a> <span class="pv-iast">Arthabījapraveśāntaruccārādyanusārataḥ|<br />
Na hi tatkiñcanāpyasti yatpurā na nirūpitam||30.122||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v30.123"><a class="ta-num" href="#v30.123">30.123</a> <span class="pv-iast">Niṣphalā punaruktistu nāsmabhyaṁ jātu rocate|<br />
Ityevaṁ mantravidyādisvarūpamupavarṇitam||30.123||</span></p>
