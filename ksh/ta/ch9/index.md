# Глава 9 — Tattvādhvā

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">314 строф · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [перевод главы у источника](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/ru/node/869)</p>

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Глава 8 — Deśādhvā](/ksh/ta/ch8/) · [Глава 10 — Tattvabhedanam →](/ksh/ta/ch10/)</p>

<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloke navamamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.1">9.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha tattvapravibhāgo vistarataḥ kathyate kramaprāptaḥ||9.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.2">9.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yānyuktāni purāṇyamūni vividhairbhedairyadeṣvanvitaṁ rūpaṁ bhāti paraṁ prakāśaniviḍaṁ devaḥ sa ekaḥ śivaḥ|<br />
Tatsvātantryarasātpunaḥ śivapadādbhede vibhāte paraṁ yadrūpaṁ bahudhānugāmi tadidaṁ tattvaṁ vibhoḥ śāsane||9.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.3">9.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathāhi kālasadanādvīrabhadrapurāntagam|<br />
Dhṛtikāṭhinyagarimādyavabhāsāddharātmatā||9.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.4">9.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ jalāditattveṣu vācyaṁ yāvatsadāśive|<br />
Svasminkārye'tha dharmaughe yadvāpi svasadṛgguṇe||9.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.5">9.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Āste sāmānyakalpena tananādvyāptṛbhāvataḥ|<br />
Tattattvaṁ kramaśaḥ pṛthvīpradhānaṁ puṁśivādayaḥ||9.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.6">9.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dehānāṁ bhuvanānāṁ ca na prasaṅgastato bhavet|<br />
Śrīmanmataṅgaśāstrādau taduktaṁ parameśinā||9.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.7">9.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatraiṣāṁ darśyate dṛṣṭaḥ siddhayogīśvarīmate|<br />
Kāryakāraṇabhāvo yaḥ śivecchāparikalpitaḥ||9.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.8">9.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vastutaḥ sarvabhāvānāṁ karteśānaḥ paraḥ śivaḥ|<br />
Asvatantrasya kartṛtvaṁ nahi jātūpapadyate||9.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.9">9.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svatantratā ca cinmātravapuṣaḥ parameśituḥ|<br />
Svatantraṁ ca jaḍaṁ ceti tadanyonyaṁ virudhyate||9.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.10">9.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jāḍyaṁ pramātṛtantratvaṁ svātmasiddhimapi prati|<br />
Na kartṛtvādṛte cānyat kāraṇatvaṁ hi labhyate||9.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.11">9.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasminsati hi tadbhāva ityapekṣaikajīvitam|<br />
Nirapekṣeṣu bhāveṣu svātmaniṣṭhatayā katham||9.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.12">9.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sa pūrvamatha paścātsa iti cetpūrvapaścimau|<br />
Svabhāve'natiriktau cetsama ityavaśiṣyate||9.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.13">9.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bījamaṅkura ityasmin satattve hetutadvatoḥ|<br />
Ghaṭaḥ paṭaśceti bhavet kāryakāraṇatā na kim||9.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.14">9.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bījamaṅkurapatrāditayā pariṇameta cet|<br />
Atatsvabhāvavapuṣaḥ sa svabhāvo na yujyate||9.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.15">9.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sa tatsvabhāva iti cettarhi bījāṅkurau nije|<br />
Tāvatyeva na viśrāntau tadanyātyantasambhavāt||9.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.16">9.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tataśca citrākāro'sau tāvānkaścitprasajyate|<br />
Astu cet na jaḍe'nyonyaviruddhākārasambhavaḥ||9.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.17">9.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Krameṇa citrākāro'stu jaḍaḥ kiṁ nu viruddhyate|<br />
Kramo'kramo vā bhāvasya na svarūpādhiko bhavet||9.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.18">9.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathopalambhamātraṁ tau upalambhaśca kiṁ tathā|<br />
Upalabdhāpi vijñānasvabhāvo yo'sya so'pi hi||9.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.19">9.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kramopalambharūpatvātkrameṇopalabheta cet|<br />
Tasya tarhi kramaḥ ko'sau tadanyānupalambhataḥ||9.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.20">9.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svabhāva iti cennāsau svarūpādadhiko bhavet|<br />
Svarūpānadhikasyāpi kramasya svasvabhāvataḥ||9.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.21">9.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svātantryādbhāsanaṁ syāccet kimanyadbrūmahe vayam|<br />
Itthaṁ śrīśiva evaikaḥ karteti paribhāṣyate||9.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.22">9.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kartṛtvaṁ caitadetasya tathāmātrāvabhāsanam|<br />
Tathāvabhāsanaṁ cāsti kāryakāraṇabhāvagam||9.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.23">9.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathā hi ghaṭasāhityaṁ paṭasyāpyavabhāsate|<br />
Tathā ghaṭānantaratā kintu sā niyamojjhitā||9.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.24">9.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ato yanniyamenaiva yasmādābhātyanantaram|<br />
Tattasya kāraṇaṁ brūmaḥ sati rūpānvaye'dhike||9.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.25">9.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Niyamaśca tathārūpabhāsanāmātrasārakaḥ|<br />
Bījādaṅkura ityevaṁ bhāsanaṁ nahi sarvadā||9.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.26">9.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yogīcchānantarodbhūtatathābhūtāṅkuro yataḥ|<br />
Iṣṭe tathāvidhākāre niyamo bhāsate yataḥ||9.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.27">9.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svapne ghaṭapaṭādīnāṁ hetutadvatsvabhāvatā|<br />
Bhāsate niyamenaiva bādhāśūnyena tāvati||9.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.28">9.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tato yāvati yādrūpyānniyamo bādhavarjitaḥ|<br />
Bhāti tāvati tādrūpyāddṛḍhahetuphalātmatā||9.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.29">9.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathābhūte ca niyame hetutadvattvakāriṇi|<br />
Vastutaścinmayasyaiva hetutā taddhi sarvagam||9.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.30">9.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ata eva ghaṭodbhūtau sāmagrī heturucyate|<br />
Sāmagrī ca samagrāṇāṁ yadyekaṁ neṣyate vapuḥ||9.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.31">9.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Hetubhedānna bhedaḥ syāt phale taccāsamañjasam|<br />
Yadyasyānuvidhatte tāmanvayavyatirekitām||9.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.32">9.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tattasya hetu cetso'yaṁ kuṇṭhatarko na naḥ priyaḥ|<br />
Samagrāśca yathā daṇḍasūtracakrakarādayaḥ||9.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.33">9.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dūrāśca bhāvinaścetthaṁ hetutveneti manmahe|<br />
Yadi tatra bhavenmerurbhaviṣyanvāpi kaścana||9.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.34">9.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na jāyeta ghaṭo nūnaṁ tatpratyūhavyapohitaḥ|<br />
Yathā ca cakraṁ niyate deśe kāle ca hetutām||9.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.35">9.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yāti karkisumervādyāstadvatsvasthāvadhi sthitāḥ|<br />
Tathā ca teṣāṁ hetunāṁ saṁyojanaviyojane||9.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.36">9.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Niyate śiva evaikaḥ svatantraḥ kartṛtāmiyāt|<br />
Kumbhakārasya yā saṁvic cakradaṇḍādiyojane||9.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.37">9.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śiva eva hi sā yasmātsaṁvidaḥ kā viśiṣṭatā|<br />
Kaumbhakārī tu saṁvittiravacchedāvabhāsanāt||9.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.38">9.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhinnakalpā yadi kṣepyā daṇḍacakrādimadhyataḥ|<br />
Tasmādekaikanirmāṇe śivo viśvaikavigrahaḥ||9.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.39">9.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Karteti puṁsaḥ kartṛtvābhimāno'pi vibhoḥ kṛtiḥ|<br />
Ata eva tathābhānaparamārthatayā sthiteḥ||9.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.40">9.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kāryakāraṇabhāvasya loke śāstre ca citratā|<br />
Māyāto'vyaktakalayoriti rauravasaṅgrahe||9.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.41">9.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīpūrve tu kalātattvādavyaktamiti kathyate|<br />
Tata eva niśākhyānātkalībhūtādaliṅgakam||9.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.42">9.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti vyākhyāsmadukte'sminsati nyāye'tiniṣphalā|<br />
Loke ca gomayātkīṭāt saṅkalpātsvapnataḥ smṛteḥ||9.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.43">9.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yogīcchāto dravyamantraprabhāvādeśca vṛścikaḥ|<br />
Anya eva sa cet kāmaṁ kutaścitsvaviśeṣataḥ||9.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.44">9.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sa tu sarvatra tulyastatparāmarśaikyamasti tu|<br />
Tata eva svarūpe'pi krame'pyanyādṛśī sthitiḥ||9.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.45">9.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śāstreṣu yujyate citrāttathābhāvasvabhāvataḥ|<br />
Puṁrāgavitkalākālamāyā jñānottare kramāt||9.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.46">9.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Niyatirnāsti vairiñce kalordhve niyatiḥ śrutā|<br />
Puṁrāgavittrayādūrdhvaṁ kālaniyatisampuṭam||9.46||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.47">9.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kalā māyeti kathitaḥ kramaḥ kiraṇaśāstragaḥ|<br />
Pumānniyatyā kālaśca rāgavidyākalānvitaḥ||9.47||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.48">9.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ityeṣa krama uddiṣṭo mātaṅge pārameśvare|<br />
Kāryakāraṇabhāvīye tattva itthaṁ vyavasthite||9.48||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.49">9.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīpūrvaśāstre kathitāṁ vacmaḥ kāraṇakalpanām|<br />
Śivaḥ svatantradṛgrūpaḥ pañcaśaktisunirbharaḥ||9.49||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.50"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.50">9.50</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svātantryabhāsitabhidā pañcadhā pravibhajyate|<br />
Cidānandeṣaṇājñānakriyāṇāṁ susphuṭatvataḥ||9.50||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.51"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.51">9.51</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śivaśaktisadeśānavidyākhyaṁ tattvapañcakam|<br />
Ekaikatrāpi tattve'smin sarvaśaktisunirbhare||9.51||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.52"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.52">9.52</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tattatprādhānyayogena sa sa bhedo nirūpyate|<br />
Tathāhi svasvatantratvaparipūrṇatayā vibhuḥ||9.52||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.53"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.53">9.53</a> <span class="pv-iast">Niḥsaṅkhyairbahubhī rūpairbhātyavacchedavarjanāt|<br />
Śāmbhavāḥ śaktijā mantramaheśā mantranāyakāḥ||9.53||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.54"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.54">9.54</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mantrā iti viśuddhāḥ syuramī pañca gaṇāḥ kramāt|<br />
Svasminsvasmin gaṇe bhāti yadyadrūpaṁ samanvayi||9.54||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.55"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.55">9.55</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadeṣu tattvamityuktaṁ kālāgnyāderdharādivat|<br />
Tena yatprāhurākhyānasādṛśyena viḍambitāḥ||9.55||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.56"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.56">9.56</a> <span class="pv-iast">Gurūpāsāṁ vinaivāttapustakābhīṣṭadṛṣṭayaḥ|<br />
Brahmā nivṛttyadhipatiḥ pṛthaktattvaṁ na gaṇyate||9.56||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.57"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.57">9.57</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sadāśivādyāstu pṛthaggaṇyanta iti ko nayaḥ|<br />
Brahmaviṣṇuhareśānasuśivānāśritātmani||9.57||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.58"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.58">9.58</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ṣaṭke kāraṇasañjñe'rdhajaratīyamiyaṁ kutaḥ|<br />
Iti tanmūlato dhvastaṁ gaṇitaṁ nahi kāraṇam||9.58||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.59"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.59">9.59</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathā pṛthivyadhipatirnṛpastattvāntaraṁ nahi|<br />
Tathā tattatkaleśānaḥ pṛthaktattvāntaraṁ katham||9.59||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.60"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.60">9.60</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadevaṁ pañcakamidaṁ śuddho'dhvā paribhāṣyate|<br />
Tatra sākṣācchivecchaiva kartryābhāsitabhedikā||9.60||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.61"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.61">9.61</a> <span class="pv-iast">Īśvarecchāvaśakṣubdhabhogalolikacidgaṇān|<br />
Saṁvibhaktumaghoreśaḥ sṛjatīha sitetaram||9.61||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.62"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.62">9.62</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aṇūnāṁ lolikā nāma niṣkarmā yābhilāṣitā|<br />
Apūrṇammanyatājñānaṁ malaṁ sāvacchidojjhitā||9.62||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.63"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.63">9.63</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yogyatāmātramevaitadbhāvyavacchedasaṅgrahe|<br />
Malastenāsya na pṛthaktattvabhāvo'sti rāgavat||9.63||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.64"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.64">9.64</a> <span class="pv-iast">Niravacchedakarmāṁśamātrāvacchedatastu sā|<br />
Rāgaḥ puṁsi dhiyo dharmaḥ karmabhedavicitratā||9.64||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.65"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.65">9.65</a> <span class="pv-iast">Apūrṇamanyatā ceyaṁ tathārūpāvabhāsanam|<br />
Svatantrasya śivasyecchā ghaṭarūpo yathā ghaṭaḥ||9.65||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.66"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.66">9.66</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svātmapracchādanecchaiva vastubhūtastathā malaḥ|<br />
Yathaivāvyatiriktasya dharāderbhāvitātmatā||9.66||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.67"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.67">9.67</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathaivāsyeti śāstreṣu vyatiriktaḥ sthito malaḥ|<br />
Vyatiriktaḥ svatantrastu na ko'pi śakaṭādivat||9.67||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.68"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.68">9.68</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatsadvitīyā sāśuddhiḥ śivamuktāṇugā na kim|<br />
Malasya roddhrī kāpyasti śaktiḥ sā cāpyamuktagā||9.68||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.69"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.69">9.69</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti nyāyojjhito vādaḥ śraddhāmātraikakalpitaḥ|<br />
Roddhrī śaktirjaḍasyāsau svayaṁ naiva pravartate||9.69||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.70"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.70">9.70</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svayaṁ pravṛttau viśvaṁ syāttathā ceśanikā pramā|<br />

Kṣubhitaṁ śabdatanmātraṁ citrākārāḥ śrutīrdadhat||9.283||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.284"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.284">9.284</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nabhaḥ śabdo'vakāśātmā vācyādhyāsasaho yataḥ|<br />
Tadetatsparśatanmātrayogātprakṣobhamāgatam||9.284||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.285"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.285">9.285</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vāyutāmeti tenātra śabdasparśobhayātmatā|<br />
Anye tvāhurdhvaniḥ khaikaguṇastadapi yujyate||9.285||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.286"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.286">9.286</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yato vāyurnijaṁ rūpaṁ labhate na vināmbarāt|<br />
Uttarottarabhūteṣu pūrvapūrvasthitiryataḥ||9.286||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.287"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.287">9.287</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tata eva marudvyomnoraviyogo mithaḥ smṛtaḥ|<br />
Śabdasparśau tu rūpeṇa samaṁ prakṣobhamāgatau||9.287||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.288"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.288">9.288</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tejastattvaṁ tribhirdharmaiḥ prāhuḥ pūrvavadeva tat|<br />
Taistribhiḥ sarasairāpaḥ sagandhairbhūriti kramaḥ||9.288||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.289"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.289">9.289</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatra pratyakṣataḥ siddho dharādirguṇasañcayaḥ|<br />
Nahi gandhādidharmaughavyatiriktā vibhāti bhūḥ||9.289||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.290"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.290">9.290</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathā guṇaguṇidvaitavādināmekamapyadaḥ|<br />
Citraṁ rūpaṁ paṭe bhāti kramāddharmāstathā bhuvi||9.290||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.291"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.291">9.291</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathā ca vistṛte vastre yugapadbhāti citratā|<br />
Tathaiva yogināṁ dharmasāmastyenāvabhāti bhūḥ||9.291||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.292"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.292">9.292</a> <span class="pv-iast">Gandhādiśabdaparyantacitrarūpā dharā tataḥ|<br />
Upāyabhedādbhātyeṣā kramākramavibhāgataḥ||9.292||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.293"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.293">9.293</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tata eva kramavyaktikṛto dhībheda ucyate|<br />
Ṣaṣṭhīprayogo dhībhedādbhedyabhedakatā tathā||9.293||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.294"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.294">9.294</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tena dharmātirikto'tra dharmī nāma na kaścana|<br />
Tatrānekaprakārāḥ syurgandharūparasāḥ kṣitau||9.294||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.295"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.295">9.295</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṁsparśaḥ pākajo'nuṣṇāśītaḥ śabdo vicitrakaḥ|<br />
Śauklyaṁ mādhuryaśītatve citrāḥ śabdāśca vāriṇi||9.295||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.296"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.296">9.296</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śuklabhāsvaratoṣṇatvaṁ citrāḥ śabdāśca pāvake|<br />
Apākajaścāśītoṣṇo dhvaniścitraśca mārute||9.296||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.297"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.297">9.297</a> <span class="pv-iast">Varṇātmako dhvaniḥ śabdapratibimbānyathāmbare|<br />
Yattu na sparśavaddharmaḥ śabda ityādi bhaṇyate||9.297||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.298"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.298">9.298</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kāṇādaistatsvapratītiviruddhaṁ kena gṛhyatām|<br />
Paṭahe dhvanirityeva bhātyabādhitameva yat||9.298||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.299"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.299">9.299</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na ca hetutvamātreṇa tadādānatvavedanāt|<br />
Śrotraṁ cāsmanmate'haṅkṛtkāraṇaṁ tatra tatra tat||9.299||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.300"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.300">9.300</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vṛttibhāgīti taddeśaṁ śabdaṁ gṛhṇātyalaṁ tathā|<br />
Yastvāha śrotramākāśaṁ karṇasaṁyogabheditam||9.300||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.301"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.301">9.301</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śabdajaḥ śabda āgatya śabdabuddhiṁ prasūyate|<br />
Tasya mande'pi murajadhvanāvākarṇake sati||9.301||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.302"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.302">9.302</a> <span class="pv-iast">Amutra śrutireṣeti dūre saṁvedanaṁ katham|<br />
Nahi śabdajaśabdasya dūrādūraravoditeḥ||9.302||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.303"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.303">9.303</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrotrākāśagatasyāsti dūrādūrasvabhāvatā|<br />
Na cāsau prathamaḥ śabdastāvadvyāpīti yujyate||9.303||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.304"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.304">9.304</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatrasthaiḥ saha tīvrātmā śrūyamāṇastvanena tu|<br />
Kathaṁ śrūyeta mandaḥ sannahi dharmāntarāśrayaḥ||9.304||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.305"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.305">9.305</a> <span class="pv-iast">Etaccānyairapākāri bahudheti vṛthā punaḥ|<br />
Nāyastaṁ patitāghātadāne ko hi na paṇḍitaḥ||9.305||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.306"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.306">9.306</a> <span class="pv-iast">Amīṣāṁ tu dharādīnāṁ yāvāṁstattvagaṇaḥ purā|<br />
Guṇādhikatayā tiṣṭhanvyāptā tāvānprakāśate||9.306||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.307"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.307">9.307</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vyāpyavyāpakatā yaiṣā tattvānāṁ darśitā kila|<br />
Sā guṇādhikyataḥ siddhā na hetutvānna lāghavāt||9.307||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.308"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.308">9.308</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ahetunāpi rāgo hi vyāpto vidyādinā sphuṭam|<br />
Tadvinā na bhavedyattadvyāptamityucyate yataḥ||9.308||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.309"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.309">9.309</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na lāghavaṁ ca nāmāsti kiñcidatra svadarśane|<br />
Guṇādhikyādato jñeyā vyāpyavyāpakatā sphuṭā||9.309||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.310"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.310">9.310</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yo hi yasmādguṇotkṛṣṭaḥ sa tasmādūrdhva ucyate|<br />
Ūrdhvatā vyāptṛtā śrīmanmālinīvijaye sphuṭā||9.310||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.311"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.311">9.311</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ataḥ śivatvātprabhṛti prakāśatāsvarūpamādāya nijātmani dhruvam|<br />
Samastatattvāvalidharmasañcayairvibhāti bhūrvyāptṛtayā sthitairalam||9.311||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.312"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.312">9.312</a> <span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ jalāderapi śaktitattvaparyantadhāmno vapurasti tādṛk|<br />
Kintūttaraṁ śaktitayaiva tattvaṁ pūrvaṁ tu taddharmatayeti bhedaḥ||9.312||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.313"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.313">9.313</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anuttaraprakriyāyāṁ vaitatyena pradarśitam|<br />
Etattasmāttataḥ paśyedvistarārthī vivecakaḥ||9.313||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v9.314"><a class="ta-num" href="#v9.314">9.314</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti tattvasvarūpasya kṛtaṁ samyak prakāśanam||9.314||</span></p>
