# Глава 14 — Dīkṣopakramaṇam

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">46 строф · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [перевод главы у источника](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/ru/node/878)</p>

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Глава 13 — Śaktipātatirohitī](/ksh/ta/ch13/) · [Глава 15 — Dīkṣā sāmayī →](/ksh/ta/ch15/)</p>

<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloke caturdaśamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.1">14.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tirobhāvasvarūpaṁ tu kathyamānaṁ vivicyatām|<br />
Svabhāvātparameśāno niyatyaniyatikramam||14.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.2">14.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Spṛśanprakāśate yena tataḥ svacchanda ucyate|<br />
Niyatiṁ karmaphalayorāśrityaiṣa maheśvaraḥ||14.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.3">14.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sṛṣṭisaṁsthitisaṁhārānvidhatte'vāntarasthitīn|<br />
Mahāsarge punaḥ sṛṣṭisaṁhārānantyaśālini||14.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.4">14.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ekaḥ sa devo viśvātmā niyatityāgataḥ prabhuḥ|<br />
Avāntare yā ca sṛṣṭiḥ sthitiścātrāpyayantritam||14.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.5">14.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nojjhatyeṣa vapustyaktaniyatiśca sthito'tra tat|<br />
Niyatyaiva yadā caiṣa svarūpācchādanakramāt||14.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.6">14.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhuṅkte duḥkhavimohādi tadā karmaphalakramaḥ|<br />
Tyaktvā tu niyamaṁ kārmaṁ duḥkhamohaparītatām||14.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.7">14.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bibhāsayiṣurāste'yaṁ tirodhāne'napekṣakaḥ|<br />
Yathā prakāśasvātantryātpratibuddho'pyabuddhavat||14.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.8">14.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Āste tadvadanuttīrṇo'pyuttīrṇa iva ceṣṭate|<br />
Yathā ca buddhastāṁ mūḍhaceṣṭāṁ kurvannapi dviṣan||14.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.9">14.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Hṛdyāste mūḍha evaṁ hi prabuddhānāṁ viceṣṭitam|<br />
Śrīvidyādhipatiścāha mānastotre tadīdṛśam||14.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.10">14.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ye yauṣmāke śāsanamārge kṛtadīkṣāḥ saṅgacchanto mohavaśādvipratipattim|<br />
Nūnaṁ teṣāṁ nāsti bhavadbhānuniyogaḥ saṅkocaḥ kiṁ sūryakaraistāmarasānām||14.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.11">14.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jñātajñeyā dhātṛpadasthā api santo ye tvanmārgātkāpathagāste'pi na samyak|<br />
Prāyasteṣāṁ laiṅgikabuddhyādisamuttho mithyābodhaḥ sarpavasādīpajakalpaḥ||14.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.12">14.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yasmādviddhaṁ sūtakamukhyena nu tāmraṁ tadyadbhūyaḥ svāṁ prakṛtiṁ no samupeyāt|<br />
No taiḥ pītaṁ bhūtalasaṁsthairamṛtaṁ tadyeṣāṁ tṛṭkṣudduḥkhavibādhāḥ punarasmin||14.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.13">14.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tataḥ prabuddhaceṣṭāsau mantracaryārcanādikā|<br />
Dveṣeddhāntardahatyenaṁ dāhaḥ śaṅkaiva sā yataḥ||14.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.14">14.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na cāsya karmamahimā tādṛgyenetthamāsta saḥ|<br />
Kiṁ hi tatkarma kasmādvā pūrveṇātra samo vidhiḥ||14.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.15">14.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasmātsā parameśecchā yayāyaṁ mohitastathā|<br />
Anantakālasaṁvedyaduḥkhapātratvamīhate||14.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.16">14.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatrāpi cecchāvaicitryādihāmutrobhayātmakaḥ|<br />
Duḥkhasyāpi vibhedo'sti ciraśaighryakṛtastathā||14.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.17">14.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kālakāmāndhakādīnāṁ paulastyapuravāsinām|<br />
Tathānyeṣāṁ tirobhāvastāvadduḥkho hyamutra ca||14.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.18">14.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anyo'pi ca tirobhāvaḥ samayollaṅghanātmakaḥ|<br />
Yaduktaṁ parameśena śrīmadānandagahvare||14.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.19">14.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Samayollaṅghanāddevi kravyādatvaṁ śataṁ samāḥ|<br />
Tatrāpi mandatīvrādibhedādbahuvidhaḥ kramaḥ||14.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.20">14.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svātantryācca maheśasya tirobhūto'pyasau svayam|<br />
Paradvāreṇa vābhyeti bhūyo'nugrahamapyalam||14.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.21">14.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhūyo'nugrahataḥ prāyaścittādyācaraṇe sati|<br />
Anusāreṇa dīkṣādau kṛte syācchivatāmayaḥ||14.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.22">14.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tirobhūtaḥ paretāsurapi bandhusuhṛdgurūn|<br />
Ālambya śaktipātena dīkṣādyairanugṛhyate||14.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.23">14.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatrāpi kālaśīghratvaciratvādivibhedatām|<br />
Tathaiti śaktipāto'sau yenāyāti śivātmatām||14.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.24">14.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Itthaṁ sṛṣṭisthitidhvaṁsatirobhāvamanugrahaḥ|<br />
Iti pañcasu kartṛtvaṁ śivatvaṁ saṁvidātmanaḥ||14.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.25">14.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pañcakṛtyasvatantratvasampūrṇasvātmamāninaḥ|<br />
Yogino'rcājapadhyānayogāḥ saṁsyuḥ sadoditāḥ||14.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.26">14.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Aindrajālikavṛttānte na rajyeta kadācana|<br />
Sādāśivo'pi yo bhogo bandhaḥ so'pyucitātmanām||14.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.27">14.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jñātṛtvameva śivatā svātantryaṁ tadihocyate|<br />
Kulālavattu kartṛtvaṁ na mukhyaṁ tadadhiṣṭhiteḥ||14.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.28">14.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti jñātvā grahītavyā naiva jātvapi khaṇḍanā|<br />
Śivo'haṁ cenmadicchānuvarti kiṁ na jagattviti||14.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.29">14.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mamecchāmanuvartantāmityatrāhaṁvidi sphuret|<br />
Śivo vā parameśāno dehādiratha nirmitaḥ||14.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.30">14.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śivasya tāvadastyetaddehastveṣa tathā tvayā|<br />
Kṛtaḥ kānyā dehatāsya tatkiṁ syādvācyatāpadam||14.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.31">14.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktaṁ ca siddhasantānaśrīmadūrmimahākule|<br />
Pavanabhramaṇaprāṇavikṣepādikṛtaśramāḥ||14.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.32">14.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kuhakādiṣu ye bhrāntāste bhrāntāḥ parame pade|<br />
Sarvatra bahumānena yāpyutkrāntirvimuktaye||14.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.33">14.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Proktā sā sāraśāstreṣu bhogopāyatayoditā|<br />
Yadi sarvagato devo vadotkramya kva yāsyati||14.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.34">14.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Athāsarvagatastarhi ghaṭatulyastadā bhavet|<br />
Utkrāntividhiyogo'yamekadeśena kathyate||14.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.35">14.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Niraṁśe śivatattve tu kathamutkrāntisaṅgatiḥ|<br />
Yathā dharādau vāyvante bhṛgvambvagnyupavāsakaiḥ||14.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.36">14.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ātmano yojanaṁ vyomni tadvadutkrāntivartanā|<br />
Tasmānnotkramayejjīvaṁ paratattvasamīhayā||14.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.37">14.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīpūrvaśāstre tūktaṁ yadutkrānterlakṣaṇaṁ na tat|<br />
Muktyupāyatayā kintu bhogahānyai tathaiṣaṇāt||14.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.38">14.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Japadhyānādisaṁsiddhaḥ svātantryācchaktipātataḥ|<br />
Bhogaṁ prati viraktaśceditthaṁ dehaṁ tyajediti||14.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.39">14.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svacchandamṛtyorapi yad bhīṣmādeḥ śrūyate kila|<br />
Bhogavairasyasamprāptau jīvitāntopasarpaṇam||14.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.40">14.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yogamantrāmṛtadravyavarādyaiḥ siddhibhāktanuḥ|<br />
Hātuṁ na hyanyathā śakyā vinoktakramayogataḥ||14.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.41">14.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uktaṁ ca mālinītantre parameśena tādṛśam|<br />
Sarvamapyathavā bhogaṁ manyamāno virūpakam||14.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.42">14.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ityādi vadatā sarvairalakṣyāntaḥ satattvakam|<br />
Evaṁ sṛṣṭyādikartavyasvasvātantryopadeśanam||14.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.43">14.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yatsaiva mukhyadīkṣā syācchiṣyasya śivadāyinī|<br />
Uktaṁ śrīniśicāre ca bhairavīyeṇa tejasā||14.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.44">14.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vyāptaṁ viśvaṁ prapaśyanti vikalpojjhitacetasaḥ|<br />
Vikalpayuktacittastu piṇḍapātācchivaṁ vrajet||14.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.45">14.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bāhyadīkṣādiyogena caryāsamayakalpanaiḥ|<br />
Avikalpastathādyaiva jīvanmukto na saṁśayaḥ||14.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v14.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v14.46">14.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṁsārajīrṇatarumūlakalāpakalpasaṅkalpasāntaratayā paramārthavahneḥ|<br />
Syurvisphuliṅgakaṇikā api cettadante dedīpyate vimalabodhahutāśarāśiḥ||14.46||</span></p>
