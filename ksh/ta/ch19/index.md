# Глава 19 — Sadyaḥsamutkrama

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">56 строф · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [английский перевод у источника — русского там нет](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/en/node/887)</p>

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Глава 18 — Sūkṣmā dīkṣā](/ksh/ta/ch18/) · [Глава 20 — Tulādīkṣā →](/ksh/ta/ch20/)</p>

<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloka ekānnaviṁśamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.1">19.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atha sadyaḥsamutkrāntipradā dīkṣā nirūpyate|<br />
Tatkṣaṇāccopabhogādvā dehapāte śivaṁ vrajet|<br />
Ityuktyā mālinīśāstre sūcitāsau maheśinā||19.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.2">19.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dehapāte samīpasthe śaktipātasphuṭatvataḥ|<br />
Āsādya śāṅkarīṁ dīkṣāṁ tasmāddīkṣākṣaṇātparam||19.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.3">19.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śivaṁ vrajedityartho'tra pūrvāparavivecanāt|<br />
Vyākhyātaḥ śrīmatāsmākaṁ guruṇā śambhumūrtinā||19.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.4">19.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yadā hyāsannamaraṇe śaktipātaḥ prajāyate|<br />
Tatra mande'tha gurvādisevayāyuḥ kṣayaṁ vrajet||19.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.5">19.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Athavā bandhumitrādidvārā sāsya vibhoḥ patet|<br />
Pūrvaṁ vā samayī naiva parāṁ dīkṣāmavāptavān||19.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.6">19.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Āptadīkṣo'pi vā prāṇāñjihāsuḥ kleśavarjitam|<br />
Antyāngurustadā kuryātsadya utkrāntidīkṣaṇam||19.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.7">19.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na tvapakvamale nāpi śeṣakārmikavigrahe|<br />
Kuryādutkramaṇaṁ śrīmadgahvare ca nirūpitam||19.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.8">19.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dṛṣṭvā śiṣyaṁ jarāgrastaṁ vyādhinā paripīḍitam|<br />
Utkramayya tatastvenaṁ paratattve niyojayet||19.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.9">19.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Viśeṣaṇaviśeṣyatve kāmacāravidhānataḥ|<br />
Pūrvoktamarthajātaṁ śrīśambhunātra nirūpitam||19.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.10">19.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vidhiṁ pūrvoditaṁ sarvaṁ kṛtvā samayaśuddhitaḥ|<br />
Kṣurikāmasya vinyasyejjvalantīṁ marmakartarīm||19.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.11">19.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kṛtvā pūrvoditaṁ nyāsaṁ kālānalasamaprabham|<br />
Saṁhṛtikramataḥ sārdhaṁ sṛkchindiyugalena tu||19.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.12">19.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Āgneyīṁ dhāraṇāṁ kṛtvā sarvamarmapratāpanīm|<br />
Pūrayedvāyunā dehamaṅguṣṭhānmastakāntakam||19.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.13">19.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tamutkṛṣya tato'ṅguṣṭhādūrdhvāntaṁ vakṣyamāṇayā|<br />
Kṛntenmarmāṇi randhrāntātkālarātryā visarjayet||19.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.14">19.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anena kramayogena yojito hutivarjitaḥ|<br />
Samayyapyeti tāṁ dīkṣāmiti śrīmālinīmate||19.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.15">19.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ṣoḍaśādhāraṣaṭcakralakṣyatrayakhapañcakāt|<br />
Kvacidanyataratrātha prāguktapaśukarmavat||19.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.16">19.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Praviśya mūlaṁ kandādeśchindannaikyavibhāvanāt|<br />
Pūrṇāhutiprayogeṇa sveṣṭe dhāmni niyojayet||19.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.17">19.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jñānatriśūlaṁ sandīptaṁ dīptacakratrayojjvalam|<br />
Cintayitvāmunā tasya vedhanaṁ bodhanaṁ bhramam||19.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.18">19.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dīpanaṁ tāḍanaṁ todaṁ calanaṁ ca punaḥ punaḥ|<br />
Kandādicakragaṁ kuryādviśeṣeṇa hṛdambuje||19.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.19">19.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dvādaśānte tataḥ kṛtvā binduyugmagate kṣipet|<br />
Nirlakṣye vā pare dhāmni saṁyuktaḥ parameśvaraḥ||19.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.20">19.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na tasya kuryātsaṁskāraṁ kañcidityāha gahvare|<br />
Devaḥ kimasya pūrṇasya śrāddhādyairiti bhāvitaḥ||19.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.21">19.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrīmaddīkṣottare tveṣa vidhirvahnipuṭīkṛtaḥ|<br />
Haṁsaḥ pumānadhastasya rudrabindusamanvitaḥ||19.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.22">19.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śiṣyadehe niyojyaitadanudvignaḥ śataṁ japet|<br />
Utkramyordhvaṁ nimeṣeṇa śiṣya itthaṁ paraṁ vrajet||19.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.23">19.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Eṣa eva vidhiḥ śrīmatsiddhayogīśvarīmate|<br />
Iyamutkrāmaṇī dīkṣā kartavyā yogino guroḥ||19.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.24">19.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Anabhyastaprāṇacāraḥ kathamenāṁ kariṣyati|<br />
Vakṣyamāṇāṁ brahmavidyāṁ sakalāṁ niṣkalombhitām||19.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.25">19.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Karṇe'sya vā paṭhedbhūyo bhūyo vāpyatha pāṭhayet|<br />
Svayaṁ ca karma kurvīta tattvaśuddhyādikaṁ guruḥ||19.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.26">19.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mantrakriyābalātpūrṇāhutyetthaṁ yojayetpare|<br />
Yogābhyāsamakṛtvāpi sadya utkrāntidāṁ guruḥ||19.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.27">19.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jñānamantrakriyādhyānabalātkartuṁ bhavetprabhuḥ|<br />
Anayotkramyate śiṣyo balādevaikakaṁ kṣaṇam||19.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.28">19.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kālasyollaṅghya bhogo hi kṣaṇiko'syāstu kiṁ tataḥ|<br />
Sadya utkrāntidā cānyā yasyāṁ pūrṇāhutiṁ tadā||19.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.29">19.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dadyādyadāsya prāṇāḥ syurdhruvaṁ niṣkramaṇecchavaḥ|<br />
Vināpi kriyayā bhāvibrahmavidyābalādguruḥ||19.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.30">19.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Karṇajāpaprayogeṇa tattvakañcukajālataḥ|<br />
Niḥsārayanyathābhīṣṭe sakale niṣkale dvaye||19.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.31">19.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tattve vā yatra kutrāpi yojayetpudgalaṁ kramāt|<br />
Samayī putrako vāpi paṭhedvidyāmimāṁ tathā||19.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.32">19.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatpāṭhāttu samayyuktāṁ rudrāṁśāpattimaśnute|<br />
Etau jape cādhyayane yasmādadhikṛtāvubhau||19.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.33">19.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nādhyāpanopadeśe vā sa eṣo'dhyayanādṛte|<br />
Paṭhatostvanayorvastusvabhāvāttasya sā gatiḥ||19.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.34">19.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathā niṣiddhabhūtādikarmā mantraṁ smaransvayam|<br />
Āviṣṭe'pi kvacinnaiti lopaṁ kartṛtvavarjanāt||19.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.35">19.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yathā ca vācayañchāstraṁ samayī śūnyaveśmani|<br />
Na lupyate tadantaḥsthaprāṇivargopakārataḥ||19.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.36">19.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathā svayaṁ paṭhanneṣa vidyāṁ vastusvabhāvataḥ|<br />
Tasminmukte na lupyeta yanno kiñcitkaro'tra saḥ||19.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.37">19.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nanu cādīkṣitāgre sa noccarecchāstrapaddhatim||19.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.38">19.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Hanta kuḍyāgrato'pyasya niṣedhastvatha kathyate|<br />
Paryudāsena yaḥ śrotumavadhārayituṁ kṣamaḥ||19.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.39">19.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sa evātra niṣiddho no kuḍyakīṭapatatriṇaḥ|<br />
Tarhi pāṣāṇatulyo'sau vilīnendriyavṛttikaḥ||19.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.40">19.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasyāgre paṭhatastasya niṣedhollaṅghanā katham|<br />
Sa tu vastusvabhāvena galitākṣo'pi budhyate||19.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.41">19.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Akṣānapekṣayaivāntaścicchaktyā svaprakāśayā|<br />
Prāgdehaṁ kila tityakṣurnottare cādhitaṣṭhivān||19.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.42">19.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Madhye prabodhakabalātpratibudhyeta pudgalaḥ|<br />
Mantrāḥ śabdamayāḥ śuddhavimarśātmatayā svayam||19.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.43">19.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Arthātmanā cāvabhāntastadarthapratibodhakāḥ|<br />
Tenāsya galitākṣasya prabodho jāyate svayam||19.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.44">19.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svacitsamānajātīyamantrāmarśanasannidheḥ|<br />
Yathā hyalpajavo vāyuḥ sajātīyavimiśritaḥ||19.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.45">19.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Javī tathātmā saṁsuptāmarśo'pyevaṁ prabudhyate|<br />
Prabuddhaḥ sa ca sañjāto na cādīkṣita ucyate||19.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.46">19.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dīkṣā hi nāma saṁskāro na tvanyatso'sti cāsya hi|<br />
Ata eva nijaṁ śāstraṁ paṭhati kvāpi sāmaye||19.46||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.47">19.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tacchrutvā ko'pi dhanyaścenmucyate nāsya sā kṣatiḥ|<br />
Śāstranindāṁ maiṣa kārṣīddvayoḥ pātityadāyinīm||19.47||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.48">19.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ityevaṁ parametannādīkṣitāgre paṭhediti|<br />
Yathā ca samayī kāṣṭhe loṣṭe vā mantrayojanām||19.48||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.49">19.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kurvaṁstasmiṁścalatyeti na lopaṁ tadvadatra hi|<br />
Yato'sya pratyayaprāptiprepsoḥ samayinastathā||19.49||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.50"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.50">19.50</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pravṛttasya svabhāvena tasminmukte na vai kṣatiḥ|<br />
Sādhakastu sadā sādhye phale niyatiyantraṇāt||19.50||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.51"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.51">19.51</a> <span class="pv-iast">Makṣikāśrutamantro'pi prāyaścittaucitīṁ caret|<br />
Itthaṁ sadyaḥsamutkrāntiryoktā tāmājñayā guroḥ||19.51||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.52"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.52">19.52</a> <span class="pv-iast">Samayyādirapi proktakāle proktārthasiddhaye|<br />
Svayaṁ kuryātsamabhyastaprāṇacāragamāgamaḥ||19.52||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.53"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.53">19.53</a> <span class="pv-iast">Akṛtādhikṛtirvāpi guruḥ samayaśuddhaye|<br />
Adhastanapadāvastho na tu jñāneddhacetanaḥ||19.53||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.54"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.54">19.54</a> <span class="pv-iast">Itīyaṁ sadya utkrāntiḥ sūcitā mālinīmate|<br />
Svayaṁ vā guruṇā vātha kāryatvena maheśinā||19.54||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.55"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.55">19.55</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sarvaṁ bhogaṁ virūpaṁ tu matvā dehaṁ tyajedyadi|<br />
Tadā tena krameṇāśu yojitaḥ samayī śivaḥ||19.55||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v19.56"><a class="ta-num" href="#v19.56">19.56</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ukteyaṁ sadya utkrāntiryā gopyā prāṇavadbudhaiḥ||19.56||</span></p>
