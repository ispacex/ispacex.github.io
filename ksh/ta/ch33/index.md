# Глава 33 — Ekīkāraḥ

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">32 строфы · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [английский перевод у источника — русского там нет](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/en/node/904)</p>

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Глава 32 — Mudrikāvidhiḥ](/ksh/ta/ch32/) · [Глава 34 — Svasvarūpe praveśaḥ →](/ksh/ta/ch34/)</p>

<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloke trayastriṁśamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.1">33.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Athāvasarasamprāpta ekīkāro nigadyate|<br />
Yaduktaṁ cakrabhedena sārdhaṁ pūjyamiti trikam|<br />
Tatraiṣa cakrabhedānāmekīkāro diśānayā||33.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.2">33.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Viśvā tadīśā hāraudrī vīranetryambikā tathā|<br />
Gurvīti ṣaḍare devyaḥ śrīsiddhāvīradarśitāḥ||33.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.3">33.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Māheśī brāhmaṇī skāndī vaiṣṇavyaindrī yamātmikā|<br />
Cāmuṇḍā caiva yogīśītyaṣṭāghoryādayo'thavā||33.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.4">33.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Agninirṛtivāyvīśamātṛbhirdvādaśānvitāḥ|<br />
Nandā bhadrā jayā kālī karālī vikṛtānanā||33.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.5">33.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kroṣṭukī bhīmamudrā ca vāyuvegā hayānanā|<br />
Gambhīrā ghoṣaṇī ceti caturviṁśatyare vidhiḥ||33.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.6">33.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Siddhirvṛddhirdyutirlakṣmīrmedhā kāntiḥ svadhā dhṛtiḥ|<br />
Dīptiḥ puṣṭirmatiḥ kīrtiḥ susthitiḥ sugatiḥ smṛtiḥ||33.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.7">33.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Suprabhā ṣoḍaśī ceti śrīkaṇṭhādikaśaktayaḥ|<br />
Baliśca balinandaśca daśagrīvo haro hayaḥ||33.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.8">33.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mādhavaḥ ṣaḍare cakre dvādaśāre tvamī smṛtāḥ|<br />
Dakṣaścaṇḍo haraḥ śauṇḍī pramatho bhīmamanmathau||33.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.9">33.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śakuniḥ sumatirnando gopālaśca pitāmahaḥ|<br />
Śrīkaṇṭho'nantasūkṣmau ca trimūrtiḥ śambareśvaraḥ||33.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.10">33.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Arghīśo bhārabhūtiśca sthitiḥ sthāṇurharastathā|<br />
Jhaṇṭhibhautikasadyojānugrahakrūrasainikāḥ||33.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.11">33.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dvyaṣṭau yadvāmṛtastena yuktāḥ pūrṇābhataddravāḥ|<br />
Oghormisyandanāṅgāśca vapurudgāravaktrakāḥ||33.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.12">33.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tanusecanamūrtīśāḥ sarvāmṛtadharo'paraḥ|<br />
Strīpāṭhācchaktayaścaitāḥ ṣoḍaśaiva prakīrtitāḥ||33.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.13">33.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṁvartalakulibhṛgusitabakakhaḍgipinākibhujagabalikālāḥ|<br />
Dviśchagalāṇḍau śikhiśoṇameṣamīnatridaṇḍi sāṣāḍhi||33.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.14">33.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Devīkāntatadardhau dārukahalisomanāthaśarmāṇaḥ|<br />
Jayavijayajayantājitasujayajayarudrakīrtanāvahakāḥ||33.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.15">33.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tanmūrtyutsāhadavardhanāśca balasubalabhadradāvahakāḥ|<br />
Tadvāndātā ceśo nandanasamabhadratanmūrtiḥ||33.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.16">33.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śivadasumanaḥspṛhaṇakā durgo bhadrākhyakālaśca|<br />
Ceto'nugakauśikakālaviśvasuśivāstathāparaḥ kopaḥ||33.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.17">33.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śrutyagnyare syurete strīpāṭhācchaktayastvetāḥ|<br />
Juṅkāro'thāgnipatnīti ṣaḍare ṣaṇṭhavarjitāḥ||33.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.18">33.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dvādaśāre tatsahitāḥ ṣoḍaśāre svarāḥ kramāt|<br />
Halastaddviguṇe'ṣṭāre yādyaṁ hāntaṁ tu tattrike||33.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.19">33.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dvātriṁśadarake sāntaṁ binduḥ sarveṣu mūrdhani|<br />
Evamanyānbahūṁścakrabhedānasmātprakalpayet||33.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.20">33.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Eka eva cidātmaiṣa viśvāmarśanasārakaḥ|<br />
Śaktistadvānato mātā śabdarāśiḥ prakīrtitau||33.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.21">33.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tayoreva vibhāge tu śaktitadvatprakalpane|<br />
Śabdarāśirmālinī ca kṣobhātma vapurīdṛśam||33.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.22">33.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathāntaḥsthaparāmarśabhedane vastutastrikam|<br />
Anuttarecchonmeṣākhyaṁ yato viśvaṁ vimarśanam||33.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.23">33.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ānandeśormiyoge tu tatṣaṭkaṁ samudāhṛtam|<br />
Antaḥsthoṣmasamāyogāttadaṣṭakamudāhṛtam||33.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.24">33.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadāmṛtacatuṣkonabhāve dvādaśakaṁ bhavet|<br />
Tadyoge ṣoḍaśākhyaṁ syādevaṁ yāvadasaṅkhyatā||33.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.25">33.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Viśvamekaparāmarśasahatvātprabhṛti sphuṭam|<br />
Aṁśāṁśikāparāmarśānparyante sahate yataḥ||33.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.26">33.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ataḥ pañcāśadaikātmyaṁ svaravyaktivirūpatā|<br />
Vargāṣṭakaṁ varṇabheda ekāśītikalodayaḥ||33.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.27">33.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti pradarśitaṁ pūrvamardhamātrāsahatvataḥ|<br />
Svarārdhamapyasti yataḥ svaritasyārdhamātrakam||33.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.28">33.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tasyādita udāttaṁ tatkathitaṁ padavedinā|<br />
Itthaṁ saṁvidiyaṁ yājyasvarūpāmarśarūpiṇī||33.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.29">33.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Abhinnaṁ saṁvidaścaitaccakrāṇāṁ cakravālakam|<br />
Svāmyāvaraṇabhedena bahudhā tatprayojayet||33.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.30">33.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Parā parāparā cānyā sṛṣṭisthititirodhayaḥ|<br />
Mātṛsadbhāvarūpā tu turyā viśrāntirucyate||33.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.31">33.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tacca prakāśaṁ vaktrasthaṁ sūcitaṁ tu pade pade|<br />
Turye viśrāntirādheyā mātṛsadbhāvasāriṇi||33.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v33.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v33.32">33.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathāsya viśvamābhāti svātmatanmayatāṁ gatam|<br />
Ityeṣa śāstrārthasyokta ekīkāro gurūditaḥ||33.32||</span></p>
