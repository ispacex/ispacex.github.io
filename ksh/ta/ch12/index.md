# Глава 12 — Adhvopayogaḥ

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">25 строф · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [перевод главы у источника](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/ru/node/874)</p>

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Глава 11 — Kalādyadhvā](/ksh/ta/ch11/) · [Глава 13 — Śaktipātatirohitī →](/ksh/ta/ch13/)</p>

<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloke dvādaśamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.1">12.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Athādhvano'sya prakṛta upayogaḥ prakāśyate||12.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.2">12.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Itthamadhvā samasto'yaṁ yathā saṁvidi saṁsthitaḥ|<br />
Taddvārā śūnyadhīprāṇanāḍīcakratanuṣvatho||12.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.3">12.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bahiśca liṅgamūrtyagnisthaṇḍilādiṣu sarvataḥ|<br />
Tathā sthitaḥ samastaśca vyastaścaiṣa kramākramāt||12.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.4">12.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Āsaṁvittattvamābāhyaṁ yo'yamadhvā vyavasthitaḥ|<br />
Tatra tatrocitaṁ rūpaṁ svaṁ svātantryeṇa bhāsayet||12.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.5">12.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sarvaṁ sarvatra rūpaṁ ca tasyāpi na na bhāsate|<br />
Nahyavaccheditāṁ kvāpi svapne'pi viṣahāmahe||12.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.6">12.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Evaṁ viśvādhvasampūrṇaṁ kālavyāpāracitritam|<br />
Deśakālamayaspandasadma dehaṁ vilokayet||12.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.7">12.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathā vilokyamāno'sau viśvāntardevatāmayaḥ|<br />
Dhyeyaḥ pūjyaśca tarpyaśca tadāviṣṭo vimucyate||12.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.8">12.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Itthaṁ ghaṭaṁ paṭaṁ liṅgaṁ sthaṇḍilaṁ pustakaṁ jalam|<br />
Yadvā kiñcitkvacitpaśyettatra tanmayatāṁ vrajet||12.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.9">12.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatrārpaṇaṁ hi vastūnāmabhedenārcanaṁ matam|<br />
Tathā sampūrṇarūpatvānusandhirdhyānamucyate||12.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.10">12.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sampūrṇatvānusandhānamakampaṁ dārḍhyamānayan|<br />
Tathāntarjalpayogena vimṛśañjapabhājanam||12.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.11">12.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatrārpitānāṁ bhāvānāṁ svakabhedavilāpanam|<br />
Kurvaṁstadraśmisadbhāvaṁ dadyāddhomakriyāparaḥ||12.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.12">12.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathaivaṅkurvataḥ sarvaṁ samabhāvena paśyataḥ|<br />
Niṣkampatā vrataṁ śuddhaṁ sāmyaṁ nandiśikhoditam||12.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.13">12.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathārcanajapadhyānahomavratavidhikramāt|<br />
Paripūrṇāṁ sthitiṁ prāhuḥ samādhiṁ guravaḥ purā||12.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.14">12.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Atra pūjājapādyeṣu bahirantardvayasthitau|<br />
Dravyaughe na vidhiḥ ko'pi na kāpi pratiṣiddhatā||12.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.15">12.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kalpanāśuddhisandhyādernopayogo'tra kaścana|<br />
Uktaṁ śrītrikasūtre ca jāyate yajanaṁ prati||12.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.16">12.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Avidhijño vidhijñaścetyevamādi suvistaram|<br />
Yadā yathā yena yatra svā samvittiḥ prasīdati||12.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.17">12.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tadā tathā tena tatra tattadbhogyaṁ vidhiśca saḥ|<br />
Laukikālaukikaṁ sarvaṁ tenātra viniyojayet||12.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.18">12.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Niṣkampatve sakampastu kampaṁ nirhrāsayedbalāt|<br />
Yathā yenābhyupāyena kramādakramato'pi vā||12.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.19">12.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vicikitsā galatyantastathāsau yatnavānbhavet|<br />
Dhīkarmākṣagatā devīrniṣiddhaireva tarpayet||12.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.20">12.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vīravrataṁ cābhinandediti bhargaśikhāvacaḥ|<br />
Tathāhi śaṅkā mālinyaṁ glāniḥ saṅkoca ityadaḥ||12.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.21">12.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Saṁsārakārāgārāntaḥ sthūlasthūṇā ghaṭāyate|<br />
Mantrā varṇasvabhāvā ye dravyaṁ yatpāñcabhautikam||12.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.22">12.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yaccidātma prāṇijātaṁ tatra kaḥ saṅkaraḥ katham|<br />
Saṅkarābhāvataḥ keyaṁ śaṅkā tasyāmapi sphuṭam||12.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.23">12.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na śaṅketa tathā śaṅkā vilīyetāvahelayā|<br />
Śrīsarvācāravīrālīniśācarakramādiṣu||12.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.24">12.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śāstreṣu vitataṁ caitattatra tatrocyate yataḥ|<br />
Śaṅkayā jāyate glāniḥ śaṅkayā vighnabhājanam||12.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v12.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v12.25">12.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Uvācotpaladevaśca śrīmānasmadgurorguruḥ|<br />
Sarvāśaṅkāśaniṁ mārgaṁ numo māheśvaraṁ tviti||12.25||</span></p>
