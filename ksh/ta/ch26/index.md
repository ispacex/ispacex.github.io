# Глава 26 — Śeṣavṛttinirūpaṇam

<p class="ta-meta nosearch" markdown="1">76 строф · только санскрит в транслитерации IAST · [английский перевод у источника — русского там нет](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/en/node/894)</p>

<p class="ta-open pv-iast">Atha śrītantrāloke ṣaḍviṁśamāhnikam|</p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.1"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.1">26.1</a> <span class="pv-iast">Athocyate śeṣavṛttirjīvatāmupayoginī||26.1||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.2"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.2">26.2</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dīkṣā bahuprakāreyaṁ śrāddhāntā yā prakīrtitā|<br />
Sā saṁskriyāyai mokṣāya bhogāyāpi dvayāya vā||26.2||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.3"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.3">26.3</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatra saṁskārasiddhyai yā dīkṣā sākṣānna mocanī|<br />
Anusandhivaśādyā ca sākṣānmoktrī sabījikā||26.3||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.4"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.4">26.4</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tayobhayyā dīkṣitā ye teṣāmājīvavartanam|<br />
Vaktavyaṁ putrakādīnāṁ tanmayatvaprasiddhaye||26.4||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.5"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.5">26.5</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bubhukṣorvā mumukṣorvā svasaṁvidguruśāstrataḥ|<br />
Pramāṇādyā saṁskriyāyai dīkṣā hi guruṇā kṛtā||26.5||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.6"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.6">26.6</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tataḥ sa saṁskṛtaṁ yogyaṁ jñātvātmānaṁ svaśāsane|<br />
Taduktavastvanuṣṭhānaṁ bhuktyai muktyai ca sevate||26.6||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.7"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.7">26.7</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ācāryapratyayādeva yo'pi syādbhuktimuktibhāk|<br />
Tatpratyūhodayadhvastyai brūyāttasyāpi vartanam||26.7||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.8"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.8">26.8</a> <span class="pv-iast">Svasaṁvidgurusaṁvittyostulyapratyayabhāgapi|<br />
Śeṣavṛttyā samādeśyastadvighnādipraśāntaye||26.8||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.9"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.9">26.9</a> <span class="pv-iast">Yaḥ sarvathā parāpekṣāmujjhitvā tu sthito nijāt|<br />
Pratyayādyo'pi cācāryapratyayādeva kevalāt||26.9||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.10"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.10">26.10</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tau sāṁsiddhikanirbījau ko vadeccheṣavṛttaye|<br />
Kramāttanmayatopāyagurvarcanaratau tu tau||26.10||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.11"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.11">26.11</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatraiṣāṁ śeṣavṛttyarthaṁ nityanaimittike bruve|<br />
Kāmyavarjaṁ yataḥ kāmāścitrāścitrābhyupāyakāḥ||26.11||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.12"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.12">26.12</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatra nityo vidhiḥ sandhyānuṣṭhānaṁ devatāvrate|<br />
Gurvagniśāstrasahite pūjā bhūtadayetyayam||26.12||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.13"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.13">26.13</a> <span class="pv-iast">Naimittikastu sarveṣāṁ parvaṇāṁ pūjanaṁ japaḥ|<br />
Viśeṣavaśataḥ kiñca pavitrakavidhikramaḥ||26.13||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.14"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.14">26.14</a> <span class="pv-iast">Ācāryasya ca dīkṣeyaṁ bahubhedā vivecitā|<br />
Vyākhyādikaṁ ca tattasyādhikaṁ naimittikaṁ dhruvam||26.14||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.15"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.15">26.15</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatrādau śiśave brūyādgururnityavidhiṁ sphuṭam|<br />
Tadyogyatāṁ samālokya vitatāvitatātmanām||26.15||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.16"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.16">26.16</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mukhyetarādimantrāṇāṁ vīryavyāptyādiyogyatām|<br />
Dṛṣṭvā śiṣye tamevāsmai mūlamantraṁ samarpayet||26.16||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.17"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.17">26.17</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tacchāstradīkṣito hyeṣa niryantrācāraśaṅkitaḥ|<br />
Na mukhye yogya ityanyasevātaḥ syāttu yogyatā||26.17||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.18"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.18">26.18</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sādhakasya bubhukṣostu sādhakībhāvino'pivā|<br />
Puṣpapātavaśātsiddho mantro'rpyaḥ sādhyasiddhaye||26.18||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.19"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.19">26.19</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vitate guṇabhūte vā vidhau diṣṭe punarguruḥ|<br />
Jñātvāsmai yogyatāsāraṁ saṅkṣiptaṁ vidhimācaret||26.19||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.20"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.20">26.20</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatraiṣa niyamo yadyanmāntraṁ rūpaṁ na tadguruḥ|<br />
Likhitvā prathayecchiṣye viśeṣādūrdhvaśāsane||26.20||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.21"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.21">26.21</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mantrā varṇātmakāste ca parāmarśātmakāḥ sa ca|<br />
Gurusaṁvidabhinnaścetsaṅkrāmetsā tataḥ śiśau||26.21||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.22"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.22">26.22</a> <span class="pv-iast">Lipisthitastu yo mantro nirvīryaḥ so'tra kalpitaḥ|<br />
Saṅketabalato nāsya pustakātprathate mahaḥ||26.22||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.23"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.23">26.23</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pustakādhītavidyāścetyuktaṁ siddhāmate tataḥ|<br />
Ye tu pustakalabdhe'pi mantre vīryaṁ prajānate||26.23||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.24"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.24">26.24</a> <span class="pv-iast">Te bhairavīyasaṁskārāḥ proktāḥ sāṁsiddhikā iti|<br />
Iti jñātvā guruḥ samyakparamānandaghūrṇitaḥ||26.24||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.25"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.25">26.25</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tādṛśe tādṛśe dhāmni pūjayitvā vidhiṁ caret|<br />
Yathānyaśiṣyānuṣṭhānaṁ nānyaśiṣyeṇa budhyate||26.25||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.26"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.26">26.26</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tathā kuryādgururguptihānirdoṣavatī yataḥ|<br />
Devīnāṁ tritayaṁ śuddhaṁ yadvā yāmalayogataḥ||26.26||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.27"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.27">26.27</a> <span class="pv-iast">Devīmekāmatho śuddhāṁ vadedvā yāmalātmikām|<br />
Tatra mantraṁ sphuṭaṁ vaktrādguruṇopāṁśu coditam||26.27||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.28"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.28">26.28</a> <span class="pv-iast">Avadhāryāpravṛttestamabhyasyenmanasā svayam|<br />
Tataḥ suśikṣitāṁ sthānadehāntaḥśodhanatrayīm||26.28||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.29"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.29">26.29</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nyāsaṁ dhyānaṁ japaṁ mudrāṁ pūjāṁ kuryātprayatnataḥ|<br />
Tatra prabhāte sambudhya sveṣṭāṁ prāgdevatāṁ smaret||26.29||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.30"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.30">26.30</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kṛtāvaśyakakartavyaḥ śuddho bhūtvā tato gṛham|<br />
Āśrityottaradigvaktraḥ sthānadehāntaratraye||26.30||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.31"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.31">26.31</a> <span class="pv-iast">Śuddhiṁ vidhāya mantrāṇāṁ yathāsthānaṁ niveśanam|<br />
Mudrāpradarśanaṁ dhyānaṁ bhedābhedasvarūpataḥ||26.31||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.32"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.32">26.32</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dehāsudhīvyomabhūṣu manasā tatra cārcanam|<br />
Japaṁ cātra yathāśakti devāyaitannivedanam||26.32||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.33"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.33">26.33</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tanmayībhāvasiddhyarthaṁ pratisandhyaṁ samācaret|<br />
Anye tu prāgudakpaścāddakṣadikṣu catuṣṭayīm||26.33||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.34"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.34">26.34</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sandhyānāmāhuretacca tāntrikīyaṁ na no matam|<br />
Yāsau kālādhikāre prāksandhyā proktā catuṣṭayī||26.34||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.35"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.35">26.35</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tāmevāntaḥ samādhāya sāndhyaṁ vidhimupācaret|<br />
Sandhyācatuṣṭayīkṛtyamekasyāmathavā śiśuḥ||26.35||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.36"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.36">26.36</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kuryātsvādhyāyavijñānagurukṛtyāditatparaḥ|<br />
Sandhyādhyānoditānantatanmayībhāvayuktitaḥ||26.36||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.37"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.37">26.37</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatsaṁskāravaśātsarvaṁ kālaṁ syāttanmayo hyasau|<br />
Tato yatheṣṭakāle'sau pūjāṁ puṣpāsavādibhiḥ||26.37||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.38"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.38">26.38</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sthaṇḍilādau śiśuḥ kuryādvibhavādyanurūpataḥ|<br />
Suśuddhaḥ sanvidhiṁ sarvaṁ kṛtvāntarajapāntakam||26.38||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.39"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.39">26.39</a> <span class="pv-iast">Arghapātraṁ purā yadvadvidhāya sveṣṭamantrataḥ|<br />
Tena sthaṇḍilapuṣpādi sarvaṁ samprokṣayedbudhaḥ||26.39||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.40"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.40">26.40</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatastatraiva saṅkalpya dvārāsanagurukramam|<br />
Pūjayecchivatāviṣṭaḥ svadehārcāpuraḥsaram||26.40||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.41"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.41">26.41</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tatastatsthaṇḍilaṁ vīdhravyomasphaṭikanirmalam|<br />
Bodhātmakaṁ samālokya tatra svaṁ devatāgaṇam||26.41||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.42"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.42">26.42</a> <span class="pv-iast">Pratibimbatayā paśyedbimbatvena ca bodhataḥ|<br />
Etadāvāhanaṁ mukhyaṁ vyajanānmarutāmiva||26.42||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.43"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.43">26.43</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sarvago'pi marudyadvadvyajanenopavījitaḥ|<br />
Arthakṛtsarvagaṁ mantracakraṁ rūḍhestathā bhavet||26.43||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.44"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.44">26.44</a> <span class="pv-iast">Catuṣkapañcāśikāyāṁ tadetattattvamucyate|<br />
Śrīnirmaryādaśāstre ca tadetadvibhunoditam||26.44||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.45"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.45">26.45</a> <span class="pv-iast">Devaḥ sarvagato deva nirmaryādaḥ kathaṁ śivaḥ|<br />
Āvāhyate kṣamyate vetyevaṁ pṛṣṭo'bravīdvibhuḥ||26.45||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.46"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.46">26.46</a> <span class="pv-iast">Vāsanāvāhyate devi vāsanā ca visṛjyate|<br />
Paramārthena devasya nāvāhanavisarjane||26.46||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.47"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.47">26.47</a> <span class="pv-iast">Āvāhito mayā devaḥ sthaṇḍile ca pratiṣṭhitaḥ|<br />
Pūjitaḥ stuta ityevaṁ dṛṣṭvā devaṁ visarjayet||26.47||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.48"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.48">26.48</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prāṇināmaprabuddhānāṁ santoṣajananāya vai|<br />
Āvāhanādikaṁ teṣāṁ pravṛttiḥ kathamanyathā||26.48||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.49"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.49">26.49</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kālena tu vijānanti pravṛttāḥ patiśāsane|<br />
Anukrameṇa devasya prāptiṁ bhuvanapūrvikām||26.49||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.50"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.50">26.50</a> <span class="pv-iast">Jñānadīpadyutidhvastasamastājñānasañcayāḥ|<br />
Kuto vānīyate devaḥ kutra vā nīyate'pi saḥ||26.50||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.51"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.51">26.51</a> <span class="pv-iast">Sthūlasūkṣmādibhedena sa hi sarvatra saṁsthitaḥ|<br />
Āvāhite mantragaṇe puṣpāsavanivedanaiḥ||26.51||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.52"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.52">26.52</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dhūpaiśca tarpaṇaṁ kāryaṁ śraddhābhaktibalocitaiḥ|<br />
Dīptānāṁ śaktināthādimantrāṇāmāsavaiḥ palaiḥ||26.52||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.53"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.53">26.53</a> <span class="pv-iast">Raktaiḥ prāktarpaṇaṁ paścātpuṣpadhūpādivistaraiḥ|<br />
Āgatasya tu mantrasya na kuryāttarpaṇaṁ yadi||26.53||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.54"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.54">26.54</a> <span class="pv-iast">Haratyardhaśarīraṁ sa ityuktaṁ kila śambhunā|<br />
Yadyadevāsya manasi vikāsitvaṁ prayacchati||26.54||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.55"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.55">26.55</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tenaiva kuryātpūjāṁ sa iti śambhorviniścayaḥ|<br />
Sādhakānāṁ bubhukṣūṇāṁ vidhirniyatiyantritaḥ||26.55||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.56"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.56">26.56</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mumukṣūṇāṁ tattvavidāṁ sa eva tu nirargalaḥ|<br />
Kārye viśeṣamādhitsurviśiṣṭaṁ kāraṇaṁ spṛśet||26.56||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.57"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.57">26.57</a> <span class="pv-iast">Raktakarpāsatūlecchustulyatadbījapuñjavat|<br />
Santi bhoge viśeṣāśca vicitrāḥ kāraṇeritāḥ||26.57||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.58"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.58">26.58</a> <span class="pv-iast">Deśakālānusandhānaguṇadravyakriyādibhiḥ|<br />
Svalpā kriyā bhūyasī vā hṛdayāhlādadāyibhiḥ||26.58||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.59"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.59">26.59</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bāhyaiḥ saṅkalpajairvāpi kārakaiḥ parikalpitā|<br />
Mumukṣorna viśeṣāya naiḥśreyasavidhiṁ prati||26.59||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.60"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.60">26.60</a> <span class="pv-iast">Na hi brahmaṇi śaṁsanti bāhulyālpatvadurdaśāḥ|<br />
Citaḥ svātantryasāratvāttasyānandaghanatvataḥ||26.60||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.61"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.61">26.61</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kriyā syāttanmayībhūtyai hṛdayāhlādadāyibhiḥ|<br />
Śivābhedabharādbhāvavargaḥ ścyotati yaṁ rasam||26.61||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.62"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.62">26.62</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tameva parame dhāmni pūjanāyārpayedbudhaḥ|<br />
Stotreṣu bahudhā caitanmayā proktaṁ nijāhnike||26.62||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.63"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.63">26.63</a> <span class="pv-iast">Adhiśayya pāramārthikabhāvaprasaraprakāśamullasati|<br />
Yā paramāmṛtadṛktvāṁ tayārcayante rahasyavidaḥ||26.63||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.64"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.64">26.64</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kṛtvādhāradharāṁ camatkṛtirasaprokṣākṣaṇakṣālitāmāttairmānasataḥ svabhāvakusumaiḥ svāmodasandohibhiḥ|<br />
Ānandāmṛtanirbharasvahṛdayānarghārghapātrakramāttvāṁ devyā saha dehadevasadane devārcaye'harniśam||26.64||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.65"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.65">26.65</a> <span class="pv-iast">Nānāsvādarasāmimāṁ trijagatīṁ hṛccakrayantrārpitāmūrdhvādhyastavivekagauravabharānniṣpīḍya niḥṣyanditam|<br />
Yatsaṁvitparamāmṛtaṁ mṛtijarājanmāpahaṁ jṛmbhate tena tvāṁ haviṣā pareṇa parame santarpaye'harniśam||26.65||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.66"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.66">26.66</a> <span class="pv-iast">Iti ślokatrayopāttamarthamantarvibhāvayan|<br />
Yena kenāpi bhāvena tarpayeddevatāgaṇam||26.66||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.67"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.67">26.67</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mudrāṁ pradarśayetpaścānmanasā vāpi yogataḥ|<br />
Vacasā mantrayogena vapuṣā sanniveśataḥ||26.67||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.68"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.68">26.68</a> <span class="pv-iast">Kṛtvā japaṁ tataḥ sarvaṁ devatāyai samarpayet|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.69"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.69">26.69</a> <span class="pv-iast">Tato visarjanaṁ kāryaṁ bodhaikātmyaprayogataḥ|<br />
Kṛtvā vā vahnigāṁ mantratṛptiṁ proktavidhānataḥ||26.69||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.70"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.70">26.70</a> <span class="pv-iast">Dvārapīṭhaguruvrātasamarpitanivedanāt|<br />
Ṛte'nyatsvayamaśnīyādagādhe'mbhasyatha kṣipet||26.70||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.71"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.71">26.71</a> <span class="pv-iast">Prāṇino jalajāḥ pūrvadīkṣitāḥ śambhunā svayam|<br />
Vidhinā bhāvinā śrīmanmīnanāthāvatāriṇā||26.71||</span></p>

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.72"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.72">26.72</a> <span class="pv-iast">Mārjāramūṣikādyairyadadīkṣaiścāpi bhakṣitam|<br />

<p class="ta-st" id="v26.75"><a class="ta-num" href="#v26.75">26.75</a> <span class="pv-iast">Bhavettathā yathānyeṣāṁ śaṅkā no manasi sphuret|<br />
