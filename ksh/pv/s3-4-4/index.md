## Введение

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">Parātrīśikāvivaraṇa continues. Abhinavagupta finishes commenting on the stanzas 3 and 4 of Parātrīśikā.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">This is the fourth <span class="pv-w">(final)</span> part of the commentary on the stanzas 3-4.</p>

Под переводом я буду писать «сплошное длинное пояснение»: если, как обычно, пользоваться отдельными «примечаниями», примечаний окажется столько, что работать с ними станет невозможно. <p class="pv-sa" lang="sa">इतीदृग् व्याख्यानं त्यक्त्वा यद् अन्यैर्व्याख्यातं तत्प्रदर्शनं दूषणम्। यद्यपि पदवाक्यसंस्कारविहीनैः सह व्रीडावहा गोष्ठी कृता भवति तथापि सचेतसोऽनुत्तरमवबोधयितुं तद् एकवारं तावल्लिख्यते — अनुत्तरमित्यादिना सार्धेन श्लोकेन शिवविषयः प्रश्नः। हृदयस्थेत्यादिना श्लोकेन शक्तिविषयः। तथा शृणु देवीत्यत्र प्रतिवचनग्रन्थ उत्तरस्याप्यनुत्तरमिति तत्रार्थ उत्तरं च शृण्वनुत्तरं चेति। अत्र यद्येषा त्रिकार्थाभिप्रायेण व्याख्या तन् नरविषयतृतीयप्रश्नप्रसङ्गः। अथ तु यामलाभिप्रायेण तत्रापि न द्वे वस्तुनी शिवशक्त्यात्मके यामलमुच्यते येन पृथक् प्रश्नविषयतोपपत्तिरथशब्दार्थश्च न सङ्गच्छते — स हि सजातीयनिश्चयानन्तर्यवृत्तिरुत्तरस्वरूपावधारणमन्तरेण चानुत्तरविषयस्यैकप्रश्नस्यानुपपत्तिः। तथाहि केषुचिद् वृद्धपुस्तकेष्वीदृक् श्लोकान्तरं दृश्यते</p>

There is fully a condition of satisfaction <span class="pv-w">(sarvam... kṛtakṛtyatā)</span> indeed <span class="pv-w">(tu)</span> for those whose numerous residual impressions <span class="pv-w">(analpa-saṁskārāṇam)</span> derived from thoughts <span class="pv-w">(vikalpa)</span> _(based on)_ dualistic <span class="pv-w">(bheda)</span> conceptions <span class="pv-w">(abhimāna)</span> have been destroyed <span class="pv-w">(nirdalita)</span> by such a <span class="pv-w">(etāvad)</span> firm <span class="pv-w">(dṛḍha)</span> teaching <span class="pv-w">(upadeśa)</span> _<span class="pv-w">(and)</span>_ who have achieved the state <span class="pv-w">(āviṣṭa... bhāvānām)</span> of liberated while living <span class="pv-w">(jīvat-mukta)</span>, which is subject to <span class="pv-w">(vaśa)</span> the acquisition <span class="pv-w">(prāpti)</span> of the State <span class="pv-w">(pada)</span> of Anuttara <span class="pv-w">(anuttara)</span>. _(How did they manage to attain that condition of satisfaction?)_ By _<span class="pv-w">(the portion of text in Parātrīśikā)</span>_ whose extension is this <span class="pv-w">(etāvatā eva)</span>: The question <span class="pv-w">(praśnena)</span> that begins with <span class="pv-w">(ādi)</span> "how <span class="pv-w">(katham)</span> does Anuttara... (anuttaram... iti)?" <span class="pv-note">— in stanza 1 —</span> _<span class="pv-w">(and)</span>_ comprises <span class="pv-w">(nigamitena)</span> two and a half (sa-ardha... yugala) stanzas <span class="pv-w">(śloka)</span> <span class="pv-note">— i.e. stanzas 1, 1 ½ and 2 —</span>, along with <span class="pv-w">(ca)</span> the answer <span class="pv-w">(uttareṇa)</span> that begins with <span class="pv-w">(ādinā)</span> "Listen <span class="pv-w">(śṛṇu)</span>, oh Goddess (devi... iti)" <span class="pv-note">— in stanza 3 —</span> _(and ends in)_ a definite ascertainment <span class="pv-w">(nirṇītena)</span> _<span class="pv-w">(comprehending)</span>_ one and a half <span class="pv-w">(sa-ardha)</span> stanzas <span class="pv-w">(śloka)</span> <span class="pv-note">— stanzas 3 and 4 —</span>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">For this reason <span class="pv-w">(atas)</span>, I proclaim aloud <span class="pv-w">(phūt-kurmaḥ)</span> with uplifted arms <span class="pv-w">(ut-bhujāḥ)</span> that so much <span class="pv-w">(tāvat)</span> alone <span class="pv-w">(mātraḥ eva)</span> <span class="pv-note">— i.e. such quantity and quality of teachings given in the first four stanzas —</span> _<span class="pv-w">(received)</span>_ by the ones who have been purified <span class="pv-w">(pavitrī-kṛtaiḥ)</span> through a firm <span class="pv-w">(dṛḍha)</span> conviction <span class="pv-w">(pratipatti)</span> amounts to a repose <span class="pv-w">(viśramaṇīyam)</span>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">_(Now,)_ it is said so <span class="pv-w">(iti-uktam)</span>: The contents _(of the posterior portion)_ <span class="pv-w">(antaram)</span> of the _<span class="pv-w">(present)</span>_ book <span class="pv-w">(grantha)</span> descend <span class="pv-w">(avatarati)</span> for the discernment <span class="pv-w">(praviviktaye)</span> of the "kaulika" <span class="pv-w">(kaulika)</span> state <span class="pv-w">(pada)</span> that stays <span class="pv-w">(niviṣṭa)</span> in <span class="pv-w">(antar)</span> the spotless <span class="pv-w">(vimala)</span> Mirror <span class="pv-w">(darpaṇa)</span> _(known as)_ the State <span class="pv-w">(para)</span> of the Supreme <span class="pv-w">(para)</span> Bhairava <span class="pv-w">(bhairava)</span> who is that <span class="pv-w">(tad)</span> _<span class="pv-w">(very)</span>_ Anuttara <span class="pv-w">(anuttara)</span>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">_[In this way, Abhinavagupta closes the "Bimba" or "Mirror" section of the scripture, in which the Real Nature of the things was dealt with, and now he opens the "Pratibimba" <span class="pv-note">— Reflection —</span> section of the scripture, in which all that is reflected in that Mirror of Consciousness is going to be studied. The "Bimba" section has to do with a withdrawal into one's own essential nature, while the "Pratibimba" one has to do with the manifestation or expansion of Consciousness in the form of the universe]_||4||.</p>
