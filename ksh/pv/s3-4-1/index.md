## Введение

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">Parātrīśikāvivaraṇa continues. Abhinavagupta begins commenting on the stanzas 3 and 4 of Parātrīśikā.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">This is the first part of the commentary on the stanzas 3-4.</p>

Разумеется, я буду приводить и сами исходные строфы, которые Абхинавагупта комментирует. Под переводом я буду писать «сплошное длинное пояснение»: если, как обычно, пользоваться отдельными «примечаниями», примечаний окажется столько, что работать с ними станет невозможно. К тому же запрограммировать такое количество примечаний, одно за другим, отняло бы уйму времени. Этим «сплошным длинным пояснением» я постараюсь сделать текст настолько понятным обычному читателю, насколько сумею!

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">Therefore <span class="pv-w">(tad)</span> here <span class="pv-w">(atra)</span>, with reference to the question in its entirety <span class="pv-w">(praśna-sarvasve)</span>:</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">This <span class="pv-note">— the Past Tense in the word "said" —</span> <span class="pv-w">(etad)</span> was _<span class="pv-w">(already)</span>_ explained <span class="pv-w">(vyākhyātam)</span> previously <span class="pv-w">(prāk eva)</span>. What is the point <span class="pv-w">(kim)</span> of speaking about _<span class="pv-w">(it)</span>_ <span class="pv-w">(uktatā-āpādanena)</span> again <span class="pv-w">(punar)</span>? Bhairava <span class="pv-w">(bhairavaḥ)</span> is <span class="pv-w">(ātmakaḥ)</span> the One who supports and carries _(the universe)_ <span class="pv-w">(bharaṇa)</span> and <span class="pv-w">(ca)</span> is <span class="pv-w">(ātmakaḥ)</span> the sound <span class="pv-w">(rava)</span> of the Great <span class="pv-w">(mahā)</span> Mantra <span class="pv-w">(mantra)</span> <span class="pv-note">— Aham or "I" —</span>. In this context <span class="pv-w">(atra)</span> _(there is)_ only <span class="pv-w">(kevalam)</span> predominance <span class="pv-w">(prādhānyam)</span> _(of Bhairava as)_ the Possessor of Śakti <span class="pv-w">(śaktimat)</span> in the form of <span class="pv-w">(rūpeṇa)</span> withdrawal <span class="pv-w">(saṁhāra)</span>, i.e. "ma-ha-a" (ma-ha-a iti) whose "nature" <span class="pv-w">(rūpam iti)</span> has thus been described (evam... uktam) before <span class="pv-w">(prāk)</span> _(as well)_, and <span class="pv-w">(ca)</span> it will be made clear <span class="pv-w">(sphuṭī-bhaviṣyati)</span> in the first place (agratas eva) _(later on)_.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">Consequently <span class="pv-w">(tad)</span>, the meaning of the sentence _("Venerable Bhairava said")_ <span class="pv-w">(tātparya-arthaḥ)</span> _<span class="pv-w">(is)</span>_ here <span class="pv-w">(atra)</span> the following <span class="pv-note">— lit. of such qualities —</span> <span class="pv-w">(iyān)</span>:</p>

I pay homage <span class="pv-w">(vande)</span> to the God (tam... devam) who is an Ocean <span class="pv-w">(sāgaram)</span> of Bliss <span class="pv-w">(ānanda... iti)</span>".</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">**_7)_** _<span class="pv-w">(Abhinavagupta will now interpret "Mahābhāgā" as an attributive compound consisting of "ma-ha-a-bhāgā":)</span>_ As <span class="pv-w">(yad)</span> has been established <span class="pv-w">(uktam)</span> by the aforesaid method <span class="pv-w">(prāk-nayena)</span>, _<span class="pv-w">(She is known as Mahābhāgā because of Her being One)</span>_ whose <span class="pv-w">(yasyāḥ)</span> form <span class="pv-w">(rūpam)</span> is "ma-ha-a" <span class="pv-w">(ma-ha-a)</span> _<span class="pv-w">(and)</span>_ that very _<span class="pv-w">(form)</span>_ (tad eva) is worthy of reverence <span class="pv-w">(bhajanīyam)</span> <span class="pv-note">— in a nutshell, She is called "Mahābhāgā" because She is One whose form is the revered "ma-ha-a" —</span>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">That <span class="pv-w">(tad)</span> very <span class="pv-w">(eva)</span> "Aham" <span class="pv-note">— "I" —</span> (aham iti) which <span class="pv-w">(yad)</span> is verily nourished <span class="pv-w">(hi... bṛṁhitam)</span> by the own <span class="pv-w">(sva)</span> Delight <span class="pv-w">(camatkāra)</span> of the Supreme Lord <span class="pv-w">(parama-īśvarasya)</span> has the beautiful form <span class="pv-w">(vapus)</span> of Śakti <span class="pv-w">(śāktam)</span>. _(In other words,)_ that _("Aham")_ (tad eva ca) is said to be <span class="pv-w">(ucyate)</span> of the nature <span class="pv-w">(rūpam)</span> of the Most Venerable <span class="pv-w">(bhaṭṭārikā)</span> Parā <span class="pv-note">— the Supreme Śakti —</span> <span class="pv-w">(parā)</span>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">For this very reason (atas eva), She <span class="pv-w">(sā eva ca)</span>, the Supreme Mistress <span class="pv-w">(parama-īśvarī)</span> hears <span class="pv-w">(śṛṇoti)</span> all <span class="pv-w">(sarvam)</span>. Remaining <span class="pv-w">(tiṣṭhantī)</span> as the reality <span class="pv-w">(sattayā)</span> called <span class="pv-w">(ākhyayā)</span> power of hearing <span class="pv-w">(śravaṇa)</span>, She has Absolute Freedom <span class="pv-note">— lit. Absolute Freedom is of Hers —</span> <span class="pv-w">(tasyāḥ... svātantryam)</span> known as <span class="pv-w">(ākhyam)</span> a congruous or suitable connection <span class="pv-w">(anusandhāna)</span> derived from the act of joining together <span class="pv-w">(saṅkalana)</span> whose essence is producing <span class="pv-w">(āpādana-rūpa)</span> unity <span class="pv-w">(aikātmya)</span>. _(This Absolute Freedom then)_ refers to <span class="pv-w">(niṣṭham)</span> the multitude <span class="pv-w">(rāśi)</span> of letters <span class="pv-w">(varṇa)</span> consisting of <span class="pv-w">(maya)</span> their own <span class="pv-w">(sva)</span> successive <span class="pv-w">(kramika)</span> vibrations <span class="pv-w">(spanda)</span> _(which sound)_ clear <span class="pv-w">(sphuṭa)</span> to the ears <span class="pv-note">— lit. auditory concavity —</span> <span class="pv-w">(śravaṇa-sampuṭa)</span>. Without <span class="pv-w">(vinā)</span> It <span class="pv-note">— without that Absolute Freedom of Hers joining letters together into coherent words and sentences —</span> (tena hi), an experient <span class="pv-note">— a subject —</span> <span class="pv-w">(pramātā)</span>, even <span class="pv-w">(api)</span> hearing <span class="pv-w">(śṛṇvan)</span> particular <span class="pv-w">(viśeṣam)</span> words <span class="pv-w">(śabda)</span> dissolved in <span class="pv-w">(līna)</span> a confused noise <span class="pv-w">(kalakala)</span>, commonly behaves _(this way)_ <span class="pv-w">(vyavaharati)</span>: "I do not hear <span class="pv-w">(na śṛṇomi iti)</span>"... _(this is what he would say)_.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">However <span class="pv-w">(tu)</span>, an object <span class="pv-w">(viṣayam eva)</span> which is merely <span class="pv-w">(mātra)</span> a confused noise <span class="pv-w">(kalakala)</span>, i.e. "_(which is merely the result of)_ the act of joining _<span class="pv-w">(sounds)</span>_ together" <span class="pv-w">(saṅkalanam iti)</span>, in common usage <span class="pv-note">— in the ordinary life —</span> <span class="pv-w">(vyavahāraḥ)</span> that _(is also said)_ to be heard <span class="pv-w">(tatra eva śrutam iti)</span>. But <span class="pv-w">(tu)</span> as a matter of fact <span class="pv-w">(vastutas)</span>, that <span class="pv-w">(saḥ)</span> sound <span class="pv-w">(dhvaniḥ)</span> appearing as a confused noise <span class="pv-w">(kalakala)</span>, _<span class="pv-w">(though)</span>_ it enters <span class="pv-w">(anupraviśan)</span> into the auditory space <span class="pv-note">— the ear —</span> <span class="pv-w">(śrotra-ākāśe)</span>, by not allowing the words to enter <span class="pv-w">(na varṇān anupraveśayan)</span>, remains <span class="pv-w">(bhavet)</span> like that <span class="pv-w">(tathā)</span> <span class="pv-note">— like confused noise —</span> due to the presence <span class="pv-w">(bhāvāt)</span> of confused noise (kalakalasya eva) different from <span class="pv-w">(atiriktasya)</span> those <span class="pv-w">(tad)</span> letters <span class="pv-w">(varṇa)</span>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">When there is the intention to speak <span class="pv-w">(vivakṣāyām)</span> those <span class="pv-w">(tad)</span> particular <span class="pv-w">(viśeṣa)</span> letters <span class="pv-w">(varṇa... ca)</span>, there is no production <span class="pv-note">— lit. there is non-production —</span> <span class="pv-w">(anutpattiḥ syāt)</span> of confused noise (kalakalasya ca) because of the absence <span class="pv-w">(abhāvāt eva)</span> of its cause <span class="pv-w">(kāraṇa)</span>. During the act of forming <span class="pv-w">(kāryatve)</span> words <span class="pv-w">(śabda)</span> made out of <span class="pv-w">(maya)</span> clear <span class="pv-w">(sphuṭa)</span> letters <span class="pv-w">(varṇa)</span> arising <span class="pv-w">(utpanna)</span> from that <span class="pv-w">(tad)</span> intention to speak <span class="pv-w">(vivakṣā)</span>, it is impossible that there is confused noise <span class="pv-w">(anupapatteḥ kalakalasya)</span> on account of the production <span class="pv-w">(utpattyā)</span> of words <span class="pv-w">(śabda)</span> of the same kind <span class="pv-note">— i.e. so clear as the aforesaid letters such words are composed of —</span> <span class="pv-w">(sajātīya)</span>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">_<span class="pv-w">(When)</span>_ those very (te eva) letters <span class="pv-w">(varṇāḥ)</span> do not join together <span class="pv-w">(saṅkalanām agacchantaḥ)</span> entirely <span class="pv-w">(sarvathā)</span> in that clear form <span class="pv-w">(tena sphuṭa-rūpeṇa)</span>, _<span class="pv-w">(then)</span>_ they express <span class="pv-w">(vācyāḥ)</span> words <span class="pv-w">(śabda)</span> _(which sound like)_ confused noise <span class="pv-w">(kalakala)</span>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">For someone who is attentively intent on <span class="pv-w">(avadhāna-udyuktasya)</span> joining that together <span class="pv-note">— that mass of words sounding like confused noise —</span> <span class="pv-w">(tad-saṅkalana)</span> there may be (bhavet eva) a clear <span class="pv-w">(sphuṭa)</span> understanding _(of their meaning)_ <span class="pv-w">(upalambhaḥ)</span> but only in a little measure <span class="pv-w">(kiyat-mātra)</span>. In this way <span class="pv-w">(iti)</span>, the act of joining _<span class="pv-w">(letters)</span>_ together _(into clear words)_ <span class="pv-w">(saṅkalanam eva)</span> is of some use <span class="pv-w">(upayogi)</span> here <span class="pv-w">(atra)</span>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">And <span class="pv-w">(ca)</span> She Herself <span class="pv-w">(sā eva)</span>, the Consort of the Fortunate One <span class="pv-w">(bhagavatī)</span>, the Highest One <span class="pv-w">(parā)</span>, the Supreme Mistress <span class="pv-w">(parama-īśvarī)</span>, performs <span class="pv-w">(karoti)</span> _(such an)_ act of joining _<span class="pv-w">(letters)</span>_ together _(into clear words)_ <span class="pv-w">(saṅkalanam)</span>.</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">As <span class="pv-w">(yad)</span> has been said <span class="pv-w">(uktam)</span> _<span class="pv-w">(in Spandakārikā-s II.1 by the sage Vasugupta:)</span>_</p>

<p class="pv-en" lang="en">"Grabbing hold <span class="pv-w">(ākramya)</span> of that <span class="pv-w">(tad)</span> Force <span class="pv-w">(balam)</span>, the Mantra-s <span class="pv-w">(mantrāḥ)</span>".</p>

<p class="pv-iast">etcetera (iti-ādi)...</p>
