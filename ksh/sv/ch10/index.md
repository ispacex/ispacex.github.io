# Гимн 10 — Avicchedabhaṅgākhyaṁ daśamaṁ stotram

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Гимн 9 — Svātantryavijayākhyaṁ navamaṁ stotram](/ksh/sv/ch9/) · [Гимн 11 — Autsukyaviśvasitanāmaikādaśaṁ stotram →](/ksh/sv/ch11/)</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न सोढव्यमवश्यं ते जगदेकप्रभोरिदम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na soḍhavyamavaśyaṁ te jagadekaprabhoridam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">माहेश्वराश्च लोकानामितरेषां समाश्च यत्॥१०.१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Māheśvarāśca lokānāmitareṣāṁ samāśca yat||10.1||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ये सदैवानुरागेण भवत्पादानुगामिनः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ye sadaivānurāgeṇa bhavatpādānugāminaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यत्र तत्र गता भोगांस्ते कांश्चिदुपभुञ्जते॥१०.२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yatra tatra gatā bhogāṁste kāṁścidupabhuñjate||10.2||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भर्ता कालान्तको यत्र भवांस्तत्र कुतो रुजः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhartā kālāntako yatra bhavāṁstatra kuto rujaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्र चेतरभोगाशा का लक्ष्मीर्यत्र तावकी॥१०.३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatra cetarabhogāśā kā lakṣmīryatra tāvakī||10.3||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">क्षनमात्रसुखेनापि विभुर्येनासि लभ्यसे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kṣanamātrasukhenāpi vibhuryenāsi labhyase|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदैव सर्वः कालोऽस्य त्वदानन्देन पूर्यते॥१०.४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadaiva sarvaḥ kālo'sya tvadānandena pūryate||10.4||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आनन्दरसबिन्दुस्ते चन्द्रमा गलितो भुवि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ānandarasabinduste candramā galito bhuvi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सूर्यस्तथा ते प्रसृतः संहारी तेजसः कणः॥१०.५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sūryastathā te prasṛtaḥ saṁhārī tejasaḥ kaṇaḥ||10.5||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बलिं यामस्तृतीयाय नेत्रायास्मै तव प्रभो।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Baliṁ yāmastṛtīyāya netrāyāsmai tava prabho|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अलौकिकस्य कस्यापि माहात्म्यस्यैकलक्ष्मणे॥१०.६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Alaukikasya kasyāpi māhātmyasyaikalakṣmaṇe||10.6||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तेनैव दृष्टोऽसि भवद्दर्शनाद्योऽतिहृष्यति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tenaiva dṛṣṭo'si bhavaddarśanādyo'tihṛṣyati|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कथञ्चिद्यस्य वा हर्षः कोऽपि तेन त्वमीक्षितः॥१०.७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kathañcidyasya vā harṣaḥ ko'pi tena tvamīkṣitaḥ||10.7||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">येषां प्रसन्नोऽसि विभो यैर्लब्धं हृदयं तव।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yeṣāṁ prasanno'si vibho yairlabdhaṁ hṛdayaṁ tava|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आकृष्य त्वत्पुरात्तैस्तु बाह्यमाभ्यन्तरीकृतम्॥१०.८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ākṛṣya tvatpurāttaistu bāhyamābhyantarīkṛtam||10.8||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वदृते निखिलं विश्वं समदृग्यातमीक्ष्यताम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvadṛte nikhilaṁ viśvaṁ samadṛgyātamīkṣyatām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ईश्वरः पुनरेतस्य त्वमेको विषमेक्षणः॥१०.९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Īśvaraḥ punaretasya tvameko viṣamekṣaṇaḥ||10.9||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आस्तां भवत्प्रभावेण विना सत्तैव नास्ति यत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Āstāṁ bhavatprabhāveṇa vinā sattaiva nāsti yat|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वद्दूषणकथा येषां त्वदृते नोपपद्यते॥१०.१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvaddūṣaṇakathā yeṣāṁ tvadṛte nopapadyate||10.10||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.11" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बाह्यान्तरान्तरायालीकेवले चेतसि स्थितिः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bāhyāntarāntarāyālīkevale cetasi sthitiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वयि चेत्स्यान्मम विभो किमन्यदुपयुज्यते॥१०.११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvayi cetsyānmama vibho kimanyadupayujyate||10.11||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अन्ये भ्रमन्ति भगवन्नात्मन्येवातिदुःस्थिताः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anye bhramanti bhagavannātmanyevātiduḥsthitāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अन्ये भ्रमन्ति भगवन्नात्मन्येवातिसुस्थिताः॥१०.१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anye bhramanti bhagavannātmanyevātisusthitāḥ||10.12||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अपीत्वापि भवद्भक्तिसुधामनवलोक्य च।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Apītvāpi bhavadbhaktisudhāmanavalokya ca|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वामीश त्वत्समाचारमात्रात्सिद्ध्यन्ति जन्तवः॥१०.१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvāmīśa tvatsamācāramātrātsiddhyanti jantavaḥ||10.13||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भृत्या वयं तव विभो तेन त्रिजगतां यथा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhṛtyā vayaṁ tava vibho tena trijagatāṁ yathā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बिभर्ष्यात्मानमेवं ते भर्त्तव्या वयमप्यलम्॥१०.१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bibharṣyātmānamevaṁ te bharttavyā vayamapyalam||10.14||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.15" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">परानन्दामृतमये दृष्टोऽपि जगदात्मनि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Parānandāmṛtamaye dṛṣṭo'pi jagadātmani|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वयि स्पर्शरसेऽत्यन्ततरसुत्कण्ठितोऽस्मि ते॥१०.१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvayi sparśarase'tyantatarasutkaṇṭhito'smi te||10.15||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.16" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">देव दुःखान्यशेषाणि यानि संसारिणामपि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Deva duḥkhānyaśeṣāṇi yāni saṁsāriṇāmapi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">घृत्याख्यभवदीयात्मयुतान्यायान्ति सह्यताम्॥१०.१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ghṛtyākhyabhavadīyātmayutānyāyānti sahyatām||10.16||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.17" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वज्ञे सर्वशक्तौ च त्वय्येव सति चिन्मये।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvajñe sarvaśaktau ca tvayyeva sati cinmaye|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वथाप्यसतो नाथ युक्तास्य जगतः प्रथा॥१०.१७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvathāpyasato nātha yuktāsya jagataḥ prathā||10.17||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.18" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वत्प्राणिताः स्फुरन्तीमे गुणा लोष्टोपमा अपि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvatprāṇitāḥ sphurantīme guṇā loṣṭopamā api|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नृत्यन्ति पवनोद्धूताः कार्पासाः पिचवो यथा॥१०.१८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nṛtyanti pavanoddhūtāḥ kārpāsāḥ picavo yathā||10.18||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.19" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यदि नाथ गुणेष्वात्माभिमानो न भवेत्ततः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yadi nātha guṇeṣvātmābhimāno na bhavettataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">केन हीयेत जगतस्त्वदेकात्मतया प्रथा॥१०.१९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kena hīyeta jagatastvadekātmatayā prathā||10.19||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.20" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वन्द्यास्तेऽपि महीयांसः प्रलयोपगता अपि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vandyāste'pi mahīyāṁsaḥ pralayopagatā api|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वत्कोपपावकस्पर्शपूता ये परमेश्वर॥१०.२०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvatkopapāvakasparśapūtā ye parameśvara||10.20||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.21" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">महाप्रकाशवपुषि विस्पष्टे भवति स्थिते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mahāprakāśavapuṣi vispaṣṭe bhavati sthite|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वतोऽपीश तत्कस्मात्तमसि प्रसराम्यहम्॥१०.२१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvato'pīśa tatkasmāttamasi prasarāmyaham||10.21||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.22" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अविभागो भवानेव स्वरूपममृतं मम।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Avibhāgo bhavāneva svarūpamamṛtaṁ mama|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तथापि मर्त्यधर्माणामहमेवैकमास्पदम्॥१०.२२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tathāpi martyadharmāṇāmahamevaikamāspadam||10.22||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.23" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">महेश्वरेति यस्यास्ति नामकं वाग्विभूषणम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Maheśvareti yasyāsti nāmakaṁ vāgvibhūṣaṇam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रणामाङ्कश्च शिरसि स एवैकः प्रभावितः॥१०.२३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Praṇāmāṅkaśca śirasi sa evaikaḥ prabhāvitaḥ||10.23||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.24" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सदसच्च भवानेव येन तेनाप्रयासतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sadasacca bhavāneva yena tenāprayāsataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वरसेनैव भगवंस्तथा सिद्धिः कथं न मे॥१०.२४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svarasenaiva bhagavaṁstathā siddhiḥ kathaṁ na me||10.24||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.25" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शिवदासः शिवैकात्मा किं यन्नासादयेत्सुखम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śivadāsaḥ śivaikātmā kiṁ yannāsādayetsukham|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तर्प्योऽस्मि देवमुख्यानामपि येनामृतासवैः॥१०.२५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tarpyo'smi devamukhyānāmapi yenāmṛtāsavaiḥ||10.25||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v10.26" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हृन्नाभ्योरन्तरालस्थः प्राणिनां पित्तविग्रहः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Hṛnnābhyorantarālasthaḥ prāṇināṁ pittavigrahaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ग्रससे त्वं महावह्निः सर्वं स्थावरजङ्गमम्॥१०.२६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Grasase tvaṁ mahāvahniḥ sarvaṁ sthāvarajaṅgamam||10.26||</span></p>
