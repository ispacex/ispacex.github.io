# Гимн 20 — Carvarṇābhidhānaṁ viṁśaṁ stotram

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Гимн 19 — Udyotanābhidhānamekonaviṁśaṁ stotram](/ksh/sv/ch19/)</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नथं त्रिभुवननाथं भूतिसितं त्रिनयनं त्रिशूलधरम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nathaṁ tribhuvananāthaṁ bhūtisitaṁ trinayanaṁ triśūladharam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उपवीतीकृतभोगिनमिन्दुकलाशेखरं वन्दे॥२०.१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Upavītīkṛtabhoginamindukalāśekharaṁ vande||20.1||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नौमि निजतनुविनिस्मरदंशुकपरिवेषधवलपरिधानम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Naumi nijatanuvinismaradaṁśukapariveṣadhavalaparidhānam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विलसत्कपालमालाकल्पितनृत्तोत्सवाकल्पम्॥२०.२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vilasatkapālamālākalpitanṛttotsavākalpam||20.2||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वन्दे तान्दैवतं येषां हरश्चेष्टा हरोचिताः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vande tāndaivataṁ yeṣāṁ haraśceṣṭā harocitāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हरैकप्रवणाः प्राणाः सदा सौभाग्यसद्मनाम्॥२०.३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Haraikapravaṇāḥ prāṇāḥ sadā saubhāgyasadmanām||20.3||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">क्रीडितं तव महेश्वरतायाः पृष्ठतोऽन्यदिदमेव यथैतत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Krīḍitaṁ tava maheśvaratāyāḥ pṛṣṭhato'nyadidameva yathaitat|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इष्टमात्रघटितेष्ववदानेष्वात्मना परमुपायमुपैमि॥२०.४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Iṣṭamātraghaṭiteṣvavadāneṣvātmanā paramupāyamupaimi||20.4||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वद्धाम्नि विश्ववन्द्येऽस्मिन्नियति क्रीडने सति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvaddhāmni viśvavandye'sminniyati krīḍane sati|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तव नाथ कियान्भूयान्नानन्दरससम्भवः॥२०.५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tava nātha kiyānbhūyānnānandarasasambhavaḥ||20.5||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कथं स सुभगो मा भूद्यो गौर्या वल्लभो हरः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kathaṁ sa subhago mā bhūdyo gauryā vallabho haraḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हरोऽपि मा भूदथ किं गौर्याः परमवल्लभः॥२०.६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Haro'pi mā bhūdatha kiṁ gauryāḥ paramavallabhaḥ||20.6||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ध्यानामृतमयं यस्य स्वात्ममूलमनश्वरम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dhyānāmṛtamayaṁ yasya svātmamūlamanaśvaram|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">संविल्लतास्तथारूपास्तस्य कस्यापि सत्तरोः॥२०.७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Saṁvillatāstathārūpāstasya kasyāpi sattaroḥ||20.7||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तिकण्डूसमुल्लासावसरे परमेश्वर।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktikaṇḍūsamullāsāvasare parameśvara|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">महानिकषपाषाणस्थूणा पूजैव जायते॥२०.८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mahānikaṣapāṣāṇasthūṇā pūjaiva jāyate||20.8||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सदा स्र्ष्टिविनोदाय सदा स्थितिसुखासिने।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sadā srṣṭivinodāya sadā sthitisukhāsine|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सदा त्रिभुवनाहारतृप्ताय स्वामिने नमः॥२०.९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sadā tribhuvanāhāratṛptāya svāmine namaḥ||20.9||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न क्वापि गत्वा हित्वापि न किञ्चिदिदमेव ये।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na kvāpi gatvā hitvāpi na kiñcididameva ye|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भव्यं त्वद्धाम पश्यन्ति भव्यास्तेभ्यो नमो नमः॥२०.१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavyaṁ tvaddhāma paśyanti bhavyāstebhyo namo namaḥ||20.10||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.11" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तिलक्ष्मीसमृद्धानां किमन्यदुपयाचितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktilakṣmīsamṛddhānāṁ kimanyadupayācitam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एतया वा दरिद्रणां किमन्यदुपयाचितम्॥२०.११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Etayā vā daridraṇāṁ kimanyadupayācitam||20.11||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दुःखान्यपि सुखायन्ते विषमप्यमृतायते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Duḥkhānyapi sukhāyante viṣamapyamṛtāyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मोक्षायते च संसारो यत्र मार्गः स शङ्करः॥२०.१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mokṣāyate ca saṁsāro yatra mārgaḥ sa śaṅkaraḥ||20.12||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मूले मध्येऽवसाने च नास्ति दुःखं भवज्जुषां।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mūle madhye'vasāne ca nāsti duḥkhaṁ bhavajjuṣāṁ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तथापि वयमीशान सीदामः कथमुच्यताम्॥२०.१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tathāpi vayamīśāna sīdāmaḥ kathamucyatām||20.13||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ज्ञानयोगादिनान्येषामप्यपेक्षितुमर्हति।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jñānayogādinānyeṣāmapyapekṣitumarhati|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रकाशः स्वैरिणामिव भवान्भक्तिमतां प्रभो॥२०.१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prakāśaḥ svairiṇāmiva bhavānbhaktimatāṁ prabho||20.14||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.15" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तानां नार्तयो नाप्यस्त्याध्यानं स्वात्मनस्तव।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktānāṁ nārtayo nāpyastyādhyānaṁ svātmanastava|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तथाप्यस्ति शिवेत्येतत्किमप्येषां बहिर्मुखे॥२०.१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tathāpyasti śivetyetatkimapyeṣāṁ bahirmukhe||20.15||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.16" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वाभासावभासो यो विमर्शवलितोऽखिलम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvābhāsāvabhāso yo vimarśavalito'khilam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अहमेतदिति स्तौमि तां क्रियाशक्तिमीश ते॥२०.१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ahametaditi staumi tāṁ kriyāśaktimīśa te||20.16||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.17" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वर्तन्ते जन्तवोऽशेषा अपि ब्रह्मेन्द्रविष्णवः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vartante jantavo'śeṣā api brahmendraviṣṇavaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ग्रसमानास्ततो वन्दे देव विश्वं भवन्मयम्॥२०.१७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Grasamānāstato vande deva viśvaṁ bhavanmayam||20.17||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.18" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सतो विनाशसम्बन्धान्मत्परं निखिलं मृषा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sato vināśasambandhānmatparaṁ nikhilaṁ mṛṣā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एवमेवोद्यते नाथ त्वया संहारलीलया॥२०.१८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Evamevodyate nātha tvayā saṁhāralīlayā||20.18||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.19" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ध्यातमात्मुपतिष्ठत एव त्वद्वपुर्वरद भक्तिधनानाम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dhyātamātmupatiṣṭhata eva tvadvapurvarada bhaktidhanānām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अप्यचिन्त्यमखिलाद्भुतचिन्ताकर्तृतां प्रति च ते विजयन्ते॥२०.१९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Apyacintyamakhilādbhutacintākartṛtāṁ prati ca te vijayante||20.19||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.20" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तावकभक्तिरसासवसेकादिव सुखितमर्ममण्डलस्फुरितैः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tāvakabhaktirasāsavasekādiva sukhitamarmamaṇḍalasphuritaiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नृत्यति वीरजनो निशि वेतालकुलैः कृतोत्साहः॥२०.२०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nṛtyati vīrajano niśi vetālakulaiḥ kṛtotsāhaḥ||20.20||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v20.21" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आरब्धा भवदभिनुतिरमुना येनाङ्गकेन मम शम्भो।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ārabdhā bhavadabhinutiramunā yenāṅgakena mama śambho|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तेनापर्यन्तमिमं कालं दृढमखिलमेव भविषीष्ट॥२०.२१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tenāparyantamimaṁ kālaṁ dṛḍhamakhilameva bhaviṣīṣṭa||20.21||</span></p>
