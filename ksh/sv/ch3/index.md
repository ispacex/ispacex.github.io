# Гимн 3 — Praṇayaprasādākhyaṁ tṛtīyaṁ stotram

<p class="pv-pager nosearch" markdown="1">[← Гимн 2 — Sarvātmaparibhāvanākhyaṁ dvitīyaṁ stotram](/ksh/sv/ch2/) · [Гимн 4 — Surasodbalākhyaṁ caturthaṁ stotram →](/ksh/sv/ch4/)</p>

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सदसत्त्वेन भावानां युक्ता या द्वितयी गतिः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sadasattvena bhāvānāṁ yuktā yā dvitayī gatiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तामुल्लङ्घ्य तृतीयस्मै नमश्चित्राय शम्भवे॥३.१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tāmullaṅghya tṛtīyasmai namaścitrāya śambhave||3.1||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आसुरर्षिजनादस्मिन्नस्वतन्त्रे जगत्त्रये।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Āsurarṣijanādasminnasvatantre jagattraye|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वतन्त्रास्ते स्वतन्त्रस्य ये तवैवानुजीविनः॥३.२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svatantrāste svatantrasya ye tavaivānujīvinaḥ||3.2||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अशेषविश्वखचितभवद्वपुरनुस्मृतिः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aśeṣaviśvakhacitabhavadvapuranusmṛtiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">येषां भवरुजामेकं भेषजं ते सुखासिनः॥३.३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yeṣāṁ bhavarujāmekaṁ bheṣajaṁ te sukhāsinaḥ||3.3||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सितातपत्रं यस्येन्दुः स्वप्रभापरिपूरितः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sitātapatraṁ yasyenduḥ svaprabhāparipūritaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चामरं स्वर्धुनीस्रोतः स एकः परमेश्वरः॥३.४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Cāmaraṁ svardhunīsrotaḥ sa ekaḥ parameśvaraḥ||3.4||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रकाशां शीतलामेकां शुद्धां शशिकलामिव।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prakāśāṁ śītalāmekāṁ śuddhāṁ śaśikalāmiva|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दृशं वितर मे नाथ कामप्यमृतवाहिनीम्॥३.५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dṛśaṁ vitara me nātha kāmapyamṛtavāhinīm||3.5||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वच्चिदानन्दजलधेश्च्युताः संवित्तिविप्रुषः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvaccidānandajaladheścyutāḥ saṁvittivipruṣaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इमाः कथं मे भगवन्नामृतास्वादसुन्दराः॥३.६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Imāḥ kathaṁ me bhagavannāmṛtāsvādasundarāḥ||3.6||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वयि रागरसे नाथ न मग्नं हृदयं प्रभो।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvayi rāgarase nātha na magnaṁ hṛdayaṁ prabho|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">येषामहृदया एव तेऽवज्ञस्पदमीदृशाः॥३.७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yeṣāmahṛdayā eva te'vajñaspadamīdṛśāḥ||3.7||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रभुणा भवता यस्य जातं हृदयमेलनम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prabhuṇā bhavatā yasya jātaṁ hṛdayamelanam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्राभवीणां विभूतीनां परमेकः स भाजनम्॥३.८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prābhavīṇāṁ vibhūtīnāṁ paramekaḥ sa bhājanam||3.8||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हर्षाणामथ शोकानां सर्वेषां प्लावकः समम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Harṣāṇāmatha śokānāṁ sarveṣāṁ plāvakaḥ samam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवद्ध्यानामृतापूरो निम्नाणिम्नभुवामिव॥३.९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavaddhyānāmṛtāpūro nimnāṇimnabhuvāmiva||3.9||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">केव न स्याद्दृशा तेषां सुखसम्भारनिर्भरा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Keva na syāddṛśā teṣāṁ sukhasambhāranirbharā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">येषामात्माधिकेनेश न क्वापि विरहस्त्वया॥३.१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yeṣāmātmādhikeneśa na kvāpi virahastvayā||3.10||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.11" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">गर्जामि बत नृत्यामि पूर्णा मम मनोरथाः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Garjāmi bata nṛtyāmi pūrṇā mama manorathāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वामी ममैष घटितो यत्त्वमत्यन्तरोचनः॥३.११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svāmī mamaiṣa ghaṭito yattvamatyantarocanaḥ||3.11||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नान्यद्वेद्यं क्रिया यत्र नान्यो योगो विदा च यत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nānyadvedyaṁ kriyā yatra nānyo yogo vidā ca yat|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ज्ञानं स्यात्किं तु विश्वैकपूर्णा चित्त्वं विजृम्भते॥३.१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jñānaṁ syātkiṁ tu viśvaikapūrṇā cittvaṁ vijṛmbhate||3.12||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दुर्जयानामनन्तानां दुःखानां सहसैव ते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Durjayānāmanantānāṁ duḥkhānāṁ sahasaiva te|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हस्तात्पलायिता येषां वाचि शश्वच्छिवध्वनिः॥३.१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Hastātpalāyitā yeṣāṁ vāci śaśvacchivadhvaniḥ||3.13||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उत्तमः पुरुषोऽन्योऽस्ति युष्मच्छेषविशेषितः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Uttamaḥ puruṣo'nyo'sti yuṣmaccheṣaviśeṣitaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वं महापुरुषस्त्वेको निःशेषपुरुषाश्रयः॥३.१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvaṁ mahāpuruṣastveko niḥśeṣapuruṣāśrayaḥ||3.14||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.15" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जयन्ति ते जगद्वन्द्या दासास्ते जगतां विभो।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jayanti te jagadvandyā dāsāste jagatāṁ vibho|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">संसारार्णव एवैष येषां क्रीडामहासरः॥३.१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Saṁsārārṇava evaiṣa yeṣāṁ krīḍāmahāsaraḥ||3.15||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.16" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आसतां तावदन्यानि दैन्यानीह भवज्जुषाम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Āsatāṁ tāvadanyāni dainyānīha bhavajjuṣām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वमेव प्रकटीभूया इत्यनेनैव लज्ज्यते॥३.१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvameva prakaṭībhūyā ityanenaiva lajjyate||3.16||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.17" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मत्परं नास्ति तत्रापि जापकोऽस्मि तदैक्यतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Matparaṁ nāsti tatrāpi jāpako'smi tadaikyataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्त्वेन जप इत्यक्षमालया दिशसि क्वचित्॥३.१७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tattvena japa ityakṣamālayā diśasi kvacit||3.17||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.18" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सतोऽवश्यं परमसत्सच्च तस्मात्परं प्रभो।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sato'vaśyaṁ paramasatsacca tasmātparaṁ prabho|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वं चासतः सतश्चान्यस्तेनासि सदसन्मयः॥३.१८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvaṁ cāsataḥ sataścānyastenāsi sadasanmayaḥ||3.18||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.19" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सहस्रसूर्यकिरणाधिकशुद्धप्रकाशवान्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sahasrasūryakiraṇādhikaśuddhaprakāśavān|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अपि त्वं सर्वभुवनव्यापकोऽपि न दृश्यसे॥३.१९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Api tvaṁ sarvabhuvanavyāpako'pi na dṛśyase||3.19||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.20" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जडे जगति चिद्रूपः किल वेद्येऽपि वेदकः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jaḍe jagati cidrūpaḥ kila vedye'pi vedakaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विभुर्मिते च येनासि तेन सर्वोत्तमो भवान्॥३.२०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vibhurmite ca yenāsi tena sarvottamo bhavān||3.20||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v3.21" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अलमाक्रन्दितैरन्यैरियदेव पुरः प्रभोः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Alamākranditairanyairiyadeva puraḥ prabhoḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तीव्रं विरौमि यन्नाथ मुह्याम्येवं विदन्नपि॥३.२१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tīvraṁ viraumi yannātha muhyāmyevaṁ vidannapi||3.21||</span></p>
