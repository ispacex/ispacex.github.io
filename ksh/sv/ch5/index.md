# Гимн 5 — Svabalanideśanākhyaṁ pañcamaṁ stotram

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वत्पादपद्मसम्पर्कमात्रसम्भोगसङ्गिनम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvatpādapadmasamparkamātrasambhogasaṅginam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">गलेपादिकया नाथ मां स्ववेश्म प्रवेशय॥५.१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Galepādikayā nātha māṁ svaveśma praveśaya||5.1||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवत्पादाम्बुजरजोराजिरञ्जितमूर्धजः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavatpādāmbujarajorājirañjitamūrdhajaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अपाररभसारब्धनर्तनः स्यामहं कदा॥५.२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Apārarabhasārabdhanartanaḥ syāmahaṁ kadā||5.2||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वदेकनाथो भगवन्नियदेवार्थये सदा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvadekanātho bhagavanniyadevārthaye sadā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वदन्तर्वसतिर्मूको भवेयं मान्यथा बुधः॥५.३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvadantarvasatirmūko bhaveyaṁ mānyathā budhaḥ||5.3||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अहो सुधानिधे स्वामिन्नहो मृष्ट त्रिलोचन।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aho sudhānidhe svāminnaho mṛṣṭa trilocana|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अहो स्वादो विरूपक्षेत्येव नृत्येयमारटन्॥५.४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aho svādo virūpakṣetyeva nṛtyeyamāraṭan||5.4||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वत्पादपद्मसंस्पर्शपरिमीलितलोचनः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvatpādapadmasaṁsparśaparimīlitalocanaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विजृम्भेयभवद्भक्तिमदिरामदघूर्णितः॥५.५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vijṛmbheyabhavadbhaktimadirāmadaghūrṇitaḥ||5.5||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चित्तभूभृद्भुवि विभो वसेयं क्वापि यत्र सा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Cittabhūbhṛdbhuvi vibho vaseyaṁ kvāpi yatra sā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">निरन्तरत्वत्प्रलापमयी वृत्तिर्महारसा॥५.६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Nirantaratvatpralāpamayī vṛttirmahārasā||5.6||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यत्र देवीसमेतस्त्वमासौधादा च गोपुरात्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yatra devīsametastvamāsaudhādā ca gopurāt|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बहुरूपः स्थितस्तस्मिन्वास्तव्यः स्यामहं पुरे॥५.७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bahurūpaḥ sthitastasminvāstavyaḥ syāmahaṁ pure||5.7||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">समुल्लसन्तु भगवन्भवद्भानुमरीचयः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Samullasantu bhagavanbhavadbhānumarīcayaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विकसत्वेष यावन्मे हृत्पद्मः पूजनाय ते॥५.८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vikasatveṣa yāvanme hṛtpadmaḥ pūjanāya te||5.8||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रसीद भगवन्येन त्वत्पदे पतितं सदा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prasīda bhagavanyena tvatpade patitaṁ sadā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मनो मे तत्तदास्वाद्य क्षीवेदिव गलेदिव॥५.९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mano me tattadāsvādya kṣīvediva galediva||5.9||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रहर्षाद्वाथ शोकाद्वा यदि कुङ्याद्धटादपि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Praharṣādvātha śokādvā yadi kuṅyāddhaṭādapi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बाह्यादथान्तराद्भावात्प्रकटीभव मे प्रभो॥५.१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bāhyādathāntarādbhāvātprakaṭībhava me prabho||5.10||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.11" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">बहिरप्यन्तरपि तत्स्यन्दमानं सदास्तु मे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bahirapyantarapi tatsyandamānaṁ sadāstu me|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवत्पादाम्बुजस्पर्शामृतमत्यन्तशीतलम्॥५.११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavatpādāmbujasparśāmṛtamatyantaśītalam||5.11||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वत्पादसंस्पर्शसुधासरसोऽन्तर्निमज्जनम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvatpādasaṁsparśasudhāsaraso'ntarnimajjanam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कोऽप्येष सर्वसम्भोगलङ्घी भोगोऽस्तु मे सदा॥५.१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ko'pyeṣa sarvasambhogalaṅghī bhogo'stu me sadā||5.12||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">निवेदितमुपादत्स्व रागादि भगवन्मया।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Niveditamupādatsva rāgādi bhagavanmayā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आदाय चामृतीकृत्य भुङ्क्ष्व भक्तजनैः समम्॥५.१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ādāya cāmṛtīkṛtya bhuṅkṣva bhaktajanaiḥ samam||5.13||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अशेषभुवनाहारनित्यतृप्तः सुखासनम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aśeṣabhuvanāhāranityatṛptaḥ sukhāsanam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वामिन्गृहाण दासेषु प्रसादालोकनक्षणम्॥५.१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svāmingṛhāṇa dāseṣu prasādālokanakṣaṇam||5.14||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.15" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अन्तर्भक्तिचमत्कारचर्वणामीलितेक्षणः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Antarbhakticamatkāracarvaṇāmīlitekṣaṇaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नमो मह्यं शिवायेति पूजयन्स्यां तृणान्यपि॥५.१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Namo mahyaṁ śivāyeti pūjayansyāṁ tṛṇānyapi||5.15||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.16" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अपि लब्धभवद्भावः स्वात्मोल्लासमयं जगत्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Api labdhabhavadbhāvaḥ svātmollāsamayaṁ jagat|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पश्यन्भक्तिरसाभोगैर्भवेयमवियोजितः॥५.१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Paśyanbhaktirasābhogairbhaveyamaviyojitaḥ||5.16||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.17" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आकाम्क्षणीयमपरं येन नाथ न विद्यते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ākāmkṣaṇīyamaparaṁ yena nātha na vidyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तव तेनाद्वितीयस्य युक्तं यत्परिपूर्णता॥५.१७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tava tenādvitīyasya yuktaṁ yatparipūrṇatā||5.17||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.18" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">हस्यते नृत्यते यत्र रागद्वेषादि भुज्यते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Hasyate nṛtyate yatra rāgadveṣādi bhujyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पीयते भक्तिपीयूषरसस्तत्प्राप्नुयां पदम्॥५.१८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pīyate bhaktipīyūṣarasastatprāpnuyāṁ padam||5.18||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.19" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तत्तदपूर्वामोदत्वच्चिन्ताकुसुमवासना दृढताम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tattadapūrvāmodatvaccintākusumavāsanā dṛḍhatām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एतु मम मनसि यावन्नश्यतु दुर्वासनागन्धः॥५.१९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Etu mama manasi yāvannaśyatu durvāsanāgandhaḥ||5.19||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.20" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">क्व नु रागादिषु रागः क्व च हरचरणाम्बुजेषु रागित्वम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kva nu rāgādiṣu rāgaḥ kva ca haracaraṇāmbujeṣu rāgitvam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इत्थं विरोधरसिकं बोधय हितममर मे हृदयम्॥५.२०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Itthaṁ virodharasikaṁ bodhaya hitamamara me hṛdayam||5.20||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.21" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विचरन्योगदशास्वपि विषयव्यावृत्तिवर्तमानोऽपि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vicaranyogadaśāsvapi viṣayavyāvṛttivartamāno'pi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वच्चिन्तामदिरामदतरलीकृतहृदय एव स्याम्॥५.२१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvaccintāmadirāmadataralīkṛtahṛdaya eva syām||5.21||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.22" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वाचि मनोमतिषु तथा शरीरचेष्टासु करणरचितासु।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vāci manomatiṣu tathā śarīraceṣṭāsu karaṇaracitāsu|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वत्र सर्वदा मे पुरःसरो भवतु भक्तिरसः॥५.२२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvatra sarvadā me puraḥsaro bhavatu bhaktirasaḥ||5.22||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.23" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शिवशिवशिवेति नामनि तव निरवधि नाथ जप्यमानेऽस्मिन्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śivaśivaśiveti nāmani tava niravadhi nātha japyamāne'smin|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">आस्वादयन्भवेयं कमपि महारसमपुनरुक्तम्॥५.२३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Āsvādayanbhaveyaṁ kamapi mahārasamapunaruktam||5.23||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.24" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्फुरदनन्तचिदात्मकविष्टपे परिनिपीतसमस्तजडाध्वनि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sphuradanantacidātmakaviṣṭape parinipītasamastajaḍādhvani|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अगणितापरचिन्मयगण्डिके प्रविचरेयमहं भवतोऽर्चिता॥५.२४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Agaṇitāparacinmayagaṇḍike pravicareyamahaṁ bhavato'rcitā||5.24||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.25" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्ववपुषि स्फुटभासिनि शाश्वते स्थितिकृते न किमप्युपयुज्यते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svavapuṣi sphuṭabhāsini śāśvate sthitikṛte na kimapyupayujyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इति मतिः सुदृढा भवतात्परं मम भवच्चरणाब्जरजः शुचेः॥५.२५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Iti matiḥ sudṛḍhā bhavatātparaṁ mama bhavaccaraṇābjarajaḥ śuceḥ||5.25||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v5.26" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">किमपि नाथ कदाचन चेतसि स्फुरति तद्भवदङ्घ्रितलस्पृशाम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kimapi nātha kadācana cetasi sphurati tadbhavadaṅghritalaspṛśām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">गलति यत्र समस्तमिदं सुधासरसि विश्वमिदं दिश मे सदा॥५.२६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Galati yatra samastamidaṁ sudhāsarasi viśvamidaṁ diśa me sadā||5.26||</span></p>
