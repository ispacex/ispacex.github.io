<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.1" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अहो कोऽपि जयत्येष स्वादुः पूजामहोत्सवः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aho ko'pi jayatyeṣa svāduḥ pūjāmahotsavaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यतोऽमृतरसास्वादमश्रूण्यपि ददत्यलम्॥१७.१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yato'mṛtarasāsvādamaśrūṇyapi dadatyalam||17.1||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.2" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">व्यापाराः सिद्धिदाः सर्वे ये त्वत्पूजापुरःसराः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vyāpārāḥ siddhidāḥ sarve ye tvatpūjāpuraḥsarāḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तानां त्वन्मयाः सर्वे स्वयं सिद्धय एव ते॥१७.२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktānāṁ tvanmayāḥ sarve svayaṁ siddhaya eva te||17.2||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.3" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सर्वदा सर्वभावेषु युगपत्सर्वरूपिणम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sarvadā sarvabhāveṣu yugapatsarvarūpiṇam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वामर्चयन्त्यविश्रन्तं ये ममैतेऽधिदेवताः॥१७.३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvāmarcayantyaviśrantaṁ ye mamaite'dhidevatāḥ||17.3||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.4" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ध्यानायसतिरस्कारसिद्धस्त्वत्स्पर्शनोत्सवः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dhyānāyasatiraskārasiddhastvatsparśanotsavaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूजाविधिरिति ख्यातो भक्तानां स सदास्तु मे॥१७.४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūjāvidhiriti khyāto bhaktānāṁ sa sadāstu me||17.4||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.5" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तानां समतासारविषुवत्समयः सदा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktānāṁ samatāsāraviṣuvatsamayaḥ sadā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वद्भावरसपीयूषरसेन्नैषां सदार्चनम्॥१७.५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvadbhāvarasapīyūṣarasennaiṣāṁ sadārcanam||17.5||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.6" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यस्यानारम्भपर्यन्तौ न च कालक्रमः प्रभो।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yasyānārambhaparyantau na ca kālakramaḥ prabho|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूजात्मासौ क्रिया तस्याः कर्तारस्त्वज्जुषः परम्॥१७.६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūjātmāsau kriyā tasyāḥ kartārastvajjuṣaḥ param||17.6||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.7" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ब्रह्मादीनामपीशास्ते ते च सौभाग्यभागिनः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Brahmādīnāmapīśāste te ca saubhāgyabhāginaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">येषां स्वप्नेऽपि मोहेऽपि स्थितस्त्वत्पूजनोत्सवः॥१७.७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yeṣāṁ svapne'pi mohe'pi sthitastvatpūjanotsavaḥ||17.7||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.8" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जपतां जुह्वतां स्नातां ध्यायतां न च केवलम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Japatāṁ juhvatāṁ snātāṁ dhyāyatāṁ na ca kevalam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तानां भवदभ्यर्चामहो यावद्यदा तदा॥१७.८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktānāṁ bhavadabhyarcāmaho yāvadyadā tadā||17.8||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.9" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवत्पूजासुधास्वादसम्भोगसुखिनः सदा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavatpūjāsudhāsvādasambhogasukhinaḥ sadā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">इन्द्रादीनामथ ब्रह्ममुख्यानामस्ति कः समः॥१७.९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Indrādīnāmatha brahmamukhyānāmasti kaḥ samaḥ||17.9||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.10" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जगत्क्षोभैकजनके भवत्पूजामहोत्सवे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jagatkṣobhaikajanake bhavatpūjāmahotsave|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यत्प्राप्यं प्राप्यते किञ्चिद्भक्ता एव विदन्ति तत्॥१७.१०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yatprāpyaṁ prāpyate kiñcidbhaktā eva vidanti tat||17.10||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.11" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वद्धाम्नि चिन्मये स्थित्वा षट्त्रिंशत्तत्त्वकर्मभिः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvaddhāmni cinmaye sthitvā ṣaṭtriṁśattattvakarmabhiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कायवाक्चित्तचेष्टाद्यैरर्चये त्वां सदा विभो॥१७.११॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kāyavākcittaceṣṭādyairarcaye tvāṁ sadā vibho||17.11||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.12" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवत्पूजामयासङ्गसम्भोगसुखिनो मम।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavatpūjāmayāsaṅgasambhogasukhino mama|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">प्रयातु कालः सकलोऽप्यनन्तोऽपीयदर्थये॥१७.१२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Prayātu kālaḥ sakalo'pyananto'pīyadarthaye||17.12||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.13" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवत्पूजामृतरसाभोगलम्पटत विभो।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavatpūjāmṛtarasābhogalampaṭata vibho|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विवर्धतामनुदिनं सदा च फलतां मम॥१७.१३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vivardhatāmanudinaṁ sadā ca phalatāṁ mama||17.13||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.14" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जगद्विलयसञ्जातसुधैकरसनिर्भरे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jagadvilayasañjātasudhaikarasanirbhare|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">ज्वदब्धौ त्वां महात्मानमर्चन्नासीय सर्वदा॥१७.१४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jvadabdhau tvāṁ mahātmānamarcannāsīya sarvadā||17.14||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.15" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अशेषवासनाग्रन्थिविच्छेदसरलं सदा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aśeṣavāsanāgranthivicchedasaralaṁ sadā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">मनो निवेद्यते भक्तैः स्वादु पूजाविधौ तव॥१७.१५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Mano nivedyate bhaktaiḥ svādu pūjāvidhau tava||17.15||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.16" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अधिष्ठायैव विषयानिमाः करणवृत्तयः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Adhiṣṭhāyaiva viṣayānimāḥ karaṇavṛttayaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तानां प्रेषयन्ति स्वत्पूजार्थममृतासवम्॥१७.१६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktānāṁ preṣayanti svatpūjārthamamṛtāsavam||17.16||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.17" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तानां भक्तिसंवेगमहोष्मविवशात्मनाम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktānāṁ bhaktisaṁvegamahoṣmavivaśātmanām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कोऽन्यो निर्वाणहेतुः स्यात्त्वत्पूजामृतमज्जनात्॥१७.१७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ko'nyo nirvāṇahetuḥ syāttvatpūjāmṛtamajjanāt||17.17||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.18" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सततं त्वत्पदाभ्यर्चासुधापानमहोत्सवः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Satataṁ tvatpadābhyarcāsudhāpānamahotsavaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वत्प्रसादैकसम्प्राप्तिहेतुर्मे नाथ कल्पताम्॥१७.१८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvatprasādaikasamprāptiheturme nātha kalpatām||17.18||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.19" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अनुभूयासमीशान प्रतिकर्म क्षणात्क्षणम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Anubhūyāsamīśāna pratikarma kṣaṇātkṣaṇam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवत्पूजामृतापानमदास्वादमहामुदम्॥१७.१९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavatpūjāmṛtāpānamadāsvādamahāmudam||17.19||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.20" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">दृष्टर्थ एव भक्तानां भवत्पूजामहोद्यमः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Dṛṣṭartha eva bhaktānāṁ bhavatpūjāmahodyamaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तदैव यदसम्भाव्यं सुखमास्वादयन्ति ते॥१७.२०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tadaiva yadasambhāvyaṁ sukhamāsvādayanti te||17.20||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.21" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यावन्न लब्धस्त्वत्पूजासुधास्वादमहोत्सवः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yāvanna labdhastvatpūjāsudhāsvādamahotsavaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तावन्नास्वादितो मन्ये लवोऽपि सुखसम्पदः॥१७.२१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tāvannāsvādito manye lavo'pi sukhasampadaḥ||17.21||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.22" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तानां विषयन्वेषाभासायासाद्विनैव सा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktānāṁ viṣayanveṣābhāsāyāsādvinaiva sā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अयत्नसिद्धं त्वद्धामस्थितिः पूजासु जायते॥१७.२२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ayatnasiddhaṁ tvaddhāmasthitiḥ pūjāsu jāyate||17.22||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.23" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">न प्राप्यमस्ति भक्तनां नाप्येषामस्ति दुर्लभम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Na prāpyamasti bhaktanāṁ nāpyeṣāmasti durlabham|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">केवलं विचरन्त्येते भवत्पूजामदोन्मदाः॥१७.२३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kevalaṁ vicarantyete bhavatpūjāmadonmadāḥ||17.23||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.24" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अहो भक्तिभरोदारचेतसां वरद त्वयि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aho bhaktibharodāracetasāṁ varada tvayi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्लाघ्यः पूजाविधिः कोऽपि यो न याच्ञाकलङ्कितः॥१७.२४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Slāghyaḥ pūjāvidhiḥ ko'pi yo na yācñākalaṅkitaḥ||17.24||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.25" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">का न शोभा न को ह्लादः का समृद्धिर्न वापरा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kā na śobhā na ko hlādaḥ kā samṛddhirna vāparā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">को वा न मोक्षः कोऽप्येष महादेवो यदर्च्यते॥१७.२५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ko vā na mokṣaḥ ko'pyeṣa mahādevo yadarcyate||17.25||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.26" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अन्तरुल्लसदच्छाच्छभक्तिपीयूषपोषितम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Antarullasadacchācchabhaktipīyūṣapoṣitam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवत्पूजोपयोगाय शरीरमिदमस्तु मे॥१७.२६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavatpūjopayogāya śarīramidamastu me||17.26||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.27" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वत्पादपूजासम्भोगपरतन्त्रः सदा विभो।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvatpādapūjāsambhogaparatantraḥ sadā vibho|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भूयासं जगतामीश एकः स्वच्छन्दचेष्टितः॥१७.२७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhūyāsaṁ jagatāmīśa ekaḥ svacchandaceṣṭitaḥ||17.27||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.28" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">त्वद्ध्यानदर्शनस्पर्शतृषि केषामपि प्रभो।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tvaddhyānadarśanasparśatṛṣi keṣāmapi prabho|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जायते शीतलस्वादु भवत्पूजामहासरः॥१७.२८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jāyate śītalasvādu bhavatpūjāmahāsaraḥ||17.28||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.29" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यथा त्वमेव जगतः पूजासम्भोगभाजनम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yathā tvameva jagataḥ pūjāsambhogabhājanam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">तथेश भक्तिमानेव पूजासम्भोगभाजनम्॥१७.२९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Tatheśa bhaktimāneva pūjāsambhogabhājanam||17.29||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.30" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कोऽप्यसौ जयति स्वामिन्भवत्पूजामहोत्सवः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ko'pyasau jayati svāminbhavatpūjāmahotsavaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">षट्त्रिंशतोऽपि तत्त्वानां क्षोभो यत्रोल्लसत्यलम्॥१७.३०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ṣaṭtriṁśato'pi tattvānāṁ kṣobho yatrollasatyalam||17.30||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.31" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">नमस्तेभ्यो विभो येषां भक्तिपीयूषवारिणा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Namastebhyo vibho yeṣāṁ bhaktipīyūṣavāriṇā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूज्यान्येव भवन्ति त्वत्पूजोपकरणान्यपि॥१७.३१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūjyānyeva bhavanti tvatpūjopakaraṇānyapi||17.31||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.32" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूजारम्भे विभो ध्यात्वा मन्त्राधेयां त्वदात्मताम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūjārambhe vibho dhyātvā mantrādheyāṁ tvadātmatām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वात्मन्येव परे भक्ता मान्ति हर्षेण न क्वचित्॥१७.३२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svātmanyeva pare bhaktā mānti harṣeṇa na kvacit||17.32||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.33" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">राज्यलाभादिवोत्फुल्लैः कैश्चित्पूजामहोत्सवे।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Rājyalābhādivotphullaiḥ kaiścitpūjāmahotsave|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सुधासवेन सकला जगती संविभज्यते॥१७.३३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sudhāsavena sakalā jagatī saṁvibhajyate||17.33||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.34" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूजामृतापानमयो येषां भोगः प्रतिक्षणम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūjāmṛtāpānamayo yeṣāṁ bhogaḥ pratikṣaṇam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">किं देवा उत मुक्तास्ते किं वा केऽप्येव ते जनाः॥१७.३४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kiṁ devā uta muktāste kiṁ vā ke'pyeva te janāḥ||17.34||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.35" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूजोपकरणीभूतविश्ववेशेन गौरवम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūjopakaraṇībhūtaviśvaveśena gauravam|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अहो किमपि भक्तानां किमप्येव च लाघवम्॥१७.३५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aho kimapi bhaktānāṁ kimapyeva ca lāghavam||17.35||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.36" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूजामयाक्षविक्षेपक्षोभादेवामृतोद्गमः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūjāmayākṣavikṣepakṣobhādevāmṛtodgamaḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तानां क्षीरजलधिक्षोभादिव दिवौकसाम्॥१७.३६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktānāṁ kṣīrajaladhikṣobhādiva divaukasām||17.36||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.37" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">पूजां के चन मन्यन्ते धेनुं कामदुघामिव।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Pūjāṁ ke cana manyante dhenuṁ kāmadughāmiva|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सुधाधाराधिकरसां धयन्त्यन्तर्मुखाः परे॥१७.३७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sudhādhārādhikarasāṁ dhayantyantarmukhāḥ pare||17.37||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.38" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तानामक्षविक्षेपोऽप्येष संसारसम्मतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktānāmakṣavikṣepo'pyeṣa saṁsārasammataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उपनीय किमप्यन्तः पुष्णात्यर्चामहोत्सवम्॥१७.३८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Upanīya kimapyantaḥ puṣṇātyarcāmahotsavam||17.38||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.39" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तिक्षोभवशादीश स्वात्मभूतेऽर्चनं त्वयि।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktikṣobhavaśādīśa svātmabhūte'rcanaṁ tvayi|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">चित्रं दैन्याय नो यावद्दीनतायाः परं फलम्॥१७.३९॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Citraṁ dainyāya no yāvaddīnatāyāḥ paraṁ phalam||17.39||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.40" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उपचारपदं पूजा केषाञ्चित्त्वत्पदाप्तये।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Upacārapadaṁ pūjā keṣāñcittvatpadāptaye|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भक्तानां भवदैकात्म्यनिर्वृत्तिप्रसरस्तु सः॥१७.४०॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhaktānāṁ bhavadaikātmyanirvṛttiprasarastu saḥ||17.40||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.41" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अप्यसम्बद्धरूपार्चाभक्त्युन्मादनिरर्गलैः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Apyasambaddharūpārcābhaktyunmādanirargalaiḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">वितन्यमाना लभते प्रतिष्ठां त्वयि कामपि॥१७.४१॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Vitanyamānā labhate pratiṣṭhāṁ tvayi kāmapi||17.41||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.42" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">स्वादुभक्तिरसास्वादस्तब्धीभूतमनश्च्युताम्।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Svādubhaktirasāsvādastabdhībhūtamanaścyutām|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">शम्भो त्वमेव ललितः पूजानां किल भाजनम्॥१७.४२॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Śambho tvameva lalitaḥ pūjānāṁ kila bhājanam||17.42||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.43" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">परिपूर्णानि शुद्धानि भक्तिमन्ति स्थिराणि च।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Paripūrṇāni śuddhāni bhaktimanti sthirāṇi ca|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">भवत्पूजाविधौ नाथ साधनानि भवन्तु मे॥१७.४३॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Bhavatpūjāvidhau nātha sādhanāni bhavantu me||17.43||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.44" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अशेषपूजासत्कोशे त्वत्पूजाकर्मणि प्रभो।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aśeṣapūjāsatkośe tvatpūjākarmaṇi prabho|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">अहो करणवृन्दस्य कापि लक्ष्मीर्विजृम्भते॥१७.४४॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Aho karaṇavṛndasya kāpi lakṣmīrvijṛmbhate||17.44||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.45" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">एषा पेशलिमा नाथ तवैव किल दृश्यते।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Eṣā peśalimā nātha tavaiva kila dṛśyate|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">विश्वेश्वरोऽपि भृत्यैर्यदर्च्यसे यश्च लभ्यसे॥१७.४५॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Viśveśvaro'pi bhṛtyairyadarcyase yaśca labhyase||17.45||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.46" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">सदामुर्त्तादमूर्त्ताद्वा भावाद्यद्वाप्यभावतः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Sadāmurttādamūrttādvā bhāvādyadvāpyabhāvataḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">उत्थेयान्मे प्रशस्तस्य भवत्पूजामहोत्सवः॥१७.४६॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Uttheyānme praśastasya bhavatpūjāmahotsavaḥ||17.46||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.47" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">कामक्रोधाभिमानैस्त्वामुपहरीकृतैः सदा।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Kāmakrodhābhimānaistvāmupaharīkṛtaiḥ sadā|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">येऽर्चयन्ति नमस्तेभ्यस्तेषां तुष्टोऽस्मि तत्त्वतः॥१७.४७॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Ye'rcayanti namastebhyasteṣāṁ tuṣṭo'smi tattvataḥ||17.47||</span></p>

<hr class="pv-rule" />

<p class="pv-pair pv-c" id="v17.48" data-pv="w1"><span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">जयत्येष भवद्भक्तिभाजां पूजाविधिः परः।</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Jayatyeṣa bhavadbhaktibhājāṁ pūjāvidhiḥ paraḥ|</span><br />
<span class="pv-sa pv-src" lang="sa">यस्तृणैः क्रियमानोऽपि रत्नैरेवोपकल्पते॥१७.४८॥</span><br />
<span class="pv-iast">Yastṛṇaiḥ kriyamāno'pi ratnairevopakalpate||17.48||</span></p>
